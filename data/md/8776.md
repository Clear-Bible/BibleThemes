# 8776: lies

Statements or answers which are untrue. Lying, false witness, deceit and false dealings are all condemned in Scripture.

## Lying is condemned
**[PSA 34:12-34:13](bibleref:19034012-19034013)**; **[EPH 4:25](bibleref:49004025)**; **[COL 3:9-3:10](bibleref:51003009-51003010)** *See also* [EXO 20:16](bibleref:02020016) parallel DEU 5:20; [LEV 19:11-19:12](bibleref:03019011-03019012); [PRO 6:16-6:17](bibleref:20006016-20006017), [PRO 12:22](bibleref:20012022); [JER 17:9-17:10](bibleref:24017009-24017010)
