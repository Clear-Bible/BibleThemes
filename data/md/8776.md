# 8776: lies

Statements or answers which are untrue. Lying, false witness, deceit and false dealings are all condemned in Scripture.



## Lying is condemned
**PSA 34:12-34:13**; **EPH 4:25**; **COL 3:9-3:10** *See also* EXO 20:16 parallel DEU 5:20; LEV 19:11-19:12; PRO 6:16-6:17, PRO 12:22; JER 17:9-17:10

## Examples of lying
 GEN 3:4-3:5 *the serpent to Eve*; ; GEN 4:9 *Cain to the LORD*; ; GEN 12:11-12:13 *Abraham urges Sarah to lie to the Egyptians*; ; GEN 18:15 *Sarah to the LORD*; ; GEN 20:1-20:2 *Abraham to Abimelech*; ; GEN 26:6-26:7 *Isaac to the men of Gerar*; GEN 27:18-27:19, GEN 27:24; 
GEN 39:14-39:18 *Potiphar's wife to her household servants*; ; 2KI 5:22-5:27 *Gehazi to Naaman and then to Elisha*; ; ACT 5:1-5:10 *Ananias and Sapphira*; ACT 6:11, ACT 6:13; 
ACT 25:7; ; ROM 3:8

## Lying will not go unnoticed or unpunished
**PRO 19:9** *See also* PSA 5:6, PSA 52:2-52:5, PSA 55:23, PSA 101:7; JER 6:13-6:15, JER 23:32; NAM 3:1; 1TI 1:9-1:10; REV 21:8, REV 21:27, REV 22:15

## God's people should resist the temptation to lie
 JOB 27:2-27:5; ; REV 2:2

## Lying can be an act of malice
**PRO 26:24-26:28**

## Lying can be the sign of a dead conscience
**1TI 4:1-4:2**

## A lying spirit
 *See also* 1KI 22:21-22:23 parallel 2CH 18:20-18:22

## Lying is the hallmark of the devil and the antichrist
**JHN 8:44** *See also* 1JN 2:22; REV 13:14, REV 20:7-20:10
