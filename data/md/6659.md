# 6659: freedom, acts of in OT

The OT provides many examples of God delivering his people from bondage, of which the greatest is the exodus. These acts of deliverance foreshadow and prepare the way for the redeeming work of God through Jesus Christ, revealed in the NT.



## God's people regain their freedom


### The exodus from Egypt as an act of deliverance
**[EXO 20:2](bibleref:02020002)** *See also* [EXO 12:42](bibleref:02012042), [EXO 16:6](bibleref:02016006), [EXO 16:32](bibleref:02016032) *The people of Israel were frequently reminded that God had delivered them from Egypt* ; [JOS 24:6](bibleref:06024006); [JDG 6:8](bibleref:07006008); [2SA 7:6](bibleref:10007006); [1KI 8:16](bibleref:11008016); [2CH 7:22](bibleref:14007022); [PSA 80:8](bibleref:19080008); [JER 2:6](bibleref:24002006), [JER 11:4](bibleref:24011004); [HOS 12:9](bibleref:28012009); [AMO 2:10](bibleref:30002010); [MIC 6:4](bibleref:33006004); [HAG 2:5](bibleref:37002005); [HEB 8:9](bibleref:58008009); [JUD 1:5](bibleref:65001005)

## God's purposes in bringing about his people's freedom


### God sets his people free to be his own
 [EXO 19:4](bibleref:02019004); [NUM 15:41](bibleref:04015041); [HOS 13:4](bibleref:28013004)

### God sets his people free to serve him
 [EXO 19:3-19:6](bibleref:02019003-02019006); [LEV 25:55](bibleref:03025055)

### God sets his people free to praise him
 [ISA 43:21](bibleref:23043021)

### God sets his people free to be holy
 [LEV 11:45](bibleref:03011045); [DEU 28:9-28:10](bibleref:05028009-05028010)

### God sets his people free to receive his promised gifts
 [EXO 3:8](bibleref:02003008) *The inheritance is the promised land of Canaan, which foreshadows the gospel inheritance of eternal life in the kingdom of God* ; [NUM 14:7-14:8](bibleref:04014007-04014008); [DEU 8:7-8:9](bibleref:05008007-05008009); [EZK 20:6](bibleref:26020006)
