# 7386: incense

An expensive resin from southern Arabia and the fragrant aroma it emits when offered in worship ritual. It is also called frankincense.

## Regulations governing the offering of incense


### The priest was to keep incense burning on the altar daily
**[2CH 13:11](bibleref:14013011)** *See also* [EXO 30:7-30:9](bibleref:02030007-02030009); [NUM 4:16](bibleref:04004016); [DEU 33:10](bibleref:05033010); [1SA 2:28](bibleref:09002028); [1CH 6:49](bibleref:13006049), [1CH 9:29](bibleref:13009029); [LUK 1:9](bibleref:42001009)

Korah, Dathan and Abiram:
 [NUM 16:16-16:18](bibleref:04016016-04016018), [NUM 16:35](bibleref:04016035), [NUM 16:40](bibleref:04016040), [NUM 16:46-16:47](bibleref:04016046-04016047)
[2CH 26:16-26:19](bibleref:14026016-14026019) *Uzziah's pride* 

### Failure to burn incense is disobedience
 [2CH 29:7-29:8](bibleref:14029007-14029008)

**[EXO 30:34-30:38](bibleref:02030034-02030038)** *See also* [EXO 25:6](bibleref:02025006) parallel EXO 35:8; [EXO 35:28](bibleref:02035028)
