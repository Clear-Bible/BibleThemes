# 8149: revival, nature of

Scripture indicates that a number of characteristics precede revival, including repentance, humility and obedience.



## Characteristics which precede revival


### God's people long for renewal of their lives
**[PSA 80:18](bibleref:19080018)** *See also* [PSA 74:22](bibleref:19074022), [PSA 80:1-80:17](bibleref:19080001-19080017), [PSA 80:19](bibleref:19080019), [PSA 85:6](bibleref:19085006)

### God's people must repent
**[2CH 7:14](bibleref:14007014)** *See also* [1KI 8:46-8:50](bibleref:11008046-11008050) parallel 2CH 6:26-6:27; [ISA 64:1-64:7](bibleref:23064001-23064007); [HOS 5:15](bibleref:28005015); [ACT 3:19](bibleref:44003019)

### God's people experience a new awareness of sin
**[2KI 22:11](bibleref:12022011)** *See also* [PSA 32:3-32:5](bibleref:19032003-19032005)

### God's people need to be humble
**[ISA 57:15](bibleref:23057015)** *See also* [PSA 149:4](bibleref:19149004); [ISA 66:2](bibleref:23066002); [MIC 6:6-6:8](bibleref:33006006-33006008)

### God's people are revived through God's initiative
**[ISA 59:16](bibleref:23059016)** *See also* [JER 24:7](bibleref:24024007), [JER 33:6-33:9](bibleref:24033006-24033009); [TIT 3:5](bibleref:56003005)

## Characteristic results of revival


### People experience inward change
**[HEB 8:10-8:12](bibleref:58008010-58008012)** *See also* [JER 31:33-31:34](bibleref:24031033-24031034); [EZK 11:19](bibleref:26011019); [ACT 2:42-2:47](bibleref:44002042-44002047)

### People live obedient lives
**[EZK 11:20](bibleref:26011020)** *See also* [EPH 4:1-4:3](bibleref:49004001-49004003); [1TH 1:7-1:8](bibleref:52001007-52001008)

### People are zealous for God's work
**[EZR 5:1-5:2](bibleref:15005001-15005002)** *See also* [HAG 1:12-1:15](bibleref:37001012-37001015)

### People are generous in giving
**[EXO 36:5](bibleref:02036005)** *See also* [1CH 29:6-29:9](bibleref:13029006-13029009); [2CH 31:3-31:8](bibleref:14031003-14031008); [ACT 11:28-11:30](bibleref:44011028-44011030)

### People delight in worshipping God
**[EZR 3:11](bibleref:15003011)** *See also* [ISA 12:1-12:6](bibleref:23012001-23012006)

### People are joyful
**[ACT 13:49-13:52](bibleref:44013049-44013052)** *See also* [ISA 35:1-35:10](bibleref:23035001-23035010); [ACT 8:5-8:8](bibleref:44008005-44008008)
