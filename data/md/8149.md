# 8149: revival, nature of

Scripture indicates that a number of characteristics precede revival, including repentance, humility and obedience.



## Characteristics which precede revival


### God's people long for renewal of their lives
**PSA 80:18** *See also* PSA 74:22, PSA 80:1-80:17, PSA 80:19, PSA 85:6

### God's people must repent
**2CH 7:14** *See also* 1KI 8:46-8:50 parallel 2CH 6:26-6:27; ISA 64:1-64:7; HOS 5:15; ACT 3:19

### God's people experience a new awareness of sin
**2KI 22:11** *See also* PSA 32:3-32:5

### God's people need to be humble
**ISA 57:15** *See also* PSA 149:4; ISA 66:2; MIC 6:6-6:8

### God's people are revived through God's initiative
**ISA 59:16** *See also* JER 24:7, JER 33:6-33:9; TIT 3:5

## Characteristic results of revival


### People experience inward change
**HEB 8:10-8:12** *See also* JER 31:33-31:34; EZK 11:19; ACT 2:42-2:47

### People live obedient lives
**EZK 11:20** *See also* EPH 4:1-4:3; 1TH 1:7-1:8

### People are zealous for God's work
**EZR 5:1-5:2** *See also* HAG 1:12-1:15

### People are generous in giving
**EXO 36:5** *See also* 1CH 29:6-29:9; 2CH 31:3-31:8; ACT 11:28-11:30

### People delight in worshipping God
**EZR 3:11** *See also* ISA 12:1-12:6

### People are joyful
**ACT 13:49-13:52** *See also* ISA 35:1-35:10; ACT 8:5-8:8
