# 5225: barrenness

A lack of fertility, especially the inability of a woman to conceive.



## Barren land


### Barrenness describes waste and unproductive land
**JER 2:6** *See also* DEU 32:10; JOL 2:20

### Barren land as a sign of God's disfavour
**GEN 3:18** *Thorns and thistles are often used to designate barren or unproductive land.* *See also* HOS 10:8; HEB 6:8

## Fertility, as opposed to barrenness, a sign of God's blessing
**EXO 23:25-23:26** *Consequently, the inability to conceive was regarded as a sign of God's displeasure.* *See also* DEU 7:13-7:14, DEU 11:14-11:15; PSA 113:9; ISA 41:18ISA 54:1; GAL 4:27

## The barren woman


### Her desire for children
**GEN 30:1-30:2** *See also* PRO 30:16

### Examples of barren women
 GEN 16:1 *Sarah*; ; GEN 29:31 *Rachel*; ; JDG 13:3 *Manoah's wife*; ; 1SA 2:5 *Hannah*; ; LUK 1:7 *Elizabeth*; ; LUK 1:36-1:37 *The ability to bear a child in these circumstances, especially in old age, was seen as a sign of God's blessing and his miraculous power.*

### Barrenness was sometimes seen as a sign of God's disfavour
 GEN 16:1-16:4, GEN 20:17-20:18, GEN 29:31-29:34; ; 1SA 1:1-1:20; ; ISA 4:1; ; LUK 1:5-1:25

### Childlessness was not always the result of barrenness
**DEU 28:41**

### Barrenness might be an advantage in some circumstances
**LUK 23:29** *Referring to the disasters about to come upon Jerusalem.*

## Metaphorically, barrenness may describe an unproductive or desperate condition
**HEB 6:7-6:8** *See also* JOB 3:7; LUK 13:6-13:9
