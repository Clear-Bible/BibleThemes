# 5225: barrenness

A lack of fertility, especially the inability of a woman to conceive.

## Barren land


### Barrenness describes waste and unproductive land
**[JER 2:6](bibleref:24002006)** *See also* [DEU 32:10](bibleref:05032010); [JOL 2:20](bibleref:29002020)

### Barren land as a sign of God's disfavour
**[GEN 3:18](bibleref:01003018)** *Thorns and thistles are often used to designate barren or unproductive land.*  *See also* [HOS 10:8](bibleref:28010008); [HEB 6:8](bibleref:58006008)

## Fertility, as opposed to barrenness, a sign of God's blessing
**[EXO 23:25-23:26](bibleref:02023025-02023026)** *Consequently, the inability to conceive was regarded as a sign of God's displeasure.*  *See also* [DEU 7:13-7:14](bibleref:05007013-05007014), [DEU 11:14-11:15](bibleref:05011014-05011015); [PSA 113:9](bibleref:19113009); [ISA 41:18](bibleref:23041018)[ISA 54:1](bibleref:23054001); [GAL 4:27](bibleref:48004027)

## The barren woman


### Her desire for children
**[GEN 30:1-30:2](bibleref:01030001-01030002)** *See also* [PRO 30:16](bibleref:20030016)

### Examples of barren women
 [GEN 16:1](bibleref:01016001) *Sarah* ; [GEN 29:31](bibleref:01029031) *Rachel* ; [JDG 13:3](bibleref:07013003) *Manoah's wife* ; [1SA 2:5](bibleref:09002005) *Hannah* ; [LUK 1:7](bibleref:42001007) *Elizabeth* ; [LUK 1:36-1:37](bibleref:42001036-42001037) *The ability to bear a child in these circumstances, especially in old age, was seen as a sign of God's blessing and his miraculous power.* 

### Barrenness was sometimes seen as a sign of God's disfavour
 [GEN 16:1-16:4](bibleref:01016001-01016004), [GEN 20:17-20:18](bibleref:01020017-01020018), [GEN 29:31-29:34](bibleref:01029031-01029034); [1SA 1:1-1:20](bibleref:09001001-09001020); [ISA 4:1](bibleref:23004001); [LUK 1:5-1:25](bibleref:42001005-42001025)

### Childlessness was not always the result of barrenness
**[DEU 28:41](bibleref:05028041)**

### Barrenness might be an advantage in some circumstances
**[LUK 23:29](bibleref:42023029)** *Referring to the disasters about to come upon Jerusalem.* 

## Metaphorically, barrenness may describe an unproductive or desperate condition
**[HEB 6:7-6:8](bibleref:58006007-58006008)** *See also* [JOB 3:7](bibleref:18003007); [LUK 13:6-13:9](bibleref:42013006-42013009)
