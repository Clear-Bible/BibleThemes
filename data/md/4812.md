# 4812: darkness, and God's judgment

Darkness is used figuratively of God's present and final judgment upon individuals, unfaithful Israel and the heathen nations.



## Darkness as a symbol of judgment upon ungodly individuals
**MAT 25:30** *See also* 1SA 2:9-2:10; JOB 5:13-5:14, JOB 12:25, JOB 15:22-15:23, JOB 15:30, JOB 18:5-18:6, JOB 18:18, JOB 20:24-20:26, JOB 22:10-22:11 *Eliphaz reasons on the assumption that Job's suffering proves his wickedness*; PSA 35:5-35:6; ISA 8:22; JER 23:11-23:12; MAT 22:13; 2PE 2:17; JUD 1:13; REV 16:10-16:11

## Darkness as a symbol of judgment upon unfaithful Israel
**DEU 28:28-28:29**; **ISA 5:29-5:30** *This will be fulfilled at the time of Israel's captivity.* *See also* ISA 59:9-59:10; ROM 11:10; JER 4:27-4:28, JER 13:16; LAM 3:2, LAM 3:6; MAT 8:12

## Darkness as a symbol of judgment upon heathen nations
 ISA 47:5-47:7; ; EZK 30:18, EZK 32:7-32:8; ; NAM 1:8

## Darkness as a symbol of judgment at the day of the Lord
**JOL 2:31** *See also* ISA 13:9-13:10, ISA 34:4; JOL 2:1-2:2, JOL 2:10, JOL 3:14-3:15; AMO 5:18-5:20, AMO 8:9; ZEP 1:14-1:15; ZEC 14:6; MAT 24:29; ACT 2:19-2:20; 1TH 5:1-5:3; REV 6:12-6:17
