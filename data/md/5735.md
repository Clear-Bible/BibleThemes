# 5735: sexuality

Men and women are intended to complement one another and to take pleasure in one another. God has provided for the full and glad expression of this in marriage.



## Sexuality comes from God


### It was created by God
**GEN 1:27**

### God intended men and women to complement one another
**1CO 11:11** *See also* GEN 2:18-2:22

## Sexual differences


### In strength
 1PE 3:7

### In role
 1CO 11:3-11:5, 1CO 14:34-14:35; ; EPH 5:22-5:25; ; COL 3:18-3:19; ; 1TI 2:12-2:14; ; 1PE 3:1

### In dress
**DEU 22:5** *See also* 1CO 11:14-11:15

## Sexual activity


### It was ordained by God
**GEN 1:28** *This command was made before the fall, and no guilt attaches to it.*

### Marriage in its proper context
**GEN 2:23-2:24** *“one flesh” implies both bodily union and an inseparable intimate relationship.* *See also* MAT 19:4-19:6 parallel MRK 10:6-10:9; 1CO 7:2-7:4; EPH 5:31-5:33

### Sexual desire
**SNG 4:3-4:15** *“Song of Songs” celebrates the beauty of sex in a loving context. It contains much delicate and intimate imagery that applies directly to human lovers and can also be applied to Jesus Christ and the church.* *See also* GEN 3:16; SNG 1:2-1:4, SNG 4:16, SNG 7:11-7:13, SNG 8:10

### Abstinence is possible
 MAT 19:12; ; 1CO 7:1, 1CO 7:5

## Perversions of sexuality


### Adultery
**EXO 20:14** parallel DEU 5:18 *See also* HEB 13:4

### Lust
**MAT 5:28** *Lust may also lead to other sexual sins.* *See also* EPH 4:19, EPH 5:3; COL 3:5; 1PE 4:3

### Promiscuity
 DEU 22:13-22:21; ; ROM 1:24

### Prostitution
 LEV 19:29

### Homosexuality
**ROM 1:26-1:27** *See also* GEN 19:4-19:7; LEV 18:22

### Bestiality
 EXO 22:19

### Sexual perversion will be punished
 1CO 6:9-6:10; ; REV 21:8, REV 22:15

### Sexual perversion can be cleansed
 1CO 6:11

## Sexuality is transcended in heaven
**MAT 22:30**
