# 5735: sexuality

Men and women are intended to complement one another and to take pleasure in one another. God has provided for the full and glad expression of this in marriage.

## Sexuality comes from God


### It was created by God
**[GEN 1:27](bibleref:01001027)**

### God intended men and women to complement one another
**[1CO 11:11](bibleref:46011011)** *See also* [GEN 2:18-2:22](bibleref:01002018-01002022)

### In strength

### In role

### In dress
**[DEU 22:5](bibleref:05022005)** *See also* [1CO 11:14-11:15](bibleref:46011014-46011015)

## Sexual activity


### It was ordained by God
**[GEN 1:28](bibleref:01001028)** *This command was made before the fall, and no guilt attaches to it.* 

### Marriage in its proper context
**[GEN 2:23-2:24](bibleref:01002023-01002024)** *“one flesh” implies both bodily union and an inseparable intimate relationship.*  *See also* [MAT 19:4-19:6](bibleref:40019004-40019006) parallel MRK 10:6-10:9; [1CO 7:2-7:4](bibleref:46007002-46007004); [EPH 5:31-5:33](bibleref:49005031-49005033)

### Sexual desire
**[SNG 4:3-4:15](bibleref:22004003-22004015)** *“Song of Songs” celebrates the beauty of sex in a loving context. It contains much delicate and intimate imagery that applies directly to human lovers and can also be applied to Jesus Christ and the church.*  *See also* [GEN 3:16](bibleref:01003016); [SNG 1:2-1:4](bibleref:22001002-22001004), [SNG 4:16](bibleref:22004016), [SNG 7:11-7:13](bibleref:22007011-22007013), [SNG 8:10](bibleref:22008010)

### Abstinence is possible
 [MAT 19:12](bibleref:40019012); [1CO 7:1](bibleref:46007001), [1CO 7:5](bibleref:46007005)
