# 7370: guilt offering

An offering that resembled the sin offering, but was offered especially in cases where restitution could be made for an unintentional sin.

## Circumstances for making the guilt offering


### Unintentional sin against God
**[LEV 5:17](bibleref:03005017)** *See also* [LEV 5:15](bibleref:03005015)

### Sin against a neighbour where restitution can be made
 [LEV 5:16](bibleref:03005016) *Where restitution is possible it is a requirement* ; [LEV 6:1-6:5](bibleref:03006001-03006005) *A surcharge of twenty per cent was to be added* ; [NUM 5:5-5:8](bibleref:04005005-04005008) *Restitution must be made to God if the wronged person is not accessible.* 

## The nature of the guilt offering


### A ram without defect
**[LEV 5:18](bibleref:03005018)**

### Regulations governing the offering
 *See also* [LEV 7:1-7:6](bibleref:03007001-03007006); [NUM 18:9](bibleref:04018009)

## Other situations requiring a guilt offering
 [LEV 14:12-14:14](bibleref:03014012-03014014) *cleansing from infectious diseases* ; [LEV 19:20-19:22](bibleref:03019020-03019022) *wronging a man by intercourse with a slave girl promised to him* ; [NUM 6:12](bibleref:04006012) *cleansing a Nazirite after contact with a dead body* ; [EZR 10:19](bibleref:15010019) *for returning exiles who had intermarried*
