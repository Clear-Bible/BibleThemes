# 5572: sword

A hand-wielded blade in common use throughout biblical times as a weapon of war and in OT times as an instrument of execution. The sword is also a symbol of aggression, authority and power.

## The sword as the principal weapon of war


### It was in common use
**[EXO 17:13](bibleref:02017013)** *See also* [NUM 21:24-21:35](bibleref:04021024-04021035); [JOS 11:10-11:12](bibleref:06011010-06011012), [JOS 19:47](bibleref:06019047)

### Swordsmen fought and fell in great numbers
**[JDG 3:28-3:30](bibleref:07003028-07003030)** *See also* [JDG 20:17](bibleref:07020017); [2SA 24:9](bibleref:10024009)

### Some swordsmen showed great strength and ability
**[2SA 23:10](bibleref:10023010)** *See also* [1SA 21:8-21:9](bibleref:09021008-09021009); [2SA 23:12](bibleref:10023012)

## The sword used for other purposes


### As an instrument of execution and decapitation
**[2SA 1:14-1:16](bibleref:10001014-10001016)** *See also* [1SA 15:33](bibleref:09015033), [1SA 17:51](bibleref:09017051), [1SA 22:18-22:19](bibleref:09022018-09022019); [1KI 19:1](bibleref:11019001); [ACT 12:2](bibleref:44012002); [ROM 8:35](bibleref:45008035)

### For murder
**[2KI 19:37](bibleref:12019037)** *See also* [JOS 6:21](bibleref:06006021); [1KI 2:34](bibleref:11002034); [ACT 12:2](bibleref:44012002)

### For committing suicide
**[1SA 31:4-31:6](bibleref:09031004-09031006)** parallel 1CH 10:4 *See also* [ACT 16:27](bibleref:44016027)

## Metaphorical use of the sword


### To symbolise God's opposition to the sinful
**[GEN 3:24](bibleref:01003024)** *See also* [NUM 22:21-22:23](bibleref:04022021-04022023), [NUM 22:31](bibleref:04022031); [DEU 33:29](bibleref:05033029)

### To symbolise warfare
 [LEV 26:6-26:8](bibleref:03026006-03026008)

### To symbolise temporal power
**[EZK 32:11-32:12](bibleref:26032011-26032012)** *See also* [PSA 45:3](bibleref:19045003); [MIC 5:6](bibleref:33005006)

### To symbolise suffering
**[LUK 2:34-2:35](bibleref:42002034-42002035)**

**[EXO 5:3](bibleref:02005003)**; **[MAT 10:34](bibleref:40010034)** *See also* [JDG 7:20-7:22](bibleref:07007020-07007022); [2CH 20:9](bibleref:14020009); [PSA 7:12](bibleref:19007012); [ISA 31:8](bibleref:23031008); [JER 50:35-50:37](bibleref:24050035-24050037); [EZK 11:8-11:10](bibleref:26011008-26011010), [EZK 21:3](bibleref:26021003), [EZK 33:2](bibleref:26033002)
