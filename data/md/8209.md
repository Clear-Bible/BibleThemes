# 8209: commitment, to Jesus Christ

Commitment to Jesus Christ is grounded in the knowledge of his saving power and divinity, and expresses itself in adoration and obedience to Jesus Christ.



## Jesus Christ commanded people to make the commitment to follow him
**MAT 4:19** parallel MRK 1:17**MAT 9:9** parallels MRK 2:14, LUK 5:27 *See also* MAT 19:21-19:22; JHN 1:43, JHN 21:19, JHN 21:22; ROM 15:5; 1CO 1:12 *There may have been a “Christ-party” at Corinth, or in contrast with the sectarianism, Paul may be asserting his own commitment to Jesus Christ.*

## Jesus Christ frequently spelt out the cost of commitment to him
**MAT 10:37-10:38** parallel LUK 14:26-14:27**MAT 16:24** parallels MRK 8:34, LUK 9:23**JHN 12:25-12:26** *See also* MAT 8:22, MAT 10:39 parallels MRK 8:35, LUK 17:33; MAT 19:21 parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22; LUK 14:28-14:33; 1PE 2:21

## Jesus Christ's demand for commitment was sometimes met with a refusal
**LUK 9:59** *See also* LUK 9:61

## Sometimes people followed Jesus Christ without being truly committed to him
**JHN 6:2** *This would have been true for many in the crowds.* *See also* JHN 6:66

## Some committed themselves wholeheartedly to his invitation
 MAT 4:20 parallel MRK 1:18; ; MAT 4:22 parallel MRK 1:20; ; MAT 9:9 parallels MRK 2:14, LUK 5:28; ; JHN 1:40

## Secret commitment to Jesus Christ
**JHN 12:42** *See also* JHN 3:1-3:2

## True commitment to Jesus Christ is seen in love and obedience to him
**JHN 14:21**; **PHP 2:12** *In response to who Jesus Christ is and what he has done.* *See also* JHN 8:31, JHN 14:15, JHN 14:23-14:24; 1CO 11:1, 1CO 16:22; EPH 6:5-6:6, EPH 6:24; 1JN 2:3, 1JN 3:22-3:24; REV 14:4
