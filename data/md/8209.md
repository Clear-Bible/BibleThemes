# 8209: commitment, to Jesus Christ

Commitment to Jesus Christ is grounded in the knowledge of his saving power and divinity, and expresses itself in adoration and obedience to Jesus Christ.



## Jesus Christ commanded people to make the commitment to follow him
**[MAT 4:19](bibleref:40004019)** parallel MRK 1:17**[MAT 9:9](bibleref:40009009)** parallels MRK 2:14, LUK 5:27 *See also* [MAT 19:21-19:22](bibleref:40019021-40019022); [JHN 1:43](bibleref:43001043), [JHN 21:19](bibleref:43021019), [JHN 21:22](bibleref:43021022); [ROM 15:5](bibleref:45015005); [1CO 1:12](bibleref:46001012) *There may have been a “Christ-party” at Corinth, or in contrast with the sectarianism, Paul may be asserting his own commitment to Jesus Christ.* 

## Jesus Christ frequently spelt out the cost of commitment to him
**[MAT 10:37-10:38](bibleref:40010037-40010038)** parallel LUK 14:26-14:27**[MAT 16:24](bibleref:40016024)** parallels MRK 8:34, LUK 9:23**[JHN 12:25-12:26](bibleref:43012025-43012026)** *See also* [MAT 8:22](bibleref:40008022), [MAT 10:39](bibleref:40010039) parallels MRK 8:35, LUK 17:33; [MAT 19:21](bibleref:40019021) parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22; [LUK 14:28-14:33](bibleref:42014028-42014033); [1PE 2:21](bibleref:60002021)
