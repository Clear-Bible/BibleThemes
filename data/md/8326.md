# 8326: purity, moral and spiritual

Moral and spiritual purity is demanded by God of his people. God calls his people to be holy, just as he is holy.



## Divine purity


### The purity of God
**HAB 1:13** *See also* JOB 4:17; PSA 18:26; 1JN 1:5

### The purity of Jesus Christ
**1JN 3:3** *See also* MAT 17:2; HEB 7:26

## Fellowship with God demands purity
**PSA 24:3-24:4**; **MAT 5:8** *See also* PSA 51:6, PSA 73:1

## God's people are to live pure lives
**COL 3:5** *See also* LEV 18:30; JOB 31:1; PSA 51:10; 1CO 5:7-5:8 *Yeast is a symbol of sin*; 1CO 6:15-6:20; 1TH 4:3; TIT 1:15, TIT 2:5; HEB 13:4; JAS 1:27, JAS 4:8

## Purity is unobtainable by human effort


### Because of the sinful human nature
**JOB 14:4** *See also* JOB 15:14, JOB 25:4; ECC 7:20

### Because of the tendency to idolatry
**HOS 8:5**

### Because of self-deception
**PRO 30:12** *See also* PRO 20:9

### Because of an unwillingness to be cleansed
**EZK 24:13**

## Symbols of purification from sin
**PSA 51:7** *See also* ISA 6:6 *fire*; MRK 1:4 *water baptism*; 2CO 11:2 *virginity*; 2PE 3:14 *spotlessness*

## God promises to purify his people
**EZK 36:25** *See also* ZEP 3:9; ZEC 13:1

## Purification through Jesus Christ


### Through pardon offered in his death
**HEB 9:14** *See also* HEB 12:24; 1JN 1:7

### Through his word
**JHN 15:3** *See also* JHN 13:10

### Through his presence in resisting temptation
**JAS 4:7-4:8** *See also* 1PE 5:9

### Through hope built on his promises
**TIT 2:12-2:13** *See also* 1JN 3:3

## Purification through judgment


### In the OT
**LEV 18:28** *See also* DEU 13:5, DEU 17:7, DEU 17:12; JDG 20:13; MAL 3:2-3:4

### In the NT
**1CO 5:13**

## Symbols of purity


### Whiteness
**DAN 7:9** *See also* PSA 51:7; ISA 1:18; MAT 17:2 parallel MRK 9:3; MAT 28:3; MRK 16:5; JHN 20:12; REV 1:14

### Light
**1JN 1:7** *See also* JOB 25:5; EZK 1:27; 2CO 6:14; EPH 5:8-5:9

### Washing
**PSA 26:6** *See also* PSA 51:2

### Fire
**DAN 7:9-7:10** *See also* ISA 33:14; EZK 8:2; REV 1:14-1:15
