# 8741: failure

Scripture indicates various ways in which believers may fail God, while emphasising that God will never fail his people.

## God will never fail his people
**[1CH 28:20](bibleref:13028020)** *See also* [JOS 1:8-1:9](bibleref:06001008-06001009); [PSA 89:28](bibleref:19089028)

### God's righteousness will not fail
 [ISA 51:6](bibleref:23051006)

### God's compassion will not fail
**[LAM 3:22-3:23](bibleref:25003022-25003023)**

## God's word will not fail
**[ISA 55:10-55:11](bibleref:23055010-23055011)** *See also* [ROM 9:6](bibleref:45009006)
