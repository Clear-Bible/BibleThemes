# 8741: failure

Scripture indicates various ways in which believers may fail God, while emphasising that God will never fail his people.



## God will never fail his people
**1CH 28:20** *See also* JOS 1:8-1:9; PSA 89:28

### God's righteousness will not fail
 ISA 51:6

### God's compassion will not fail
**LAM 3:22-3:23**

## God's word will not fail
**ISA 55:10-55:11** *See also* ROM 9:6

## Examples of failing God


### Failing to abide in him
**PSA 14:3** *See also* PSA 58:3, PSA 78:57; JER 2:19, JER 3:22, JER 15:6; JHN 15:6; 2PE 3:17

### Failing to believe in him
**JHN 20:27** *See also* NUM 14:11-14:12; PSA 78:21-78:33; HEB 3:12

### Failing to fear him
**GEN 20:11** *See also* EXO 9:30; DEU 25:18; JOB 6:14; LUK 23:40

### Failing to honour him
**MAL 2:2** *See also* NUM 20:12; 1SA 2:29-2:30; DAN 5:22-5:23; ROM 2:23

### Failing to love him
**1CO 16:22** *See also* JOS 23:10-23:13; MAT 10:37; REV 2:4

### Failing to obey him
 2KI 17:14-17:20; ; MAT 7:26

### Failing to remember him
**DEU 8:11, DEU 32:18** *See also* JDG 3:7, JDG 8:33-8:34; 1SA 12:9; PSA 78:11, PSA 106:21; ISA 17:10

### Failing to seek him
**2CH 12:14** *See also* PSA 14:2-14:3, PSA 119:155; ISA 9:13; ZEP 1:6

### Failing to serve him
**DEU 28:47-28:48** *See also* JOB 21:14-21:15; JER 2:20; MAL 3:14; ROM 1:25

### Failing to trust him
**MAT 6:30** parallel LUK 12:28 *See also* MAT 14:31, MAT 16:8, MAT 17:17-17:20

### Peter's failure of Jesus Christ
 MAT 26:33-26:35 parallel MRK 14:29-14:31; ; LUK 22:31-22:34; ; JHN 18:25-18:27
