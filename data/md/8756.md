# 8756: folly, examples of

Human folly is seen in rebellion against God, ignoring his way and will, a failure to understand him: and moral or spiritual irresponsibility.



## Examples of human folly


### The folly of God's people
**[JER 4:22](bibleref:24004022)** *See also* [DEU 32:6](bibleref:05032006) *rejecting God* ; [JER 10:8](bibleref:24010008) *worshipping idols* ; [HOS 7:11](bibleref:28007011) *seeking foreign help rather than trusting God* 

### The folly of Israel's leaders
**[JER 10:21](bibleref:24010021)** *The shepherds are those entrusted with the task of guiding and caring for God's people* ; **[EZK 13:3](bibleref:26013003)** *See also* [MAT 23:16-23:17](bibleref:40023016-40023017)

[1SA 25:25](bibleref:09025025) *Nabal. In Hebrew this name means “fool”.* [2SA 24:10](bibleref:10024010) parallel 1CH 21:8
[2CH 16:7-16:9](bibleref:14016007-14016009) *Asa, king of Judah* ; [ISA 19:11-19:13](bibleref:23019011-23019013) *Pharaoh's advisors* ; [MAT 7:26](bibleref:40007026) *the foolish builder* ; [MAT 25:1-25:3](bibleref:40025001-40025003) *the foolish virgins* ; [LUK 12:16-12:21](bibleref:42012016-42012021) *the rich fool* ; [GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013) *the Galatian Christians*
