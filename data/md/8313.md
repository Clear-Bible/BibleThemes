# 8313: nurture

The activity of caring for others and nourishing them in the life of faith. This is necessary not only for children in the home but also for new or weak believers in the church.



## The nurture of children in the home


### By parental instruction and example
**DEU 4:9-4:10** *See also* DEU 6:4-6:9, DEU 11:18-11:21; PRO 22:6; EPH 6:4; COL 3:21; 2TI 1:5, 2TI 3:15

### Examples of parental care
**PRO 1:8-1:10** *See also* PRO 2:1-2:5, PRO 3:1-3:2, PRO 4:1-4:4, PRO 5:1-5:2, PRO 6:1-6:3, PRO 6:20-6:24

## The nurture of young believers in the church


### By the caring attitude of leaders
**1TH 2:7-2:12** *See also* JHN 21:15-21:17; ACT 20:18-20:20; 1CO 4:14-4:15; 1TH 3:10; TIT 1:9; 1PE 5:1-5:3

### By the leader's pattern of life
**TIT 2:6-2:7** *See also* ACT 20:33-20:35; 1CO 4:16-4:17, 1CO 11:1; PHP 4:9; 1TH 1:5-1:6

### By the caring attitude of fellow believers
**1TH 5:14-5:15** *See also* ROM 14:1; 1CO 8:9-8:13; GAL 6:1-6:2; 1TH 5:11; TIT 2:3-2:5

### By appropriate teaching
**1PE 2:2** *See also* 1CO 3:2, 1CO 14:20; HEB 5:11-5:14

### By encouraging spiritual growth
**2PE 3:18** *See also* 2CO 3:18; EPH 4:15; 2TH 1:3
