# 3281: Holy Spirit, inspiration of

The Holy Spirit inspires prophecy and gives knowledge of God, insight and wisdom. Scripture declares itself to have been inspired by God.



## The Holy Spirit inspired the writers of Scripture
**2TI 3:16** *See also* MAT 22:43 parallel MRK 12:36; ACT 1:16, ACT 4:25, ACT 28:25; HEB 3:7, HEB 9:8, HEB 10:15

## The Holy Spirit inspires believers to honour Jesus Christ
**1CO 12:3** *See also* 1JN 4:2

## Purposes of the Holy Spirit's inspiration
**EPH 1:17** *See also* 2TI 3:16-3:17

## The Holy Spirit inspired prophecy
**NUM 24:2-24:3**; **2PE 1:21** *See also* NUM 11:25-11:29; 1SA 10:6, 1SA 10:10, 1SA 19:20, 1SA 19:23; 2SA 23:2; 1CH 12:18; 2CH 15:1, 2CH 20:14, 2CH 24:20; ACT 11:28, ACT 19:6; 1PE 1:11-1:12

## People aware of the Holy Spirit's inspiration
**2SA 23:2** *See also* ISA 48:16 *Isaiah*; MIC 3:8 *Micah*; ACT 15:28 *the Council of Jerusalem*1CO 2:13, 1CO 7:40
