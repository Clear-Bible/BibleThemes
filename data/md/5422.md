# 5422: musicians

Players of musical instruments. Levitical musicians played an extensive role in Israelite worship. Other musicians are also mentioned in Scripture, in both sacred and secular contexts.



## Levitical musicians in the tabernacle


### Appointed by David
**1CH 6:31-6:47** *See also* 1CH 15:16-15:17, 1CH 25:1-25:5

### Responsibilities
**1CH 15:19-15:21** *See also* 1CH 15:16, 1CH 25:6-25:7

### Allocation of duties
**1CH 25:8-25:31**

## Levitical musicians in the temple
**2CH 5:12-5:13** *See also* 1CH 9:33; 2CH 35:15

## Other musicians


### Jubal was the first musician
**GEN 4:17-4:21**

### King David was a renowned musician
**1SA 16:15-16:18** *See also* 1SA 16:23, 1SA 18:10-18:11, 1SA 19:9-19:10; 2SA 6:5, 2SA 23:1

### Unidentified musicians
**PSA 68:24-68:25** *See also* 2KI 3:11-3:19; PSA 137:1-137:3; REV 18:21-18:22
