# 8634: amen

A Hebrew term expressing the ideas of truth and reliability, often used in prayer and adoration.



## Amen as an expression of worship
**1CH 16:36**; **EPH 3:21**; **REV 7:11-7:12** *See also* NEH 5:13, NEH 8:6 *The repetition of “Amen” here conveys Ezra's intensity of feeling as he read the Book of the Law. The response of the people represents a solemn, formal acceptance of God's commandments*; PSA 72:19, PSA 106:48; ROM 9:5 *“Amen” can mean either “Yes indeed, it is so” or “So be it”*; ROM 11:36, ROM 16:25-16:27; 1CO 14:16; GAL 1:3-1:5; PHP 4:20; 1TI 1:17, 1TI 6:15-6:16; 2TI 4:18; HEB 13:20-13:21; 1PE 4:11, 1PE 5:10-5:11; 2PE 3:17-3:18; JUD 1:24-1:25; REV 1:4-1:7, REV 19:4 *the worship of heaven*

## Amen confirms a grace or blessing
**GAL 6:18** *See also* ROM 15:33; 1CO 16:24; PHP 4:23; REV 22:20-22:21

## Amen confirms a vow or action
**JER 28:5-28:6** *See also* NUM 5:20-5:22; DEU 27:15; 1KI 1:32-1:36; JER 11:1-11:5

## Amen as a title of Jesus Christ
**REV 3:14** *See also* 2CO 1:18-1:20 *The implication is that all God's promises find their fulfilment in Jesus Christ.*
