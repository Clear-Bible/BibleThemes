# 8634: amen

A Hebrew term expressing the ideas of truth and reliability, often used in prayer and adoration.

## Amen as an expression of worship
**[1CH 16:36](bibleref:13016036)**; **[EPH 3:21](bibleref:49003021)**; **[REV 7:11-7:12](bibleref:66007011-66007012)** *See also* [NEH 5:13](bibleref:16005013), [NEH 8:6](bibleref:16008006) *The repetition of “Amen” here conveys Ezra's intensity of feeling as he read the Book of the Law. The response of the people represents a solemn, formal acceptance of God's commandments* ; [PSA 72:19](bibleref:19072019), [PSA 106:48](bibleref:19106048); [ROM 9:5](bibleref:45009005) *“Amen” can mean either “Yes indeed, it is so” or “So be it”* ; [ROM 11:36](bibleref:45011036), [ROM 16:25-16:27](bibleref:45016025-45016027); [1CO 14:16](bibleref:46014016); [GAL 1:3-1:5](bibleref:48001003-48001005); [PHP 4:20](bibleref:50004020); [1TI 1:17](bibleref:54001017), [1TI 6:15-6:16](bibleref:54006015-54006016); [2TI 4:18](bibleref:55004018); [HEB 13:20-13:21](bibleref:58013020-58013021); [1PE 4:11](bibleref:60004011), [1PE 5:10-5:11](bibleref:60005010-60005011); [2PE 3:17-3:18](bibleref:61003017-61003018); [JUD 1:24-1:25](bibleref:65001024-65001025); [REV 1:4-1:7](bibleref:66001004-66001007), [REV 19:4](bibleref:66019004) *the worship of heaven*
