# 5791: anger, human

Human anger arises for a number of reasons, some of which are acceptable and others which are not. Scripture stresses the potentially destructive aspects of human anger and urges moderation.



## Reasons for human anger


### Jealousy
**GEN 4:4-4:5** *See also* 1SA 17:28, 1SA 18:8; 1KI 21:4; LUK 15:28

### Pride
**JON 4:1** *See also* 2SA 3:8, 2SA 6:8; 2KI 5:11; 2CH 26:19

### The truth
**2CH 16:10** *Hanani had prophesied against the king.* *See also* NUM 24:10; JER 37:15

### Human anger is sometimes justified
**EXO 32:19** *See also* EXO 11:8, EXO 16:20; NUM 16:15; LEV 10:16; 1SA 11:6, 1SA 20:34; 2SA 12:5; NEH 5:6; MRK 3:5; LUK 14:21

## Results of human anger


### Strife
**GEN 4:8** *See also* 1SA 20:30; PSA 124:2-124:3; PRO 29:22, PRO 30:33

### God's judgment
**MAT 5:22** *See also* GEN 49:5-49:7

### Sin
**JAS 1:20** *See also* PSA 4:4; PRO 14:17; 2CO 12:20; EPH 4:26

## Dealing with anger


### Becoming slow to anger
**1CO 13:5** *See also* ECC 7:9; TIT 1:7; JAS 1:19

### Not taking revenge
**ROM 12:19**

### Renouncing anger
**EPH 4:31** *See also* PSA 37:8; COL 3:8; 1TI 2:8
