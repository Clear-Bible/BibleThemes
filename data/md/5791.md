# 5791: anger, human

Human anger arises for a number of reasons, some of which are acceptable and others which are not. Scripture stresses the potentially destructive aspects of human anger and urges moderation.

### Jealousy
**[GEN 4:4-4:5](bibleref:01004004-01004005)** *See also* [1SA 17:28](bibleref:09017028), [1SA 18:8](bibleref:09018008); [1KI 21:4](bibleref:11021004); [LUK 15:28](bibleref:42015028)

### Pride
**[JON 4:1](bibleref:32004001)** *See also* [2SA 3:8](bibleref:10003008), [2SA 6:8](bibleref:10006008); [2KI 5:11](bibleref:12005011); [2CH 26:19](bibleref:14026019)
