# 8845: unfruitfulness

A lack of fruitfulness, whether material or spiritual, which is often seen as a sign of God's disfavour or anger.



## Unfruitfulness as a moral or spiritual condition
**MAT 21:19** parallel MRK 11:14 *See also* JOB 15:32-15:33; EZK 17:9, EZK 19:10-19:12; MAT 21:43; HEB 6:7-6:8

## Reasons for unfruitfulness


### Disobedience
**LEV 26:18-26:20** *See also* DEU 11:17, DEU 28:15-28:16; PSA 78:46, PSA 107:33-107:34; ISA 5:10, ISA 32:10; HOS 8:7; HAG 2:19

### A continuing preoccupation with the world
**MAT 13:22** parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14

### A broken relationship with Jesus Christ
**JHN 15:4** *See also* JHN 15:6

## Some results of unfruitfulness


### Unfruitfulness condemned
 ISA 5:1-5:7

### Unfruitfulness leads to rejection
 MAT 3:8-3:10 parallel LUK 3:9; ; MAT 7:19

## Unfruitfulness retrieved
**LUK 13:6-13:9** *The parable holds out the possibility of restoration.*

### Discipline with a view to renewed fruitfulness
**JHN 15:2**
