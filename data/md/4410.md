# 4410: banquets

Grand, celebratory meals consisting usually of rich food and flowing wine. Banquets usually accompanied weddings, religious festivals and other celebrations. Prophecies of the Messianic age refer to a banquet to which the righteous will be invited.

## Characteristics of banquets


### They were usually held in a hall
**[SNG 2:4](bibleref:22002004)** *See also* [EST 7:8](bibleref:17007008); [DAN 5:10](bibleref:27005010)

### Honoured guests were given special places
**[LUK 14:7-14:11](bibleref:42014007-42014011)** *See also* [PRO 25:6-25:7](bibleref:20025006-20025007); [MAT 23:6](bibleref:40023006) parallels MRK 12:39, LUK 20:46

### Guests reclined on couches
 [EST 1:6](bibleref:17001006); [MAT 26:7](bibleref:40026007) parallels MRK 14:3, LUK 7:36; [JHN 12:2](bibleref:43012002)

### Invitations were sent out
 [LUK 14:17](bibleref:42014017) parallel MAT 22:3

## Occasions for holding banquets


### To celebrate weddings
**[MAT 22:2](bibleref:40022002)** *See also* [GEN 29:21-29:22](bibleref:01029021-01029022) *a feast celebrates Jacob's wedding* ; [JDG 14:10](bibleref:07014010) *Samson gives a wedding feast* ; [EST 2:18](bibleref:17002018) *Xerxes celebrates Esther's choice as queen with a banquet* ; [MAT 25:10](bibleref:40025010); [LUK 12:36](bibleref:42012036); [JHN 2:8-2:9](bibleref:43002008-43002009); [REV 19:9](bibleref:66019009) *the marriage supper of the Lamb* 

### To celebrate birthdays
 [GEN 40:20](bibleref:01040020); [MRK 6:21](bibleref:41006021)

### To celebrate other events
 [GEN 21:8](bibleref:01021008) *Abraham celebrates Isaac's weaning.* [EST 8:17](bibleref:17008017), [EST 9:17-9:19](bibleref:17009017-17009019)
[LUK 15:23](bibleref:42015023) *The father celebrates the prodigal son's return.* 

### Religious celebrations
**[PSA 22:29](bibleref:19022029)** *See also* [2CH 30:21-30:22](bibleref:14030021-14030022), [2CH 35:13](bibleref:14035013); [ZEC 7:6](bibleref:38007006)
