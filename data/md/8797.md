# 8797: persecution, attitudes to

Scripture outlines the attitudes which believers should adopt in the face of persecution, laying particular emphasis upon the faithfulness of God, the example of Jesus Christ and the need for patience and hope by believers.



## The call to endure in the face of persecution


### Passively
**PSA 119:87** *See also* MAT 10:22, MAT 10:28; 1CO 4:12; 2TH 1:4; JAS 5:8

### Actively
**HEB 12:1** *See also* EPH 6:10-6:20; 2TI 2:3; 1PE 5:9; REV 2:3

## Attitudes believers are to adopt in facing persecution


### Living holy and forgiving lives
**1PE 2:12** *See also* MAT 5:44; ROM 12:19-12:20; 1PE 2:15, 1PE 2:23, 1PE 3:16

## Rejoicing in suffering
**ROM 5:3-5:5** *See also* PSA 30:5; MAT 5:11-5:12 parallel LUK 6:22-6:23; ACT 5:41, ACT 16:22-16:25; ROM 12:15; COL 1:24; 1PE 1:6, 1PE 1:8, 1PE 4:12, 1PE 4:16

### Relying on the promise of God's grace to endure
**LAM 3:22-3:23** *See also* PSA 18:17-18:19; NAM 1:7-1:8; ROM 8:18, ROM 8:35-8:39; 2TI 3:10-3:11; HEB 13:6; REV 3:10

### Trusting in the promise of God's presence
**HEB 13:5** *See also* PSA 23:4; ISA 43:2, ISA 43:5; MAT 28:20; 2CO 4:9

### Praying always
**EPH 6:18** *See also* PSA 35:24, PSA 38:15-38:16, PSA 129:5, PSA 143:9; MIC 7:7-7:8; LUK 6:28, LUK 18:7-18:8; 1PE 4:7

## The certain prospect of victory over persecution


### The triumph of Jesus Christ
**1CO 15:25** *See also* OBA 1:21; PHP 3:20-3:21; 2TH 1:6-1:8; REV 11:15

### The vindication of the saints
**JUD 1:24** *See also* EZR 6:8; EST 6:11-6:13; PSA 126:1; DAN 6:26; HAG 2:22-2:23; 2TI 1:12; REV 2:10, REV 7:13-7:17, REV 12:11

## Believers are to take heart from the example of Jesus Christ in facing persecution


### The persecution of Jesus Christ was predicted in the OT
 PSA 22:1-22:18; ; ISA 50:6, ISA 53:7-53:12

### The fulfilment of these predictions
