# 3290: Holy Spirit, the life-giver

Through the Holy Spirit, God gives birth to and supports both natural and spiritual life. For this reason, Scripture likens the Holy Spirit to life-giving water.



## The creating Spirit (or breath) of God gives and sustains life
**[GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002)** *See also* [GEN 2:7](bibleref:01002007); [JOB 26:13](bibleref:18026013), [JOB 27:3](bibleref:18027003), [JOB 32:8](bibleref:18032008), [JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004), [JOB 34:14](bibleref:18034014); [PSA 33:6](bibleref:19033006), [PSA 104:30](bibleref:19104030)

## The Holy Spirit gives new life
**[EZK 37:1-37:14](bibleref:26037001-26037014)** *See also* [EZK 36:26-36:28](bibleref:26036026-26036028)

## The Holy Spirit is the life-giver in the conception of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 1:18](bibleref:40001018)** *See also* [MAT 1:20](bibleref:40001020); [LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035)

## The Holy Spirit gives resurrection life to Jesus Christ
**[1PE 3:18](bibleref:60003018)** *See also* [ROM 1:4](bibleref:45001004)

## The Holy Spirit brings new spiritual life
**[JHN 3:5](bibleref:43003005)** *See also* [JHN 3:6](bibleref:43003006), [JHN 3:8](bibleref:43003008), [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063)

**[ROM 8:2](bibleref:45008002)** *See also* [ROM 8:6](bibleref:45008006), [ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013); [GAL 6:8](bibleref:48006008)
