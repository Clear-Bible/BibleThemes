# 2203: Jesus Christ, titles and names of

These, in their rich variety, throw light on either the person of Jesus Christ or on some aspect of his ministry.



## Titles relating to Jesus Christ's identity


### The exact image of God
**HEB 1:3** *See also* JHN 14:9; 2CO 4:4; COL 1:15

### The first and last, the Alpha and Omega
**REV 22:13** *“Alpha” and “Omega” are the first and last letters of the Greek alphabet.* *See also* REV 1:17, REV 2:8, REV 21:6

### The Word of God
**JHN 1:1** *See also* MAL 3:1; JHN 1:14; 1JN 1:1; REV 19:13

### The last Adam
**1CO 15:45** *See also* ROM 5:14

### The bright Morning Star
**REV 22:16** *See also* 2PE 1:19

### The rising sun
 MAL 4:2; ; LUK 1:78

### The Living One
**REV 1:18** *See also* JHN 5:26, JHN 11:25

### The Amen
**REV 3:14** *See also* 2CO 1:20

### The true light
**JHN 1:3-1:9** *See also* ISA 9:2; LUK 2:32; JHN 3:19-3:21, JHN 8:12, JHN 12:46

### The Righteous One
**ACT 3:14** *See also* JER 23:6, JER 33:15-33:16; ACT 7:52, ACT 22:14

### The Lion of Judah
 REV 5:5

### The king of the Jews
 MAT 2:1-2:2, MAT 27:37

## The ``I am'' sayings of John's Gospel
**JHN 8:58** *See also* JHN 6:35 *the bread of life*JHN 8:12, JHN 9:5
JHN 10:7-10:10 *the gate*; JHN 10:11-10:14 *the good shepherd*; JHN 11:25 *the resurrection and the life*; JHN 14:6 *the way, the truth and the life*; JHN 15:1-15:5 *the true vine*

## Titles relating to Jesus Christ's ministry


### The seed of Abraham
**GAL 3:16** *See also* GEN 12:7, GEN 13:15, GEN 24:7

### The Root and Offspring of David
**REV 22:16**

### The faithful witness
**REV 1:5** *See also* ISA 55:4; JHN 18:37; REV 3:14

### Immanuel
**MAT 1:23** *See also* ISA 7:14, ISA 8:8

### The capstone
**MAT 21:42** parallels MRK 12:10, LUK 20:17 *See also* PSA 118:22; ACT 4:11; EPH 2:20-2:21; 1PE 2:6-2:7

### The rock
**1CO 10:4** *See also* ISA 8:14, ISA 28:16; ROM 9:32-9:33; 1PE 2:8

### The bridegroom
**JHN 3:29** *John the Baptist describing himself as the bridegroom's friend, and Jesus Christ as the bridegroom.* *See also* MAT 9:15 parallels MRK 2:19-2:20, LUK 5:34-5:35; MAT 25:1-25:10; REV 19:7, REV 21:2

### The firstborn among many brothers
 ROM 8:29

### The firstfruits
 1CO 15:23

### The firstborn from the dead
 REV 1:5

### The heir of all things
 HEB 1:2

## Titles relating to Jesus Christ's authority


### Lord
**ACT 2:25** *See also* MAT 7:21; LUK 6:46; JHN 6:68; ROM 10:13; 1CO 3:5; COL 3:23; 1TH 4:16-4:17; 2PE 1:11

### The head of the church
 EPH 1:22-1:23, EPH 4:15, EPH 5:23; ; COL 2:19

### The Chief Shepherd
**1PE 5:4** *See also* MAT 2:6; MIC 5:2; JHN 10:11; 1PE 2:25; HEB 13:20

### Prince
 ACT 5:31

### Rabbi
 JHN 1:38, JHN 1:49, JHN 20:16

 *See also* ISA 53:3

 *See also* LUK 1:69
