# 7335: circumcision, physical

In the OT, circumcision is seen as an outward sign of membership of Israel, the people of God.



## The significance of circumcision in God's covenant with Abraham
**[GEN 17:10-17:14](bibleref:01017010-01017014)** *See also* [ACT 7:8](bibleref:44007008)

### Circumcision is also for Abraham's descendants
 [GEN 17:7](bibleref:01017007)

## Circumcision as a sign of national identity
Dinah's brothers and the Shechemites:
 [GEN 34:8-34:9](bibleref:01034008-01034009), [GEN 34:14-34:17](bibleref:01034014-01034017)
[EXO 4:24-4:26](bibleref:02004024-02004026); [JOS 5:4-5:8](bibleref:06005004-06005008)

## Circumcision is integrated into the Mosaic law
 [LEV 12:3](bibleref:03012003); [JHN 7:22](bibleref:43007022) *It was later sometimes forgotten that circumcision predated the Mosaic law.* 

## Circumcision as an important Passover restriction
 [EXO 12:44](bibleref:02012044), [EXO 12:48-12:49](bibleref:02012048-02012049); [JDG 14:3](bibleref:07014003) *Some of Israel's neighbours practised circumcision, but not the Philistines.* 

## Circumcision was not necessarily a sign of consecration to God
 [JER 9:25-9:26](bibleref:24009025-24009026)

## NT accounts of circumcision practised in obedience to the law
 [LUK 1:59](bibleref:42001059) *John* ; [LUK 2:21](bibleref:42002021) *Jesus Christ* ; [JHN 7:23](bibleref:43007023) *Circumcision was permitted on the Sabbath* ; [PHP 3:5](bibleref:50003005) *Paul refers to his own circumcision* ; [ACT 16:3](bibleref:44016003) *Timothy* 

## The subject of circumcision debated by the first Christians
 [ACT 10:45](bibleref:44010045), [ACT 11:2](bibleref:44011002), [ACT 15:5](bibleref:44015005); [GAL 2:3](bibleref:48002003) *That Titus, a Greek, was not required to be circumcised illustrates the change in the attitude of the church concerning the relevance of the law to Gentile believers* ; [EPH 2:11](bibleref:49002011); [COL 3:11](bibleref:51003011); [TIT 1:10](bibleref:56001010)
