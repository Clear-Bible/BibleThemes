# 8315: orthodoxy, in OT

The acceptance of God's revelation of himself to his people that should lead to worship and obedience.



## Foundational truths about Israel's God


### God is one
**DEU 6:4**

### God is unique
**DEU 4:39** *See also* DEU 4:35; PSA 97:9; ISA 44:6-44:8

### God is self-existent
**EXO 3:14**

### God is the Creator
**ISA 40:28** *See also* GEN 1:1, GEN 2:7; JOB 38:4

### God will not tolerate rivals
 EXO 20:3-20:6 parallel DEU 5:7-5:8; ; DEU 8:19-8:20, DEU 13:6-13:8; ; ISA 42:8

### God commands universal worship
**PSA 86:9-86:10** *See also* ISA 19:21; ZEC 8:20-8:22

## Right belief is passed on through the community of faith
**PSA 145:4** *See also* EXO 12:24-12:27; DEU 6:6-6:8, DEU 6:20-6:25; JOS 4:20-4:24; PSA 78:2-78:6

## Right belief demands right practice


### Reverent fear
**DEU 10:12-10:13**

### Worship
 *See also* DEU 12:4-12:7; 2KI 17:35-17:39; 1CH 16:28-16:29 parallel PSA 96:7-96:9; PSA 95:6-95:7

### Observance of festivals
 DEU 16:1-16:2, DEU 16:9-16:17

### Offering acceptable sacrifices
 LEV 1:2-1:4, LEV 5:5-5:6; ; MAL 1:7-1:8

### Faithfulness to the covenant
 DEU 29:9-29:15; ; PSA 25:10

### Devotion to God
**DEU 6:5** *See also* DEU 11:1; 1CH 22:19; PSA 31:23; HOS 6:6

### Trust in God's faithfulness
**PSA 4:5, PSA 33:4** *See also* PSA 37:3-37:5, PSA 115:9-115:11, PSA 119:89-119:93

### Obedience
**DEU 5:32** *See also* DEU 6:1-6:3, DEU 26:16-26:19; 1SA 15:22; ISA 30:21; JER 7:21-7:23

### Justice in society
**MIC 6:8** *See also* PSA 106:3; PRO 21:3; ISA 1:11-1:17, ISA 56:1

## Walking in God's ways


### God's ways are right
**HOS 14:9** *See also* PRO 10:29

### Walking in God's ways brings his blessing
 GEN 18:19; ; DEU 5:33, DEU 28:9, DEU 30:19-30:20

### Examples of kings who walked in God's ways
 2SA 22:22 parallel PSA 18:21 *David*; ; 2CH 14:2-14:4 parallel 1KI 15:11-15:12 *Asa*; ; 2CH 17:3-17:6 *Jehoshaphat*; ; 2CH 26:4 parallel 2KI 15:3 *Uzziah (and Amaziah)*; ; 2CH 29:2 parallel 2KI 18:3 *Hezekiah*; ; 2CH 34:2 parallel 2KI 22:2 *Josiah*
