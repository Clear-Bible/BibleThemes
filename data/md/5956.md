# 5956: strength, human

All human strength derives from God. Among non-believers, strength may become a source of arrogant self-confidence, the oppression of others or rebellion against God.



## The strength of the ungodly


### Physical prowess
**1SA 17:4-17:10** *See also* NUM 13:31-13:33

### Intelligence
**1CO 1:22** *The Gentile world relies on fallen reason rather than revelation.* *See also* ACT 17:19-17:21, ACT 17:32; COL 2:8

### Political and military might
**PSA 20:7** *See also* GEN 10:8-10:12; PSA 33:16; ISA 31:1, ISA 36:18-36:20

### Prosperity
**PSA 49:6** *See also* JER 49:4; LUK 12:18-12:19; 1TI 6:17

## The ultimate futility of human strength
**1CO 1:25** *See also* 2CH 32:7-32:8; PSA 49:17, PSA 146:3; ECC 5:15; ISA 30:1-30:3; LUK 12:20; 1CO 1:19-1:20, 1CO 2:6; 1TI 6:7; JAS 1:11
