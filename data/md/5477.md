# 5477: property, land

All land belongs to God, and he allows people to care for it and use it for their own needs and the needs of others. The land promised to Israel is a special case illustrating the spiritual inheritance of all believers and God's ultimate purpose for the world.



## All land belongs to God


### It is in his possession and care
**PSA 24:1** *See also* EXO 9:29; PSA 33:5; 1CO 10:26

### It is given to human beings to manage
**GEN 2:15** *All exploitation of land is therefore contrary to God's purpose.* *See also* PSA 8:6, PSA 115:16

## The land promised to Israel


### It was promised on oath
**GEN 12:6-12:7** *See also* GEN 13:14-13:17, GEN 15:7, GEN 15:18, GEN 17:8, GEN 50:24; EXO 6:8; DEU 6:10-6:11, DEU 31:20-31:21; JDG 2:1

### It was a fruitful land
**EXO 3:8** *See also* LEV 20:24; NUM 14:7-14:8; JOS 5:6

### The distribution of the land
 LEV 25:34; ; NUM 33:53-33:54, NUM 35:2-35:5

## Individual land rights


### Legitimate purchase or sale
 GEN 23:15-23:16, GEN 33:19, GEN 47:20; ; 2SA 24:24; ; PRO 31:16; ; JER 32:6-32:15 *Jeremiah's purchase was an act of faith.*; MAT 27:7-27:10; ; ACT 1:18

### Examples of renting
 SNG 8:11; ; MAT 21:33-21:41 parallels MRK 12:1-12:9, LUK 20:9-20:16

### Laws of inheritance
 NUM 36:6-36:9; ; MIC 2:2

### Encroachment was forbidden
 DEU 19:14 *Land boundaries were marked out by stones, the removal of which was a serious offence*; ; DEU 27:17; ; JOB 24:2; ; PRO 22:28, PRO 23:10; ; HOS 5:10

### Redemption of land
 LEV 25:23-25:28; ; RUT 4:2-4:9

## God's ultimate purpose for the world


### The heavenly land
**HEB 11:13-11:16** *See also* HEB 11:8-11:10

### A new heaven and earth
**ISA 65:17** *See also* ISA 66:22; REV 21:1-21:2

### The inheritance of the righteous
**MAT 5:5** *See also* PSA 37:11, PSA 37:29; 2PE 3:13
