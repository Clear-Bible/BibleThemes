# 8718: disobedience

The refusal to obey someone, especially someone in a position of authority. Scripture insists on the need to obey God at all times.



## Disobedience enters people's hearts for various reasons


### Disobedience comes through greed and lust
**GEN 3:6** *See also* GEN 3:11-3:12; EXO 16:19-16:20; JOS 7:20-7:21; 2SA 11:2-11:4

### Disobedience arises from impatience
**EXO 32:23** *See also* NUM 20:10-20:11; 1SA 13:8-13:14

### Disobedience comes through fear
 JER 43:2-43:7; ; JHN 7:13, JHN 12:42; ; GAL 2:12

### Disobedience results from pride and arrogance
**LEV 10:1** *See also* EXO 5:2; 2CH 26:16

## Unbelief is disobedience
**HEB 3:12** *See also* JER 7:23-7:28; ROM 11:30-11:32 *Ro 11:20 makes it clear that the disobedience spoken of here was unbelief*; HEB 3:18-3:19, HEB 4:2, HEB 4:6

## Lack of love is disobedience
**JHN 14:24** *See also* 1JN 2:9, 1JN 3:15

## Disobedience to God leads to punishment


### Punishment is applied to individuals
**GEN 3:17-3:19** *See also* GEN 3:23-3:24; LEV 10:1-10:2; NUM 20:12; 1SA 28:18; 2SA 12:14; JON 1:10-1:12; EPH 5:5-5:6

### Punishment for the disobedience of unbelief
**2TH 1:8-1:9** *See also* HEB 2:2-2:3

### Nations are also punished
**GEN 15:14** *See also* ACT 7:7**DEU 11:26-11:28**; **1SA 12:15** *See also* EXO 32:35; DEU 28:15; ISA 13:11

### Disobedience, like all sin, can be forgiven
**ROM 5:19** *See also* EPH 2:1-2:5
