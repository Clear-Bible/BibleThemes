# 8718: disobedience

The refusal to obey someone, especially someone in a position of authority. Scripture insists on the need to obey God at all times.

## Disobedience enters people's hearts for various reasons


### Disobedience comes through greed and lust
**[GEN 3:6](bibleref:01003006)** *See also* [GEN 3:11-3:12](bibleref:01003011-01003012); [EXO 16:19-16:20](bibleref:02016019-02016020); [JOS 7:20-7:21](bibleref:06007020-06007021); [2SA 11:2-11:4](bibleref:10011002-10011004)

### Disobedience arises from impatience
**[EXO 32:23](bibleref:02032023)** *See also* [NUM 20:10-20:11](bibleref:04020010-04020011); [1SA 13:8-13:14](bibleref:09013008-09013014)
