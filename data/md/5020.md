# 5020: human nature

Human nature was created by God, is corrupted by sin and can only be renewed and recreated through the grace of God. Redemption was achieved by Jesus Christ taking upon himself sinful human nature on the cross, so delivering from the power and penalty of sin.



## Human nature is created by God


### God created humanity
**EXO 4:11**; **ISA 45:12** *See also* GEN 1:26-1:27; PSA 95:6, PSA 100:3; PRO 22:2; JER 27:5

### God created human beings as male and female
**GEN 1:27, GEN 5:2** *See also* MRK 10:6 parallel MAT 19:4

### God created human beings in his image and likeness
**COL 3:9-3:10** *See also* GEN 1:26-1:27, GEN 5:1, GEN 9:6; JAS 3:9

### Human nature is created inferior to God himself
**PSA 8:3-8:5** *See also* NUM 23:19; PSA 144:3; HOS 11:9

### Human nature is given life by the Spirit of God
**GEN 2:7** *See also* JOB 12:10, JOB 27:3; ISA 42:5, ISA 57:16; JER 38:16

### Human beings are dependent upon God
**JER 10:23** *See also* 2CH 20:12; PSA 16:2; MAT 4:4; 2CO 3:5

### Human beings have freedom to choose between good and evil
**DEU 11:26-11:28** *See also* DEU 30:15-30:16, DEU 30:19; JOS 24:15; 1CH 28:9; JER 26:3; EZK 18:21-18:23

## The components of human nature


### The soul
**PSA 116:7** *The Hebrew and Greek words for “soul” are translated in various ways by the NIV, including “breath” and “life”. It refers chiefly to the principle of life.* *See also* MRK 8:35-8:37; LUK 1:46; 1PE 1:9, 1PE 2:11

### The spirit
**JAS 2:26** *The Hebrew and Greek words for “spirit” are translated in various ways by the NIV, including “breath”, “heart” and “mind”. It often refers to the identity-giving aspect of human nature.* *See also* JOB 34:14; PSA 77:3, PSA 142:3; MAT 26:41; LUK 8:55; ACT 7:59; ROM 8:16; HEB 4:12

### The flesh
**JER 17:5** *The Hebrew and Greek words for “flesh” are translated in various ways by the NIV, including “body”, “flesh” and “human nature”. The term can refer specifically to the frailty of human nature or to human nature in opposition to God.* *See also* GEN 6:3; JOB 10:4; PSA 78:39; MAT 26:41; PHP 3:3-3:5

## Human nature became corrupted by sin


### Sin has its origins in the disobedience of Adam and Eve
 GEN 2:16, GEN 3:1-3:6; ; ROM 5:12-5:19; ; 2CO 11:3; ; 1TI 2:14; ; JAS 1:13-1:15

### Sin affects human nature universally
**ISA 64:6** *See also* JOB 25:4; JER 17:9; JHN 3:19; ROM 3:23

### Human nature is trapped in its sinful condition
**ROM 7:24** *See also* GEN 6:5; JHN 8:34-8:36; ROM 6:16-6:17, ROM 7:5; EPH 2:3; 2PE 2:18-2:19

### Sin hardens human nature
**1SA 6:6** *See also* EXO 8:15, EXO 8:32; PSA 95:8; PRO 28:14; HEB 3:8, HEB 3:15, HEB 4:7

### Human nature is mortal on account of sin
**ROM 5:12, ROM 8:10** *See also* GEN 6:5-6:7; JOB 14:1-14:5; 1CO 15:22; 2CO 5:1; 2TI 1:10

### Jesus Christ took human nature upon himself
**JHN 1:14**

### Jesus Christ mediated between God and humanity in his human nature
**1TI 2:3-2:6** *See also* HEB 8:6, HEB 9:15; 1JN 2:1

### Jesus Christ makes possible the reversal of Adam's disobedience
**ROM 5:19** *See also* ROM 5:12-5:17

### Jesus Christ makes possible a sharing in God's nature
 2PE 1:4
