# 5730: orphans

Children whose parents are dead; a vulnerable group of people to whom God shows special kindness. He expects his people to do the same. Those who exploit them incur God's judgment.



## Orphans as symbols


### Of weakness
**PSA 82:3** *See also* JOB 29:12; ISA 9:17

### Of disgrace
**LAM 5:1-5:3** *See also* PSA 109:9, PSA 109:12

## God cares for orphans
**DEU 10:18** *See also* PSA 10:14, PSA 10:17-10:18, PSA 68:5, PSA 146:9; JER 49:11; HOS 14:3

## God expects his people to care for orphans


### They are not to be oppressed
**ZEC 7:10** *See also* DEU 24:17, DEU 26:12-26:13; JER 22:3

### Caring for them brings blessing
**DEU 24:19** *See also* DEU 14:28-14:29; ISA 1:17; JER 7:5-7:7; JAS 1:27

## Exploitation of orphans is a mark of wickedness
**PSA 94:5-94:6** *See also* JOB 6:27, JOB 22:9, JOB 24:3, JOB 24:9, JOB 31:17, JOB 31:21; ISA 1:23; JER 5:28; EZK 22:7

## God punishes those who oppress orphans
**DEU 27:19** *See also* EXO 22:22-22:24; PRO 23:10-23:11; ISA 10:1-10:4; MAL 3:5

## Orphans are included among God's people
 DEU 16:11, DEU 16:14

## Jesus Christ includes those who are spiritually fatherless
**JHN 14:18** *See also* GAL 4:5, GAL 4:7

## Examples of orphans
 GEN 11:27-11:28 *Lot*; ; NUM 27:1-27:5 *the daughters of Zelophedad*; ; EST 2:7 *Esther*
