# 4522: threshing

The process of beating out or separating grain from harvested stalks.

## Threshing and the harvest
**[LEV 26:5](bibleref:03026005)** *See also* [JDG 6:11](bibleref:07006011); [1CH 21:20](bibleref:13021020); [1CO 9:9](bibleref:46009009); [DEU 25:4](bibleref:05025004); [1TI 5:18](bibleref:54005018) *Paul is adapting a humane OT law to the support of Christian workers.* 

## Threshing implements
**[2SA 24:22](bibleref:10024022)** parallel 1CH 21:23 *A threshing-sledge was a board studded underneath with sharp stones which was dragged by oxen over the wheat ears in order to separate the kernels.*  *See also* [JOB 41:30](bibleref:18041030); [ISA 28:27-28:28](bibleref:23028027-23028028), [ISA 41:15](bibleref:23041015)
