# 9250: woe

An exclamation of judgment upon God's enemies, or of misfortune on oneself, or, in the ministry of Jesus Christ, of sadness over those who fail to recognise the true misery of their condition.

### Woe to God's enemies
**[ISA 33:1](bibleref:23033001)** *Probably referring to Assyria.*  *See also* [JER 48:1-48:2](bibleref:24048001-24048002); [NAM 3:1-3:7](bibleref:34003001-34003007); [HAB 2:6-2:20](bibleref:35002006-35002020); [ZEP 2:5](bibleref:36002005)
