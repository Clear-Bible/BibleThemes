# 5175: reading

The gaining of information through written documents. The Law was read regularly in the synagogue. Jesus Christ expected the Scriptures to be read. NT letters were read in the churches to which they were sent.



## Reading of the Law to Israel


### By Moses
**EXO 24:7** *See also* DEU 31:10-31:11

### By Joshua
 JOS 8:34-8:35

### By Josiah
 2KI 23:2 parallel 2CH 34:30

### In Ezra's time
 NEH 8:2-8:3, NEH 8:18, NEH 9:3, NEH 13:1

## Reading of the Law in the synagogue
**ACT 15:21** *See also* LUK 4:16; ACT 13:15, ACT 13:27; 2CO 3:14-3:15

## Other examples of reading
 DEU 17:18-17:20; JER 36:6-36:8, JER 51:61; 
DAN 9:2 *Daniel foresaw the end of the exile through reading the Scriptures.*

## Jesus Christ expected the Scriptures to be read
**MAT 12:3-12:5** parallels MRK 2:25-2:26, LUK 6:3-6:4 *See also* MAT 19:4, MAT 21:16, MAT 21:42 parallels MRK 12:10-12:11, LUK 20:17; MAT 22:31, MAT 24:15 parallel MRK 13:14

## Reading of NT letters
**COL 4:16** *See also* ACT 15:31; 1TH 5:27

## Reasons for reading the Scriptures


### They are God-breathed
 2TI 3:16

### God's word is true
 JHN 17:7

### To understand the truth about Jesus Christ
 LUK 24:26-24:27; ; EPH 3:4

### To believe and be saved from God's wrath
 JHN 20:31; ; 2TI 3:15

### To learn how to serve and please God
 PSA 119:105, PSA 119:130; ; 2TI 3:16; ; REV 1:3

### They teach Christians and give them hope
 ROM 15:4; ; 1JN 2:1, 1JN 2:12-2:14, 1JN 2:21

### They enable Christians to discern truth from error
**ACT 18:28** *See also* ACT 17:11; GAL 1:6-1:9; 2PE 3:14-3:16; 1JN 2:26, 1JN 4:1
