# 5175: reading

The gaining of information through written documents. The Law was read regularly in the synagogue. Jesus Christ expected the Scriptures to be read. NT letters were read in the churches to which they were sent.

### By Moses
**[EXO 24:7](bibleref:02024007)** *See also* [DEU 31:10-31:11](bibleref:05031010-05031011)

### By Joshua

## Reasons for reading the Scriptures


### They are God-breathed
 [2TI 3:16](bibleref:55003016)

### God's word is true
 [JHN 17:7](bibleref:43017007)

### To understand the truth about Jesus Christ
 [LUK 24:26-24:27](bibleref:42024026-42024027); [EPH 3:4](bibleref:49003004)

### To believe and be saved from God's wrath
 [JHN 20:31](bibleref:43020031); [2TI 3:15](bibleref:55003015)

### To learn how to serve and please God
 [PSA 119:105](bibleref:19119105), [PSA 119:130](bibleref:19119130); [2TI 3:16](bibleref:55003016); [REV 1:3](bibleref:66001003)

### They teach Christians and give them hope
 [ROM 15:4](bibleref:45015004); [1JN 2:1](bibleref:62002001), [1JN 2:12-2:14](bibleref:62002012-62002014), [1JN 2:21](bibleref:62002021)

### They enable Christians to discern truth from error
**[ACT 18:28](bibleref:44018028)** *See also* [ACT 17:11](bibleref:44017011); [GAL 1:6-1:9](bibleref:48001006-48001009); [2PE 3:14-3:16](bibleref:61003014-61003016); [1JN 2:26](bibleref:62002026), [1JN 4:1](bibleref:62004001)
