# 5870: greed, condemnation of

Greed is condemned by Scripture as contrary to the purposes of God.



## Greed is a feature of the fallen world
**PSA 10:3**; **ROM 1:29** *See also* 1CO 5:10

## Greed is an expression of sinful human nature
**MRK 7:21-7:22** parallel MAT 15:19-15:20 *See also* EZK 33:31; ROM 7:8

## Examples of greed


### Of individuals
 JOS 7:21; ; EZK 22:12; ; DAN 5:23

### Of false teachers
**MAT 23:25** *See also* JER 6:13, JER 8:10; LUK 11:39; 2PE 2:3, 2PE 2:14

## Greed invites God's judgment both now and later
**EPH 5:5** *See also* PRO 15:27, PRO 28:8, PRO 28:25, PRO 29:4; EZK 18:10-18:13, EZK 28:5-28:7; AMO 8:4-8:7; MIC 2:1-2:3; HAB 2:5-2:6; 1CO 6:9-6:10
