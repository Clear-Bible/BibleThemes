# 5630: work, divine and human

God has ordained that some of his purposes be fulfilled through the co-operation (conscious or unconscious) of human beings.



## God works in co-operation with people's work


### Human labour is futile apart from God
**[PSA 127:1](bibleref:19127001)** *See also* [GEN 11:8](bibleref:01011008); [JHN 15:5](bibleref:43015005)

### God often works through human means
**[PSA 77:20](bibleref:19077020)** *See also* [ACT 9:15](bibleref:44009015); [ROM 13:6](bibleref:45013006); [2CO 7:6](bibleref:47007006)

### God gives the gifts needed for work
 [EXO 31:3](bibleref:02031003), [EXO 31:6](bibleref:02031006); [LUK 12:48](bibleref:42012048); [1PE 4:11](bibleref:60004011)

### Human workers should acknowledge their dependence upon God
**[DEU 8:18](bibleref:05008018)** *See also* [PSA 90:17](bibleref:19090017); [NEH 6:9](bibleref:16006009), [NEH 6:16](bibleref:16006016); [2CO 3:5](bibleref:47003005)

**[1CO 3:9](bibleref:46003009)**; **[2CO 6:1](bibleref:47006001)** *See also* [MRK 16:20](bibleref:41016020)
