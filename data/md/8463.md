# 8463: priority, of faith, hope and love

Putting God first--living according to his word--serves the glory of God and also the best interests of believers, by giving them true wisdom for daily living.



## The wisdom of making faith a priority
**HEB 11:6**

### Putting the fear of God before other considerations
**PSA 31:19** *See also* EXO 1:17; NEH 5:15; PRO 1:7

### Preferring God's way to sinful advice
**PSA 1:1-1:2** PRO 3:13-3:14, PRO 8:19, PRO 8:32; NUM 31:16; ; 1KI 12:28

### Choosing imaginative action rather than laziness or fearfulness
**NUM 13:30** *See also* 1SA 25:33; EPH 5:16MAT 25:10, MAT 25:26-25:27, MAT 25:44-25:45, MAT 26:40

### Patient waiting rather than impetuous action
**PSA 40:1** *See also* PSA 27:14, PSA 37:5-37:6, PSA 62:5, PSA 123:2; PRO 3:5-3:6; ISA 40:31; HOS 12:6; JHN 2:4-2:5; REV 2:10

## The wisdom of making hope a priority
**HEB 6:19** *See also* PSA 147:10-147:11

### Putting true riches above earthly wealth
**LUK 16:9** *See also* DEU 15:8; MAT 6:24, MAT 19:21 parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22; MAT 19:29 parallels MRK 10:29-10:30, LUK 18:29-18:30; HEB 11:26

### Putting eternal joy before temporal hardship
**2CO 4:17-4:18** *See also* ACT 14:22, ACT 16:25; ROM 8:18; 2TI 2:10; HEB 10:34

## The wisdom of making love a priority
**1CO 13:13** *Paul's concern that love, rather than competition over spiritual gifts, should be made a priority.*

### Loyalty rather than opportunism
**PRO 18:24** *See also* RUT 1:16; 2TI 4:10 *Demas' love for this world rather than loyalty*

### Following the Spirit rather than sinful human nature
**GAL 5:16** *Merely “human” activity betrays a failure of love for God.* *See also* PSA 147:10-147:11; ISA 31:1-31:3; ROM 8:5

### Inner sincerity rather than outward appearance
**ROM 12:9** *See also* 1SA 16:7; EPH 6:5; COL 3:22

### Actions rather than mere words
**JAS 2:18** *See also* MAT 7:21-7:22; 1JN 3:18

### Peace rather than disharmony
**ROM 12:18** *See also* ROM 14:19; HEB 12:14

### Truth rather than falsehood
**PRO 12:19** *See also* ACT 5:4; COL 3:9
