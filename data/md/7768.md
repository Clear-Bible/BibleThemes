# 7768: priests, function in OT times

Priests had a unique role: administering the sanctuary, offering sacrifice and leading and teaching the people. Accordingly they were set apart by a special code of conduct.



## The priests were set apart


### They were consecrated to the LORD
**[LEV 22:9](bibleref:03022009)** *See also* [EXO 28:1](bibleref:02028001), [EXO 28:4](bibleref:02028004), [EXO 28:41](bibleref:02028041), [EXO 29:1](bibleref:02029001), [EXO 29:44](bibleref:02029044), [EXO 30:34](bibleref:02030034), [EXO 31:10](bibleref:02031010), [EXO 35:19](bibleref:02035019), [EXO 39:41](bibleref:02039041)

### They were not to incur uncleanness
**[LEV 21:1](bibleref:03021001)** *Some exceptions to uncleanness by touching a corpse are listed in verses 2-3.*  *See also* [EZK 44:25](bibleref:26044025)

### They were not to shave or mutilate themselves
**[LEV 21:5](bibleref:03021005)** *See also* [EZK 44:20](bibleref:26044020)

### They were not to marry any but a virtuous Israelite
**[LEV 21:7](bibleref:03021007)** *See also* [EZK 44:22](bibleref:26044022)

### They were not to drink wine before entering the sanctuary
**[LEV 10:9](bibleref:03010009)** *See also* [EZK 44:21](bibleref:26044021)

### They were disqualified by physical defects
**[LEV 21:17](bibleref:03021017)** *See also* [LEV 21:18-21:22](bibleref:03021018-03021022)

### They were not to allow outsiders to eat the sacred offerings
**[LEV 22:15-22:16](bibleref:03022015-03022016)** *See also* [LEV 24:9](bibleref:03024009); [1SA 21:4](bibleref:09021004); [MAT 12:4](bibleref:40012004)

### They were not to own land in Israel
**[NUM 18:20](bibleref:04018020)** *See also* [EZK 44:28](bibleref:26044028)

### They were not to offer unacceptable sacrifices
**[LEV 22:20](bibleref:03022020)** *See also* [MAL 1:7-1:9](bibleref:39001007-39001009)

### They were to obey God's law meticulously
**[LEV 22:31](bibleref:03022031)** *See also* [EZK 44:24](bibleref:26044024)
