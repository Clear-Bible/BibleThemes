# 1620: beatitudes, the

The blessings pronounced by Jesus Christ in the Sermon on the Mount on those whose lives exhibit particular characteristics or qualities. These contrast sharply with popular values and outlooks.



## The poor in spirit
**MAT 5:3** parallel LUK 6:20 *See also* PSA 69:32-69:33; ISA 61:1; MAT 23:12; LUK 18:9-18:14

## Those who mourn
**MAT 5:4** parallel LUK 6:21 *See also* PSA 51:17; ISA 57:18-57:21, ISA 61:1-61:3; JER 31:13; REV 7:17, REV 21:4

## The meek
**MAT 5:5** *See also* PSA 37:11; ZEP 3:11-3:12; MAT 11:29

## Those who hunger after righteousness
**MAT 5:6** parallel LUK 6:21 *See also* ISA 55:1-55:2; MAT 6:33; 2TI 2:22; 1PE 3:12

## The merciful
**MAT 5:7** *See also* EXO 34:6-34:7; DAN 9:9; MAT 6:12 parallel LUK 11:4; MAT 6:14-6:15, MAT 18:21-18:35; LUK 6:36; JAS 2:13

## The pure in heart
**MAT 5:8** *See also* PSA 24:3-24:5, PSA 51:10, PSA 73:1; 1JN 3:2-3:3

## The peacemakers
**MAT 5:9** *See also* PSA 34:14; MAT 5:44-5:45; ROM 12:18, ROM 14:19; 2TI 2:22; JAS 3:17-3:18

## The righteous who suffer persecution
**MAT 5:10-5:12** parallel LUK 6:22 *See also* JHN 15:18-15:21; 2TI 3:10-3:12; JAS 1:12; 1PE 3:14, 1PE 3:17, 1PE 4:12-4:16
