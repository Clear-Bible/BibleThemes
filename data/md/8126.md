# 8126: guidance, need for God's

God's guidance is needed because people are naturally ignorant and rebellious and there are many who lead others astray.



## The need to know and do the will of God
**JER 10:23** *See also* EZR 7:18; 1TH 4:3; HEB 13:20-13:21; 1PE 2:15; 1JN 2:17; MAT 6:10, MAT 7:21, MAT 12:50 parallels MRK 3:35, LUK 8:21; JHN 7:17

### The concern of Jesus Christ to do the will of God
**JHN 6:38** *See also* JHN 4:34

## By themselves people are ignorant of the right way in life
**PRO 14:12** *See also* ECC 6:12, ECC 8:7; ISA 53:6, ISA 59:10 *blindness the result of disobedience*; MAT 9:36; 1PE 2:25

## Warnings against false guidance


### False prophets and teachers
**JER 23:25-23:27**; **LUK 11:52** parallel MAT 23:13 *See also* ISA 9:16; JER 23:13, JER 27:9-27:10; MIC 3:5; 2PE 2:1-2:3; JUD 1:17-1:19
