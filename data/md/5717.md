# 5717: monogamy

The practice of having and remaining faithful to only one marriage partner at one time. It is set out as God's ideal for human relationships; an ideal demonstrated in the use of the marriage bond to portray the relationship between God and his people.



## Monogamy as God's original intention for marriage
**GEN 2:22-2:24** *See also* MAL 2:15; MAT 19:4-19:6 parallel MRK 10:6-10:9; EPH 5:31

## Monogamy is shown in God's relationship with his people


### God's marriage with Israel
**EZK 16:8** *See also* ISA 54:5-54:8; JER 2:2-2:3; HOS 2:16-2:20, HOS 3:1; EPH 5:32-5:33

### God's exclusive commitment to Israel
**DEU 14:2**; **AMO 3:2** *See also* EXO 19:5-19:6; DEU 26:18, DEU 32:8; PSA 135:4

## Monogamy in marriage


### The commitment to a lifelong, loving companionship
**GEN 2:18**; **PRO 5:15-5:19** *The satisfaction that may be found in marriage makes marital unfaithfulness and polygamy unnecessary.* *See also* PRO 2:17; ECC 9:9; MAL 2:14

### Polygamy undermines full commitment
**DEU 21:15** *Favouritism within polygamous relationships undermines the close companionship which should characterise marriage.* *See also* GEN 29:30; 1SA 1:1-1:8; 2CH 11:21-11:22

## Monogamy is implicit in teaching about divorce
**MAT 19:9** parallel MRK 10:11 *See also* LUK 16:18

## Monogamy is commanded
**1CO 7:2-7:4** *This is an implicit, but clear prohibition of polygamy*; **1TI 3:12** *Monogamy is obligatory in leaders.* *See also* 1TI 3:2; TIT 1:6
