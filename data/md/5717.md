# 5717: monogamy

The practice of having and remaining faithful to only one marriage partner at one time. It is set out as God's ideal for human relationships; an ideal demonstrated in the use of the marriage bond to portray the relationship between God and his people.

## Monogamy as God's original intention for marriage
**[GEN 2:22-2:24](bibleref:01002022-01002024)** *See also* [MAL 2:15](bibleref:39002015); [MAT 19:4-19:6](bibleref:40019004-40019006) parallel MRK 10:6-10:9; [EPH 5:31](bibleref:49005031)

## Monogamy in marriage


### The commitment to a lifelong, loving companionship
**[GEN 2:18](bibleref:01002018)**; **[PRO 5:15-5:19](bibleref:20005015-20005019)** *The satisfaction that may be found in marriage makes marital unfaithfulness and polygamy unnecessary.*  *See also* [PRO 2:17](bibleref:20002017); [ECC 9:9](bibleref:21009009); [MAL 2:14](bibleref:39002014)

### Polygamy undermines full commitment
**[DEU 21:15](bibleref:05021015)** *Favouritism within polygamous relationships undermines the close companionship which should characterise marriage.*  *See also* [GEN 29:30](bibleref:01029030); [1SA 1:1-1:8](bibleref:09001001-09001008); [2CH 11:21-11:22](bibleref:14011021-14011022)
