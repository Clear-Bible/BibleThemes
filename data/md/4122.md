# 4122: Satan, as tempter

God's people are incited to evil by Satan. He seeks to exploit their weaknesses in order to deflect them from obedience to God.



## Satan incites God's people to evil


### Satan exploits human weaknesses
**[GEN 3:1-3:5](bibleref:01003001-01003005)** *Rev 12:9 identifies the serpent with Satan.*  *See also* [JOB 2:4-2:5](bibleref:18002004-18002005); [EPH 4:26-4:27](bibleref:49004026-49004027)

### He tempts Christians to fall away
**[1TH 3:5](bibleref:52003005)** *See also* [LUK 22:31](bibleref:42022031); [1TI 3:7](bibleref:54003007); [2TI 2:26](bibleref:55002026)

### He inspires people to put God to the test
**[ACT 5:3-5:9](bibleref:44005003-44005009)** *See also* [PSA 78:18](bibleref:19078018), [PSA 78:41](bibleref:19078041), [PSA 106:14](bibleref:19106014); [ACT 15:10](bibleref:44015010); [1CO 10:9](bibleref:46010009)

### He makes sin attractive
Lack of integrity:
 [MAT 5:37](bibleref:40005037); [ACT 5:3](bibleref:44005003)[1CO 7:5](bibleref:46007005); [1TI 5:14-5:15](bibleref:54005014-54005015)
[2CO 2:10-2:11](bibleref:47002010-47002011) *unforgiveness* ; [JAS 3:14-3:16](bibleref:59003014-59003016) *envy and selfish ambition*
