# 5311: extortion

Literally “twisting out”; obtaining money, etc., by violence, threats or unreasonable demands.



## Extortion condemned
**PSA 62:10** *See also* ECC 7:7; ISA 10:1-10:2; EZK 22:13; 1CO 6:9-6:10

## Extortioners can be saved
 1CO 6:9-6:11

## What God requires from extortioners


### Repentance
**LUK 3:12-3:14**

### Restitution
 *See also* LEV 6:4-6:5; LUK 19:8

## Curses upon those who are guilty of extortion
**JER 22:17-22:19** *See also* EZK 18:18, EZK 22:12-22:14; AMO 5:11; HAB 2:6

## Blessings upon those who avoid extortion
**ISA 33:15-33:16**

## Examples of extortion
 *See also* 1SA 2:12-2:17 *Eli's sons*; 1SA 8:11-8:18 *the wicked king foretold by Samuel*; 1SA 13:19-13:21 *the Philistines*; NEH 5:1-5:5 *the Jews in Nehemiah's day*; EZK 22:29 *Israel under the kings*; MAT 18:28 *the servant in the parable*; MAT 23:25 *the Pharisees*; LUK 18:11 *sinners in the eyes of the Pharisee*; 1CO 5:9-5:11 *some Christians before conversion*
