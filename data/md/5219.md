# 5219: authority, of human institutions

The exercise of God-established freedom to regulate society for the good of its members.



## Without authority sin is unrestrained
**[ROM 1:24-1:26](bibleref:45001024-45001026)** *See also* [GEN 6:5](bibleref:01006005) *This was before the giving of the law* ; [JDG 21:25](bibleref:07021025); [ROM 13:4](bibleref:45013004)

## All human authorities are instituted and established by God
**[ROM 13:1-13:2](bibleref:45013001-45013002)** *See also* [GEN 9:5-9:6](bibleref:01009005-01009006); [DAN 2:21](bibleref:27002021), [DAN 2:37-2:38](bibleref:27002037-27002038), [DAN 4:17](bibleref:27004017), [DAN 5:18-5:21](bibleref:27005018-27005021), [DAN 5:26](bibleref:27005026); [JHN 19:11](bibleref:43019011); [1PE 2:13](bibleref:60002013)

## Rulers are God's servants to do good and restrain evil
**[ROM 13:4](bibleref:45013004)** *See also* [ISA 45:1](bibleref:23045001); [JER 25:9](bibleref:24025009), [JER 27:6](bibleref:24027006), [JER 43:10](bibleref:24043010); [ROM 13:6](bibleref:45013006); [1TI 2:2](bibleref:54002002); [1PE 2:14](bibleref:60002014)

## The proper response to human authorities


### They are to be obeyed by everyone
**[ROM 13:1](bibleref:45013001)** *See also* [ECC 8:2](bibleref:21008002); [MAT 22:17-22:21](bibleref:40022017-40022021) parallels MRK 12:14-12:17, LUK 20:22-20:25; [ROM 13:5-13:7](bibleref:45013005-45013007); [TIT 3:1](bibleref:56003001); [1PE 2:13-2:14](bibleref:60002013-60002014)

### They are to be honoured and respected
**[PRO 24:21](bibleref:20024021)** *See also* [ROM 13:7](bibleref:45013007); [1PE 2:17](bibleref:60002017)

### They are not to be obeyed if their demands conflict with the law of God
**[ACT 4:19](bibleref:44004019)** *See also* [EXO 1:17](bibleref:02001017); [1KI 21:3](bibleref:11021003); [DAN 3:18](bibleref:27003018), [DAN 6:12](bibleref:27006012); [MAT 22:21](bibleref:40022021) parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:25; [HEB 11:23](bibleref:58011023)
