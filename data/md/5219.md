# 5219: authority, of human institutions

The exercise of God-established freedom to regulate society for the good of its members.



## Without authority sin is unrestrained
**ROM 1:24-1:26** *See also* GEN 6:5 *This was before the giving of the law*; JDG 21:25; ROM 13:4

## All human authorities are instituted and established by God
**ROM 13:1-13:2** *See also* GEN 9:5-9:6; DAN 2:21, DAN 2:37-2:38, DAN 4:17, DAN 5:18-5:21, DAN 5:26; JHN 19:11; 1PE 2:13

## Rulers are God's servants to do good and restrain evil
**ROM 13:4** *See also* ISA 45:1; JER 25:9, JER 27:6, JER 43:10; ROM 13:6; 1TI 2:2; 1PE 2:14

## The proper response to human authorities


### They are to be obeyed by everyone
**ROM 13:1** *See also* ECC 8:2; MAT 22:17-22:21 parallels MRK 12:14-12:17, LUK 20:22-20:25; ROM 13:5-13:7; TIT 3:1; 1PE 2:13-2:14

### They are to be honoured and respected
**PRO 24:21** *See also* ROM 13:7; 1PE 2:17

### They are not to be obeyed if their demands conflict with the law of God
**ACT 4:19** *See also* EXO 1:17; 1KI 21:3; DAN 3:18, DAN 6:12; MAT 22:21 parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:25; HEB 11:23

### They are to be prayed for
**1TI 2:1-2:2** *See also* JER 29:7

### Christians are to use rights granted by the authorities
 ACT 16:35-16:39, ACT 22:22-22:29, ACT 25:1-25:12
