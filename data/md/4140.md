# 4140: angel of the Lord

A title given to those angelic servants of God who are given specific tasks in furthering God's purposes among humanity. These tasks include the giving of revelations, guidance, encouragement, warnings and the execution of judgment on those who oppose God's purposes.

## The status of the angel of the Lord


### He is identified with the Lord
**[EXO 3:2-3:6](bibleref:02003002-02003006)** *The angel of the LORD here speaks for God and is recognised as God and as a messenger from God. This may indicate that the angel speaks on God's behalf (compare with the role of the prophets) or, as some believe, this may be a manifestation of the pre-incarnate Jesus Christ.*  *See also* [GEN 16:7](bibleref:01016007), [GEN 16:13](bibleref:01016013), [GEN 31:11-31:13](bibleref:01031011-01031013); [JDG 2:1-2:2](bibleref:07002001-07002002), [JDG 6:11-6:12](bibleref:07006011-07006012), [JDG 6:14](bibleref:07006014), [JDG 13:19-13:22](bibleref:07013019-07013022); [ZEC 3:1-3:2](bibleref:38003001-38003002)

### He is distinguished from the Lord
**[2SA 24:16](bibleref:10024016)** parallel 1CH 21:15 *See also* [ZEC 1:12-1:13](bibleref:38001012-38001013); [LUK 1:19](bibleref:42001019); [HEB 1:4](bibleref:58001004)

## The angel of the Lord is active in various ways


### He calls and speaks
 [GEN 16:7-16:8](bibleref:01016007-01016008), [GEN 21:17](bibleref:01021017), [GEN 22:11](bibleref:01022011), [GEN 22:15](bibleref:01022015); [2KI 1:3](bibleref:12001003), [2KI 1:15](bibleref:12001015)

### He has physical contact with people
 [1KI 19:5](bibleref:11019005), [1KI 19:7](bibleref:11019007); [ACT 12:7](bibleref:44012007)

### He appears in human likeness
 [GEN 18:2](bibleref:01018002), [GEN 18:16](bibleref:01018016), [GEN 18:22](bibleref:01018022), [GEN 19:1](bibleref:01019001); [JDG 6:11-6:12](bibleref:07006011-07006012); [ACT 27:23](bibleref:44027023)

### He appears in supernatural form, often evoking fear
 [EXO 3:2](bibleref:02003002); [NUM 22:22-22:24](bibleref:04022022-04022024), [NUM 22:31](bibleref:04022031); [1CH 21:16](bibleref:13021016); [LUK 1:11-1:12](bibleref:42001011-42001012), [LUK 2:9](bibleref:42002009)

### He appears in dreams and visions
 [GEN 31:11](bibleref:01031011); [ZEC 1:8](bibleref:38001008), [ZEC 1:11](bibleref:38001011), [ZEC 3:1](bibleref:38003001), [ZEC 3:5](bibleref:38003005); [MAT 2:13](bibleref:40002013), [MAT 2:19](bibleref:40002019); [ACT 10:3](bibleref:44010003)
