# 9023: death, of unbelievers

The departure from the present life of unrepentant people to await final banishment from God.



## Death is inevitable
**[PSA 49:10](bibleref:19049010), [PSA 89:48](bibleref:19089048)** *See also* [2SA 14:14](bibleref:10014014); [JOB 30:23](bibleref:18030023); [PSA 37:36](bibleref:19037036); [ECC 3:19-3:20](bibleref:21003019-21003020), [ECC 8:8](bibleref:21008008)

## The premature death of unbelievers is the consequence of sin
**[PRO 10:27](bibleref:20010027)** *OT writers, for whom God's blessing on the righteous occurs mostly in the present life, often saw early or sudden death as divine judgment on the wicked.*  *See also* [1CH 10:13](bibleref:13010013); [JOB 22:16](bibleref:18022016); [PSA 9:17](bibleref:19009017), [PSA 37:1-37:2](bibleref:19037001-19037002), [PSA 37:22](bibleref:19037022), [PSA 37:38](bibleref:19037038), [PSA 55:23](bibleref:19055023), [PSA 139:19](bibleref:19139019); [PRO 5:21-5:23](bibleref:20005021-20005023), [PRO 11:19](bibleref:20011019), [PRO 14:12](bibleref:20014012); [ISA 17:14](bibleref:23017014); [EZK 3:19](bibleref:26003019), [EZK 18:4](bibleref:26018004), [EZK 18:20](bibleref:26018020); [DAN 5:30](bibleref:27005030); [LUK 12:20](bibleref:42012020)
