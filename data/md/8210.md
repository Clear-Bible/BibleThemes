# 8210: commitment, to God's people

Commitment to God means a commitment to his people. Believers are meant to be nourished and supported by the church, and to work towards its edification.



## Commitment to the church
**ACT 2:42**; **EPH 4:3-4:4**; **COL 3:15** ROM 12:4-12:10; ; 1CO 12:12-12:27

## Commitment to other Christians is an expression of love made known in Christ
**JHN 13:34-13:35**; **COL 3:13-3:14**; **1PE 1:22** *See also* JHN 15:12, JHN 15:17; ROM 12:10, ROM 12:16, ROM 13:8; GAL 5:13; EPH 4:32-5:2; 1TH 3:12, 1TH 4:9; 2TH 1:3; HEB 13:1; 1PE 2:17; 1JN 3:11, 1JN 3:23, 1JN 4:7, 1JN 4:21, 1JN 5:2

## Such commitment is expressed in mutual responsibility and concern
**GAL 6:2**; **HEB 10:24-10:25**; **1PE 3:8** *See also* ROM 14:13, ROM 15:7, ROM 15:14; 1CO 1:10 *Paul's concern for unity among believers*; EPH 5:21; COL 3:13 *the responsibility of believers to forgive one another*; COL 3:16; 1TI 5:11; HEB 3:13; JAS 4:11; 1PE 4:9, 1PE 5:5

## For its leaders, commitment to the church may prove a joy and a burden
**ACT 20:28** *Paul speaking to the elders of the church at Ephesus*; **2CO 11:28** *See also* COL 1:24; 1TH 2:8, 1TH 5:12-5:13; 1TI 3:1; HEB 13:17; 1PE 5:1-5:3
