# 1120: God, repentance of

A change in God's plan or intention, often in response to human repentance, but without implying any fault or moral imperfection on God's part.



## God's repentance may show his pain and sorrow
**[GEN 6:6-6:7](bibleref:01006006-01006007)** *God's change of purpose here involves his intention to punish.*  *See also* [1SA 15:11](bibleref:09015011), [1SA 15:35](bibleref:09015035)

## God's repentance may show his compassion
**[JON 4:2](bibleref:32004002)** *God's change of purpose here involves his compassion for sinners: “relent” in this and other passages may also be translated “repent”.*  *See also* [DEU 32:36](bibleref:05032036); [2SA 24:15-24:16](bibleref:10024015-10024016) parallel 1CH 21:14-21:15; [PSA 106:45](bibleref:19106045); [ISA 38:1-38:5](bibleref:23038001-23038005); [HOS 11:8](bibleref:28011008); [AMO 7:1-7:6](bibleref:30007001-30007006)

### God may repent in response to human repentance
**[JER 18:8](bibleref:24018008)** *See also* [EXO 32:14](bibleref:02032014); [2CH 12:1-12:8](bibleref:14012001-14012008); [JER 26:13](bibleref:24026013), [JER 26:19](bibleref:24026019); [JOL 2:13](bibleref:29002013); [JON 3:6-3:10](bibleref:32003006-32003010)

## God never repents of his oath or changes his character
 [NUM 23:19](bibleref:04023019); [PSA 110:4](bibleref:19110004); [HEB 6:17-6:18](bibleref:58006017-58006018)
