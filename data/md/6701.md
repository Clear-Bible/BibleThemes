# 6701: peace, human search for

Scripture teaches that peace is only found in God, but people try to find it elsewhere. A person's desire for peace varies according to his or her circumstances.



## The places where people search for peace


### People search for peace in others
**[GEN 5:28-5:29](bibleref:01005028-01005029)** *See also* [SNG 8:10](bibleref:22008010)

### People search for peace in material possessions
**[ECC 4:8](bibleref:21004008)** *See also* [JOB 21:7-21:13](bibleref:18021007-18021013); [DAN 4:4](bibleref:27004004); [MAT 19:16-19:22](bibleref:40019016-40019022); [LUK 12:16-12:19](bibleref:42012016-42012019)

### People search for peace in God
**[PSA 4:8](bibleref:19004008)** *See also* [JOB 22:21](bibleref:18022021); [PRO 19:23](bibleref:20019023); [ISA 26:3](bibleref:23026003); [LUK 7:37-7:50](bibleref:42007037-42007050)

## The dangers in searching for peace


### It can lead to greed
**[ECC 4:6](bibleref:21004006)** *See also* [MAT 23:25](bibleref:40023025)

### It can lead to suffering and bondage
 [GEN 42:1-42:17](bibleref:01042001-01042017); [JOS 9:3-9:27](bibleref:06009003-06009027); [MRK 5:25-5:26](bibleref:41005025-41005026)

### It can lead to destruction
**[1TH 5:3](bibleref:52005003)** *See also* [2KI 20:12-20:19](bibleref:12020012-12020019); [LUK 16:19-16:26](bibleref:42016019-42016026)

## People's search for peace varies according to their circumstances


### The desire can increase in times of suffering and difficulties
**[EXO 2:23](bibleref:02002023)** *See also* [JER 14:19](bibleref:24014019), [JER 47:2-47:6](bibleref:24047002-24047006); [LAM 3:7-3:24](bibleref:25003007-25003024); [EZK 7:23-7:27](bibleref:26007023-26007027); [MRK 5:24-5:34](bibleref:41005024-41005034) parallels MAT 9:18-9:22, LUK 8:42-8:48

### The desire can diminish in times of comfort
**[EXO 8:12-8:15](bibleref:02008012-02008015)** *See also* [JOB 12:5](bibleref:18012005); [HOS 10:1](bibleref:28010001); [AMO 6:1](bibleref:30006001)
