# 7908: baptism, significance of

The NT uses a variety of images to explain the meaning of baptism, such as dying and rising with Christ, sharing in his death and being cleansed from sin.



## Baptism is a symbol of the death of Jesus Christ
**[LUK 12:50](bibleref:42012050)** *Jesus Christ described his death as a baptism or a flood overwhelming him.*  *See also* [PSA 42:7](bibleref:19042007), [PSA 69:1-69:2](bibleref:19069001-19069002), [PSA 88:7](bibleref:19088007); [MRK 10:38-10:39](bibleref:41010038-41010039)

## Baptism is a symbol of the burial of Jesus Christ
**[ROM 6:3-6:4](bibleref:45006003-45006004)** *Immersion in water symbolises how the old sinful life is buried with Christ.*  [ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013); [COL 3:5](bibleref:51003005)

## Baptism is a symbol of being saved from the flood
**[1PE 3:21](bibleref:60003021)** *The flood/baptism symbolises both judgment (the death of sinners and the death of Jesus Christ) and salvation (those in the ark and those in Christ passing safely through judgment).*  *See also* [GEN 7:6-7:7](bibleref:01007006-01007007)

## Baptism is the gospel equivalent of circumcision
**[COL 2:11-2:12](bibleref:51002011-51002012)** *The “circumcision in Christ” may refer either to his death on the cross or to baptism.*  *See also* [DEU 10:16](bibleref:05010016)

## Baptism recalls the exodus
**[1CO 10:1-10:2](bibleref:46010001-46010002)** *In and through the exodus experience Israel was united with and obedient to Moses, as Jesus Christ's people are to him.*  *See also* [EXO 14:19-14:24](bibleref:02014019-02014024)

## Baptism symbolises washing from sin
**[ACT 22:16](bibleref:44022016)** *Baptism is an outward sign of cleansing from sin and from a defiled life and conscience.*  *See also* [1CO 6:11](bibleref:46006011); [TIT 3:5](bibleref:56003005); [1PE 3:21](bibleref:60003021)

## Baptism is a symbol of putting on Christ
**[GAL 3:27](bibleref:48003027)** *Baptism is seen as putting on Christ, as one might put on a coat or garment, and thus be “clothed with Christ”.* 

## Baptism as a symbol of unity
