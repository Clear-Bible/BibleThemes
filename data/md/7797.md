# 7797: teaching

The apostles' teaching formed the basis of the instruction given to the first Christians. Parts of the NT draw a distinction between preaching and teaching, seeing the former as a means of converting individuals, and the latter as a means of instructing them after conversion.



## The importance of teaching
**[ECC 12:11-12:12](bibleref:21012011-21012012)** *“goads” prod the sluggish to action, while “embedded nails” furnish a kind of mental anchorage. “Shepherd” is almost certainly a reference to God himself.*  *See also* [PRO 1:8-1:9](bibleref:20001008-20001009), [PRO 3:1-3:2](bibleref:20003001-20003002), [PRO 4:1-4:4](bibleref:20004001-20004004), [PRO 6:20-6:23](bibleref:20006020-20006023); [MAT 5:19](bibleref:40005019); [1CO 14:6](bibleref:46014006); [2TI 4:2-4:3](bibleref:55004002-55004003)
