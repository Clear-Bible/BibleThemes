# 5541: society, negative aspects of

Often pictured as an immoral city or a world of evil, it is regarded as corrupt and godless, hostile to true faith and therefore destined for judgment.



## The city as a symbol of corrupt society
**[REV 18:2-18:3](bibleref:66018002-66018003)** *Babylon stands for the corrupt Roman state and also for godless society in general.*  *See also* [GEN 11:1-11:9](bibleref:01011001-01011009), [GEN 19:1-19:13](bibleref:01019001-01019013); [EZK 22:1-22:5](bibleref:26022001-26022005); [MAT 23:37-23:39](bibleref:40023037-40023039) parallel LUK 13:34-13:35; [REV 14:8](bibleref:66014008)

## ``The world'' as a description of society alienated from, and hostile to, both God and his people
**[GAL 3:22](bibleref:48003022)** *The Greek word translated “world” often means not the physical world, but the world of human life.*  *See also* [JHN 15:18](bibleref:43015018); [EPH 6:12](bibleref:49006012); [1JN 5:19](bibleref:62005019); [REV 12:9](bibleref:66012009)

## Fallen society becomes pluralistic


### Israel was continually threatened by the pluralism of the surrounding societies
**[JOS 23:6-23:8](bibleref:06023006-06023008)** *See also* [EXO 23:32-23:33](bibleref:02023032-02023033); [JOS 24:14-24:23](bibleref:06024014-06024023); [1KI 11:1-11:6](bibleref:11011001-11011006), [1KI 18:16-18:40](bibleref:11018016-11018040) *Baal was a name used for a variety of local Canaanite deities* ; [EZR 9:1-9:2](bibleref:15009001-15009002); [ISA 2:6-2:8](bibleref:23002006-23002008); [DAN 3:1-3:12](bibleref:27003001-27003012)

### Pluralism was also a feature of the societies of the NT
**[ACT 17:16-17:23](bibleref:44017016-44017023)** *See also* [1CO 8:4-8:6](bibleref:46008004-46008006); [2TI 4:3-4:4](bibleref:55004003-55004004); [1JN 5:21](bibleref:62005021)

### Pluralism is to be resisted and faith in one God and Saviour maintained
**[1TI 2:5](bibleref:54002005)** *See also* [ISA 43:10-43:11](bibleref:23043010-23043011); [JHN 14:6](bibleref:43014006); [ACT 4:12](bibleref:44004012)

## Fallen society becomes secular


### Life in a godless society becomes spiritually futile and morally depraved
**[ROM 1:21](bibleref:45001021)** *See also* [ROM 1:18-1:32](bibleref:45001018-45001032); [2TI 3:1-3:5](bibleref:55003001-55003005); [REV 18:1-18:3](bibleref:66018001-66018003)

### Israel ignores warnings about becoming a society which has forgotten God
**[PSA 81:11-81:12](bibleref:19081011-19081012)** *See also* [JER 3:19-3:21](bibleref:24003019-24003021); [HEB 3:7-3:11](bibleref:58003007-58003011)

### Israel became an example of a society which, though outwardly religious, lived without reference to God
**[AMO 5:21-5:24](bibleref:30005021-30005024)** *Amos here summarises, and rejects, current religious practices in Israel in the 8th century B.C.*  *See also* [AMO 2:6-2:7](bibleref:30002006-30002007), [AMO 5:4-5:15](bibleref:30005004-30005015) *Bethel and Gilgal were popular national shrines, where religion had taken the place of righteousness and relationship* ; [AMO 8:4-8:10](bibleref:30008004-30008010)
