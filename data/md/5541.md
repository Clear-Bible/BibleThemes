# 5541: society, negative aspects of

Often pictured as an immoral city or a world of evil, it is regarded as corrupt and godless, hostile to true faith and therefore destined for judgment.



## The city as a symbol of corrupt society
**REV 18:2-18:3** *Babylon stands for the corrupt Roman state and also for godless society in general.* *See also* GEN 11:1-11:9, GEN 19:1-19:13; EZK 22:1-22:5; MAT 23:37-23:39 parallel LUK 13:34-13:35; REV 14:8

## ``The world'' as a description of society alienated from, and hostile to, both God and his people
**GAL 3:22** *The Greek word translated “world” often means not the physical world, but the world of human life.* *See also* JHN 15:18; EPH 6:12; 1JN 5:19; REV 12:9

## Fallen society becomes pluralistic


### Israel was continually threatened by the pluralism of the surrounding societies
**JOS 23:6-23:8** *See also* EXO 23:32-23:33; JOS 24:14-24:23; 1KI 11:1-11:6, 1KI 18:16-18:40 *Baal was a name used for a variety of local Canaanite deities*; EZR 9:1-9:2; ISA 2:6-2:8; DAN 3:1-3:12

### Pluralism was also a feature of the societies of the NT
**ACT 17:16-17:23** *See also* 1CO 8:4-8:6; 2TI 4:3-4:4; 1JN 5:21

### Pluralism is to be resisted and faith in one God and Saviour maintained
**1TI 2:5** *See also* ISA 43:10-43:11; JHN 14:6; ACT 4:12

## Fallen society becomes secular


### Life in a godless society becomes spiritually futile and morally depraved
**ROM 1:21** *See also* ROM 1:18-1:32; 2TI 3:1-3:5; REV 18:1-18:3

### Israel ignores warnings about becoming a society which has forgotten God
**PSA 81:11-81:12** *See also* JER 3:19-3:21; HEB 3:7-3:11

### Israel became an example of a society which, though outwardly religious, lived without reference to God
**AMO 5:21-5:24** *Amos here summarises, and rejects, current religious practices in Israel in the 8th century B.C.* *See also* AMO 2:6-2:7, AMO 5:4-5:15 *Bethel and Gilgal were popular national shrines, where religion had taken the place of righteousness and relationship*; AMO 8:4-8:10
