# 8605: prayer, and God's will

Prayer is concerned not only with the well-being of the one who prays. A vital aspect of its purpose is to allow the will of God to be done, and to bring glory and honour to his name.



## True motives for prayer


### The desire that God's name be honoured
**MAT 6:9-6:13** parallel LUK 11:2-11:4 *See also* NUM 14:13-14:16; JOS 7:7-7:9; 2SA 7:25-7:26; 1KI 18:36-18:37; PSA 115:1; JHN 17:1

### The desire that God's will be fulfilled
**MAT 6:9-6:13** parallel LUK 11:2-11:4 *See also* MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; MAT 26:42; HEB 10:7 *Submission to God's will characterised Jesus Christ's prayer life.*

## God answers prayer that accords with his will
**1JN 5:14-5:15**

### Petitioners may enquire of God to discover his will
**PSA 143:10** *See also* GEN 25:22-25:23; JDG 1:1-1:2; 2SA 2:1; 1CH 14:14-14:15

### The Holy Spirit helps believers to pray in God's will
**ROM 8:26-8:27**

## God's response to prayers allows believers to discern his will
**2CO 12:7-12:9** *See also* EXO 33:18-33:20; 2SA 12:15-12:18; JOB 19:7-19:8; PSA 35:13-35:14

## God does not respond to the prayers of the wicked
**JHN 9:31** *See also* PSA 66:18; PRO 15:8; ISA 1:15, ISA 59:1-59:2; LAM 3:44; 1PE 3:12
