# 7435: sacrifice, in OT

An act that involved offering to God the life of an animal. It expressed gratitude for God's goodness or acknowledgment of sin. It was also associated with establishing a covenant.



## Sacrifice was an integral part of worship
 *See also* GEN 46:1; EXO 10:24-10:26; JDG 13:19; 1SA 1:3; 1KI 3:4

## Sacrifices were a means of offering thanks to God
 GEN 4:4 *Abel*; ; GEN 8:20 *Noah delivered from the flood*; ; EXO 18:12 *Jethro after God had delivered the Israelites*; ; JDG 11:31 *Jephthah*; ; 1SA 6:15 *after the safe return of the ark*; EZR 3:3, EZR 8:35; PSA 27:6, PSA 54:6, PSA 107:17-107:22

## Sacrifices were offered at regular religious festivals
 LEV 23:5-23:8 *Passover*; ; LEV 23:18-23:20 *the Feast of Weeks*; ; LEV 23:23-23:25 *the Feast of Trumpets*; LEV 16:6-16:10, LEV 23:26-23:32; 
LEV 23:33-23:36 *the Feast of Tabernacles*

## Special occasions were marked by sacrifices
 1KI 8:63 *the dedication of Solomon's temple*; ; 2CH 29:31-29:33 *after Hezekiah had purified the temple*; ; EZR 3:2-3:3 *the Jews who returned from the Babylonian exile*; ; EZR 6:17 *at the completion of the second temple*

## Sacrifices as signs of individual and national penitence
 LEV 4:1-4:3, LEV 4:13-4:14; ; JDG 2:1-2:5, JDG 20:26; ; 1SA 7:8-7:9; ; 2SA 24:10-24:25

## The place of sacrifice


### It was divinely chosen
**DEU 12:13-12:14** *See also* LEV 17:3-17:5; DEU 12:2-12:6

### Rival places of sacrifice caused the Israelites to sin
 1KI 12:28-12:29, 1KI 12:32; ; 2CH 15:17, 2CH 31:1 *Under Hezekiah the high places were destroyed.*

## God condemned certain sacrifices


### Sacrifices to other gods
**1KI 11:7-11:8** *See also* NUM 25:1-25:3; 2KI 16:4, 2KI 16:15; PSA 106:28; ISA 57:7, ISA 65:3, ISA 65:7; JER 19:4

### Human sacrifices
 LEV 18:21; ; DEU 12:31; ; 1KI 16:34; ; 2KI 3:26-3:27, 2KI 16:3, 2KI 17:31, 2KI 21:6; ; EZK 20:31

## Sacrifice to the LORD may be rejected


### If it is a substitute for obedience
 1SA 15:20-15:22; ; JER 7:21-7:22; ; HOS 8:11-8:13; ; AMO 4:4; ; MRK 12:33

### If it is a substitute for justice and mercy

### If it is imperfect
