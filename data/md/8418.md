# 8418: endurance

The ability to persevere in a task or calling. The Christian is called to endure in the face of trial or opposition, and his endurance brings spiritual rewards.



## Endurance commended as a virtue for God's people
**JOS 1:7**; **GAL 6:9** *See also* DEU 5:32; JOS 23:8; JOB 17:9; ACT 11:23, ACT 13:43, ACT 14:22; EPH 4:14; 1TI 6:11; 2TI 2:3; 1PE 2:20; REV 13:10

## Endurance is a hallmark of true Christian profession
**MAT 10:22** parallel MRK 13:13**LUK 9:62** *See also* ACT 20:24; 2TI 2:12

## Christian endurance originates with God
**ROM 15:5** *See also* 2CO 1:8-1:9; COL 1:10-1:11; 2TH 3:5

## Christian endurance involves standing firm
**1CO 15:58**; **PHP 1:27** *See also* GAL 5:1; PHP 4:1; 2TH 2:15; 2TI 3:14, 2TI 4:5

## The results of enduring


### Salvation
**HEB 10:36**; **JAS 1:12** *See also* ROM 2:7, ROM 15:4; 1TI 4:16; 2TI 2:10; HEB 12:7-12:9

### Protection
**REV 3:10** *See also* 2TH 3:3-3:4

### Spiritual fruit
**JAS 1:4** *See also* LUK 8:15; ROM 5:3-5:5

### Encouragement for others
**2CO 1:6** *See also* 1PE 5:9; HEB 12:1-12:3; REV 1:9
