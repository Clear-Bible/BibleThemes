# 8418: endurance

The ability to persevere in a task or calling. The Christian is called to endure in the face of trial or opposition, and his endurance brings spiritual rewards.

**[JOS 1:7](bibleref:06001007)**; **[GAL 6:9](bibleref:48006009)** *See also* [DEU 5:32](bibleref:05005032); [JOS 23:8](bibleref:06023008); [JOB 17:9](bibleref:18017009); [ACT 11:23](bibleref:44011023), [ACT 13:43](bibleref:44013043), [ACT 14:22](bibleref:44014022); [EPH 4:14](bibleref:49004014); [1TI 6:11](bibleref:54006011); [2TI 2:3](bibleref:55002003); [1PE 2:20](bibleref:60002020); [REV 13:10](bibleref:66013010)
