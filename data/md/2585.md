# 2585: Jesus Christ, trial of

Jesus Christ, falsely accused of blasphemy towards God and treason towards Rome, humbly endured his trial as a fulfilment of the purposes of God.



## The preliminary hearing before Annas
 *See also* JHN 18:12-18:14 *Annas, though deposed from office by the Romans, was probably still regarded by many as the true high priest*; JHN 18:19-18:23

## The trial before Caiaphas and the Sanhedrin


### Jesus Christ is sent to the ruling high priest
 MAT 26:57 parallels MRK 14:53, JHN 18:24; ; LUK 22:54

### False evidence is sought
**MAT 26:59-26:60** parallel MRK 14:55-14:56 *The Sanhedrin was the high court of the Jews and consisted of 71 chief priests, elders and teachers of the law, including the high priest who presided.*

### False accusations are made
 *See also* MAT 26:60-26:61 parallel MRK 14:57-14:59 *A person could only be convicted on the evidence of two or more witnesses.*

## Jesus Christ's trial centres upon his Messianic claims


### Jesus declares himself to be the Christ
**MAT 26:63-26:64** parallel MRK 14:61-14:62 *See also* LUK 22:66-22:70

### Jesus Christ is charged with blasphemy
 MAT 26:65-26:66 parallel MRK 14:63-14:64; ; MAT 27:1 parallel MRK 15:1 *The Sanhedrin decided that Jesus Christ deserved death but charged him with treason rather than blasphemy.*

### Jesus is mocked as the Christ
 MAT 26:67-26:68 parallels MRK 14:65, LUK 22:63-22:65

## The trial before Pilate


### The Sanhedrin hand Jesus Christ over to Pilate
 MAT 27:2 parallel MRK 15:1; ; LUK 23:1; ; JHN 18:28-18:32

### Jesus Christ accepts the title of king
**JHN 18:36-18:37** *See also* MAT 27:11 parallel MRK 15:2; LUK 23:2-23:3; JHN 19:9-19:12; 1TI 6:13

### Pilate decides Jesus Christ is innocent
**LUK 23:4** *See also* LUK 23:14-23:15, LUK 23:22; JHN 18:38, JHN 19:4-19:6; ACT 13:28

### Pilate sends Jesus Christ to Herod
 LUK 23:5-23:10

### Herod sends Jesus Christ back to Pilate
 LUK 23:11-23:12

### Pilate seeks to release Jesus Christ
**JHN 19:12** *See also* MAT 27:15-27:18 parallel MRK 15:6-15:10 *Pilate appeals to the crowd on the basis of Jesus Christ's kingship*; MAT 27:19 *Pilate is warned by his wife not to get involved*; LUK 23:16, LUK 23:20, LUK 23:22; JHN 18:38-18:39, JHN 19:6, JHN 19:15

### The crowd demand the release of Barabbas and the crucifixion of Jesus Christ
**MAT 27:20** parallel MRK 15:11 *See also* MAT 27:21-27:23 parallel MRK 15:12-15:14; LUK 23:18, LUK 23:21, LUK 23:23; JHN 18:40, JHN 19:6-19:7, JHN 19:12, JHN 19:15

### The crowd's demands prevail
**MRK 15:15** parallel MAT 27:26 *See also* MAT 27:24 *Pilate denies responsibility*; MAT 27:25; LUK 23:23-23:25; JHN 19:16; ACT 3:13-3:14 *The emphasis is on the people's responsibility.*

### Jesus Christ is mocked as king
**JHN 19:2-19:3** *See also* MAT 27:27-27:31 parallel MRK 15:16-15:20; JHN 19:15

## Jesus Christ's response to his trial
**ISA 53:7** *See also* MAT 26:62-26:63 parallel MRK 14:60-14:61; MAT 27:12, MAT 27:14 parallel MRK 15:5; LUK 23:9; JHN 19:9; ACT 8:32-8:35; 1PE 2:23

## Jesus Christ's trial confirms he is God's servant
**ACT 4:27-4:28** *See also* ISA 53:10; JHN 19:8-19:11; ACT 2:23 *The outcome of the trial fulfilled God's declared purpose.*
