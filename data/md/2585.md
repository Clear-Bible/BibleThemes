# 2585: Jesus Christ, trial of

Jesus Christ, falsely accused of blasphemy towards God and treason towards Rome, humbly endured his trial as a fulfilment of the purposes of God.



## The preliminary hearing before Annas
 *See also* [JHN 18:12-18:14](bibleref:43018012-43018014) *Annas, though deposed from office by the Romans, was probably still regarded by many as the true high priest* ; [JHN 18:19-18:23](bibleref:43018019-43018023)

## The trial before Caiaphas and the Sanhedrin


### Jesus Christ is sent to the ruling high priest
 [MAT 26:57](bibleref:40026057) parallels MRK 14:53, JHN 18:24; [LUK 22:54](bibleref:42022054)

### False evidence is sought
**[MAT 26:59-26:60](bibleref:40026059-40026060)** parallel MRK 14:55-14:56 *The Sanhedrin was the high court of the Jews and consisted of 71 chief priests, elders and teachers of the law, including the high priest who presided.* 

### False accusations are made
 *See also* [MAT 26:60-26:61](bibleref:40026060-40026061) parallel MRK 14:57-14:59 *A person could only be convicted on the evidence of two or more witnesses.* 

## Jesus Christ's trial centres upon his Messianic claims


### Jesus declares himself to be the Christ
**[MAT 26:63-26:64](bibleref:40026063-40026064)** parallel MRK 14:61-14:62 *See also* [LUK 22:66-22:70](bibleref:42022066-42022070)

### Jesus Christ is charged with blasphemy
 [MAT 26:65-26:66](bibleref:40026065-40026066) parallel MRK 14:63-14:64; [MAT 27:1](bibleref:40027001) parallel MRK 15:1 *The Sanhedrin decided that Jesus Christ deserved death but charged him with treason rather than blasphemy.* 

### Jesus is mocked as the Christ
 [MAT 26:67-26:68](bibleref:40026067-40026068) parallels MRK 14:65, LUK 22:63-22:65
