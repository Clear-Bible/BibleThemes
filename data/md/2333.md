# 2333: Jesus Christ, attitude to OT

Jesus Christ's teaching was based largely on the OT, which he treated as God's inspired and authoritative word, but which he had the authority to interpret.



## Jesus Christ recognises the OT as Scripture


### Jesus Christ calls the OT ``Scripture''
**JHN 10:35** *See also* MRK 12:10, MRK 12:24 parallel MAT 22:29; LUK 4:21; JHN 5:39, JHN 7:38, JHN 13:18

## Jesus Christ underlines the authority of the OT
**JHN 5:46-5:47**

### The OT is authoritative over Jesus Christ's opponents
**MAT 23:23** *See also* MAT 5:19-5:20, MAT 23:2-23:3; MRK 2:24-2:27 parallels MAT 12:2-12:6, LUK 6:2-6:4

### The OT is authoritative over Satan
**MAT 4:4** parallel LUK 4:4 *See also* DEU 8:3; MAT 4:7 parallel LUK 4:12; DEU 6:16; MAT 4:10 parallel LUK 4:8; DEU 6:13

## Jesus Christ regards the OT as being fulfilled in himself


### Jesus Christ regards his ministry as fulfilling the OT
**MAT 5:17-5:18** *See also* MAT 11:4-11:6 parallel LUK 7:22-7:23; MAT 26:56 parallel MRK 14:49; LUK 4:17-4:21; ISA 61:1-61:2; JHN 15:25; PSA 35:19, PSA 69:4

### Jesus Christ regards his death as fulfilling OT prophecy
**LUK 18:31-18:32** *See also* MAT 12:39-12:40, MAT 26:28 parallel LUK 22:20; MAT 26:31 parallel MRK 14:27; ZEC 13:7

### Other aspects of the OT fulfilled or affirmed by Jesus Christ
 MAT 7:12, MAT 11:10 parallel LUK 7:27; ; MAL 3:1; ; MAT 13:14-13:15 parallels MRK 4:12, LUK 8:10; ; ISA 6:9-6:10; ; MAT 17:11-17:12 parallel MRK 9:12-9:13; ; MAL 4:5; ; MAT 24:15 parallels MRK 13:14, LUK 21:20; ; DAN 9:27, DAN 11:31, DAN 12:11

## Jesus Christ confirms the OT teaching about judgment
**MAT 12:41-12:42** parallel LUK 11:30-11:32 *See also* MAT 11:23 parallel LUK 10:12-10:15

## Jesus Christ appeals to the OT in its entirety
**LUK 11:50-11:51** *The murders of Abel and Zechariah occur in the first (Ge 4:8) and last (2Ch 24:21) books of the Hebrew OT (Chronicles comes at the close of the OT according to the Hebrew arrangement).* *See also* LUK 24:27, LUK 24:44 *The Law, the Prophets and the Psalms are the three divisions of the Hebrew OT.*
