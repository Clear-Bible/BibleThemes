# 5273: creativity

Imagination and skill used in the expression of visual or performing arts. Human creativity has its source in God, but may be used either to his honour or dishonour.

## God's creativity
**[ISA 64:8](bibleref:23064008)** *See also* [JOB 38:1-38:41](bibleref:18038001-18038041); [PSA 139:13-139:16](bibleref:19139013-19139016); [ECC 3:11](bibleref:21003011); [ISA 45:7-45:12](bibleref:23045007-23045012), [ISA 45:18](bibleref:23045018); [EPH 2:10](bibleref:49002010) *Redemption may be described in terms of re-creation.*
