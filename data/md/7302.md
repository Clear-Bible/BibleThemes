# 7302: altar

A construction, usually of wood, stone or metal, for the offering of sacrifice. The conflict between true and false religion often focused on altars, which were of key significance in biblical times.

**[GEN 8:20](bibleref:01008020)** *These early altars were erected at sites where God's presence or power had been experienced.*  [GEN 12:7-12:8](bibleref:01012007-01012008), [GEN 13:18](bibleref:01013018), [GEN 22:9](bibleref:01022009)
