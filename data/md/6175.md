# 6175: guilt, removal of

Through the death of Jesus Christ, God has provided a means of removing guilt, thus opening the way for believers to know the blessings of a right relationship with him.



## The removal of guilt is God's work
**[JER 2:22](bibleref:24002022), [JER 50:20](bibleref:24050020)**; **[ROM 8:33](bibleref:45008033)** *“Justify” is a legal term which includes the meaning “to declare not guilty”.*  *See also* [2SA 24:10](bibleref:10024010) parallel 1CH 21:8; [PSA 32:1-32:5](bibleref:19032001-19032005); [ISA 6:1-6:6](bibleref:23006001-23006006)
