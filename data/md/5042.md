# 5042: name of God, significance of

God's name is synonymous with his person, his presence and his power, and is therefore held in the highest honour.



## God is referred to as the Name
**ACT 5:41** *See also* LEV 24:10-24:16; 2SA 6:2 parallel 1CH 13:6; 3JN 1:7

## God's person is revealed in his name
**EXO 34:5-34:7** *See also* GEN 14:18-14:20, GEN 17:1, GEN 21:33, GEN 32:29-32:30; EXO 3:13-3:15, EXO 6:3, EXO 33:19; JDG 13:17-13:18; JER 16:21

## God's name is synonymous with his presence
**NUM 6:22-6:27** *See also* DEU 12:5-12:7; 1KI 8:15-8:21 parallel 2CH 6:4-6:11; NEH 1:9; PSA 20:1, PSA 54:1, PSA 74:7, PSA 75:1; ISA 30:27; JHN 17:11-17:12

## The name of God identifies his people
**DEU 28:10** *See also* 2CH 7:14; ISA 43:6-43:7; MIC 4:5, MIC 5:4

## Dishonouring God's name is a serious offence
**EXO 20:7** *See also* LEV 19:12, LEV 22:2, LEV 22:32; PSA 74:10, PSA 74:18, PSA 139:20; EZK 43:7-43:8; MAL 1:6

## The righteous honour God's name
**PSA 29:2** *See also* NEH 1:11; PSA 86:11-86:12; ISA 26:13; MAL 2:5, MAL 3:16-4:2
