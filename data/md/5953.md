# 5953: stability

The state of being in a firm or unwavering situation, whether emotional, political or personal. Stability is an important element of a settled life before God.



## Stability in the life of faith


### Stability for the believer is based on God's unchanging nature
**DEU 32:4**; **PSA 103:19** *See also* 1SA 2:2; 2SA 22:32; JOB 25:2; PSA 62:2, PSA 89:2, PSA 93:2; ISA 26:4; MAT 7:24-7:27

### Stability gives resistance to spiritual assault
**1CO 16:13**; **COL 1:22-1:23** *See also* PSA 20:7-20:8, PSA 40:2; PRO 10:25; MAT 10:22; ROM 16:25; 1CO 15:58; 2CO 1:24; GAL 5:1; PHP 1:27, PHP 1:27; COL 4:12; 1TH 3:8; 2TH 2:15; HEB 6:19, HEB 10:23; JAS 1:23-1:25, JAS 5:8; 2PE 1:12

### Stability depends upon firmness of faith
**COL 2:5-2:7** *See also* PSA 57:7, PSA 119:5; ISA 26:3; EPH 3:17; HEB 3:14; 1PE 5:9-5:10

### Stability is an important aim of Christian ministry
**EPH 4:14** *See also* COL 1:28

## Political stability
**2SA 7:16**; **PRO 29:4** *See also* 1SA 13:13; 2SA 5:12; 1KI 2:12; PRO 16:12; ISA 16:5
