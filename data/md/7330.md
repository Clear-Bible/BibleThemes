# 7330: chief priests

A group of individuals in charge of temple worship in Jerusalem, and regarded as leading representatives of the Jewish people, who came into conflict with Jesus Christ and plotted his death.

## OT references to the chief priest
**[2CH 19:11](bibleref:14019011)** *See also* [2KI 25:18](bibleref:12025018) parallel JER 52:24 *Seraiah* [2CH 24:6](bibleref:14024006), [2CH 24:11](bibleref:14024011)
[2CH 31:10](bibleref:14031010) *Azariah* ; [EZR 7:5](bibleref:15007005) *Ezra was descended from Aaron the chief priest* 

## The chief priests were in charge of temple worship in Jerusalem
 [MAT 2:4](bibleref:40002004) *They were expected to pronounce on matters of Jewish law and interpretation of Scripture* ; [MAT 21:15](bibleref:40021015) *Temple discipline was their responsibility.* 

## Jesus Christ predicted his suffering at the hands of the chief priests and elders
**[MAT 16:21](bibleref:40016021)** parallels MRK 8:31, LUK 9:22 *See also* [MAT 20:18](bibleref:40020018) parallel MRK 10:33

## The response of the chief priests to Jesus Christ


### They were puzzled by Jesus Christ's teaching
**[MAT 21:23](bibleref:40021023)** parallels MRK 11:27-11:28, LUK 20:1-20:2 *See also* [MAT 21:45-21:46](bibleref:40021045-40021046) parallel LUK 20:19; [JHN 7:32](bibleref:43007032), [JHN 7:45](bibleref:43007045), [JHN 11:47](bibleref:43011047) *As official representatives of the Jewish people, they feared the political repercussions of Jesus Christ's popularity.* 

### They and the elders plotted Jesus Christ's death
**[MAT 26:3-26:5](bibleref:40026003-40026005)** parallel MRK 14:1 *See also* [MAT 26:59](bibleref:40026059) parallel MRK 14:55; [MAT 27:1](bibleref:40027001)

### They collaborated with Judas
 [MAT 26:14](bibleref:40026014) parallels MRK 14:10, LUK 22:4; [MAT 26:47](bibleref:40026047) parallels MRK 14:43, LUK 22:52, JHN 18:3; [MAT 27:3-27:6](bibleref:40027003-40027006)

## The role of the chief priests in the trial of Jesus Christ


### They made accusations against Jesus Christ
 [MAT 27:12](bibleref:40027012) parallel MRK 15:3; [LUK 22:66](bibleref:42022066), [LUK 23:10](bibleref:42023010)

### They persuaded the crowd against Jesus Christ
 [MAT 27:20](bibleref:40027020); [LUK 23:4](bibleref:42023004), [LUK 23:13](bibleref:42023013); [JHN 19:6](bibleref:43019006)

### They mocked Jesus Christ
 [MAT 27:41](bibleref:40027041) parallel MRK 15:31

### They handed Jesus Christ over to Pilate
 [MRK 15:10](bibleref:41015010); [LUK 24:20](bibleref:42024020); [JHN 18:35](bibleref:43018035)

### They persuaded Pilate to have the tomb guarded
 [MAT 27:62-27:66](bibleref:40027062-40027066)

### They bribed the soldiers
 [MAT 28:11-28:15](bibleref:40028011-40028015)

### They rejected Jesus Christ as king
 [JHN 19:15](bibleref:43019015), [JHN 19:21](bibleref:43019021)
