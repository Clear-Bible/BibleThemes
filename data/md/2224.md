# 2224: Jesus Christ, the Lord

A title that signifies Jesus Christ's absolute authority and the basis on which people may know him. It is especially associated with his resurrection and return.



## Lord as a title of respect for the earthly Jesus Christ
**[MAT 8:25](bibleref:40008025)** *See also* [MAT 15:25](bibleref:40015025), [MAT 15:27](bibleref:40015027) parallel MRK 7:28; [MAT 21:3](bibleref:40021003) parallels MRK 11:3, LUK 19:31

## Lord as a mark of Jesus Christ's authority as a teacher
**[1TH 4:15](bibleref:52004015)** *See also* [ACT 11:16](bibleref:44011016), [ACT 20:35](bibleref:44020035); [1CO 7:10](bibleref:46007010)

## Lord as a sign of Jesus Christ's divinity


### Jesus Christ's divinity
**[JHN 20:28](bibleref:43020028)** *“Lord” is a common divine title in the Greek OT and is instinctively transferred to Jesus Christ in the NT.*  *See also* [1CO 8:6](bibleref:46008006); [2CO 3:17-3:18](bibleref:47003017-47003018); [EPH 4:5](bibleref:49004005); [2TH 2:16](bibleref:53002016); [2PE 1:2](bibleref:61001002)

### The day of Christ Jesus
**[PHP 1:6](bibleref:50001006)** *The “day of Christ Jesus” is equivalent to the OT “day of the LORD”.*  *See also* [1CO 1:8](bibleref:46001008); [2CO 1:14](bibleref:47001014); [PHP 1:10](bibleref:50001010), [PHP 2:16](bibleref:50002016); [REV 1:10](bibleref:66001010)

### Jesus Christ is ruler over all
**[REV 17:14](bibleref:66017014)** *See also* [DEU 10:17](bibleref:05010017); [REV 19:16](bibleref:66019016)

### Jesus Christ is the Saviour
**[ROM 10:13](bibleref:45010013)** *See also* [JOL 2:32](bibleref:29002032); [ACT 2:21](bibleref:44002021)

### Jesus Christ is Lord of the Sabbath
**[MAT 12:8](bibleref:40012008)** parallels MRK 2:28, LUK 6:5
