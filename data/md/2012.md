# 2012: Jesus Christ, authority of

The right of Jesus Christ to speak and act on his Father's behalf in forgiving sin, pronouncing judgment and promising eternal life to those who believe in him.



## Jesus Christ's authority underlines his divine status
**[JHN 8:28](bibleref:43008028)** *See also* [JHN 8:58](bibleref:43008058), [JHN 14:6](bibleref:43014006); [1CO 1:24](bibleref:46001024); [COL 2:10](bibleref:51002010)

## The origin of Jesus Christ's authority


### It is given by God the Father
**[JHN 3:35](bibleref:43003035)** *See also* [MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018); [LUK 10:22](bibleref:42010022); [JHN 5:26-5:27](bibleref:43005026-43005027), [JHN 7:16](bibleref:43007016), [JHN 10:18](bibleref:43010018), [JHN 12:49-12:50](bibleref:43012049-43012050), [JHN 17:2](bibleref:43017002)

### It is limited by the Father
**[MRK 10:40](bibleref:41010040)** *See also* [MAT 24:36](bibleref:40024036) parallel MRK 13:32; [PHP 2:6-2:8](bibleref:50002006-50002008)

### It is questioned by others
**[MAT 12:24-12:28](bibleref:40012024-40012028)** parallels MRK 3:22-3:26, LUK 11:15-11:20 *See also* [MAT 21:23-21:27](bibleref:40021023-40021027) parallels MRK 11:27-11:33, LUK 20:1-20:8; [JHN 2:18](bibleref:43002018)

## The authority of Jesus Christ is seen in his works


### He has authority over nature
 [MAT 8:23-8:27](bibleref:40008023-40008027) parallels MRK 4:36-4:41, LUK 8:22-8:25; [MAT 14:22-14:33](bibleref:40014022-40014033) parallels MRK 6:45-6:51, JHN 6:15-6:21; [MAT 21:18-21:22](bibleref:40021018-40021022) parallels MRK 11:12-11:14, MRK 11:20-11:24

### He has authority over sin
 [MAT 9:1-9:8](bibleref:40009001-40009008) parallels MRK 2:3-2:12, LUK 5:18-5:26; [LUK 7:37-7:38](bibleref:42007037-42007038), [LUK 7:44-7:50](bibleref:42007044-42007050)

### He has authority over sickness
 [MAT 8:14-8:15](bibleref:40008014-40008015) parallels MRK 1:30-1:31, LUK 4:38-4:39; [MAT 20:29-20:34](bibleref:40020029-40020034) parallels MRK 10:46-10:52, LUK 18:35-18:43; [ACT 3:16](bibleref:44003016), [ACT 4:30](bibleref:44004030)

### He has authority over evil
 [MRK 1:23-1:27](bibleref:41001023-41001027) parallel LUK 4:33-4:36; [LUK 10:17](bibleref:42010017)

### He has authority over death
 [MAT 9:18-9:25](bibleref:40009018-40009025) parallels MRK 5:22-5:42, LUK 8:40-8:55; [MAT 28:1-28:7](bibleref:40028001-40028007) parallels MRK 16:1-16:6, LUK 24:1-24:8, JHN 20:1-20:17; [LUK 7:11-7:16](bibleref:42007011-42007016); [JHN 11:38-11:44](bibleref:43011038-43011044)

## The authority of Jesus Christ is seen in his teaching


### Jesus Christ claims authority for his own words
**[LUK 4:18](bibleref:42004018)** *See also* [MAT 5:21-5:22](bibleref:40005021-40005022), [MAT 5:27-5:28](bibleref:40005027-40005028), [MAT 12:41-12:42](bibleref:40012041-40012042), [MAT 17:5](bibleref:40017005) parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; [MRK 2:5](bibleref:41002005), [MRK 2:10](bibleref:41002010), [MRK 8:38](bibleref:41008038), [MRK 10:15](bibleref:41010015) *The use of the phrase “I tell you the truth” emphasises Jesus Christ's authority* ; [MRK 13:26](bibleref:41013026), [MRK 13:31](bibleref:41013031); [JHN 4:24-4:26](bibleref:43004024-43004026), [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063), [JHN 7:15-7:18](bibleref:43007015-43007018), [JHN 12:48-12:50](bibleref:43012048-43012050), [JHN 13:13](bibleref:43013013), [JHN 14:6](bibleref:43014006) *The “I am” sayings in John's Gospel emphasise the divine nature of Jesus Christ's authority* ; [JHN 15:3](bibleref:43015003)

### His authority is recognised by others
**[MAT 7:28-7:29](bibleref:40007028-40007029)** *See also* [MAT 22:33](bibleref:40022033), [MAT 22:46](bibleref:40022046); [MRK 1:22](bibleref:41001022), [MRK 1:27](bibleref:41001027) parallel LUK 4:32

 [MAT 26:65-26:68](bibleref:40026065-40026068); [LUK 4:28-4:30](bibleref:42004028-42004030)
