# 7209: congregation

A group of people gathered or called together for a purpose. In Scripture a congregation refers particularly to the Israelites gathered for worship; it is also used of Christians in the NT.



## The congregation of the Israelites


### For the giving of the Ten Commandments
 *See also* EXO 19:17; DEU 5:22

### For hearing God's word
**NEH 8:1-8:9** *See also* DEU 5:1; NEH 8:18, NEH 9:1-9:3

### For praising God
**PSA 22:22** *See also* PSA 40:9-40:10, PSA 68:26; HEB 2:12

### To make a sin offering for the sin of the community
 LEV 4:13-4:14; ; NUM 15:24

### For the ordination of priests and Levites
 LEV 8:3-8:4; ; NUM 8:9

### For census, retribution and major events
 LEV 24:14; ; NUM 1:18; ; JOS 18:1; ; JDG 20:1; ; 1SA 10:17

### Exclusion from the assembly
 DEU 23:1-23:8

## In the NT


### Jewish congregations in the synagogue
**LUK 4:20** *See also* ACT 13:5, ACT 13:14-13:15, ACT 14:1

### Christian congregations
**ACT 2:46-2:47** *See also* ACT 5:12, ACT 11:26, ACT 20:7; 1CO 14:33-14:34; JAS 2:2
