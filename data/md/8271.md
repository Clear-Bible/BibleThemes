# 8271: holiness, purpose of

God in his holiness desires a holy people amongst whom he can dwell, and who can effectively worship, witness to and serve him as they prepare for a future with God and to be like God.



## The goal of holiness is to be like God
**LEV 19:2**; **MAT 5:48** *See also* ROM 8:29; HEB 12:10; 1PE 1:15-1:16; 1JN 3:2-3:3

## God dwells with holy people


### God dwelt with the people of Israel
**DEU 23:14** *See also* EXO 29:42-29:46; NUM 5:1-5:3; 1KI 9:3; EZK 37:26-37:28; ZEC 2:10-2:12

### God dwells with Christians
**EPH 2:19-2:22** *See also* 1CO 3:16-3:17

## Holiness is required for acceptable worship
**HEB 10:19-10:22** *See also* LEV 22:17-22:22; 2CH 29:15-29:31; ISA 56:6-56:7; MAT 15:7-15:9 parallel MRK 7:6; ISA 29:13; ROM 12:1

## Holiness is needed for effective witness
**1PE 2:9-2:12** *See also* EZK 20:41, EZK 36:20, EZK 39:7; 1PE 3:1-3:2

## Holiness is needed for godly service
**HEB 9:13-9:14** *See also* EXO 28:41; LEV 21:6-21:8; 2CH 35:3; ZEP 3:9; LUK 1:74-1:75; 2TI 2:20-2:21; TIT 2:14

## Holiness leads to a future hope


### Holy people will see God
**HEB 12:14** *See also* MAT 5:8; 1TH 3:13; 2TH 1:10

### Holy people will receive eternal life
**ROM 6:22** *See also* 2PE 3:11

### Holy people will inherit the kingdom
**COL 1:12** *See also* DAN 7:18, DAN 7:22, DAN 7:27; EPH 1:18; REV 20:6

### Holy people will judge the world
 1CO 6:2

### Believers' ultimate destiny is to share God's holiness for ever
**EPH 5:25-5:27**; **REV 21:2-21:3**
