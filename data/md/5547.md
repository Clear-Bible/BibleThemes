# 5547: speech, power and significance of

A person's speech has an enormous influence for good or ill and is a sure guide to character.



## The impact of speech


### It has the power of life and death
**PRO 18:21**; **JAS 3:1-3:12** *See also* PRO 15:2, PRO 15:4

### It can bring ruin or joy to the speaker
**PRO 12:13-12:14** *See also* PSA 140:9; PRO 10:14, PRO 13:3, PRO 14:3, PRO 18:6-18:7, PRO 18:20, PRO 21:23; ECC 10:12-10:14

### It can destroy others
**PRO 11:9** *See also* JOB 19:2; PSA 55:20-55:21, PSA 57:4, PSA 64:3-64:4, PSA 140:3; PRO 11:11, PRO 12:6, PRO 12:18, PRO 16:27

### It can bring life to others
**PRO 10:11** *See also* PRO 10:20-10:21, PRO 10:31-10:32, PRO 16:21, PRO 16:23-16:24, PRO 25:12; ISA 50:4; EPH 4:29

## The timing of speech


### There is an appropriate time for speaking
**ECC 3:1-3:8** *See also* PRO 15:23, PRO 15:28, PRO 25:11-25:12

### There is an appropriate time for silence
**PRO 11:12-11:13** *See also* 1SA 10:27; PRO 10:19, PRO 17:27-17:28, PRO 21:23, PRO 26:4; ECC 5:2-5:3; AMO 5:13; HAB 2:20; ZEP 1:7; JAS 1:19

## Spoken witness produces faith
**ROM 10:17** *See also* ISA 50:4; ACT 2:40-2:41, ACT 28:23-28:24; 1PE 3:15

## Jesus Christ was silent before his accusers
**1PE 2:22-2:23** *See also* MAT 26:63 parallel MRK 14:61; MAT 27:14 parallel MRK 15:5; LUK 23:9; JHN 19:8-19:9

## Speech is an expression of a person's character
**MAT 12:34-12:37** *See also* PSA 5:9, PSA 15:1-15:5; MAT 15:10-15:20 parallel MRK 7:14-7:23; LUK 6:45; JAS 1:26; REV 14:5
