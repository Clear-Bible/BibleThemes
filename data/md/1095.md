# 1095: God, patience of

God shows forbearance in his character, decisions and actions. Above all, he waits patiently for people to turn to him for salvation.



## God's patient character


### God is slow to anger
**[NUM 14:18](bibleref:04014018)** *“slow to anger” is the main OT expression for God's patient character.*  *See also* [EXO 34:6](bibleref:02034006); [NEH 9:16-9:17](bibleref:16009016-16009017); [PSA 86:15](bibleref:19086015), [PSA 103:8](bibleref:19103008), [PSA 145:8](bibleref:19145008); [JOL 2:13](bibleref:29002013); [JON 4:2](bibleref:32004002); [NAM 1:3](bibleref:34001003)

### God imparts patience
**[ROM 15:5](bibleref:45015005)** *The Greek can be translated “God of endurance/patience”.  The phrase suggests that God is patient and imparts patience.*  *See also* [JER 15:15](bibleref:24015015); [2TH 3:5](bibleref:53003005); [1PE 3:20](bibleref:60003020)
