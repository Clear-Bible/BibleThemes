# 5682: family, significance of

God created the family and is deeply concerned for its welfare. Scripture uses family metaphors to describe the relationship between God and his people.



## God created the family
**PSA 68:6** *See also* GEN 1:26-1:27, GEN 2:23-2:24

## God cares for the families of those who honour him
**GEN 7:1** *See also* GEN 6:18 parallel DEU 5:8-5:10; 2SA 6:11; JER 35:18-35:19, JER 38:17-38:18

## God judges families who disobey him
**EXO 20:4-20:5** *See also* NUM 16:23-16:32; DEU 11:6; JOS 7:24-7:26; 1SA 3:12-3:14; 2SA 24:17

## Families in Israel


### The extended family was the basic social unit
**NEH 7:5** *See also* LEV 25:10, LEV 27:16; NUM 2:32, NUM 4:40

### Family heads were community leaders
**NUM 36:1** *See also* NUM 1:4, NUM 31:26; 1KI 8:1; 2CH 1:2

### Families had religious obligations
**DEU 12:7** *See also* EXO 12:22-12:23; DEU 12:11-12:12; 1SA 1:21

### Families were to teach their children God's law
**PSA 78:5-78:7** *See also* GEN 18:19; EXO 12:26-12:27; DEU 4:9, DEU 6:6-6:7; PRO 1:8-1:9

## The relationship between God and his people described in family metaphors


### They are his family
**GAL 6:10** *See also* AMO 3:1; EPH 3:15; 1PE 4:17

### They are his children
**JHN 1:12-1:13** *See also* DEU 14:1; ISA 63:16; HOS 11:1-11:4; ROM 8:15-8:17; GAL 4:4-4:7; EPH 1:5

### They are brothers and sisters in God's family
**HEB 2:11** *See also* PSA 22:22; ISA 8:17-8:18; MAT 12:50 parallel LUK 8:21; MRK 3:31-3:34; HEB 2:12-2:17

## The salvation of families
**ACT 16:34** *See also* ACT 11:14, ACT 18:8; 1CO 1:16

## Good family management as a qualification for church office
**1TI 3:4-3:5** *See also* 1TI 3:12

## Christians are to support their families
**1TI 5:8** *See also* 1TI 5:4

## The urgent demands of the kingdom of God should take priority over family responsibilities
**LUK 9:59-9:60** parallel MAT 8:21-8:22 *See also* MRK 3:31-3:34; LUK 9:61-9:62; MAT 10:34-10:38 parallel LUK 12:51-12:53; MIC 7:6
