# 4636: dove

A common species of bird. In Scripture the dove symbolises the Spirit of God, and also certain kinds of human behaviour.



## A kind of bird
 GEN 8:8-8:12; ; SNG 2:12; ; JER 8:7

## Doves as sacrifices for those who were poor
**LEV 5:7** *See also* GEN 15:9; LEV 1:14, LEV 5:11, LEV 12:6-12:8, LEV 14:22, LEV 14:30, LEV 15:14, LEV 15:29; NUM 6:10; MAT 21:12 parallel MRK 11:15; LUK 2:24; JHN 2:14

## ``Dove'' as a term of endearment
 SNG 2:14, SNG 5:2, SNG 6:9

### Israel as God's ``dove''
 PSA 68:13, PSA 74:19

## ``Dove'' used figuratively
 PSA 55:6; ; SNG 1:15, SNG 4:1, SNG 5:12; ; ISA 38:14, ISA 59:11; ; JER 48:28; ; EZK 7:16; ; HOS 7:11, HOS 11:11; ; NAM 2:7; ; MAT 10:16

## The dove as a symbol of the Spirit of God
**MAT 3:16** parallels MRK 1:10, LUK 3:22, JHN 1:32-1:34
