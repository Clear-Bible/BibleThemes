# 4636: dove

A common species of bird. In Scripture the dove symbolises the Spirit of God, and also certain kinds of human behaviour.

## A kind of bird
 [GEN 8:8-8:12](bibleref:01008008-01008012); [SNG 2:12](bibleref:22002012); [JER 8:7](bibleref:24008007)

## Doves as sacrifices for those who were poor

## ``Dove'' as a term of endearment
 [SNG 2:14](bibleref:22002014), [SNG 5:2](bibleref:22005002), [SNG 6:9](bibleref:22006009)
