# 5167: mouth

In addition to its normal physical functions, it has great potential for good and evil. The words of God's mouth are always effective. Mouth is also used in the sense of an entrance or opening.



## The words of God's mouth


### They are always effective
**LAM 3:38** *See also* ISA 55:11

### They are creative and life-giving
**DEU 8:3** *See also* PSA 33:6; MAT 4:4

### They bring judgment
**HOS 6:5** *See also* JOB 15:30; 2TH 2:8; REV 2:16

### They are reliable
**NUM 23:19** *See also* 1KI 8:15 parallel 2CH 6:4; 1KI 8:24 parallel 2CH 6:15; HEB 6:13-6:18

### They are to be treasured
**PSA 119:72** *See also* JOS 1:8; JOB 23:12; PSA 119:13-119:14, PSA 119:103
