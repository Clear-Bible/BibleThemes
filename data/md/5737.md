# 5737: sisters

The daughter of one's parents, or of either mother or father. The relationship with a sister is a close one, especially with an unmarried sister.



## The relationship with a sister was a close one
**EZK 44:25** *See also* LEV 21:1-21:3; NUM 6:6-6:7; JOB 1:1-1:4

## A brother had some measure of responsibility for his sister
**SNG 8:8-8:9** *See also* GEN 24:28-24:60; 2SA 13:19-13:20

## Sexual relationships with sisters


### Marrying a sister was permissible in pre-Mosaic times
**GEN 20:12**

### Sexual relationships with a sister were later forbidden in Mosaic law
**LEV 18:9** *See also* LEV 18:11-18:13, LEV 18:18, LEV 20:17; DEU 27:22; EZK 22:11

### Rape
 GEN 34:1-34:31 *Dinah's rape was avenged by her brothers*; ; 2SA 13:1-13:19 *Tamar was raped by her half-brother*; ; 2SA 13:23-13:36 *Absalom avenged Tamar's rape.*

## Sometimes the wives of the patriarchs posed as their sisters
**GEN 20:13** *See also* GEN 12:10-12:20, GEN 20:1-20:7, GEN 26:1-26:10

## Examples of the behaviour of sisters


### Jealousy
**GEN 30:1** *See also* GEN 30:7-30:8

### Criticism
**LUK 10:38-10:42** *See also* NUM 12:1-12:15

### Kindness and concern
**2CH 22:11** parallel 2KI 11:2 *See also* EXO 2:1-2:8; JOB 42:11; JHN 11:1-11:3, JHN 11:17-11:32

## Sisters-in-law


### Marriage to a sister-in-law was allowed in specific circumstances
**DEU 25:5-25:10** *See also* GEN 38:6-38:11

### Sexual relationships with sisters-in-law were forbidden under normal circumstances
**LEV 18:16** *See also* LEV 20:21; MRK 6:17-6:20; LUK 3:19-3:20

## Sister used as a term of affection


### By a husband
**SNG 4:9-4:10** *See also* SNG 4:12, SNG 5:1-5:2

### In the church
**ROM 16:1** *See also* 1TI 5:1-5:2; PHM 1:1-1:2; JAS 2:15-2:17; 2JN 1:13

## The metaphorical use of sister


### Of death
**JOB 17:13-17:16**

### Of wisdom
**PRO 7:1-7:5**

### Of nations
**JER 3:6-3:10** *See also* EZK 16:44-16:63, EZK 23:1-23:49

## Sister as a theological term


### Describing the relationship of believers to Jesus Christ
**MAT 12:46-12:50** parallel MRK 3:31-3:35

### Jesus Christ requires his followers to put him before family ties
**LUK 14:26** *See also* MAT 19:29 parallel MRK 10:29-10:30
