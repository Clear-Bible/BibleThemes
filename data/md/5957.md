# 5957: strength, spiritual

In contrast to human strength, God is the source and supplier of all spiritual strength through which believers can do all that God asks.



## God is the source of spiritual strength


### God gives strength
**EPH 6:10** *See also* JDG 16:3, JDG 16:6, JDG 16:18-16:20; PSA 68:35; ISA 40:29-40:31; LUK 24:49; ACT 1:8; EPH 3:16, EPH 3:20; 2TH 2:16-2:17; 1TI 1:12; 2TI 1:7

### Strength comes from the promise of his presence
**JOS 1:9** *See also* DEU 31:7-31:8; PSA 119:28; ISA 41:10; JER 1:8; HAG 2:4; 2TI 4:17

### Strength comes from the realisation of his grace
**2TI 2:1** *See also* ACT 20:32; 1CO 15:10; HEB 13:9

### Strength comes through the Holy Spirit
**ZEC 4:6-4:7** *See also* JDG 14:6; LUK 4:14; EPH 3:7, EPH 3:16

### Overcoming strength is often veiled in weakness
**2CO 12:7-12:10** *See also* PSA 8:2; ACT 14:19-14:20; 2CO 4:8-4:12; COL 2:15; HEB 11:32-11:38
