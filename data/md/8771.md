# 8771: idolatry, objections to

Scripture presents idolatry as absurd and irrational, offensive to God and a source of spiritual and moral danger.



## Idolatry is disparaged


### The absurdity of making idols
**HAB 2:18** *See also* DEU 4:28; ISA 40:18-40:20, ISA 41:6-41:7; JER 10:3-10:9; HOS 10:6; HAB 2:19

### Idols represent falsehood and fraud
**PSA 40:4** *See also* JER 2:5, JER 10:14, JER 16:19, JER 51:17-51:18; HOS 12:1; AMO 2:4

### Bowing down to idols is inappropriate
**PSA 135:17-135:18** parallel PSA 115:5-115:8 *See also* ISA 2:8, ISA 44:15-44:17, ISA 46:6; JER 1:16; MIC 5:13

## Idols contrasted with the LORD
**ISA 46:5** *See also* ISA 40:25-40:26; JER 5:24, JER 10:10; HOS 2:8; ACT 17:24-17:25

## Idolatry is forbidden by God
**EXO 20:3-20:4** parallel DEU 5:7-5:8

## Idolatry incurs severe penalties
**ZEP 1:4** *See also* DEU 13:1-13:9, DEU 13:12-13:15; ISA 66:4; AMO 5:27; NAM 1:14

## God abominates idols
**EZK 7:20** *Two terms, that is “idols” and “vile images”, meaning “abomination” are found here in parallel. A third, punning on the word for “dung” is often used by Ezekiel for idols.* *See also* DEU 7:25; 2KI 23:24; EZK 5:9, EZK 5:11, EZK 6:4

## Pressure to worship idols must be resisted and idols abandoned
**DAN 3:18** *See also* ISA 2:20, ISA 30:22, ISA 31:7

## The dangers of idolatry


### Blindness
**ISA 44:18**

### Becoming like idols
**PSA 115:8** *See also* JER 2:5; HOS 9:10

### Spiritual adultery
**DEU 31:16** *See also* JDG 8:33; ISA 1:21; JER 13:27; HOS 1:2, HOS 2:7, HOS 8:9

### Injustice
**AMO 5:7**
