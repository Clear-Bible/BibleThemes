# 5621: wheel

Early wheels were solid wooden discs. These were replaced around 1500 B.C. by lighter, spoked wheels. The most common use of wheels is on chariots and carts; they were also used by potters and in machinery for drawing water from a well. The chariot throne of God has wheels which are vividly described.



## Chariot wheels
**EXO 14:25**; **NAM 3:2** *The sound of chariot wheels announces the approach of the enemy.* *See also* 2KI 7:6; ISA 5:28; JER 47:3; JOL 2:5; REV 9:9

## Wagon wheels
The Hebrew word for ``wheel'' is here used for ``wagon'':
 EZK 23:24, EZK 26:10

## Threshing wheels
**PRO 20:26** *Threshing was carried out by driving a sledge or a cart with heavy wheels over the grain.* *See also* ISA 28:27-28:28

## Other examples of wheels
**1KI 7:32-7:33** *The stands for basins in Solomon's temple were mounted on wheels.* *See also* 1KI 7:30; ECC 12:6 *A wheel formed part of the machinery for drawing water from a well*; JER 18:3 *Potters turned their clay on a wheel.*

## The wheels of God's chariot throne
**EZK 11:22-11:23**; **DAN 7:9** *See also* 2KI 2:11; ISA 66:15; EZK 1:15-1:21, EZK 3:12-3:13, EZK 10:1-10:19
