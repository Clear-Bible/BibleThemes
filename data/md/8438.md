# 8438: giving, of time

People are stewards of a deposit of time given by God, and will be held responsible for the way in which they use it.



## Time is governed by God


### God determines every lifespan
**JOB 14:5** *See also* PSA 31:15, PSA 74:16-74:17, PSA 139:16; ACT 17:26

### God will demand an account of how time has been spent
**ECC 3:17** *See also* MAT 12:36; 1PE 4:3-4:5

## Time may be squandered


### In ungodly living
**1PE 4:3** *See also* JOL 2:25; EPH 2:2-2:3; COL 3:5-3:7

### By neglecting God-given opportunities
**JER 8:20** *See also* PRO 20:4; MAT 25:11-25:12; LUK 13:25, LUK 19:44; ACT 24:25; REV 2:21

## Time may be saved


### By understanding its brevity
**PSA 90:12** *See also* JHN 12:35; 1CO 7:29-7:31

### By remembering its uncertainty
**JHN 9:4** *See also* ECC 8:5-8:7; JAS 4:13-4:15

### By making the most of present opportunities
**EPH 5:16** *See also* PSA 32:6; ECC 12:1; EST 4:14; MAT 4:17; MRK 1:15; 2CO 6:1-6:2; GAL 6:10; COL 4:5; HEB 3:7-3:13

### By allocating it wisely
**1CH 12:32** *See also* EXO 18:13-18:26; ACT 6:1-6:4

## Time is to be spent wisely


### In worship and meditation
**PSA 119:164** *See also* PSA 4:4, PSA 55:17; LAM 3:40; DAN 6:10, DAN 6:13; MRK 1:35; 1CO 11:28

### In work
**PSA 104:23** *See also* EXO 20:9 parallel DEU 5:13; ROM 13:6

### In rest
**EXO 20:8-20:10** *See also* LEV 16:29; MRK 6:31

### With one's family
**DEU 24:5** *See also* DEU 6:7; ECC 9:9; 1TI 3:4

### In fellowship
**1CO 16:7** *See also* ACT 2:42, ACT 20:7; HEB 10:25

## Time is to be allocated according to priorities
**LUK 10:41-10:42** *See also* PSA 1:1-1:3; HAG 1:2-1:4; MAT 6:33; ACT 13:46-13:47, ACT 17:16-17:17
