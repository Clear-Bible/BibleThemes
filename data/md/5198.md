# 5198: weeping

The physical act of shedding tears as the result of sadness or joy or in recognition of sin.



## Weeping in personal grief
**GEN 23:2**; **PSA 42:3**; **REV 5:4** *See also* GEN 27:38, GEN 37:35; JDG 11:37; RUT 1:9; 1SA 1:8, 1SA 20:41; 2SA 3:16, 2SA 13:19, 2SA 15:30, 2SA 18:33 *David weeps for Absalom*; 2KI 8:11, 2KI 13:14, 2KI 20:3; JOB 16:16, JOB 16:20; PSA 39:12, PSA 56:8, PSA 102:9; JHN 11:33, JHN 20:11

## Weeping in shared grief
**DEU 34:8**; **PSA 137:1** *See also* NUM 14:1; 1SA 11:4-11:5, 1SA 20:41, 1SA 30:4; 2SA 1:12 *David and his men mourn for Saul and Jonathan*; 2SA 1:24, 2SA 3:34; JOB 2:12; PSA 80:4-80:5; ISA 15:2-15:3, ISA 33:7; MRK 5:38; ACT 21:13

## Weeping in personal remorse over failure and sin
**MAT 26:75** parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:62 *See also* 1SA 2:33; LUK 7:38; HEB 12:17

## Weeping in group response to sin
**NEH 8:9**; **JER 3:21**; **JAS 5:1** *See also* NUM 25:6; DEU 1:45; JDG 20:26, JDG 21:2; EZR 3:12-3:13 *the people's response to the rebuilding of the temple*; ISA 22:12-22:13; JER 25:34, JER 31:9, JER 50:4; LAM 1:2, LAM 2:18; JOL 2:12; AMO 5:16-5:17, AMO 8:10; ZEC 12:10-12:11; MAT 8:12

## Weeping in response to others' sin and judgment
**PSA 119:136**; **JER 13:17**; **2CO 2:4** *See also* EZR 10:1; NEH 1:4; ISA 16:9; JER 9:1, JER 14:17, JER 22:10; LAM 1:16, LAM 3:48; EZK 27:31; MIC 1:8; LUK 19:41 *Jesus Christ weeps over Jerusalem*; LUK 23:28; ACT 20:31; PHP 3:18; REV 18:15-18:16
