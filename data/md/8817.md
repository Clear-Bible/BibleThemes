# 8817: ridicule, objects of

Ridicule of God and of believers is to be expected, but it will bring divine retribution upon its perpetrators.



## God ridiculed
**PSA 74:10-74:18** *See also* 2KI 19:4 parallel ISA 37:4

## Jesus Christ mocked
**LUK 22:63-22:65** parallels MAT 26:67-26:68, MRK 14:65 *See also* LUK 23:11; MAT 27:27-27:31 parallel MRK 15:16-15:20; JHN 19:1-19:3; MAT 27:39-27:44 parallels MRK 15:29-15:32, LUK 23:35-23:37

## God's messengers scorned
**2CH 36:15-36:16** *See also* 2CH 30:6-30:10; ACT 2:1-2:13, ACT 17:17-17:18, ACT 17:32; HEB 11:36
