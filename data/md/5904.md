# 5904: maturity, spiritual

The development of Christlike character and behaviour in the Christian through a renewed mind and tested faith.



## Christlikeness as the goal and model for spiritual maturity
**EPH 4:13-4:15** *See also* COL 2:6-2:7; HEB 12:2

## Marks of spiritual maturity


### Spiritual understanding
**COL 2:2** *See also* ROM 15:14; 1CO 2:6, 1CO 14:20; EPH 1:17-1:18; HEB 5:12-6:1

### Discernment of God's will and changed behaviour
**COL 1:9-1:10** *See also* ROM 12:2; 1CO 3:1-3:3; GAL 5:22-5:23; EPH 4:22-4:23; PHP 1:9-1:11; 2TH 1:3

### Stability
**COL 4:12** *See also* EPH 4:14; 2PE 3:17-3:18

### Care for the weaker brother
**ROM 15:1** *See also* GAL 6:1-6:2

## Maturity is to be the aim of the Christian
**PHP 3:13-3:15** *See also* LUK 8:14 *a reason for spiritual immaturity*; 2CO 7:1, 2CO 13:11; 1TI 6:11

## The process of maturity


### God causes spiritual growth
**PHP 1:6** *See also* GAL 3:3; HEB 10:14

### Possessing gifts of ministry
**EPH 4:11-4:13** *See also* ROM 1:11

### Being equipped by the word of God
**2TI 3:16-3:17** *See also* ROM 15:4; 1PE 2:2; 1JN 2:5

### Persevering through trials
**ROM 5:3-5:5** *See also* HEB 2:10; JAS 1:3-1:4; 1PE 5:10

## The concern of the pastor is to help others to maturity
**COL 1:28-1:29** *See also* 2CO 13:9-13:10; GAL 4:19
