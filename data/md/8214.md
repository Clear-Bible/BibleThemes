# 8214: confidence, basis of

The confidence of believers, is grounded in God's character, actions and trustworthy promises, rather than human achievements.



## Confidence originates with God


### In his unchanging and faithful nature
**NUM 23:19**; **TIT 1:1-1:2** *See also* 1SA 15:29; PSA 33:11, PSA 102:27; PRO 3:26; ISA 49:23; MAL 3:6; 2TI 1:12; JAS 1:17

### In his covenant
**JER 32:40**; **HEB 9:15** *See also* GEN 9:16 *with all living creatures*; GEN 17:7 *with Abraham*; 2SA 7:13 *with the house of David*; 2SA 23:5; PSA 89:34; ISA 54:10, ISA 55:3; EZK 16:60; ACT 3:25

### In election
**DEU 7:6**; **ROM 8:28** *See also* ISA 41:9-41:10; JER 31:3; ROM 8:33; 1CO 1:9; 1TH 5:24; 2TI 2:19

### In his past deliverances
**PSA 22:4-22:5**; **ROM 8:31-8:32** *See also* DEU 7:18, DEU 20:1; JOS 1:5; 1SA 17:37; 1KI 8:57; PSA 44:1-44:5, PSA 78:2-78:4; 2CO 1:10

### In his word
**ISA 55:10-55:11** *See also* PSA 89:34, PSA 119:74, PSA 130:5; ROM 15:4; 1PE 1:25; 2PE 1:19

### In his promises
**JOS 23:14**; **HEB 10:23** *See also* 1KI 8:20; PSA 119:140; 2PE 1:4

## Confidence is centred on Jesus Christ
**ROM 8:38-8:39** *See also* ACT 17:31; ROM 8:1; 2CO 1:20; COL 1:27; 1TI 3:13; HEB 7:22, HEB 7:25, HEB 9:15; 1PE 2:6; ISA 28:16

## Confidence is mediated by the Holy Spirit
**ROM 8:16** *See also* 2CO 1:21-1:22, 2CO 5:5; EPH 1:13-1:14
