# 8307: moderation

The classic virtue of temperate conduct, especially the avoidance of extremes. Scripture commends moderation as a way of life for Christians, while stressing the need for total commitment to God.



## The ideal of moderation
**ECC 7:16-7:18**

## Moderation in a legal context
**EXO 21:23-21:25** *The purpose of this legislation was not to encourage vengeance but to moderate or restrain it.* *See also* LEV 24:17-24:22; NUM 35:9-35:15 parallels DEU 4:41-4:43, DEU 19:1-19:10 *One purpose of cities of refuge was to moderate the revenge of families of the murdered.*

## Moderation in personal and social relations
**PRO 17:27** *See also* GEN 43:29-43:31; PRO 12:16, PRO 23:4; GAL 5:22-5:23

## Moderation in personal ethics
**TIT 2:11-2:12** *See also* PRO 15:16 *Moderation in matters of wealth is affirmed.*MAT 5:33-5:37; COL 4:6
EPH 5:18; 1TH 5:6-5:8; 1TI 3:2-3:3, 1TI 3:8, 1TI 3:11; TIT 1:7-1:8, TIT 2:2-2:8

## Moderation in religious activity
**ECC 7:16-7:17** *See also* MAT 23:23-23:26; 1CO 14:26-14:33

## Moderation must not lead to being spiritually lukewarm
**REV 3:15-3:16** *See also* REV 3:1-3:2
