# 5669: children, examples of

In Scripture specific children are marked out as belonging to the Lord. Mention of others is made in association with miracles or notable situations.



## The child Jesus Christ
**MAT 1:23** *See also* MAT 2:11; LUK 2:34, LUK 2:49-2:51

## Other notable children
 EXO 2:7-2:8 *Moses*; ; JDG 11:36 *Jephthah's daughter*; 1SA 2:18, 1SA 2:26; 1SA 16:11, 1SA 17:33, 1SA 17:41-17:42; 
2KI 5:2 *the young girl who helped Naaman*; ; LUK 1:80 *John the Baptist*; ; 2TI 3:15 *Timothy*

## Children in the miracles of Jesus Christ
Jairus' daughter:
 MAT 9:18 parallels MRK 5:22-5:23, LUK 8:41-8:42; ; MAT 9:23-9:25 parallels MRK 5:38-5:43, LUK 8:49-8:55; ; MAT 15:21-15:28 parallel MRK 7:24-7:30 *Canaanite woman's daughter*; ; MAT 17:14-17:18 parallels MRK 9:17-9:27, LUK 9:38-9:43 *boy with epilepsy*; ; LUK 7:11-7:15 *widow's son at Nain*; ; JHN 4:46-4:52 *official's son at Capernaum*; 
JHN 6:9 *the boy who gave his lunch to help feed the crowd*
