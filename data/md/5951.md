# 5951: slander

False and malicious talk about others. It not only harms those against whom it is directed, but also brings ruin to those who propagate it.



## Slander condemned


### Slander is sinful
**MRK 7:21-7:23** parallel MAT 15:19-15:20 *See also* JER 9:4-9:6; EZK 22:9; ROM 1:29-1:31; 2CO 12:20; 2TI 3:2-3:4

### Slander is forbidden
**LEV 19:16** *See also* EXO 23:1; PRO 3:30, PRO 10:18, PRO 12:22; EPH 4:31; COL 3:8; TIT 2:3, TIT 3:1-3:2; JAS 4:11; 1PE 2:1

### Slander is destructive
**PRO 16:27** *See also* PRO 11:9, PRO 26:28, PRO 30:10

## Dealing with slander


### Committing one's cause to God
**PSA 120:1-120:2** *See also* PSA 12:1-12:7, PSA 31:14-31:20, PSA 64:1-64:4, PSA 69:6-69:21, PSA 109:1-109:5

### Maintaining one's integrity
**1PE 2:12** *See also* PSA 119:23; 1PE 2:15, 1PE 3:9, 1PE 3:15-3:17

### Rejoicing in the Lord
**MAT 5:11-5:12** *See also* ACT 5:41

### Not listening to slander
**1SA 24:9** *See also* PRO 17:4

## Judgment upon slanderers
**PRO 21:28** *See also* DEU 22:13-22:19; PRO 19:5, PRO 19:9, PRO 21:28; ISA 29:20-29:21, ISA 51:7-51:8; 1CO 6:9-6:10

## Examples of slander
**JER 18:18** *See also* GEN 39:7-39:20; 1KI 21:1-21:14 *Naboth's vineyard*; NEH 6:5-6:7; PSA 31:13, PSA 35:15-35:16, PSA 38:19-38:20, PSA 41:5-41:9; LUK 23:2; ACT 6:13-6:14, ACT 24:5-24:9; ROM 3:8; 3JN 1:9-1:10
