# 5029: knowledge, of God

Human knowledge of God may be a purely intellectual phenomenon, as when one knows about God through observing the results of his actions, but God's people know him in a personal way because of their relationship with him.



## Knowledge of God through his actions


### God acts in creation
**ROM 1:20** *See also* PSA 19:1-19:4; ACT 14:17

### God acts in judgment
**EZK 38:22-38:23** *See also* EXO 7:5, EXO 14:4; PSA 83:9-83:18; EZK 6:13, EZK 39:6, EZK 39:23

### God acts in saving his people
**ISA 52:9-52:10** *See also* 1SA 17:46-17:47; ISA 37:20, ISA 49:26

## God's people know him through his dealings with them


### God delivers his people
**EXO 6:6-6:7** *See also* EXO 10:2, EXO 16:6; DEU 4:32-4:35; JOS 3:10, JOS 4:23-4:24; 1KI 20:13, 1KI 20:28

### God provides and cares for his people
**EXO 16:8** *See also* 1KI 8:56-8:60; ISA 41:17-41:20, ISA 45:4-45:6; EZK 37:26-37:28

### In giving them his law
**EZK 20:19-20:20** *See also* EXO 31:13; EZK 20:11-20:12

### God judges and restores his people
**EZK 11:10-11:12** *See also* NUM 14:34; JER 24:5-24:7, JER 31:33-31:34; EZK 6:2-6:10, EZK 7:4, EZK 12:15-12:16, EZK 20:38, EZK 36:11, EZK 37:14

## Knowledge of God leads to confidence in him
**PSA 9:10** *See also* GEN 22:7-22:8; JOB 19:25; PSA 18:2, PSA 37:25, PSA 71:5-71:6; ISA 12:1-12:2; DAN 11:32
