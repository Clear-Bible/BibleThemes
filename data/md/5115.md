# 5115: Peter, preacher and teacher

Peter preached mainly to the Jews. He taught both through his preaching and his letters.

## Peter wrote his first letter to encourage Christians
**[1PE 1:1](bibleref:60001001), [1PE 5:12](bibleref:60005012)** *See also* [1PE 2:1](bibleref:60002001) *He exhorted them to live holy lives* ; [1PE 2:13-2:14](bibleref:60002013-60002014) *They must obey authority* ; [1PE 3:16](bibleref:60003016)[1PE 4:12-4:16](bibleref:60004012-60004016), [1PE 4:19](bibleref:60004019)

## Peter wrote his second letter to strengthen Christians who were at risk from false teaching
**[2PE 1:12](bibleref:61001012)** *See also* [2PE 1:16](bibleref:61001016) *He reminded them of the truth of the gospel* ; [2PE 2:3](bibleref:61002003) *He warned them of the judgment awaiting false teachers* ; [2PE 3:13](bibleref:61003013) *They must keep alive their hope in the coming day of the Lord.*
