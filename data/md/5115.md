# 5115: Peter, preacher and teacher

Peter preached mainly to the Jews. He taught both through his preaching and his letters.



## Peter's commission
**JHN 21:17**

## Peter quoted from the OT in his teaching
Referring to the death of Judas:
 ACT 1:16, ACT 1:20; ACT 2:16-2:20, ACT 2:25-2:28, ACT 2:34-2:35; 
ACT 3:22-3:23, ACT 4:11; 1PE 1:15-1:16, 1PE 1:24-1:25, 1PE 2:6-2:8, 1PE 3:5-3:6, 1PE 3:12, 1PE 3:14, 1PE 3:20, 1PE 4:18, 1PE 5:5; ; 2PE 2:5-2:9

## Peter taught about Jesus Christ


### About the life and miracles of Jesus Christ
**ACT 2:22** *See also* ACT 10:36-10:39; 2PE 1:17-1:18

### About Jesus Christ's suffering and death
**ACT 2:23** *See also* ACT 3:13-3:15, ACT 4:10, ACT 5:30, ACT 10:39; 1PE 2:22-2:23, 1PE 3:18

### About the resurrection of Jesus Christ
**ACT 2:24** *See also* ACT 2:31-2:32, ACT 3:15, ACT 4:10, ACT 5:30, ACT 10:40-10:41; 1PE 1:3, 1PE 1:21, 1PE 3:18

### About the ascension and exaltation of Jesus Christ
**ACT 5:31** *See also* ACT 2:33, ACT 2:36, ACT 3:13; 1PE 1:21, 1PE 3:22

### About the second coming of Jesus Christ
**1PE 5:4** *See also* 1PE 1:7, 1PE 1:13, 1PE 4:13; 2PE 3:10-3:13

## Peter taught about salvation through repentance and faith in Jesus Christ
**ACT 4:12** *See also* ACT 2:38, ACT 3:19, ACT 10:43

## Peter taught that God cared for all people
 ACT 10:34, ACT 15:7-15:9

## Peter wrote his first letter to encourage Christians
**1PE 1:1, 1PE 5:12** *See also* 1PE 2:1 *He exhorted them to live holy lives*; 1PE 2:13-2:14 *They must obey authority*; 1PE 3:161PE 4:12-4:16, 1PE 4:19
1PE 5:10 *God will vindicate and save his people.*

## Peter wrote his second letter to strengthen Christians who were at risk from false teaching
**2PE 1:12** *See also* 2PE 1:16 *He reminded them of the truth of the gospel*; 2PE 2:3 *He warned them of the judgment awaiting false teachers*; 2PE 3:13 *They must keep alive their hope in the coming day of the Lord.*
