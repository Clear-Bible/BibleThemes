# 5707: male and female

God created humanity as male and female. Although gender differences are evident in behaviour and role, Scripture teaches the equality and complementarity of the sexes.



## The creation of male and female
**GEN 1:27** *See also* GEN 2:20-2:24, GEN 5:2

## The fall disrupted relationships between male and female
**GEN 3:14-3:19** *See also* 2SA 13:1-13:19; PRO 5:3-5:14; MAT 5:28; ROM 1:26-1:27

## The equality of male and female


### Equality under many old covenant regulations
**NUM 5:5-5:7** *See also* LEV 20:10-20:12; NUM 5:1-5:4; DEU 15:12-15:15, DEU 23:17-23:18

### Equality in religious observance
**EXO 35:22** *See also* NUM 6:1-6:4; DEU 31:12; 2SA 6:19

### Equality of accountability
**LAM 2:21** *See also* LEV 20:27; 2CH 36:15-36:17; ACT 5:1-5:11

### Equality in Christ
**GAL 3:26-3:28** *See also* ACT 2:17-2:18; JOL 2:28-2:29; 1CO 11:11-11:12

## Variations in the treatment of male and female


### In OT patriarchal society
**LEV 27:1-27:7** *See also* GEN 17:9-17:14; EXO 13:1-13:2, EXO 13:14-13:15; NUM 3:14-3:15, NUM 5:11-5:31, NUM 18:8-18:10

### In legislation protecting women under the old covenant
**NUM 27:1-27:7** *See also* NUM 36:1-36:12; DEU 22:13-22:19, DEU 22:25-22:29

### In roles within the NT church community
**1CO 14:33-14:35** *See also* 1CO 11:3-11:10; EPH 5:22-5:24 *The idea of “headship” is complex, but is generally regarded as including the ideas of responsibility and authority within a relationship*; 1TI 2:11-2:15; TIT 2:1-2:5

### In behaviour within the NT church community
**1TI 2:8-2:10** *See also* 1CO 11:3-11:16; 1TI 5:1-5:2

## Male and female roles within marriage
**1CO 7:1-7:7** *See also* PRO 31:10-31:31; EPH 5:22-5:33; COL 3:18-3:19; 1PE 3:1-3:7

## Male and female imagery used of God


### Female imagery
**MAT 23:37** parallel LUK 13:34 *See also* PRO 8:1-8:11; ISA 66:12-66:13

### Male imagery
**PSA 47:2** *See also* EXO 15:3; PSA 23:1; MAT 6:6; LUK 11:11-11:13 parallel MAT 7:9-7:11
