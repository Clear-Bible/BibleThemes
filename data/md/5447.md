# 5447: poverty, causes of

To want for the necessities of life is ultimately the result of human sin.



## Poverty arises from human sin
**GEN 3:17-3:19** *Compare this with the description of Eden in Ge 2:9-14.*

## Causes of poverty


### Divine retribution brings poverty
**DEU 28:47-28:48** *See also* PRO 22:16; ISA 3:1; AMO 4:6, AMO 5:11

### Idleness brings poverty
**PRO 6:10-6:11** *See also* PRO 10:4, PRO 14:23, PRO 20:13, PRO 24:33-24:34

### Dissolute living brings poverty
**PRO 21:17** *See also* PRO 6:26, PRO 28:19; LUK 15:14

### Lack of discipline brings poverty
 PRO 13:18, PRO 21:5

### Rash promises bring poverty
 PRO 22:26-22:27

### Debt brings poverty
 MAT 18:23-18:25

### Neglect of God's law of giving brings poverty
 PRO 11:24, PRO 28:22; ; ACT 20:35

### Neglect of God's work brings poverty
 HAG 1:9

### Oppression brings poverty
 JDG 6:6; ; JOB 20:19; ; PRO 13:23; ; ISA 1:7; ; JAS 5:4

### Famine brings poverty
 GEN 45:11, GEN 47:20-47:21

### Misfortune brings poverty
 JOB 1:13-1:21 *These trials were inflicted by Satan but allowed by God as a test of Job's faith.*

## Poverty affects good as well as evil people


### The righteous often escape poverty
 DEU 8:7-8:9; ; PSA 34:9-34:10, PSA 37:25

### Many of the godly have been poor
 2KI 4:1; ; LUK 16:20; ; ROM 15:26

### Jesus Christ was poor
 MAT 8:20

## Results of poverty


### Poverty results in ruin
**PRO 10:15**

### Poverty results in shame
 *See also* PRO 13:18; LUK 16:3

### Poverty results in misery
 PRO 31:7

### Poverty results in crime
 PRO 6:30, PRO 30:8-30:9

### Poverty need not lead to ungodliness
 PSA 119:71; ; MRK 12:44 parallel LUK 21:4; ; 2CO 8:2; ; JAS 2:5; ; REV 2:9

## Voluntary poverty


### Jesus Christ's example of voluntary poverty
**2CO 8:9**

### Voluntary poverty commanded of some
**MAT 19:21-19:22** *Jesus Christ did not say this to everyone.*

### Voluntary poverty experienced by others
 *See also* HEB 10:34

### Love essential in voluntary poverty
 1CO 13:3; ; 2CO 6:10
