# 5447: poverty, causes of

To want for the necessities of life is ultimately the result of human sin.



## Poverty arises from human sin
**[GEN 3:17-3:19](bibleref:01003017-01003019)** *Compare this with the description of Eden in Ge 2:9-14.* 

## Causes of poverty


### Divine retribution brings poverty
**[DEU 28:47-28:48](bibleref:05028047-05028048)** *See also* [PRO 22:16](bibleref:20022016); [ISA 3:1](bibleref:23003001); [AMO 4:6](bibleref:30004006), [AMO 5:11](bibleref:30005011)

### Idleness brings poverty
**[PRO 6:10-6:11](bibleref:20006010-20006011)** *See also* [PRO 10:4](bibleref:20010004), [PRO 14:23](bibleref:20014023), [PRO 20:13](bibleref:20020013), [PRO 24:33-24:34](bibleref:20024033-20024034)

### Dissolute living brings poverty
**[PRO 21:17](bibleref:20021017)** *See also* [PRO 6:26](bibleref:20006026), [PRO 28:19](bibleref:20028019); [LUK 15:14](bibleref:42015014)

### Lack of discipline brings poverty
 [PRO 13:18](bibleref:20013018), [PRO 21:5](bibleref:20021005)

### Rash promises bring poverty
 [PRO 22:26-22:27](bibleref:20022026-20022027)

### Debt brings poverty
 [MAT 18:23-18:25](bibleref:40018023-40018025)

### Neglect of God's law of giving brings poverty
 [PRO 11:24](bibleref:20011024), [PRO 28:22](bibleref:20028022); [ACT 20:35](bibleref:44020035)

### Neglect of God's work brings poverty
 [HAG 1:9](bibleref:37001009)

### Oppression brings poverty
 [JDG 6:6](bibleref:07006006); [JOB 20:19](bibleref:18020019); [PRO 13:23](bibleref:20013023); [ISA 1:7](bibleref:23001007); [JAS 5:4](bibleref:59005004)

### Famine brings poverty
 [GEN 45:11](bibleref:01045011), [GEN 47:20-47:21](bibleref:01047020-01047021)

### Misfortune brings poverty
 [JOB 1:13-1:21](bibleref:18001013-18001021) *These trials were inflicted by Satan but allowed by God as a test of Job's faith.* 

## Poverty affects good as well as evil people


### The righteous often escape poverty
 [DEU 8:7-8:9](bibleref:05008007-05008009); [PSA 34:9-34:10](bibleref:19034009-19034010), [PSA 37:25](bibleref:19037025)

### Many of the godly have been poor
 [2KI 4:1](bibleref:12004001); [LUK 16:20](bibleref:42016020); [ROM 15:26](bibleref:45015026)

### Jesus Christ was poor
 [MAT 8:20](bibleref:40008020)

### Poverty results in ruin
**[PRO 10:15](bibleref:20010015)**

 *See also* [PRO 13:18](bibleref:20013018); [LUK 16:3](bibleref:42016003)

 [PRO 6:30](bibleref:20006030), [PRO 30:8-30:9](bibleref:20030008-20030009)

 [PSA 119:71](bibleref:19119071); [MRK 12:44](bibleref:41012044) parallel LUK 21:4; [2CO 8:2](bibleref:47008002); [JAS 2:5](bibleref:59002005); [REV 2:9](bibleref:66002009)
