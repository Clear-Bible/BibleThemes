# 4801: black

A dark colour often associated with impending judgment or with evil.

**MAT 5:36** *See also* EXO 10:15 *the plague of locusts*; LEV 11:13-11:14 parallel DEU 14:12-14:13 *unclean food*; LEV 13:31; DEU 4:11; 1KI 18:45; JOB 28:3, JOB 30:28, JOB 30:30; SNG 5:11; LAM 4:7-4:8ZEC 6:2, ZEC 6:6; REV 6:5
