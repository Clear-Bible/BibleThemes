# 2412: cross, Gospel accounts of

Each of the four Gospels provides a detailed account of Jesus Christ's death on the cross.



## The way to the cross


### Simon is compelled to carry the cross
**[MRK 15:21](bibleref:41015021)** parallels MAT 27:32, LUK 23:26

### Women follow the cross and Jesus Christ is offered relief from pain
**[LUK 23:27-23:31](bibleref:42023027-42023031)** *See also* [MAT 27:34](bibleref:40027034) parallel MRK 15:23

## The crucifixion of Jesus Christ


### Jesus Christ crucified between two criminals
**[MRK 15:22-15:27](bibleref:41015022-41015027)** parallels MAT 27:33-27:38, LUK 23:32-23:43 *“Golgotha” is a transliteration of the Aramaic word for “a skull”. Its actual location is uncertain.* 

### The humiliation of Jesus Christ on the cross
**[MRK 15:29-15:32](bibleref:41015029-41015032)** parallels MAT 27:39-27:44, LUK 23:35-23:39

### The words of Jesus Christ on the cross
**[JHN 19:30](bibleref:43019030)** *See also* [MAT 27:46](bibleref:40027046) parallel MRK 15:34; [MAT 27:50](bibleref:40027050); [MRK 15:37](bibleref:41015037); [LUK 23:28-23:31](bibleref:42023028-42023031), [LUK 23:34](bibleref:42023034), [LUK 23:43](bibleref:42023043), [LUK 23:46](bibleref:42023046); [JHN 19:26-19:28](bibleref:43019026-43019028)

### The death of Jesus Christ
**[MRK 15:33-15:37](bibleref:41015033-41015037)** parallels MAT 27:45-27:50, LUK 23:44-23:46 *See also* [JHN 19:28-19:35](bibleref:43019028-43019035) *According to John, Jesus Christ was on the cross while the Passover lambs were being sacrificed.* 

## Supernatural events at the cross
**[MRK 15:38](bibleref:41015038)** parallels MAT 27:51-27:53, LUK 23:45

## Witnesses of the crucifixion
**[MRK 15:39-15:41](bibleref:41015039-41015041)** parallels MAT 27:54-27:56, LUK 23:47-23:49
