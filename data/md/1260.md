# 1260: finger of God

A figure of speech which gives expression to God's creative power and authority over his creation.

## God creates with his finger
**[PSA 8:3](bibleref:19008003)** *See also* [ISA 48:13](bibleref:23048013), [ISA 64:8](bibleref:23064008)

## God writes with his finger


### He writes the law
**[EXO 31:18](bibleref:02031018)** *See also* [EXO 24:12](bibleref:02024012), [EXO 32:16](bibleref:02032016), [EXO 34:1](bibleref:02034001)

### He writes judgment
**[DAN 5:5](bibleref:27005005)** *See also* [DAN 5:24-5:28](bibleref:27005024-27005028)

## God works miracles with his finger


### He sends plagues
**[EXO 8:19](bibleref:02008019)**
