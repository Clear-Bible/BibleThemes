# 5960: success

The achieving of objectives or personal fulfilment. For believers, success depends on faithfulness to God. Success can lead to dependence on one's own strength, instead of upon God.



## Causes of success


### The favour of God
 GEN 39:23; ; JOS 1:7; ; 2CH 20:20, 2CH 26:5

### Human skill
 ECC 10:10

### Many advisers
 PRO 15:22

## Nothing can succeed that is against God
**PRO 21:30** *See also* ACT 5:34-5:39

## Examples of prayers for success
 GEN 24:12-24:15 *success in personal relationships*; 1CH 4:10; ; 2CH 14:11; 
NEH 1:11 *success in personal achievement*; ; ACT 4:18-4:31 *success in ministry*

## The dangers of success


### Pride
**2CH 25:19** *See also* 1SA 2:3; PRO 11:2, PRO 16:5; MIC 2:3

### Boasting
**EPH 2:8** *See also* JAS 4:13-4:17; PSA 10:3-10:4; ISA 10:15-10:16

### Forgetting God
**DEU 32:18** *See also* DEU 8:11-8:17; JDG 3:7, JDG 8:33-8:34

### Lack of thankfulness
 ROM 1:21; ; 2TI 3:2
