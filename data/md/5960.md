# 5960: success

The achieving of objectives or personal fulfilment. For believers, success depends on faithfulness to God. Success can lead to dependence on one's own strength, instead of upon God.

## Causes of success


### The favour of God
 [GEN 39:23](bibleref:01039023); [JOS 1:7](bibleref:06001007); [2CH 20:20](bibleref:14020020), [2CH 26:5](bibleref:14026005)

### Human skill
 [ECC 10:10](bibleref:21010010)

### Many advisers
 [PRO 15:22](bibleref:20015022)

## Nothing can succeed that is against God
**[PRO 21:30](bibleref:20021030)** *See also* [ACT 5:34-5:39](bibleref:44005034-44005039)

## Examples of prayers for success
 [GEN 24:12-24:15](bibleref:01024012-01024015) *success in personal relationships* [1CH 4:10](bibleref:13004010); [2CH 14:11](bibleref:14014011)
[NEH 1:11](bibleref:16001011) *success in personal achievement* ; [ACT 4:18-4:31](bibleref:44004018-44004031) *success in ministry* 

## The dangers of success


### Pride
**[2CH 25:19](bibleref:14025019)** *See also* [1SA 2:3](bibleref:09002003); [PRO 11:2](bibleref:20011002), [PRO 16:5](bibleref:20016005); [MIC 2:3](bibleref:33002003)

### Boasting
**[EPH 2:8](bibleref:49002008)** *See also* [JAS 4:13-4:17](bibleref:59004013-59004017); [PSA 10:3-10:4](bibleref:19010003-19010004); [ISA 10:15-10:16](bibleref:23010015-23010016)

### Forgetting God
**[DEU 32:18](bibleref:05032018)** *See also* [DEU 8:11-8:17](bibleref:05008011-05008017); [JDG 3:7](bibleref:07003007), [JDG 8:33-8:34](bibleref:07008033-07008034)

### Lack of thankfulness
 [ROM 1:21](bibleref:45001021); [2TI 3:2](bibleref:55003002)
