# 9155: millennium

The period of 1,000 years referred to in Revelation chapter 20, during which Satan is bound and believers reign with Jesus Christ. This millennium is understood in different ways: as a period of unparalleled peace and prosperity for the church and the gospel at the end of this age, after which Christ will return (postmillennialism); as the time following Christ's return when he will reign on earth (premillennialism); as a symbolic reference to the period between Christ's first and second comings (amillennialism).

## Characteristics of the millennium


### Satan is bound
**[REV 20:1-20:3](bibleref:66020001-66020003)** *“the Abyss” is the subterranean place of confinement for Satan and evil spirits.*  *See also* [LUK 8:31](bibleref:42008031) parallels MAT 8:29, MRK 5:7

### Martyred believers are raised to reign with Jesus Christ
**[REV 20:4-20:6](bibleref:66020004-66020006)** *Those who have suffered for the gospel receive their promised reward.*  *See also* [ROM 8:17](bibleref:45008017); [2TI 2:11-2:12](bibleref:55002011-55002012); [REV 2:10](bibleref:66002010)

### Satan's release and final overthrow
**[REV 20:7-20:10](bibleref:66020007-66020010)** *See also* [EZK 38:1-38:4](bibleref:26038001-26038004), [EZK 38:15-38:16](bibleref:26038015-26038016), [EZK 38:18-38:22](bibleref:26038018-26038022), [EZK 39:2-39:5](bibleref:26039002-26039005); [ZEC 14:2](bibleref:38014002); [ROM 16:20](bibleref:45016020)

## The millennium will be a golden age on earth
**[ISA 2:2-2:4](bibleref:23002002-23002004)** parallel MIC 4:1-4:3 *The OT looks forward to a period of universal peace and prosperity on earth. Both pre- and postmillennialists see this as a reference to Jesus Christ's future earthly reign.*  *See also* [ISA 11:6-11:9](bibleref:23011006-23011009), [ISA 51:4-51:5](bibleref:23051004-23051005), [ISA 65:25](bibleref:23065025)

## The premillennial interpretation


### The premillennial order of events
 [REV 19:11-19:13](bibleref:66019011-66019013) *Jesus Christ's return. Premillennialists understand the events of Revelation chapter 19 to precede those of Revelation chapter 20* ; [REV 20:2-20:3](bibleref:66020002-66020003) *Satan bound for 1,000 years* ; [REV 20:4-20:6](bibleref:66020004-66020006) *a “first resurrection” of believers to share Jesus Christ's reign over the earth* ; [REV 20:7-20:10](bibleref:66020007-66020010) *Satan's release and final overthrow* ; [REV 20:11-20:15](bibleref:66020011-66020015) *the general resurrection of all people to face God's judgment* 

### A first resurrection of believers
**[1CO 15:22-15:25](bibleref:46015022-46015025)** *This passage allows the idea of a gap between the resurrection of believers and the eternal age.*  *See also* [1TH 4:16-4:17](bibleref:52004016-52004017)

### God's future earthly reign
**[ZEC 14:9](bibleref:38014009)** *See also* [DAN 2:44](bibleref:27002044); [ZEP 3:11](bibleref:36003011); [ZEC 14:16-14:21](bibleref:38014016-38014021)

### Believers will reign with Jesus Christ
**[MAT 19:28](bibleref:40019028)** *Premillennialists take this to refer to an earthly reign.*  *See also* [LUK 22:29-22:30](bibleref:42022029-42022030); [1CO 6:2](bibleref:46006002); [REV 5:9-5:10](bibleref:66005009-66005010)

### Difficulties with premillennialism
Scriptures that depict a single resurrection of both the righteous and the wicked:
 [DAN 12:2](bibleref:27012002); [JHN 5:28-5:29](bibleref:43005028-43005029)[MAT 13:40-13:43](bibleref:40013040-40013043), [MAT 13:49-13:50](bibleref:40013049-40013050), [MAT 25:31-25:32](bibleref:40025031-40025032)

## The postmillennial interpretation


### The rapid spread of the gospel
**[MAT 24:14](bibleref:40024014)** parallel MRK 13:10 *See also* [MAT 13:31-13:33](bibleref:40013031-40013033) parallels MRK 4:31-4:32, LUK 13:18-13:21

### A future turning to God
 [PSA 22:27-22:28](bibleref:19022027-19022028); [ISA 19:19-19:24](bibleref:23019019-23019024); [ZEC 2:11](bibleref:38002011)

### Difficulties with postmillennialism
The fortunes of the gospel and the church appear to be at a low ebb before Jesus Christ returns:
 [MAT 24:9-24:12](bibleref:40024009-40024012) parallel LUK 21:12-21:17; [2TI 3:1-3:5](bibleref:55003001-55003005), [2TI 3:12-3:13](bibleref:55003012-55003013)[MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018); [ACT 2:33-2:36](bibleref:44002033-44002036), [ACT 7:55-7:56](bibleref:44007055-44007056)

## The amillennial interpretation


### Satan was defeated at Jesus Christ's first coming
**[JHN 12:31](bibleref:43012031)** *See also* [MAT 12:29](bibleref:40012029) parallels MRK 3:27, LUK 11:21-11:22; [LUK 10:18-10:20](bibleref:42010018-42010020); [COL 2:14-2:15](bibleref:51002014-51002015)

### Interpreting the ``first resurrection''
As a reference to the soul entering heaven after death:
 [LUK 23:43](bibleref:42023043); [2TI 4:7-4:8](bibleref:55004007-55004008)[ROM 6:5](bibleref:45006005); [EPH 2:6](bibleref:49002006); [COL 3:1](bibleref:51003001)

### Dangers of the amillennialist view
The future hope of believers is not only a spiritual reality:
 [ROM 8:19-8:21](bibleref:45008019-45008021); [REV 21:1](bibleref:66021001)[1PE 5:8](bibleref:60005008); [1JN 5:19](bibleref:62005019); [REV 12:17](bibleref:66012017)
