# 5258: cloth

A woven material from which garments or coverings were made. Different kinds of material are associated with different ranks and social positions.



## Cloth for daily wear
**PRO 31:22** *See also* NUM 15:38; JDG 5:30, JDG 14:12; 2SA 1:24; 1CH 15:27; PSA 45:14; EZK 16:10, EZK 16:13; MAT 9:16; JHN 19:23; ACT 9:39

**1SA 21:9** *See also* 2SA 17:19; PRO 7:16

**EXO 26:1** *See also* EXO 26:4, EXO 36:8; 2CH 3:14
