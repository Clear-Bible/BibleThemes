# 6661: freedom, and the law

Jesus Christ sets his people free from the condemnation and oppression of the law. Through the Holy Spirit, believers are able to fulfil the law obediently according to God's intentions.



## Jesus Christ sets believers free from the law


### Jesus Christ's life of perfect obedience to the law was the fulfilment of it
**MAT 5:17** *See also* GAL 4:4-4:5; 1PE 2:22

### Jesus Christ's death fulfils the demands of the law
**2CO 5:21** *See also* HEB 7:27, HEB 9:11, HEB 9:26-9:28, HEB 10:11-10:14; 1PE 2:24, 1PE 3:18

### On account of Jesus Christ's death, believers can be righteous independently of the law
**ROM 3:21-3:26** *See also* JHN 1:17; ROM 10:4; PHP 3:9

## Believers are set free from the law's condemnation
**GAL 3:21-3:25** *See also* ROM 6:1-6:14, ROM 7:1-7:6; GAL 3:13, GAL 4:5, GAL 4:21-4:31 *The lives of Abraham, Sarah, Hagar, Ishmael and Isaac are interpreted figuratively, to illustrate the believer's freedom from the law's condemnation*; GAL 5:1; EPH 2:14-2:15; COL 2:13-2:14

## The freedom of believers rests on the power of the Holy Spirit
**ROM 7:1-7:6**; **2CO 3:17-3:18** *See also* ROM 8:1-8:17; GAL 5:16-5:18, GAL 5:22-5:26

## The believer's freedom to obey the law voluntarily
**PSA 119:32** *See also* PSA 37:31, PSA 119:45, PSA 119:97; JER 31:33; 2CO 3:3; JAS 1:25, JAS 2:12
