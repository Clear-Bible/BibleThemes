# 6112: banishment

A form of punishment in which the guilty party is expelled from a region and forbidden to return. An important example is the banishment of Adam and Eve from Eden as a result of their disobedience to God.

## Banishment is a punishment for disobedience
**[EZR 7:26](bibleref:15007026)** *See also* [EXO 12:15](bibleref:02012015), [EXO 31:14](bibleref:02031014); [LEV 7:20-7:21](bibleref:03007020-03007021); [NUM 15:30](bibleref:04015030)

## Banishment by God


### Adam and Eve were banished from Eden because of their disobedience
**[GEN 3:22-3:24](bibleref:01003022-01003024)**

### God banished kings as a punishment
Nebuchadnezzar was banished from every human being because of his sin:
 [DAN 4:25](bibleref:27004025), [DAN 4:32-4:33](bibleref:27004032-27004033)
[EZK 28:16-28:17](bibleref:26028016-26028017) *The king of Tyre was banished from God's presence because of his sin.* 

### God punished the people of Judah by banishing them from their land
**[JER 23:39](bibleref:24023039)** *God also promised their return (Jer 32:37).*  *See also* [JER 8:3](bibleref:24008003), [JER 24:9](bibleref:24024009), [JER 25:10](bibleref:24025010), [JER 27:10](bibleref:24027010)

### At the final judgment, God will banish all evildoers
**[MAT 7:23](bibleref:40007023)** *See also* [MAT 8:12](bibleref:40008012), [MAT 22:13](bibleref:40022013), [MAT 25:46](bibleref:40025046)

## Kings used banishment as a punishment
Saul expelled all mediums and spiritists from the land:
 [1SA 28:3](bibleref:09028003), [1SA 28:9](bibleref:09028009)
[1KI 15:12](bibleref:11015012) *Asa banished all the shrine-prostitutes from the land.* 

## The psalmists recognised banishment as the severest of punishments
 [PSA 5:10](bibleref:19005010), [PSA 31:22](bibleref:19031022), [PSA 37:22](bibleref:19037022), [PSA 37:28](bibleref:19037028), [PSA 37:34](bibleref:19037034), [PSA 37:38](bibleref:19037038), [PSA 51:11](bibleref:19051011), [PSA 71:9](bibleref:19071009), [PSA 125:5](bibleref:19125005); [JON 2:4](bibleref:32002004) *This portion of the book of Jonah is written in the same manner as the Psalms, i.e., as Hebrew poetry.* 

## The church is to use banishment as a means of discipline
**[1CO 5:12-5:13](bibleref:46005012-46005013)** *See also* [DEU 17:7](bibleref:05017007), [DEU 19:19](bibleref:05019019), [DEU 21:21](bibleref:05021021), [DEU 22:21](bibleref:05022021), [DEU 22:24](bibleref:05022024), [DEU 24:7](bibleref:05024007)
