# 5555: stealing

Theft is always condemned in Scripture, though the possibility of mitigating circumstances is recognised. Converted thieves must show their repentance, if possible, in a practical way.



## Stealing is condemned as a sin


### It is prohibited
**EXO 20:15** parallel DEU 5:19 *See also* LEV 19:11; PSA 62:10; MRK 10:19 parallel LUK 18:20; ROM 2:21, ROM 13:9

### Punishment for stealing
**1CO 6:9-6:10** *See also* EXO 21:16, EXO 22:1-22:4; LEV 6:2-6:5

### The disgrace of thieves
**JER 2:26**

### The company of thieves is to be avoided
 *See also* PRO 29:24; ISA 1:23

### Stealing is denied
 GEN 44:8; ; 1SA 12:3-12:5

## Mitigating circumstances for stealing


### Poverty
**PRO 6:30-6:31** *See also* PRO 30:7-30:9

## Stealing is commonplace in this world


### It is part of life in a sinful world
**HOS 4:2**; **MAT 6:19** *See also* MAT 15:19

### Roadside robbery
**LUK 10:30** *See also* PRO 1:10-1:15; 2CO 11:26

### Sheep stealing
 JOB 24:2; ; 2SA 12:4

### Dishonesty in trade
 JER 22:17; ; EZK 28:18; ; HOS 12:7; ; AMO 8:5

### Dishonest tax collectors
 LUK 3:13, LUK 19:8

### Theft in the temple
 MAT 21:12-21:13 parallels MRK 11:15-11:17, LUK 19:45-19:46; ; JER 7:11

### Theft in the family
 GEN 31:19; ; JDG 17:1-17:2

### Wife stealing
 2SA 11:3

### Kidnapping
 2KI 11:2 parallel 2CH 22:11

### Temple robbers
 ACT 19:37; ; ROM 2:22

### Body stealing

### Of forbidden pleasures
 PRO 9:17

### Of a captivated heart

**1TH 5:2** *See also* JER 49:9-49:10 parallel OBA 1:5-1:6; MAT 24:42-24:44; 2PE 3:10

### Stealing from God
**MAL 3:8-3:9**

### Lying prophets
 *See also* JER 23:30

 LUK 23:39-23:43 *the thief on the cross*; ; 1CO 6:9-6:11 *some of the Corinthians*
