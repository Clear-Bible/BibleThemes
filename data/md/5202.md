# 5202: accusation, false

Scripture expressly forbids making false accusations, but God's people have often been subjected to them and many false accusations were made against Jesus Christ.



## False accusation forbidden
**EXO 23:7** *See also* EXO 23:1; LEV 19:16; LUK 3:14

## Satan associated with false accusation
**ZEC 3:1** *Although taken as a proper name in English, “Satan” is in fact simply the Hebrew word meaning “accuser”.* *See also* JOB 1:6-1:12; REV 12:10

## Examples of false accusation


### In the OT
**PSA 41:5-41:6** *See also* GEN 39:7-39:18; 2SA 10:1-10:3; 1KI 21:7-21:14; EZR 4:6-4:16; NEH 6:5-6:7; PSA 35:19-35:20

### In the experience of Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:59-26:61** parallel MRK 14:55-14:59 *See also* MAT 9:34, MAT 12:24 parallels MRK 3:22, LUK 11:15; LUK 23:2

### In the experience of the first Christians
**ACT 16:19-16:21** *See also* MAT 5:11-5:12; ACT 6:11-6:14
