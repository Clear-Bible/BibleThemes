# 5202: accusation, false

Scripture expressly forbids making false accusations, but God's people have often been subjected to them and many false accusations were made against Jesus Christ.

## False accusation forbidden
**[EXO 23:7](bibleref:02023007)** *See also* [EXO 23:1](bibleref:02023001); [LEV 19:16](bibleref:03019016); [LUK 3:14](bibleref:42003014)

## Satan associated with false accusation
**[ZEC 3:1](bibleref:38003001)** *Although taken as a proper name in English, “Satan” is in fact simply the Hebrew word meaning “accuser”.*  *See also* [JOB 1:6-1:12](bibleref:18001006-18001012); [REV 12:10](bibleref:66012010)
