# 5147: deafness

The condition of being wholly, or partly, without hearing. It can be expressed in physical or spiritual terms and is often used to depict spiritual rebellion.

## God's control of deafness
**[EXO 4:11](bibleref:02004011)** *See also* [PSA 94:9](bibleref:19094009); [PRO 20:12](bibleref:20020012); [LUK 1:20](bibleref:42001020), [LUK 1:62](bibleref:42001062)

## Physical aspects of deafness


### Compassion for the deaf
**[LEV 19:14](bibleref:03019014)**

### Deafness sometimes caused by Satan
**[MRK 9:25](bibleref:41009025)**

### Healed by Jesus Christ
**[MAT 11:5](bibleref:40011005)** parallel LUK 7:22 *See also* [MRK 7:32-7:37](bibleref:41007032-41007037); [LUK 22:49-22:51](bibleref:42022049-42022051)

## Spiritual aspects of deafness


### God deaf to a rebellious people
**[DEU 1:45](bibleref:05001045)** *See also* [PSA 28:1](bibleref:19028001), [PSA 38:13](bibleref:19038013), [PSA 39:12](bibleref:19039012)

### Rebellious humanity deaf to God
**[JER 6:10](bibleref:24006010)** *See also* [PRO 28:9](bibleref:20028009); [ISA 6:10](bibleref:23006010), [ISA 42:19-42:20](bibleref:23042019-23042020); [EZK 33:32](bibleref:26033032), [EZK 37:4](bibleref:26037004); [MIC 7:16](bibleref:33007016); [ZEC 7:11](bibleref:38007011); [MAT 13:15](bibleref:40013015); [MRK 4:9-4:12](bibleref:41004009-41004012); [LUK 8:10](bibleref:42008010); [JHN 12:40](bibleref:43012040); [ACT 28:26-28:27](bibleref:44028026-44028027); [2TI 4:4](bibleref:55004004); [REV 2:7](bibleref:66002007) *Sin brings spiritual deafness.*
