# 5147: deafness

The condition of being wholly, or partly, without hearing. It can be expressed in physical or spiritual terms and is often used to depict spiritual rebellion.



## God's control of deafness
**EXO 4:11** *See also* PSA 94:9; PRO 20:12; LUK 1:20, LUK 1:62

## Physical aspects of deafness


### Compassion for the deaf
**LEV 19:14**

### Deafness sometimes caused by Satan
**MRK 9:25**

### Healed by Jesus Christ
**MAT 11:5** parallel LUK 7:22 *See also* MRK 7:32-7:37; LUK 22:49-22:51

## Spiritual aspects of deafness


### God deaf to a rebellious people
**DEU 1:45** *See also* PSA 28:1, PSA 38:13, PSA 39:12

### Rebellious humanity deaf to God
**JER 6:10** *See also* PRO 28:9; ISA 6:10, ISA 42:19-42:20; EZK 33:32, EZK 37:4; MIC 7:16; ZEC 7:11; MAT 13:15; MRK 4:9-4:12; LUK 8:10; JHN 12:40; ACT 28:26-28:27; 2TI 4:4; REV 2:7 *Sin brings spiritual deafness.*

## The deafness of idols
**DEU 4:28** *See also* PSA 115:6, PSA 135:17; DAN 5:23; REV 9:20

## Release from deafness


### Through repentance
**ISA 29:18** *See also* ISA 35:5, ISA 42:18

### Through the coming of Jesus Christ
**LUK 7:22** parallel MAT 11:5 *See also* MRK 7:32-7:37
