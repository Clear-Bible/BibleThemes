# 5449: poverty, remedies for

Although poverty can never be totally eliminated from this sinful world, it can be alleviated. Scripture provides guidance concerning how this may be done.



## Poverty will always exist in this world
**MAT 26:11** parallel JHN 12:8 *See also* DEU 15:11

## God's concern over poverty


### God cares for the poor
**PSA 35:10, PSA 68:10** *See also* DEU 15:4-15:5; JOB 5:16; PSA 14:6, PSA 113:7, PSA 140:12; ISA 25:4

### The law made concessions for poverty
 LEV 5:7, LEV 5:11, LEV 12:8, LEV 14:21-14:22, LEV 27:8

### God judges those who oppress the poor
 EZK 18:12-18:13; ; AMO 4:1-4:2

## The poor should be encouraged to help themselves


### Mosaic law enabled the poor to help themselves
 EXO 23:11; ; LEV 19:10, LEV 23:22, LEV 25:25, LEV 25:28, LEV 25:39, LEV 25:41 *The jubilee helped towards release from the poverty trap*; ; DEU 23:25

### Paul exhorts the poor to work
 1TH 4:11; ; 2TH 3:10

### Paul sets the poor an example
 ACT 20:34; ; 1TH 2:9; ; 2TH 3:8

## The relatives of the poor should help
**1TI 5:4** *See also* GEN 45:9-45:11; LEV 25:25; 1TI 5:8, 1TI 5:16

## Those better off should help the poor


### The poor can be helped by means of gifts
**ACT 10:4** *See also* DEU 15:7-15:8; PSA 112:9; PRO 31:20; MAT 19:21 parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22; LUK 19:8; ACT 9:36; EPH 4:28; 1TI 6:17-6:18

### The poor can be helped in practical ways
 MAT 25:34-25:40; ; LUK 11:41, LUK 14:13; ; ACT 9:36, ACT 9:39; ; 1TI 5:9-5:10; ; JAS 2:15-2:16; ; 1JN 3:17

### The poor can be helped by others upholding justice
**PSA 82:3** *See also* DEU 24:12-24:15; PRO 22:22, PRO 29:7, PRO 29:14, PRO 31:9; ISA 10:1-10:2; JER 22:16

## The church should help the poor
**GAL 2:10** *See also* ACT 24:17; ROM 15:26; 1TI 5:16

## God's greatest gift to the poor is the good news of Jesus Christ
**LUK 4:18** *See also* ISA 61:1; MAT 11:5 parallel LUK 7:22; LUK 14:21 *Scripture regards spiritual and social welfare as closely connected.*
