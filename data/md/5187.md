# 5187: taste

The act of tasting is used figuratively of experiencing the goodness of God and his word and of a variety of experiences: misery, sin, wisdom, love, death and blessing.

## The taste of food or drink
**[EXO 16:31](bibleref:02016031)** *See also* [GEN 25:28](bibleref:01025028), [GEN 27:4](bibleref:01027004), [GEN 27:7](bibleref:01027007), [GEN 27:9](bibleref:01027009), [GEN 27:14](bibleref:01027014), [GEN 27:17](bibleref:01027017), [GEN 27:31](bibleref:01027031); [NUM 11:7-11:8](bibleref:04011007-04011008); [2SA 19:34-19:35](bibleref:10019034-10019035); [JHN 2:9](bibleref:43002009)

## Legalistic prohibitions against tasting certain foods are condemned by Paul
**[COL 2:20-2:22](bibleref:51002020-51002022)**
