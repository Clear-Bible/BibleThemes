# 2357: Jesus Christ, parables of

A central feature of Jesus Christ's teaching was the use of extended similies and short stories about the kingdom of God, based on human experience. Christ used them frequently to call for a response to his message.



## Parables are a central feature of Jesus Christ's teaching
**[MAT 13:34-13:35](bibleref:40013034-40013035)** *See also* [PSA 78:2](bibleref:19078002); [MRK 4:33-4:34](bibleref:41004033-41004034)

## Different characteristics of Jesus Christ's parables


### They vary in complexity and length
 [MAT 7:6](bibleref:40007006), [MAT 13:18-13:23](bibleref:40013018-40013023) parallels MRK 4:13-4:20, LUK 8:11-8:15; [MAT 13:37-13:45](bibleref:40013037-40013045), [MAT 13:52](bibleref:40013052)

### They vary in their use of metaphors, word pictures, objects and actions
 [MAT 5:13-5:16](bibleref:40005013-40005016), [MAT 13:44](bibleref:40013044), [MAT 18:2-18:3](bibleref:40018002-40018003), [MAT 21:18-21:22](bibleref:40021018-40021022) parallels MRK 11:12-11:14, MRK 11:20-11:24; [LUK 15:8-15:10](bibleref:42015008-42015010)

### They have different levels of meaning
Often the full impact of a parable may be lost if the Jewish context is not understood:
 [LUK 10:30-10:37](bibleref:42010030-42010037), [LUK 15:11-15:32](bibleref:42015011-42015032)

### They draw on human experience
 [MAT 13:33](bibleref:40013033) parallel LUK 13:20-13:21; [MAT 18:12-18:14](bibleref:40018012-40018014) parallel LUK 15:4-15:7; [LUK 7:41-7:43](bibleref:42007041-42007043)
