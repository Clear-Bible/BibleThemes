# 5882: impartiality

The ability to be unbiased towards any one race, class or person. This attitude is demonstrated by God and is to be emulated by the human race.

## God is impartial
**[DEU 10:17](bibleref:05010017)** *See also* [2CH 19:7](bibleref:14019007)

### God treats people impartially
**[MAT 5:45](bibleref:40005045)** *See also* [JOB 25:3](bibleref:18025003), [JOB 34:19](bibleref:18034019); [PRO 22:2](bibleref:20022002); [ROM 2:11](bibleref:45002011); [EPH 6:9](bibleref:49006009)

### God judges impartially
**[COL 3:25](bibleref:51003025)** *See also* [JOB 34:11-34:12](bibleref:18034011-18034012); [PSA 62:12](bibleref:19062012); [JER 17:10](bibleref:24017010); [ROM 2:9-2:11](bibleref:45002009-45002011); [2CO 5:10](bibleref:47005010); [1PE 1:17](bibleref:60001017); [REV 22:12](bibleref:66022012)

### God does not distinguish between people on the basis of external appearance
**[1SA 16:7](bibleref:09016007)** *See also* [PSA 147:10-147:11](bibleref:19147010-19147011); [JER 9:25](bibleref:24009025); [LUK 16:15](bibleref:42016015); [GAL 2:6](bibleref:48002006); [REV 2:23](bibleref:66002023)
