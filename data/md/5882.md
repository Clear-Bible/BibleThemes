# 5882: impartiality

The ability to be unbiased towards any one race, class or person. This attitude is demonstrated by God and is to be emulated by the human race.



## God is impartial
**DEU 10:17** *See also* 2CH 19:7

### God treats people impartially
**MAT 5:45** *See also* JOB 25:3, JOB 34:19; PRO 22:2; ROM 2:11; EPH 6:9

### God judges impartially
**COL 3:25** *See also* JOB 34:11-34:12; PSA 62:12; JER 17:10; ROM 2:9-2:11; 2CO 5:10; 1PE 1:17; REV 22:12

### God does not distinguish between people on the basis of external appearance
**1SA 16:7** *See also* PSA 147:10-147:11; JER 9:25; LUK 16:15; GAL 2:6; REV 2:23

### God does not discriminate against different races or classes
**ACT 10:34-10:35**; **GAL 3:28** *See also* DEU 10:18; JOB 34:19; ACT 10:28, ACT 15:7-15:9

## The impartiality of Jesus Christ


### Jesus Christ's teaching was impartial
**LUK 20:21** parallels MAT 22:16, MRK 12:14 *See also* MAT 20:1-20:16 *a parable of Jesus Christ about divine impartiality*

### Jesus Christ showed impartiality in his dealings with people
**JHN 4:9** *Jesus Christ contravened accepted boundaries of partiality in this encounter.* *See also* MAT 9:10-9:12 parallels MRK 2:15-2:17, LUK 5:29-5:31; MAT 15:22-15:28 parallel MRK 7:26-7:29; MAT 20:20-20:23 parallel MRK 10:35-10:40

## Impartiality commended
**JAS 2:1** *See also* PRO 28:21; 1CO 3:3-3:4, 1CO 4:6-4:7; 1TI 5:21; JAS 2:9

### Humanity should judge impartially
**PRO 24:23-24:25** *See also* EXO 18:16; DEU 1:17, DEU 16:19, DEU 24:17; PRO 17:15, PRO 18:5; MAL 2:9

## Showing impartiality to all people is commanded


### Impartiality to foreigners
**EXO 22:21** *See also* EXO 23:9; LEV 19:33-19:34; NUM 15:15-15:16; ROM 10:12

### Impartiality to children
 DEU 21:15-21:17

### Impartiality to the poor
**EXO 23:6** *See also* EXO 23:3; LEV 19:15; PRO 29:7; JAS 2:2-2:7

## Ways to avoid partiality


### By not accepting bribes
**EXO 23:8** *See also* JOB 36:18; PRO 15:27; ISA 1:23

### By not following the crowd
 EXO 23:2; ; MRK 15:15

## Christians should be partial towards spiritual principles
**MAT 6:24** parallel LUK 16:13 *See also* MAT 12:30 parallel LUK 11:23; MAT 7:13-7:14; MRK 9:39-9:40 parallel LUK 9:49-9:50

## Examples where impartiality is absent
 GEN 25:27-25:28 *Isaac and Rebekah to Jacob and Esau*; ; GEN 29:30-29:32 *Jacob to Leah and Rachel*; ; GEN 37:3-37:4 *Jacob to Joseph and his brothers*; ; 1SA 1:4-1:6 *Elkanah to Hannah and Peninnah*
