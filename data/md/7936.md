# 7936: love feast

The first Christians generally celebrated the Lord's Supper with the accompaniment of a meal, called the “agape” or “love feast”.

## OT antecedents of the love feast


### The Passover meal
 *See also* [EXO 12:3-12:11](bibleref:02012003-02012011), [EXO 12:15-12:16](bibleref:02012015-02012016); [MAT 26:17-26:18](bibleref:40026017-40026018) parallels MRK 14:12-14:15, LUK 22:7-22:12; [1CO 11:23-11:25](bibleref:46011023-46011025)

### Other festive occasions
 [DEU 12:7](bibleref:05012007); [NEH 8:10](bibleref:16008010)

## The NT practice of the love feast


### It was linked with the Lord's Supper
**[ACT 2:46](bibleref:44002046)** *See also* [ACT 20:7](bibleref:44020007); [1CO 11:17-11:34](bibleref:46011017-46011034)

### The name ``love feasts''
**[JUD 1:12](bibleref:65001012)** *See also* [2PE 2:13](bibleref:61002013); [JHN 13:2](bibleref:43013002), [JHN 13:34](bibleref:43013034) *It was at the Last Supper that Jesus Christ gave his new commandment to his disciples to love one another as he had loved them.* 

### The charitable distribution of food
 [ACT 6:1](bibleref:44006001)

## Abuses of the love feast


### Selfishness, indulgence and ostentation
**[1CO 11:20-11:22](bibleref:46011020-46011022)** *See also* [1CO 11:33-11:34](bibleref:46011033-46011034)

### Licentiousness
**[2PE 2:13](bibleref:61002013)**

### Ungodly participants, especially false teachers
**[JUD 1:4](bibleref:65001004), [JUD 1:12](bibleref:65001012)** *See also* [2PE 2:1](bibleref:61002001), [2PE 2:13](bibleref:61002013)

## The heavenly love feast


### Foretold by Jesus Christ
**[MAT 26:29](bibleref:40026029)** parallel MRK 14:25

### The marriage supper of the Lamb
**[REV 19:9](bibleref:66019009)** *See also* [ISA 25:6](bibleref:23025006); [MAT 22:2-22:14](bibleref:40022002-40022014) *The parable of the marriage supper indicates the prerequisites for attendance at the supper.*
