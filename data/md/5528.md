# 5528: shouting

A loud cry of joy, excitement or distress. Scripture often speaks of praise in terms of shouting, reflecting both the joy of the speaker and the greatness of God.



## Shouting to be heard
**1SA 17:20**; **LUK 23:21**; **ACT 22:23-22:24** *See also* JDG 7:20, JDG 15:14; 2KI 9:27; 2CH 13:15; JOB 39:25; PSA 60:8; ZEP 1:14; MAT 20:30-20:31; JHN 19:12; ACT 16:17, ACT 17:6, ACT 21:36

## Shouting in distress
**NUM 16:34**; **LUK 4:41** *See also* 1KI 18:26-18:28; LUK 8:28; ACT 14:14, ACT 19:28, ACT 21:28, ACT 25:24

## Shouting for joy
**EZR 3:11-3:13**; **JHN 12:13**; **REV 19:6** *See also* EXO 32:17; LEV 9:24; NUM 23:21; 1SA 4:6; 2SA 6:15; 1KI 1:39; 1CH 15:28; 2CH 15:14; JOB 3:7, JOB 8:21, JOB 33:26, JOB 38:7; PSA 20:5, PSA 33:3, PSA 42:4, PSA 65:13; PRO 11:10; ISA 16:9-16:10, ISA 26:19; MAT 21:15; LUK 17:15; REV 5:12, REV 19:1

## Joyful shouting commanded
**JOS 6:5**; **PSA 47:1**; **ZEP 3:14** *See also* PSA 66:1, PSA 95:1, PSA 100:1; ISA 40:9, ISA 44:23, ISA 58:1; JER 31:7; ZEC 9:9
