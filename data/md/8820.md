# 8820: self-confidence

A positive assessment of one's own abilities and resources. Scripture censures excessive self-confidence, in that such an attitude lacks humility and fails to recognise the role of God's grace in the Christian life.



## Excessive self-confidence


### In one's own abilities
**ISA 28:15**; **MAT 26:33-26:35** parallels MRK 14:29-14:31, LUK 23:33-23:34, JHN 13:37-13:38 *See also* LUK 22:33-22:34; JDG 16:20; 1KI 20:11; 2KI 19:23-19:24; JOB 8:13-8:14; PSA 49:5-49:6; JER 17:5; HOS 12:8

### Excessive self-confidence leads to pride
**ISA 9:9-9:10**; **OBA 1:3** *See also* GEN 11:4, GEN 37:5; EXO 15:9; 2CH 26:16; PRO 16:18; ISA 16:6; EZK 28:2; ACT 8:9; ROM 11:17-11:18; 1CO 5:2; 1JN 2:16

### Challenges to excessive self-confidence
**NEH 6:16**; **1CO 4:7** *See also* PSA 49:12-49:13, PSA 52:7; PRO 3:5-3:7, PRO 11:28, PRO 28:26; ISA 5:21, ISA 14:13-14:15; JER 49:7, JER 49:4-49:5; JAS 4:13-4:16

### Excessive self-confidence is a barrier to justification
 LUK 18:9-18:14

## Accurate self-confidence


### Of one's natural advantages
**PHP 3:4-3:6** *See also* ACT 22:3, ACT 22:28

### Of one's experience of God
**2CO 12:2** *See also* ACT 22:4-22:21; 2CO 12:1, 2CO 13:5-13:6; GAL 1:13-1:17, GAL 6:4

### Of one's calling to serve
**2CO 10:8** *See also* 2CO 10:13-10:17; GAL 2:7

### Of one's service
**ACT 20:19**; **ROM 12:3** *See also* ACT 20:18, ACT 20:20-20:21; 1CO 9:15-9:18; 2CO 1:12; PHP 2:16; 1TH 2:1-2:12

### Of one's own weakness
**2CO 11:29-11:30** *See also* 2CO 11:21-11:28, 2CO 12:9

### Accurate self-estimates lead to justification
 LUK 18:9-18:14

### Justified self-confidence may appear as pride
**2CO 11:17-11:18**

## Jesus Christ's accurate assessment of himself
**MAT 16:15-16:17** parallels MRK 8:29, LUK 9:20-9:21 *See also* MAT 26:62-26:64; JHN 13:13

## The proper source of all confidence
**JER 9:23-9:24** *See also* PSA 34:2, PSA 44:6-44:7; ISA 10:15; 1CO 1:27-1:31
