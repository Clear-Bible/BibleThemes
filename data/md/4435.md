# 4435: drinking





## Wine as a gift of God


### A sign of his blessing
**DEU 7:13** *See also* GEN 27:28; DEU 33:28; PRO 3:9-3:10; JOL 2:18-2:19, JOL 2:22-2:24, JOL 3:18; AMO 9:13-9:14

### Drinking wine has God's approval
**DEU 14:26** *See also* JHN 2:1-2:10; 1TI 5:23 *approval implicit in the advice given*

## Withdrawal of wine is evidence of divine judgment
**ISA 24:6-24:7** *See also* JER 48:32-48:33

### Of suffering God's judgment
 JOB 21:20; ; PSA 75:8; ; ISA 29:9; ; JER 25:15-25:17; ; EZK 23:31-23:34; ; HAB 2:16; ; REV 18:3

### Of love
 PRO 7:18

**MAT 26:42** *See also* MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; MAT 20:22-20:23 parallel MRK 10:38-10:39; JHN 18:11

**JHN 7:37**
