# 8325: purity, nature of

The OT recognises a close link between outer cleanliness and inner purity. In the NT, special emphasis is placed on inner purity resulting from spiritual renewal.



## Physical cleansing from impurity by washing or refining
 GEN 18:4; ; JOB 9:30; ; PSA 12:6

## Ritual impurity must be avoided or cleansed
**LEV 10:10-10:11** *See also* ISA 35:8, ISA 52:1, ISA 52:11; MRK 7:2, MRK 7:4

## Sexual sources of ritual impurity


### Childbirth
**LEV 12:2** *See also* LEV 12:5; LUK 2:22

### Sexual intercourse
 EXO 19:15; ; LEV 15:18; ; 1SA 21:4

### Seminal emission
 LEV 15:16-15:17; ; DEU 23:10-23:11; ; 1SA 20:26

### Menstruation
 LEV 15:19-15:20; ; 2SA 11:2-11:4; ; EZK 18:6

### Abnormal discharge
 LEV 15:1-15:2, LEV 15:25-15:27; ; MAT 9:20 parallels MRK 5:25, LUK 8:43

## Other sources of ritual impurity


### Dead bodies
**NUM 19:11-19:16**

### Unclean creatures
**GEN 7:2** *See also* LEV 11:24, LEV 11:46-11:47; DEU 14:3; HOS 9:3

### Skin diseases
**LEV 13:45** *See also* MAT 8:2-8:4 parallels MRK 1:40-1:44, LUK 5:12-5:14

### Mildew
 LEV 14:36

### Contact with a sin offering
**LEV 16:26**

## Cleansing from ritual impurity
**LEV 14:8** *See also* LEV 16:26; NUM 19:17-19:21, NUM 19:9, NUM 19:17-19:21, NUM 31:22-31:23 *Fire is also a purifying agent.*

## Contact with God demanded purity
**EXO 19:10** *See also* EXO 19:14-19:15, EXO 30:18-30:21; HEB 10:22

## Ritual purity is not required under the new covenant
**MAT 15:11** parallel MRK 7:15 *What concerns God is moral impurity, associated with actions, not ritual impurity, associated with food, etc.* *See also* ACT 10:12-10:15, ACT 21:26 *Paul took part in Jewish purification rites in accordance with his Jewish heritage*; ROM 14:14; GAL 2:11-2:12 *Peter temporarily relapsed into the habit of treating Gentiles as unclean*; GAL 2:14-2:15; 1TI 4:4
