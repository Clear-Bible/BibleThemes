# 5507: rope and cord

Items normally used in domestic and military situations, but also used in Scripture in a symbolic sense to refer to bondage or constraint.

## Various uses for rope and cord


### In clothes and furnishing
 [EXO 28:28](bibleref:02028028), [EXO 28:37](bibleref:02028037); [NUM 15:38](bibleref:04015038); [1KI 20:31](bibleref:11020031); [EST 1:6](bibleref:17001006); [EZK 27:24](bibleref:26027024)

### For tents
 [EXO 35:18](bibleref:02035018), [EXO 39:40](bibleref:02039040); [NUM 3:37](bibleref:04003037), [NUM 4:32](bibleref:04004032); [ISA 54:2](bibleref:23054002)

### In sailing
 [ACT 27:17](bibleref:44027017), [ACT 27:32](bibleref:44027032), [ACT 27:40](bibleref:44027040)

### In military use
 [2SA 17:13](bibleref:10017013)[PSA 7:12](bibleref:19007012), [PSA 11:2](bibleref:19011002); [JER 51:3](bibleref:24051003)

### For rescue and escape
 [JOS 2:15](bibleref:06002015); [1SA 19:12](bibleref:09019012); [JER 38:6](bibleref:24038006), [JER 38:13](bibleref:24038013); [ACT 9:25](bibleref:44009025); [2CO 11:33](bibleref:47011033)

### To restrain animals
 [JOB 39:5](bibleref:18039005), [JOB 41:1-41:2](bibleref:18041001-18041002); [PSA 32:9](bibleref:19032009); [MAT 21:2](bibleref:40021002) parallels MRK 11:2, LUK 19:30

### To restrain people
 [JDG 15:13-15:14](bibleref:07015013-07015014); [EZK 3:25](bibleref:26003025), [EZK 4:8](bibleref:26004008); [MAT 14:3](bibleref:40014003) parallel MRK 6:17
