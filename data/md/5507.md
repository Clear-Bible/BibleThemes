# 5507: rope and cord

Items normally used in domestic and military situations, but also used in Scripture in a symbolic sense to refer to bondage or constraint.



## Various uses for rope and cord


### In clothes and furnishing
 EXO 28:28, EXO 28:37; ; NUM 15:38; ; 1KI 20:31; ; EST 1:6; ; EZK 27:24

### For tents
 EXO 35:18, EXO 39:40; ; NUM 3:37, NUM 4:32; ; ISA 54:2

### In sailing
 ACT 27:17, ACT 27:32, ACT 27:40

### In military use
 2SA 17:13; PSA 7:12, PSA 11:2; ; JER 51:3

### For rescue and escape
 JOS 2:15; ; 1SA 19:12; ; JER 38:6, JER 38:13; ; ACT 9:25; ; 2CO 11:33

### To restrain animals
 JOB 39:5, JOB 41:1-41:2; ; PSA 32:9; ; MAT 21:2 parallels MRK 11:2, LUK 19:30

### To restrain people
 JDG 15:13-15:14; ; EZK 3:25, EZK 4:8; ; MAT 14:3 parallel MRK 6:17

## Rope and cord as symbols of bondage


### Of evil
**PRO 5:22** *See also* PSA 129:4, PSA 140:5; ISA 5:18, ISA 58:6; ROM 7:6

### Of suffering
**LUK 13:16** *See also* JOB 16:8, JOB 36:8; PSA 119:61; DAN 3:21; MAT 14:3 parallel MRK 6:17; MRK 15:1; JHN 18:12, JHN 18:24

### Of death
**PSA 18:4-18:5** parallel 2SA 22:6 *See also* PSA 116:3
