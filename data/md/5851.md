# 5851: excuse

The attempt to deny responsibility for an action or for a lack of action. There is no excuse for sin.

## Examples of excuses given for wrong actions
 [GEN 3:12-3:13](bibleref:01003012-01003013) *by Adam and Eve for eating the forbidden fruit* ; [EXO 32:22-32:24](bibleref:02032022-02032024) *by Aaron for making the golden calf* ; [1SA 13:11-13:12](bibleref:09013011-09013012) *by Saul for acting as priest* [1SA 15:20-15:21](bibleref:09015020-09015021), [1SA 15:24-15:25](bibleref:09015024-09015025)

## Examples of excuses given for inaction
By Moses:
 [EXO 3:11](bibleref:02003011), [EXO 4:10](bibleref:02004010)
[JDG 6:15](bibleref:07006015) *by Gideon* [PRO 22:13](bibleref:20022013), [PRO 26:13](bibleref:20026013)
[JER 1:6](bibleref:24001006) *by Jeremiah* [MAT 25:14-25:30](bibleref:40025014-40025030); [LUK 19:12-19:27](bibleref:42019012-42019027)
[MAT 25:41-25:45](bibleref:40025041-40025045) *by the ignorant*
