# 5851: excuse

The attempt to deny responsibility for an action or for a lack of action. There is no excuse for sin.



## Examples of excuses given for wrong actions
 GEN 3:12-3:13 *by Adam and Eve for eating the forbidden fruit*; ; EXO 32:22-32:24 *by Aaron for making the golden calf*; ; 1SA 13:11-13:12 *by Saul for acting as priest*; 1SA 15:20-15:21, 1SA 15:24-15:25

## Examples of excuses given for inaction
By Moses:
 EXO 3:11, EXO 4:10; 
JDG 6:15 *by Gideon*; PRO 22:13, PRO 26:13; 
JER 1:6 *by Jeremiah*; MAT 25:14-25:30; ; LUK 19:12-19:27; 
MAT 25:41-25:45 *by the ignorant*

## Excuses given by those unwilling to follow Jesus Christ
**LUK 14:16-14:24** *See also* MAT 22:2-22:3; LUK 9:59-9:62

## There is no excuse for sin
**ROM 1:20** *See also* JHN 9:41, JHN 15:22; ROM 2:1, ROM 3:19-3:23, ROM 5:12; 1JN 1:8, 1JN 1:10
