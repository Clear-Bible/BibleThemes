# 5829: defiance

Open refusal to obey any authority. Defiance is seen in the Bible as evil when authority is legitimate but good when authority is ungodly.



## Defiance of God is condemned
**JER 50:29** *See also* 1KI 13:21-13:22, 1KI 13:26; PSA 139:19-139:22; ISA 3:8, ISA 65:7; JER 48:26, JER 48:42

### To defy God's people is to defy God
 1SA 17:10, 1SA 17:23, 1SA 17:25-17:26, 1SA 17:36, 1SA 17:45

## Defiance of legitimate authority
**ROM 13:2** *See also* NUM 15:30; 1PE 2:13

## Defiance of ungodly authority
**DAN 3:28** *See also* DAN 6:13; ACT 4:19, ACT 5:29, ACT 17:7; REV 20:4
