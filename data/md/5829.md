# 5829: defiance

Open refusal to obey any authority. Defiance is seen in the Bible as evil when authority is legitimate but good when authority is ungodly.

## Defiance of God is condemned
**[JER 50:29](bibleref:24050029)** *See also* [1KI 13:21-13:22](bibleref:11013021-11013022), [1KI 13:26](bibleref:11013026); [PSA 139:19-139:22](bibleref:19139019-19139022); [ISA 3:8](bibleref:23003008), [ISA 65:7](bibleref:23065007); [JER 48:26](bibleref:24048026), [JER 48:42](bibleref:24048042)

### To defy God's people is to defy God
 [1SA 17:10](bibleref:09017010), [1SA 17:23](bibleref:09017023), [1SA 17:25-17:26](bibleref:09017025-09017026), [1SA 17:36](bibleref:09017036), [1SA 17:45](bibleref:09017045)
