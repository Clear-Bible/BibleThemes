# 5731: parents

Scripture stipulates that children should show respect and concern for their parents.



## Honouring parents
**EXO 20:12** parallel DEU 5:16 *See also* LEV 19:3; PRO 23:22; MAT 19:16-19:19 parallels MRK 10:17-10:19, LUK 18:18-18:20; EPH 6:1-6:3; COL 3:20; 1TI 5:4

## Dishonouring parents
**2TI 3:1-3:2** *See also* EXO 21:15-21:17; DEU 21:18-21:21; EZK 22:7; MAT 15:1-15:9 parallel MRK 7:1-7:13; ROM 1:28-1:32

## Love between parents and children can be suppressed
**LUK 21:16-21:17** parallels MAT 10:21, MRK 13:12 *See also* ZEC 13:2-13:3

## Love for God must surpass love for parents
**LUK 14:26** *See also* DEU 33:8-33:11; LUK 18:28-18:30 parallels MAT 19:27-19:29, MRK 10:28-10:30

## Divine love surpasses parental love
**PSA 27:10**

## The belief that children were punished for their parents' sins
**JHN 9:1-9:3** *See also* EXO 20:5-20:6 parallel DEU 5:9-5:10; JOB 21:19-21:21; EZK 18:1-18:4

## Parents caring for their children
**2CO 12:14** *See also* PRO 19:14, PRO 23:24-23:25; HEB 11:23

## Children caring for their parents
**1SA 22:3-22:4** *See also* JOS 2:8-2:20, JOS 6:22-6:23; PRO 17:6

## Mourning for parents
**LEV 21:1-21:2** *See also* LEV 21:10-21:11; NUM 6:2-6:7; DEU 21:10-21:13; EZK 44:25

### A man leaves his parents to marry his wife
**GEN 2:21-2:24** *See also* MAT 19:3-19:6 parallel MRK 10:2-10:9; EPH 5:25-5:33

 DEU 22:13-22:19
