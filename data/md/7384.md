# 7384: household gods

Known in the Hebrew as “teraphim”, miniature idols small enough for their owners to keep in their homes or to carry with them when travelling.

## Examples of the use of household gods
**[GEN 31:19](bibleref:01031019)** *See also* [GEN 31:34](bibleref:01031034); [JDG 17:1-17:6](bibleref:07017001-07017006), [JDG 18:18](bibleref:07018018), [JDG 18:30-18:31](bibleref:07018030-07018031); [1SA 19:13-19:16](bibleref:09019013-09019016); [EZK 21:21](bibleref:26021021) *Three methods of divination are described here, shaking the arrows (belomancy), consulting the household gods (necromancy) and the examination of the liver of a sacrificed victim (hepatoscopy).*
