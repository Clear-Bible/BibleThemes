# 7398: New Moon festival

The festival which marked the consecration to God of each new month in the year.

## New Moon festivals were required under the Mosaic law
**[NUM 28:11](bibleref:04028011)**

## New Moon festivals were times of celebration


### They were marked by blowing of trumpets
**[NUM 10:10](bibleref:04010010)** *See also* [PSA 81:3](bibleref:19081003)

### They were celebrated with offerings
 [NUM 28:11-28:15](bibleref:04028011-04028015); [1CH 23:30-23:31](bibleref:13023030-13023031); [2CH 2:4](bibleref:14002004), [2CH 8:12-8:13](bibleref:14008012-14008013), [2CH 31:3](bibleref:14031003) *reformed temple worship under Hezekiah* ; [NEH 10:32-10:33](bibleref:16010032-16010033) *New Moon festivals were restored on the return from exile* ; [EZK 45:17](bibleref:26045017), [EZK 46:1](bibleref:26046001), [EZK 46:3](bibleref:26046003), [EZK 46:6](bibleref:26046006)

### They were times when normal work ceased
David was to dine with Saul:
 [1SA 20:5](bibleref:09020005), [1SA 20:18](bibleref:09020018), [1SA 20:24](bibleref:09020024)
[2KI 4:23](bibleref:12004023) *the Shunammite woman and her husband* ; [AMO 8:5](bibleref:30008005) *Some saw them as a hindrance to business.* 

## A wrong inner attitude at New Moon celebrations incurs God's anger
 [ISA 1:13-1:14](bibleref:23001013-23001014); [HOS 2:11](bibleref:28002011), [HOS 5:7](bibleref:28005007)

## NT reference to the New Moon festival
**[COL 2:16-2:17](bibleref:51002016-51002017)**
