# 8763: forgetting

The process of accidentally or deliberately failing to remember people, events or ideas. Scripture rebukes those who forget the past work of God in history or in their lives or who neglect their present responsibilities towards God in order to please themselves.



## Causes of forgetting


### Forgetting can be deliberate
**2PE 3:5**

### Forgetting can be caused by idolatry
**JER 23:25-23:27** *See also* DEU 32:15-32:18; JDG 3:7; JER 18:15; HOS 2:13

### Forgetting can be caused by pride
**HOS 13:4-13:6** *See also* 2CH 26:16; PSA 10:4

### Forgetting can be caused by drinking
**PRO 31:4-31:5** *See also* GEN 9:20-9:21; MAT 24:45-24:51 parallel LUK 12:42-12:46

### Forgetting can be caused by affliction
**PSA 102:4** *See also* GEN 41:28-41:31; LAM 3:17-3:20

## Forgetting people
**GEN 40:23** *See also* JOB 19:13-19:15; PSA 31:11-31:12

## Forgetting God
**DEU 32:18** *See also* JDG 2:10-2:13; 1SA 12:9-12:10

## Forgetting God's acts in the past
**PSA 78:11-78:20** *See also* JDG 8:33-8:34; NEH 9:16-9:17; PSA 106:7-106:13

## Consequences of forgetting God


### Forgetting God brings suffering on the individual
**PSA 50:22** *See also* 1SA 12:9; ISA 17:10-17:11

### Forgetting God brings suffering on others
**2SA 12:9** *David's forgetting of God in his adultery with Bathsheba led to her husband Uriah suffering death through David's manipulation of events.* *See also* PSA 106:13-106:15; JER 3:21

### Forgetting God brings destruction on the nation
**DEU 8:19-8:20** *See also* ISA 65:11-65:12; HOS 8:14

## Warnings not to forget God
**DEU 6:12** *See also* DEU 4:9, DEU 8:10-8:14; 2KI 17:35-17:38

## People may sometimes feel that God has forgotten them
**PSA 13:1** *See also* PSA 42:9-42:10, PSA 44:23-44:24; ISA 49:14; MAT 27:46 parallel MRK 15:34; PSA 22:1

## God promises never to forget his people
**ISA 44:21** *See also* ISA 49:15-49:16; HEB 6:10

## Things God urges believers to forget
**ISA 43:18** *See also* PHP 3:13-3:15
