# 5831: depression

A deep sense of despondency, discouragement and sadness, often linked with a sense of personal powerlessness and a loss of meaning in and enthusiasm for life. Many biblical characters show evidence of such behaviour which originates in a number of different ways.



## Some causes of depression


### External circumstances
**2CO 1:8** *See also* 1KI 19:3-19:4; JOB 9:23; PSA 42:1-42:11, PSA 88:1-88:18; JOL 1:11

### Physical illness or exhaustion
**1KI 19:5-19:8** *See also* GEN 21:15-21:16; PSA 6:1-6:7, PSA 22:14-22:17, PSA 31:9-31:12; ISA 38:1-38:2; JON 1:5

### Fear of others
**1KI 19:1-19:3**

### Fear of failure
**1KI 19:4** *See also* EXO 4:1; NUM 11:11-11:15

### Serious sin
**MAT 27:3-27:4** *See also* PSA 25:16-25:18; JER 14:2-14:3, JER 14:7, JER 16:9-16:10; EZK 4:16-4:17, EZK 7:26-7:27, EZK 12:19; REV 16:10-16:11

### A sense of the futility of life
**ECC 2:17-2:20**

### Loss of sense of God's presence
**PSA 22:1-22:2** *See also* MAT 27:46; MRK 15:34
