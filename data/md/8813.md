# 8813: riches, spiritual

Out of his infinite abundance God gives his people spiritual treasure. This, much more valuable than material wealth, is to be held in the highest esteem and sought after eagerly.



## The riches of God


### His wisdom and knowledge
**ROM 11:33-11:36**

### His mercy, love and grace
**EPH 2:4, EPH 3:8** *See also* ROM 2:4; EPH 2:7

### His riches in Jesus Christ
**COL 2:2-2:3** *See also* PHP 4:19; COL 1:27

## God bestows spiritual riches on believers


### His precious word
 JOB 23:12; ; PSA 19:9-19:10, PSA 119:72, PSA 119:111, PSA 119:127

### A wealth of spiritual qualities
**ISA 33:5-33:6** *See also* PRO 2:1-2:5, PRO 15:6; 2CO 9:10-9:11; EPH 1:7-1:8; COL 2:2-2:3; JAS 2:5

### A glorious gospel
**2CO 4:7**

### A costly kingdom
 *See also* MAT 13:44-13:46

### A rich reward
**RUT 2:12** *See also* 1SA 26:23; PSA 18:20; PRO 25:21-25:22; COL 3:24; HEB 10:35

### A heavenly inheritance
**ACT 20:32** *See also* EPH 1:13-1:14, EPH 1:18; COL 1:12, COL 3:24; 1PE 1:4

## Christians and spiritual riches


### Christians need to appreciate their spiritual riches
**EPH 1:7-1:8** *See also* EPH 1:18; REV 2:9

**LUK 12:33** *See also* MAT 6:20; LUK 12:20-12:21; 1TI 6:19; HEB 11:26; REV 3:18
