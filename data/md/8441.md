# 8441: goals

Jesus Christ's basic aim was to do his Father's will. Believers have the same objective, which Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit enable them to achieve.



## God's goals


### To live with his people
**LEV 26:11-26:12** *See also* PSA 23:6; ZEC 2:10; JHN 14:23; REV 21:3

### To build his church in Christ
**MAT 16:18** *See also* EPH 2:19-2:22; REV 21:2

### To perfect his church in Christ
**EPH 5:26-5:27** *See also* PSA 138:8; PHP 1:6; COL 2:10

## Jesus Christ's goals


### To do his Father's will
**JHN 6:38** *See also* MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; JHN 4:34, JHN 5:30; HEB 10:7, HEB 10:9

### To preach the gospel
**LUK 4:43** parallel MRK 1:38 *See also* LUK 4:18-4:19; ISA 61:1-61:2

### To save the lost
**LUK 19:10** *See also* MAT 15:21-15:28 parallel MRK 7:24-7:30; LUK 15:7 parallel MAT 18:14; LUK 15:10

### Jesus Christ achieved his goals
**JHN 17:4** *See also* LUK 13:32; JHN 12:31, JHN 19:28, JHN 19:30

## Believers' goals


### To please God
**2CO 5:9** *See also* ROM 14:18; EPH 5:10, EPH 5:17; COL 1:9-1:10

### To know God
**PHP 3:8** *See also* JHN 17:3; PHP 3:10; 2PE 3:18

### To be faithful to Jesus Christ
**JHN 15:4** *See also* MAT 10:22 parallel MRK 13:13; MAT 24:13; PHP 3:14; 2TI 4:7; HEB 12:2; REV 2:26

### To love one another
**JHN 13:34** *See also* MAT 22:34-22:40 parallels MRK 12:28-12:31, LUK 10:25-10:28; JHN 15:12; 1CO 13:13, 1CO 16:14; 1TI 1:5; 1JN 4:11-4:12

### To live at peace
**ROM 12:18** *See also* MRK 9:50; 2CO 13:11; 1TH 4:11

### To spread the gospel
**MAT 28:19-28:20** *See also* ACT 20:24; ROM 11:14, ROM 15:20; 1CO 9:16; 2TI 4:2

### To grow to maturity
**EPH 4:13** *See also* MAT 5:48; ROM 8:29; 2CO 3:18, 2CO 7:1, 2CO 13:11

### Believers' final goal is heaven
**REV 7:9-7:10** *See also* JHN 6:40; 1CO 15:51-15:52; 2CO 5:4-5:5; 1TH 4:17; 1JN 3:2

### God guarantees that believers will reach their goal
**JHN 14:2-14:3** *See also* JHN 6:39; ROM 8:38-8:39; EPH 1:13-1:14; PHP 1:6; 2TI 1:12, 2TI 2:11-2:13; REV 3:5, REV 3:21
