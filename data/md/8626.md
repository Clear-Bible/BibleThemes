# 8626: worship, places of

Under the old covenant, there were rules governing the places where worship might be offered but under the new covenant the earthly location is of no importance.



## Worship at places commemorating some act of God
**GEN 12:7** *See also* GEN 8:20, GEN 26:23-26:25, GEN 35:1

## Worship at a place chosen by God
**DEU 12:13-12:14** *See also* GEN 22:2; DEU 14:23-14:25; 1CH 21:18-21:19; 2CH 7:15-7:16

## Worship in certain sacred places
**DEU 12:5** *See also* EXO 3:12; DEU 26:2; 1SA 1:3, 1SA 1:28; ISA 27:13; JHN 4:20

## Worship at the Tent of Meeting
**EXO 25:8-25:9** *See also* EXO 29:42-29:43, EXO 33:10; LEV 17:1-17:5

## Worship at the temple in Jerusalem
**1CH 22:1** *See also* 2CH 7:15-7:16, 2CH 29:27-29:30; NEH 8:6; LUK 1:8-1:10, LUK 2:37; ACT 8:27

## Worship in a synagogue
**LUK 4:16** *See also* ACT 13:15, ACT 15:21, ACT 17:2

## Worship in the home
**DAN 6:10** *See also* MAT 6:6; ROM 16:5; 1CO 16:19; COL 4:15; PHM 1:2

## The earthly location for worship is unimportant
**JHN 4:21-4:24** *See also* GEN 24:26, GEN 47:31; JDG 7:15; JOB 1:20

## In heaven worship is perfect
 LUK 2:13; ; HEB 12:22; ; REV 4:9-4:11, REV 5:13-5:14, REV 7:9-7:12, REV 19:4-19:7
