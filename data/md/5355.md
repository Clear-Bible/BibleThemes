# 5355: invitations

A request for attendance, often to a celebration or feast. Invitations to the blessings of the gospel are sometimes presented as requests to attend a banquet. In a negative sense one can be invited to sin.



## Invitations to receive hospitality
**ACT 16:15** *See also* GEN 24:31-24:33 *Laban shows hospitality to Abraham's servant and his animals*; GEN 31:54; EXO 2:20; NUM 10:29-10:32; 1SA 9:22-9:24 *Saul dines with Samuel*; 1KI 13:7; JOB 1:4; JER 35:2LUK 7:36, LUK 11:37
JHN 21:12; ACT 10:23; 1CO 10:27

## Invitations to a wedding or a banquet
**LUK 14:8-14:14** *See also* EST 5:4, EST 5:12; JHN 2:2

## Invitations requesting help
**ACT 16:9** *See also* JDG 1:3, JDG 11:6; 2KI 16:7; ACT 8:30-8:31
