# 4807: colours

A wide range of colours are mentioned in Scripture, some being used symbolically.



## Colours mentioned in Scripture


### White
**GEN 49:12**; **2KI 5:27**

### Blue
**EST 1:6**; **EZK 27:24** *See also* EXO 26:1, EXO 39:22

### Yellow
**REV 9:17** *See also* LEV 13:30

### Green
**MRK 6:39** *An indication as to the season; the grass is green in Galilee in spring.* GEN 1:30; ; PSA 23:2; ; JER 17:8; ; REV 8:7

### Red
**2KI 3:22**; **MAT 16:2** *See also* GEN 25:30; EXO 36:19; REV 12:3

### Purple
**MRK 15:17** NUM 4:13; ; PRO 31:22; ; DAN 5:7; ; ACT 16:14

### Scarlet
**ISA 1:18** *See also* GEN 38:28; EXO 35:23 *Purple, scarlet, crimson and red are closely related and sometimes interchangeable in Scripture*; NUM 4:8; SNG 4:3; MAT 27:28; REV 17:4

### Crimson
**ISA 63:1** *See also* 2CH 2:7

### Black
**SNG 5:11** *See also* REV 6:12

### The whole spectrum
**EZK 1:28** *See also* REV 4:3, REV 10:1

## Colours used symbolically
**REV 6:2** *White here symbolises conquest*; **REV 17:3** *See also* REV 6:4 *bloodshed and war*; REV 6:8 *death*REV 7:9, REV 19:14, REV 19:11
REV 20:11 *holiness*
