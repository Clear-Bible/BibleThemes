# 8282: intolerance

Unwillingness to allow or permit something beyond certain levels. God's intolerance should be the model for God's people, but they should avoid becoming wrongly intolerant.



## God is intolerant of evil


### God is pure and cannot tolerate evil
**HAB 1:13** *See also* NUM 25:3; JOS 7:1; 2SA 6:7 parallel 1CH 13:10; 2KI 17:11; PSA 5:6; ROM 1:18; REV 21:27

### God demonstrates his intolerance as the last resort
**JER 44:22** *See also* EXO 4:10-4:14, EXO 34:6-34:7; NUM 14:18; NEH 9:30; ACT 17:30; ROM 2:3-2:5

## Human intolerance should reflect God's attitudes


### Human intolerance of God's enemies
**JDG 5:31** *See also* 1SA 17:26; PSA 69:9; MAT 26:51 parallels MRK 14:47, LUK 22:50; JHN 18:10; EPH 5:6-5:7

### Human intolerance of ungodliness
**EXO 32:19-32:20** *See also* DEU 6:13-6:15; PSA 119:115; 1TI 4:7; 2TI 2:16, 2TI 3:5; TIT 2:12

### Human intolerance of error
**TIT 3:10** *See also* ACT 17:11, ACT 20:30-20:31; GAL 5:11-5:12; EPH 5:6-5:7; COL 2:8; HEB 13:9

### Human intolerance of evil and sinfulness
**REV 2:20** *See also* JOB 28:28; PSA 34:14, PSA 101:4-101:5; MAT 5:30; ROM 6:12, ROM 12:9; EPH 4:25; 2TH 3:6; REV 2:2

## Human intolerance can be misguided


### It can be a form of hypocrisy
**LUK 6:41-6:42** *See also* MAT 23:29-23:30; LUK 13:14-13:15; JHN 12:5-12:6

### It can amount to exclusiveness
**MAT 23:13** *See also* ACT 15:1

### It can be a form of oppression
Intolerance of minority, afflicted or underprivileged groups oftens leads to oppression:
 EXO 1:11-1:14, EXO 23:9; ; PRO 14:31, PRO 28:3; ; ECC 5:8; ; MAT 20:31 parallels MRK 10:48, LUK 18:39

### It can be harshly condemnatory
**JHN 8:5** *See also* NUM 21:4-21:5; EST 3:8; MAT 23:34-23:35; JHN 7:45-7:51, JHN 16:2

### It can be a form of bigotry
**JHN 15:19** *See also* NUM 11:27-11:29; JDG 12:5-12:6; MAT 21:15-21:16, MAT 23:16, MAT 23:18

## Intolerance is usually a matter of decision


### Intolerance leading to a good decision
**LUK 18:4-18:5** *See also* LUK 11:7-11:8

### Intolerance leading to a bad decision
**MAT 19:13-19:14** parallels MRK 10:13-10:14, LUK 18:15-18:16 *The disciples' intolerance is counteracted by Jesus Christ's tolerance.*
