# 8282: intolerance

Unwillingness to allow or permit something beyond certain levels. God's intolerance should be the model for God's people, but they should avoid becoming wrongly intolerant.

## God is intolerant of evil


### God is pure and cannot tolerate evil
**[HAB 1:13](bibleref:35001013)** *See also* [NUM 25:3](bibleref:04025003); [JOS 7:1](bibleref:06007001); [2SA 6:7](bibleref:10006007) parallel 1CH 13:10; [2KI 17:11](bibleref:12017011); [PSA 5:6](bibleref:19005006); [ROM 1:18](bibleref:45001018); [REV 21:27](bibleref:66021027)

### God demonstrates his intolerance as the last resort
**[JER 44:22](bibleref:24044022)** *See also* [EXO 4:10-4:14](bibleref:02004010-02004014), [EXO 34:6-34:7](bibleref:02034006-02034007); [NUM 14:18](bibleref:04014018); [NEH 9:30](bibleref:16009030); [ACT 17:30](bibleref:44017030); [ROM 2:3-2:5](bibleref:45002003-45002005)

## Human intolerance can be misguided


### It can be a form of hypocrisy
**[LUK 6:41-6:42](bibleref:42006041-42006042)** *See also* [MAT 23:29-23:30](bibleref:40023029-40023030); [LUK 13:14-13:15](bibleref:42013014-42013015); [JHN 12:5-12:6](bibleref:43012005-43012006)

### It can amount to exclusiveness
**[MAT 23:13](bibleref:40023013)** *See also* [ACT 15:1](bibleref:44015001)

### It can be a form of oppression
Intolerance of minority, afflicted or underprivileged groups oftens leads to oppression:
 [EXO 1:11-1:14](bibleref:02001011-02001014), [EXO 23:9](bibleref:02023009); [PRO 14:31](bibleref:20014031), [PRO 28:3](bibleref:20028003); [ECC 5:8](bibleref:21005008); [MAT 20:31](bibleref:40020031) parallels MRK 10:48, LUK 18:39

### It can be harshly condemnatory
**[JHN 8:5](bibleref:43008005)** *See also* [NUM 21:4-21:5](bibleref:04021004-04021005); [EST 3:8](bibleref:17003008); [MAT 23:34-23:35](bibleref:40023034-40023035); [JHN 7:45-7:51](bibleref:43007045-43007051), [JHN 16:2](bibleref:43016002)

### It can be a form of bigotry
**[JHN 15:19](bibleref:43015019)** *See also* [NUM 11:27-11:29](bibleref:04011027-04011029); [JDG 12:5-12:6](bibleref:07012005-07012006); [MAT 21:15-21:16](bibleref:40021015-40021016), [MAT 23:16](bibleref:40023016), [MAT 23:18](bibleref:40023018)
