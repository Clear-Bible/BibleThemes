# 8629: worship, times for

Scripture stresses the importance of regular worship, while at the same time recognising that believers may worship God spontaneously.



## Examples of regular worship


### On a daily basis
**ACT 2:46-2:47** *See also* EXO 29:38-29:43; PSA 141:2; EZK 46:13-46:15

### Several times a day
**DAN 6:10** *See also* 1CH 16:37; PSA 119:164; ACT 3:1; HEB 10:25

### On holy days
**2CH 8:12-8:13** *See also* EZK 46:3

### At the three annual pilgrim festivals
**DEU 16:16** EXO 12:1-12:20; ; LUK 2:41; EXO 34:22; ; ACT 2:1; EXO 23:16; ; LEV 23:33-23:36; ; NUM 29:12-29:39; ; DEU 16:13-16:15

### On the Day of Atonement
**LEV 16:34** *See also* EXO 30:10; LEV 16:3-16:33, LEV 23:26-23:32

### On Sabbath days
**LUK 4:16** *See also* LEV 24:5-24:8; NUM 28:9-28:10

## Examples of spontaneous worship


### In response to an awareness of the closeness of God
 EXO 34:8; ; JDG 7:15

### In response to the experience of God's mercy
**EXO 4:29-4:31** *See also* 1SA 1:19-1:28

### In response to the presence and power of Jesus Christ
**MAT 28:8-28:9** *See also* MAT 14:33; LUK 24:52

## Worship should not be dependent on circumstances
**PHP 4:6** *See also* JOB 1:20-1:21; DAN 6:10; ACT 16:25; 1TH 5:16-5:18

## The continuous worship of God in heaven
 REV 4:10-4:11, REV 5:14, REV 7:11, REV 11:1, REV 19:4
