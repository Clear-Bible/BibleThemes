# 5274: credit

Money or goods lent under pledge of repayment (with or without interest). In the spiritual sense, Jesus Christ's righteousness is credited to believers.

## Standing security for another may be hazardous
**[PRO 22:26-22:27](bibleref:20022026-20022027)** *See also* [PRO 6:1-6:5](bibleref:20006001-20006005), [PRO 11:15](bibleref:20011015), [PRO 17:18](bibleref:20017018) *The binding nature of the commitment cautions against a hasty and unwise engagement.*
