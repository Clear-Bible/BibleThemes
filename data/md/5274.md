# 5274: credit

Money or goods lent under pledge of repayment (with or without interest). In the spiritual sense, Jesus Christ's righteousness is credited to believers.



## Credit in Israel: charitable, not commercial
**DEU 23:19-23:20** *See also* EXO 22:25; PSA 15:5; EZK 18:7, EZK 18:16-18:17

## The pledge (security):  laws protecting the borrower
**DEU 24:10-24:13** *See also* EXO 22:26-22:27; LEV 25:39-25:40, LEV 25:42; DEU 24:6

## Standing security for another may be hazardous
**PRO 22:26-22:27** *See also* PRO 6:1-6:5, PRO 11:15, PRO 17:18 *The binding nature of the commitment cautions against a hasty and unwise engagement.*

## Violations of Israel's ``credit controls'' roundly condemned
 NEH 5:1-5:12; ; JOB 22:6, JOB 24:3, JOB 24:9; ; EZK 18:12; ; AMO 2:8

## The credit system not condemned by Jesus Christ
 MAT 25:27 parallel LUK 19:23

## Christians to view this subject in the light of God's grace
**LUK 6:34-6:35** *See also* MAT 18:23-18:27 *God is a model creditor.*

## Creditors demanding a pledge to assure repayment


### A personal effect
 DEU 24:6, DEU 24:10-24:13; ; JOB 24:3; ; PRO 22:26-22:27

### Mortgage of property
 NEH 5:3

### The surety of a guarantor
 PRO 6:1-6:5, PRO 11:15, PRO 17:18, PRO 22:26, PRO 27:13

## Creditors exacting repayments cruelly
**2KI 4:1** *See also* JOB 22:6, JOB 24:3, JOB 24:9; PSA 109:11; ISA 50:1; EZK 18:12-18:13, EZK 22:12; AMO 2:6, AMO 2:8; MAT 5:25-5:26, MAT 18:25-18:26, MAT 18:34

## Creditors to be considerate in their demands
**DEU 24:6** *See also* EXO 22:26-22:27; LEV 25:35-25:43; DEU 24:10-24:13

## Creditors perhaps remitting the debt entirely
**MAT 18:27** *See also* NEH 5:10-5:12; MAT 6:12; LUK 7:41-7:43

## Under Mosaic law creditors to cancel debts eventually
**DEU 15:1-15:3** *See also* DEU 31:10; NEH 10:31

## Spiritual aspects of credit


### Faith credited as righteousness
**ROM 4:1-4:11** *See also* GEN 15:6; PSA 32:2, PSA 106:31; EZK 18:20; ROM 4:22-4:24; JAS 2:23

### Jesus Christ's righteousness credited to believers
**2CO 5:21** *See also* ROM 3:22; 1CO 1:30; PHP 3:9

## Moral aspects of credit
**PHP 4:17** *See also* MAT 10:40-10:42, MAT 25:34-25:36; LUK 6:32-6:35; 2CO 5:10; EPH 6:7-6:8 *Good deeds will be remembered and rewarded in heaven.*
