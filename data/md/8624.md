# 8624: worship, reasons for

The supreme reason for human existence is to worship God for his love, greatness and saving deeds.



## To worship is a divine command
**MAT 4:10** parallel LUK 4:8 *See also* DEU 6:13; EXO 23:25; 2KI 17:36; 1CH 16:29; PSA 22:23, PSA 29:2, PSA 68:26, PSA 113:1, PSA 117:1, PSA 148:11-148:13, PSA 150:6; 1TI 2:8; REV 14:7

## God's people are to be a worshipping people
**1PE 2:9** *See also* EXO 19:5-19:6; PSA 105:1-105:6; ISA 43:21; REV 1:5-1:6

## Worship is the response of God's people


### To God's love
**EXO 4:31** *See also* DEU 6:5, DEU 12:7, DEU 26:10-26:11; 2CH 7:3; PSA 95:6-95:7, PSA 117:1-117:2, PSA 138:2

### To God's holy presence
**1CH 16:29** parallel PSA 96:8-96:9 *See also* EXO 33:10; LEV 10:3; JOS 5:13-5:15; PSA 29:2, PSA 99:5; REV 4:8, REV 15:4

### To God's greatness
**PSA 95:1-95:3** *See also* EXO 3:12; PSA 22:27-22:28, PSA 66:1-66:4, PSA 96:1-96:3; REV 15:3-15:4

## To the deeds of God
**GEN 8:20** *Noah's response to God's deliverance from the flood.* *See also* GEN 12:7 *God's promise of an inheritance to Abraham*EXO 4:29-4:31, EXO 12:27, EXO 15:1, EXO 15:20
EZR 3:10-3:11; ISA 19:21; DAN 3:28 *God's deliverance of Daniel in Babylon*; MAT 9:7-9:8 parallels MRK 2:12, LUK 5:25-5:26 *the healing of the paralytic*; ACT 3:8 *the healing of the crippled beggar*

### To the fear of God
**PSA 22:23**; **HEB 12:28** *See also* PSA 2:11; ACT 10:2
