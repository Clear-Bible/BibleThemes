# 5900: laughter

The act or sound of laughing, often associated with joy and delight, yet occasionally linked with ridicule and scorn.



## People laughing


### Out of pleasure
**GEN 21:6** *See also* PRO 31:25; ECC 3:4, ECC 10:19

### In scorn and derision
**MAT 9:24** parallels MRK 5:40, LUK 8:53 *See also* JOB 39:18, JOB 41:29; PSA 52:6; LAM 1:7; HAB 1:10

## God laughing
**PSA 2:4** *See also* PSA 37:13, PSA 59:8; PRO 1:26

## God helping people to laugh
**PSA 126:2**; **LUK 6:21** *See also* JOB 5:22, JOB 8:21; JER 51:39 *as a prelude to judgment*

## Laughter qualified
**PRO 14:13** *See also* ECC 2:2, ECC 7:3

## Laughter rebuked
**LUK 6:25** *See also* ECC 7:6; JAS 4:9
