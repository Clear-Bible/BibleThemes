# 1511: Trinity, relationships between the persons

The actual term “the Trinity” is not found in Scripture, but the truths implied in a trinitarian understanding of God are clearly set out. The OT hints at a plurality of persons in the Godhead. The NT affirms that the Son and the Holy Spirit are divine.



## There is only one God
**[DEU 6:4](bibleref:05006004)** *See also* [ISA 43:10-43:11](bibleref:23043010-23043011), [ISA 44:8](bibleref:23044008); [1TI 1:17](bibleref:54001017), [1TI 2:5](bibleref:54002005); [JAS 2:19](bibleref:59002019)

## OT indications of plurality in the Godhead


### God refers to himself in the plural
**[GEN 1:26](bibleref:01001026)** *See also* [GEN 3:22](bibleref:01003022), [GEN 11:7](bibleref:01011007); [ISA 6:8](bibleref:23006008)

### The angel of the LORD
**[GEN 16:11-16:13](bibleref:01016011-01016013)** *The “angel of the LORD” is identified with, yet distinct from, God.*  *See also* [GEN 18:1-18:33](bibleref:01018001-01018033); [EXO 3:2-3:6](bibleref:02003002-02003006); [JDG 13:3-13:22](bibleref:07013003-07013022)

### The word of God
The ``word of the LORD'' or ``wisdom of God'' is personified and identified with, yet distinct from, God:
 [PSA 33:4](bibleref:19033004); [PRO 8:22-8:31](bibleref:20008022-20008031)

### The Spirit of God
The Spirit of God is God's personal agent:
 [GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002); [NEH 9:20](bibleref:16009020); [JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004); [ISA 40:13](bibleref:23040013)

### The Messiah
The Messiah's divine nature is emphasised:
 [PSA 110:1](bibleref:19110001); [ISA 9:6](bibleref:23009006); [JER 23:5-23:6](bibleref:24023005-24023006)

### Interchangeable expressions
Word, Spirit (or breath) and LORD are used interchangeably for God:
 [PSA 33:6](bibleref:19033006); [ISA 48:16](bibleref:23048016), [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001)
