# 1511: Trinity, relationships between the persons

The actual term “the Trinity” is not found in Scripture, but the truths implied in a trinitarian understanding of God are clearly set out. The OT hints at a plurality of persons in the Godhead. The NT affirms that the Son and the Holy Spirit are divine.



## There is only one God
**DEU 6:4** *See also* ISA 43:10-43:11, ISA 44:8; 1TI 1:17, 1TI 2:5; JAS 2:19

## OT indications of plurality in the Godhead


### God refers to himself in the plural
**GEN 1:26** *See also* GEN 3:22, GEN 11:7; ISA 6:8

### The angel of the LORD
**GEN 16:11-16:13** *The “angel of the LORD” is identified with, yet distinct from, God.* *See also* GEN 18:1-18:33; EXO 3:2-3:6; JDG 13:3-13:22

### The word of God
The ``word of the LORD'' or ``wisdom of God'' is personified and identified with, yet distinct from, God:
 PSA 33:4; ; PRO 8:22-8:31

### The Spirit of God
The Spirit of God is God's personal agent:
 GEN 1:2; ; NEH 9:20; ; JOB 33:4; ; ISA 40:13

### The Messiah
The Messiah's divine nature is emphasised:
 PSA 110:1; ; ISA 9:6; ; JER 23:5-23:6

### Interchangeable expressions
Word, Spirit (or breath) and LORD are used interchangeably for God:
 PSA 33:6; ; ISA 48:16, ISA 61:1

## NT trinitarian references
**MAT 28:19** *The unity of the three persons is reflected in the singular name.* *See also* 2CO 13:14; EPH 4:4-4:6; REV 1:4-1:5

## The unity of the three persons


### The Son is fully united with the Father
**JHN 10:30** *See also* MRK 9:37 parallel LUK 9:48; LUK 10:16; JHN 10:38, JHN 12:44-12:45, JHN 13:20, JHN 14:7, JHN 14:9-14:11, JHN 15:23

### The Spirit is identified with God
**2SA 23:2-23:3** *See also* PSA 51:11; MAT 28:19; 1CO 3:16

## The three persons are distinct from one another


### Jesus Christ addresses the Father directly
 MAT 11:25-11:26 parallel LUK 10:21; ; MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; ; MAT 26:42, MAT 27:46 parallel MRK 15:34; ; LUK 23:46; ; JHN 11:41-11:42, JHN 17:1

### The Father speaks to the Son from heaven
 MAT 3:17 parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; ; MAT 17:5 parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; ; JHN 12:27-12:28

### The Spirit speaks to the Father on behalf of believers
 ROM 8:26-8:27

### Other examples of the difference between the persons
 MAT 12:32, MAT 24:36; ; JHN 7:39, JHN 16:7; ; 1TI 2:5; ; 1JN 2:1

## The relationship between the Father and the Son


### Jesus Christ is God's unique Son
**JHN 1:14** *The Greek word for “One and Only”, traditionally rendered “only begotten”, is actually used to signify “the only one of its kind”; “unique”.* *See also* JHN 1:18, JHN 3:16, JHN 3:18; ACT 13:33; HEB 1:5; PSA 2:7; 1JN 4:9

### The relationship of Father and Son is unique
**MAT 11:27** parallel LUK 10:22 *See also* JHN 6:46, JHN 7:28-7:29, JHN 8:55, JHN 10:15, JHN 17:25

### The Father loves the Son
**JHN 3:35** *See also* JHN 5:20, JHN 10:17, JHN 15:9, JHN 17:24

### The Father shares his divine life with the Son
**COL 2:9** *See also* JHN 5:26, JHN 6:57; COL 1:19

### The Father delegates his authority to the Son
**JHN 5:27** *See also* MAT 28:18; JHN 3:35, JHN 5:21-5:22, JHN 16:15; REV 2:26-2:27

### Father and Son indwell each other
**JHN 14:10-14:11** *See also* JHN 10:38, JHN 14:20, JHN 17:21-17:23

### The Spirit's unique relationship with God
**MAT 10:20** *See also* 1CO 2:10-2:11

### The Spirit's unique relationship with the Son
**JHN 1:33** *See also* ISA 61:1; JHN 14:16-14:17, JHN 14:26; ACT 10:38
