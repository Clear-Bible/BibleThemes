# 9024: death, spiritual

A condition in this life of alienation from God because of sin. It leads ultimately to the “second death”, the permanent separation of unbelievers from God.



## Spiritual death means alienation from God in this life
**[EPH 2:1-2:3](bibleref:49002001-49002003)**; **[COL 1:21](bibleref:51001021)** *See also* [MAT 8:22](bibleref:40008022); [ROM 6:23](bibleref:45006023); [COL 2:13](bibleref:51002013)

## Spiritual death is a result of Adam's sin
**[ROM 5:15-5:16](bibleref:45005015-45005016)** *See also* [ROM 5:12](bibleref:45005012)

### Spiritual ignorance
 [MAT 4:16](bibleref:40004016); [ISA 9:2](bibleref:23009002); [LUK 1:79](bibleref:42001079); [EPH 5:8](bibleref:49005008)

**[ROM 8:5-8:8](bibleref:45008005-45008008)** *See also* [PRO 8:36](bibleref:20008036), [PRO 14:12](bibleref:20014012), [PRO 16:25](bibleref:20016025), [PRO 21:16](bibleref:20021016); [EPH 4:18](bibleref:49004018)

### Unbelief

 [LUK 15:13](bibleref:42015013); [1TI 5:6](bibleref:54005006)
