# 6135: coveting, and other sins

Covetousness is associated with other sins and brings trouble with it.



## Coveting is linked with other sins


### Idolatry
**[EPH 5:5](bibleref:49005005)** *See also* [1CO 10:6-10:7](bibleref:46010006-46010007); [COL 3:5](bibleref:51003005)

### Pride
 [PSA 10:3](bibleref:19010003)

### Sexual sin
**[MAT 5:28](bibleref:40005028)** *See also* [1TH 4:5](bibleref:52004005)

### Laziness
 [PRO 21:25-21:26](bibleref:20021025-20021026)

## Covetousness is one of many sins which pollute a person
**[MRK 7:21-7:23](bibleref:41007021-41007023)**

## Love of money is covetousness


### It is a source of evil
**[1TI 6:9-6:10](bibleref:54006009-54006010)** *See also* [PRO 10:2](bibleref:20010002), [PRO 22:16](bibleref:20022016), [PRO 28:20](bibleref:20028020), [PRO 28:22](bibleref:20028022), [PRO 30:8-30:9](bibleref:20030008-20030009)

### Examples of those who love money
**[LUK 16:14](bibleref:42016014)**; **[JHN 12:5-12:6](bibleref:43012005-43012006)** *See also* [MAT 23:25](bibleref:40023025) parallel LUK 11:39; [2PE 2:3](bibleref:61002003), [2PE 2:14](bibleref:61002014) *False teachers are “experts in greed”.* 

### Believers must not be lovers of money
**[HEB 13:5](bibleref:58013005)**; **[1PE 5:2](bibleref:60005002)** *Elders must not be greedy for money.*  *See also* [EXO 18:21](bibleref:02018021); [EPH 5:3](bibleref:49005003); [1TI 3:1-3:3](bibleref:54003001-54003003)

### Love of money characterises the last days
**[2TI 3:1-3:2](bibleref:55003001-55003002)**

## Coveting brings trouble
**[JAS 4:1-4:2](bibleref:59004001-59004002)** *See also* [PRO 15:27](bibleref:20015027), [PRO 28:25](bibleref:20028025), [PRO 29:4](bibleref:20029004)

## Even a gift can be given covetously
 [2CO 9:5](bibleref:47009005) *The gift translated as “one grudgingly given” literally means a “covetous gift”.*
