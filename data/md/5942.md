# 5942: security

Guaranteed safety, whether natural or spiritual, from danger or loss. Scripture stresses that ultimate security is to be found only in God himself.

## The security of Israel


### Their security was found in God himself
**[DEU 33:27](bibleref:05033027)** *See also* [DEU 33:26](bibleref:05033026), [DEU 33:28-33:29](bibleref:05033028-05033029); [EZR 8:21-8:23](bibleref:15008021-15008023); [JER 31:10-31:11](bibleref:24031010-24031011)

### They were secure from danger and loss
**[ISA 54:17](bibleref:23054017)** *See also* [EZK 34:27-34:28](bibleref:26034027-26034028)
