# 5942: security

Guaranteed safety, whether natural or spiritual, from danger or loss. Scripture stresses that ultimate security is to be found only in God himself.



## The security of Israel


### Their security was found in God himself
**DEU 33:27** *See also* DEU 33:26, DEU 33:28-33:29; EZR 8:21-8:23; JER 31:10-31:11

### They were secure from danger and loss
**ISA 54:17** *See also* EZK 34:27-34:28

### Their security was conditional upon obedience
**EXO 19:5** *See also* DEU 28:1-28:6, DEU 28:15-28:19

## The security of believers is guaranteed


### By the everlasting covenant
**2SA 23:5** *See also* ROM 8:29-8:30

### In spiritual security in Jesus Christ
**JHN 10:28-10:29** *See also* JHN 17:12, JHN 17:15; ROM 8:35-8:39; PHP 1:6; HEB 7:25

### In the provision of needs
**MAT 6:33** *See also* PSA 23:1, PSA 23:4; PHP 4:19; HEB 13:5-13:6; PSA 118:6-118:7

### In safety from danger
**PSA 46:1** *See also* PSA 91:1-91:15, PSA 121:3-121:5; JUD 1:24-1:25

### In a sure hope
**2TI 1:12** *See also* JOB 19:25-19:27; HEB 6:19; 1PE 1:3-1:5

### In prophecies of security
 ISA 2:4, ISA 11:6-11:9, ISA 65:21-65:25; ; MIC 4:3-4:4; ; REV 21:4

## Provision for the security of human beings


### Protection for the weak

### Financial pledges
