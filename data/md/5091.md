# 5091: Deborah

An outstanding leader in Israel when the nation faced a crisis of resurgent Canaanite power in the time of the judges. Her name means “bee”.

## Deborah's calling


### As a judge
 [JDG 4:4-4:5](bibleref:07004004-07004005), [JDG 5:7](bibleref:07005007) *She was probably called upon to settle intertribal disputes, “mother in Israel” meaning one who promotes the unity of God's family.* 

### As a prophetess
 [JDG 4:4](bibleref:07004004), [JDG 4:7](bibleref:07004007), [JDG 4:9](bibleref:07004009), [JDG 4:14](bibleref:07004014)

## Deborah's response to the Canaanite threat


### She was petitioned by the people
 [JDG 5:11-5:12](bibleref:07005011-07005012)

### She appointed Barak commander-in-chief
 [JDG 4:6](bibleref:07004006)

### She accompanied the army into battle
 [JDG 4:8-4:10](bibleref:07004008-07004010)

### She led the people in victory celebration
 [JDG 5:1-5:3](bibleref:07005001-07005003), [JDG 5:28-5:31](bibleref:07005028-07005031) *In this respect she is like Moses and Miriam (Ex 15:1,20).* 

## Deborah's character


### She was wise, decisive, courageous, inspiring and bold
 [JDG 4:5-4:9](bibleref:07004005-07004009), [JDG 4:14](bibleref:07004014), [JDG 5:9](bibleref:07005009), [JDG 5:15-5:17](bibleref:07005015-07005017) *Her confidence was in God's word and promise and she did not shrink from rebuking God's people.* 

### She gave credit to others
 [JDG 5:14-5:15](bibleref:07005014-07005015), [JDG 5:18-5:19](bibleref:07005018-07005019), [JDG 5:24-5:27](bibleref:07005024-07005027)

### She gave glory to God
 [JDG 5:20-5:23](bibleref:07005020-07005023) *These verses are a poetic acknowledgment that God has fought on Israel's behalf.* 

## She was God's person for the hour
