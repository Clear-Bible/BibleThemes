# 5091: Deborah

An outstanding leader in Israel when the nation faced a crisis of resurgent Canaanite power in the time of the judges. Her name means “bee”.



## Deborah's calling


### As a judge
 JDG 4:4-4:5, JDG 5:7 *She was probably called upon to settle intertribal disputes, “mother in Israel” meaning one who promotes the unity of God's family.*

### As a prophetess
 JDG 4:4, JDG 4:7, JDG 4:9, JDG 4:14

## Deborah's response to the Canaanite threat


### She was petitioned by the people
 JDG 5:11-5:12

### She appointed Barak commander-in-chief
 JDG 4:6

### She accompanied the army into battle
 JDG 4:8-4:10

### She led the people in victory celebration
 JDG 5:1-5:3, JDG 5:28-5:31 *In this respect she is like Moses and Miriam (Ex 15:1,20).*

## Deborah's character


### She was wise, decisive, courageous, inspiring and bold
 JDG 4:5-4:9, JDG 4:14, JDG 5:9, JDG 5:15-5:17 *Her confidence was in God's word and promise and she did not shrink from rebuking God's people.*

### She gave credit to others
 JDG 5:14-5:15, JDG 5:18-5:19, JDG 5:24-5:27

### She gave glory to God
 JDG 5:20-5:23 *These verses are a poetic acknowledgment that God has fought on Israel's behalf.*

## She was God's person for the hour
**JDG 5:31** *See also* EST 4:14
