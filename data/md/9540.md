# 9540: Sheol

A Hebrew term used to refer to the grave, the pit or the tomb. Some older translations have the term incorrectly translated as “hell”.

## Sheol is the destiny of all
**[PSA 89:48](bibleref:19089048)** *See also* [GEN 37:35](bibleref:01037035), [GEN 44:29](bibleref:01044029); [1KI 2:9](bibleref:11002009); [PSA 18:5](bibleref:19018005), [PSA 107:20](bibleref:19107020); [PRO 1:12](bibleref:20001012), [PRO 30:16](bibleref:20030016); [EZK 31:15-31:17](bibleref:26031015-26031017); [JON 2:2](bibleref:32002002)

## Sheol is often described in terms suggesting a grave
**[PSA 88:11-88:12](bibleref:19088011-19088012)** *See also* [JOB 3:13-3:19](bibleref:18003013-18003019), [JOB 7:9](bibleref:18007009), [JOB 17:13-17:16](bibleref:18017013-18017016); [PSA 6:5](bibleref:19006005); [ECC 9:10](bibleref:21009010); [ISA 14:9-14:11](bibleref:23014009-23014011), [ISA 38:18](bibleref:23038018)
