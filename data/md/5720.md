# 5720: mothers, examples of

Examples are given in Scripture of notable mothers, mothers-in-law and grandmothers.



## Eve, the mother of all the living
**GEN 3:20** *See also* GEN 3:16

## Hagar, the mother of Ishmael
 GEN 16:1-16:4, GEN 16:15

## Sarah, the mother of Isaac
**GEN 21:1-21:3** *See also* GEN 17:15-17:19, GEN 18:10-18:14

## Rebekah, mother of Esau and Jacob
 GEN 25:21, GEN 25:24, GEN 27:5-27:17

 GEN 30:22-30:24, GEN 35:16-35:18
