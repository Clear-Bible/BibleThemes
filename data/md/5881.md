# 5881: immaturity

The result of insufficient growth, or a failure to develop to one's full potential.



## Marks of physical immaturity


### Pride
 2SA 15:1-15:4

### Rejection of wise counsel
 DEU 21:18; ; 1KI 12:8

### Tactlessness
 1KI 12:13-12:14

### Lack of respect
 2KI 2:23; ; JOB 19:18

### Weakness of character
 2CH 13:7

### Foolishness
 PRO 22:15

### Lack of understanding
 DEU 1:39; ; PRO 1:4; ; JON 4:11; ; 1CO 13:11

### Lack of self-control
 TIT 2:6

### Disgrace
 PRO 29:15

### Waste
 LUK 15:13

## Spiritual immaturity is to be avoided
**1CO 14:20**; **HEB 6:1** *See also* 2CO 13:9, 2CO 13:11 *The word “perfection” may be understood in terms of maturity*; EPH 4:13, EPH 4:15; COL 1:28, COL 4:12; JAS 1:4; 2PE 3:18

## Marks of spiritual immaturity


### Backsliding
**MAT 13:20-13:21** parallels MRK 4:16-4:17, LUK 8:13 *Jesus Christ to his disciples.* *See also* GAL 4:8-4:9

### An over-sensitive conscience
**ROM 14:2**

### Lack of spiritual understanding
**1CO 3:1-3:2** *See also* MAT 11:16-11:19 parallel LUK 7:31-7:35; HEB 5:11-5:14

### Divisiveness
**1CO 3:3**

### Instability
**EPH 4:14** *See also* JAS 1:6-1:7

### Pride
**1TI 3:6** *The church overseer must be a spiritually mature person.* *See also* 1PE 5:5

## Remedies for immaturity


### Confession of sin
**PRO 28:13** *See also* JER 3:13-3:22; JAS 5:16; 1JN 1:9

### Discipline
**PRO 3:11-3:12** *See also* PSA 119:67-119:71; 1PE 4:1-4:2; REV 3:19

### Prayer and meditation
**PSA 119:97-119:99** *See also* PSA 119:27; JHN 17:17-17:19; ROM 8:26; HEB 4:16; JAS 1:25
