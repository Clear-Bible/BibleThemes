# 9125: footstool

Used mostly symbolically to refer to God's supreme authority over his creation. It is used especially to refer to his final victory over all those who oppose him.

## The footstool as a rest for the feet
**[2CH 9:18](bibleref:14009018)**; **[JAS 2:3](bibleref:59002003)**

## The footstool used symbolically


### God's footstool as a symbol for the ark and the temple
**[PSA 132:7](bibleref:19132007)** *See also* [1CH 28:2](bibleref:13028002); [PSA 99:5](bibleref:19099005); [LAM 2:1](bibleref:25002001)

### God's footstool as a symbol for the earth
**[ISA 66:1](bibleref:23066001)** *God cannot be confined to any man-made temple.*  *See also* [MAT 5:35](bibleref:40005035); [ACT 7:49](bibleref:44007049)

### Defeated enemies can be regarded as a footstool
 [JOS 10:24-10:25](bibleref:06010024-06010025); [1KI 5:3](bibleref:11005003); [PSA 66:12](bibleref:19066012); [ISA 51:23](bibleref:23051023); [MIC 7:10](bibleref:33007010)

### Jesus Christ will make all God's enemies his footstool
**[HEB 1:13](bibleref:58001013)** *This passage affirms Jesus Christ's supremacy over the angels.*  *See also* [PSA 110:1](bibleref:19110001); [MAT 22:44](bibleref:40022044) parallels MRK 12:36, LUK 20:43; [ACT 2:34-2:35](bibleref:44002034-44002035); [EPH 1:22](bibleref:49001022)

### Jesus Christ will complete his victory when he returns
**[HEB 10:13](bibleref:58010013)** *See also* [1CO 15:25](bibleref:46015025); [HEB 2:8](bibleref:58002008); [PSA 8:6](bibleref:19008006)
