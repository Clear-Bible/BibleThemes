# 1445: revelation, responses to

God requires and imparts a frame of mind that receives and responds to what he has made known.



## God commands that his word be heeded
**MRK 4:3** *See also* ISA 1:10; MRK 4:9; REV 2:7

## People do not naturally understand what God has revealed


### They fail to recognise God's revelation in Jesus Christ
**MAT 11:25-11:27** parallel LUK 10:21-10:22 *See also* JHN 5:37-5:40, JHN 6:44-6:45, JHN 10:24-10:26, JHN 12:37-12:41, JHN 14:9; ROM 9:31-10:4; 1CO 1:18-1:25, 1CO 2:8; 2CO 4:4

### They fail to understand God's revelation in general
**1CO 2:14** *See also* MRK 4:11-4:12 parallels MAT 13:13-13:15, LUK 8:10; ISA 6:9-6:10; JHN 8:43-8:47; 2CO 3:14-3:16; 2TH 2:11-2:13

## God has given his Spirit to illuminate the human mind


### He reveals and teaches truth
**1CO 2:12-2:13** *See also* JER 31:31-31:34; MAT 16:17; JHN 3:3-3:10, JHN 14:16-14:17, JHN 14:25-14:26, JHN 16:12-16:15; PHP 3:15

### He reveals through prayer for understanding
**PSA 119:18** *See also* PSA 119:12, PSA 119:27; EPH 1:17-1:18

## Understanding God's revelation carries special responsibility
**LUK 12:47-12:48** *See also* MAT 13:11-13:12, MAT 25:14-25:30 parallel LUK 19:12-19:27

## God's revelation of himself will only be fully understood at the second coming of Jesus Christ
**1CO 13:12** *See also* 1PE 1:13; 1JN 3:2

## The consequences of responding to revelation


### Repentance
**ACT 2:38** *See also* MAT 4:17; ACT 3:19, ACT 26:20; 2CO 7:10

### Faith
**ROM 10:17** *See also* ROM 10:8-10:10, ROM 10:14-10:15; 1CO 2:4-2:5

### Obedience
**ROM 1:5** *See also* JDG 2:17; PHM 1:21; 1PE 1:2
