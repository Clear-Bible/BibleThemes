# 4165: exorcism

The expulsion of an evil spirit or spirits from a person. Jesus Christ has unique authority as an exorcist. He delegated this authority to the disciples.

## Exorcism was practised with varying degrees of success by Jews and pagans
 [MAT 7:22](bibleref:40007022), [MAT 12:27](bibleref:40012027) parallel LUK 11:19; [ACT 19:13-19:16](bibleref:44019013-44019016)

## Jesus Christ had unique authority as an exorcist
**[MRK 1:27](bibleref:41001027)** parallel LUK 4:36**[MAT 12:25-12:28](bibleref:40012025-40012028)** parallel LUK 11:17-11:18 *The authority Jesus Christ exercised over evil spirits demonstrated that God's kingly authority was present in him.*  *See also* [MAT 12:29](bibleref:40012029) parallels MRK 3:27, LUK 11:21-11:22 *Jesus Christ is the “strong man”. His power over evil spirits shows that Satan is defeated.* 

## Jesus Christ had a general ministry of exorcism
 [MAT 8:16](bibleref:40008016) parallels MRK 1:34, LUK 4:41; [MRK 1:39](bibleref:41001039); [LUK 6:17-6:18](bibleref:42006017-42006018), [LUK 7:21](bibleref:42007021)

## Jesus Christ gave the apostles power to cast out evil spirits
 [MAT 10:1](bibleref:40010001) parallels MRK 6:7, LUK 9:1; [MRK 3:14-3:15](bibleref:41003014-41003015), [MRK 16:17](bibleref:41016017); [LUK 10:17](bibleref:42010017)

## After the ascension the apostles cast out evil spirits
