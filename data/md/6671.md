# 6671: grace, and Christian life

The Christian life, from its beginning to its end, is totally dependent upon the grace of God.



## God's grace compensates for human weaknesses
**2CO 12:8-12:9**; **1PE 5:10** *See also* 1CO 2:1-2:5; 2CO 9:8; HEB 2:14, HEB 4:15, HEB 5:2; JAS 4:6; 2PE 3:17-3:18

## Believers are to pray for grace
**HEB 4:16** *See also* PSA 25:16; HOS 14:1-14:2; COL 1:9, COL 4:12

## Christian experience may be summed up in terms of grace
**1CO 15:10** *See also* ACT 18:27; ROM 5:2; GAL 1:15; PHP 1:7

## Believers should go on to experience more of God's grace
**ACT 20:32** *See also* ACT 13:43; COL 1:3-1:6; HEB 13:9; 1PE 5:12; 2PE 3:18

## Believers are enabled to serve Jesus Christ by his grace
**1PE 4:10** *See also* ACT 15:39-15:40; ROM 5:17, ROM 12:6, ROM 15:15; 1CO 3:10; 2CO 12:9; GAL 2:9; EPH 3:7-3:9

## God's grace is seen in Christian character, especially in generosity
**2CO 8:6-8:7** *Referring to a collection being taken up for the poor Christians in Jerusalem.* *See also* ACT 4:33, ACT 11:22-11:23; 2CO 9:13-9:14; COL 4:6

## An ongoing experience of God's grace requires the believer's co-operation
**2CO 6:1** *See also* PHP 2:12-2:13; HEB 12:15; 1PE 5:5; JAS 4:6
