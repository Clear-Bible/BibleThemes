# 7944: ministry, qualifications for

God, who calls his people to minister, also equips his people. The chief qualifications are a response to God's call, faithfulness, godliness and Christlikeness.



## God calls people to minister


### Qualification is by call, not gifting or achievement
**[DEU 7:7-7:8](bibleref:05007007-05007008)** *See also* [DEU 9:4-9:5](bibleref:05009004-05009005)

### God calls those who the world regards as weak or foolish
 [1CO 1:27-1:29](bibleref:46001027-46001029)

### Feelings of inadequacy to God's call are common
 [EXO 3:11](bibleref:02003011) *Moses* ; [JDG 6:15](bibleref:07006015) *Gideon* ; [1SA 9:21](bibleref:09009021) *Saul* ; [1SA 18:18](bibleref:09018018) *David* ; [1KI 3:7](bibleref:11003007) *Solomon* ; [ISA 6:5](bibleref:23006005) *Isaiah* ; [JER 1:6](bibleref:24001006) *Jeremiah* 

## Responding to God's call to minister


### Readiness and availability
 [1SA 3:10](bibleref:09003010); [ISA 6:8](bibleref:23006008)

### Faith, rather than natural talent or moral perfection, is required
**[HEB 11:1-11:2](bibleref:58011001-58011002)** *See also* [GEN 27:19-27:24](bibleref:01027019-01027024) *Jacob was a deceiver* ; [NUM 27:12-27:14](bibleref:04027012-04027014) *Moses and Aaron disobeyed God.* [2SA 11:4](bibleref:10011004), [2SA 11:14-11:15](bibleref:10011014-10011015)
[1KI 11:9-11:13](bibleref:11011009-11011013) *Solomon disobeyed God's command.* 

## NT ministers are recognised by call rather than their achievement


### The Twelve
 [MAT 10:1-10:4](bibleref:40010001-40010004) parallels MRK 3:14-3:19, LUK 6:12-6:16 *The Twelve, including Peter and Judas, failed Jesus Christ at critical times.* 

### Paul
 [ACT 9:15](bibleref:44009015), [ACT 26:6](bibleref:44026006); [2CO 4:7-4:12](bibleref:47004007-47004012), [2CO 12:7](bibleref:47012007) *Though greatly gifted, Paul was kept humble by his sense of unworthiness, difficulties and disappointments and his “thorn in the flesh”* ; [1TI 1:16](bibleref:54001016)

## Ministry in the NT is described as service


### Serving God
 [ROM 1:9](bibleref:45001009); [JAS 1:1](bibleref:59001001)

### Serving Jesus Christ
 [ROM 1:1](bibleref:45001001); [JUD 1:1](bibleref:65001001); [REV 1:1](bibleref:66001001)

### Serving the gospel
 [EPH 3:7](bibleref:49003007); [COL 1:23](bibleref:51001023)

### Serving the church
 [ROM 15:31](bibleref:45015031), [ROM 16:1](bibleref:45016001); [1CO 16:15](bibleref:46016015); [2CO 9:1](bibleref:47009001); [EPH 6:21](bibleref:49006021); [COL 1:7](bibleref:51001007), [COL 1:25](bibleref:51001025)

## Ministry is described in terms of its source, content or nature


### Its source
It is of the Spirit:
 [2CO 3:6](bibleref:47003006), [2CO 3:8](bibleref:47003008)
[2CO 4:1](bibleref:47004001) *It is from God.* 

### Its content
 [ACT 6:2-6:4](bibleref:44006002-44006004) *the word of God* ; [2CO 5:18](bibleref:47005018) *reconciliation* 

### Its nature
Apostolic:
 [ACT 1:25](bibleref:44001025); [GAL 2:8](bibleref:48002008)
[ROM 15:16](bibleref:45015016) *priestly* 

## Various ministries are equally linked by qualifications of character
 [ACT 1:21](bibleref:44001021) *the replacement for Judas* ; [ACT 6:3](bibleref:44006003) *the Seven* [1TI 3:2-3:7](bibleref:54003002-54003007); [TIT 1:7-1:9](bibleref:56001007-56001009)
[1TI 3:8-3:13](bibleref:54003008-54003013) *deacons* ; [1TI 6:11](bibleref:54006011) *Timothy* 

## The personal qualifications for ministry


### Faithfulness
 [1TI 6:11-6:14](bibleref:54006011-54006014) *Timothy* ; [2TI 4:7](bibleref:55004007) *Paul's claim for his own ministry* [COL 1:7](bibleref:51001007), [COL 4:7](bibleref:51004007)

### Godliness
 [ACT 8:21](bibleref:44008021) *Simon was not right with God.* [1TI 6:11](bibleref:54006011), [1TI 6:20-6:21](bibleref:54006020-54006021)

### Christlikeness
 [ACT 1:21-1:22](bibleref:44001021-44001022) *The replacement apostle for Judas had to have been with Jesus Christ from the beginning.*
