# 8258: fruitfulness, spiritual

Fruitfulness is used by Scripture as a symbol of spiritual maturity and well-being.



## Spiritual fruitfulness described
**[GAL 5:22-5:23](bibleref:48005022-48005023)** *The results of the Holy Spirit's activity in the life of a Christian believer.*  *See also* [PSA 92:12](bibleref:19092012); [PRO 11:30](bibleref:20011030); [ISA 32:17](bibleref:23032017); [JER 11:16](bibleref:24011016); [EZK 19:10](bibleref:26019010); [EPH 5:9](bibleref:49005009); [COL 1:10](bibleref:51001010); [JAS 3:17](bibleref:59003017)

## Spiritual fruitfulness expected in God's people
**[JHN 15:16](bibleref:43015016)** *See also* [ISA 5:1-5:2](bibleref:23005001-23005002); [MAT 7:16-7:20](bibleref:40007016-40007020) parallel LUK 6:43-6:44 *Such fruitfuless, seen in a person's behaviour, is evidence of their true character* ; [ROM 7:4](bibleref:45007004)

## The conditions for spiritual fruitfulness


### Spiritual fruitfulness through an intimate relationship with Jesus Christ
**[JHN 15:4-15:5](bibleref:43015004-43015005)**

### Spiritual fruitfulness through a commitment to God's word
**[PSA 1:1-1:3](bibleref:19001001-19001003)** *See also* [MAT 13:23](bibleref:40013023); [JHN 15:2-15:3](bibleref:43015002-43015003)

### Spiritual fruitfulness through a death to self-interest
**[JHN 12:24](bibleref:43012024)** *Although Jesus Christ is speaking here primarily of his own death, the principle remains true for Christian living.*
