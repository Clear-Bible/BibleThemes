# 5778: adorning

A form of ornamentation, using especially jewels and precious metals, to enhance the beauty of buildings or people.

## Buildings were adorned with various materials
**[2CH 3:6](bibleref:14003006)** *See also* [PSA 45:8](bibleref:19045008); [ISA 60:13](bibleref:23060013); [AMO 3:15](bibleref:30003015)

## Objects of worship were adorned
**[HOS 10:1](bibleref:28010001)** *See also* [JER 10:3-10:4](bibleref:24010003-24010004)

## Women adorned themselves with jewellery and fine clothes
**[EZK 16:11-16:12](bibleref:26016011-26016012)** *See also* [2SA 1:24](bibleref:10001024); [JER 4:30](bibleref:24004030); [EZK 16:13](bibleref:26016013)

## Personal adornment should not be allowed to detract from worship
**[1TI 2:9](bibleref:54002009)** *See also* [1PE 3:3-3:4](bibleref:60003003-60003004)
