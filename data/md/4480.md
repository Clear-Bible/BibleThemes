# 4480: milk

A common source of food and nourishment, associated with rich and productive land. The term is also used figuratively as a form of spiritual food suitable for those who have yet to reach maturity in the Christian faith.

## Milk as a source of nourishment
**[DEU 32:13-32:14](bibleref:05032013-05032014)** *See also* [GEN 18:8](bibleref:01018008); [JDG 4:19](bibleref:07004019); [2SA 17:29](bibleref:10017029); [PRO 27:27](bibleref:20027027); [EZK 25:4](bibleref:26025004); [1CO 9:7](bibleref:46009007) *illustrating the support of the ministry*
