# 8782: mockery

A form of ridicule directed especially against individuals, with the purpose of humiliating them.  Scripture condemns mockers as arrogant, unrighteous and foolish.



## Targets of mockery


### God
**ISA 52:5** *See also* PSA 74:10, PSA 74:18, PSA 74:22

### Job
**JOB 30:1** *See also* JOB 17:2, JOB 21:3, JOB 30:9

### God's servants
**2CH 36:16** *See also* NEH 2:19; PSA 22:7, PSA 35:15-35:16, PSA 69:12, PSA 80:6, PSA 89:50-89:51, PSA 119:51; JER 20:7; LAM 3:14, LAM 3:63ACT 2:13, ACT 17:32, ACT 26:24

### Jesus Christ
**MAT 20:17-20:19** parallels MRK 10:32-10:34, LUK 18:31-18:33 *See also* MAT 26:67-26:68 parallels MRK 14:65, LUK 22:63-22:65; LUK 23:11; MAT 27:27-27:31 parallel MRK 15:16-15:20; MAT 27:41-27:44 parallels MRK 15:31-15:32, LUK 23:35; LUK 23:36

## Those who oppose God will become objects of mockery
**PRO 3:34** *See also* 2KI 19:20-19:28 parallel ISA 37:22-37:29; JOB 22:17-22:20; JER 10:14-10:16 parallel JER 51:17-51:19; EZK 22:1-22:5

## False accusations of mockery
**JOB 11:1-11:3** *See also* JOB 9:21-9:24 *Job accuses God of mocking the plight of innocent sufferers.*

## Mockery in metaphorical use
**PRO 20:1**

## Characteristics of mockers


### They are proud and arrogant
**PRO 21:24** *See also* PRO 3:34

### They instigate strife
**PRO 22:10** *See also* PRO 9:7, PRO 29:8

### They do not respond to correction
**PRO 15:12** *See also* PRO 1:22-1:26, PRO 13:1, PRO 19:25

## Scripture condemns mockers as unrighteous
**PSA 1:1** *See also* PRO 14:6 *Resistance to correction accounts for the failure to find wisdom*; PRO 14:9, PRO 24:9; ISA 57:4; HOS 7:5
