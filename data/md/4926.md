# 4926: delay, human

Human delay is often the result of fear, indecision or disobedience to God. Scripture indicates that delay is to be avoided.

### Israel entering the promised land
**[NUM 13:31-13:32](bibleref:04013031-04013032)** *See also* [NUM 13:1-13:3](bibleref:04013001-04013003), [NUM 13:17-13:20](bibleref:04013017-04013020), [NUM 13:27-13:30](bibleref:04013027-04013030), [NUM 14:33-14:35](bibleref:04014033-04014035)

### Gideon responding to God's call
 [JDG 6:36-6:40](bibleref:07006036-07006040)
