# 5903: maturity, physical

In the OT, reaching adulthood brought with it certain privileges and responsibilities.



## Growth to physical and psychological maturity
**LUK 2:52** *Jesus Christ's normal childhood is evidence of his full humanity.* *See also* 1SA 2:26; LUK 1:80, LUK 2:40; 1CO 13:11

## The responsibilities of maturity in the Israelite community


### The payment of atonement money
 EXO 30:11-30:16, EXO 38:25-38:26

### Eligibility for military service
 NUM 1:2-1:3, NUM 1:45, NUM 26:2; ; 2CH 25:5

### Accountability for actions
 NUM 14:29, NUM 32:11

### Inheritance of the land
 NUM 26:52-26:54

### Serving Levites
 NUM 8:24-8:25; ; 1CH 23:24, 1CH 23:27; ; EZR 3:8

## Maturity should produce wisdom
**JOB 12:12** *See also* JOB 15:10, JOB 32:6-32:9; PRO 7:7; ECC 4:13

## Respect should be shown to the mature
**LEV 19:32** *See also* JOB 32:4; PRO 23:22; 1TI 5:1-5:2; 1PE 5:5

## Maturity of years seen as God's blessing
 GEN 24:1; ; RUT 4:15; ; 1CH 29:28

## The maturity of crops for harvest used as a metaphor
**JOL 3:13**; **JHN 4:35-4:36** *See also* AMO 8:1-8:2; MAT 9:37-9:38 parallel LUK 10:1-10:2; MRK 4:26-4:29; REV 14:15
