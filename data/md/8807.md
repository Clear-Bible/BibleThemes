# 8807: profanity

Behaviour which dishonours God's name or brings it into disrepute. It is often the result of idolatry, injustice or irreverence. Scripture strongly admonishes God's people to avoid it.



## General warnings against profanity
**LEV 22:31-22:33** *See also* LEV 21:5-21:6

## God's name profaned through idolatry


### Through child sacrifice
**LEV 18:21** *See also* LEV 20:3; DEU 12:29-12:31, DEU 18:9-18:13; 2KI 16:1-16:4 parallel 2CH 28:1-28:4; 2KI 17:16-17:17, 2KI 21:1-21:6 parallel 2CH 33:1-33:6; PSA 106:36-106:39; ISA 57:3-57:5; JER 32:32-32:35; EZK 16:20-16:22

### Through the worship of idols
**EZK 20:39** *See also* JER 44:25-44:28; EZK 20:31, EZK 43:6-43:9
