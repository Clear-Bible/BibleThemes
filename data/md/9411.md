# 9411: heaven, nature of

Scripture refers to heaven as God's habitation but also uses the term as an alternative for God himself.



## Heaven as God's habitation


### It is the place where he dwells
**DEU 26:15** *See also* GEN 28:17; 2CH 6:21; ECC 5:2; REV 13:6

### It is insufficient as God's dwelling-place
**1KI 8:27** parallel 2CH 6:18 *See also* PSA 113:5-113:6

## Heaven as the place of God's throne
**PSA 11:4** *See also* EXO 24:9-24:11; 1KI 22:19; ISA 6:1, ISA 63:15; DAN 7:9; MAT 5:34, MAT 23:22; ACT 7:49; ISA 66:1; HEB 8:1; REV 4:1-4:6, REV 20:11

## Heaven as God's vantage point
**PSA 33:13** *See also* PSA 53:2, PSA 102:19-102:20; ECC 5:2

## Heaven as an alternative term for God
**LUK 15:18** *See also* MAT 8:11, MAT 13:11 *Matthew is writing especially for Jews who, out of reverence, tried to avoid using the word “God”. Mk 4:11 and Lk 8:10 prefer “kingdom of God”*; MAT 16:19, MAT 18:18, MAT 21:25 parallels MRK 11:30, LUK 20:4; JHN 3:27, JHN 17:1

## Heaven and the sovereignty of God


### Heaven is the place of God's rule
**DAN 4:26** *See also* PSA 45:6, PSA 103:19; ACT 17:24

### God's voice from heaven speaks with divine authority
**DEU 4:36** *See also* DAN 4:31; JHN 12:28; 1TH 4:16; 2PE 1:17-1:18

## Heaven glimpsed by human eyes


### At the baptism of Jesus Christ
**MRK 1:10** parallels MAT 3:16, LUK 3:21-3:22 *See also* JHN 1:32

### In visions
**ACT 7:56** *See also* 2CO 12:2-12:4; REV 4:1

## Prayer addressed to God in heaven
**1KI 8:30** *See also* DEU 26:15; 2CH 30:27; NEH 1:4; PSA 20:6; REV 5:8

## The association of oaths with heaven
**MAT 23:22** *See also* GEN 24:3; DAN 12:7; MAT 5:34; JAS 5:12

## The place of Jesus Christ in heaven


### His pre-existence in heaven
**JHN 6:38** *See also* JHN 3:13

### His ascension into heaven after his resurrection
**LUK 24:51** parallel MRK 16:19 *See also* EPH 1:20

### His place is now with God the Father
**HEB 9:24** *See also* COL 3:1

### His second coming will be from heaven
**1TH 4:16** *See also* MAT 25:31; ACT 1:11; 1TH 1:10

## The new heaven


### The new heaven completely replaces the old
**REV 21:1** *See also* ISA 65:17, ISA 66:22; 2PE 3:13

### The new Jerusalem is divinely created in heaven
**REV 21:2** *See also* ISA 2:2-2:5; HEB 11:16, HEB 13:14; REV 3:12, REV 21:10

### Fulness of life in heaven is wholly sustained by God
**REV 21:22-21:23, REV 22:1-22:2** *See also* ISA 55:1; EZK 47:8-47:9, EZK 47:12; JHN 7:38-7:39; REV 2:7
