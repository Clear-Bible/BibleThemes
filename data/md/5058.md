# 5058: rest, spiritual

To be at rest spiritually is to find peace with God through Jesus Christ. This rest is promised to all who put their trust in God and obey him.



## The human desire for spiritual rest
**LAM 5:19-5:22** *See also* PSA 55:4-55:8; LAM 3:25-3:26; 1TI 2:2

## Spiritual rest is to be found in God alone
**MAT 11:28-11:30** *See also* EXO 33:14; PSA 23:2, PSA 51:12, PSA 62:1-62:2, PSA 62:5-62:8, PSA 91:1-91:13; ISA 26:3-26:4; JHN 14:27; ROM 5:1; GAL 1:3, GAL 5:22; COL 1:20

## God promises his people spiritual rest
**HEB 4:9-4:11** *See also* PSA 1:3, PSA 37:7, PSA 116:7, PSA 119:165, PSA 125:1, PSA 131:1-131:3; PRO 29:25; ISA 30:15, ISA 32:17-32:18, ISA 58:11; JER 6:16, JER 17:7-17:8; JHN 14:1; ROM 8:6; 1PE 3:4

## Spiritual rest is not for the wicked
**PSA 1:4-1:5** *See also* PSA 112:10; ECC 2:23; ISA 48:22, ISA 57:20-57:21

## God's offer of rest rejected by a faithless people
**ISA 28:12**; **JER 6:16**
