# 5077: Abraham, character of

Despite some shortcomings as a husband and father, his obedience, faith, hospitality and effective intercession were exemplary. Such qualities led to his being called “friend of God”.



## Abraham, the friend of God


### He was blessed by God
**[GEN 14:18-14:19](bibleref:01014018-01014019)** *See also* [GEN 21:22](bibleref:01021022), [GEN 24:1](bibleref:01024001), [GEN 24:35](bibleref:01024035)

### He worshipped God
**[GEN 21:33](bibleref:01021033)** *See also* [GEN 12:8](bibleref:01012008), [GEN 13:4](bibleref:01013004), [GEN 13:18](bibleref:01013018)

**[GEN 15:6](bibleref:01015006)** *See also* [ROM 4:18-4:22](bibleref:45004018-45004022); [HEB 6:15](bibleref:58006015) *Abraham's faith is seen in the length of time (25 years) that he waited for the realisation of the promise* ; [HEB 11:8-11:12](bibleref:58011008-58011012), [HEB 11:17-11:19](bibleref:58011017-58011019)

### He was obedient
**[GEN 22:1-22:19](bibleref:01022001-01022019)** *See also* [GEN 12:4](bibleref:01012004), [GEN 26:5](bibleref:01026005); [JAS 2:20-2:22](bibleref:59002020-59002022)

**[GEN 20:7](bibleref:01020007)** *See also* [GEN 18:16-18:33](bibleref:01018016-01018033) *As a prophet, God reveals his will to Abraham; as an intercessor, Abraham brings the revelation back to God and pleads for him to change his mind.*
