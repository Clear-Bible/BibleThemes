# 5077: Abraham, character of

Despite some shortcomings as a husband and father, his obedience, faith, hospitality and effective intercession were exemplary. Such qualities led to his being called “friend of God”.



## Abraham, the friend of God


### He was blessed by God
**GEN 14:18-14:19** *See also* GEN 21:22, GEN 24:1, GEN 24:35

### He worshipped God
**GEN 21:33** *See also* GEN 12:8, GEN 13:4, GEN 13:18

### He had faith in God
**GEN 15:6** *See also* ROM 4:18-4:22; HEB 6:15 *Abraham's faith is seen in the length of time (25 years) that he waited for the realisation of the promise*; HEB 11:8-11:12, HEB 11:17-11:19

### He was obedient
**GEN 22:1-22:19** *See also* GEN 12:4, GEN 26:5; JAS 2:20-2:22

### He was a prophet and intercessor
**GEN 20:7** *See also* GEN 18:16-18:33 *As a prophet, God reveals his will to Abraham; as an intercessor, Abraham brings the revelation back to God and pleads for him to change his mind.*

### He was called the friend of God
**JAS 2:23** *See also* GEN 15:6; 2CH 20:7; ISA 41:8

## Abraham's weaknesses as head of his household


### He was held responsible by God for his family
**GEN 18:19** *As the following references show, Abraham did not discharge this responsibility well.*

### He exploited his wife in a cowardly way
 *See also* GEN 12:10-12:20, GEN 20:1-20:18

### He fathered Ishmael as a result of wavering faith
 GEN 16:1-16:4

### He allowed Sarah to mistreat Hagar
 GEN 16:4-16:6, GEN 21:9-21:14

## Abraham's strengths as a member of society


### He was well-respected outside his household
 GEN 21:22, GEN 23:6

### He sought to resolve disputes amicably
 GEN 13:5-13:12, GEN 21:25-21:31

### His wealth was a source of prestige
**GEN 24:35** *See also* GEN 13:2, GEN 24:1

### He was honoured for his military prowess
 GEN 14:13-14:21

### He was concerned to maintain his independence from the Canaanites
**GEN 14:22-14:24** *See also* GEN 23:7-23:16, GEN 24:1-24:4

### He showed hospitality to strangers
**GEN 18:1-18:8**
