# 5842: eloquence

The ability to speak with fluency and persuasive power, using words appropriate to the circumstances.



## Examples of lack of eloquence
**EXO 4:10** *See also* EXO 6:12, EXO 6:30; 2CO 10:10, 2CO 11:6

## Preaching the gospel does not rely on human eloquence
**1CO 1:17** *See also* 1CO 1:19-1:21, 1CO 2:1, 1CO 13:1

## Eloquence that comes from God
**ACT 6:9-6:10** *See also* EXO 4:11, EXO 4:15-4:16; ISA 32:4; MRK 13:11; LUK 21:15; ACT 18:4, ACT 19:8-19:10

## Examples of eloquence in speeches
 ACT 5:35-5:40, ACT 17:22-17:23, ACT 18:24-18:28

## Eloquent words may lead to sin
**PRO 7:21** *See also* PSA 12:3-12:4, PSA 55:21; PRO 2:16, PRO 5:3, PRO 6:24, PRO 29:5

## Sayings concerning eloquence
**PSA 45:1** *See also* PRO 10:31-10:32, PRO 12:18, PRO 22:11; ECC 10:12
