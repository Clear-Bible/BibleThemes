# 7456: synagogue

A word meaning literally “a gathering together”. In the NT it sometimes refers to a group of Jews meeting for worship but most often to the building in which they met.

## Synagogue worship may have begun during the exile
**[EZK 14:1](bibleref:26014001)** *Unable to sacrifice in the temple, the community met together with spiritual leaders.*  *See also* [EZK 20:1](bibleref:26020001)

## A synagogue might be established anywhere
**[MRK 1:21](bibleref:41001021)** *See also* [ACT 6:9](bibleref:44006009) *in Jerusalem* [ACT 16:12-16:13](bibleref:44016012-44016013), [ACT 16:16](bibleref:44016016)
[ACT 17:1](bibleref:44017001), [ACT 17:10](bibleref:44017010), [ACT 18:19](bibleref:44018019)

## The role of the synagogue


### It was a focus for prayer and the reading of Scripture
**[ACT 13:15](bibleref:44013015)** *See also* [NEH 8:2-8:8](bibleref:16008002-16008008), [NEH 9:3](bibleref:16009003); [LUK 4:15-4:33](bibleref:42004015-42004033)

### It was a centre for community affairs
 [MAT 10:17](bibleref:40010017); [MRK 13:9](bibleref:41013009); [LUK 12:11](bibleref:42012011)

### Exclusion from the synagogue was used as a punishment
 [JHN 9:22](bibleref:43009022), [JHN 12:42](bibleref:43012042)

## The leading of synagogue worship


### It was the responsibility of the synagogue ruler
 [MRK 5:22](bibleref:41005022); [ACT 18:8](bibleref:44018008)

### Qualified laymen were allowed to teach
**[LUK 4:16](bibleref:42004016)** *See also* [ACT 13:15](bibleref:44013015)

## Attendance at the synagogue


### It was the practice of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 9:35](bibleref:40009035)** *See also* [MRK 6:2](bibleref:41006002); [LUK 4:16](bibleref:42004016); [JHN 6:59](bibleref:43006059)

### It was the practice of Paul and other Jewish Christians
**[ACT 9:19-9:20](bibleref:44009019-44009020)** *See also* [ACT 14:1-14:2](bibleref:44014001-44014002), [ACT 17:2](bibleref:44017002), [ACT 19:8](bibleref:44019008)

### The first Christians continued to worship in the synagogues
 [LUK 24:53](bibleref:42024053); [ACT 19:8-19:9](bibleref:44019008-44019009)
