# 3272: Holy Spirit, in OT

The OT portrays the Holy Spirit as being active in creation, in equipping individuals for skilled tasks and in inspiring prophecy and revelation.



## The Holy Spirit as the presence of God


### He is active in creation
**[GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002)** *See also* [JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004); [PSA 104:30](bibleref:19104030)

### He is present everywhere
**[PSA 139:7](bibleref:19139007)**

### His sovereignty
**[ISA 34:16](bibleref:23034016)** *See also* [EZK 36:27](bibleref:26036027), [EZK 37:14](bibleref:26037014)

### All life is dependent on the Spirit
**[JOB 34:14-34:15](bibleref:18034014-18034015)** *NIV footnote at verse 14.*  *See also* [GEN 6:3](bibleref:01006003)

### He is present with God's people
**[ISA 63:11-63:14](bibleref:23063011-23063014)** *See also* [NEH 9:20](bibleref:16009020); [HAG 2:5](bibleref:37002005)
