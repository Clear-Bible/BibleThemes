# 5967: thrift

Saving by the careful use of resources. In general this is encouraged in Scripture, though the dangers of meanness, materialism and anxiety are recognised.



## Encouragements to thrift


### To provide for the future
**PRO 21:20** *See also* PRO 6:6-6:8, PRO 10:5, PRO 12:27, PRO 27:23-27:27, PRO 30:25

### To provide for children
 PSA 17:14; ; PRO 13:22; ; 2CO 12:14

### To provide for others
 DEU 14:28-14:29; ; 1CO 16:2; ; EPH 4:28

## Examples of thrift


### Noah
**GEN 6:21**

### Joseph
**GEN 41:48** *See also* GEN 41:28-41:36, GEN 41:53

### Solomon
 1KI 9:17-9:19 parallel 2CH 8:4-8:6

### Jehoshaphat
 2CH 17:12

### Hezekiah
 2CH 32:27-32:29

### The good wife
 PRO 31:13-31:16

### Jesus Christ
**JHN 6:12-6:13**

## The dangers of thrift


### Meanness
**JHN 12:4-12:6** parallels MAT 26:8-26:9, MRK 14:4-14:5 *See also* PRO 3:27-3:28, PRO 11:24, PRO 11:26, PRO 23:6-23:8, PRO 28:22; ECC 5:13; 2CO 9:6

### Materialism
**LUK 12:15** *See also* ECC 4:8; MIC 6:14; LUK 12:16-12:21

### Anxiety
**LUK 12:22-12:31** parallel MAT 6:25-6:34

## Lack of thrift
**LUK 15:13** *See also* PRO 21:17, PRO 21:20, PRO 23:20-23:21, PRO 29:3; LUK 16:1

## Spiritual thrift
**MAT 6:19-6:21**; **EPH 5:15-5:16** *See also* PRO 10:14; 1TI 6:19

### The wicked store up judgment
**ROM 2:5-2:6** *See also* ROM 2:7-2:10; REV 18:6-18:7
