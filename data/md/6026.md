# 6026: sin, God's judgment on

Sin comes under the judgment of God, in that it contradicts his nature and opposes his purposes.



## God's judgment on sin


### God is the judge of sin
**[ISA 26:21](bibleref:23026021)** *See also* [PSA 99:8](bibleref:19099008); [ISA 13:11](bibleref:23013011); [AMO 3:14](bibleref:30003014); [ZEP 1:12](bibleref:36001012); [1TH 4:6](bibleref:52004006)

### God's judgment is certain
**[ROM 2:12](bibleref:45002012)** *See also* [PSA 37:38](bibleref:19037038); [PRO 11:21](bibleref:20011021); [HEB 9:27](bibleref:58009027)

### God's judgment is in proportion to the seriousness of the sin
**[JER 21:14](bibleref:24021014)** *See also* [DEU 25:2](bibleref:05025002); [ISA 59:18](bibleref:23059018); [MAT 7:1-7:5](bibleref:40007001-40007005); [LUK 12:47-12:48](bibleref:42012047-42012048); [ROM 2:5-2:6](bibleref:45002005-45002006)

### God's judgment under the Sinaitic covenant
**[EXO 20:5](bibleref:02020005)** parallel DEU 5:9 *The Sinaitic covenant makes references to the judgment of future generations for the sins of their parents but, as a principle, this is abolished by later prophets.*  [LEV 26:14-26:39](bibleref:03026014-03026039); [DEU 28:15-28:68](bibleref:05028015-05028068)
[JER 31:29-31:30](bibleref:24031029-24031030); [EZK 18:1-18:32](bibleref:26018001-26018032)

### John the Baptist and Jesus Christ warn of judgment
**[MAT 3:10](bibleref:40003010)** parallel LUK 3:9 *See also* [MAT 3:12](bibleref:40003012), [MAT 7:19](bibleref:40007019), [MAT 12:36-12:37](bibleref:40012036-40012037); [MRK 9:42-9:49](bibleref:41009042-41009049)
