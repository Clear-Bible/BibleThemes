# 5509: rulers

Officials who, acting as God's servants, govern others and exercise authority over them. Also the originators of the laws of their realms. In the NT, rulers are also spiritual powers.



## Officials exercising authority over people
**MAT 20:25** parallel MRK 10:42 *See also* 2CH 23:20; PSA 2:2; PRO 28:2; ISA 16:1; DAN 5:7; HOS 13:10; LUK 1:52, LUK 12:11; ACT 7:27; EXO 2:14; 1CO 2:6-2:8

### Godly rulers in foreign lands
 PSA 105:16-105:22; ; DAN 2:48

### Importance of submitting to civil authorities
 ROM 13:3; ; TIT 3:1

## Examples of rulers


### Rulers of areas apart from Israel
 GEN 25:16, GEN 34:2, GEN 41:41, GEN 45:8; ; 2CH 32:31; ; EZK 28:2, EZK 31:11; ; DAN 9:1; ; ACT 7:10, ACT 7:18

### The five Philistine rulers
**JOS 13:3** *The Hebrew word translated “rulers” is related to the word “tyrant” and is only used of Philistines rulers.* *See also* JDG 3:3, JDG 15:11, JDG 16:18; 1SA 5:8, 1SA 6:16, 1SA 29:2; 1CH 12:19

### The king of Israel is referred to as a ruler
**2SA 5:1-5:2** parallel 1CH 11:1-11:2 *See also* 1KI 1:34-1:35, 1KI 11:34; 1CH 5:2; MIC 5:1

### Other rulers of Israel (or parts of it)
 1KI 22:26 parallel 2CH 18:25; ; NEH 3:9; ; ISA 1:23; ; JER 30:21; ; MIC 3:1; ; MAT 2:6; ; LUK 18:18

### One of three groups that made up the Sanhedrin
**ACT 4:5** *See also* LUK 23:13, LUK 24:20; JHN 7:48; ACT 4:8, ACT 13:27

### Jesus Christ is described as a ruler
**REV 1:5** *See also* MAT 2:6; MIC 5:2; REV 3:14

### God the Father is also called a ruler
 1TI 6:15

## A ruler as the originator of the laws of his realm
 PRO 8:15

## Rulers are appointed by God
**ROM 13:1-13:2** *See also* 2SA 6:21, 2SA 7:7-7:8 parallel 1CH 17:6-17:7; 1KI 11:34; PSA 2:7; ISA 44:28; JER 27:6; DAN 2:38, DAN 4:17; JHN 19:11; ROM 9:17; EXO 9:16

## Rulers are God's servants
**ROM 13:4** *See also* ISA 45:1; JER 25:9, JER 43:10

## Rulers are God's representatives
**PSA 58:1** *The Hebrew word translated “rulers” literally means “gods” a title given to those whose position called for them to act as earthly representatives of God's heavenly court.* *See also* PSA 82:1; JHN 10:34-10:35; PSA 82:6; ACT 23:5; EXO 22:28

## Rulers as spiritual powers
**EPH 3:10** *See also* EPH 1:21 *In the NT, rulers are spiritual powers, some of whom exercise a Satanic authority*; EPH 6:12; COL 1:16

## Synagogue rulers
**MRK 5:22** parallels MAT 9:18, LUK 8:41 *The ruler of a synagogue looked after the building and supervised the worship in it.* *See also* MRK 5:35-5:36 parallel LUK 8:49-8:50; MRK 5:38; LUK 13:14; ACT 13:15, ACT 18:8, ACT 18:17
