# 5575: talk, idle

Loose or careless speech, often motivated by curiosity, which can lead to unacceptable consequences. Scripture emphasises the importance of avoiding careless talk, and considering one's words with care.



## Unfounded boasting is idle talk
**2PE 2:17-2:19** *See also* PSA 10:3, PSA 49:6, PSA 52:1; PRO 25:14, PRO 27:1; ACT 8:9-8:10; JAS 4:16

## Human questioning of God is idle talk
**ROM 9:20-9:21** *See also* ISA 29:16, ISA 45:9

## Flattery is idle talk
**ROM 16:18** *See also* PRO 7:21

## Meaningless conversation is idle talk
**JOB 27:11-27:12** *Job referring to the words of his comforters.* JOB 8:2, JOB 11:2, JOB 35:16; 
PRO 20:14

## Examples of idle talk
**1KI 20:10-20:11** *See also* NUM 12:1-12:2; JDG 9:26-9:41; 1SA 17:41-17:44

## Idle talk is folly


### A mark of the fool
**JOB 15:2-15:3** *See also* PRO 14:3, PRO 14:23; ECC 10:12-10:14; 1TI 1:6-1:7

### Talking too much
**ECC 5:7** *See also* PRO 10:19, PRO 17:28, PRO 21:23; ECC 5:3

### Speaking rashly
**PRO 20:25** *See also* ECC 5:6; JAS 1:19

## Idle talk is combated by upright living
**1PE 2:15**

## Idle talk has no place in the church
**1CO 4:20** *See also* EPH 4:29, EPH 5:4; TIT 1:10-1:11

## All idle talk will be judged
**MAT 12:36-12:37**
