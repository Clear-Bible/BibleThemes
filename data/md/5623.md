# 5623: witnesses, legal

God gave detailed instructions about the conduct of witnesses in the legal processes of Israel and also in the affairs of the church.



## God himself as a witness
 [GEN 31:50](bibleref:01031050); [MAL 2:14](bibleref:39002014)

## General legal requirements about witnesses


### A witness to a crime was required to give evidence
**[LEV 5:1](bibleref:03005001)** *See also* [PRO 29:24](bibleref:20029024)

### Two or more witnesses were required to prove someone guilty
**[DEU 19:15](bibleref:05019015)** *See also* [DEU 17:6](bibleref:05017006); [MAT 18:15-18:16](bibleref:40018015-40018016); [2CO 13:1](bibleref:47013001)

## The role of witnesses in capital cases


### At least two witnesses were required
**[NUM 35:30](bibleref:04035030)** *See also* [DEU 17:2-17:6](bibleref:05017002-05017006); [HEB 10:28](bibleref:58010028)

### Witnesses were required to participate in the execution
**[DEU 17:7](bibleref:05017007)** *See also* [LEV 24:13-24:16](bibleref:03024013-03024016); [DEU 13:6-13:11](bibleref:05013006-05013011); [ACT 7:57-7:58](bibleref:44007057-44007058)
