# 8366: wisdom, source of human

True wisdom belongs to God and may be given by him alone. It cannot be received by those who put confidence in worldly wisdom which is based upon human cleverness and insight without God's revelation and which will come to nothing.

### It is given by God alone
**[JOB 12:13](bibleref:18012013)** *See also* [EXO 28:3](bibleref:02028003); [JOB 38:36](bibleref:18038036); [ECC 2:26](bibleref:21002026); [JAS 3:17](bibleref:59003017)

## The revelation of divine wisdom


### It is revealed in Jesus Christ
**[1CO 1:23-1:24](bibleref:46001023-46001024)** *God's wisdom, which is hidden from those who rely on human wisdom, is the message of Christ crucified.*  *See also* [1CO 1:30](bibleref:46001030), [1CO 2:6-2:8](bibleref:46002006-46002008); [COL 1:15-1:17](bibleref:51001015-51001017)

### It is rejected by the worldly-wise
**[1CO 2:14](bibleref:46002014)** *See also* [1CO 1:18](bibleref:46001018), [1CO 1:22-1:23](bibleref:46001022-46001023)

**[MAT 11:25](bibleref:40011025)** parallel LUK 10:21**[1CO 3:18](bibleref:46003018)** *God's wisdom may be received only by those willing to give up all reliance on their own human insight and understanding.*  *See also* [1CO 1:26-1:27](bibleref:46001026-46001027)
