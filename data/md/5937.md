# 5937: rivalry

Viewing other people as a threat to oneself in the accomplishment of some aim, or being in active competition with others for recognition or achievement.



## Examples of rivalry at a national level


### Between Israel and Judah
 1KI 15:25-15:31 *This is typical of the conflict that existed for 80 years after the division of the kingdom.*

### Within Israel
**1KI 16:21-16:22**

### Between Assyria and Judah
 *See also* 2CH 32:13-32:22; ISA 37:10-37:13 *Assyria claimed that behind the rivalry of the nations lay the rivalry of their gods. The LORD vindicated himself in answer to Hezekiah's prayer (see Isa 37:14-38).*

## Examples of rivalry at an individual level


### Between Esau and Jacob
 GEN 25:29-25:34, GEN 27:41

### Between Peninnah and Hannah
**1SA 1:6-1:7**

### Between Saul and David
**1SA 18:7-18:9** *See also* 1SA 20:31

### Between Absalom and David
 2SA 15:1-15:13

### Between Rehoboam and Jeroboam
 1KI 12:26-12:27

## Condemnation of rivalry at an individual level


### By the Mosaic law
 LEV 18:18

### By Jesus Christ
**LUK 9:46-9:48** parallels MAT 18:1-18:4, MRK 9:34-9:37 *See also* MAT 20:20-20:24 parallel MRK 10:35-10:41; MAT 23:8-23:11

### By Paul
**1CO 3:3-3:4** *See also* 1CO 1:12-1:13

## Rivalry between individuals is overcome in Christ
**GAL 3:26-3:28** *See also* MRK 10:42-10:45 parallel MAT 20:25-20:28; PHP 1:15-1:18
