# 5493: retribution

Recompense for evil. In Scripture this is the prerogative of God and of the authorities ordained by God, and never of individuals.



## The principle of retribution
**EXO 20:5-20:6**; **ROM 12:19**; **HEB 10:30** *See also* DEU 32:35; EXO 32:33-32:34; LEV 26:23-26:25; DEU 7:9-7:10, DEU 32:41-32:43; JOB 34:11; PRO 24:12; ISA 1:24; NAM 1:2

## Divine retribution is just
**GEN 18:25** *See also* DEU 32:3-32:4; 2CH 12:1-12:6; PSA 58:10-58:11; JER 5:7-5:9, JER 5:26-5:29; HOS 4:6

## Retribution is often built into the nature of wrongdoing
**PSA 7:15-7:16** *See also* PSA 37:14-37:15; PRO 1:18, PRO 26:27, PRO 28:10; ECC 10:8; MAT 26:52; GAL 6:7-6:8

## Retribution is placed in the hands of governments
**GEN 9:6**; **EXO 21:23-21:25** *This “lex talionis” (law of exacting like for like) was not for private retribution. It insisted that justice be fair and not out of proportion to the crime*; **ROM 13:4** *See also* LEV 24:17-24:22; NUM 35:16-35:25; 1PE 2:14

## Private retribution is forbidden
**PRO 24:29** *See also* MAT 5:38-5:39; ROM 12:19

## Prayer for retribution
**PSA 28:4** *Christians can agree with these prayers, hating all that opposes the Lord and goodness, and gladly anticipating the ultimate vindication of God's glory. Christians leave judgment in God's hands, but nonetheless grieve over the world's pain and over the death of every unrepentant sinner*; **2TH 1:6-1:10** *See also* PSA 79:10, PSA 94:1-94:2, PSA 137:8-137:9; LAM 3:64-3:66

## National retribution
 JOS 10:13-10:14; ; JDG 11:36; ; EST 9:5 *The special case of God's dealings with the nation of Israel and her enemies is not a basis on which to argue for a concept of a war, just or otherwise.  A just war has to be in defence of the nation or of the defenceless, and involve a minimum of suffering.*
