# 5337: hiring





## Laws relating to hiring


### Hired workers must be paid on time
**LEV 19:13** *See also* DEU 24:14-24:15; JAS 5:4

### Regular rates of pay must be established
 LEV 25:50

### Hired workers are to be treated with kindness
 LEV 25:6, LEV 25:39-25:40, LEV 25:53

### Hired foreigners were excluded from the Passover
 EXO 12:43-12:45

### Hired workers must be carefully chosen
 PRO 26:10

### Loss of a hired animal is covered by the price
 EXO 22:15

## Various kinds of hiring


### A hired husband
 GEN 30:16

### A hired prophet
**DEU 23:4** *See also* NEH 6:12-6:13, NEH 13:2; JUD 1:11

### A hired priest
 JDG 17:10, JDG 18:4

### Hired soldiers
 JDG 9:4; ; 2SA 10:6; ; 2KI 7:6; ; 1CH 19:6-19:7; ; 2CH 25:6; ; JER 46:21 *No hired mercenary army was ever approved by the LORD (see 2Ch 25:7-10).*

### Other hirings
 1SA 2:5 *hirings for food*; ; 2CH 24:12 *masons and carpenters*; ; EZR 4:5 *counsellors*; ; ISA 7:20 *a razor, symbolising the judgment to be brought by the king of Assyria*; ; ISA 23:17 *prostitutes*; ; ISA 46:6 *goldsmiths*; ; MRK 1:20 *fishermen*

## Teachings illustrated by hired workers


### The brevity and hardness of life
**JOB 7:1-7:3** *See also* JOB 14:6

### Christian workers
**MAT 20:1-20:15** *This parable teaches that believers should not serve in a mercenary spirit but recognise that God's reward is based upon grace.*

### The severity of the world and the kindness of God
 *See also* LUK 15:15-15:19

## The difference between hired and good shepherds
**JHN 10:11-10:13** *See also* 1PE 5:2
