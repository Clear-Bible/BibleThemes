# 5337: hiring


## Laws relating to hiring


### Hired workers must be paid on time
**[LEV 19:13](bibleref:03019013)** *See also* [DEU 24:14-24:15](bibleref:05024014-05024015); [JAS 5:4](bibleref:59005004)

### Regular rates of pay must be established
 [LEV 25:50](bibleref:03025050)

### Hired workers are to be treated with kindness
 [LEV 25:6](bibleref:03025006), [LEV 25:39-25:40](bibleref:03025039-03025040), [LEV 25:53](bibleref:03025053)

### Hired foreigners were excluded from the Passover
 [EXO 12:43-12:45](bibleref:02012043-02012045)

### Hired workers must be carefully chosen
 [PRO 26:10](bibleref:20026010)

### Loss of a hired animal is covered by the price
 [EXO 22:15](bibleref:02022015)

## Various kinds of hiring


### A hired husband
 [GEN 30:16](bibleref:01030016)

### A hired prophet
**[DEU 23:4](bibleref:05023004)** *See also* [NEH 6:12-6:13](bibleref:16006012-16006013), [NEH 13:2](bibleref:16013002); [JUD 1:11](bibleref:65001011)

### A hired priest
 [JDG 17:10](bibleref:07017010), [JDG 18:4](bibleref:07018004)

### Hired soldiers
 [JDG 9:4](bibleref:07009004); [2SA 10:6](bibleref:10010006); [2KI 7:6](bibleref:12007006); [1CH 19:6-19:7](bibleref:13019006-13019007); [2CH 25:6](bibleref:14025006); [JER 46:21](bibleref:24046021) *No hired mercenary army was ever approved by the LORD (see 2Ch 25:7-10).*

 [1SA 2:5](bibleref:09002005) *hirings for food* ; [2CH 24:12](bibleref:14024012) *masons and carpenters* ; [EZR 4:5](bibleref:15004005) *counsellors* ; [ISA 7:20](bibleref:23007020) *a razor, symbolising the judgment to be brought by the king of Assyria* ; [ISA 23:17](bibleref:23023017) *prostitutes* ; [ISA 46:6](bibleref:23046006) *goldsmiths* ; [MRK 1:20](bibleref:41001020) *fishermen*
