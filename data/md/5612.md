# 5612: weapons

Instruments of warfare. God's people are to trust in him rather than in weapons. Scripture speaks of a day when weapons will become instruments of peace. The term is also used metaphorically.



## Weapons of warfare
**1CH 12:23, 1CH 12:33**; **NEH 4:16-4:18** *See also* GEN 49:5; 1SA 8:12, 1SA 17:45, 1SA 17:54, 1SA 21:8; 2SA 1:27; 2KI 11:8 parallel 2CH 23:7; 2KI 11:11 parallel 2CH 23:10; 1CH 12:34-12:37; 2CH 32:5; NEH 4:23; EZK 26:7-26:11, EZK 32:27; JHN 18:3

## Trust in God rather than in weapons
**PSA 20:7-20:8**; **ISA 31:1** *See also* DEU 1:41-1:44, DEU 20:1; 1SA 17:45-17:47; 2CH 20:20-20:24, 2CH 32:7-32:8; PSA 33:16-33:19, PSA 76:3, PSA 147:10-147:11; PRO 21:31; ECC 9:18; ISA 22:8-22:11, ISA 54:16-54:17; ZEC 4:6

## A day when weapons of war will become instruments of peace
**ISA 2:4** parallel MIC 4:3 *See also* PSA 46:9; ISA 9:5; EZK 39:7-39:10; HOS 2:18; ZEC 9:10

## Weapons used metaphorically by God
**ISA 59:15-59:18**; **JER 50:25** *See also* PSA 7:11-7:13; ISA 13:5; JER 51:20; EZK 9:1-9:8

## Weapons used metaphorically by Christians
**2CO 10:4**; **EPH 6:11-6:17** *See also* ROM 13:12; 2CO 6:7; 1TH 5:8
