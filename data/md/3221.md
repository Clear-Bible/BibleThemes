# 3221: Holy Spirit, and prayer

The Holy Spirit intercedes for God's people and also prompts their petitions, supplications and thanksgivings.



## The Holy Spirit enables God's children to address him as ``Abba''
**[ROM 8:15](bibleref:45008015)** *See also* [GAL 4:6](bibleref:48004006)

## The role of the Holy Spirit in prayer


### He prompts supplication
**[ZEC 12:10](bibleref:38012010)** *“a spirit” (NIV text) or “the Spirit” (NIV footnote).* 

### He inspires prayers of praise
**[LUK 10:21](bibleref:42010021)** *See also* [EPH 5:18-5:20](bibleref:49005018-49005020)

### He inspires praying in tongues
**[ACT 2:4](bibleref:44002004)** *See also* [ACT 10:44-10:46](bibleref:44010044-44010046), [ACT 19:6](bibleref:44019006); [1CO 14:14-14:18](bibleref:46014014-46014018)

### He intercedes for believers
**[ROM 8:26-8:27](bibleref:45008026-45008027)**

## The evidence of the Holy Spirit's work prompts praise and thanks
**[1TH 1:2-1:5](bibleref:52001002-52001005)** *See also* [ACT 11:15-11:18](bibleref:44011015-44011018)

## Believers are exhorted to pray in the Spirit
