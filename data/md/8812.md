# 8812: riches, ungodly use of

Ungodly people often make the possession of wealth a priority. They do not understand the true nature of wealth, and they do not realise that they will have to face God in judgment.



## The ungodly grow rich
**PSA 73:12** *See also* LUK 19:21; JAS 2:6-2:7

## The ungodly misuse riches


### They often obtain their riches unjustly
**PRO 10:2** *See also* 1KI 20:5; PRO 13:11, PRO 21:6, PRO 22:16; JER 17:11; MIC 2:1-2:5, MIC 6:10-6:12; HAB 2:6-2:11

### They accumulate and hoard their riches
**PRO 11:26** *See also* JOB 27:16-27:17; PRO 3:27; ECC 2:26, ECC 5:10-5:14; JAS 5:1-5:3

### They trust in their riches
**PSA 49:5-49:6** *See also* JOB 31:24; PSA 52:7; PRO 11:28; JER 9:23-9:24, JER 48:7

## The ungodly suffer difficulties because of their riches
**PRO 15:6** *See also* PRO 10:16, PRO 13:7-13:8, PRO 15:16-15:17

## The ungodly, like all people, must leave their riches to others
**PSA 49:10** *See also* PSA 39:6; ECC 2:18-2:21; LUK 12:20

## Jesus Christ warned the rich of their danger
**LUK 6:24** *See also* LUK 12:15, LUK 16:25

### Nabal
 1SA 25:2-25:3

### Rich young ruler
 MRK 10:17-10:23 parallels MAT 19:16-19:24, LUK 18:18-18:25
