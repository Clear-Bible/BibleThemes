# 5777: admonition

A loving attempt to correct another's attitude or behaviour. Christians are to admonish fellow believers.

## The prophets admonished Israel
**[2KI 17:13](bibleref:12017013)**; **[NEH 9:29-9:30](bibleref:16009029-16009030)** *See also* [JDG 6:7-6:10](bibleref:07006007-07006010); [MAL 2:1-2:4](bibleref:39002001-39002004)

## Parents are to admonish their children
**[PRO 22:6](bibleref:20022006)**; **[EPH 6:4](bibleref:49006004)** *See also* [GEN 18:19](bibleref:01018019); [DEU 11:19](bibleref:05011019); [PRO 13:24](bibleref:20013024), [PRO 19:18](bibleref:20019018), [PRO 22:15](bibleref:20022015), [PRO 23:13](bibleref:20023013)

## Leaders are to admonish those in their care
**[2TI 4:2](bibleref:55004002)** *See also* [1CO 4:14](bibleref:46004014); [COL 1:28](bibleref:51001028); [1TH 5:12](bibleref:52005012); [TIT 2:15](bibleref:56002015), [TIT 3:10](bibleref:56003010)
