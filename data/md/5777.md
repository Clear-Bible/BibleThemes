# 5777: admonition

A loving attempt to correct another's attitude or behaviour. Christians are to admonish fellow believers.



## The prophets admonished Israel
**2KI 17:13**; **NEH 9:29-9:30** *See also* JDG 6:7-6:10; MAL 2:1-2:4

## Parents are to admonish their children
**PRO 22:6**; **EPH 6:4** *See also* GEN 18:19; DEU 11:19; PRO 13:24, PRO 19:18, PRO 22:15, PRO 23:13

## Leaders are to admonish those in their care
**2TI 4:2** *See also* 1CO 4:14; COL 1:28; 1TH 5:12; TIT 2:15, TIT 3:10

## Christians are to admonish fellow believers
**COL 3:16** *See also* PSA 141:5; LUK 17:3; ROM 15:14; 1TH 5:14; 2TI 3:16

## It is wise to accept legitimate admonition
**PRO 13:18, PRO 25:12** *See also* PRO 15:5; ECC 7:5; JER 2:30, JER 5:3, JER 7:28; ZEP 3:1-3:2, ZEP 3:7-3:8
