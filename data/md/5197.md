# 5197: walking

The action of travelling on foot at a normal pace. The image of walking with God is used in Scripture to represent the behaviour of believers in their relationship with God.



## The physical activity of walking


### For travel
**GEN 13:17**; **DEU 6:7** *See also* JDG 5:10; 1KI 18:7; AMO 3:3; MAT 4:18; LUK 24:17

### As a miracle
**MAT 11:5, MAT 14:25-14:26** *See also* ACT 3:6-3:8, ACT 14:10

### God walking
**LEV 26:12** *See also* GEN 3:8

## Walking used figuratively of human behaviour


### Walking in sin and darkness
**1KI 15:26**; **PRO 2:12-2:13** *See also* PSA 1:1, PSA 82:5; PRO 2:12-2:13; 1JN 1:6

### Sometimes darkness represents depressing circumstances
**LAM 3:2** *See also* PSA 23:4; ISA 9:2, ISA 59:9

### Walking with God
**GEN 5:22-5:24, GEN 17:1**; **REV 3:4** *See also* GEN 6:9; DEU 8:6, DEU 10:12; 1KI 2:3-2:4; ISA 2:3; JER 7:23; MIC 4:5, MIC 6:8; ZEC 3:7; MAL 2:6

### Walking in the truth or in the light
**DEU 5:33**; **1JN 1:7** *See also* PSA 15:2, PSA 26:2-26:3, PSA 56:13, PSA 86:11, PSA 89:15; PRO 4:12; ISA 2:5; 2JN 1:4-1:6; 3JN 1:3-1:4; REV 21:24

### Walking as a way of living or behaving
**EPH 2:1-2:2** *See also* ROM 8:1; 2CO 5:7; EPH 4:1, EPH 5:2; COL 1:10
