# 7203: ark, Noah's

The craft built at God's command by Noah and his family as a means of escape from the flood. The ark is seen as corresponding to the redemption offered to believers through Jesus Christ.

## The significance of the ark elsewhere in Scripture


### An instance of judgment
**[MAT 24:38-24:39](bibleref:40024038-40024039)** parallel LUK 17:26-17:27 *Judgment clearly divides humanity into the saved and the lost.*  *See also* [2PE 2:5](bibleref:61002005)

### An example of faith
**[HEB 11:7](bibleref:58011007)** *Noah took God at his word in circumstances which must have appeared ridiculous.* 

### A symbol of salvation
**[1PE 3:20-3:21](bibleref:60003020-60003021)** *The link between the ark and baptism is in the twin ideas of water and salvation.*
