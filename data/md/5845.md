# 5845: emptiness

Scripture stresses the emptiness of human life without God, and notes that Jesus Christ chose to empty himself in order to redeem humanity.



## Emptiness and creation


### The world created to be filled
**ISA 45:18**; **HAB 2:14** *See also* GEN 1:2

### Emptiness as a symbol of judgment
**JER 4:23**

## Human life can be empty
**RUT 1:21** *See also* ECC 1:2, ECC 3:19, ECC 12:8

## Human words can be empty
**JOB 35:13** *See also* JOB 35:16; ISA 16:6, ISA 36:5 parallel 2KI 18:20; ISA 59:4; EPH 5:6; 2PE 2:18

## Human attempts at fulfilment are empty


### Work does not satisfy
**ISA 55:2** *See also* PSA 127:1-127:2; ECC 2:17, ECC 6:7; HAG 1:5-1:6

### Pleasure does not last
**JOB 20:5** *See also* PRO 21:17; ECC 2:1-2:3, ECC 2:25

### Wealth does not satisfy
**ECC 5:10** *See also* ECC 4:7-4:8; LUK 12:15; 1TI 6:9-6:10

### Material possessions are unreliable
**LUK 12:18-12:20** *See also* JOB 8:13-8:15, JOB 15:31; PSA 49:10, PSA 49:12, PSA 49:16-49:17; PRO 11:4; ECC 5:15; 1TI 6:7

### False religion cannot save
**ISA 57:13** *See also* JDG 10:14; ISA 44:17-44:18, ISA 45:20, ISA 46:7; JER 11:12; ACT 14:15

## Emptiness as the result of sin
**MIC 6:13-6:14** *See also* LEV 26:14-26:16; PSA 78:32-78:33; HOS 4:10; HAG 1:7-1:9

## God wants to remove the emptiness of people's lives
**1PE 1:18** *See also* PSA 81:10; JHN 1:16, JHN 10:10; EPH 4:13; COL 2:10

## Jesus Christ accepts emptiness in order to redeem humanity
**PHP 2:7** *See also* 2CO 8:9
