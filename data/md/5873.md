# 5873: habits

Set patterns of behaviour. These patterns can be helpful when they lead to spiritual discipline and a regular pattern of prayer and worship; others can be detrimental to faith.



## Examples of good habits


### Reading and obeying God's word
**PSA 119:56**; **HEB 5:14** *See also* PSA 119:44, PSA 119:97

### Private prayer
**LUK 5:16** *See also* PSA 5:3; MAT 6:5-6:6, MAT 14:23 parallel MRK 6:46; MRK 1:35 parallel LUK 4:42; LUK 6:12, LUK 9:18, LUK 11:1

### Attendance at public worship
 LUK 4:16; ; ACT 2:46, ACT 14:1, ACT 17:2

**HEB 10:25** *See also* 1TI 5:13
