# 1346: covenants, nature of

A solemn agreement or promise, sometimes confirmed by sacrifice or by sharing in a meal, by which two or more parties commit themselves to the rights and responsibilities demanded by their relationship and their agreed course of action, and accept the serious consequences of breaking faith.

## Sealing a covenant


### By sharing a meal
 [GEN 26:30](bibleref:01026030), [GEN 31:53-31:54](bibleref:01031053-01031054)[MAT 26:26-26:29](bibleref:40026026-40026029) parallels MRK 14:22-14:25, LUK 22:17-22:20; [1CO 11:23-11:25](bibleref:46011023-46011025)

### By offering a sacrifice
 [JER 34:18-34:19](bibleref:24034018-24034019) *The Hebrew expression for making a covenant means, literally, “to cut a covenant”. This may refer to the solemn practice, described here, of cutting an animal in half and walking between its pieces* ; [GEN 15:9-15:18](bibleref:01015009-01015018); [EXO 24:4-24:8](bibleref:02024004-02024008); [PSA 50:5](bibleref:19050005); [HEB 12:24](bibleref:58012024) *Sacrifice is an important part in sealing covenants made by God.*
