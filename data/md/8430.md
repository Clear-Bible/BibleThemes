# 8430: fasting, nature of

Scripture frequently refers to individuals fasting and describes the different kinds of fast which may be undertaken.



## Kinds of fasting


### Abstaining from food and drink
**EZR 10:6** *See also* DAN 9:3; ACT 23:12

### Abstaining from food only
**1SA 1:7** *See also* DAN 6:18

## The duration of fasting


### One day: the normal fast period
 LEV 23:32; ; JDG 20:26; ; 2SA 1:12

### Three days
 EST 4:16; ; ACT 9:9

### Seven days
 1SA 31:13 parallel 1CH 10:12; ; 2SA 12:16-12:20

### Forty days
These 40-day fasts are all in unique circumstances and are not prescribed:
 EXO 34:28; ; 1KI 19:8; ; MAT 4:2 parallel LUK 4:2

## Regular observances of fasting


### Those prescribed by the Law
**LEV 16:29-16:31** *NIV footnote at verse 29.* *See also* LEV 23:26-23:32; NUM 29:7; ACT 27:9

### Those observed in Jewish tradition
**ZEC 8:19** *See also* EST 9:30-9:31; ZEC 7:2-7:5

### Those observed by the Pharisees
 LUK 18:11-18:12

## Involuntary fasts


### Those imposed by decree
**2CH 20:3** *See also* 1SA 14:241KI 13:8-13:9, 1KI 13:16
1KI 21:9; JER 36:9

### Those imposed by circumstances
 ACT 27:33; ; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 11:27

## Voluntary fasts
**NEH 1:4** *See also* LUK 2:37
