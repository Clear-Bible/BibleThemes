# 8430: fasting, nature of

Scripture frequently refers to individuals fasting and describes the different kinds of fast which may be undertaken.



## Kinds of fasting


### Abstaining from food and drink
**[EZR 10:6](bibleref:15010006)** *See also* [DAN 9:3](bibleref:27009003); [ACT 23:12](bibleref:44023012)

### Abstaining from food only
**[1SA 1:7](bibleref:09001007)** *See also* [DAN 6:18](bibleref:27006018)

## The duration of fasting


### One day: the normal fast period
 [LEV 23:32](bibleref:03023032); [JDG 20:26](bibleref:07020026); [2SA 1:12](bibleref:10001012)

### Three days
 [EST 4:16](bibleref:17004016); [ACT 9:9](bibleref:44009009)

### Seven days
 [1SA 31:13](bibleref:09031013) parallel 1CH 10:12; [2SA 12:16-12:20](bibleref:10012016-10012020)

### Forty days
These 40-day fasts are all in unique circumstances and are not prescribed:
 [EXO 34:28](bibleref:02034028); [1KI 19:8](bibleref:11019008); [MAT 4:2](bibleref:40004002) parallel LUK 4:2

## Regular observances of fasting


### Those prescribed by the Law
**[LEV 16:29-16:31](bibleref:03016029-03016031)** *NIV footnote at verse 29.*  *See also* [LEV 23:26-23:32](bibleref:03023026-03023032); [NUM 29:7](bibleref:04029007); [ACT 27:9](bibleref:44027009)

### Those observed in Jewish tradition
**[ZEC 8:19](bibleref:38008019)** *See also* [EST 9:30-9:31](bibleref:17009030-17009031); [ZEC 7:2-7:5](bibleref:38007002-38007005)

### Those observed by the Pharisees
 [LUK 18:11-18:12](bibleref:42018011-42018012)

## Involuntary fasts


### Those imposed by decree

[1KI 21:9](bibleref:11021009); [JER 36:9](bibleref:24036009)

### Those imposed by circumstances
 [ACT 27:33](bibleref:44027033); [2CO 6:5](bibleref:47006005), [2CO 11:27](bibleref:47011027)
