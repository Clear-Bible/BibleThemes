# 5389: lending

Freely giving the temporary use of property or possessions at no personal advantage. Scripture lays down conditions for lending and emphasises the responsibilities that it brings.



## The motivation for lending


### Generosity
**PSA 37:25-37:26** *See also* DEU 15:11; PRO 3:27-3:28; MAT 5:42

### Pity
**DEU 15:7-15:8** *See also* LEV 25:35-25:36; MRK 14:7

### Not necessarily expecting repayment
 LUK 6:34-6:36

## The dangers of lending


### Domination
**PRO 22:7** *See also* DEU 15:6, DEU 28:12-28:13, DEU 28:43-28:44; NEH 5:4-5:5

### Loss
 EXO 22:14; ; 2KI 6:5; ; ISA 24:1-24:2

### Exploitation
**LEV 25:36-25:37**

### Usury
**EXO 22:25** *See also* DEU 23:19; PSA 15:5; EZK 18:8, EZK 18:12-18:13

## Safeguards for the lender


### Under the old covenant
**EXO 22:14** *This specific case probably represents a general principle of restitution governing all kinds of lending.*

### Under the new covenant
**LUK 6:30-6:31**

### Generosity may be risky but brings its reward
**PRO 19:17** *See also* PSA 112:5; MAT 5:7

## Jesus Christ was lent a donkey
 MAT 21:3 parallels MRK 11:3, LUK 19:31; ; LUK 19:34
