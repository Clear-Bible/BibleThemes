# 8800: prejudice

Seen mainly in Scripture as a bias against places, peoples and ideas. It is sometimes so firmly rooted as to resist all contrary evidence.



## Prejudice against places
**JHN 1:46** *See also* JHN 7:41, JHN 7:52

## Prejudice against people


### Prejudice due to an attitude of superiority
**JHN 7:49** *See also* 1SA 10:27, 1SA 25:10-25:11; JOB 30:1-30:10; ISA 65:5; LUK 18:11

### Prejudice based on fear or jealousy
 EXO 1:9-1:10, EXO 1:12-1:13; ; 1SA 18:8-18:9, 1SA 18:29

### Prejudice against Jesus Christ
**JHN 12:37** *See also* MAT 9:34; MRK 6:2-6:3; JHN 9:29, JHN 10:20

## Prejudice may be based on false assumptions about people's behaviour


### Hostility incorrectly assumed
**GEN 12:11-12:12** *See also* GEN 20:1-20:2, GEN 26:1-26:11

### Motives misunderstood
**NUM 32:5-32:9** *The leaders of Reuben and Gad chose to inhabit the fertile land in Transjordan but they were also prepared to cross the Jordan to help their brothers conquer the land.* *See also* NUM 32:14, NUM 32:17; JOS 22:10-22:24; 2SA 10:3

## Prejudice in terms of partiality


### Favouritism
 GEN 25:28, GEN 37:3, GEN 44:20, GEN 44:27-44:29

### People are often prejudiced in favour of their own kind
**MAT 5:46-5:47** parallel LUK 6:32-6:34 *See also* LUK 7:29, LUK 17:16; ACT 10:45; LUK 11:18

### Prejudice on grounds of outward appearance or behaviour
**JAS 2:3-2:4** *See also* 1SA 16:7; 2SA 14:25; ECC 9:16; JHN 8:15; ROM 14:1, ROM 14:3, ROM 14:10, ROM 14:13; 1CO 4:5; GAL 2:6

### Prejudice due to race
**EST 3:5-3:6** *See also* EZR 4:15-4:16; NEH 4:1-4:4; EST 3:8-3:14, EST 7:4; DAN 3:8-3:12, DAN 6:3-6:9, DAN 6:13

### Partiality forbidden
**EXO 23:3** *See also* LEV 19:15; JAS 2:9

## God is not prejudiced


### God is impartial
**ACT 10:34** *See also* ROM 2:11; EPH 6:9; COL 3:25

### Job falsely accuses God of partiality
 JOB 9:16, JOB 9:20, JOB 9:30-9:31, JOB 10:15-10:17

## Prejudice which no contrary evidence would change
**LUK 16:31** *See also* 1SA 22:13-22:15; MAT 11:21-11:23, MAT 21:32; ACT 7:52, ACT 13:41

## The Sadducees' prejudice against the resurrection
**MAT 22:23** parallels MRK 12:18, LUK 20:27 *See also* ACT 4:1-4:3, ACT 23:8
