# 5969: treachery

A betrayal of trust by a close companion, often involving a preconceived plan. Those found guilty of treachery are severely punished by God.



## Treachery against God
**ZEP 3:4** *See also* LEV 26:40; ISA 48:8, ISA 59:13

## Treachery against other people
**2SA 20:9-20:10** *See also* JDG 16:19 *Delilah against Samson*; 1SA 18:17 *Saul against David*; 2SA 3:27 *Joab against Abner*; 2SA 4:6 *Recab and Baanah against Ish-Bosheth*; 2SA 11:15 *David against Uriah*; 2SA 13:28-13:29 *Absalom against Amnon*; 1KI 21:7-21:13 *Jezebel against Naboth*; NEH 6:2 *Sanballat and Geshem against Nehemiah*; EST 3:8 *Haman against Mordecai*; MAT 26:49 parallels MRK 14:45, LUK 22:47-22:48 *Judas against Jesus Christ*; ACT 7:18-7:19 *Pharaoh against the Israelites*

## Punishment of the treacherous
**ACT 1:18** *See also* 2SA 18:14-18:15 *Absalom*; 1KI 2:31-2:34 *Joab*; 2KI 9:30-9:37 *Jezebel*; EST 7:10 *Haman*; PSA 25:3, PSA 59:5; ISA 33:1

## Treachery in the purposes of God
**ACT 4:27-4:28** *See also* GEN 50:20; JDG 9:22-9:24; 2KI 9:23, 2KI 11:14 parallel 2CH 23:13
