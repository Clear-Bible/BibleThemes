# 5263: communication

The effective conveyance of a message. God has communicated with humanity, especially through his Son. Christians are encouraged to communicate God's word effectively to others.



## God communicates with people


### Through his creation
**PSA 19:1-19:4** *See also* ROM 1:20

### Through the Scriptures
**2PE 1:20-1:21** *See also* JER 36:2; EZK 1:3; ZEC 7:12; 2TI 3:16

### Through his Son
**JHN 1:18**; **HEB 1:1-1:3** *See also* JHN 1:14; COL 1:15, COL 2:9

### Through the human conscience
 JHN 8:9; ; ROM 2:14-2:15

### Through the providential ordering of life
 ACT 14:16-14:17, ACT 17:26-17:27

### Through angelic appearances
 JDG 6:11-6:12, JDG 13:3; ; ZEC 1:9; ; MAT 1:20; ; LUK 1:11-1:13, LUK 1:28, LUK 2:9-2:11; ; ACT 8:26; ; REV 1:1

### Through dreams
 1KI 3:5; ; DAN 7:1; ; MAT 2:13

### Through visions
 ISA 1:1; ; EZK 11:24-11:25; ; DAN 8:1; ; OBA 1:1-1:21; ; ACT 10:3, ACT 10:9-10:17

### Through a donkey
 NUM 22:27-22:31

### Through miracles
 JHN 11:25, JHN 11:43-11:44; ; ACT 2:22 *The miracles of Jesus Christ were not merely wonderful occurrences; they were also signs that conveyed spiritual truth.*

## God has communicated directly with individuals
**EXO 3:2-3:6** *See also* EXO 33:9, EXO 33:21; 1KI 19:12-19:13

## Jesus Christ and communication


### Jesus Christ communicated his Father's will
**JHN 12:49** *See also* JHN 14:9-14:10, JHN 14:24, JHN 15:15, JHN 17:8

### Jesus Christ used parables in order to communicate with some but not with others
 MRK 4:10-4:12 parallels MAT 13:10-13:15, LUK 8:9-8:10; ; ISA 6:9-6:10; ; JHN 9:39

## The Holy Spirit and communication
**JHN 16:15** *See also* JHN 14:26, JHN 15:26, JHN 16:8, JHN 16:13; ROM 8:16; GAL 4:6; 1JN 3:24

## Christians are to communicate the good news to other people
**LUK 24:46-24:48** *See also* MAT 10:7, MAT 28:19-28:20; MRK 6:12, MRK 16:15, MRK 16:20; ACT 1:8, ACT 4:20, ACT 20:27, ACT 26:17-26:20; ROM 10:14-10:15; 1TH 1:8

## The failure of communication


### Inadequate perception of the truth
**MRK 8:16-8:21** *See also* MRK 4:13; JHN 1:10, JHN 8:27, JHN 10:6, JHN 13:22, JHN 14:9, JHN 16:18

### Wilful rejection of the truth
**EPH 4:18** *See also* JHN 8:44, JHN 8:47; ROM 1:21-1:25; HEB 3:12-3:13
