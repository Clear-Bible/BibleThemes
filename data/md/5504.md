# 5504: rights

The basis of a claim to respect, and just and fair treatment. God has rights over his creation as a result of his sovereignty and authority. Through being the height of his creation, human beings also have rights, which are to be respected.



## The rights that God gives


### God gives rights to the poor and needy
**EXO 22:22** *See also* EXO 22:25-22:27; LEV 19:9-19:10; JOB 36:6

### God gives rights to slaves
**LEV 22:11** *See also* EXO 21:2-21:6 parallel DEU 15:12-15:18; EXO 21:7-21:11, EXO 21:26-21:27

### God gives rights to strangers
**LEV 19:33-19:34** *See also* EXO 22:21; LEV 19:10

### God gives rights to those who commit manslaughter
**NUM 35:9-35:12** *See also* DEU 4:41-4:42; JOS 20:1-20:6

### God gives rights in marriage
**1CO 7:3-7:5** *See also* EXO 21:7-21:11; DEU 22:13-22:21; EPH 5:22-5:33

### God gives rights to parents
**COL 3:20** parallel EPH 6:1 *See also* EXO 20:12 parallel DEU 5:16; 1TI 5:4

### God gives rights to children
**HEB 12:16** *See also* GEN 25:29-25:34; DEU 21:15-21:17; 1CH 5:1-5:2; EPH 6:4

### God gives rights to Christians
**JHN 1:12** *See also* GAL 3:26-4:7; REV 2:7, REV 3:21, REV 22:14

### God gives rights to leaders
**1CO 9:18** *See also* 1CO 9:1-9:17; 1TI 5:17-5:18

## Why God gives rights


### God gives rights to keep an orderly society
**LEV 19:29** *See also* DEU 15:1-15:6

### God gives rights to protect people
**DEU 19:15** *See also* LEV 25:23-25:43, LEV 25:47-25:55

## The defence of rights


### God, the defender of rights
**MIC 7:8-7:10** *The implied speaker here is Israel.* *See also* DEU 10:18; PSA 68:5; PRO 23:10-23:11

### Abuse of rights punished by God
**ISA 10:1-10:4** *See also* JER 5:26-5:29; AMO 2:6-2:8

### Leaders are to protect the rights of others
**PSA 72:1-72:4** *See also* PRO 31:4-31:9; ECC 5:8

## The rights which God has


### God has a right to be worshipped
**2KI 17:36** *See also* MAT 4:10 parallel LUK 4:8; DEU 6:13; JHN 4:24

### God has a right to be obeyed
**DEU 10:12-10:13** *See also* DEU 26:16-26:18, DEU 30:16
