# 5321: furnace

A closed fireplace made of brick or stone for domestic or industrial usage. Used figuratively, it represents God's presence, testing and judgment.

## Everyday use of furnaces


### As a stove or oven
 [NEH 3:11](bibleref:16003011), [NEH 12:38](bibleref:16012038); [LAM 5:10](bibleref:25005010); [DAN 3:6](bibleref:27003006)

### As a kiln
 [EXO 9:8](bibleref:02009008)

### For smelting metals
 [PSA 12:6](bibleref:19012006); [PRO 27:21](bibleref:20027021)

## Figurative use of furnaces


### As a symbol of God's majestic presence
 [GEN 15:17](bibleref:01015017); [ISA 31:9](bibleref:23031009)

### As a symbol of affliction and testing
**[ISA 48:10](bibleref:23048010)** *See also* [DEU 4:20](bibleref:05004020); [1KI 8:51](bibleref:11008051); [PRO 17:3](bibleref:20017003); [JER 11:4](bibleref:24011004)

### As a symbol of God's wrath
**[MAT 13:42](bibleref:40013042)** *See also* [GEN 19:28](bibleref:01019028); [PSA 21:9](bibleref:19021009); [EZK 22:17-22:22](bibleref:26022017-26022022); [MAL 4:1](bibleref:39004001); [MAT 13:50](bibleref:40013050); [REV 9:2](bibleref:66009002)
