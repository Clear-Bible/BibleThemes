# 5463: proclamations

Edicts or public declarations issued by the civil or religious authorities.



## Examples of proclamations in biblical times
 EXO 1:15-1:22 *Pharaoh's edict that all Hebrew boys were to be drowned*; ; EXO 32:5 *Aaron's proclamation of a festival to the LORD*; ; 1KI 21:9-21:10 *Jezebel's proclamation to ensnare Naboth*; ; 2KI 10:20 *Jehu's proclamation of an assembly in honour of Baal*; ; 2CH 24:9 *Joash's proclamation of a tax for the LORD*; ; 2CH 30:1-30:10 *Hezekiah's invitation to celebrate the Passover*; EZR 1:1, EZR 5:13, EZR 6:3, EZR 7:13; 
NEH 8:14-8:15 *the Levites' proclamation of festival arrangements*; EST 1:20, EST 2:8, EST 3:14, EST 4:3, EST 8:11; DAN 6:7, DAN 6:15; 
JON 3:7 *the king of Nineveh's proclamation of a fast of repentance*; ; LUK 2:1 *Caesar Augustus' proclamation of a census*
