# 5463: proclamations

Edicts or public declarations issued by the civil or religious authorities.

## Examples of proclamations in biblical times
 [EXO 1:15-1:22](bibleref:02001015-02001022) *Pharaoh's edict that all Hebrew boys were to be drowned* ; [EXO 32:5](bibleref:02032005) *Aaron's proclamation of a festival to the LORD* ; [1KI 21:9-21:10](bibleref:11021009-11021010) *Jezebel's proclamation to ensnare Naboth* ; [2KI 10:20](bibleref:12010020) *Jehu's proclamation of an assembly in honour of Baal* ; [2CH 24:9](bibleref:14024009) *Joash's proclamation of a tax for the LORD* ; [2CH 30:1-30:10](bibleref:14030001-14030010) *Hezekiah's invitation to celebrate the Passover* [EZR 1:1](bibleref:15001001), [EZR 5:13](bibleref:15005013), [EZR 6:3](bibleref:15006003), [EZR 7:13](bibleref:15007013)
[NEH 8:14-8:15](bibleref:16008014-16008015) *the Levites' proclamation of festival arrangements* [EST 1:20](bibleref:17001020), [EST 2:8](bibleref:17002008), [EST 3:14](bibleref:17003014), [EST 4:3](bibleref:17004003), [EST 8:11](bibleref:17008011)[DAN 6:7](bibleref:27006007), [DAN 6:15](bibleref:27006015)
[JON 3:7](bibleref:32003007) *the king of Nineveh's proclamation of a fast of repentance* ; [LUK 2:1](bibleref:42002001) *Caesar Augustus' proclamation of a census*
