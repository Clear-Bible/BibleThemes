# 5523: servants, good

Scripture identifies certain qualities which are found in good servants and requires that these qualities should be found in believers' service of God.



## Qualities found in good servants


### Diligence
**ECC 9:10** *See also* 1KI 11:28; PRO 10:4, PRO 22:29; MAT 25:14-25:23 parallel LUK 19:12-19:19

### Faithfulness
**1CO 4:2** *See also* EXO 21:5-21:6; 2SA 15:21; 2CH 34:11-34:12; NEH 13:13; PRO 25:13; DAN 6:4

### Honesty
**2KI 22:7** *See also* GEN 39:6; 2KI 12:15; TIT 2:9-2:10

### Obedience
**EPH 6:5** *See also* LUK 17:10; COL 3:22

### Perseverance
**GEN 31:40-31:41** *See also* 2TH 3:12-3:13

### Respect
**1TI 6:1-6:2** *See also* MAL 1:6; 1PE 2:18

### Willingness
**EPH 6:6-6:7** *See also* 1CH 28:21; TIT 2:9

## Examples of good servants
Abraham's servant:
 GEN 24:9, GEN 24:49; 2SA 12:18, 2SA 23:13-23:17; 
2KI 5:13 *Naaman's servants*; ; MAT 8:9 *a centurion's servants*

## Good servants are often rewarded by their employers


### They are advanced by them
 GEN 39:4-39:5; ; PRO 27:18; ; MAT 25:21, MAT 25:23

### They have their confidence
 GEN 24:2-24:4, GEN 24:10

## Good servants often bring blessings on their employers
 GEN 30:27-30:30, GEN 39:2-39:4

## Good servants have God with them
 GEN 24:7, GEN 24:27, GEN 31:7, GEN 31:42, GEN 39:3, GEN 39:21; ; MAT 24:46; ; ACT 7:9-7:10
