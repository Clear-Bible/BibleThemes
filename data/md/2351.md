# 2351: Jesus Christ, miracles of

Supernatural acts of Jesus Christ, revealing and confirming his Messianic credentials, and the coming of God's kingdom. The miracles of Christ are to be seen as an integral part of his ministry.



## Examples of the kind of miracles Jesus Christ performed


### Authority over natural forces
**[MAT 8:27](bibleref:40008027)** parallels MRK 4:41, LUK 8:25 *See also* [MAT 14:19-14:20](bibleref:40014019-40014020) parallels MRK 6:41-6:43, LUK 9:16-9:17, JHN 6:11-6:13 *the feeding of 5,000* ; [MAT 14:25](bibleref:40014025) parallels MRK 6:48, JHN 6:19 *Jesus Christ walks on water* ; [MAT 17:27](bibleref:40017027); [LUK 5:4](bibleref:42005004), [LUK 5:6](bibleref:42005006); [JHN 2:9](bibleref:43002009) *Jesus Christ turns water into wine.* 

### Healing the sick
**[MAT 4:24](bibleref:40004024)** *See also* [MAT 8:13](bibleref:40008013) parallel LUK 7:10; [MAT 8:15](bibleref:40008015) parallels MRK 1:31, LUK 4:39 *Simon's mother-in-law* ; [MAT 9:22](bibleref:40009022) parallels MRK 5:29, LUK 8:44 *the woman with the issue of blood* ; [MAT 9:29-9:30](bibleref:40009029-40009030), [MAT 12:13](bibleref:40012013) parallels MRK 3:5, LUK 6:10 *the man with a shrivelled hand* ; [MRK 7:35](bibleref:41007035); [LUK 17:14](bibleref:42017014); [JHN 4:52-4:53](bibleref:43004052-43004053), [JHN 5:8-5:9](bibleref:43005008-43005009), [JHN 9:6-9:7](bibleref:43009006-43009007)

### Casting out demons
**[MRK 1:34](bibleref:41001034)** parallels MAT 8:16, LUK 4:41 *See also* [MAT 9:33](bibleref:40009033), [MAT 12:22](bibleref:40012022) parallel LUK 11:14; [MAT 17:18](bibleref:40017018) parallels MRK 9:25-9:26, LUK 9:42

### Raising the dead
**[LUK 7:14-7:15](bibleref:42007014-42007015)** *See also* [MAT 9:25](bibleref:40009025) parallels MRK 5:41-5:42, LUK 8:54-8:55; [JHN 11:43-11:44](bibleref:43011043-43011044)
