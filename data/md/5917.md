# 5917: plans

Designs or schemes, prepared in advance of doing something. Scripture indicates the sovereign plans of God and the varied plans of men and women.

### For judgment
**JER 18:11** *See also* NUM 33:55-33:56; 2KI 19:25-19:26; JOB 23:14-23:16; ISA 25:1-25:2, ISA 37:26-37:27; JER 26:3; LAM 2:17; MIC 2:3ISA 14:24-14:27, ISA 19:12, ISA 19:17, ISA 23:8-23:9; JER 49:20, JER 50:45; MIC 4:11-4:13

**EPH 1:11** *See also* PSA 40:5; HEB 11:40

### For the tabernacle and the temple
**EXO 26:30-26:37** *See also* 1CH 28:11-28:12, 1CH 28:18-28:19; EZK 43:10-43:11
