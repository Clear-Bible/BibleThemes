# 2021: Jesus Christ, faithfulness of

The total reliability and constancy of Jesus Christ is shown in his personal character, and made known by his words and works.



## Jesus Christ's faithfulness is seen in his character


### He is faithful by nature
**[REV 19:11](bibleref:66019011)** *A description of the exalted Christ.*  *See also* [REV 1:5](bibleref:66001005), [REV 3:14](bibleref:66003014)

### The promised Messiah is faithful
**[ISA 11:5](bibleref:23011005)** *See also* [ISA 42:3](bibleref:23042003)

### Jesus Christ is faithful because he never changes
**[HEB 13:8](bibleref:58013008)** *See also* [HEB 1:11-1:12](bibleref:58001011-58001012); [PSA 102:26-102:27](bibleref:19102026-19102027)

### Jesus Christ is faithful because he is the truth
**[JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014)** *“grace” and “truth” are often associated in Hebrew with love and faithfulness.*  *See also* [JHN 1:17](bibleref:43001017), [JHN 14:6](bibleref:43014006)

## Jesus Christ's faithfulness demonstrated in his obedience


### He is faithful to his Father
**[HEB 3:6](bibleref:58003006)** *See also* [LUK 2:49](bibleref:42002049); [JHN 5:30](bibleref:43005030), [JHN 6:38](bibleref:43006038), [JHN 8:29](bibleref:43008029), [JHN 14:31](bibleref:43014031); [HEB 3:2](bibleref:58003002)

### He kept faithfully to his work
**[LUK 4:43](bibleref:42004043)** parallel MRK 1:38 *See also* [JHN 4:34](bibleref:43004034), [JHN 9:4](bibleref:43009004), [JHN 12:27](bibleref:43012027), [JHN 17:4](bibleref:43017004), [JHN 19:30](bibleref:43019030); [HEB 2:17-2:18](bibleref:58002017-58002018)

## Jesus Christ's faithfulness is seen in his words


### In his predictions about events
**[MAT 16:28](bibleref:40016028)** parallels LUK 9:27, MRK 9:1 *This prediction could refer to either Jesus Christ's transfiguration or his ascension* ; **[MAT 24:2](bibleref:40024002)** parallels MRK 13:2, LUK 21:6 *This prediction was fulfilled in A.D. 70 when the Romans destroyed Jerusalem and the temple.*  *See also* [MAT 21:2](bibleref:40021002) parallels MRK 11:2, LUK 19:30 *finding a donkey and her colt* ; [MAT 26:21](bibleref:40026021) parallels MRK 14:18, LUK 22:21, JHN 13:21 *Jesus Christ's betrayer* ; [MAT 26:34](bibleref:40026034) parallels MRK 14:30, LUK 22:34, JHN 13:38 *Peter's denial* ; [JHN 16:20](bibleref:43016020), [JHN 21:18-21:19](bibleref:43021018-43021019)

### In his promises about eternal life
**[JHN 5:24](bibleref:43005024)** *See also* [MAT 19:28-19:29](bibleref:40019028-40019029) parallels MRK 10:29-10:30, LUK 18:29-18:30; [LUK 23:43](bibleref:42023043); [JHN 6:47](bibleref:43006047), [JHN 6:53-6:54](bibleref:43006053-43006054), [JHN 8:51](bibleref:43008051)

### In his promise of authority for believers
**[MAT 17:20](bibleref:40017020)** *See also* [MAT 16:19](bibleref:40016019), [MAT 18:18](bibleref:40018018), [MAT 21:21](bibleref:40021021) parallel MRK 11:23; [MAT 28:18-28:19](bibleref:40028018-40028019); [LUK 10:19](bibleref:42010019)

### In his promise about the Holy Spirit
**[JHN 14:26](bibleref:43014026)** *See also* [LUK 24:49](bibleref:42024049); [JHN 14:16](bibleref:43014016), [JHN 15:26](bibleref:43015026), [JHN 16:7](bibleref:43016007); [ACT 1:4](bibleref:44001004), [ACT 1:8](bibleref:44001008), [ACT 2:38-2:39](bibleref:44002038-44002039), [ACT 10:44-10:46](bibleref:44010044-44010046); [ROM 5:5](bibleref:45005005); [1CO 12:7](bibleref:46012007)
