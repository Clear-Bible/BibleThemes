# 5888: inferiority

A discouraging sense of being of less importance or value in comparison with others.



## Inferiority as a depressing lack of self-worth
**JDG 6:15**; **PSA 22:6**; **1CO 2:3** *See also* 1SA 18:23; NEH 4:4, NEH 6:9; PSA 31:9-31:10, PSA 70:5, PSA 74:21, PSA 86:1, PSA 109:22, PSA 119:25, PSA 119:141; ECC 9:16; ISA 33:8; JER 22:28; LAM 1:11; MAL 2:9

## Inferiority as a healthy sense of unworthiness
**ISA 6:5**; **MAT 5:3**; **LUK 5:8** *See also* 2SA 12:13; JOB 42:6; PSA 38:1-38:22, PSA 51:3-51:5; REV 1:17, REV 3:17

## God's judgment will bring inferiority on on proud nations
**OBA 1:2** *See also* ISA 16:14; JER 49:15; EZK 29:15

## Refusing to be intimidated by a sense of inferiority
**JOB 12:3**; **1TI 4:12** *See also* JOB 13:2; 2CO 11:5, 2CO 12:11; TIT 2:15

## God values those regarded as inferior and uses them
**DEU 7:7-7:8**; **1CO 1:27-1:28** *See also* 1SA 2:8; PSA 12:5, PSA 34:6, PSA 35:10, PSA 36:7, PSA 40:17, PSA 72:13, PSA 136:23, PSA 140:12; ISA 11:4, ISA 25:4, ISA 29:19, ISA 41:14, ISA 49:7, ISA 61:1-61:3; HOS 11:1-11:4

## Jesus Christ valued those traditionally regarded as inferior
 MAT 8:2-8:4 *the diseased and ritually unclean*; ; MAT 9:10-9:13 *the morally unacceptable*; ; MAT 15:21-15:29 *non-Jews*; ; MAT 19:13-19:14 *children*; ; LUK 18:13 *those who are ashamed of their sins*; ; JHN 4:9 *women*

## Believers are to help and encourage those who feel inferior
**LUK 14:13**; **ROM 12:16** *See also* PSA 82:3-82:4; 1TH 5:14; HEB 12:12-12:13
