# 8162: spiritual vitality

The quality of possessing a living faith, which is commended by Scripture.

## Spiritual vitality commended
**[2TH 1:3](bibleref:53001003)** *See also* [JOB 17:9](bibleref:18017009); [PSA 84:7](bibleref:19084007), [PSA 92:12](bibleref:19092012); [PRO 4:18](bibleref:20004018); [1TI 4:15](bibleref:54004015); [2PE 1:5-1:7](bibleref:61001005-61001007)

**[JHN 15:4](bibleref:43015004)**
