# 8162: spiritual vitality

The quality of possessing a living faith, which is commended by Scripture.



## Spiritual vitality commended
**2TH 1:3** *See also* JOB 17:9; PSA 84:7, PSA 92:12; PRO 4:18; 1TI 4:15; 2PE 1:5-1:7

## The means of developing spiritual vitality


### Abiding in Christ
**JHN 15:4**

### Seeking to imitate Jesus Christ
**EPH 4:15** *See also* JHN 13:15; ROM 12:1-12:2, ROM 13:14; 1CO 11:1; GAL 3:27; PHP 3:10; 1TH 1:6; 1PE 2:21; 1JN 2:6, 1JN 2:28

### Resisting temptation
**1JN 2:14** *See also* MAT 26:41; 1CO 7:5, 1CO 10:13; 1TH 3:5; HEB 2:18; JAS 1:2-1:3, JAS 1:12; REV 3:10

### Developing discipline
**1TI 6:12** *See also* HEB 12:7-12:11; REV 3:18-3:19

### Exhorting others
 1KI 2:2; ; 2CH 15:7; ; ISA 35:4; ; HAG 2:4; ; 1CO 16:13; ; EPH 6:10; ; 2TI 2:1

### Being filled with the Holy Spirit
**EPH 5:18** *See also* GAL 5:16-5:18; 1TH 1:5

## Obstacles to spiritual vitality


### Lack of sound teaching
 2TI 4:1-4:3

### A failure to progress beyond basic teachings
 HEB 5:12; ; 1PE 2:2

## Examples of individuals with spiritual vitality
 1SA 2:26 *Samuel*; ; LUK 1:80 *John the Baptist*; LUK 2:40, LUK 2:52; 
ACT 9:22 *Paul*

## The consequences of spiritual vitality


### Spiritual growth
**GAL 5:22-5:23**; **2PE 3:18** *See also* ROM 12:1-12:2; 2CO 3:18; EPH 4:11-4:15; PHP 3:12; COL 1:10; 1TH 4:3-4:7

### Loving others
**1JN 3:23** *See also* GAL 5:6; EPH 1:15, EPH 6:23; COL 1:4-1:5; 1TI 1:5

### Rejoicing in the Lord
**PHP 4:4-4:7** *See also* PSA 5:11, PSA 13:5, PSA 33:21; ISA 35:10; ACT 16:34; 1PE 4:12-4:13

### Spiritual perseverance
**HEB 6:12** *See also* 1TH 5:8; 1TI 3:9; 2TI 1:13; REV 13:10
