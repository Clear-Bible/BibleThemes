# 5580: thirst

Water is basic to human life. In Scripture the physical need to drink is often used as a picture of human spiritual need that only God can satisfy.



## Physical thirst quenched
**EXO 17:1-17:6** *See also* NUM 20:2-20:11; DEU 8:15; JDG 15:18-15:19; NEH 9:15, NEH 9:20; PSA 78:15-78:16, PSA 105:41, PSA 107:4-107:9, PSA 107:33, PSA 107:35, PSA 114:8; ISA 48:21

## The satisfaction of spiritual thirst


### It is to be found in God
**ISA 41:17-41:18** *See also* PSA 23:1-23:2; ISA 12:2-12:3, ISA 35:6-35:7, ISA 58:11

### It is experienced through Jesus Christ
**1CO 10:3-10:4** *See also* JHN 4:7-4:14, JHN 6:35

### It is known by the indwelling of the Holy Spirit
**JHN 7:38-7:39**

### The blessing is mediated through believers to others
 *See also* ISA 32:2; JHN 7:38

## There is no satisfaction of spiritual thirst apart from God
**JER 2:13** *See also* ISA 65:13; JER 2:17-2:18; 2PE 2:17 *False teachers promise blessing and refreshment to others, but prove to be empty and worthless.*

## Invitations to seek satisfaction for spiritual thirst in God
**ISA 55:1** *See also* JHN 7:37; REV 22:17

## Those seeking satisfaction for spiritual thirst in God


### Examples of seekers
**PSA 42:1-42:2** *See also* PSA 38:9, PSA 63:1, PSA 143:6; ISA 26:9

### They will be rewarded
**MAT 5:6**

### Heaven is their final reward
**REV 7:16-7:17** *See also* EZK 47:1-47:12; ZEC 14:8-14:9; 2CO 5:2; 2TI 4:8; HEB 11:16; REV 21:6-21:7, REV 22:1-22:3
