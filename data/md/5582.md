# 5582: tiredness

A natural outcome of work and stress for which God has provided relief, rest and renewal.  Scripture recognises that tiredness may have spiritual, as well as physical, causes.



## Causes of tiredness


### Work
**MAT 8:24** parallels MRK 4:38, LUK 8:23 *See also* ECC 10:15, ECC 12:12

### Afflictions and stress
**JOB 16:7** *See also* 2SA 17:29; 1KI 19:3-19:5; PSA 69:3; 2CO 12:15

### Concern and anxiety
**PSA 31:9-31:10** *See also* PSA 6:6, PSA 32:3-32:4, PSA 119:28; JER 45:3; LUK 22:45

### Desire for money and possessions
**PRO 23:4**

### Travelling
 *See also* DEU 25:18; JDG 8:4; 2SA 16:14; JHN 4:6

### Old age
 ECC 12:3

### Zeal for the Lord's work
 PSA 119:139

### Battles
 JDG 4:21; ; 1SA 14:31; ; 2SA 21:15, 2SA 23:10; ; JER 51:30

### Sin and rebellion
 ISA 57:10; ; JER 9:5; ; EZK 23:43

### Foolishness
 ECC 1:8; ; ISA 46:1, ISA 47:13; ; JER 12:13

## Help from God to deal with tiredness


### God's promises of help and rest
**ISA 40:28-40:31** *See also* ISA 46:4; 2CO 9:8

### Jesus Christ offers rest and encouragement
**MAT 11:28-11:30**; **REV 2:2-2:3** *See also* MRK 6:31

### Trusting God as the antidote to anxiety and sorrow
**MAT 6:25-6:27** parallel LUK 12:25-12:27 *See also* PSA 119:28

### God's provision of times for rest
 GEN 1:5, GEN 2:2 parallels EXO 20:8-20:11, DEU 5:12-5:15; ; PSA 127:2; ; JER 31:25
