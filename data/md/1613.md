# 1613: Scripture, purpose of

Scripture has been given by God to lead people to faith and salvation. Through Scripture believers are nurtured in faith and led to spiritual maturity.



## Scripture is intended to lead people to salvation
**2TI 3:14-3:15** *See also* PSA 19:7-19:11; JHN 20:30-20:31; ROM 10:8

## Scripture is intended to lead believers to maturity in faith


### By its teaching
**2TI 3:16** *See also* DEU 6:6-6:9; PSA 19:7-19:8, PSA 119:9, PSA 119:130; COL 3:16

### By its rebuke and correction
**2TI 3:16** *See also* PSA 19:11-19:13; 1CO 10:11-10:12; HEB 4:12-4:13

### By training in righteousness
**2TI 3:16** *See also* DEU 29:29

### By its illumination
**PSA 119:105, PSA 119:130** *See also* 2PE 1:19; 1JN 2:8

### By its encouragement and reassurance
**ROM 15:4**; **1JN 5:13** *See also* PSA 19:8-19:9, PSA 119:50-119:51, PSA 119:76; HEB 12:5-12:6

### By its record of God's promises
**1KI 8:56**; **PSA 119:140** *See also* EZK 12:25; LUK 24:44; 2CO 1:19-1:22

### By its trustworthiness
**1KI 17:24**; **PSA 19:7-19:11, PSA 33:4** *See also* PSA 119:151, PSA 119:160; JHN 21:24; REV 21:5

## Scripture is essential for spiritual growth and maturity
**PSA 1:1-1:3** *See also* MAT 4:4; JHN 15:5-15:8, JHN 17:17; EPH 6:10-6:17; 2TI 3:14-3:17
