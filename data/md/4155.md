# 4155: divination

Attempts to discover the unknown or the future by supernatural means, involving communication with the spirit world.  It is expressly forbidden by God and has disastrous consequences.

## Divination was part of early beliefs and practices
 [GEN 41:24](bibleref:01041024); [EXO 7:11](bibleref:02007011); [ISA 31:1-31:2](bibleref:23031001-23031002), [ISA 47:9](bibleref:23047009); [JER 27:9](bibleref:24027009); [EZK 21:21](bibleref:26021021); [DAN 2:2](bibleref:27002002), [DAN 5:7](bibleref:27005007)
