# 8618: prayerfulness

An attitude by which a constant relationship between God and believers is developed.



## Prayerfulness is a way of life
**[LUK 2:37](bibleref:42002037)** *See also* [PSA 5:3](bibleref:19005003), [PSA 55:17](bibleref:19055017), [PSA 109:4](bibleref:19109004); [DAN 6:10-6:11](bibleref:27006010-27006011), [DAN 6:13](bibleref:27006013)

**[PSA 130:5-130:6](bibleref:19130005-19130006)** *See also* [PSA 42:1-42:4](bibleref:19042001-19042004), [PSA 84:1-84:2](bibleref:19084001-19084002)

## Prayerfulness arises from an awareness of need that can only be met by God
**[1TI 5:5](bibleref:54005005)** *See also* [PSA 86:1](bibleref:19086001), [PSA 105:4](bibleref:19105004)

## Prayerfulness demonstrates a continuing trust in God
**[JHN 15:5-15:8](bibleref:43015005-43015008)** *See also* [PSA 63:1-63:8](bibleref:19063001-19063008)

## Prayerfulness involves a heart that is right with God
