# 5236: bow and arrow

A weapon used for hunting and in warfare. In the Scriptures the tongue is likened to a bow and unfaithful Israel to a faulty bow. The bow is also symbolic of military power.



## The bow and arrow used for hunting
**GEN 27:3** *See also* ISA 7:24

## The bow and arrow used in warfare
**GEN 48:22**; **1KI 22:34** parallel 2CH 18:33 *See also* JOS 24:12; 1SA 2:4, 1SA 18:4; 2SA 1:22, 2SA 22:35 parallel PSA 18:34; 2KI 9:24; 2CH 14:8 *Asa's army*; 2CH 26:14; NEH 4:13, NEH 4:16; ISA 5:28, ISA 13:18; JER 6:23, JER 50:14, JER 50:29

## The tongue likened to a bow
**JER 9:3** *See also* PSA 64:2-64:4

## Unfaithful Israel likened to a faulty bow
**PSA 78:57** *See also* HOS 7:16

## The bow as a symbol of power and strength


### Of military power
**PSA 44:6-44:7, PSA 60:4** *See also* GEN 49:24; PSA 46:9; HOS 1:5, HOS 1:7, HOS 2:18; ZEC 9:10

### Of God's power in judgment
**PSA 7:11-7:12** *See also* PSA 21:11-21:12; LAM 2:4, LAM 3:12; HAB 3:9; ZEC 9:13; REV 6:2

### Of personal strength and vigour
**JOB 29:20** *See also* JOB 30:11
