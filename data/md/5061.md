# 5061: sanctity of life

Scripture treats human life as a divine gift and something for which humans are responsible. It is thus to be valued and respected. No human being has the right to take the life of another.

## The sanctity of all human life


### It proceeds from God
**[GEN 2:7](bibleref:01002007)**; **[ACT 17:25](bibleref:44017025)**

### It is precious to God
**[GEN 1:27](bibleref:01001027)** *See also* [GEN 4:10-4:11](bibleref:01004010-01004011); [2SA 18:12-18:13](bibleref:10018012-10018013); [MAT 6:25-6:26](bibleref:40006025-40006026)

### It is protected by God
**[DEU 22:8](bibleref:05022008)** *See also* [GEN 4:15](bibleref:01004015); [NUM 35:11-35:12](bibleref:04035011-04035012); [JER 22:3](bibleref:24022003)

### Death is by God's appointment
**[HEB 9:27](bibleref:58009027)** *See also* [JOB 14:5](bibleref:18014005); [PSA 90:3](bibleref:19090003), [PSA 90:5](bibleref:19090005)

### God prohibits unlawful taking of life
**[EXO 20:13](bibleref:02020013)** parallel DEU 5:17 *See also* [MAT 19:18](bibleref:40019018); [ROM 13:9](bibleref:45013009) *So precious is human life that some situations which required the death penalty in the OT are dealt with differently in the NT (e.g., Jn 8:5,11).* 

### God punishes unlawful taking of life
**[GEN 9:6](bibleref:01009006)** *See also* [EXO 21:14](bibleref:02021014); [2SA 12:9](bibleref:10012009); [2KI 21:16](bibleref:12021016)
