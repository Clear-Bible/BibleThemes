# 5137: bones

Associated in Hebrew thought with both death and life. A symbol of the whole person, and also the state of one's well-being.



## Bones represent dead bodies


### Human remains to be treated with respect
**1SA 31:13** parallel 1CH 10:12 *See also* 2SA 21:12-21:14; 1KI 13:31; 2KI 23:18; EZK 39:15

### Non-burial as a sign of disrespect
**AMO 2:1-2:2**

### Non-burial as a sign of God's judgment
**PSA 53:5** *See also* 2KI 9:34-9:37; PSA 141:7; JER 8:1-8:2; EZK 24:4-24:5, EZK 24:10

## Bones were considered to be unclean


### Bones made anyone who touched them unclean
**NUM 19:16** *See also* NUM 19:18; MAT 23:27

### Bones used to desecrate objects of pagan worship
**2KI 23:14** *See also* 1KI 13:2; 2KI 23:16, 2KI 23:20; 2CH 34:5; EZK 6:5

## Bones as a sign of hope


### The hope of God's promise
**GEN 50:25** *See also* EXO 13:19; JOS 24:32; HEB 11:22 *The presence of Joseph's bones among the Israelites was a constant reminder of God's promise to Joseph concerning the promised land.*

### The hope of life after death
**EZK 37:1-37:14** *See also* 2KI 13:21

### The hope of the Passover lamb
**JHN 19:36** *See also* EXO 12:46; NUM 9:12; PSA 34:20 *Jesus Christ fulfilled the symbolism of the unbroken bones of the Passover lamb.*

## Bones represent living individuals


### Bones indicate a living body
**LUK 24:39**

### Bones represent a whole person
**PSA 35:10** *“My whole being” is literally “my bones”.* *See also* JOB 2:4-2:5, JOB 10:11; PSA 6:2, PSA 51:7-51:8; ISA 66:14

### Bones represent kinship
**GEN 2:23** GEN 29:14; ; JDG 9:2; ; 2SA 5:1 parallel 1CH 11:1; ; 2SA 19:12-19:13

## Bones represent physical health


### Bones associated with good health
**PRO 15:30** *See also* PRO 3:7-3:8, PRO 16:24

### Bones symbolise strength and vigour
**JOB 21:23-21:24** *See also* JOB 20:11, JOB 40:18; PRO 25:15

### Bones associated with God's protection
**PSA 34:19-34:20** *See also* JHN 19:36

### Mere skin and bone represents bad health
**JOB 19:20** *See also* JOB 33:21; PSA 102:5; LAM 4:8

### Broken bones symbolise defeat or oppression
**NUM 24:8** *See also* ISA 38:13; JER 50:17; LAM 3:4; DAN 6:24; MIC 3:2-3:3

### Health can be damaged by negative attitudes
**PRO 14:30** *See also* JOB 4:14; PRO 12:4, PRO 17:22; HAB 3:16

### Health can be damaged by sin
**PSA 38:3** *See also* PSA 32:3, PSA 109:18; LAM 1:13

### Repentance brings restoration
**PSA 6:2** *See also* PSA 51:18

### Health can be damaged through obedience to God's will
**JER 23:9** *See also* JOB 30:17, JOB 33:19; PSA 22:14, PSA 22:17, PSA 31:10, PSA 42:10; JER 20:9
