# 5137: bones

Associated in Hebrew thought with both death and life. A symbol of the whole person, and also the state of one's well-being.

## Bones represent dead bodies


### Human remains to be treated with respect
**[1SA 31:13](bibleref:09031013)** parallel 1CH 10:12 *See also* [2SA 21:12-21:14](bibleref:10021012-10021014); [1KI 13:31](bibleref:11013031); [2KI 23:18](bibleref:12023018); [EZK 39:15](bibleref:26039015)

### Non-burial as a sign of disrespect
**[AMO 2:1-2:2](bibleref:30002001-30002002)**

### Non-burial as a sign of God's judgment
**[PSA 53:5](bibleref:19053005)** *See also* [2KI 9:34-9:37](bibleref:12009034-12009037); [PSA 141:7](bibleref:19141007); [JER 8:1-8:2](bibleref:24008001-24008002); [EZK 24:4-24:5](bibleref:26024004-26024005), [EZK 24:10](bibleref:26024010)

## Bones were considered to be unclean


### Bones made anyone who touched them unclean
**[NUM 19:16](bibleref:04019016)** *See also* [NUM 19:18](bibleref:04019018); [MAT 23:27](bibleref:40023027)

### Bones used to desecrate objects of pagan worship
**[2KI 23:14](bibleref:12023014)** *See also* [1KI 13:2](bibleref:11013002); [2KI 23:16](bibleref:12023016), [2KI 23:20](bibleref:12023020); [2CH 34:5](bibleref:14034005); [EZK 6:5](bibleref:26006005)

## Bones as a sign of hope


### The hope of God's promise
**[GEN 50:25](bibleref:01050025)** *See also* [EXO 13:19](bibleref:02013019); [JOS 24:32](bibleref:06024032); [HEB 11:22](bibleref:58011022) *The presence of Joseph's bones among the Israelites was a constant reminder of God's promise to Joseph concerning the promised land.* 

### The hope of life after death
**[EZK 37:1-37:14](bibleref:26037001-26037014)** *See also* [2KI 13:21](bibleref:12013021)

### The hope of the Passover lamb
**[JHN 19:36](bibleref:43019036)** *See also* [EXO 12:46](bibleref:02012046); [NUM 9:12](bibleref:04009012); [PSA 34:20](bibleref:19034020) *Jesus Christ fulfilled the symbolism of the unbroken bones of the Passover lamb.*
