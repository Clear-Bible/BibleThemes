# 8638: benedictions

Prayers invoking God's blessing upon others. They are often spoken at a time of impending separation, although they may sometimes constitute greetings.



## Benedictions pronounced by priests
**NUM 6:23-6:27** *See also* DEU 21:5; 1SA 2:20; 2CH 30:27

## Benedictions pronounced by kings
**1KI 8:54-8:61** *See also* GEN 14:18-14:20; 2SA 6:20; 2CH 6:3-6:11

## Benedictions pronounced by other leaders of the nation of Israel
**JOS 22:6-22:8** *See also* DEU 33:1-33:29

## Benedictions pronounced by family members
**RUT 1:8-1:9** *See also* GEN 24:60, GEN 27:27-27:29, GEN 48:9-48:22, GEN 49:1-49:28

## Apostolic benedictions on the church


### The blessing of grace and peace
**ROM 1:7** *See also* ROM 16:20; 1CO 1:3, 1CO 16:23; GAL 1:2-1:5, GAL 6:16; EPH 1:2, EPH 6:23; 1TI 1:2; REV 22:21

### The blessing of spiritual growth
**HEB 13:20-13:21** *See also* 1PE 5:10

### The blessing of God in all his fulness
**2CO 13:14**
