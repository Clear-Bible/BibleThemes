# 5810: complacency

A self-satisfied state of negligence or carelessness, especially in relation to one's personal situation.



## Spiritual complacency
**ZEP 1:12**; **LUK 12:19** *See also* DEU 8:14; 2CH 20:33; PSA 10:4; ISA 43:22, ISA 64:7; JER 10:21; EZK 33:31; DAN 9:13; HOS 7:7; ZEP 1:6; HEB 6:12, HEB 12:25

## Examples of complacency
**JER 6:14**; **OBA 1:3**; **1CO 5:1-5:2** *See also* PRO 30:20; ISA 16:6; EZK 28:2; HAG 1:2; MAL 1:6-1:14; MAT 7:26, MAT 22:5, MAT 26:33; LUK 12:47-12:48, LUK 14:18; 1CO 4:8; REV 3:15-3:17

## Warnings against complacency
**AMO 6:1**; **2CO 13:5** *See also* PRO 1:32, PRO 10:4, PRO 12:27; ECC 10:18; ISA 32:9, ISA 32:11, ISA 47:8-47:9; EZK 30:9; 1CO 10:12; HEB 2:3
