# 3236: Holy Spirit, and Scripture

The Holy Spirit inspired the original writing of all the Scriptures, and illumines their meaning to believers.



## The Holy Spirit inspired the writing of Scripture


### The Spirit inspired the writers of the OT
**[2SA 23:1-23:2](bibleref:10023001-10023002)** *See also* [NEH 9:30](bibleref:16009030); [EZK 2:2](bibleref:26002002), [EZK 11:24-11:25](bibleref:26011024-26011025); [MIC 3:8](bibleref:33003008); [ZEC 7:12](bibleref:38007012)

### The NT recognises OT writings as inspired by the Holy Spirit
**[2TI 3:16](bibleref:55003016)** *“God-breathed” means “breathed out by God” rather than “inspired”* ; **[2PE 1:20-1:21](bibleref:61001020-61001021)** *See also* [MAT 22:43](bibleref:40022043) parallel MRK 12:36; [ACT 1:16](bibleref:44001016), [ACT 4:25](bibleref:44004025), [ACT 28:25-28:27](bibleref:44028025-44028027); [HEB 3:7-3:11](bibleref:58003007-58003011), [HEB 10:15-10:17](bibleref:58010015-58010017); [1PE 1:11](bibleref:60001011)

### The Spirit inspired the writers of the NT
**[EPH 3:4-3:5](bibleref:49003004-49003005)** *This revelation by the Spirit probably includes the apostles' understanding of the gospel and their preaching as well as the writings of the NT.*  *See also* [1CO 7:40](bibleref:46007040); [REV 2:7](bibleref:66002007), [REV 2:11](bibleref:66002011), [REV 2:17](bibleref:66002017), [REV 2:29](bibleref:66002029), [REV 3:6](bibleref:66003006), [REV 3:13](bibleref:66003013), [REV 3:22](bibleref:66003022)

## The Holy Spirit illumines the meaning of Scripture
**[1CO 2:12-2:16](bibleref:46002012-46002016)** *“what God has freely given” refers to “God's secret wisdom” (verse 7), the “deep things of God” (verse 10) and the “thoughts of God” (verse 11).*  *See also* [JHN 14:26](bibleref:43014026), [JHN 16:13-16:15](bibleref:43016013-43016015); [2CO 3:14-3:17](bibleref:47003014-47003017); [HEB 9:8](bibleref:58009008); [1JN 2:20](bibleref:62002020), [1JN 2:27](bibleref:62002027)

## The relationship between the word and the Holy Spirit


### The association of God's Spirit and breath with his word(s)
 [PSA 33:6](bibleref:19033006); [ISA 59:21](bibleref:23059021); [JHN 3:34](bibleref:43003034), [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063); [EPH 6:17](bibleref:49006017) *“word” here probably means “the gospel” rather than Scripture as a whole.* 

### The Spirit is essential for obeying God's law
**[ROM 2:29](bibleref:45002029)** *Reliance on the letter of the law alone is useless.*  *See also* [ROM 7:6](bibleref:45007006); [2CO 3:6](bibleref:47003006); [GAL 3:2](bibleref:48003002), [GAL 3:5](bibleref:48003005), [GAL 5:18](bibleref:48005018)

### The Spirit enables believers to fulfil the law
**[ROM 8:4](bibleref:45008004)** *See also* [GAL 5:4-5:5](bibleref:48005004-48005005)
