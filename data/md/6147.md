# 6147: deceit, practice of

Deceit is condemned by God as an attempt to deny his sovereignty and his revelation of himself.



## Deceit is forbidden by God


### Condemnation of deception in general
**[LEV 19:11](bibleref:03019011)** *See also* [PRO 12:22](bibleref:20012022), [PRO 24:28](bibleref:20024028)

### False witnesses condemned
**[EXO 20:16](bibleref:02020016)** parallel DEU 5:20 *See also* [PRO 12:17](bibleref:20012017), [PRO 14:5](bibleref:20014005), [PRO 19:9](bibleref:20019009)

### False measures prohibited
**[LEV 19:35](bibleref:03019035)** *See also* [DEU 25:13](bibleref:05025013); [PRO 11:1](bibleref:20011001), [PRO 20:10](bibleref:20020010); [MIC 6:11](bibleref:33006011)

### Spiritual deception condemned
**[TIT 1:16](bibleref:56001016)** *See also* [MAL 1:14](bibleref:39001014); [MAT 23:27-23:28](bibleref:40023027-40023028); [LUK 6:46](bibleref:42006046); [ROM 2:21](bibleref:45002021)
