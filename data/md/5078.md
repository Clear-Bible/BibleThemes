# 5078: Abraham, significance of

Whereas Jews see Abraham as the father of their nation on account of God's covenant with him, Christians see Abraham as the father of all who believe in Jesus Christ.



## The significance of Abraham for nations other than Israel


### Abraham was the father of many nations
**[GEN 17:3-17:6](bibleref:01017003-01017006)** *See also* [GEN 17:20](bibleref:01017020), [GEN 25:1-25:4](bibleref:01025001-01025004), [GEN 25:12-25:18](bibleref:01025012-01025018) *Muslim Arabs trace their ancestry through Ishmael to Abraham.* 

### Abraham was to be a means of blessing to many nations
**[GEN 12:3](bibleref:01012003)** *See also* [GEN 18:18](bibleref:01018018), [GEN 22:17-22:18](bibleref:01022017-01022018); [ACT 3:25](bibleref:44003025)

**[JHN 8:39](bibleref:43008039)** *See also* [PSA 105:5-105:6](bibleref:19105005-19105006); [ISA 41:8](bibleref:23041008), [ISA 51:1-51:2](bibleref:23051001-23051002); [LUK 1:54-1:55](bibleref:42001054-42001055); [JHN 8:33](bibleref:43008033); [ACT 13:26](bibleref:44013026); [ROM 11:1](bibleref:45011001)

## The significance of Abraham for Christians


### Abraham was an ancestor of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 1:1](bibleref:40001001)** parallel LUK 3:34

### Jesus Christ attacks Jewish confidence in mere physical descent from Abraham
**[MAT 3:9-3:10](bibleref:40003009-40003010)** parallel LUK 3:8-3:9 *See also* [JHN 8:33-8:41](bibleref:43008033-43008041)

### Jesus Christ makes a point of identifying outcasts as children of Abraham
**[LUK 19:7-19:9](bibleref:42019007-42019009)** *See also* [LUK 13:10-13:16](bibleref:42013010-42013016)

### God's promise to Abraham is not inherited by those who obey the law
**[GAL 3:15-3:18](bibleref:48003015-48003018)** *See also* [ROM 4:13-4:16](bibleref:45004013-45004016); [GAL 4:21-4:31](bibleref:48004021-48004031)

### Abraham is the father of those who are justified by faith
**[GAL 3:6-3:9](bibleref:48003006-48003009)** *See also* [GEN 15:6](bibleref:01015006); [ROM 4:1-4:25](bibleref:45004001-45004025)
