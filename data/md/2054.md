# 2054: Jesus Christ, mind of

The centre of Jesus Christ's thought, understanding and motivation, characterised by a total dedication to God. Christians are called upon to have the same mind as Christ.



## The intellectual capacity of Jesus Christ's mind


### In childhood
**LUK 2:47** *See also* LUK 2:40, LUK 2:52

### In adulthood
**JHN 7:15** *See also* MAT 7:28, MAT 13:54 parallel MRK 6:2; MRK 1:22 parallel LUK 4:32; MRK 11:18

## Jesus Christ's mind was pure
**HEB 7:26** *The purity of Jesus Christ's mind is declared by his words and actions and by his oneness with God.* *See also* LUK 1:35; JHN 10:30; HEB 4:15
