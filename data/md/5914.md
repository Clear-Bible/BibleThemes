# 5914: optimism

The ability to face the future with confidence. For believers this is possible because of their knowledge of God.



## Optimism resulting from faith


### Confidence in God
**PSA 52:8, PSA 125:1** *See also* PSA 25:4-25:5, PSA 56:4, PSA 71:5, PSA 91:2; NAM 1:7

### Confidence in what God can do
**2CO 1:10-1:11** *See also* PSA 27:1-27:3; JER 17:7; MIC 7:7; HEB 13:6; 1JN 5:14

### Confidence in what God will do in the future
**2CO 5:5-5:8** *See also* ACT 24:14-24:15; GAL 5:5; PHP 1:3-1:6

### Confidence founded on God's grace
**ISA 12:1-12:2** *See also* EZR 10:2; 2CO 3:4-3:5; EPH 3:12; 2TH 2:16-2:17; 1JN 3:21-3:22
