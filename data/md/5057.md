# 5057: rest, physical

The need for physical rest is recognised in Scripture, and provision is made for ensuring that people are allowed time for rest.



## Jesus Christ needed physical rest
**JHN 4:6** *See also* MRK 4:38, MRK 6:31; LUK 9:58

## The provision of rest


### The need for rest
**MRK 6:31** *See also* PSA 23:2-23:3; PRO 17:1; ECC 4:6; MAT 11:28-11:30

### Provision of sleep for rest
**PSA 4:8** *See also* PSA 127:2; ECC 5:12

### Provision of a Sabbath-rest
**EXO 34:21**; **MRK 2:27** *See also* EXO 20:8-20:11 parallel DEU 5:12-5:15; EXO 31:15, EXO 35:2; LEV 23:3; NEH 13:15; ISA 58:13-58:14

## The purpose of rest


### To renew strength
**LAM 5:5** *See also* ISA 40:29-40:31

### To know peace
**MAT 6:25-6:34** *See also* JOB 11:18-11:19; PSA 3:5, PSA 16:8; PRO 3:24; PHP 4:6-4:7

## The character of rest


### The absence of war
**JOS 11:23** *See also* JOS 14:15; 1KI 5:4; 1CH 22:9; PSA 46:9-46:10; PRO 1:33; ISA 14:3

### The absence of social strife
**2CO 13:11** *See also* ECC 10:4; 1CO 1:10; 1TH 4:11; HEB 12:14; JAS 3:17-3:18; 1PE 3:8

### The absence of fear
**MRK 4:37-4:38** parallels MAT 8:24-8:25, LUK 8:23-8:24 *See also* GEN 32:11; PSA 127:2; MIC 4:4; MAT 6:31 parallel LUK 12:29

### The absence of anxiety
**1PE 5:7** *See also* MAT 6:25; PHP 4:6

### The presence of God
**EXO 33:14** *See also* DEU 33:27; MAT 11:28

### The experience of security
**DEU 33:12** *See also* PRO 19:23

### The experience of peace in death
**REV 14:13** *See also* DEU 31:16; JOB 3:13-3:17

## The destruction of rest


### By business
**PSA 39:6** *See also* GEN 31:40; ECC 1:13, ECC 2:23, ECC 8:16; LUK 21:34

### By conflict
**2CO 7:5** *See also* LAM 5:5; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 11:27

### By grief
**JER 45:3** *See also* JOB 3:26; PSA 77:4; DAN 6:18

### By unconfessed guilt
**PSA 32:3-32:5** *See also* GEN 4:12; DEU 28:65-28:67; ISA 48:22, ISA 57:20-57:21; REV 14:11

### By sickness
**JOB 30:17** *See also* JOB 7:4-7:5
