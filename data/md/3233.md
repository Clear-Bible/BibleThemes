# 3233: Holy Spirit, and sanctification

The work of the Holy Spirit in enabling believers to lead holy lives, dedicated to the service of God and conformed to his likeness.



## The Spirit of holiness is promised
**[MAT 3:11](bibleref:40003011)** parallel LUK 3:16 *“fire” implies the Holy Spirit's work of purification and judgment.* 

## Sanctification is a special work of the Holy Spirit
**[ROM 15:16](bibleref:45015016)** *See also* [1CO 6:11](bibleref:46006011); [GAL 5:5](bibleref:48005005); [1PE 1:2](bibleref:60001002)

## The Holy Spirit requires believers to be sanctified
**[2TH 2:13](bibleref:53002013)** *Sanctification is a necessary part of being a Christian.*  *See also* [1CO 6:18-6:19](bibleref:46006018-46006019)

## The Holy Spirit enables believers to be sanctified
**[ROM 8:4](bibleref:45008004)** *See also* [ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013); [EPH 5:18](bibleref:49005018)

## The Holy Spirit produces sanctification
**[GAL 5:22-5:23](bibleref:48005022-48005023)** *See also* [ROM 14:17](bibleref:45014017); [2TI 1:7](bibleref:55001007)

## The process of sanctification


### The Holy Spirit makes believers more like Jesus Christ
**[2CO 3:18](bibleref:47003018)**

### The Holy Spirit helps mortify sinful human nature
**[ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013)** *See also* [GAL 5:17](bibleref:48005017)

### The Holy Spirit is opposed to natural desires
**[GAL 5:16-5:17](bibleref:48005016-48005017)** *See also* [ROM 8:5-8:9](bibleref:45008005-45008009); [JUD 1:19](bibleref:65001019)
