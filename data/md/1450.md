# 1450: signs, kinds of

Signs are miraculous events, supernatural phenomena or everyday things that are given a special significance. The term is used in John's Gospel to describe the miracles of Jesus Christ.



## Miraculous signs
**2CO 12:12** *See also* EXO 10:1; PSA 78:43, PSA 105:27; MAT 24:24 parallel MRK 13:22; MRK 16:17; ACT 2:43; ROM 15:19

## The signs of Jesus Christ in John's Gospel
**JHN 2:11** *John refers to the miracles of Jesus Christ as “signs” in order to bring out the significance of these deeds.* *See also* JHN 6:26 *Jesus Christ, the bread of life*; JHN 9:16 *Jesus Christ, the light of the world*; JHN 11:47 *Jesus Christ, the resurrection and the life*
