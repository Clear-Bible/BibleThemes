# 1450: signs, kinds of

Signs are miraculous events, supernatural phenomena or everyday things that are given a special significance. The term is used in John's Gospel to describe the miracles of Jesus Christ.



## Miraculous signs
**[2CO 12:12](bibleref:47012012)** *See also* [EXO 10:1](bibleref:02010001); [PSA 78:43](bibleref:19078043), [PSA 105:27](bibleref:19105027); [MAT 24:24](bibleref:40024024) parallel MRK 13:22; [MRK 16:17](bibleref:41016017); [ACT 2:43](bibleref:44002043); [ROM 15:19](bibleref:45015019)

## The signs of Jesus Christ in John's Gospel
**[JHN 2:11](bibleref:43002011)** *John refers to the miracles of Jesus Christ as “signs” in order to bring out the significance of these deeds.*  *See also* [JHN 6:26](bibleref:43006026) *Jesus Christ, the bread of life* ; [JHN 9:16](bibleref:43009016) *Jesus Christ, the light of the world* ; [JHN 11:47](bibleref:43011047) *Jesus Christ, the resurrection and the life*
