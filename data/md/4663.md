# 4663: lamb

A common source of clothing and meat, this animal was given heightened significance by its place in the Jewish sacrificial system and was used figuratively of Jesus Christ.



## The economic importance of lambs


### As food
**2SA 12:4** *See also* DEU 28:4; AMO 6:4

### As a means of exchange
**GEN 30:32** *See also* 2KI 3:4

## The sacrificial significance of lambs


### Their general use
**GEN 22:7** *See also* EXO 29:38; LEV 3:7, LEV 14:12, LEV 14:24; 1SA 7:9

### The Passover lamb
**EXO 12:21** *See also* EXO 12:3, EXO 12:7; 2CH 30:15-30:17; EZR 6:20; LUK 22:7

### The best had to be chosen
**NUM 6:14** *See also* LEV 4:32, LEV 23:12; NUM 28:3-28:4, NUM 28:11; EZK 46:4

### They were costly
**LEV 12:8** *See also* EXO 34:20

## The figurative use of lambs


### In general
**PSA 114:4**; **LUK 10:3** *See also* ISA 40:11, ISA 65:25; JER 11:19; JHN 21:15

### Describing Jesus Christ and his work
**JHN 1:36**; **1PE 1:18-1:19** *See also* ACT 8:32; ISA 53:7; 1CO 5:7; REV 5:12, REV 22:3
