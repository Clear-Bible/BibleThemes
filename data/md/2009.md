# 2009: Jesus Christ, anger of

Jesus Christ's controlled emotion arising from his unswerving opposition to evil and his determination to eradicate it.



## Causes of Jesus Christ's anger


### Petty legalism in religious observance
 MAT 15:3, MAT 23:1-23:4; ; MRK 3:4-3:5

### Attempts to prevent access to him
 MRK 10:14

### People leading others into sin
 MAT 18:6-18:7 parallels MRK 9:42, LUK 17:1-17:2

## Demonstrations of Jesus Christ's anger


### Purging the temple
 MAT 21:12-21:13 parallels MRK 11:15-11:17, LUK 19:45-19:46; ; JHN 2:14-2:16

### Cursing the fig-tree
 MRK 11:14 parallel MAT 21:19

## Jesus Christ's words in anger


### Against demons
**MAT 17:18** parallels MRK 9:25, LUK 9:42 *See also* MRK 1:25-1:26 parallel LUK 4:35

### Against disciples
**LUK 9:55-9:56** *See also* MAT 16:23 parallel MRK 8:33

### Against Pharisees
**MAT 23:13** *See also* MAT 12:34, MAT 15:7-15:9 parallel MRK 7:6-7:8; MAT 23:15-23:16, MAT 23:23-23:33; LUK 11:42-11:44, LUK 13:15; JHN 8:44

### Against unbelief
**MAT 17:17** parallels MRK 9:19, LUK 9:41 *See also* MAT 12:39-12:45 parallel LUK 11:29-11:32; MRK 8:38; LUK 11:50-11:51

### Against false prophets
**MAT 7:15**

### Against the rich
**LUK 6:24-6:26**

### Against unrepentant cities
**MAT 11:20** *See also* MAT 11:21-11:24 parallel LUK 10:13-10:15

## Jesus Christ reflects the anger of God
