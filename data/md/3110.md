# 3110: Holy Spirit, titles and names of

The names and terms for the Holy Spirit relate to his power, activity and presence in the world, often indicating the nature of his actions or the gifts which he conveys to believers.

## Titles relating the Holy Spirit to God


### The Spirit of God
**[GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002)** *See also* [GEN 41:38](bibleref:01041038); [1SA 10:10](bibleref:09010010), [1SA 19:20](bibleref:09019020), [1SA 19:23](bibleref:09019023); [ROM 8:9](bibleref:45008009); [1CO 6:11](bibleref:46006011); [2CO 3:3](bibleref:47003003); [EPH 4:30](bibleref:49004030); [PHP 3:3](bibleref:50003003)

[1SA 10:6](bibleref:09010006), [1SA 16:13](bibleref:09016013); [2SA 23:2](bibleref:10023002)[ISA 11:2](bibleref:23011002), [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001)
