# 7631: Twelve, calling of

Jesus Christ's chosen companions, whom he called and trained to carry on his mission after his death and resurrection.



## The calling of the Twelve


### Their names
**[MAT 10:1-10:4](bibleref:40010001-40010004)** parallel MRK 3:14-3:19 *See also* [LUK 6:12-6:16](bibleref:42006012-42006016); [ACT 1:12-1:13](bibleref:44001012-44001013)

### Their selection was preceded by prayer
 [LUK 6:12-6:13](bibleref:42006012-42006013)

### As Jesus Christ's companions, they were to share in his mission
 [MRK 3:14-3:15](bibleref:41003014-41003015), [MRK 6:7-6:13](bibleref:41006007-41006013) parallels MAT 10:1, MAT 10:9-10:14, LUK 9:1-9:6; [LUK 8:1](bibleref:42008001)

### Jesus Christ promised them authority at the final judgment
 [MAT 19:28](bibleref:40019028) parallel LUK 22:30

## After Judas' death the Twelve became the Eleven
 [MAT 28:16](bibleref:40028016); [MRK 16:14](bibleref:41016014); [LUK 24:9](bibleref:42024009), [LUK 24:33](bibleref:42024033); [ACT 2:14](bibleref:44002014)

### The Eleven became the Twelve again with the election of Matthias
 [ACT 1:26](bibleref:44001026)

## The ministry of the Twelve after the resurrection supplemented by the appointment of deacons
 [ACT 6:2-6:3](bibleref:44006002-44006003)

## The symbolic significance of the Twelve


### The twelve tribes of Israel
 [GEN 49:28](bibleref:01049028); [EZK 47:13](bibleref:26047013); [MAT 19:28](bibleref:40019028) parallel LUK 22:30 *Twelve symbolises completeness and is associated with the rule of God.*
