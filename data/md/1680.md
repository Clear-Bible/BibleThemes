# 1680: types

An OT institution, person, place or event regarded as anticipating the person of Jesus Christ or some aspect of the Christian faith or life.



## OT foreshadowings of Jesus Christ
**LUK 24:25-24:27** *See also* LUK 24:44; 1PE 1:10-1:12

## OT institutions as types


### The Sabbath:  Jesus Christ's finished work
**HEB 4:3-4:6** *See also* GEN 2:2-2:3; HEB 4:9-4:10

### Marriage:  Jesus Christ's union with his people
**EPH 5:30-5:32** *See also* GEN 2:22-2:24 *Adam may be viewed as a figure of Jesus Christ, and Eve, of the church, formed in Christ, loved and united with him*; PSA 45:10-45:11; MAT 9:15, MAT 25:1; JHN 3:29; EPH 5:25-5:27; REV 19:7, REV 21:2

### Circumcision: believers' union with Christ
**COL 2:11** *See also* GEN 17:9-17:14; ROM 2:29; PHP 3:3

### The tabernacle: Jesus Christ's coming among his people
**JHN 1:14** *The context shows that “The Word” is Jesus Christ; “made his dwelling” involves the idea of pitching a tent and reflects the tabernacle which was pitched among the Israelites and filled with God's glory; so Christ has pitched his tent among his people and revealed his glory.* *See also* EXO 25:8, EXO 40:34-40:35

### The tabernacle: the way to God
**HEB 9:11-9:12** *See also* EXO 25:22, EXO 30:6, EXO 30:36; NUM 17:4; HEB 9:8, HEB 10:19-10:22

### The high priesthood: Jesus Christ as intercessor
**HEB 7:23-7:28** *See also* EXO 29:1-29:7; LEV 16:11-16:17; HEB 4:14-4:16, HEB 5:1-5:5 *Jesus Christ, the great high priest, is both human and divine*; HEB 9:11-9:12, HEB 10:11-10:12 *Note the contrast between the many OT sacrifices and Jesus Christ's once-for-all offering of himself.*

### The priesthood: Christian worship and service
**HEB 13:15-13:16** *See also* EXO 19:6, EXO 29:8-29:9; 1PE 2:5; REV 1:6

### Sacrifices: Jesus Christ as substitute
**ROM 3:25** *See also* LEV 4:13-4:15, LEV 4:25-4:35 *Hands laid on the head of the sacrifice symbolised the transfer of guilt and its consequences to another. A perfect animal was required*; ISA 53:4-53:12; MRK 15:25-15:37; 2CO 5:15; GAL 2:20; 1PE 3:18

### Sacrifice: believers' consecration
**ROM 12:1** *See also* LEV 1:1-1:9; NUM 15:1-15:12; PHP 2:17; 2TI 4:6

### The day of atonement: Jesus Christ as sin-bearer
**HEB 9:28** *See also* LEV 16:1-16:34; HEB 9:7

### Passover: deliverance from judgment
**1CO 5:7** *See also* EXO 12:1-12:16; MAT 26:17 parallels MRK 14:12, LUK 22:7; MAT 26:26-26:29 parallels MRK 14:22-14:25, LUK 22:14-22:20; JHN 1:29; 1PE 1:18-1:19

### Redemption: release from sin
**MAT 20:28** parallel MRK 10:45 *“Redemption” and “ransom” both involve buying back that which has been lost.* *See also* EXO 13:11-13:13, EXO 21:28-21:32; JOB 19:25; PSA 78:35; ISA 41:14; ROM 3:24; 1CO 6:20GAL 3:13, GAL 4:5
EPH 1:7 *Blood is the ransom price for the forgiveness of sin*; 1TI 2:6; TIT 2:14; HEB 9:15

### The temple: the church
**2CO 6:16** *See also* 1KI 6:1, 1KI 8:10-8:13; 1CO 3:16; EPH 2:21-2:22; 1PE 2:5

## OT places as types


### The promised land: rest in Christ
**HEB 4:8-4:11** *See also* JOS 1:13, JOS 11:23, JOS 14:15

### Jerusalem: the glorified church
**REV 21:1-21:3** *See also* 2SA 5:4-5:5; PSA 122:1-122:9; ISA 62:6-62:7; ZEC 2:3-2:5; HEB 12:22-12:23; REV 3:12, REV 21:9-21:10

### Babylon: enmity to Jesus Christ
**REV 17:5** *Some take Babylon to represent the world, some the apostate church, some a city.* *See also* ISA 13:19-13:22; REV 18:2-18:3

## OT people as types


### Israel: the church
**1PE 2:9** *See also* GEN 12:1-12:3; EXO 19:6; ROM 9:6-9:8; GAL 6:16; EPH 2:19

### Adam: Jesus Christ as the head of his people
**1CO 15:21-15:22** *See also* GEN 3:17-3:19; ROM 5:12-5:20 *Just as Adam was the representative of all humanity in the fall, so Jesus Christ is the representative of all who inherit salvation.*

### Abraham: justifying faith in Jesus Christ
**ROM 4:18-4:25** *See also* GEN 15:6; GAL 3:6-3:9

### Melchizedek: Jesus Christ as priest-king
**HEB 7:1-7:3** *See also* GEN 14:17-14:20; PSA 110:4; HEB 7:11-7:17

### Sarah and Hagar: grace and law
**GAL 4:22-4:26** *See also* GEN 16:1-16:6, GEN 21:10; GAL 4:27-4:31

### David: Jesus Christ as king, shepherd and sufferer
**LUK 1:31-1:33** *See also* 2SA 7:11-7:16; PSA 78:70-78:72, PSA 89:19-89:37; JHN 13:18; PSA 41:9; REV 22:16

### Solomon: Jesus Christ as wise ruler
**MAT 12:42** *See also* 1KI 3:5-3:12

## OT events as types


### Noah's ark: salvation
**1PE 3:20-3:21** *See also* GEN 7:7

### Abraham offering Isaac: God giving his Son
**ROM 8:32** *See also* GEN 22:9-22:14

### Crossing the Red Sea: Christian commitment
**1CO 10:1-10:2** *See also* EXO 14:15-14:22

### Water from a rock: Jesus Christ's provision
**1CO 10:4** *See also* EXO 17:5-17:6

### The bronze snake: Jesus Christ, the object of faith
**JHN 3:14-3:15** *See also* NUM 21:4-21:9
