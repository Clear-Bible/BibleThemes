# 1680: types

An OT institution, person, place or event regarded as anticipating the person of Jesus Christ or some aspect of the Christian faith or life.

## OT foreshadowings of Jesus Christ
**[LUK 24:25-24:27](bibleref:42024025-42024027)** *See also* [LUK 24:44](bibleref:42024044); [1PE 1:10-1:12](bibleref:60001010-60001012)

## OT institutions as types


### The Sabbath:  Jesus Christ's finished work
**[HEB 4:3-4:6](bibleref:58004003-58004006)** *See also* [GEN 2:2-2:3](bibleref:01002002-01002003); [HEB 4:9-4:10](bibleref:58004009-58004010)

### Marriage:  Jesus Christ's union with his people
**[EPH 5:30-5:32](bibleref:49005030-49005032)** *See also* [GEN 2:22-2:24](bibleref:01002022-01002024) *Adam may be viewed as a figure of Jesus Christ, and Eve, of the church, formed in Christ, loved and united with him* ; [PSA 45:10-45:11](bibleref:19045010-19045011); [MAT 9:15](bibleref:40009015), [MAT 25:1](bibleref:40025001); [JHN 3:29](bibleref:43003029); [EPH 5:25-5:27](bibleref:49005025-49005027); [REV 19:7](bibleref:66019007), [REV 21:2](bibleref:66021002)

### Circumcision: believers' union with Christ
**[COL 2:11](bibleref:51002011)** *See also* [GEN 17:9-17:14](bibleref:01017009-01017014); [ROM 2:29](bibleref:45002029); [PHP 3:3](bibleref:50003003)

### The tabernacle: Jesus Christ's coming among his people
**[JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014)** *The context shows that “The Word” is Jesus Christ; “made his dwelling” involves the idea of pitching a tent and reflects the tabernacle which was pitched among the Israelites and filled with God's glory; so Christ has pitched his tent among his people and revealed his glory.*  *See also* [EXO 25:8](bibleref:02025008), [EXO 40:34-40:35](bibleref:02040034-02040035)

### The tabernacle: the way to God
**[HEB 9:11-9:12](bibleref:58009011-58009012)** *See also* [EXO 25:22](bibleref:02025022), [EXO 30:6](bibleref:02030006), [EXO 30:36](bibleref:02030036); [NUM 17:4](bibleref:04017004); [HEB 9:8](bibleref:58009008), [HEB 10:19-10:22](bibleref:58010019-58010022)

### The high priesthood: Jesus Christ as intercessor
**[HEB 7:23-7:28](bibleref:58007023-58007028)** *See also* [EXO 29:1-29:7](bibleref:02029001-02029007); [LEV 16:11-16:17](bibleref:03016011-03016017); [HEB 4:14-4:16](bibleref:58004014-58004016), [HEB 5:1-5:5](bibleref:58005001-58005005) *Jesus Christ, the great high priest, is both human and divine* ; [HEB 9:11-9:12](bibleref:58009011-58009012), [HEB 10:11-10:12](bibleref:58010011-58010012) *Note the contrast between the many OT sacrifices and Jesus Christ's once-for-all offering of himself.* 

### The priesthood: Christian worship and service
**[HEB 13:15-13:16](bibleref:58013015-58013016)** *See also* [EXO 19:6](bibleref:02019006), [EXO 29:8-29:9](bibleref:02029008-02029009); [1PE 2:5](bibleref:60002005); [REV 1:6](bibleref:66001006)

### Sacrifices: Jesus Christ as substitute
**[ROM 3:25](bibleref:45003025)** *See also* [LEV 4:13-4:15](bibleref:03004013-03004015), [LEV 4:25-4:35](bibleref:03004025-03004035) *Hands laid on the head of the sacrifice symbolised the transfer of guilt and its consequences to another. A perfect animal was required* ; [ISA 53:4-53:12](bibleref:23053004-23053012); [MRK 15:25-15:37](bibleref:41015025-41015037); [2CO 5:15](bibleref:47005015); [GAL 2:20](bibleref:48002020); [1PE 3:18](bibleref:60003018)

### Sacrifice: believers' consecration
**[ROM 12:1](bibleref:45012001)** *See also* [LEV 1:1-1:9](bibleref:03001001-03001009); [NUM 15:1-15:12](bibleref:04015001-04015012); [PHP 2:17](bibleref:50002017); [2TI 4:6](bibleref:55004006)

### The day of atonement: Jesus Christ as sin-bearer
**[HEB 9:28](bibleref:58009028)** *See also* [LEV 16:1-16:34](bibleref:03016001-03016034); [HEB 9:7](bibleref:58009007)

### Passover: deliverance from judgment
**[1CO 5:7](bibleref:46005007)** *See also* [EXO 12:1-12:16](bibleref:02012001-02012016); [MAT 26:17](bibleref:40026017) parallels MRK 14:12, LUK 22:7; [MAT 26:26-26:29](bibleref:40026026-40026029) parallels MRK 14:22-14:25, LUK 22:14-22:20; [JHN 1:29](bibleref:43001029); [1PE 1:18-1:19](bibleref:60001018-60001019)

### Redemption: release from sin
**[MAT 20:28](bibleref:40020028)** parallel MRK 10:45 *“Redemption” and “ransom” both involve buying back that which has been lost.*  *See also* [EXO 13:11-13:13](bibleref:02013011-02013013), [EXO 21:28-21:32](bibleref:02021028-02021032); [JOB 19:25](bibleref:18019025); [PSA 78:35](bibleref:19078035); [ISA 41:14](bibleref:23041014); [ROM 3:24](bibleref:45003024); [1CO 6:20](bibleref:46006020)[GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013), [GAL 4:5](bibleref:48004005)
[EPH 1:7](bibleref:49001007) *Blood is the ransom price for the forgiveness of sin* ; [1TI 2:6](bibleref:54002006); [TIT 2:14](bibleref:56002014); [HEB 9:15](bibleref:58009015)

### The temple: the church
**[2CO 6:16](bibleref:47006016)** *See also* [1KI 6:1](bibleref:11006001), [1KI 8:10-8:13](bibleref:11008010-11008013); [1CO 3:16](bibleref:46003016); [EPH 2:21-2:22](bibleref:49002021-49002022); [1PE 2:5](bibleref:60002005)
