# 5571: surgery

The physical removal of part of the human body, either in response to severe illness or as a form of punishment.



## Medical surgery
 EZK 30:21

## Punitive surgery
 DEU 25:12; ; JDG 1:6-1:7; ; 1SA 17:51, 1SA 31:9; ; 2SA 4:7, 2SA 4:12; ; 2KI 25:7

## Ritual or cultural surgery


### Castration
The primary meaning of ``eunuch'' in the OT is ``court officer'' and need not always imply castration; similarly with the NT counterpart:
 2KI 20:18; ; EST 2:3; ; ISA 56:3; ; MAT 19:12; ; ACT 8:27

### Circumcision
 GEN 17:10-17:14; ; EXO 4:25; ; LEV 12:3; ; JOS 5:8; ; LUK 2:21

### Dismemberment
A solemn call to the nation to act:
 JDG 19:29; ; 1SA 11:7

### Ear piercing
 EXO 21:5-21:6 *There may be a reference to this in Ps 40:6.*

### Pagan mutilation
 LEV 19:28; ; DEU 14:1; ; 1KI 18:28

### Animal sacrifices
 GEN 15:10; ; EXO 29:17; ; LEV 1:6, LEV 1:12, LEV 3:9

## Miraculous surgery
 GEN 2:21-2:22; ; LUK 22:50-22:51

### Heart transplant
**EZK 36:26**

### Amputation
**MAT 5:29-5:30** *Jesus Christ is using hyperbole (deliberate exaggeration) to stress the need at times for radical action.* *See also* MAT 18:8-18:9; MRK 9:43-9:47

### Grafting
 ROM 11:17-11:24

### Pruning
 ISA 18:5; ; JHN 15:1-15:2

### Felling
