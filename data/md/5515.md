# 5515: scroll

A long strip of leather or papyrus on which people wrote.



## Writing on a scroll
 EXO 17:14; ; NUM 5:23; ; DEU 17:18; ; EZR 6:2; ; PSA 40:7; ; ISA 30:8; ; JER 36:2, JER 36:4, JER 36:18; ; LUK 4:17; ; HEB 10:7; ; REV 1:11

 ISA 8:1

### In ink
 JER 36:18

### In columns
