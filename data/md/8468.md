# 8468: renunciation

The deliberate rejection or disavowal, especially of a person or object.



## Renunciation of the LORD
**JDG 2:12**; **ISA 1:28**; **ACT 3:13** *See also* EXO 32:8; JOS 22:16; 2KI 22:17; 1CH 28:9; JER 5:19; MAT 24:10; 2TI 2:12

## Renunciation of evil


### In repentance
**PSA 37:27** *See also* JER 35:15; EZK 14:6; DAN 4:27; 2CO 4:2; 1TI 6:20

### To find God's mercy
**2CH 7:14** *See also* PRO 28:13; EZK 18:21; JON 3:10

## Self-renunciation and the Christian life


### An integral part of discipleship
**MAT 16:24** parallels MRK 8:34, LUK 9:23 *See also* MRK 10:28; LUK 5:28, LUK 14:33; PHP 3:8

### The example of Jesus Christ
**MRK 10:45** *See also* JHN 10:17; PHP 2:7-2:8
