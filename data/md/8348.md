# 8348: spiritual growth, nature of

Having given spiritual life to his people, God expects them to grow to maturity.



## God desires the spiritual growth of his people
**MAT 5:48**; **HEB 6:1** *See also* 2CO 13:9-13:11; EPH 1:4, EPH 2:10, EPH 3:17-3:19; PHP 3:12; 1TH 4:1, 1TH 4:7; 2TI 1:9

## Christlikeness is the goal of spiritual growth
**ROM 8:29** *See also* EPH 4:13-4:15; PHP 2:5; 1JN 3:2-3:3

## Aspects of spiritual growth


### Growth in grace
**2PE 3:18** *See also* PRO 4:18; 1PE 2:1-2:3

### Growth in faith
**2TH 1:3** *See also* 2CO 10:15

### Growth in love
**1TH 3:12** *See also* ROM 5:5; 1CO 14:1; PHP 1:9; 1TH 4:9-4:10; HEB 10:24; 1JN 4:7-4:21, 1JN 5:1-5:3

### Growth in understanding
**PSA 119:27**; **1CO 14:20** *See also* PSA 119:97-119:99; ROM 12:2, ROM 16:19; 1CO 13:11; EPH 1:17-1:19; PHP 1:9-1:10; COL 1:9; HEB 5:14

### Growth in holiness
**2CO 7:1** *See also* EPH 5:25-5:26; HEB 2:11, HEB 10:10-10:14, HEB 12:14, HEB 13:12; 1PE 1:15-1:16

### Growth in fruitfulness
**JHN 15:16** *See also* MAT 13:23 parallels MRK 4:20, LUK 8:15; JHN 15:2, JHN 15:8; PHP 1:11; COL 1:10

### Growth in contentment
**PHP 4:11-4:12** *See also* 1TI 6:6; HEB 13:5

## Examples of spiritual growth


### In individuals
 1SA 2:26 *Samuel*; ; LUK 1:80 *John the Baptist*; LUK 2:40, LUK 2:52; ACT 9:22; ; 1CO 9:26-9:27; ; PHP 3:12-3:14; 
ACT 18:26 *Apollos*; ; PHM 1:11 *Onesimus*; ; 3JN 1:2-1:3 *Gaius*

### In the church
 ACT 9:31, ACT 11:26, ACT 16:5; ; 2CO 10:15; ; COL 1:6; ; 1TH 2:13; ; 2TH 1:3
