# 8279: innocence, examples of

Innocence is displayed most purely in Jesus Christ but is also ascribed to others.



## Examples of innocence


### Innocence before the fall
**GEN 2:25**

### The innocence of Jesus Christ
**1PE 2:22** *See also* ISA 53:9; MAT 27:19; LUK 23:41; JHN 8:46; 2CO 5:21; HEB 4:15; 1JN 3:4-3:5

### Innocence in others
**JOB 9:21**; **JHN 1:47** *See also* GEN 6:9 *Noah*; JDG 21:20-21:22 *the assembly of Israel*; 2SA 11:6-11:13 *Uriah*; JOB 9:15 *Job*; DAN 6:22 *Daniel*; 2CO 7:10-7:11 *the Corinthians*

## The varied experiences of innocence


### Loss of innocence
 GEN 3:1-3:13

### The innocent wronged
**ISA 5:22-5:23** *See also* 1SA 19:4-19:5; 2SA 13:1-13:14; ISA 29:20-29:21; MAT 2:16, MAT 12:1-12:7; JAS 5:6

### Admissions of lack of innocence
**2KI 10:9** *See also* GEN 44:1-44:16

### Claims to innocence
**JOB 34:5-34:6** *See also* GEN 20:1-20:5; 2SA 3:26-3:28; ISA 50:8-50:9; ACT 20:25-20:27

### False claims to innocence
**JER 2:34-2:35** *See also* MAT 27:24

### Washing hands to signify innocence
**MAT 27:24** *See also* PSA 26:6, PSA 73:13

### A distorted view of innocence
**PRO 16:2** *See also* JOB 4:7, JOB 9:23, JOB 9:28
