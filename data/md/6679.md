# 6679: justification, results of

Justification brings a changed relationship with God and a future hope. It will also bring a change in behaviour.



## The results of justification


### Peace with God, access to his presence and the hope of his glory
**[ROM 5:1-5:2](bibleref:45005001-45005002)** *See also* [ROM 8:30](bibleref:45008030); [TIT 3:7](bibleref:56003007)

### Assurance of forgiveness
 [ROM 5:9](bibleref:45005009); [EPH 1:13-1:14](bibleref:49001013-49001014)

### Knowing Jesus Christ and participating in his resurrection
**[PHP 3:10-3:11](bibleref:50003010-50003011)** *See also* [ROM 6:5](bibleref:45006005)

### Freedom from condemnation
**[ROM 8:31-8:34](bibleref:45008031-45008034)** *See also* [ROM 8:1-8:4](bibleref:45008001-45008004); [GAL 3:13-3:14](bibleref:48003013-48003014)

### Freedom from domination by sin
**[ROM 6:14](bibleref:45006014), [ROM 6:17-6:18](bibleref:45006017-45006018)**

### Adoption into God's family
 *See also* [ROM 8:15-8:17](bibleref:45008015-45008017); [GAL 4:6-4:7](bibleref:48004006-48004007)

### Righteousness in the sight of God
**[ROM 5:17](bibleref:45005017)**; **[PHP 3:8-3:9](bibleref:50003008-50003009)** *See also* [ROM 3:20-3:22](bibleref:45003020-45003022); [1CO 1:30](bibleref:46001030)

### Justification must lead to good works
**[JAS 2:24](bibleref:59002024)** *See also* [ROM 6:15-6:18](bibleref:45006015-45006018); [GAL 5:13-5:16](bibleref:48005013-48005016); [JAS 2:14-2:26](bibleref:59002014-59002026)
