# 1035: God, faithfulness of

God's perfect loyalty and consistency in being true to his name, his character and his word.



## God's faithfulness is an integral part of his nature
**[NUM 23:19](bibleref:04023019)** *God's limitless power and steadfast love imply complete faithfulness which contrasts with human nature* ; **[LAM 3:22-3:23](bibleref:25003022-25003023)** *See also* [EXO 34:6](bibleref:02034006); [DEU 7:8-7:9](bibleref:05007008-05007009), [DEU 32:4](bibleref:05032004); [ROM 4:21](bibleref:45004021); [2TI 2:13](bibleref:55002013)

## God is faithful to his name and character
**[2TI 2:13](bibleref:55002013)** *See also* [NEH 9:8](bibleref:16009008); [PSA 106:8](bibleref:19106008); [1TH 5:24](bibleref:52005024); [HEB 6:13-6:18](bibleref:58006013-58006018)

## God's faithfulness is known through fulfilled promises
