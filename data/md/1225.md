# 1225: God, as Spirit

God has no material body and he is not subject to human limitations. While his spiritual nature is clearly inferred in Scripture, it is seldom directly stated; the emphasis is on his dynamic, living personhood.



## God as spirit
**JHN 4:24** *See also* ISA 28:5-28:6, ISA 31:3

### God is not subject to physical limitations
 1SA 16:7; ; 1KI 18:27 *Elijah's mockery of Baal implies a contrast with the true God who is not subject to the limitations suggested*; ; JOB 10:4-10:8; ; PSA 121:3-121:4

### God as the Spirit
**PSA 139:7** *See also* PSA 51:11 *The Spirit and the presence of God are equated*; PSA 143:10 *God's guidance and the Spirit's leading are equated*; ISA 34:16 *God's Spirit participates in God's plans*; 2CO 3:17-3:18

## God cannot be represented physically


### Images of God are prohibited
 EXO 20:4, EXO 34:17; ; LEV 26:1; ; ACT 17:29

### God transcends spacial limitations
**ACT 17:24** *See also* 1KI 8:27 parallel 2CH 6:18; 2CH 2:6; ACT 7:48-7:50

### God is invisible
**1TI 6:15-6:16** *See also* EXO 33:20; DEU 4:15-4:19; JHN 1:18; COL 1:15; 1TI 1:17
