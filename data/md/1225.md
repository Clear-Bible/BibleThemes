# 1225: God, as Spirit

God has no material body and he is not subject to human limitations. While his spiritual nature is clearly inferred in Scripture, it is seldom directly stated; the emphasis is on his dynamic, living personhood.



## God as spirit
**[JHN 4:24](bibleref:43004024)** *See also* [ISA 28:5-28:6](bibleref:23028005-23028006), [ISA 31:3](bibleref:23031003)

### God is not subject to physical limitations
 [1SA 16:7](bibleref:09016007); [1KI 18:27](bibleref:11018027) *Elijah's mockery of Baal implies a contrast with the true God who is not subject to the limitations suggested* ; [JOB 10:4-10:8](bibleref:18010004-18010008); [PSA 121:3-121:4](bibleref:19121003-19121004)

### God as the Spirit
**[PSA 139:7](bibleref:19139007)** *See also* [PSA 51:11](bibleref:19051011) *The Spirit and the presence of God are equated* ; [PSA 143:10](bibleref:19143010) *God's guidance and the Spirit's leading are equated* ; [ISA 34:16](bibleref:23034016) *God's Spirit participates in God's plans* ; [2CO 3:17-3:18](bibleref:47003017-47003018)
