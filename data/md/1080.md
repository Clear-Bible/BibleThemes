# 1080: God, living and self-sustaining

God is alive and active. In contrast to idols, he is uncreated and the source of all continuing life.



## God is alive
**JER 10:10** *See also* GEN 1:1; DEU 5:26; JOS 3:10; JER 23:36; MAT 26:63; 1TI 3:15; HEB 3:12, HEB 12:22

## God lives for ever
**ISA 57:15** *See also* DAN 4:34, DAN 12:7; REV 4:9-4:10, REV 10:6, REV 15:7

## God is self-existent
**JHN 5:26** *See also* EXO 3:14; JHN 8:58; ACT 17:25

## The living God is Father, Son and Holy Spirit
**JHN 6:57**; **2CO 3:3** *See also* MAT 16:16; 1CO 8:6; HEB 7:24

## The living God is to be worshipped
**DAN 6:26** *See also* DAN 6:20; ROM 9:26; HOS 1:10; 2CO 6:16; 1TI 4:10; HEB 9:14

## Turning away from the living God is sin
**HEB 3:12** *See also* 1SA 17:26, 1SA 17:36; 2KI 19:4 parallel ISA 37:4; 2KI 19:16 parallel ISA 37:17; HEB 10:31

## The living God contrasts with dead idols
**ACT 14:15** *See also* 1KI 18:26-18:29; PSA 115:2-115:7; ISA 46:1-46:4; JER 10:3-10:6; 1TH 1:9

## God is the source of life
**DEU 30:20** *See also* JHN 1:4

### God gives life to created beings
**GEN 2:7** *See also* JOB 27:3; PSA 104:30; ISA 40:28; ACT 17:25, ACT 17:28

### God sustains the life of all people
**PSA 54:4** *See also* 1CH 29:14; PSA 3:5, PSA 18:35, PSA 146:9, PSA 147:6; ISA 46:4; HEB 1:3

### God gives spiritual life
**JHN 10:28** *See also* JHN 5:21, JHN 11:25, JHN 14:6; 1PE 1:23

### God is the source of new life
**PSA 42:2** *See also* PSA 63:1, PSA 84:2; JHN 3:1-3:8
