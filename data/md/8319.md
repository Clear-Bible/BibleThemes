# 8319: perception, spiritual

The ability to see beneath the outward form to the underlying, often hidden, reality. It is a necessary gift in dealing with people, understanding spiritual teaching and interpreting events. Perception is important for effective service, though is often lacking, even among God's people.



## Perception in dealing with people


### God's knowledge of human hearts
**PSA 139:1-139:4** *See also* PSA 17:3, PSA 44:21; PRO 24:12

### Jesus Christ's perception of those around him
**JHN 2:24-2:25** *See also* ISA 11:1-11:4; MAT 9:3-9:4 parallels MRK 2:7-2:8, LUK 5:21-5:22; JHN 4:16-4:19; REV 2:23

### True understanding by God's servants
 NEH 6:1-6:2; ; JOB 21:27; ; ACT 5:1-5:3

## Perception of spiritual truth


### Recognising God's ultimate authority
**REV 4:1-4:2** *See also* 2KI 6:15-6:17; DAN 7:8-7:14

### Understanding Jesus Christ's teaching
**MAT 13:10-13:11** parallels MRK 4:10-4:11, LUK 8:9-8:10 *See also* MAT 13:16-13:23 parallels MRK 4:13-4:20, LUK 8:11-8:15 *The parable of the sower is about the way Jesus Christ's teaching in parables is received.*

### Understanding who Jesus Christ is
**MAT 16:15-16:17** parallels MRK 8:29, LUK 9:20 *See also* ACT 8:30-8:35; ISA 53:7-53:8

### Understanding about Jesus Christ's death and resurrection
**LUK 24:45-24:46** *See also* LUK 24:30-24:35; JHN 12:16

## Perception to interpret events
**2SA 5:11-5:12** *The building of a palace in the newly captured Jerusalem confirms to David his election by God*; **ISA 43:9-43:12** *Through his miraculous direction of his people's history, God is perceived as the one, true God.* *See also* ISA 41:18-41:20, ISA 64:4; JON 1:12 *Jonah recognises God's hand in the storm*; ACT 10:34-10:35 *Through the circumstances of his guidance, Peter realises that God's salvation is for Gentiles too.*

## Perception leading to effective service
**1KI 3:9** parallel 2CH 1:10 *See also* 1SA 16:7-16:13 *Samuel perceives that David is to be king*; 2KI 4:8-4:10 *A Shunammite's perception of Elisha leads her to extend hospitality to him*; HEB 11:27 *Moses' perception of the invisible God*

## Lack of perception


### By those in the OT
**ISA 43:19** *See also* EXO 5:2 *Pharaoh*; NUM 22:31-22:34 *Balaam*; DEU 32:28-32:29 *Israel in the wilderness*; ISA 29:10-29:12 *the people of Jerusalem*; ISA 42:20 *Israel*

### By some Jews in the NT
 MAT 13:13-13:15 parallels MRK 4:12, LUK 8:10; ; ISA 6:9-6:10; ; LUK 12:56; ; JHN 3:3-3:4; ; ACT 28:26

### By Jesus Christ's disciples
 MAT 15:16 parallel MRK 7:18 *not understanding what makes a person unclean*; ; MAT 16:9 parallel MRK 8:17-8:18 *not understanding about the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees*; ; MAT 16:22 parallel MRK 8:32 *Peter cannot accept that Jesus Christ must be crucified*; ; JHN 14:9 *Philip does not understand that Jesus Christ reveals the Father*; ; JHN 20:9 *not understanding about the resurrection*; JHN 20:14, JHN 21:4
