# 8837: unbelief, and life of faith

Believers may go through periods of doubt and indecision, especially on account of difficulties in trusting God or accepting certain aspects of his will for them.



## General examples of unbelief in believers


### Abraham
**GEN 17:17** *See also* GEN 15:8

### Sarah
 GEN 18:13-18:14

### Moses
 NUM 11:21-11:22

### Gideon
 JDG 6:12-6:13, JDG 6:17, JDG 6:36-6:40

### Elisha's servant
 2KI 4:42-4:44

### Zechariah
 LUK 1:20

### John the Baptist
 MAT 11:3

## The unbelief of Jesus Christ's disciples


### Unbelief concerning Jesus Christ's power over the natural world
**MAT 8:24-8:26** parallels MRK 4:38-4:40, LUK 8:23-8:25**MAT 14:26** parallel MRK 6:49**MAT 14:29-14:31, MAT 16:8-16:10** parallel MRK 8:17-8:21 *See also* MRK 6:37 parallel JHN 6:7; MRK 6:52; MAT 15:33 parallel MRK 8:4; JHN 11:39; ACT 12:15-12:17

### Unbelief concerning Jesus Christ's resurrection
**MRK 16:14**; **LUK 24:25** *See also* MRK 16:9-16:13; LUK 24:11, LUK 24:37-24:41; JHN 20:25

### Unbelief concerning Jesus Christ's power to heal
**MAT 17:17** parallels MRK 9:19, LUK 9:41 *See also* MAT 17:19-17:20

## Encouragements in overcoming unbelief
**MRK 9:24**; **JHN 14:11** *See also* MAT 11:4-11:6; JHN 10:37-10:38, JHN 20:30-20:31; ROM 4:20
