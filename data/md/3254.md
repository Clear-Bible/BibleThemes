# 3254: Holy Spirit, fruit of

The living presence of the Holy Spirit in believers leads to Christlike virtues within them, just as a living tree will bear good fruit.



## God expects his people to bear spiritual fruit
**[ISA 5:4](bibleref:23005004)** *See also* [MAT 7:12-7:20](bibleref:40007012-40007020) *“fruit” is a term used to indicate the produce or outcome of a person's life* ; [JHN 15:8](bibleref:43015008)

## The fruit of the Spirit leads to believers becoming Christlike


### The gift of the Spirit begins this lifelong process
**[2CO 3:18](bibleref:47003018)**

### Christlike qualities are contrasted with sinful ones
**[GAL 5:16-5:17](bibleref:48005016-48005017)** *See also* [ROM 8:5-8:14](bibleref:45008005-45008014); [1CO 12:7](bibleref:46012007); [GAL 6:8](bibleref:48006008); [EPH 5:8-5:16](bibleref:49005008-49005016); [COL 3:1-3:17](bibleref:51003001-51003017)

## The fruit of Spirit-filled living
**[GAL 5:22-5:23](bibleref:48005022-48005023)** *“fruit” is singular, emphasising that these qualities are mutually dependent aspects of Christian living.*  *See also* [EPH 5:9](bibleref:49005009)
