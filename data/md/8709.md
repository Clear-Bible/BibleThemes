# 8709: astrology

The study of the stars and planets, based on the pagan assumption that they exercise a decisive influence over human destinies. Scripture rejects such beliefs, insisting upon the total sovereignty of God over his entire creation.

## Astrology is to be rejected


### The worship of heavenly bodies is forbidden
**[DEU 4:19](bibleref:05004019)**; **[2KI 17:16](bibleref:12017016)** *See also* [2KI 21:3-21:5](bibleref:12021003-12021005) parallel 2CH 33:3-33:5; [2KI 23:4](bibleref:12023004), [2KI 23:11](bibleref:12023011); [JOB 31:26-31:28](bibleref:18031026-18031028); [EZK 8:16-8:18](bibleref:26008016-26008018); [ZEP 1:5](bibleref:36001005)

### The consultation of stars is condemned
**[ISA 47:13](bibleref:23047013)** *See also* [JER 8:2](bibleref:24008002)
