# 5489: rank

A person's seniority and/or status, especially in military, government or priestly positions. Christians should not be concerned about rank or status.

## Differences in rank amongst those in charge of both military and civil affairs


### Emperor
 [LUK 2:1](bibleref:42002001), [LUK 3:1](bibleref:42003001)

### King
 [2SA 5:3-5:5](bibleref:10005003-10005005); [1KI 4:1](bibleref:11004001)

### Judge
 [JDG 2:16-2:19](bibleref:07002016-07002019) *The book of Judges calls individual judges “deliverers” e.g., 3:9, 15.* 

### Prince
 [JDG 5:15](bibleref:07005015); [2SA 3:38](bibleref:10003038)

### Leader
 [NUM 2:3-2:7](bibleref:04002003-04002007), [NUM 2:10-2:12](bibleref:04002010-04002012); [JDG 4:4](bibleref:07004004); [1SA 10:1](bibleref:09010001)

## Officers in the NT church
In contrast to the priesthood, offices within the NT Church are according to gifts; individuals with different gifts are listed but do not form a hierarchy:
 [ROM 12:4-12:8](bibleref:45012004-45012008); [1CO 12:28-12:30](bibleref:46012028-46012030); [EPH 4:11](bibleref:49004011)[PHP 1:1](bibleref:50001001); [1TI 3:1-3:13](bibleref:54003001-54003013)

### Some elders are worthy of double honour
 [1TI 5:17](bibleref:54005017) *Comparison between verses 17 and 28 of Acts 20 shows that the same individuals could be called “elders”, “overseers” or “shepherds” (pastors).* 

### All believers are called priests
 [1PE 2:5](bibleref:60002005), [1PE 2:9](bibleref:60002009); [REV 1:6](bibleref:66001006), [REV 5:10](bibleref:66005010), [REV 20:6](bibleref:66020006)

## Jesus Christ taught his disciples not to seek rank or status
 [MAT 18:1-18:4](bibleref:40018001-40018004) parallels MRK 9:33-9:37, LUK 9:46-9:48
