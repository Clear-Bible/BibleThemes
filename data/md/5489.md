# 5489: rank

A person's seniority and/or status, especially in military, government or priestly positions. Christians should not be concerned about rank or status.



## Differences in rank amongst those in charge of both military and civil affairs


### Emperor
 LUK 2:1, LUK 3:1

### King
 2SA 5:3-5:5; ; 1KI 4:1

### Judge
 JDG 2:16-2:19 *The book of Judges calls individual judges “deliverers” e.g., 3:9, 15.*

### Prince
 JDG 5:15; ; 2SA 3:38

### Leader
 NUM 2:3-2:7, NUM 2:10-2:12; ; JDG 4:4; ; 1SA 10:1

## Military ranks


### Commander
 2SA 2:8; ; ACT 24:22

### Captain
 GEN 39:1; ; JDG 5:14; ; 2KI 1:9-1:13; ; ISA 3:3

### Officer
 1KI 4:5, 1KI 9:22; ; 1CH 27:1; ; ACT 25:23

### Centurion
 MAT 8:5; ; ACT 27:43

## Civil ranks


### Noble
 EST 1:3, EST 2:18

### Official
 1CH 28:1; ; JER 52:10

### Governor
 1KI 4:7-4:19; ; HAG 1:1; ; ACT 23:24

### Tetrarch
 LUK 3:1

### Heads of clans/tribes
 JOS 14:1; ; 1KI 8:1

### Heads of families
 NUM 7:2; ; JOS 2:12-2:13

### Division of labour involving different degrees of responsibility
 EXO 18:20-18:23

## Rank was conferred as a reward
 1SA 18:5

## Rank within the priesthood


### The high priest
 LEV 21:10; ; NUM 35:25, NUM 35:28, NUM 35:32; ; HAG 1:1; ; ZEC 3:1; ; LUK 3:2

### Priests
 EXO 28:1-28:2; ; LEV 8:5-8:12; ; NUM 4:16; ; JOS 22:31; ; 2KI 11:9

### Levites
 EXO 32:26; ; NUM 1:50; ; DEU 12:18-12:19; ; 2CH 11:13-11:14

## Officers in the NT church
In contrast to the priesthood, offices within the NT Church are according to gifts; individuals with different gifts are listed but do not form a hierarchy:
 ROM 12:4-12:8; ; 1CO 12:28-12:30; ; EPH 4:11; PHP 1:1; ; 1TI 3:1-3:13

### Some elders are worthy of double honour
 1TI 5:17 *Comparison between verses 17 and 28 of Acts 20 shows that the same individuals could be called “elders”, “overseers” or “shepherds” (pastors).*

### All believers are called priests
 1PE 2:5, 1PE 2:9; ; REV 1:6, REV 5:10, REV 20:6

## Jesus Christ taught his disciples not to seek rank or status
 MAT 18:1-18:4 parallels MRK 9:33-9:37, LUK 9:46-9:48
