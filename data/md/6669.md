# 6669: grace, and salvation

Deliverance through Jesus Christ is the result of accepting God's undeserved favour.



## Salvation is all God's doing
**ROM 5:6-5:8, ROM 9:14-9:16**; **EPH 1:7**; **1TI 1:15-1:16** *See also* EXO 33:19; ACT 4:12, ACT 20:24; ROM 5:15-5:17; 2CO 6:2; COL 1:13-1:14; 2TH 2:16; TIT 2:11; HEB 7:23-7:25; REV 7:10

## There is nothing human beings can do to save themselves
**TIT 3:4-3:7** *See also* LUK 18:9-18:14; ROM 11:5-11:6

## Salvation is not by keeping God's law
**GAL 5:4** *See also* ROM 5:20-5:21, ROM 6:14, ROM 8:1-8:4; GAL 2:21, GAL 3:17-3:18; 1TI 1:9

## Salvation must be accepted as a free gift by faith
**EPH 2:4-2:9** *See also* ACT 15:7-15:11, ACT 16:30-16:31; ROM 3:21-3:24, ROM 4:14-4:16, ROM 5:1-5:2; HEB 4:16
