# 5901: loneliness

God intends human beings to live together in harmony and to value one another, but Scripture recognises many reasons for individuals to feel unwanted and isolated.



## Loneliness is not part of God's plan for human beings
**GEN 2:18** *See also* PSA 68:5-68:6; ISA 49:15

## God is with his people at all times and in all circumstances
**ISA 41:10** *See also* DEU 31:8; JOS 1:9; PSA 27:10; ISA 43:2; MAT 28:20; JHN 14:18; HEB 13:5-13:6

## Reasons why people may experience loneliness


### Sin
**ISA 59:2** *See also* GEN 4:10-4:12; 1SA 28:5-28:6; PSA 81:11-81:12; ISA 64:7; HOS 5:6; EPH 4:18

### Remorse
**MAT 27:4-27:5** *See also* 1CH 21:17; MAT 26:75

### Lack of friends
**PSA 142:4** *See also* JHN 5:7, JHN 14:18

### Social ostracism
**JHN 4:9** *A moral outcast (see Jn 4:18), she was collecting water at the hottest part of the day, when she could do so alone.* *See also* LUK 19:7 *A Jew who worked for the Roman occupying power was considered a traitor.*

### Public shame
**JHN 8:3** *See also* MRK 15:29-15:32

### Desertion
**2TI 4:16** *See also* PSA 38:11, PSA 88:18

### Loss of family
**LUK 7:12** *See also* RUT 1:3-1:5; 2SA 18:33; 2KI 4:1; JOB 1:20-1:21; PSA 27:10; MAT 2:17-2:18; JER 31:15; 1TI 5:5

### Loss of identity
**LUK 8:27** parallel MRK 5:2-5:3 *See also* LUK 8:30 parallel MRK 5:9

### Discontent
**ECC 4:8** *See also* ECC 2:10-2:11, ECC 2:18; HEB 13:5-13:6

### Disability
**ACT 3:2** *See also* LEV 21:17-21:18; MAT 9:32, MAT 12:22

### Disease
**LUK 17:12** *Lepers were forbidden to mix with those not affected.* *See also* LEV 13:4; NUM 5:1-5:3; 2KI 7:3; 2CH 26:21

### Insomnia
**PSA 102:7** *See also* JOB 7:4; DAN 6:18

### Depression
**1KI 19:4** *See also* DEU 33:27; JOB 7:16; PSA 43:5, PSA 73:16-73:17; JER 15:10; MIC 7:1

### Poverty
**PRO 19:7** *See also* 1KI 17:12; PRO 19:4; LUK 15:14-15:16

### Old age
**PSA 71:9** *See also* 2SA 19:34-19:35

### Dying and the approach of death
**PSA 23:4** *See also* PSA 18:4-18:6; ROM 8:38-8:39
