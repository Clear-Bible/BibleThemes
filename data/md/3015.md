# 3015: Holy Spirit, divinity of

The Holy Spirit's attributes and activities are always those of God. The titles used of the Holy Spirit identify him as part of the triune nature of the divine being.



## The Holy Spirit possesses the attributes of God


### He is present everywhere
**[PSA 139:7-139:8](bibleref:19139007-19139008)** *See also* [JHN 14:17](bibleref:43014017)

### He knows all things
**[ISA 40:13](bibleref:23040013)** *NIV footnote.*  *See also* [JHN 16:13](bibleref:43016013); [1CO 2:10-2:11](bibleref:46002010-46002011)

### He has infinite power
**[ZEC 4:6](bibleref:38004006)** *See also* [LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035)

### He is eternal
**[HEB 9:14](bibleref:58009014)** *See also* [JHN 14:16](bibleref:43014016)

### He is unique
**[EPH 4:4-4:6](bibleref:49004004-49004006)** *See also* [1CO 12:4](bibleref:46012004)

### He is holy
**[ROM 1:4](bibleref:45001004)** *See also* [ROM 5:5](bibleref:45005005); [1CO 6:19](bibleref:46006019) *The Christian's body is also holy because it is the place where God dwells by his Spirit* ; [2CO 6:6](bibleref:47006006)[EPH 1:13](bibleref:49001013), [EPH 4:30](bibleref:49004030)
