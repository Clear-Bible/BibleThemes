# 2580: Jesus Christ, transfiguration of

The revelation of the glory of Jesus Christ, shortly before his death, at which his disciples caught sight of him in his full majesty. The transfiguration brought home to the disciples that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.



## The glory of Jesus Christ is revealed at the transfiguration


### Jesus Christ prepares for his glory to be revealed
**[LUK 9:28](bibleref:42009028)** parallels MAT 17:1, MRK 9:2

### Jesus Christ is visibly transfigured
**[MAT 17:2](bibleref:40017002)** parallels MRK 9:2-9:3, LUK 9:29 *The transfiguration was a glimpse of Jesus Christ's glory whereby his physical appearance was visibly changed.* 

### The disciples are eye-witnesses of Jesus Christ's glory
**[MAT 17:6](bibleref:40017006)** *See also* [MRK 9:9](bibleref:41009009); [LUK 9:32](bibleref:42009032); [JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014); [2PE 1:16](bibleref:61001016)

## Moses and Elijah appear at the transfiguration
**[LUK 9:30-9:31](bibleref:42009030-42009031)** parallels MAT 17:3, MRK 9:4 *“departure” refers to Jesus Christ's death.* 

## God's voice is heard at the transfiguration
**[MAT 17:5](bibleref:40017005)** parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35 *God the Father's voice is heard authenticating Jesus Christ's ministry.*  *See also* [MAT 3:17](bibleref:40003017) parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; [2PE 1:17-1:18](bibleref:61001017-61001018)

## The disciples' response to the transfiguration


### Peter's wish to build shelters
 [MAT 17:4](bibleref:40017004) parallels MRK 9:5-9:6, LUK 9:33

### Fear
 [MAT 17:6-17:7](bibleref:40017006-40017007); [MRK 9:6](bibleref:41009006); [LUK 9:34](bibleref:42009034)

### Questioning and confusion
 [MAT 17:10](bibleref:40017010) parallel MRK 9:10-9:11

### They keep to themselves what they have seen and heard
**[LUK 9:36](bibleref:42009036)** parallels MAT 17:9-17:10, MRK 9:9-9:10 *These verses reiterate that Jesus Christ's full glory was hidden from the world until his resurrection.*
