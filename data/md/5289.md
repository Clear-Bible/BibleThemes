# 5289: debt

God is the Maker and sustainer of all things and therefore all creatures owe everything to him. Scripture provides guidance concerning both debt and debtors.

**[1CO 4:7](bibleref:46004007)** *See also* [DEU 8:17-8:18](bibleref:05008017-05008018); [1CH 29:11-29:13](bibleref:13029011-13029013); [PSA 24:1-24:2](bibleref:19024001-19024002); [ECC 5:19](bibleref:21005019); [ACT 14:17](bibleref:44014017), [ACT 17:25](bibleref:44017025); [1TI 6:17](bibleref:54006017)
