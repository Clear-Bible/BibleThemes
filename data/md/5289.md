# 5289: debt

God is the Maker and sustainer of all things and therefore all creatures owe everything to him. Scripture provides guidance concerning both debt and debtors.



## Everyone is in debt to God as Creator
**1CO 4:7** *See also* DEU 8:17-8:18; 1CH 29:11-29:13; PSA 24:1-24:2; ECC 5:19; ACT 14:17, ACT 17:25; 1TI 6:17

## Everyone is in debt to God as law-giver
**ROM 3:19**; **JAS 2:10** *See also* DEU 27:26; PSA 49:7-49:8; MAL 3:8-3:10; LUK 7:41-7:42; GAL 3:10, GAL 5:3

## Believers are in debt to God as Redeemer


### He has paid their debt
**GAL 3:13** *See also* 2CO 8:9; 1PE 1:18; REV 5:9

### A sense of debt inspires devotion to God
**PSA 116:12** *See also* LUK 7:41-7:47; ROM 8:12; 1CO 6:20

### Indebtedness to God affects attitude to others
**MAT 10:8** *See also* MAT 6:12 parallel LUK 11:4; MAT 18:21-18:35; 1JN 3:16, 1JN 4:11

## Examples of people in debt
 1SA 22:2; ; 2KI 4:1; ; NEH 5:4; ; MAT 5:25-5:26, MAT 18:23-18:35; ; LUK 16:1-16:8; ; ROM 4:4; ; JAS 5:4

## Debts must be paid
 LEV 19:13; ; 2KI 4:7; ; PSA 37:21; ; ROM 13:7-13:8

## Social ills result from debt
 NEH 5:3-5:5; ; JOB 24:3, JOB 24:9; ; PSA 109:11; ; EZK 18:12-18:13, EZK 22:12; ; AMO 2:6

## OT laws sought to minimise debt


### By commanding generous lending to the poor
 EXO 22:25; ; LEV 25:35-25:36; ; DEU 15:7-15:11; ; PSA 15:5; ; PRO 19:17

### By curbing exploitation of debtors
 LEV 25:37-25:43; ; DEU 24:6

### By cancelling debts every seventh year
 DEU 15:1-15:3, DEU 31:10; ; NEH 10:31

## Preventatives against needless debt


### Keeping short accounts
 MAT 5:25; ; ROM 13:8

### Remembering the evil consequences
 PRO 22:7; ; MAT 5:26

## Debts to the state


### Paying taxes
**MAT 22:17-22:21** parallels MRK 12:14-12:17, LUK 20:22-20:25 *See also* ROM 13:6-13:7

### Civic honour
**ROM 13:7**

## Debts to employees
**ROM 4:4** *See also* LEV 19:13; JAS 5:4

## Debts within the church


### Towards Christian workers
**PHM 1:18-1:19** *See also* 1CO 9:7-9:12; GAL 6:6

### Towards the unsaved
**ROM 1:14**

### The indebtedness of the Gentile church towards the Jerusalem church
**ROM 15:26-15:27**

## Debts to be cancelled
**MAT 6:12** parallel LUK 11:4 *See also* 1CO 13:5

## Debts one should never pay
**ROM 12:17** *See also* 1PE 3:9

## A debt one can never cease paying
**ROM 13:8-13:10**
