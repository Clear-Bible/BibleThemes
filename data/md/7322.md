# 7322: burnt offering

Probably the earliest and most basic form of sacrifice. It is seen fundamentally as a gift to God, either in thanksgiving for his goodness or for atonement for sin.

## Hebrew names for the burnt offering
**[DEU 33:10](bibleref:05033010)** *The rarer Hebrew expression means something whole or complete* ; **[PSA 66:13](bibleref:19066013)** *The commoner expression in Hebrew means that which “goes up” to God.*  *See also* [1SA 7:9](bibleref:09007009); [PSA 51:19](bibleref:19051019)
