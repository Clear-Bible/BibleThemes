# 8462: priority, of God

Scripture provides examples of individuals who have allowed God to take priority in their lives, and also of those who have allowed something or someone else to take priority.



## God's glory made a priority
**[MAT 6:9](bibleref:40006009)**

### Commands to put God first
**[MAT 4:10](bibleref:40004010)** parallel LUK 4:8 *See also* [DEU 6:13](bibleref:05006013); [1CH 16:29](bibleref:13016029)

### Examples of putting God first
 [GEN 5:22](bibleref:01005022), [GEN 5:24](bibleref:01005024) *Enoch* ; [GEN 6:9](bibleref:01006009) *Noah* ; [GEN 12:4](bibleref:01012004) *Abraham* ; [JOS 24:15](bibleref:06024015) *Joshua* ; [2KI 20:3](bibleref:12020003) *Hezekiah* ; [DAN 3:16-3:18](bibleref:27003016-27003018) *Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego* ; [DAN 6:10](bibleref:27006010) *Daniel* ; [JHN 3:29-3:30](bibleref:43003029-43003030) *John the Baptist* 

### Disapproval of not putting God first
**[EXO 20:3](bibleref:02020003)** *See also* [LUK 16:13](bibleref:42016013)

### Examples of not putting God first
**[ACT 12:22-12:23](bibleref:44012022-44012023)** *See also* [1KI 11:4](bibleref:11011004) *Solomon* ; [1KI 15:3](bibleref:11015003) *Abijah* ; [2CH 25:2](bibleref:14025002) *Amaziah* ; [JER 3:10](bibleref:24003010) *Judah* ; [ACT 5:1-5:5](bibleref:44005001-44005005) *Ananias and Sapphira* ; [ROM 16:17-16:18](bibleref:45016017-45016018)

### Worship commended as the highest human activity
**[PSA 95:6](bibleref:19095006)** *See also* [PSA 96:9](bibleref:19096009), [PSA 99:5](bibleref:19099005); [ZEC 14:17](bibleref:38014017); [JHN 4:24](bibleref:43004024)
