# 8462: priority, of God

Scripture provides examples of individuals who have allowed God to take priority in their lives, and also of those who have allowed something or someone else to take priority.



## God's glory made a priority
**MAT 6:9**

### Commands to put God first
**MAT 4:10** parallel LUK 4:8 *See also* DEU 6:13; 1CH 16:29

### Examples of putting God first
 GEN 5:22, GEN 5:24 *Enoch*; ; GEN 6:9 *Noah*; ; GEN 12:4 *Abraham*; ; JOS 24:15 *Joshua*; ; 2KI 20:3 *Hezekiah*; ; DAN 3:16-3:18 *Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego*; ; DAN 6:10 *Daniel*; ; JHN 3:29-3:30 *John the Baptist*

### Disapproval of not putting God first
**EXO 20:3** *See also* LUK 16:13

### Examples of not putting God first
**ACT 12:22-12:23** *See also* 1KI 11:4 *Solomon*; 1KI 15:3 *Abijah*; 2CH 25:2 *Amaziah*; JER 3:10 *Judah*; ACT 5:1-5:5 *Ananias and Sapphira*; ROM 16:17-16:18

### Worship commended as the highest human activity
**PSA 95:6** *See also* PSA 96:9, PSA 99:5; ZEC 14:17; JHN 4:24

### Outstanding examples of worship
**2SA 6:14-6:15** *David was prepared to be despised by his wife rather than inhibit his worship.* *See also* EXO 3:6; ISA 6:3; EZK 1:28; DAN 6:10; JHN 9:38; REV 1:17

## God's kingdom made a priority
**MAT 6:10**

### Commands to participate in the kingdom
**MAT 6:33** *See also* LUK 19:11-19:27

### Examples of wholehearted participation
**ACT 26:19** *See also* HEB 6:12, HEB 12:1-12:2

### Warning against half-hearted participation
 REV 2:4-2:5

### Sacrifices for the kingdom advocated
**REV 2:10** *See also* MAT 5:29 parallel MRK 9:47; MAT 18:8

### Examples of sacrifices for the kingdom
**HEB 11:26** *See also* 2CO 11:23-11:30; PHP 2:21-2:22, PHP 2:30; REV 2:13

## God's will made a priority
**MAT 6:10**

### Conformity to God's will commanded
**EPH 5:17** *See also* 1TH 4:3, 1TH 5:18; HEB 13:20-13:21; JAS 4:15

### Outstanding examples of conformity to God's will
**MAT 26:39** parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42 *See also* JHN 4:34, JHN 6:38; ACT 20:22-20:24
