# 8701: affluence

Literally, “flowing” with prosperity. Wealth is not condemned in Scripture but is seen as a good gift of God. However, affluence has its dangers.



## Godly affluence
**EXO 3:8** *See also* DEU 28:1-28:6, DEU 28:11; PSA 1:3, PSA 112:3; PRO 8:18-8:21, PRO 22:4; ISA 60:5, ISA 61:6, ISA 66:12; MIC 4:4

### Examples of affluence
 GEN 2:8-2:12 *Eden*; ; GEN 13:2 *Abraham*; ; GEN 26:12-26:13 *Isaac*; ; GEN 33:11 *Jacob*; ; JOB 42:10-42:12 *Job*; 1KI 3:13, 1KI 10:23-10:29; 
2CH 32:27-32:29 *Hezekiah*; ; MAT 27:57 *Joseph of Arimathea*

## The dangers of affluence


### Pride
**DEU 8:17-8:18**; **1TI 6:17** *See also* PSA 10:4-10:6, PSA 73:3-73:6

### Reliance upon riches
**PRO 11:28**; **1TI 6:17** *See also* PSA 62:10; PRO 23:5; ECC 10:19; MRK 10:24; LUK 12:16-12:21

### Over-attachment to riches
**MAT 19:22** parallels MRK 10:22, LUK 18:23

### Greed for more riches
**ECC 5:10**

### Temptation
**1TI 6:9-6:10**

### Cares and anxieties
**MAT 13:22** parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14

### Spiritual complacency
**REV 3:17**

### Contempt for God
 *See also* PRO 30:8-30:9

### Contempt for the poor or unfortunate
 JOB 12:5; ; PRO 22:16; ; AMO 2:6-2:8, AMO 4:1, AMO 5:11; ; LUK 16:24-16:31; ; JAS 5:1-5:6

## Affluence is temporary


### It is for this life only
**1TI 6:7** *See also* JOB 1:21; PSA 37:35-37:36, PSA 49:10-49:12, PSA 49:16-49:20; ECC 5:15

### It is often lost
 JOB 1:13-1:15; ; ECC 5:13-5:14
