# 1466: vision

Scripture uses the term to refer to both the physical act of seeing, and also the gift of spiritual insight. All things, physical and spiritual, are seen by God.

## God's vision


### God views all life on earth
**[2CH 16:9](bibleref:14016009)**; **[PSA 11:4](bibleref:19011004)** *See also* [GEN 6:11](bibleref:01006011); [EXO 3:9](bibleref:02003009); [PSA 31:22](bibleref:19031022), [PSA 34:15](bibleref:19034015), [PSA 139:16](bibleref:19139016); [ZEC 4:10](bibleref:38004010)

### God sees the hearts and thoughts of people
**[GEN 6:5](bibleref:01006005)** *See also* [1SA 2:3](bibleref:09002003); [PSA 139:1-139:4](bibleref:19139001-19139004), [PSA 139:23-139:24](bibleref:19139023-19139024); [PRO 15:11](bibleref:20015011); [JER 20:12](bibleref:24020012); [MAT 6:8](bibleref:40006008), [MAT 6:18](bibleref:40006018); [LUK 16:15](bibleref:42016015); [HEB 4:13](bibleref:58004013); [1JN 3:20](bibleref:62003020)

## The physical vision of human beings


### It is given and can be taken by God
**[EXO 4:11](bibleref:02004011)**; **[PSA 146:8](bibleref:19146008)** *See also* [LEV 26:16](bibleref:03026016); [PRO 29:13](bibleref:20029013); [ACT 9:8-9:9](bibleref:44009008-44009009) parallel ACT 22:11

### Jesus Christ restores the sight of the blind
**[LUK 4:18](bibleref:42004018)** *See also* [MAT 9:30](bibleref:40009030); [JHN 9:7](bibleref:43009007); [ACT 9:17-9:18](bibleref:44009017-44009018) parallel ACT 22:13

### Physical sight diminishes with age
 [GEN 27:1](bibleref:01027001), [GEN 48:10](bibleref:01048010); [1SA 3:2](bibleref:09003002), [1SA 4:15](bibleref:09004015); [1KI 14:4](bibleref:11014004); [ECC 12:3](bibleref:21012003) *Ecc 12:2-5 is an allegory on ageing. It gives a graphic description of a person's progressive deterioration.*

**[2KI 6:17](bibleref:12006017)** *See also* [PRO 29:18](bibleref:20029018); [ISA 22:1](bibleref:23022001) *Jerusalem was called “the Valley of Vision”, possibly ironically, for although God spoke through his prophets and dwelt there, its inhabitants lacked insight* ; [ISA 29:10](bibleref:23029010); [MRK 8:17-8:21](bibleref:41008017-41008021); [LUK 24:31](bibleref:42024031); [JHN 9:39](bibleref:43009039); [ACT 26:17-26:18](bibleref:44026017-44026018)
