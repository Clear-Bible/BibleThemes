# 1466: vision

Scripture uses the term to refer to both the physical act of seeing, and also the gift of spiritual insight. All things, physical and spiritual, are seen by God.



## God's vision


### God views all life on earth
**2CH 16:9**; **PSA 11:4** *See also* GEN 6:11; EXO 3:9; PSA 31:22, PSA 34:15, PSA 139:16; ZEC 4:10

### God sees the hearts and thoughts of people
**GEN 6:5** *See also* 1SA 2:3; PSA 139:1-139:4, PSA 139:23-139:24; PRO 15:11; JER 20:12; MAT 6:8, MAT 6:18; LUK 16:15; HEB 4:13; 1JN 3:20

## The physical vision of human beings


### It is given and can be taken by God
**EXO 4:11**; **PSA 146:8** *See also* LEV 26:16; PRO 29:13; ACT 9:8-9:9 parallel ACT 22:11

### Jesus Christ restores the sight of the blind
**LUK 4:18** *See also* MAT 9:30; JHN 9:7; ACT 9:17-9:18 parallel ACT 22:13

### Physical sight diminishes with age
 GEN 27:1, GEN 48:10; ; 1SA 3:2, 1SA 4:15; ; 1KI 14:4; ; ECC 12:3 *Ecc 12:2-5 is an allegory on ageing. It gives a graphic description of a person's progressive deterioration.*

## Physical vision has its dangers


### It can create fear
 JOB 41:9; ; JOL 2:6; ; MAT 28:3-28:4 parallels MRK 16:5, LUK 24:4-24:5; ; REV 1:17

### It gives rise to temptation
**1JN 2:16** *See also* GEN 3:6; JOS 7:21; 2SA 11:2-11:4; JOB 31:1; PSA 119:37; MAT 5:28-5:29

## Christians are to live by faith rather than by sight
**HEB 11:1** *See also* ROM 8:24; 2CO 4:18, 2CO 5:7; HEB 11:27

## Visions of God


### Examples of those who saw God
 GEN 32:30; ; EXO 33:18-33:23; ; JOB 42:5; ; ISA 6:1, ISA 6:5; ; REV 1:12-1:18

### God's people will see him
**MAT 5:8**; **1JN 3:2** *See also* PSA 17:15; JHN 17:24; 1CO 13:12; REV 22:4

## Spiritual vision


### Seeing used metaphorically for spiritual insight
**2KI 6:17** *See also* PRO 29:18; ISA 22:1 *Jerusalem was called “the Valley of Vision”, possibly ironically, for although God spoke through his prophets and dwelt there, its inhabitants lacked insight*; ISA 29:10; MRK 8:17-8:21; LUK 24:31; JHN 9:39; ACT 26:17-26:18

### Spiritual insight given in dreams or visions
**ACT 2:17** *See also* JOL 2:28; GEN 15:1; NUM 12:6; DAN 1:17, DAN 2:19, DAN 4:5, DAN 7:1 *Daniel's dream of four beasts*; DAN 8:1, DAN 10:7-10:8 *Daniel's vision of a man*; ACT 9:12, ACT 10:9-10:17 parallel ACT 11:4-11:10 *Peter's vision*; ACT 16:9-16:10 *Paul's vision of the man of Macedonia*; ACT 26:19; 2CO 12:1

### The OT prophets had visions from God
 1SA 3:15; ; ISA 1:1; ; JER 24:1; ; EZK 1:1, EZK 8:3-8:4, EZK 11:24, EZK 40:2-40:3; ; HOS 12:10; ; OBA 1:1; ; NAM 1:1

### False prophets in the OT lacked true vision
 JER 14:14, JER 23:16; ; LAM 2:14; ; EZK 7:26, EZK 13:1-13:12
