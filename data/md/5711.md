# 5711: marriage, restrictions concerning

The OT law forbade intermarriage with people who worshipped idols because it threatened the covenant relationship with God and his people. Marriage with close relations was also forbidden. Remarriage is permissible following the death of a spouse and, in certain circumstances, following divorce.



## Intermarriage with foreigners


### Examples of marriages to foreigners
 GEN 38:2, GEN 41:45; ; EXO 2:21; ; LEV 24:10; ; RUT 1:4; ; 1CH 2:34-2:35; ; EZR 10:18-10:44

### Warnings against marrying foreigners
**EXO 34:16** *See also* DEU 7:3-7:4; JOS 23:12-23:13

### Intermarriage with foreigners led to idolatry
**JDG 3:5-3:6**; **1KI 11:1-11:8** *Royal marriages to foreigners brought disastrous consequences.* *See also* 1KI 16:31; MAL 2:11EZR 9:1-9:2, EZR 9:14, EZR 10:1-10:2; NEH 13:23-13:27

### Parents sought to avoid their children marrying foreigners
**NEH 10:30** *See also* GEN 24:3-24:4 *Abraham seeks a bride for Isaac from among his own people.*GEN 26:34-26:35, GEN 27:46
GEN 28:1-28:2 *Isaac forbids Jacob to marry a Canaanite woman*; JDG 14:3 *Samson's parents encourage him to marry an Israelite.*

### Marriage to foreigners may be permissible
 RUT 4:13 *Boaz' marriage to Ruth the Moabitess was acceptable because Ruth embraced the Israelite faith. Restrictions were on the grounds of religion not of race.*

### Foreign wives were put aside after the exile
**EZR 10:3** *See also* EZR 10:10-10:17

## Marriages between Christians and unbelievers


### Christians should not marry unbelievers
 1CO 7:39; ; 2CO 6:14

### Christians should not leave an unbelieving spouse
 1CO 7:12-7:16

## Restrictions on marriage to close relatives


### Sexual relations with close relatives is forbidden
**LEV 18:6-18:18** *See also* LEV 20:11-20:12, LEV 20:14, LEV 20:19-20:21; DEU 22:30; EZK 22:10-22:11; MAT 14:3-14:4 parallel MRK 6:17-6:18

### Examples of marriage to close relatives
 GEN 20:12 *Abraham was married to his half-sister*; ; 2SA 13:13 *Tamar suggests marriage with her half-brother, Amnon.*

## Levirate marriage


### The levirate law was instituted to preserve the dead father's name
**DEU 25:5-25:10**; **RUT 4:10** *Levirate marriage refers to the legal obligation of a brother-in-law to produce heirs for his dead brother by marrying his widow. When there was no brother-in-law responsibility fell to a near relative, also described as a “kinsman-redeemer”.* *See also* GEN 38:8, GEN 38:11; RUT 1:11-1:13, RUT 3:9, RUT 4:5; MAT 22:24-22:26 parallels MRK 12:19-12:22, LUK 20:28-20:31

### Unwillingness to fulfil the levirate law
 GEN 38:9, GEN 38:14, GEN 38:26; ; RUT 4:6

## Regulations governing seduction and rape
 EXO 22:16; ; DEU 22:28-22:29

## Remarriage


### Widows are free to remarry
**ROM 7:2-7:3** *See also* RUT 1:9; 1CO 7:8-7:9; 1TI 5:14

### Remarriage after divorce may be adultery
**LUK 16:18** parallels MAT 19:9, MRK 10:11-10:12 *See also* 1CO 7:10-7:11

### Remarriage after divorce permissible in certain circumstances
 DEU 24:1-24:4; ; MAT 5:32; ; 1CO 7:15, 1CO 7:27-7:28
