# 4690: wings

Limbs which enable flight. In Scripture these are mainly used either of angelic beings or in various symbolic senses, especially of God's protection.

## Wings of birds or insects
 [GEN 7:14](bibleref:01007014); [LEV 1:17](bibleref:03001017); [JOB 39:13](bibleref:18039013), [JOB 39:26](bibleref:18039026); [ISA 18:1](bibleref:23018001)

### Clean and unclean winged creatures
 [LEV 11:13-11:25](bibleref:03011013-03011025); [DEU 14:11-14:20](bibleref:05014011-05014020); [ACT 10:12-10:15](bibleref:44010012-44010015)
