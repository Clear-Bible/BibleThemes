# 4690: wings

Limbs which enable flight. In Scripture these are mainly used either of angelic beings or in various symbolic senses, especially of God's protection.



## Wings of birds or insects
 GEN 7:14; ; LEV 1:17; ; JOB 39:13, JOB 39:26; ; ISA 18:1

### Clean and unclean winged creatures
 LEV 11:13-11:25; ; DEU 14:11-14:20; ; ACT 10:12-10:15

## Wings of angelic beings


### Cherubim
**EXO 25:20** *See also* EXO 37:9; 1KI 8:6-8:7 parallel 2CH 5:7-5:8; 1CH 28:18; 2CH 3:11-3:13; EZK 1:5-1:25, EZK 3:13, EZK 10:5-10:22

### Other angelic beings
**ISA 6:2** *See also* ISA 6:6; REV 4:8

## Wings as a metaphor of protection


### Birds protect their young
 DEU 32:11; ; ISA 34:15; ; MAT 23:37 parallel LUK 13:34

### God's protection likened to a bird's wings
**PSA 91:4** *See also* EXO 19:4; RUT 2:12; PSA 17:8, PSA 36:7, PSA 57:1, PSA 61:4, PSA 63:7; ISA 31:5

### Egypt's protection likened to an eagle's wings
 EZK 17:7 *Zedekiah's fruitless appeal to Egypt for help against Babylon*

## Wings as a metaphor for God's judgment


### Judgment on Israel
**DEU 28:49** *See also* ISA 8:7-8:8; EZK 17:3-17:4; HOS 8:1; HAB 1:8; ZEC 5:9

### Judgment on others
 JER 48:40, JER 49:22; ; REV 9:9

## Other figurative uses of wings


### God rides on the wings of the wind
**2SA 22:11** parallel PSA 18:10 *See also* PSA 104:3

### God renews his people's strength
**ISA 40:31**

### Israel described as a winged bird
 *See also* PSA 68:13; ISA 10:14

### Wings symbolise the ability to escape
**PSA 55:6** *See also* REV 12:14

### Wings symbolise instability
**PRO 23:5** *See also* ECC 10:20

### Wings symbolise strength
**DAN 7:6** *See also* DAN 7:4

### Wings of the dawn
 PSA 139:9

### Wings of the sun
 MAL 4:2 *God is likened to the rays of the sun.*
