# 8666: praise, manner and methods of

Praise is the natural response of believers to God at all times and in all places, involving adoration in music and song. God himself assists believers to praise him through his Spirit.



## The manner of praise


### God is praised in faith
**PSA 28:7, PSA 106:12**

### God is praised through Jesus Christ
**HEB 13:15** *See also* PHP 2:9-2:11

### God can be praised at any time and in any place
**PSA 104:33, PSA 145:2** *See also* PSA 146:2

### God helps believers to praise him
**PSA 51:15** *See also* PSA 40:3; ISA 61:3

### The Holy Spirit moves believers to praise
**LUK 1:67**; **EPH 5:18-5:20** *See also* LUK 10:21; ACT 2:11, ACT 10:44-10:46; COL 3:16-3:17

## The methods of praise


### In singing
**EPH 5:19** *See also* NEH 12:46; PSA 149:1; ISA 42:10; LUK 1:46-1:47, LUK 1:68; ACT 16:25; COL 3:16

### With musical instruments
**PSA 150:3-150:5** *See also* 1CH 25:3; 2CH 7:6; PSA 33:2, PSA 92:1-92:3; ISA 38:20

### In dancing
**PSA 149:3** *See also* EXO 15:19-15:20; 2SA 6:14; PSA 150:4

### With thanksgiving
**PSA 42:4, PSA 100:4** *See also* 2CH 5:13

## Hallelujah as a frequent expression of praise to God
**PSA 106:48** *“Hallelujah” translates “praise the LORD”.* *See also* PSA 111:1, PSA 113:1, PSA 135:1, PSA 149:1, PSA 150:1; REV 19:3-19:4

### Praise for his patient love
**PSA 106:1** *See also* PSA 117:2

### Praise for his election of Israel
**PSA 135:3-135:4** *See also* 1CH 16:36; PSA 148:14

### Praise for his sovereign rule
**PSA 146:10**; **REV 19:6** *See also* REV 19:1

## Hosanna as an acclamation of praise


### At Jerusalem
**MAT 21:9** parallels MRK 11:9-11:10, JHN 12:13 *“Hosanna” is the Greek form of the Hebrew term seeking God's salvation.* *See also* MAT 21:5

### The OT background
**PSA 118:25-118:26** *See also* 1CH 16:35-16:36; PSA 79:9, PSA 106:47

## ``Selah'' in the context of praise


### Following the assurance of answered prayer
**PSA 3:4** *There are a number of possible ways of understanding this obscure term: pausing or lifting up, perhaps indicating that the singers or musicians should sing or play “forte”.* *See also* PSA 21:2, PSA 24:6, PSA 32:5, PSA 81:7, PSA 84:8

### Following an expression of deliverance
**PSA 3:8** *See also* PSA 32:7, PSA 49:15, PSA 57:3, PSA 68:19, PSA 76:9; HAB 3:13

### After a statement comparing God's greatness with human insignificance
**PSA 9:20** *See also* PSA 39:5, PSA 39:11, PSA 47:4, PSA 52:5, PSA 55:19, PSA 59:5, PSA 67:4, PSA 75:3, PSA 89:37; HAB 3:3

### After an affirmation of security in God
**PSA 46:7** *See also* PSA 46:11, PSA 48:8, PSA 61:4

### On reflection of evil opposition
**PSA 54:3** *See also* PSA 62:4, PSA 140:3, PSA 140:5
