# 5971: uniqueness

The quality of being without an equal. Scripture stresses the uniqueness of God and of the salvation that is available only from him. Jesus Christ is the unique mediator between God and humanity.



## The uniqueness of God
**ISA 40:25** *See also* EXO 8:10; 1SA 2:2; ISA 40:18; 1TI 1:17, 1TI 6:15-6:16; JUD 1:25

### As a forgiver of sins
**MRK 2:7**

## The uniqueness of the Trinity


### The three persons are distinct
All use the first person singular pronoun:
 JHN 12:28, JHN 17:4; ; ACT 13:2; 
1CO 12:4-12:6; ; EPH 4:4-4:5

### God is one
**DEU 6:4** *See also* ISA 44:6, ISA 45:5-45:6; EPH 4:6; JAS 2:9

### There is plurality in the Godhead

### As God's Son

**1TI 2:5** *See also* JHN 1:14; HEB 8:6

**HEB 7:27** *See also* ROM 6:10; HEB 9:12, HEB 10:10, HEB 10:14, HEB 10:18
