# 9615: hope, results of

Hope gives believers confidence and reassurance in this present life, allowing them to lead effective lives for God. It also reassures them of the reality of eternal life, allowing them to face death with confidence.



## Hope reassures believers in this present life


### Hope reassures believers in their faith
**HEB 3:6** *See also* EPH 1:18-1:19; HEB 7:18-7:22, HEB 10:23

### Hope encourages believers
**PSA 31:2** *See also* ISA 40:31, ISA 49:23; ROM 5:3-5:5

### Hope encourages believers to rejoice
**ROM 12:12** *See also* ROM 5:1-5:2

### Hope encourages believers to look for restoration
 PSA 37:9; ; JER 14:8, JER 31:17; ; LAM 3:29-3:31; ; HOS 2:15; ; ZEC 9:12

## Hope leads to more effective Christian living and witness


### Hope encourages believers to be bold
**2CO 3:12**

### Hope encourages believers to evangelise
 *See also* 1PE 3:15

### Hope leads to godly living
 PSA 25:21; ; HEB 6:10-6:12; ; 1JN 3:2

### Hope equips believers for spiritual warfare
 1TH 5:8

### Hope enables believers to face suffering with confidence
**ROM 5:3-5:5** *See also* PSA 22:24, PSA 147:11; PHP 1:20

## Hope enables believers to face the future with confidence


### Hope assures believers of an eternal dimension to life

### Hope enables believers to face death with confidence
**PSA 16:9-16:10** *See also* JOB 19:25-19:27; PSA 33:18; 1CO 6:14; 2CO 4:10-4:14; PHP 1:3-1:6; REV 1:17-1:18

### Hope assures believers of their eternal life
 ACT 2:26-2:27; ; ROM 8:23-8:25; ; TIT 1:1-1:2, TIT 3:7; ; 1PE 1:3

### Hope enables believers to face the coming wrath with confidence
