# 8321: perfection, divine

The state of being whole, complete or without defect in any way.



## Only God is absolutely perfect


### In his nature and character
**DEU 32:4** *See also* ECC 3:14; MAT 5:48; ROM 11:33-11:36

### In his word
**PSA 12:6** *See also* PSA 19:7; PRO 8:8-8:9, PRO 30:5; 2TI 3:16-3:17; JAS 1:25

### In his morality
**HAB 1:13** *See also* 2SA 22:26-22:27; JOB 4:17; 1JN 3:3

### In his will and providence
**2SA 22:31** parallel PSA 18:30 *See also* ROM 12:2; JAS 1:17

### In his knowledge
**JOB 37:16** *See also* PSA 139:4; ISA 40:13-40:14; HEB 4:13
