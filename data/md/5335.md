# 5335: herald

One who proclaims a message or paves the way for a promised event. In Scripture both angels and human beings are employed as heralds, and on occasions even specific events may herald future happenings.



## Heralds in human affairs
**2KI 18:28** *See also* EXO 5:10; 1SA 11:7; 2SA 18:19; 1KI 21:12; 2KI 10:20, 2KI 11:12; 2CH 30:6; EST 1:19-1:22, EST 6:11; DAN 3:4; JON 3:7

## To preach is to act as a herald


### Pronouncing judgment
**JON 3:4** *See also* JER 4:5, JER 46:14

### Bringing good news
**ISA 40:9** *See also* ISA 48:20, ISA 52:7-52:8; NAM 1:15

### Preaching the gospel
**MAT 24:14**; **ACT 5:42**; **1TI 2:7** *See also* ACT 4:2, ACT 8:5 *Philip proclaims the gospel*; ACT 9:20, ACT 10:42 *Peter at Cornelius' house*; ACT 15:7, ACT 17:3, ACT 28:31 *Paul preaches at Rome*; 1CO 1:23; 2CO 1:19; GAL 2:2; COL 1:23; 1TI 3:16; 2TI 1:11

## John the Baptist as a herald
**MAT 11:10** parallels MRK 1:2, LUK 7:27 *See also* MAL 3:1; MAT 3:1-3:3; ISA 40:3; LUK 1:17, LUK 1:76; JHN 1:15, JHN 3:28

## Angels as heralds
**LUK 2:10** *See also* JDG 6:12; MAT 1:20-1:21, MAT 28:5-28:6 parallels MRK 16:5-16:6, LUK 24:4-24:6; LUK 1:13, LUK 1:30-1:31, LUK 2:13-2:14; REV 14:6
