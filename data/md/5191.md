# 5191: thought

The human ability to reason and reflect. Believers are called to reflect upon the words and works of God as they are made known through Scripture and to avoid speculation based purely upon human reason.



## Human thought and God


### God's thoughts are infinitely higher than human thoughts
**ISA 55:8-55:9** *See also* PSA 92:5, PSA 139:17-139:18; 1CO 2:10-2:11

### God knows all human thoughts
**1CH 28:9** *See also* GEN 6:5; PSA 94:11, PSA 139:1-139:4; MAT 9:4, MAT 12:25 parallel LUK 11:17; LUK 9:47; HEB 4:12

### Meditating on God's words
**PSA 1:1-1:2** *See also* DEU 6:6-6:7, DEU 11:18; JOS 1:8; PSA 119:15, PSA 119:23, PSA 119:78, PSA 119:97

### Meditating on God's works
**PSA 77:12** *See also* 1SA 12:24; PSA 106:7, PSA 107:43, PSA 119:27, PSA 143:5, PSA 145:4-145:5; ISA 1:18

## Warnings against founding faith on speculative thought or human wisdom
**COL 2:8** *See also* ISA 29:14; 1CO 1:20, 1CO 2:6, 1CO 3:19-3:20; 2CO 10:5; JAS 3:13-3:15

## Believers are to give thought to others


### To think about their needs
**PHP 2:4** *See also* ZEC 7:10; LUK 10:36-10:37; ROM 12:13; 1CO 12:26, 1CO 14:11-14:12; 1TI 5:3; 1JN 3:17

### To think about their abilities
**PHP 2:3** *See also* 1CO 12:24-12:25, 1CO 14:26-14:33; HEB 10:24

### To think about, and learn from, their shortcomings
**GAL 6:1** *See also* ACT 8:20-8:21; ROM 1:21; EPH 4:17-4:19

## Believers are to give thought to themselves


### To think accurately about themselves and their God-given abilities
**ROM 12:3** *See also* 1CO 1:26, 1CO 3:18, 1CO 10:12, 1CO 14:20, 1CO 14:37; 2CO 11:5-11:6; GAL 6:3; PHP 2:3; 1TI 4:4

### To think about their shortcomings and temptations
**2CO 10:12** *See also* PRO 6:6; MAT 15:19; ROM 2:15; 1CO 13:11; 2CO 10:7; EPH 2:3; PHP 3:13; COL 1:21

### To think about their needs
**PHP 3:7-3:9** *See also* MAT 6:31-6:34; PHP 2:4

### To think rightly about their plans and ambitions
**1PE 1:13-1:14** *See also* DEU 15:9; PRO 5:6, PRO 14:8, PRO 14:15; ISA 55:7; HAG 1:5; LUK 14:31 *Jesus Christ wanted his followers to consider carefully the cost of being a disciple*; ACT 5:35; ROM 13:14; PHP 4:8; COL 3:1-3:2; HEB 3:1, HEB 11:15; JAS 1:2; 1PE 4:1; 2PE 3:1-3:2
