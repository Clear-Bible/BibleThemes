# 5349: injustice, examples of

Injustice can be found, in varying degrees of intensity, in every area of human relationships. It produces exploitation and suffering, both mental and physical.



## Injustice occurs in family life
**MRK 7:9-7:13** parallel MAT 15:3-15:6 *See also* GEN 27:35; LEV 20:9-20:10; MAL 2:13-2:14; 1CO 7:4-7:5; EPH 6:4; COL 3:19, COL 3:21

## Injustice occurs in the community
**ISA 59:14-59:15** *See also* LEV 19:16; DEU 19:14, DEU 23:24-23:25, DEU 24:6-24:7; PSA 101:5; PRO 3:28-3:30, PRO 22:28, PRO 23:10-23:11; JER 17:11

## Injustice occurs in the business world
**HOS 12:7** *See also* LEV 19:35; PRO 11:1; 1TI 6:10

## Injustice occurs in courts of law
**PRO 24:23-24:24** *See also* PSA 26:10; PRO 14:5, PRO 28:21; ISA 5:22-5:23, ISA 29:21; HOS 10:4; AMO 5:12; HAB 1:4; MAL 2:9; ACT 16:36-16:37
