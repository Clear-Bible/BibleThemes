# 7469: temple, Herod's

Herod's temple, familiar to Jesus Christ and his disciples and frequented by the first Christians, was destroyed by the Romans in A.D. 70.



## The building of Herod the Great's temple
 [JHN 2:20](bibleref:43002020)

## Jesus Christ visited the temple and taught in its courts


### He was taken there as a baby
 [LUK 2:22](bibleref:42002022), [LUK 2:34](bibleref:42002034), [LUK 2:36-2:38](bibleref:42002036-42002038)

### He stayed in the temple courts after his parents had returned home from the Passover Feast
 [LUK 2:41-2:43](bibleref:42002041-42002043), [LUK 2:46](bibleref:42002046)

### He cast out of the temple court those who used it for material profit
 [MAT 21:12-21:13](bibleref:40021012-40021013) parallels MRK 11:15-11:17, LUK 19:45-19:46; [JHN 2:14-2:16](bibleref:43002014-43002016)

### He taught in the temple
 [MRK 12:35](bibleref:41012035), [MRK 12:41-12:43](bibleref:41012041-41012043) parallel LUK 21:1-21:4; [JHN 7:14](bibleref:43007014)[JHN 7:32](bibleref:43007032), [JHN 7:45-7:46](bibleref:43007045-43007046)
[JHN 8:2-8:3](bibleref:43008002-43008003), [JHN 10:22-10:24](bibleref:43010022-43010024)

### The religious leaders challenged his authority
 [MAT 21:23](bibleref:40021023) parallels MRK 11:27-11:28, LUK 20:1-20:2

### He foretold the complete destruction of the temple buildings
 [MAT 24:1-24:2](bibleref:40024001-40024002) parallels MRK 13:1-13:2, LUK 21:5-21:6

## The crucifixion and the temple


### One of the charges against Jesus Christ was that he had said he would destroy the temple
**[MAT 26:60-26:61](bibleref:40026060-40026061)**

### The curtain of the temple was torn in two at Jesus Christ's death
**[MAT 27:51](bibleref:40027051)** *The curtain symbolised the barrier between God and his people caused by their sins.*
