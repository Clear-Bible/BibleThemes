# 5313: flogging

Physical beating inflicted as punishment.



## Flogging and beating in the law
**DEU 25:2-25:3** *See also* EXO 21:20

## Justified flogging
**PRO 19:25**; **1PE 2:20** *See also* NEH 13:25; PRO 17:10, PRO 18:6, PRO 19:29; LUK 12:47-12:48

## Unjustified flogging
**ISA 50:6**; **HEB 11:36** *See also* EXO 5:14; PRO 17:26; JER 20:2, JER 37:15

## Jesus Christ flogged
**MRK 15:15** parallels MAT 27:26, JHN 19:1 *See also* ISA 53:5; MAT 20:19 parallels MRK 10:34, LUK 18:32

## The apostles flogged
**MAT 10:17**; **ACT 5:40** *See also* MAT 23:34; MRK 13:9; ACT 16:22-16:23, ACT 16:37, ACT 18:17, ACT 22:19, ACT 22:24-22:25; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 11:24-11:25

## Flogging used figuratively
**2SA 7:14** *See also* JOB 5:21; PSA 89:32; ISA 10:26, ISA 54:11
