# 5787: ambition, positive aspects of

In its positive sense, ambition is a personal desire for spiritual progress or achievement.



## A longing for God and for what he can do in the believer
**MAT 5:6**; **PHP 3:13-3:14** *See also* PHP 3:10; PSA 40:8; LUK 13:24; COL 1:29; 1TH 4:11; 2TI 2:15; HEB 12:1; 1PE 2:2; 2PE 3:14

## The desire for spiritual gifts
**1CO 14:12** *See also* 1KI 3:10-3:14 parallel 2CH 1:11-1:12; 1CO 12:31, 1CO 14:1, 1CO 14:39

## The desire for a leadership role in the church
**1TI 3:1**

## The longing for communion with God
**PSA 27:4** *See also* PSA 73:25, PSA 84:2

## The compelling desire to preach the gospel
**ROM 15:20** *See also* 1CO 9:16; PHP 1:15-1:18 *Jesus Christ can be proclaimed, despite selfish personal ambition.*

## Focus on future rewards
**1CO 9:24-9:27** *See also* 2TI 4:8; HEB 10:35-10:36, HEB 11:26
