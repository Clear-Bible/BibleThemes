# 4458: grape

Fruit which grows in clusters on a vine. It was very widely cultivated in the Near East in biblical times and was used mostly for making wine.



## The cultivation of grapes indicative of rich and fertile land
**NUM 20:2-20:5** *See also* NUM 13:21-13:25

## The failure of the grape harvest indicative of misfortune or disaster
**ISA 32:10-32:13** *See also* JER 8:13; MIC 7:1; HAB 3:16-3:19

## Grapes used to make wine
**DEU 28:39** *See also* GEN 40:9-40:11, GEN 49:10-49:12; MIC 6:15

## Treading grapes
**AMO 9:13** *See also* JDG 9:27; NEH 13:15; JER 25:30

## Harvesting grapes
**JER 49:9-49:10** parallel OBA 1:5 *See also* LEV 26:5; NUM 13:20; ISA 62:8-62:9

## Regulations concerning the grape harvest
**LEV 19:10** *See also* LEV 25:1-25:7; DEU 23:24, DEU 24:21
