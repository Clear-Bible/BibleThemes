# 2360: Jesus Christ, prayers of

Prayer was the essence of Jesus Christ's relationship with the Father. He prayed for himself and his mission, and he continues to pray for all believers.



## Jesus Christ's practice of prayer


### He prayed regularly
**[LUK 5:16](bibleref:42005016)** *Luke records more instances of Jesus Christ praying than any other Gospel writer.* 

### He often prayed alone
**[MRK 1:35](bibleref:41001035)** *See also* [MAT 14:23](bibleref:40014023) parallel MRK 6:46; [LUK 6:12](bibleref:42006012), [LUK 9:18](bibleref:42009018)

## Prayer at specific times in Jesus Christ's life
Before his death:
 [MAT 26:36-26:46](bibleref:40026036-40026046) parallels MRK 14:32-14:41, LUK 22:39-22:46; [JHN 17:1-17:26](bibleref:43017001-43017026)[MAT 27:46](bibleref:40027046) parallel MRK 15:34; [LUK 23:34](bibleref:42023034), [LUK 23:46](bibleref:42023046), [LUK 3:21-3:22](bibleref:42003021-42003022) *at his baptism* ; [LUK 6:12-6:13](bibleref:42006012-42006013) *before choosing the apostles* ; [LUK 9:28-9:29](bibleref:42009028-42009029) *before his transfiguration*
