## Satan's role as adversary earns him varied titles


### The devil
He is the adversary because he deceives:
 [REV 12:9](bibleref:66012009), [REV 20:2-20:3](bibleref:66020002-66020003)

### Belial
He is the adversary because he is the troublemaker or scoundrel (the same Hebrew word is used in the OT passages as in 2Co 6:15 to describe Satan's work):
 [DEU 13:13](bibleref:05013013); [1KI 21:10](bibleref:11021010); [PRO 6:12](bibleref:20006012); [2CO 6:15](bibleref:47006015)

### The dragon
He is the adversary because he is the enemy:
 [REV 12:4](bibleref:66012004), [REV 12:13-12:14](bibleref:66012013-66012014)

### The serpent
He is the adversary because he thwarts spiritual insight:
 [GEN 3:1-3:5](bibleref:01003001-01003005), [GEN 3:13](bibleref:01003013); [2CO 11:3](bibleref:47011003)

### The prince of this world
He is the adversary because his rule is temporary, not absolute:
 [JHN 12:31](bibleref:43012031), [JHN 14:30](bibleref:43014030), [JHN 16:11](bibleref:43016011)

### The angel of the Abyss
 [REV 9:11](bibleref:66009011) *He is the adversary because he is the destroyer.*
