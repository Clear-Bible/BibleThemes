# 5353: interest

The charge for the lending of money on a commercial or private loan. The practice of charging interest (known as usury) is treated with caution by OT writers on account of its potential for exploitation.

## Usury forbidden among the Israelites
**[EXO 22:25](bibleref:02022025)** *See also* [LEV 25:35-25:37](bibleref:03025035-03025037); [DEU 23:19](bibleref:05023019)

### Lending without interest: a characteristic of the righteous
 [PSA 15:5](bibleref:19015005); [EZK 18:8](bibleref:26018008), [EZK 18:17](bibleref:26018017); [LUK 6:30](bibleref:42006030), [LUK 6:31](bibleref:42006031)

### Exacting interest: a characteristic of the wicked
 [PRO 28:8](bibleref:20028008); [JER 15:10](bibleref:24015010); [EZK 18:13](bibleref:26018013), [EZK 22:12](bibleref:26022012); [1TI 6:9](bibleref:54006009), [1TI 6:10](bibleref:54006010)

### Lending: a charitable, not a commercial, activity
 [DEU 15:8](bibleref:05015008); [PSA 112:5](bibleref:19112005); [LUK 6:35](bibleref:42006035)

### Example of oppression through usury
 [NEH 5:6-5:11](bibleref:16005006-16005011)

## Usury permissible for commercial transactions involving ``outsiders''
**[DEU 23:20](bibleref:05023020)** *This legislation permits rather than encourages usury and remains subject to considerations protecting the poor.*  *See also* [DEU 15:1-15:11](bibleref:05015001-05015011); [MAT 25:27](bibleref:40025027) parallel LUK 19:23
