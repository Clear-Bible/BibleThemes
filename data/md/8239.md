# 8239: earnestness

An attitude characterised by seriousness and commitment. Scripture commends earnestness in believers' attitudes towards God and in the concerns of the gospel.



## The significance of earnestness


### In repentance
**JOL 2:12** *See also* 1SA 7:2-7:3; REV 3:19

### In seeking God
**JER 29:13** *See also* PSA 119:2; HEB 11:6

### In salvation
**LUK 13:24** *See also* MAT 7:24-7:27 parallel LUK 6:47-6:49; HEB 2:3, HEB 6:11; JAS 1:22, JAS 2:14

### In love for God
**DEU 6:5** *See also* MAT 22:37 parallels MRK 12:30, LUK 10:27

### In prayer
**2CH 7:14** *See also* ISA 62:6-62:7; MAT 6:5-6:6

### In trusting God
**PRO 3:5** *See also* ISA 26:3-26:4

### In spiritual progress
**PHP 2:12-2:13** *See also* 1CO 9:24; HEB 12:1; 2PE 1:5-1:11

### In serving God
**MAT 6:24** parallel LUK 16:13 *See also* DEU 18:6-18:7; LUK 9:57-9:62; 1CO 12:31, 1CO 14:1; 2TI 2:15
