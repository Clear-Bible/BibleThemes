# 5379: law, Jesus Christ's attitude to

Jesus Christ accepted the authority of the OT law and saw himself as coming to fulfil its purpose.



## Jesus Christ's disputes with the Pharisees and teachers of the law


### The Pharisees and teachers of the law accuse Jesus Christ's disciples of not following tradition
**[MRK 7:5](bibleref:41007005)** parallel MAT 15:2

### Jesus Christ accuses the Pharisees and teachers of the law of hypocrisy
**[MRK 7:6-7:8](bibleref:41007006-41007008)** parallel MAT 15:7-15:9 *See also* [ISA 29:13](bibleref:23029013)

### Jesus Christ gives examples where human tradition is observed rather than God's law
 [MRK 7:10-7:13](bibleref:41007010-41007013) parallel MAT 15:3-15:6; [MAT 23:1-23:36](bibleref:40023001-40023036) parallels MRK 12:38-12:39, LUK 20:45-20:46

### The Pharisees accuse Jesus Christ of breaking the Sabbath
**[MRK 2:23-2:24](bibleref:41002023-41002024)** parallels MAT 12:1-12:2, LUK 6:1-6:2

### Jesus Christ demonstrates his authority over the Sabbath
**[MRK 2:27-3:4](bibleref:41002027-41003004)** parallels MAT 12:8-12:10, LUK 6:5-6:7

### Jesus Christ challenges the religious leaders to think about principles not rules
**[MRK 3:4](bibleref:41003004)** parallels LUK 6:9, MAT 12:11-12:12 *See also* [LUK 13:10-13:17](bibleref:42013010-42013017)

## Jesus Christ came to fulfil the law
**[MAT 5:17](bibleref:40005017), [MAT 5:21-5:22](bibleref:40005021-40005022)** *Jesus Christ went beyond the literal rules of the law, to the thought behind the act.*  *See also* [EXO 20:13](bibleref:02020013)**[MAT 5:27-5:28](bibleref:40005027-40005028)** *See also* [EXO 20:14](bibleref:02020014)

## Jesus Christ asserts the continuing validity of the law
**[MAT 5:18-5:19](bibleref:40005018-40005019)**; **[LUK 10:25-10:28](bibleref:42010025-42010028)** parallels MAT 22:37-22:39, MRK 12:29-12:34 *See also* [LUK 10:29-10:37](bibleref:42010029-42010037), [LUK 16:16](bibleref:42016016)
