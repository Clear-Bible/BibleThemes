# 7304: anointing

The application of oil was associated with times of rejoicing and celebration. It also possessed a deeper significance, including that of being singled out by God for special favour or responsibilities.

## Anointing as a social custom


### In personal grooming
**[ECC 9:8](bibleref:21009008)** *The associations here are of joy and well-being.*  *See also* [RUT 3:3](bibleref:08003003); [PSA 92:10](bibleref:19092010); [ISA 57:9](bibleref:23057009); [AMO 6:6](bibleref:30006006); [MAT 6:17](bibleref:40006017)

### Anointing guests as a mark of honour
**[PSA 23:5](bibleref:19023005)** *See also* [LUK 7:36-7:39](bibleref:42007036-42007039), [LUK 7:44-7:47](bibleref:42007044-42007047); [JHN 12:3](bibleref:43012003)

### Anointing corpses as a burial preparation
**[MRK 16:1](bibleref:41016001)** *See also* [MAT 26:6-26:12](bibleref:40026006-40026012); [JHN 19:38-19:40](bibleref:43019038-43019040)

## Anointing religious objects
**[EXO 40:9-40:11](bibleref:02040009-02040011)** *Anointed objects are set apart (consecrated), to be used only in the performance of religious ceremonies.*  *See also* [GEN 28:18](bibleref:01028018); [EXO 30:22-30:33](bibleref:02030022-02030033)

### Priests
**[EXO 40:12-40:15](bibleref:02040012-02040015)** *See also* [LEV 4:16](bibleref:03004016), [LEV 21:10-21:12](bibleref:03021010-03021012); [1CH 29:22](bibleref:13029022)

**[1KI 1:39](bibleref:11001039)** *See also* [1SA 10:1](bibleref:09010001), [1SA 16:12-16:13](bibleref:09016012-09016013); [2SA 5:3](bibleref:10005003); [2KI 11:12](bibleref:12011012)

### For purification

### For healing
**[MRK 6:13](bibleref:41006013)** *It has been suggested that anointing in a healing context may be related to the use of oil for medicinal purposes.*  *See also* [JAS 5:14](bibleref:59005014)

## The figurative use of anointing


### Anointing by God
**[1SA 26:9](bibleref:09026009)** *Israel's king is frequently referred to as “the LORD's anointed”. His physical anointing is seen as symbolising a divine anointing. The word “Messiah” literally means “the anointed one”* ; **[ISA 45:1](bibleref:23045001)** *Cyrus, as God's agent for a specific task, is referred to as “his anointed”.*  *See also* [2SA 23:1](bibleref:10023001); [PSA 2:2](bibleref:19002002), [PSA 45:2](bibleref:19045002), [PSA 89:20](bibleref:19089020); [EZK 28:14](bibleref:26028014)

### God's people in the OT
 [1CH 16:22](bibleref:13016022) parallel PSA 105:15

### Christian believers
**[2CO 1:21-1:22](bibleref:47001021-47001022)** *See also* [1JN 2:20](bibleref:62002020), [1JN 2:27](bibleref:62002027) *This anointing is not received in an outward ceremony but by sharing in the Holy Spirit's anointing of Jesus Christ.* 

## Jesus Christ as God's Anointed One (the Messiah)
**[ACT 4:26-4:27](bibleref:44004026-44004027)** *Jesus Christ's receiving of the Holy Spirit at his baptism was his “anointing” for his Messianic work* ; **[ACT 10:38](bibleref:44010038)** *Note the link between anointing and the Holy Spirit.*  *See also* [DAN 9:25-9:26](bibleref:27009025-27009026); [LUK 4:18](bibleref:42004018); [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001); [HEB 1:9](bibleref:58001009)
