# 5895: intimacy

Close relationships are part of God's will for human life. Scripture speaks of intimate relationships between Jesus Christ and the Father, between God and his people, and between various individuals.

## Jesus Christ's intimate relationship with his Father
**[MAT 11:27](bibleref:40011027)** *See also* [MAT 3:17](bibleref:40003017) parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; [JHN 5:20](bibleref:43005020), [JHN 10:30](bibleref:43010030), [JHN 10:38](bibleref:43010038), [JHN 14:10-14:11](bibleref:43014010-43014011), [JHN 17:21](bibleref:43017021)

## God's intimate relationship with his people
**[JHN 17:3](bibleref:43017003)** *“know” speaks of close personal relationship rather than theoretical or academic knowledge.*  *See also* [2CH 7:15-7:16](bibleref:14007015-14007016); [PSA 148:14](bibleref:19148014); [HOS 6:6](bibleref:28006006); [JHN 14:21](bibleref:43014021), [JHN 15:4](bibleref:43015004), [JHN 15:15](bibleref:43015015)
