# 8140: prize

Something of value given to those competing in a race, which the Christian life is likened to. Though believers must accept discipline and training, a prize is reserved for everyone who is committed to Jesus Christ.

## A prize is reserved for all believers
**[2TI 4:7-4:8](bibleref:55004007-55004008)** *The crown is the wreath given to the winner of a race.*  *See also* [JAS 1:12](bibleref:59001012); [1PE 5:4](bibleref:60005004)

### Total dedication to God
**[ACT 20:24](bibleref:44020024)** *See also* [PHP 3:7-3:8](bibleref:50003007-50003008)

### Perseverance
**[PHP 3:14](bibleref:50003014)** *See also* [PHP 2:16](bibleref:50002016); [HEB 12:1](bibleref:58012001); [REV 2:10](bibleref:66002010), [REV 3:11](bibleref:66003011)

### Keeping God's rules
**[2TI 2:5](bibleref:55002005)**

### Self-discipline
**[1CO 9:25-9:27](bibleref:46009025-46009027)**
