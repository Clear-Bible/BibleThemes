# 4687: snake

A reptile, often associated with cunning and danger, or occasionally with satanic temptation.

## Snakes as crawling reptiles
**[GEN 3:14](bibleref:01003014)** *See also* [ISA 65:25](bibleref:23065025); [MIC 7:17](bibleref:33007017)

## Snakes are dangerous


### Both to animals and human beings
**[GEN 49:17](bibleref:01049017)** *See also* [GEN 3:15](bibleref:01003015); [EXO 4:3](bibleref:02004003), [EXO 7:9-7:13](bibleref:02007009-02007013); [PRO 23:32](bibleref:20023032); [ECC 10:8](bibleref:21010008), [ECC 10:11](bibleref:21010011); [ISA 30:6](bibleref:23030006); [ACT 28:3-28:6](bibleref:44028003-44028006)

### Some are poisonous
**[ISA 14:29](bibleref:23014029)** *See also* [DEU 8:15](bibleref:05008015), [DEU 32:33](bibleref:05032033); [JOB 20:14](bibleref:18020014), [JOB 20:16](bibleref:18020016); [REV 9:19](bibleref:66009019)

### They were sometimes used to bring God's judgment
**[1CO 10:9](bibleref:46010009)** *See also* [NUM 21:4-21:6](bibleref:04021004-04021006); [AMO 5:19](bibleref:30005019), [AMO 9:3](bibleref:30009003)

### They may be overcome
**[PSA 91:13](bibleref:19091013)** *See also* [MRK 16:18](bibleref:41016018); [LUK 10:19](bibleref:42010019)

### One day they will be rendered harmless
**[ISA 11:8](bibleref:23011008)**
