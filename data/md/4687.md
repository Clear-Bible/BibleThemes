# 4687: snake

A reptile, often associated with cunning and danger, or occasionally with satanic temptation.



## Snakes as crawling reptiles
**GEN 3:14** *See also* ISA 65:25; MIC 7:17

## Snakes are dangerous


### Both to animals and human beings
**GEN 49:17** *See also* GEN 3:15; EXO 4:3, EXO 7:9-7:13; PRO 23:32; ECC 10:8, ECC 10:11; ISA 30:6; ACT 28:3-28:6

### Some are poisonous
**ISA 14:29** *See also* DEU 8:15, DEU 32:33; JOB 20:14, JOB 20:16; REV 9:19

### They were sometimes used to bring God's judgment
**1CO 10:9** *See also* NUM 21:4-21:6; AMO 5:19, AMO 9:3

### They may be overcome
**PSA 91:13** *See also* MRK 16:18; LUK 10:19

### One day they will be rendered harmless
**ISA 11:8**

## Snakes used symbolically


### Of Israel's enemies
 *See also* ISA 27:1; JER 46:22, JER 51:34

### Of cunning and intelligence
**MAT 10:16**

### Of something harmful
**MAT 7:9-7:10** parallel LUK 11:11

### Of the ungodly
**PSA 58:4-58:5** *See also* PSA 140:3; MAT 3:7, MAT 12:34, MAT 23:33; ROM 3:13

### Of Satan
**REV 12:9** *See also* GEN 3:1-3:13; 2CO 11:3; REV 20:2

### Of salvation
**JHN 3:14-3:15** *See also* NUM 21:7-21:9; 2KI 18:4
