# 8265: godliness

Reverence for or devotion to God, producing a practical awareness of God in every aspect of life.

## Examples of godliness in the OT
 [GEN 5:24](bibleref:01005024) *Enoch* ; [GEN 6:9](bibleref:01006009) *Noah* ; [1KI 18:3-18:4](bibleref:11018003-11018004) *Obadiah* ; [2CH 31:20-31:21](bibleref:14031020-14031021) *Hezekiah* ; [JOB 1:1](bibleref:18001001) *Job* 

## Examples of godliness in the NT
 [LUK 2:25](bibleref:42002025) *Simeon* ; [LUK 2:37](bibleref:42002037) *Anna* ; [JHN 1:47](bibleref:43001047) *Nathanael* [JHN 8:29](bibleref:43008029); [HEB 5:7](bibleref:58005007)
[ACT 2:5](bibleref:44002005), [ACT 8:2](bibleref:44008002)[ACT 10:2](bibleref:44010002), [ACT 10:7](bibleref:44010007)
[ACT 11:24](bibleref:44011024) *Barnabas* ; [ACT 22:12](bibleref:44022012) *Ananias* 

## God has a special concern for the godly
**[PSA 4:3](bibleref:19004003)** *“the godly” (sometimes translated “the saints”) is often used in the OT to refer to God's people.*  *See also* [PSA 32:6](bibleref:19032006); [JHN 9:31](bibleref:43009031)

## Godly living


### Godliness should be seen in the lives of believers
**[2PE 3:11](bibleref:61003011)** *See also* [1TI 2:2](bibleref:54002002), [1TI 6:11](bibleref:54006011); [TIT 2:12](bibleref:56002012)

### Jesus Christ is the beginning and end of godliness
**[1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016)** *The model and the power for godly living derives from the incarnate Christ.*  *See also* [HEB 5:7](bibleref:58005007); [2PE 1:3](bibleref:61001003)

### The basis for godly living is true teaching about Jesus Christ
**[1TI 6:3-6:4](bibleref:54006003-54006004)** *See also* [TIT 1:1](bibleref:56001001)

### Godly living demands self-discipline
 [1CO 9:24-9:27](bibleref:46009024-46009027); [2CO 10:5](bibleref:47010005); [1TI 4:7-4:8](bibleref:54004007-54004008)
