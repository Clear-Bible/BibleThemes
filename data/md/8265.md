# 8265: godliness

Reverence for or devotion to God, producing a practical awareness of God in every aspect of life.



## Examples of godliness in the OT
 GEN 5:24 *Enoch*; ; GEN 6:9 *Noah*; ; 1KI 18:3-18:4 *Obadiah*; ; 2CH 31:20-31:21 *Hezekiah*; ; JOB 1:1 *Job*

## Examples of godliness in the NT
 LUK 2:25 *Simeon*; ; LUK 2:37 *Anna*; ; JHN 1:47 *Nathanael*; JHN 8:29; ; HEB 5:7; 
ACT 2:5, ACT 8:2; ACT 10:2, ACT 10:7; 
ACT 11:24 *Barnabas*; ; ACT 22:12 *Ananias*

## God has a special concern for the godly
**PSA 4:3** *“the godly” (sometimes translated “the saints”) is often used in the OT to refer to God's people.* *See also* PSA 32:6; JHN 9:31

## Godly living


### Godliness should be seen in the lives of believers
**2PE 3:11** *See also* 1TI 2:2, 1TI 6:11; TIT 2:12

### Jesus Christ is the beginning and end of godliness
**1TI 3:16** *The model and the power for godly living derives from the incarnate Christ.* *See also* HEB 5:7; 2PE 1:3

### The basis for godly living is true teaching about Jesus Christ
**1TI 6:3-6:4** *See also* TIT 1:1

### Godly living demands self-discipline
 1CO 9:24-9:27; ; 2CO 10:5; ; 1TI 4:7-4:8

### Sorrow for sin is a sign of godliness
 2CO 7:10-7:11

## The benefits of godliness


### Its value in both this world and the next
**1TI 4:8**

### Its present blessings
**DEU 4:40**; **1TI 6:6** *See also* ISA 3:10; TIT 3:8

### The promise of future blessing
**TIT 2:11-2:13** *See also* 2PE 3:11-3:12

## Godliness does not guarantee escape from suffering
**PSA 12:1** *See also* MIC 7:2; 2TI 3:12; 2PE 2:9

## Apparent godliness may not be genuine
 1TI 6:5; ; 2TI 3:4-3:5
