# 8316: orthodoxy, in NT

Acceptance of the truth, especially about Jesus Christ, that is revealed by the Holy Spirit in the gospel and is passed on through the teaching of sound doctrine.



## Orthodoxy is based on Jesus Christ who is the truth
**[EPH 4:21](bibleref:49004021)** *See also* [JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014), [JHN 14:6](bibleref:43014006)

## Orthodoxy is revealed in the gospel

**[1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016)** *See also* [ROM 1:3-1:4](bibleref:45001003-45001004); [1CO 15:3-15:5](bibleref:46015003-46015005); [PHP 2:5-2:11](bibleref:50002005-50002011)

**[1TI 2:7](bibleref:54002007)** *See also* [1TI 4:6](bibleref:54004006); [2TI 2:2](bibleref:55002002), [2TI 2:15](bibleref:55002015)
