# 8431: fasting, reasons for

Scripture identifies a number of situations in which fasting is appropriate.



## Situations in which fasting is appropriate


### Bereavement
**1CH 10:11-10:12** parallel 1SA 31:11-31:13 *See also* 2SA 1:11-1:12, 2SA 3:31-3:35

### Distress
**EST 4:3** *See also* 1SA 1:7, 1SA 20:34; 1KI 21:4-21:6; PSA 109:24; DAN 6:18; ACT 27:33

### Penitence
**1SA 7:5-7:6** *See also* NEH 9:1-9:3; DAN 9:3-9:6 *Daniel identifies himself with the sin of the people*; DAN 9:20; JOL 1:13-1:14, JOL 2:12-2:15; JON 3:5-3:9

### Seeking God's intervention
**2SA 12:15-12:17** *See also* 2CH 20:2-20:4; EZR 8:21; EST 4:15-4:16; PSA 35:13-35:14

### Seeking guidance
**JDG 20:26-20:28** *See also* DEU 9:9 *Moses fasts before the revelation of the Law*; DAN 9:1-9:3, DAN 9:20-9:23, DAN 10:1-10:2, DAN 10:12; ACT 13:1-13:3 *The church receives direction through the Holy Spirit while fasting.*

### Indicating earnestness
 ACT 23:12-23:13
