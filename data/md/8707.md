# 8707: apostasy, dealing with personal

Individual believers can fall away from faith and be restored. Scripture gives examples of such falling away, and offers advice on how such people should be dealt with pastorally.



## Warnings of the dangers of personal apostasy
**2CO 11:2-11:3** *See also* PSA 125:4-125:5; LUK 9:59-9:62; GAL 3:1-3:3, GAL 4:8-4:10 *Those who return to strict observance of Jewish days and festivals have not understood the gospel.*

## Warnings against leading others into apostasy
**MAT 18:6** *See also* PRO 28:10; GAL 5:7-5:10

## Encouragements to guard against personal apostasy
**1CO 10:12-10:13**; **HEB 3:12-3:14** *See also* PSA 85:8; MAT 24:10-24:13, MAT 24:24-24:25 parallel MRK 13:22-13:23; COL 1:21-1:23; 1TI 6:20-6:21; HEB 10:35-10:39, HEB 12:1-12:8; 2PE 3:17-3:18; REV 13:5-13:10

## Dealing with apostate believers
**GAL 6:1** *See also* 1CO 5:4-5:5; JAS 5:19-5:20; 1JN 5:16-5:17

## Examples of those who were restored from apostasy
Samson:
 JDG 16:20, JDG 16:28-16:30; 
PSA 51:1-51:17 *David*; MAT 26:74 parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:61-22:62, JHN 18:27 *for the account of Peter's reinstatement see Jn 21:15-17*; 
ACT 15:37-15:38 *for evidence of John Mark's recovery see 2Ti 4:11*

### Punishment through defeat
 NUM 14:43; ; JDG 2:12-2:15

### Punishment through rejection
 2CH 24:20; ; HOS 4:6
