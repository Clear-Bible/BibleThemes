# 8716: dishonesty, examples of

Examples of the dishonest behaviour condemned by God.



## Dishonest words condemned


### Lying
**EPH 4:25** *See also* LEV 19:11; PSA 34:13; COL 3:9-3:10

### False witness
**EXO 20:16** parallel DEU 5:20 *See also* MAT 19:17-19:18; MRK 10:19; LUK 18:20; EXO 23:1, EXO 23:7; MAT 26:59-26:60; ACT 6:12-6:14

### Swearing falsely
**LEV 19:12** *See also* EXO 20:7; MAT 5:33-5:37

## Dishonest actions condemned


### Stealing
**EXO 20:15** parallel DEU 5:19 *See also* MAT 19:18 parallels MRK 10:19, LUK 18:20; ROM 13:9

### Dishonest dealing
**PRO 11:1** *See also* LEV 19:13; DEU 25:13-25:16; PSA 62:10; ISA 33:15-33:16; TIT 2:9-2:10

## Israel condemned for dishonest gain


### Bribery and extortion
**EZK 22:12-22:13** *See also* JER 22:13, JER 22:17-22:19; EZK 22:27-22:31; HAB 2:6-2:9

### Cheating and dishonest scales
**AMO 8:4-8:8** *See also* LEV 19:35-19:36; MIC 6:10-6:13; MAL 1:14 *In this case, they were cheating the LORD.*
