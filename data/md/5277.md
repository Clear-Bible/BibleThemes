# 5277: criminals

## Examples of delinquents


### Those who break the fifth commandment as minors may have been treated as criminals
 [EXO 20:12](bibleref:02020012)

### Manasseh, king of Judah
**[2KI 21:1-21:2](bibleref:12021001-12021002)**

### Youths who jeered Elisha
**[2KI 2:23-2:24](bibleref:12002023-12002024)**

### Those who ignore the wise counsel of Solomon
**[PRO 1:1-1:4](bibleref:20001001-20001004)** *It seems that sections of Proverbs were written with young people in mind. Those who choose to act upon its wisdom will please God and keep themselves out of the troubles common to youth.* 

### Those who succumb to the evil desires of youth
 *See also* [2TI 2:22](bibleref:55002022) *Young people are warned that the strength of their desires can easily get them into trouble. Young Christians are, with God's help, able to control such desires.*
