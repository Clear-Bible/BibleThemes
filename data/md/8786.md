# 8786: opposition, to sin and evil

Believers are called upon to opppose all that is hostile to God's purposes in the world.



## God opposes evil
**MAT 21:12-21:13** parallels MRK 11:15-11:16, LUK 19:45-19:46, JHN 2:14-2:17 *See also* EXO 4:24; LEV 20:3, LEV 26:14-26:17; NUM 22:22; MAL 3:2-3:5; JAS 4:6; 1PE 5:5; REV 2:16

## God's people should oppose evil


### Within themselves
**COL 3:5** *See also* MAT 5:29-5:30; ROM 8:13

### Among themselves
**GAL 2:11** *See also* 1CO 5:12-5:13; EPH 5:3

### Within the church

### Within the family

### Within the state
**HAB 1:4** *See also* JDG 5:23; 1SA 15:9-15:11; JER 5:28
