# 5726: old age, attainment of

Attaining old age is regarded as a blessing from God, often as a reward for obedience. The physical weakness brought by old age is recognised; so too is God's ability to strengthen and sustain the aged. Old age is often accompanied by a growth in wisdom and discernment.

 [GEN 5:5](bibleref:01005005), [GEN 5:8](bibleref:01005008), [GEN 5:11](bibleref:01005011), [GEN 5:27](bibleref:01005027) *Methuselah died in the year of the flood* ; [GEN 9:29](bibleref:01009029)

### After the flood
**[GEN 6:3](bibleref:01006003)** *See also* [GEN 11:10-11:25](bibleref:01011010-01011025) *Following the flood, the span of life gradually reduces* ; [GEN 25:7-25:8](bibleref:01025007-01025008) *Abraham's death at 175 is considered “a good old age”.*
