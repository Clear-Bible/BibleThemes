# 5726: old age, attainment of

Attaining old age is regarded as a blessing from God, often as a reward for obedience. The physical weakness brought by old age is recognised; so too is God's ability to strengthen and sustain the aged. Old age is often accompanied by a growth in wisdom and discernment.



## Life expectancy in the OT


### Before the flood
 GEN 5:5, GEN 5:8, GEN 5:11, GEN 5:27 *Methuselah died in the year of the flood*; ; GEN 9:29

### After the flood
**GEN 6:3** *See also* GEN 11:10-11:25 *Following the flood, the span of life gradually reduces*; GEN 25:7-25:8 *Abraham's death at 175 is considered “a good old age”.*

### In later generations
**PSA 90:10** *See also* 2SA 5:4-5:5, 2SA 19:34-19:35

## Attaining old age is a blessing from God


### Long life as a reward for obedience
**DEU 5:33** *See also* EXO 20:12 parallel DEU 5:16; DEU 4:40; PRO 3:1-3:2, PRO 16:31; ECC 8:13

### Reaching old age is a mark of God's favour
**GEN 15:15** *See also* GEN 24:1, GEN 35:29 *Isaac*; JDG 8:32 *Gideon*; 1CH 29:28 *David, like Abraham and Gideon is described as dying at “a good old age”*; 2CH 24:15 *Jehoiada*; JOB 42:17 *Job*

### People reaching old age indicates God's blessing on society
**ZEC 8:4** *See also* ISA 65:20

### Seeing grandchildren is a sign of blessing
**PRO 17:6** *See also* GEN 48:11, GEN 50:22-50:23; RUT 4:15; JOB 42:16; PSA 128:5-128:6; ISA 53:10

### Failure to reach old age is a result of God's judgment
 1SA 2:31-2:33

## The disabilities of old age


### Its frailties
**PSA 71:9** *See also* GEN 27:1, GEN 48:10; 1KI 1:1, 1KI 15:23; ECC 12:2-12:7 *an allegory portraying physical decline*

### The progressive inability to have children
**GEN 18:11-18:13** *See also* GEN 17:17, GEN 37:3 *Children born in old age were rare and therefore special*; 2KI 4:14; LUK 1:18

### The approach of death
 GEN 27:2; ; 1KI 1:15; ; 1CH 23:1

## God's provision for the aged


### God strengthens and sustains them
**ISA 46:4** *See also* DEU 34:7; JOS 14:10-14:11; JOB 5:26; PSA 92:14

### God gives them children
 GEN 21:7 *Abraham and Sarah*; ; 2KI 4:16-4:17 *the Shunammite woman*; ; LUK 1:36 *Elizabeth*

## Wisdom and self-control should accompany old age
**JOB 12:12** *See also* JOB 15:10; PRO 20:29; JHN 8:9; TIT 2:2
