# 5238: bribery

The practice of offering an incentive, usually money, in order to dishonestly or illegally procure a decision or action in favour of the giver.

## Bribery is sin


### It is forbidden by God on the grounds of truth and justice
**[EXO 23:8](bibleref:02023008)**; **[2CH 19:7](bibleref:14019007)** *See also* [DEU 10:17](bibleref:05010017), [DEU 16:18-16:20](bibleref:05016018-05016020); [JOB 36:18](bibleref:18036018)

### The prevalence of the sin
**[PRO 17:8](bibleref:20017008)** *See also* [PRO 6:32-6:35](bibleref:20006032-20006035), [PRO 21:14](bibleref:20021014)

### The seriousness of the sin
**[JOB 15:34](bibleref:18015034)** *See also* [DEU 27:25](bibleref:05027025); [ISA 1:23-1:24](bibleref:23001023-23001024), [ISA 5:22-5:23](bibleref:23005022-23005023), [ISA 33:15](bibleref:23033015); [AMO 5:12](bibleref:30005012); [MIC 3:9-3:12](bibleref:33003009-33003012)

## The results of bribery


### Personal corruption
**[ECC 7:7](bibleref:21007007)** *See also* [PSA 26:9-26:10](bibleref:19026009-19026010); [1TI 6:10](bibleref:54006010)

### The perversion of justice
**[PRO 17:23](bibleref:20017023)** *See also* [EXO 23:8](bibleref:02023008); [DEU 16:19](bibleref:05016019)

### The decay of society
**[PRO 29:4](bibleref:20029004)** *See also* [EZK 22:12](bibleref:26022012) *Corruption, violence and decay render society subject to God's judgment.*
