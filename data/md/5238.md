# 5238: bribery

The practice of offering an incentive, usually money, in order to dishonestly or illegally procure a decision or action in favour of the giver.



## Bribery is sin


### It is forbidden by God on the grounds of truth and justice
**EXO 23:8**; **2CH 19:7** *See also* DEU 10:17, DEU 16:18-16:20; JOB 36:18

### The prevalence of the sin
**PRO 17:8** *See also* PRO 6:32-6:35, PRO 21:14

### The seriousness of the sin
**JOB 15:34** *See also* DEU 27:25; ISA 1:23-1:24, ISA 5:22-5:23, ISA 33:15; AMO 5:12; MIC 3:9-3:12

## The results of bribery


### Personal corruption
**ECC 7:7** *See also* PSA 26:9-26:10; 1TI 6:10

### The perversion of justice
**PRO 17:23** *See also* EXO 23:8; DEU 16:19

### The decay of society
**PRO 29:4** *See also* EZK 22:12 *Corruption, violence and decay render society subject to God's judgment.*
