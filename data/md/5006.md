# 5006: human race, destiny of

All human beings must face judgment, when God will reward them according to their attitude towards him.



## The human race will face final judgment
**MAT 16:27**; **1CO 4:5** *See also* DAN 12:1-12:2; MAL 3:16-3:18; MAT 13:40-13:43; JHN 5:24-5:29; ROM 2:5-2:11

## Unbelievers will be separated from God
**2TH 1:8-1:10** *See also* MAT 25:31-25:46; MRK 9:42-9:48; LUK 13:22-13:28; 2PE 3:7; REV 20:11-20:15

## Those who have faith will live in God's presence
**REV 21:1-21:3**; **JHN 14:1-14:3** *See also* ZEC 2:10-2:13; REV 7:13-7:17, REV 22:3-22:4

### United in the praise of God
**REV 7:9-7:10** *See also* ISA 2:2-2:4 parallel MIC 4:1-4:3; MAT 8:11 parallel LUK 13:29; REV 5:9-5:10, REV 21:22-21:27

### Delivered from suffering, pain and death
**REV 21:3-21:4** *See also* ISA 25:6-25:8, ISA 65:17-65:25; REV 7:16-7:17, REV 22:1-22:3

### Sharing in the glory of Jesus Christ
**COL 3:4**; **1PE 5:4** *See also* 2TI 4:8
