# 4651: goat

A common animal used both for food and within the sacrificial system.

## The economic importance of goats


### For food
**[GEN 27:9](bibleref:01027009)**; **[JDG 13:15](bibleref:07013015)** *See also* [DEU 14:4](bibleref:05014004), [DEU 14:21](bibleref:05014021); [JDG 6:19](bibleref:07006019); [PRO 27:27](bibleref:20027027); [LUK 15:29](bibleref:42015029)

### For fabric
**[EXO 35:26](bibleref:02035026)** *The tabernacle curtains were spun from goat hair, the ordinary material for making tents.*  *See also* [EXO 26:7](bibleref:02026007), [EXO 36:14](bibleref:02036014); [REV 6:12](bibleref:66006012)

### As a measure of wealth and payment
**[GEN 30:32](bibleref:01030032)**; **[1SA 25:2](bibleref:09025002)** *See also* [GEN 32:5](bibleref:01032005), [GEN 32:13-32:14](bibleref:01032013-01032014), [GEN 47:17](bibleref:01047017); [PRO 27:26](bibleref:20027026); [EZK 27:21](bibleref:26027021)

## Goats used in sacrifice


### For worship
 [GEN 15:9](bibleref:01015009); [JDG 13:19](bibleref:07013019); [PSA 66:13-66:15](bibleref:19066013-19066015)

### For atonement for sin
**[LEV 4:23](bibleref:03004023)**; **[EZR 8:35](bibleref:15008035)** *See also* [LEV 3:12](bibleref:03003012), [LEV 4:28](bibleref:03004028), [LEV 5:6](bibleref:03005006), [LEV 9:3](bibleref:03009003); [NUM 7:16](bibleref:04007016), [NUM 7:22](bibleref:04007022), [NUM 7:28](bibleref:04007028), [NUM 15:27](bibleref:04015027)

### As a fellowship offering
 [NUM 7:17](bibleref:04007017), [NUM 7:23](bibleref:04007023), [NUM 7:29](bibleref:04007029)

### At the dedication of the temple
**[1KI 8:63](bibleref:11008063)** parallel 2CH 7:5 *See also* [2CH 30:24](bibleref:14030024); [EZR 6:17](bibleref:15006017)

### Flawless goats were required
**[EXO 12:5](bibleref:02012005)** *See also* [LEV 1:10](bibleref:03001010); [EZK 43:22](bibleref:26043022)
