# 4651: goat

A common animal used both for food and within the sacrificial system.



## The economic importance of goats


### For food
**GEN 27:9**; **JDG 13:15** *See also* DEU 14:4, DEU 14:21; JDG 6:19; PRO 27:27; LUK 15:29

### For fabric
**EXO 35:26** *The tabernacle curtains were spun from goat hair, the ordinary material for making tents.* *See also* EXO 26:7, EXO 36:14; REV 6:12

### As a measure of wealth and payment
**GEN 30:32**; **1SA 25:2** *See also* GEN 32:5, GEN 32:13-32:14, GEN 47:17; PRO 27:26; EZK 27:21

## Goats used in sacrifice


### For worship
 GEN 15:9; ; JDG 13:19; ; PSA 66:13-66:15

### For atonement for sin
**LEV 4:23**; **EZR 8:35** *See also* LEV 3:12, LEV 4:28, LEV 5:6, LEV 9:3; NUM 7:16, NUM 7:22, NUM 7:28, NUM 15:27

### As a fellowship offering
 NUM 7:17, NUM 7:23, NUM 7:29

### At the dedication of the temple
**1KI 8:63** parallel 2CH 7:5 *See also* 2CH 30:24; EZR 6:17

### Flawless goats were required
**EXO 12:5** *See also* LEV 1:10; EZK 43:22

## The sacrifice of goats is unable to actually remove sins
 ISA 1:11 *In spite of their many sacrifices there was no evidence of repentance*; ; HEB 9:12-9:14, HEB 10:3-10:4

## Goats are symbolic of sinful humanity
**MAT 25:32**
