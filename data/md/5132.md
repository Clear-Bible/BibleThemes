# 5132: biting

The use of teeth to harm, threaten or express frustration. It is illustrative of the effect of slander, cold wind, excessive wine or of God's judgment.



## Biting used literally


### An effect of demon-possession
 *See also* MRK 9:18

### Biting by reptiles and animals
**NUM 21:6** *See also* ECC 10:8, ECC 10:11; DAN 6:24

### Reptiles and animals prevented from biting
**DAN 6:21-6:22** *See also* MRK 16:18; ACT 28:4-28:5; HEB 11:33

## Biting used metaphorically


### Of God's judgment on the wicked
**JER 8:17** *See also* JOL 1:6; AMO 5:19, AMO 9:3

### Of the wicked against God's people
**ACT 7:54** *See also* PSA 35:16, PSA 37:12; LAM 2:16; 1CO 15:32 *A fulfilment of the prophecy of Isa 22:13, indicating that the devouring of God's people is a part of the greed of the wicked*; 2TI 4:17

### Of Satan and demons
 1PE 5:8; ; REV 9:8

### Of the frustration of the wicked
**LUK 13:28** *See also* PSA 112:10; MAT 8:12, MAT 13:42, MAT 22:13, MAT 24:51, MAT 25:30; REV 16:10

### Of quarrelling, slander and treachery
**GAL 5:15** *See also* GEN 49:17; PSA 57:4, PSA 58:4, PSA 140:3; ROM 3:13-3:14; 2CO 12:20

### Of a cold wind
**JOB 37:9-37:10** *See also* ISA 28:2

### Of penetrating sarcasm
**MAT 23:27-23:28** *See also* PSA 115:3-115:7; ISA 46:5-46:7; 1CO 4:8; 2CO 12:11-12:13

### Of the effect of excessive wine
 PRO 23:31-23:32
