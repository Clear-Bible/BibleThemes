# 5132: biting

The use of teeth to harm, threaten or express frustration. It is illustrative of the effect of slander, cold wind, excessive wine or of God's judgment.

## Biting used literally


### An effect of demon-possession
 *See also* [MRK 9:18](bibleref:41009018)

### Biting by reptiles and animals
**[NUM 21:6](bibleref:04021006)** *See also* [ECC 10:8](bibleref:21010008), [ECC 10:11](bibleref:21010011); [DAN 6:24](bibleref:27006024)

### Reptiles and animals prevented from biting
**[DAN 6:21-6:22](bibleref:27006021-27006022)** *See also* [MRK 16:18](bibleref:41016018); [ACT 28:4-28:5](bibleref:44028004-44028005); [HEB 11:33](bibleref:58011033)
