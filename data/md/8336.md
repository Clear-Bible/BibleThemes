# 8336: reverence, and obedience

Reverence towards God expresses itself in the actions, as well as the words and worship of believers. True reverence for God leads to obedience towards him.



## Reverent fear is expressed in worship
**JON 1:16**

### Reverence in worship is expressed in bodily attitudes
Bowing:
 EXO 12:27; ; PSA 95:6; GEN 17:3; ; NUM 14:5; ; DAN 10:15; ; REV 5:14; 
EXO 3:5 *removing shoes*; ; EXO 3:6 *covering the face*; EZR 9:5; ; DAN 6:10; ; EPH 3:14

## Reverence can be learnt through religious observances


### A shared meal in God's presence
 DEU 14:23

### Hearing God's word
 DEU 4:9-4:14, DEU 31:12-31:13

### Reading God's word
 DEU 17:18-17:19

## Reverence motivates people to avoid doing wrong
**PRO 16:6** *See also* EXO 20:20; 2CH 19:9-19:10; JOB 1:1, JOB 28:28; PRO 3:7, PRO 8:13, PRO 14:16; 2CO 7:1

## Reverence leads people to obey God's law
**ECC 12:13** *See also* DEU 10:12-10:13, DEU 13:4; NEH 5:14-5:18 *generous treatment of dependants*; 2CO 5:11 *evangelism*; EPH 5:21 *mutual submission*; COL 3:22 *obedience to masters*

## Reverence is characterised by fearing God
 ECC 8:12; ; ACT 2:5

### Gentile believers were called ``God-fearing''
 ACT 10:2, ACT 13:26, ACT 13:50, ACT 17:4, ACT 17:17
