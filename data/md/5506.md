# 5506: roof

Most buildings in biblical times had flat roofs. With the hot climate, they had a wider range of uses than merely providing shelter for the building.

## Uses of roofs


### Shelter erected on the roof to make an extra room
**[2KI 4:10](bibleref:12004010)** *See also* [2SA 16:22](bibleref:10016022); [PRO 21:9](bibleref:20021009), [PRO 25:24](bibleref:20025024)

### Guests lodged on the roof
**[JOS 2:8](bibleref:06002008)** *See also* [1SA 9:25-9:26](bibleref:09009025-09009026)

### The roof used as a place for leisure and relaxation
**[2SA 11:2](bibleref:10011002)** *See also* [DAN 4:29](bibleref:27004029); [MAT 24:17](bibleref:40024017) parallels MRK 13:15, LUK 17:31

### The roof used as a place of religious observance
**[NEH 8:16](bibleref:16008016)** *See also* [2KI 23:12](bibleref:12023012); [JER 19:13](bibleref:24019013), [JER 32:29](bibleref:24032029); [ZEP 1:5](bibleref:36001005); [ACT 10:9](bibleref:44010009)

## In cities, some roofs were open, public places
**[ISA 15:3](bibleref:23015003)** *See also* [JDG 9:50-9:52](bibleref:07009050-07009052); [2SA 18:24](bibleref:10018024); [JER 48:38](bibleref:24048038); [MAT 10:26-10:27](bibleref:40010026-40010027) parallel LUK 12:2-12:3

## Roofs were usually constructed with a thick layer of clay
**[MRK 2:4](bibleref:41002004)** parallel LUK 5:19

## Roof as a synonym for house
**[MAT 8:8](bibleref:40008008)** parallel LUK 7:6
