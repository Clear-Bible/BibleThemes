# 1654: numbers, 11-99

## Fifty


### The fiftieth year as the Year of Jubilee
**LEV 25:10-25:11** *The word “jubilee” refers to the liberation of slaves, rather than to a fixed period of time. The number 50 represents the year following seven periods of seven years, i.e., 49 years + 1.*
