# 5800: blasphemy

The profaning, desecration and taking in vain of the name of God, or the reviling of any of his works or deeds. It is strongly forbidden by God as dishonouring to his name.

## Blasphemy strongly forbidden
**[EXO 20:7](bibleref:02020007)** parallel DEU 5:11 *See also* [EXO 22:28](bibleref:02022028); [LEV 18:21](bibleref:03018021), [LEV 19:12](bibleref:03019012), [LEV 22:32](bibleref:03022032); [NUM 15:30-15:31](bibleref:04015030-04015031)

## Blasphemy against God punished
 [LEV 24:10-24:16](bibleref:03024010-03024016), [LEV 24:23](bibleref:03024023); [2KI 19:20-19:37](bibleref:12019020-12019037) parallel ISA 37:21-37:38

## Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is unpardonable
**[MRK 3:28-3:30](bibleref:41003028-41003030)** parallels MAT 12:31-12:32, LUK 12:10 *See also* [HEB 10:29](bibleref:58010029)
