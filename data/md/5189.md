# 5189: teeth

Scripture often uses teeth symbolically, to refer to the power of individuals or to human rage (as in the “gnashing of teeth”).



## Teeth in a literal sense


### In eating
**NUM 11:33** *See also* DAN 7:5, DAN 7:7, DAN 7:19; ZEC 9:7; REV 9:8

### As an object of beauty
**SNG 4:2** *See also* GEN 49:12; SNG 6:6

### As an object of fear
**JOB 41:14** *See also* DEU 32:24; JOB 20:16; JOL 1:6

## Teeth used symbolically


### To represent offensive weapons
 PSA 57:4; ; PRO 30:14

### To symbolise military power
**ISA 41:15** *See also* JOL 1:6; AMO 1:3; HAB 3:12

### God rescues his people from the teeth of their enemies
**PSA 124:6** *See also* JOB 4:10; DAN 6:20-6:21

### Breaking the teeth of the wicked ends their power
**PSA 3:7** *See also* JOB 29:17; PSA 58:6 *Enemies are often described as dangerous animals.*

## Gnashing of teeth


### In rage
**ACT 7:54** *See also* JOB 16:9; PSA 35:16, PSA 37:12

### In frustration
 PSA 112:10

### In anguish
 MAT 8:12, MAT 13:42, MAT 13:50, MAT 22:13, MAT 24:51, MAT 25:30; ; LUK 13:28

### In gloating
 LAM 2:16

### Under demonic influence
 MRK 9:18

## Proverbial sayings about teeth
 JOB 19:20; ; PRO 10:26; ; JER 31:29-31:30; ; EZK 18:2
