# 5652: babies

The gift of young children from God is usually seen as a blessing. The term is also used figuratively of those who are young or immature in the faith.



## Babies as a blessing from God
**PSA 127:3** *See also* GEN 1:28, GEN 17:16, GEN 24:59-24:60; PRO 17:6; LUK 1:41-1:42

## The promise of a baby
**GEN 17:15-17:19** *See also* GEN 18:10-18:14; 2KI 4:14-4:17; LUK 1:11-1:24

## Examples of biblical characters as babies
 GEN 21:1-21:7 *Isaac*; ; GEN 25:21-25:26 *Esau and Jacob*; ; EXO 2:1-2:10 *Moses*; ; JDG 13:2-13:25 *Samson*; ; RUT 4:13-4:17 *Obed, the father of Jesse*; ; 2SA 12:24-12:25 *Solomon*; ; LUK 1:57-1:66 *John the Baptist*

## Babies as prophetic signs
**ISA 7:14** *See also* ISA 8:3-8:4, ISA 9:6-9:7; HOS 1:2-1:11; LUK 2:8-2:12

## OT laws concerned with the birth of babies
**LUK 2:21-2:24** *See also* GEN 17:9-17:12; EXO 13:1-13:2; LEV 12:1-12:8; LUK 1:59

## A mother's love for her baby
**ISA 49:15** *See also* 1KI 3:16-3:28; ISA 66:12-66:13

## Babies are sinful from birth
**PSA 51:5** *See also* PSA 58:3; JHN 9:34

## The death of babies
**EXO 1:15-1:22** *See also* EXO 11:4-11:5; 2SA 12:13-12:23; LAM 2:11-2:12; MAT 2:16-2:18; ACT 7:17-7:19

## Jesus Christ and babies


### Jesus Christ's own birth and babyhood
**MAT 1:18-1:23** *See also* ISA 7:14; MAT 2:1-2:23; LUK 1:26-1:38, LUK 2:1-2:40

### Jesus Christ's response to babies
**LUK 18:15-18:17** parallels MAT 19:13-19:15, MRK 10:13-10:16
