# 5527: shield

A piece of armour used to fend off blows or missiles such as arrows. God is described as the shield of his people; protecting, giving help, strength and favour. Faith is the shield with which believers are to ward off spiritual attack.



## Shields are pieces of armour
 *See also* 2CH 11:11-11:12, 2CH 32:5; NEH 4:16; NAM 2:5 *A number of shields were used to make a wall of defence.*

## Kinds of shield


### Large and small shields
**JER 46:3** *See also* 1KI 10:16-10:17 parallel 2CH 9:15-9:16; 2CH 14:8

### Gold shields
**2SA 8:7** parallel 1CH 18:7 *See also* 1KI 10:16-10:17, 1KI 14:26

### Bronze shields
**1KI 14:27**

### Shields made of combustible materials
**EZK 39:9**

### Shields covered with leather
**ISA 21:5** *Leather shields were rubbed with oil to preserve them and to deflect blows from swords or arrows.*

**1CH 5:18** *See also* 1SA 17:7; 2KI 19:32; JER 46:3, JER 46:9, JER 51:11; EZK 23:24
