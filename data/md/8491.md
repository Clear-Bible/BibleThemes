# 8491: watchfulness, divine

The Lord's alertness to the needs of his people, his constant care for them and his awareness of the power and evil intent of their enemies.



## The Lord's watchfulness over his people in danger or trouble
**PSA 121:1-121:8** *See also* EXO 3:16; DEU 32:9-32:10; EZR 5:5; PSA 1:6, PSA 11:4-11:5, PSA 17:8, PSA 32:6, PSA 33:16-33:18, PSA 34:7, PSA 127:1, PSA 145:20; ZEC 9:8, ZEC 12:4; MAT 10:29-10:31; JHN 10:1-10:9; 2TI 1:12

## The Lord's watchfulness means that his people's needs are met
**PSA 146:9** *See also* GEN 28:15, GEN 48:15; DEU 2:7, DEU 11:12; 2CH 16:9; JOB 10:12, JOB 29:2; JER 24:6, JER 31:28; DAN 9:18

### The watchfulness of a shepherd
 PSA 23:1, PSA 28:9; ; ISA 40:11; ; MAT 2:6; ; JHN 10:1-10:9

**PSA 34:15** *See also* 1KI 8:28-8:30 parallel 2CH 6:20; 1KI 9:3 parallel 2CH 7:16; NEH 1:6; 1PE 3:12

## The Lord's watchfulness with a view to correction

**EZK 34:16** *See also* MAT 18:12 parallel LUK 15:4; 1PE 2:25
