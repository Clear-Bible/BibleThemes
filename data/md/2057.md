# 2057: Jesus Christ, obedience of

The selfless obedience of Jesus Christ to the will of God his Father, through which the redemption of humanity is accomplished. Christ also shows himself willing to submit to earthly authorities and sets an example which believers are called to imitate.



## Jesus Christ was totally obedient to his Father's will


### Obedience was central to Jesus Christ's life and thought
**JHN 4:34**; **HEB 10:9** *See also* MAT 3:15, MAT 8:9-8:10 parallel LUK 7:8-7:9; JHN 5:30, JHN 6:38, JHN 14:31; HEB 10:7; PSA 40:7-40:8

### Jesus Christ's obedience meant ultimate personal cost
**MAT 26:39** parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42**PHP 2:8** *See also* ISA 50:5-50:6, ISA 53:10-53:12; MAT 16:21 parallels MRK 8:31, LUK 9:22; JHN 10:18, JHN 13:1; HEB 5:8

### Jesus Christ's obedience was necessary for God's salvation plan
**ROM 5:18-5:19** *See also* MAT 27:40-27:42 parallels MRK 15:30-15:31, LUK 23:35-23:37; LUK 24:26; JHN 17:2-17:4, JHN 17:26, JHN 19:30

## Jesus Christ's obedience was shown in his human relationships


### He was obedient to his parents
**LUK 2:51**

### He was obedient to secular authorities
 *See also* MAT 17:24-17:27, MAT 22:17-22:21 parallels MRK 12:15-12:17, LUK 20:22-20:25

### He was obedient to the Jewish law
 MAT 5:17-5:18, MAT 22:37-22:40 parallels MRK 12:29-12:31, LUK 10:26-10:28; ; MRK 2:23-2:28; ; JHN 7:19, JHN 10:37-10:38 *Jesus Christ's obedience and commitment were to the spirit of God's law; his attitudes to Jewish legalism were often seen as disobedience.  Disputes over the Sabbath are one example.*

## Believers should follow Jesus Christ's example of obedience


### Obedience to God's will is paramount
**MAT 19:17** *See also* MAT 7:21, MAT 12:50 parallels MRK 3:35, LUK 8:21; JHN 15:10; EPH 6:6

### Christlike obedience expresses love for God
**2JN 1:6** *See also* JHN 14:15, JHN 14:20-14:21, JHN 15:12

### Obedience should characterise believers' lives
**ROM 1:5** *“obedience” refers to a whole lifestyle arising from a relationship with God through Jesus Christ.* *See also* ACT 5:29; 2CO 2:9
