# 8245: ethics, incentives towards

God's ethical demands are reinforced by a variety of sanctions and incentives.

**ROM 13:3-13:4** *See also* DEU 16:18, DEU 17:11-17:12, DEU 25:1; PSA 82:3-82:4; 1TI 2:1-2:4; 1PE 2:13-2:14
