# 5214: attack

A violent act of aggression often associated with intent to invade, sometimes as an act of retribution and occasionally as a strategic act of self-defence. Scripture also recognises the possibility of spiritual aggression through attack by evil forces.



## Examples of God's people attacking others
 GEN 14:14-14:16 *Abraham attacks the captors of the king of Sodom*; ; JDG 7:19-7:21 *Gideon attacks the Midianites*; ; 1SA 14:13-14:14 *Jonathan attacks the Philistines*; ; 2SA 5:6-5:7 parallel 1CH 11:4-11:5 *David attacks the Jebusites*; ; DEU 20:1-20:20 *Moses gives detailed instruction on going to war.*
