# 5214: attack

A violent act of aggression often associated with intent to invade, sometimes as an act of retribution and occasionally as a strategic act of self-defence. Scripture also recognises the possibility of spiritual aggression through attack by evil forces.

## Examples of God's people attacking others
 [GEN 14:14-14:16](bibleref:01014014-01014016) *Abraham attacks the captors of the king of Sodom* ; [JDG 7:19-7:21](bibleref:07007019-07007021) *Gideon attacks the Midianites* ; [1SA 14:13-14:14](bibleref:09014013-09014014) *Jonathan attacks the Philistines* ; [2SA 5:6-5:7](bibleref:10005006-10005007) parallel 1CH 11:4-11:5 *David attacks the Jebusites* ; [DEU 20:1-20:20](bibleref:05020001-05020020) *Moses gives detailed instruction on going to war.*
