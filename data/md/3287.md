# 3287: Holy Spirit, sealing of

Based on the practice of sealing letters, documents and property, the Holy Spirit is described as being a seal on the lives of God's people, affirming their Christian character, marking them out as belonging to God and guarding them from the world and the devil.



## The Holy Spirit's seal on the life of Jesus Christ
**[JHN 6:27](bibleref:43006027)** *The Greek implies a once-and-for-all sealing at a particular time in the past and probably alludes to the baptism of Jesus Christ when the Holy Spirit came upon him.*  *See also* [ISA 42:1](bibleref:23042001), [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001)

## The seal of the Holy Spirit in the life of believers


### The seal marks God's ownership
**[2CO 1:21-1:22](bibleref:47001021-47001022)** *See also* [REV 7:3-7:4](bibleref:66007003-66007004), [REV 9:4](bibleref:66009004), [REV 14:1](bibleref:66014001), [REV 22:4](bibleref:66022004)
