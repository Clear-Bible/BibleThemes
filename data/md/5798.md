# 5798: betrayal

The treacherous exposing or deceiving of people by those they formerly trusted. It is usually associated with an enemy masquerading as a friend, or with a broken or abused relationship.  Betrayal was suffered by Jesus Christ and can be expected by his followers.



## Betrayal is part of human experience


### Examples of those experiencing betrayal
**PSA 41:9** *See also* JDG 16:18; 1SA 18:17; 2SA 19:26-19:27; PSA 55:12-55:14 *Betrayal carries acute feelings for its victims*; JER 12:6; LAM 1:2, LAM 1:19

### Examples of those murdered through betrayal
The power struggles of Israel's early kingship period gave rise to many bloody betrayals:
 2SA 3:27, 2SA 4:6, 2SA 11:15, 2SA 13:28-13:29, 2SA 20:10; ; 1KI 21:8-21:10

## Betrayal is seen as a particularly wicked act
**ISA 33:1** *See also* 1CH 12:17; PRO 25:9-25:10; ISA 24:16-24:17; MAT 26:24 parallels MRK 14:21, LUK 22:22

## The betrayal of Jesus Christ


### Judas determines to betray Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:14-26:16** parallels MRK 14:10-14:11, LUK 22:3-22:6

### Jesus Christ's knowledge of events
**MAT 26:21-26:23** parallel MRK 14:18-14:20 *See also* MAT 20:17-20:19 parallel MRK 10:33-10:34; LUK 22:21; JHN 13:11, JHN 13:18; PSA 41:9; JHN 13:21-13:27

### Jesus Christ is betrayed by a kiss
**MAT 26:47-26:49** parallels MRK 14:43-14:45, LUK 22:47-22:48 *The kiss featured in several betrayals as part of feigned friendship (see e.g., 2Sa 20:9-10 and Pr 27:6).*

### The betrayer's remorse
**MAT 27:3-27:5**

## Betrayal may be part of Christian experience
**MAT 10:21** parallels MRK 13:12, LUK 21:16 *See also* DAN 7:25; MAT 10:35-10:36; MIC 7:6

## Betrayal as a sign of the end times
 MAT 24:10
