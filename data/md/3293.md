# 3293: Holy Spirit, witness of

The Holy Spirit bears witness to the person and work of Jesus Christ, and enables believers to bear effective witness to him.



## The Holy Spirit bears witness to Jesus Christ


### The Spirit's testimony
**[1JN 4:2-4:3](bibleref:62004002-62004003)** *See also* [ACT 5:32](bibleref:44005032); [1CO 12:3](bibleref:46012003)

### At Jesus Christ's baptism
**[JHN 1:32-1:34](bibleref:43001032-43001034)** *See also* [MAT 3:16](bibleref:40003016) parallels MRK 1:10, LUK 3:22

### After Jesus Christ's ascension
**[JHN 15:26](bibleref:43015026)** *See also* [JHN 14:26](bibleref:43014026), [JHN 16:13-16:14](bibleref:43016013-43016014); [1JN 5:6](bibleref:62005006)

## The Holy Spirit's witness enables believers to be effective


### He brings assurance to believers
**[1JN 3:24](bibleref:62003024)** *See also* [1JN 4:13](bibleref:62004013), [1JN 5:7-5:10](bibleref:62005007-62005010)

### He gives assurance of the finality of Jesus Christ's saving work
**[HEB 10:14-10:17](bibleref:58010014-58010017)**

### He gives believers confidence in their status as children of God
**[ROM 8:15-8:16](bibleref:45008015-45008016)** *See also* [GAL 4:6](bibleref:48004006)
