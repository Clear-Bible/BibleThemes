# 6645: eternal life, nature of

Triumph over death and infinitely extended life. It also means a unique quality of life, knowing God in this life and sharing the life of Christ. Its source is God himself.

**[JHN 17:3](bibleref:43017003)**; **[PSA 16:11](bibleref:19016011)** *See also* [PSA 21:4-21:6](bibleref:19021004-19021006); [EPH 1:3-1:14](bibleref:49001003-49001014)
