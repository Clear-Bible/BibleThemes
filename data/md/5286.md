# 5286: custom

An established way or rule (often unwritten) of behaviour, either for an individual or in a society.



## Examples of the customs of individuals
 1SA 27:11; ; JOB 1:5; ; MRK 10:1; ; LUK 4:16; ; ACT 17:2

## Examples of group customs
 1SA 2:13; ; 1KI 18:28; ; LUK 1:9; ; ACT 16:20-16:21

## Examples of social customs

MAT 27:15 parallels MRK 15:6, JHN 18:39 *release of prisoners at Passover*; LUK 2:27, LUK 2:42;
