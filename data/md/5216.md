# 5216: authority, nature of

The God-given freedom of action expressed in rightful power to control the actions of others.



## Ultimate authority belongs to God, who does as he pleases
**PSA 115:3** *See also* 2CH 20:6; JOB 9:12, JOB 23:13, JOB 42:2; PSA 135:6; ISA 14:27, ISA 43:13, ISA 45:9, ISA 46:10; DAN 4:35 *Nebuchadnezzar acknowledges God's authority*; ROM 9:19-9:21; EPH 1:11 *God's authority is demonstrated in election.*

## Human authority


### All human authority is given and established by God
**ROM 13:1** *See also* DAN 2:21, DAN 4:17, DAN 5:18-5:21; JHN 19:11

### It involves the rightful freedom to give orders
**MAT 8:9** parallel LUK 7:8 *The centurion to Jesus Christ.* *See also* GEN 41:35; NUM 27:20; DEU 1:15 *Moses delegates authority*; JDG 9:29; EZR 7:24; NEH 3:7; EST 9:29; ISA 22:21; JER 38:10; MAT 25:23 parallel LUK 19:17 *the parable of the talents*; MRK 13:34; LUK 20:20, LUK 23:7; JHN 19:10ACT 9:14, ACT 26:10, ACT 26:12
1PE 2:13

### It is limited, unlike God's authority
**JHN 19:10-19:11**

## Responsibilities of those with authority


### They are to act as servants, not lording it over people
**MAT 20:25-20:28** parallel MRK 10:42-10:45 *See also* JHN 13:4-13:15

### Their authority is to be used for the benefit of others
**2CO 10:8** *See also* 2CO 13:10

### It is sometimes necessary to withhold exercising authority for others' good
**1CO 9:4-9:18** *See also* 1CO 6:1-6:7, 1CO 8:8-8:13, 1CO 10:23-10:24

## Examples of the authority of believers


### Authority over possessions
**ACT 5:4** *Peter to Ananias regarding the land he sold.*

### Authority over the will
**1CO 7:37** *Paul on marriage.*

### Authority to become children of God
**JHN 1:12**

## The authority of the law over believers during their lifetime
**ROM 7:1**
