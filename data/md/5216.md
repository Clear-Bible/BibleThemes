# 5216: authority, nature of

The God-given freedom of action expressed in rightful power to control the actions of others.



## Ultimate authority belongs to God, who does as he pleases
**[PSA 115:3](bibleref:19115003)** *See also* [2CH 20:6](bibleref:14020006); [JOB 9:12](bibleref:18009012), [JOB 23:13](bibleref:18023013), [JOB 42:2](bibleref:18042002); [PSA 135:6](bibleref:19135006); [ISA 14:27](bibleref:23014027), [ISA 43:13](bibleref:23043013), [ISA 45:9](bibleref:23045009), [ISA 46:10](bibleref:23046010); [DAN 4:35](bibleref:27004035) *Nebuchadnezzar acknowledges God's authority* ; [ROM 9:19-9:21](bibleref:45009019-45009021); [EPH 1:11](bibleref:49001011) *God's authority is demonstrated in election.*
