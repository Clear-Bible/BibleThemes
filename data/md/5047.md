# 5047: opportunities, in life

Scripture views life as divinely ordained to include occasions when certain forms of behaviour are appropriate.



## The need to discern opportune moments
**ECC 3:1**

## Every possible opportunity should be  taken to do good
**GAL 6:10** *See also* LUK 10:33; JHN 9:4; EPH 5:16; COL 4:5; 2TI 4:2

## Examples of individuals who seized opportunities to serve God
Rahab saved the spies and her household:
 JOS 2:8-2:14, JOS 6:23; 
2KI 5:1-5:3 *Naaman's wife's maid helped Naaman by telling her mistress about Elisha*; ; NEH 2:2-2:5 *Nehemiah gained permission to rebuild the walls of Jerusalem.*; EST 4:14, EST 7:3-7:6; 
DAN 2:14-2:16 *Daniel offered to interpret Nebuchadnezzar's dream*; ; ACT 18:24-18:26 *Priscilla and Aquila taught Apollos.*

## Some opportunities should be rejected
 1SA 24:3-24:4, 1SA 24:10, 1SA 26:6-26:11

## Opportunities to do evil may have appalling consequences
 GEN 4:8 *Cain made an opportunity to murder Abel*; ; GEN 25:29-25:31 *Jacob took the opportunity to “buy” Esau's birthright.*; GEN 37:18-37:20, GEN 37:27; 
DAN 6:4-6:5 *Daniel's enemies sought and found an opportunity to lay a charge against him.*; MAT 26:16 parallels MRK 14:11, LUK 22:6

## Giving others an opportunity to do wrong must be avoided
 1TI 5:14
