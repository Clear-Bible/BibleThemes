# 5047: opportunities, in life

Scripture views life as divinely ordained to include occasions when certain forms of behaviour are appropriate.



## The need to discern opportune moments
**[ECC 3:1](bibleref:21003001)**

## Every possible opportunity should be  taken to do good
**[GAL 6:10](bibleref:48006010)** *See also* [LUK 10:33](bibleref:42010033); [JHN 9:4](bibleref:43009004); [EPH 5:16](bibleref:49005016); [COL 4:5](bibleref:51004005); [2TI 4:2](bibleref:55004002)

## Examples of individuals who seized opportunities to serve God
Rahab saved the spies and her household:
 [JOS 2:8-2:14](bibleref:06002008-06002014), [JOS 6:23](bibleref:06006023)
[2KI 5:1-5:3](bibleref:12005001-12005003) *Naaman's wife's maid helped Naaman by telling her mistress about Elisha* ; [NEH 2:2-2:5](bibleref:16002002-16002005) *Nehemiah gained permission to rebuild the walls of Jerusalem.* [EST 4:14](bibleref:17004014), [EST 7:3-7:6](bibleref:17007003-17007006)
[DAN 2:14-2:16](bibleref:27002014-27002016) *Daniel offered to interpret Nebuchadnezzar's dream* ; [ACT 18:24-18:26](bibleref:44018024-44018026) *Priscilla and Aquila taught Apollos.* 

## Some opportunities should be rejected
 [1SA 24:3-24:4](bibleref:09024003-09024004), [1SA 24:10](bibleref:09024010), [1SA 26:6-26:11](bibleref:09026006-09026011)

## Opportunities to do evil may have appalling consequences
 [GEN 4:8](bibleref:01004008) *Cain made an opportunity to murder Abel* ; [GEN 25:29-25:31](bibleref:01025029-01025031) *Jacob took the opportunity to “buy” Esau's birthright.* [GEN 37:18-37:20](bibleref:01037018-01037020), [GEN 37:27](bibleref:01037027)
[DAN 6:4-6:5](bibleref:27006004-27006005) *Daniel's enemies sought and found an opportunity to lay a charge against him.* [MAT 26:16](bibleref:40026016) parallels MRK 14:11, LUK 22:6

## Giving others an opportunity to do wrong must be avoided
 [1TI 5:14](bibleref:54005014)
