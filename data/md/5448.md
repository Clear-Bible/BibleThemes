# 5448: poverty, attitudes towards

The plight of those in need calls for compassionate action of which Jesus Christ is the great example and in which the early church was faithful.  Such compassion will be rewarded.



## The treatment of the poor


### They are not to be neglected
**DEU 15:7-15:8** *See also* PRO 17:5; ROM 12:13; GAL 6:10; JAS 2:15-2:16; 1JN 3:17

### They are not to be unjustly treated
**EXO 23:6** *See also* LEV 25:35-25:37; DEU 24:14-24:15; 2SA 12:1-12:4 *Nathan rebukes David in the parable about the rich man and the poor man*; JOB 24:1-24:4; PRO 22:22, PRO 29:7; JER 22:2-22:3, JER 22:16; EZK 18:10-18:13; AMO 2:6-2:7; ZEC 7:10; JAS 2:2-2:4 *favouritism forbidden in Christian meetings*

### They are to have special rights and privileges
**DEU 14:28-14:29** *See also* EXO 23:11; LEV 19:9-19:10, LEV 25:5-25:6; PRO 19:17

### They are to be cared for
**PSA 82:3-82:4** *See also* JOB 29:11-29:17; LUK 3:11; ACT 9:36

### They are to be helped generously
**DEU 15:9-15:11** *See also* DEU 14:29; RUT 2:14; PSA 112:9; JER 39:10; LUK 19:8; ACT 10:2; 2CO 9:7

### They are to be helped without ostentation
**MAT 6:2**

## Jesus Christ's compassion for the poor
**MAT 15:32** parallel MRK 8:1-8:3 *See also* LUK 4:18-4:19; JHN 13:29; ACT 10:38

## The attitude of the first Christians towards poverty
**ACT 2:44-2:45** *See also* ACT 4:32-4:35, ACT 11:29-11:30, ACT 20:35, ACT 24:17; ROM 15:25-15:27; 2CO 8:13-8:15; GAL 2:10

## Compassion for the poor will be rewarded
**PSA 41:1** *See also* PRO 19:17, PRO 28:27; MAT 19:21, MAT 25:34-25:36; MRK 9:41; HEB 13:16
