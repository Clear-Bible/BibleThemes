# 2309: Jesus Christ, as judge

Jesus Christ executed judgment against the forces of evil through his death on the cross. Individuals will be judged according to their response to his saving grace.



## Jesus Christ is appointed judge by God the Father
**[JHN 5:22](bibleref:43005022)** *See also* [JHN 5:27](bibleref:43005027); [ACT 10:42](bibleref:44010042), [ACT 17:31](bibleref:44017031)

## Jesus Christ is a just judge
**[JHN 5:30](bibleref:43005030)** *See also* [ISA 11:3-11:4](bibleref:23011003-23011004); [MIC 4:3](bibleref:33004003); [JHN 8:15-8:16](bibleref:43008015-43008016); [ACT 17:31](bibleref:44017031); [REV 19:11](bibleref:66019011)
