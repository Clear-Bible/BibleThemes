# 4834: light, natural

Scripture stresses that every source of light (sun, moon and stars) is created by God, and subject to him.



## God is the source of natural light
**[GEN 1:3-1:5](bibleref:01001003-01001005)** *See also* [GEN 1:14-1:18](bibleref:01001014-01001018); [2SA 22:13-22:15](bibleref:10022013-10022015); [JOB 41:18-41:21](bibleref:18041018-18041021); [PSA 8:3-8:4](bibleref:19008003-19008004), [PSA 74:16](bibleref:19074016), [PSA 97:4](bibleref:19097004), [PSA 136:7-136:9](bibleref:19136007-19136009); [ISA 45:7](bibleref:23045007); [JER 31:35](bibleref:24031035); [JAS 1:17](bibleref:59001017)
