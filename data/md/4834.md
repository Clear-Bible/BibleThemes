# 4834: light, natural

Scripture stresses that every source of light (sun, moon and stars) is created by God, and subject to him.



## God is the source of natural light
**GEN 1:3-1:5** *See also* GEN 1:14-1:18; 2SA 22:13-22:15; JOB 41:18-41:21; PSA 8:3-8:4, PSA 74:16, PSA 97:4, PSA 136:7-136:9; ISA 45:7; JER 31:35; JAS 1:17

## God controls natural light
**PSA 104:19** *See also* JOB 9:7, JOB 36:30-36:32; PSA 121:5-121:6; MAT 5:45; 2CO 4:6
