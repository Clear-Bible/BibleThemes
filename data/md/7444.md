# 7444: sin offering

Offerings to make atonement for both moral and ritual offences. The NT sees them as foreshadowing the death of Jesus Christ as an offering for human sin.

## Occasions when a sin offering was required
Unintentional sins against God's laws:
 [LEV 4:2](bibleref:03004002); [HEB 9:7](bibleref:58009007)
[LEV 5:1-5:4](bibleref:03005001-03005004), [LEV 4:20](bibleref:03004020) *The consequence of the offering is atonement and forgiveness.* [LEV 12:6-12:8](bibleref:03012006-03012008); [LUK 2:24](bibleref:42002024)[LEV 14:13-14:31](bibleref:03014013-03014031); [MRK 1:44](bibleref:41001044)[LEV 15:15](bibleref:03015015), [LEV 15:30](bibleref:03015030)

## The form the sin offering took depended on who committed the sin
 [LEV 4:3](bibleref:03004003) *a priest who sins* ; [LEV 4:14](bibleref:03004014) *the sin of the community as a whole* ; [LEV 4:22-4:23](bibleref:03004022-03004023) *a leader who sins unintentionally* [LEV 4:27-4:35](bibleref:03004027-03004035), [LEV 5:5-5:6](bibleref:03005005-03005006)

## The ceremony when presenting an animal as a sin offering


### The guilty identify themselves with the offering before killing it
 [LEV 4:4](bibleref:03004004), [LEV 4:15](bibleref:03004015), [LEV 4:24](bibleref:03004024)

### Confession
 [LEV 5:5](bibleref:03005005)

### Blood, representing the victim's lifeblood, is put on the altar
 [LEV 4:25](bibleref:03004025), [LEV 4:30](bibleref:03004030), [LEV 4:34](bibleref:03004034)

### The best parts, being burned on the altar, are offered to God
 [LEV 4:8-4:10](bibleref:03004008-03004010), [LEV 4:26](bibleref:03004026), [LEV 4:35](bibleref:03004035)

### Male members of priestly families may eat what remains
 [LEV 6:26](bibleref:03006026), [LEV 6:29](bibleref:03006029), [LEV 10:16-10:20](bibleref:03010016-03010020)

### Special procedures for the offerings of the poor
 [LEV 5:7-5:13](bibleref:03005007-03005013)

## The special ceremony for priests and the community presenting a sin offering


### Blood is taken into the sanctuary and placed on the altar
 [LEV 4:5-4:7](bibleref:03004005-03004007), [LEV 4:16-4:18](bibleref:03004016-03004018)

### The rest of the animal is incinerated, not eaten
 [LEV 4:11-4:12](bibleref:03004011-03004012), [LEV 4:21](bibleref:03004021) *emphasising the identity between the victim and the sin of the community* 

## Significant occasions when sin offerings were made
 [LEV 8:14-8:17](bibleref:03008014-03008017) *at the ordination of Aaron and his sons* ; [LEV 16:1-16:34](bibleref:03016001-03016034) *on the Day of Atonement* ; [2KI 12:16](bibleref:12012016) *during Josiah's reformation* ; [2CH 29:21-29:24](bibleref:14029021-14029024) *at the beginning of Hezekiah's reign* ; [EZR 6:17](bibleref:15006017) *at the dedication of the second temple* ; [EZR 8:35](bibleref:15008035) *when the exiles returned* [EZK 40:39](bibleref:26040039), [EZK 42:13](bibleref:26042013), [EZK 43:19](bibleref:26043019), [EZK 43:21](bibleref:26043021)

## The NT perspective on the sin offering


### Jesus Christ was the supreme sin offering
**[ROM 8:3](bibleref:45008003)**; **[2CO 5:21](bibleref:47005021)** *NIV footnote.* 

### Animal sacrifices had limited value
 *See also* [HEB 9:7-9:10](bibleref:58009007-58009010)

**[HEB 9:11-9:14](bibleref:58009011-58009014)** *See also* [1PE 1:18-1:19](bibleref:60001018-60001019)
