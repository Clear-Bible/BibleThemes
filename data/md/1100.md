# 1100: God, perfection of

God is complete, faultless and totally sufficient in every aspect of his being and ways. That perfection is made known in Jesus Christ, and is the ultimate goal of the Christian life.



## God's moral perfection
**PSA 25:8** *See also* EXO 33:19; PSA 18:25-18:26, PSA 92:15; ISA 26:7; MRK 10:18 parallel LUK 18:19

### Humanity should emulate God's moral perfection
**MAT 5:48** *See also* LEV 19:2, LEV 11:44-11:45; 1PE 1:16; 2CO 7:1, 2CO 13:11

**DEU 32:4** *See also* 2SA 22:31 parallel PSA 18:30; PSA 19:1, PSA 40:5, PSA 96:3, PSA 139:14, PSA 145:4-145:6; JER 32:19-32:20

## God's will is perfect
**ROM 12:2** *See also* PSA 19:7-19:11
