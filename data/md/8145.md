# 8145: renewal, of people of God

The transformation by God of the lives of his people, whether corporately or individually, leading to the recovery of lost vitality and purity.



## Individual renewal
**ISA 40:30-40:31**; **EPH 4:22-4:24** *See also* PSA 23:3, PSA 51:10-51:12, PSA 103:1-103:5; JHN 7:37-7:39; ROM 12:2; 2CO 4:16; COL 3:9-3:10; 2TI 1:6; TIT 3:5

## Corporate renewal


### The renewal of Israel
**EZK 37:1-37:14** *See also* JER 31:31-31:34; LAM 5:21; EZK 11:17-11:20, EZK 36:24-36:28; JOL 2:28-2:32

### Examples of national renewal
**2CH 34:29-34:33** *See also* JOS 24:1, JOS 24:14-24:27; EZR 10:1-10:4; NEH 10:28-10:29

### The new Jerusalem
**ISA 65:17-65:19** *See also* REV 3:12, REV 21:2-21:4, REV 21:10

### The church
**MAT 9:16-9:19** parallels MRK 2:21-2:22, LUK 5:36-5:39 *Jesus Christ here prophesies the great act of renewal by God whereby the church rather than Israel becomes the people of God.* *See also* ACT 2:1-2:4, ACT 2:42-2:47
