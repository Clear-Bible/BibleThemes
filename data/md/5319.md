# 5319: fugitives

People fleeing, especially from persecution or punishment and seeking refuge. The OT makes special provision for the needs of such people.



## Fugitives are those in flight


### From family
**GEN 35:1** *See also* GEN 35:7; JDG 9:21; 2SA 13:34, 2SA 15:13-15:15

### From war
**2SA 24:13**; **OBA 1:14** *See also* LEV 26:36; NUM 21:29; JOS 8:20, JOS 8:22; 2SA 10:14; JER 46:22, JER 48:19; EZK 17:21, EZK 24:26

### From other people
**EXO 2:15**; **MAT 2:13** *See also* EXO 14:27; 1SA 22:17; 1KI 2:39, 1KI 11:40; ISA 15:5, ISA 15:9, ISA 45:20; JER 48:6, JER 48:45; MAT 24:15-24:20 parallels MRK 13:14-13:17, LUK 21:21-21:23; ACT 8:1

**JER 50:28** *See also* GEN 4:14; JOB 15:22; PRO 28:17; JER 44:14, JER 48:19
