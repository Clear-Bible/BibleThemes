# 8710: atheism

The denial of God's existence and thus also the denial of his word, truth and authority. It is seen in the worship of created things, in human boasting and in the decision to live a life without God which results in godlessness and brings God's judgment.



## Characteristics of atheism


### Denying God's existence
**PSA 14:1** parallel PSA 53:1

### Worshipping created things
**ROM 1:25** *See also* DAN 5:23; REV 9:20

### Denying God's truth
**ISA 32:6**; **ROM 1:18** *See also* JER 5:12, JER 5:31, JER 11:21, JER 14:14, JER 27:15

### Denying God's ability to fulfil his word
**JER 17:15** *See also* ISA 5:19; ACT 17:18; 1TH 4:8; 2PE 3:3-3:4; REV 11:7-11:10

### Showing contempt for God's name
 LEV 19:12; ; JER 5:2; ; EZK 36:20, EZK 43:8

### Boasting against God
**PSA 10:3-10:5** *See also* JOB 15:25; PSA 12:3-12:4, PSA 36:1-36:2, PSA 73:11; PRO 30:9; EZK 35:13; REV 13:6

### Not turning to God in trouble
**JOB 35:10** *See also* JOB 36:13; JER 2:8, JER 17:5

### Living in ignorance of God
**JER 5:4** *See also* EPH 2:12

### Denying Jesus Christ
**JUD 1:4** *See also* 1JN 2:22, 1JN 4:3; 2JN 1:7

## Atheism results in godless living
**ROM 1:28-1:31** *See also* GEN 6:5; 1SA 2:12; PRO 11:9; ISA 9:17, ISA 26:10; JER 5:5, JER 23:11; MAL 3:14, MAL 3:18; 1TI 1:9-1:10; JUD 1:18

## Atheism brings condemnation


### It cannot be excused
 JOB 12:7-12:8; ; ACT 14:17; ; ROM 1:19-1:20

### It results in lack of hope
 JOB 8:13, JOB 15:34, JOB 20:5, JOB 27:8; ; PSA 9:17

### It results in God's judgment
 ISA 10:6, ISA 33:14; ; JER 5:5-5:6, JER 5:9-5:11, JER 5:29; ; ROM 1:18; ; 1PE 4:17; ; 2PE 3:7; ; JUD 1:14-1:15

## God desires to save the atheist
**ACT 17:27** *See also* ISA 55:7; JER 3:12; EZK 18:23, EZK 33:11; ROM 5:6-5:8
