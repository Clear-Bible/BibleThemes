# 6750: sin-bearer

A person or animal that acts in God's sight in a substitutionary capacity, to whom are transferred the sins of others, together with the corresponding penalty for those sins.

## The principle of accountability necessitates a sin-bearer


### All are held responsible for their own sin and its consequences
**[LEV 5:17](bibleref:03005017)** *See also* [LEV 5:1](bibleref:03005001), [LEV 20:16-20:21](bibleref:03020016-03020021), [LEV 24:15-24:16](bibleref:03024015-03024016); [EZK 18:19-18:20](bibleref:26018019-26018020)

### Individuals and nations come under God's judgment for their sin
**[EZK 4:4-4:6](bibleref:26004004-26004006)** *Ezekiel's behaviour foreshadowed God's judgment on the sin of Israel and Judah.*  *See also* [EZK 16:58](bibleref:26016058), [EZK 18:4](bibleref:26018004); [MAT 11:21-11:24](bibleref:40011021-40011024) parallel LUK 10:12-10:15; [ROM 1:18-1:32](bibleref:45001018-45001032), [ROM 6:23](bibleref:45006023)

## God's gracious provision of a sin-bearer in the OT


### God takes away the sin of those who repent
**[ISA 44:22](bibleref:23044022)** *See also* [2SA 12:13](bibleref:10012013); [PSA 103:12](bibleref:19103012); [ZEC 3:4](bibleref:38003004)

### The sacrifice of an animal to remove sin
**[LEV 5:5-5:6](bibleref:03005005-03005006)** *See also* [LEV 4:1-4:35](bibleref:03004001-03004035), [LEV 5:14-6:7](bibleref:03005014-03006007)

### The Day of Atonement and the removal of sin
**[LEV 16:8-16:10](bibleref:03016008-03016010)** *The sacrificial goat expiated the sins of the people in the sight of God; the driving away of the scapegoat into the desert symbolised the removal of the sin of the community.*  *See also* [LEV 16:15-16:19](bibleref:03016015-03016019) *the sin offering* ; [LEV 16:20-16:22](bibleref:03016020-03016022) *the scapegoat*
