# 4808: crimson

A deep red colour used interchangeably with scarlet and sometimes with purple. It has associations with blood and richness.

## The richness of crimson used for ornamentation
**[EXO 25:3-25:4](bibleref:02025003-02025004), [EXO 28:6](bibleref:02028006)** *It was used extensively in the construction of the tabernacle* ; **[2CH 2:7](bibleref:14002007)** *See also* [2CH 2:14](bibleref:14002014), [2CH 3:14](bibleref:14003014)
