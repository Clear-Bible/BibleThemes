# 5603: wages

Payment by an employer for work done; all labourers should be paid fairly and regularly.

## Laws relating to wages


### Payment should be made regularly
**[DEU 24:14-24:15](bibleref:05024014-05024015)** *See also* [LEV 19:13](bibleref:03019013)

### Regular rates of pay
 [LEV 25:50](bibleref:03025050); [MRK 6:37](bibleref:41006037) parallel JHN 6:7; [MRK 14:5](bibleref:41014005) parallel JHN 12:5

### Labourers should not be defrauded
**[MAL 3:5](bibleref:39003005)**; **[JAS 5:4](bibleref:59005004)** *See also* [JER 22:13](bibleref:24022013)

### A fair wage should be paid
**[MAT 20:2-20:4](bibleref:40020002-40020004)** *A normal day's pay for a labourer in NT times was one denarius.*  *See also* [COL 4:1](bibleref:51004001)

### A fair day's work should be done
**[2TH 3:10-3:12](bibleref:53003010-53003012)** *See also* [EPH 6:7-6:8](bibleref:49006007-49006008); [COL 3:23](bibleref:51003023)

### Labourers should be content with a fair wage
**[LUK 3:14](bibleref:42003014)**
