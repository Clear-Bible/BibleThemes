# 5603: wages

Payment by an employer for work done; all labourers should be paid fairly and regularly.



## Laws relating to wages


### Payment should be made regularly
**DEU 24:14-24:15** *See also* LEV 19:13

### Regular rates of pay
 LEV 25:50; ; MRK 6:37 parallel JHN 6:7; ; MRK 14:5 parallel JHN 12:5

### Labourers should not be defrauded
**MAL 3:5**; **JAS 5:4** *See also* JER 22:13

### A fair wage should be paid
**MAT 20:2-20:4** *A normal day's pay for a labourer in NT times was one denarius.* *See also* COL 4:1

### A fair day's work should be done
**2TH 3:10-3:12** *See also* EPH 6:7-6:8; COL 3:23

### Labourers should be content with a fair wage
**LUK 3:14**

## Wage earners suffer in times of trouble


### Egypt under judgment
**ISA 19:10**

### Jerusalem under judgment
**HAG 1:6** *See also* ZEC 8:10

### The world under judgment
 REV 6:6

## The example of Jacob as a wage earner
**HOS 12:12** *See also* GEN 29:15 *He agreed his wages beforehand*; GEN 29:18 *He was paid in kind*; GEN 29:25-29:27 *He was cheated by Laban*; GEN 30:28-30:34 *He makes an arrangement he can turn to advantage*; GEN 31:6-31:7 *He accuses Laban of dishonesty*; GEN 31:8-31:9 *He protests his own honesty*; GEN 31:38-31:42 *He looks back on his experience.*
