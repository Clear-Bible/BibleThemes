# 5166: liver and kidneys





## The liver and kidneys as vital internal organs of the body
**PRO 7:23** *See also* JOB 16:13, JOB 20:24-20:25

## The liver and kidneys are symbolic of human emotions and the conscience
**PSA 73:21** *The Hebrew words for liver and kidneys are occasionally translated as “heart”, “spirit” or “inmost being”.* *See also* JOB 19:27; PRO 23:16; JER 12:2; LAM 2:11, LAM 3:13

### God examines the innermost being as symbolised by the kidneys
**PSA 26:2** *See also* PSA 7:9; JER 11:20, JER 17:10, JER 20:12

### God is concerned for the innermost being as symbolised by the kidneys
**PSA 139:13** *See also* PSA 16:7

## The liver and kidneys as offered in animal sacrifice


### Liver and kidneys offered in various sacrifices
**LEV 3:3-3:5** *The liver and kidneys are associated with the fat as the choice parts of the animal.* *See also* LEV 3:9-3:11, LEV 3:14-3:16, LEV 4:8-4:10, LEV 7:3-7:6

### Liver and kidneys had a central role in the ordination services for priests
 EXO 29:13, EXO 29:22-29:25; ; LEV 8:16-8:17, LEV 8:25-8:28, LEV 9:8-9:10, LEV 9:19-9:21

## Divination by inspection of the liver
 EZK 21:21 *Divination by inspection of the liver (hepatoscopy) was a common practice. The future was predicted from the colour and configuration of the liver of a sacrificial animal, usually a sheep.*
