# 5166: liver and kidneys


## The liver and kidneys as vital internal organs of the body
**[PRO 7:23](bibleref:20007023)** *See also* [JOB 16:13](bibleref:18016013), [JOB 20:24-20:25](bibleref:18020024-18020025)

## The liver and kidneys are symbolic of human emotions and the conscience
**[PSA 73:21](bibleref:19073021)** *The Hebrew words for liver and kidneys are occasionally translated as “heart”, “spirit” or “inmost being”.*  *See also* [JOB 19:27](bibleref:18019027); [PRO 23:16](bibleref:20023016); [JER 12:2](bibleref:24012002); [LAM 2:11](bibleref:25002011), [LAM 3:13](bibleref:25003013)

### God examines the innermost being as symbolised by the kidneys
**[PSA 26:2](bibleref:19026002)** *See also* [PSA 7:9](bibleref:19007009); [JER 11:20](bibleref:24011020), [JER 17:10](bibleref:24017010), [JER 20:12](bibleref:24020012)

### God is concerned for the innermost being as symbolised by the kidneys
**[PSA 139:13](bibleref:19139013)** *See also* [PSA 16:7](bibleref:19016007)

## The liver and kidneys as offered in animal sacrifice


### Liver and kidneys offered in various sacrifices
**[LEV 3:3-3:5](bibleref:03003003-03003005)** *The liver and kidneys are associated with the fat as the choice parts of the animal.*  *See also* [LEV 3:9-3:11](bibleref:03003009-03003011), [LEV 3:14-3:16](bibleref:03003014-03003016), [LEV 4:8-4:10](bibleref:03004008-03004010), [LEV 7:3-7:6](bibleref:03007003-03007006)

### Liver and kidneys had a central role in the ordination services for priests
 [EXO 29:13](bibleref:02029013), [EXO 29:22-29:25](bibleref:02029022-02029025); [LEV 8:16-8:17](bibleref:03008016-03008017), [LEV 8:25-8:28](bibleref:03008025-03008028), [LEV 9:8-9:10](bibleref:03009008-03009010), [LEV 9:19-9:21](bibleref:03009019-03009021)

## Divination by inspection of the liver
 [EZK 21:21](bibleref:26021021) *Divination by inspection of the liver (hepatoscopy) was a common practice. The future was predicted from the colour and configuration of the liver of a sacrificial animal, usually a sheep.*
