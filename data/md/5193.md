# 5193: tongue

The organ of speech, which Scripture stresses can be the source of both praise and blame. The tongue is to be kept under control, on account of its capacity for evil words.



## The tongue as a bodily organ


### As part of the body
**JAS 3:5** *See also* JDG 7:5; JOB 41:1; MRK 7:33; LUK 16:24; REV 16:10

### Used for eating
 NUM 22:4; ; JOB 12:11, JOB 34:3; ; PSA 68:23

### Used for drinking
 JDG 7:5

### Used for licking
 1KI 21:19, 1KI 22:38; ; LUK 16:21

### Parched with thirst
 PSA 22:15, PSA 137:6; ; ISA 41:17; ; LAM 4:4

## The tongue as an organ of speech


### It is God-given
**EXO 4:10-4:11** *See also* EZK 3:26; LUK 1:20-1:22, LUK 1:62-1:64

### God is able to loose the tongue of the mute
**MRK 7:33-7:35** *See also* ISA 32:4, ISA 35:6; MAT 15:30-15:31; LUK 1:64

## Reasons for holding one's tongue


### To restrain sin
**PRO 10:19** *See also* PRO 11:12, PRO 17:27-17:28, PRO 21:23; JAS 1:26; 1PE 3:10

### As a mark of respect and awe
 JOB 29:9-29:10; ; HAB 2:20

## The tongue's potential for good


### Right use of the tongue requires an act of the will
**JOB 27:4** *See also* PSA 39:1, PSA 141:3

### The tongue can praise God
**PSA 35:28** *See also* PSA 16:9, PSA 45:1, PSA 51:14, PSA 66:17, PSA 71:24, PSA 119:172, PSA 126:2; ACT 2:26; JAS 3:9

### The tongue can speak God-given words
**2SA 23:2** *See also* PRO 16:1; ISA 50:4; MIC 3:8; LUK 21:14-21:15

### Every tongue will acknowledge God
**ISA 45:23** *See also* ROM 14:11; PHP 2:10-2:11

### The tongue can speak wisdom
**PSA 37:30** *See also* PRO 10:19-10:20, PRO 12:18, PRO 15:23, PRO 31:26

### The tongue can bring healing
**PRO 15:4** *See also* PRO 12:18

## The tongue's potential for evil


### The tongue's power needs to be controlled
**PRO 18:21**; **JAS 3:3-3:10** *See also* JOB 27:4; PSA 34:13; PRO 11:12; JAS 1:26

### The tongue can speak deceitfully

**PSA 10:7** *See also* PSA 12:3-12:4, PSA 52:2; PRO 6:24; ISA 57:4, ISA 59:3; JER 18:18
