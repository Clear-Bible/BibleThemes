# 1347: covenant, God's with Noah

God's confirmation of, and commitment to maintain, his relationship with the natural order- implicit in the act of creation- whereby he promised never again to destroy the earth with a flood. This divine pledge, given unconditionally to Noah and to every living creature on earth, was accompanied by the sign of the rainbow.

### God's relationship with the natural order
**[GEN 8:22](bibleref:01008022)**; **[JER 33:25-33:26](bibleref:24033025-24033026)** *God's relationship with the created order is here expressed as a covenant. It is probable that this is a reference to the Noahic covenant through which the implicit relationship between Creator and creation was confirmed.*  *See also* [GEN 1:14](bibleref:01001014), [GEN 1:31](bibleref:01001031), [GEN 2:1](bibleref:01002001); [PSA 74:16-74:17](bibleref:19074016-19074017); [JER 5:24](bibleref:24005024), [JER 33:20-33:21](bibleref:24033020-24033021)

## An everlasting covenant
**[GEN 9:16](bibleref:01009016)** *See also* [GEN 9:12](bibleref:01009012)**[ISA 24:5](bibleref:23024005)** *The reference here is to the Noahic covenant. Though this covenant was unconditional, the prohibition of bloodshed--often described as bringing pollution to the land--was associated with it. There may be, also, a wider reference to the effects of mankind's disregard for the norms laid down at creation.*  *See also* [GEN 9:6](bibleref:01009006); [NUM 35:33](bibleref:04035033); [ISA 26:21](bibleref:23026021)
