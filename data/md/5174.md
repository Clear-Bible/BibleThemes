# 5174: prostration

The act of falling down upon one's face in front of someone, as a gesture of fear or reverence before God or a person of high rank.



## Prostration before God


### In reverence or fear
**NUM 20:6**; **1CH 29:20**; **REV 7:11** *See also* GEN 17:3; LEV 9:24; NUM 24:4; 1SA 5:3; 1KI 18:39; NEH 8:6; EZK 1:28; MAT 2:11, MAT 17:6, MAT 26:39; LUK 5:12

### As an expression of grieving over sin
 DEU 9:18, DEU 9:25; ; JOS 7:6; ; ISA 15:3; ; EZK 9:8

## Prostration before God's messenger
**NUM 22:31** *See also* JOS 5:14; JDG 13:20; 1CH 21:16; DAN 8:17; LUK 24:5

## Prostration before other people
**1SA 24:8**; **ISA 51:23** *See also* RUT 2:10; 1SA 25:23, 1SA 28:14; 2SA 14:4, 2SA 19:18; ISA 49:23; DAN 2:46
