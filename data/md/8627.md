# 8627: worship, elements of

Praise and thankfulness are important elements of worship, which also includes confession of sin, the reading of Scripture and music.



## Worship with awe
**DEU 10:12** *See also* LEV 10:1-10:3; 2CH 7:3; PSA 2:11, PSA 68:35, PSA 96:9; ECC 5:1

## Worship includes trust
**PSA 4:5** *See also* PSA 37:7; HEB 11:6

## Worship includes praise
**PSA 22:22, PSA 107:32**; **HEB 13:15** *See also* 2CH 31:2; NEH 9:5-9:6; PSA 150:1-150:6; HEB 2:12; REV 7:11-7:12

## Worship includes thanksgiving
**PSA 100:4**; **REV 11:16-11:17** *See also* 2CH 7:3; PSA 50:14, PSA 50:23; EPH 5:19-5:20; PHP 4:6; REV 7:11-7:12

## Worship with joy
**PSA 95:1** *See also* PSA 27:6, PSA 43:4, PSA 100:2; LUK 24:52-24:53; ACT 2:46-2:47

## Worship includes the confession of Jesus Christ as Lord
**HEB 13:15**

## Worship includes confession of sin
