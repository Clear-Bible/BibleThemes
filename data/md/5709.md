# 5709: marriage, purpose of

Marriage is part of God's intention for humanity from creation and forms the basis for the family which is the primary unit of society. Where marriage flourishes it blesses both the couple and the wider community.



## Marriage is part of God's plan for the human race


### From creation
**MAT 19:4** parallel MRK 10:6 *See also* GEN 1:27; 1CO 11:11-11:12 *Man and woman are not independent of each other.*

### It is to provide companionship
**GEN 2:18** *See also* GEN 2:20-2:22, GEN 3:12; PRO 31:10-31:12

### It is to be a committed, exclusive relationship
**GEN 2:23-2:24** *See also* MAT 19:5 parallel MRK 10:7-10:8; 1CO 7:2; EPH 5:31

### It is a lifelong partnership
**MAT 19:6** parallel MRK 10:9 *See also* ROM 7:2; 1CO 7:39

### It is the intended context for raising children
**MAL 2:15** *See also* 1CO 7:14

### It will not exist in the life to come
 MAT 22:30 parallels MRK 12:25, LUK 20:34-20:35; ; 1CO 7:29-7:31

## Marriage as a covenant relationship
**MAL 2:14** *See also* PRO 2:17; EZK 16:8

## Sex and marriage


### Sex belongs within marriage
**1CO 7:9** *See also* GEN 29:21EXO 22:16-22:17; DEU 22:28-22:29
DEU 22:13-22:21 *A woman was expected to be a virgin when she married.*

### The sexual relationship is exclusive
**SNG 2:16** *See also* DEU 22:22-22:24 *Marital unfaithfulness is condemned*; PRO 5:15-5:19; 1CO 6:16, 1CO 7:3-7:5

## Love and submission in marriage


### Falling in love prior to marriage
**GEN 29:20** *See also* GEN 34:3-34:4; 1SA 18:20

### Husbands are to love their wives
**EPH 5:25** *See also* GEN 24:67; 1SA 1:5; ECC 9:9; HOS 3:1; EPH 5:28-5:29, EPH 5:33; COL 3:19

### Wives are to submit to their husbands
**GEN 3:16**; **EPH 5:22-5:24** *See also* COL 3:18; TIT 2:4-2:5; 1PE 3:1-3:6
