## Righteousness and faith in Jesus Christ


### It is God-given and not the result of human effort
**[ROM 1:17](bibleref:45001017)**; **[PHP 3:8-3:9](bibleref:50003008-50003009)** *Paul is contrasting this righteousness with his own previous efforts.*  *See also* [ACT 13:39](bibleref:44013039); [ROM 3:21](bibleref:45003021), [ROM 3:27-3:28](bibleref:45003027-45003028) *It is apparent that faith is not regarded as another kind of “work” which earns salvation* ; [ROM 4:1-4:8](bibleref:45004001-45004008), [ROM 5:17](bibleref:45005017), [ROM 9:30-9:31](bibleref:45009030-45009031); [GAL 3:11-3:12](bibleref:48003011-48003012)

### Faith is centred on Jesus Christ and what he has accomplished
**[ROM 5:1-5:2](bibleref:45005001-45005002)** *“justified” means “declared righteous” in a legal sense.*  *See also* [ROM 3:21-3:26](bibleref:45003021-45003026), [ROM 4:18-4:25](bibleref:45004018-45004025), [ROM 10:6-10:10](bibleref:45010006-45010010); [GAL 3:6-3:9](bibleref:48003006-48003009)
