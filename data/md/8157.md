# 8157: righteousness, as faith

Full faith and trust make a person pleasing in the sight of God.



## Human righteousness compared with God's righteousness


### Human beings cannot by themselves achieve righteousness in the sight of God
**ECC 7:20**; **ISA 64:6**; **MAT 5:20** *See also* PRO 21:2; DAN 9:18; MAT 23:28; LUK 16:15, LUK 18:9; ROM 3:10, ROM 3:20; PHP 3:6-3:7

## True righteousness is the result of the action of God
**ROM 8:3-8:4**; **EPH 4:24**; **1JN 2:29** *See also* ROM 6:13, ROM 6:16-6:20, ROM 8:10, ROM 14:17; GAL 5:5; EPH 5:9; PHP 1:11; HEB 12:11; JAS 3:18; 1PE 2:24; 1JN 3:10

## Faith pleases God
**GEN 15:6**; **HEB 11:6** *See also* 1SA 26:23; PSA 32:10, PSA 40:4, PSA 84:12, PSA 106:30-106:31; JER 17:7; HAB 2:4; HEB 10:38, HEB 11:4, HEB 11:7

## Righteousness and faith in Jesus Christ


### It is God-given and not the result of human effort
**ROM 1:17**; **PHP 3:8-3:9** *Paul is contrasting this righteousness with his own previous efforts.* *See also* ACT 13:39; ROM 3:21, ROM 3:27-3:28 *It is apparent that faith is not regarded as another kind of “work” which earns salvation*; ROM 4:1-4:8, ROM 5:17, ROM 9:30-9:31; GAL 3:11-3:12

### Faith is centred on Jesus Christ and what he has accomplished
**ROM 5:1-5:2** *“justified” means “declared righteous” in a legal sense.* *See also* ROM 3:21-3:26, ROM 4:18-4:25, ROM 10:6-10:10; GAL 3:6-3:9

### Saving faith is not mere belief, but acting on the basis of that belief
 JAS 2:21-2:24
