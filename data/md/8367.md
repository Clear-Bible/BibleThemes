# 8367: wisdom, importance of human

True wisdom may bring material reward but its value far exceeds earthly riches. To those who receive it, it opens a path to life and security and equips for leadership and right conduct.



## The supreme value of true wisdom


### It is priceless
**PRO 4:7** *See also* PRO 3:13, PRO 8:10-8:11

### It leads to life
**PRO 15:24** *See also* PRO 11:30, PRO 13:14, PRO 16:22, PRO 24:14; DAN 12:3

### It brings prosperity
**PRO 8:18** *The benefits of wisdom may include material prosperity, but more important is the moral and spiritual prosperity which it brings.* *See also* PRO 3:1-3:2, PRO 19:8, PRO 19:8, PRO 21:20-21:21, PRO 24:3; JER 10:21

### It gives security
**PRO 4:6** *See also* PRO 1:33, PRO 2:6-2:11, PRO 14:3, PRO 28:26; ECC 8:5

## The application of wisdom


### It touches the whole person
**1KI 3:12** *In Hebrew, the “heart” represents the centre of human personality.* *See also* JOB 38:36; PSA 51:6; PRO 2:2, PRO 16:23, PRO 22:17

### It results in right action
**COL 4:5**; **JAS 3:13** *See also* PSA 119:34; PRO 1:3, PRO 4:11, PRO 15:21, PRO 23:19; JER 4:22; HOS 14:9; ROM 16:19; EPH 5:15; COL 1:9-1:10; JAS 3:14-3:17

### It results in watchfulness
**MAT 25:1-25:10** *See also* PRO 14:8, PRO 22:3 parallel PRO 27:12

## Wisdom is necessary for leaders


### Wisdom to govern
**PRO 8:15-8:16**; **ACT 6:3** *See also* DEU 1:13; 1KI 5:7; 1CH 22:12; PSA 2:10, PSA 105:22; PRO 28:2; ECC 1:16; ISA 56:11; JER 3:15

### Wisdom to administer justice
**1KI 3:28** *See also* 1KI 3:9 parallel 2CH 1:10; PSA 37:30; PRO 20:26, PRO 24:23; MAT 24:45 parallel LUK 12:42; 1CO 6:5

## The teaching of wisdom


### The wise give instruction
**ECC 12:9** *See also* PSA 37:30, PSA 49:3; PRO 1:20-1:21, PRO 5:1, PRO 8:1, PRO 16:21, PRO 31:26; DAN 11:33; 1CO 2:6-2:7; COL 1:28, COL 3:16

### The wise listen to instruction
**PRO 1:5, PRO 15:31** *See also* PRO 4:1, PRO 9:9, PRO 10:8, PRO 13:20, PRO 15:12, PRO 19:20
