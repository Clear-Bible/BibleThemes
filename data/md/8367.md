# 8367: wisdom, importance of human

True wisdom may bring material reward but its value far exceeds earthly riches. To those who receive it, it opens a path to life and security and equips for leadership and right conduct.



## The supreme value of true wisdom


### It is priceless
**[PRO 4:7](bibleref:20004007)** *See also* [PRO 3:13](bibleref:20003013), [PRO 8:10-8:11](bibleref:20008010-20008011)

### It leads to life
**[PRO 15:24](bibleref:20015024)** *See also* [PRO 11:30](bibleref:20011030), [PRO 13:14](bibleref:20013014), [PRO 16:22](bibleref:20016022), [PRO 24:14](bibleref:20024014); [DAN 12:3](bibleref:27012003)

### It brings prosperity
**[PRO 8:18](bibleref:20008018)** *The benefits of wisdom may include material prosperity, but more important is the moral and spiritual prosperity which it brings.*  *See also* [PRO 3:1-3:2](bibleref:20003001-20003002), [PRO 19:8](bibleref:20019008), [PRO 19:8](bibleref:20019008), [PRO 21:20-21:21](bibleref:20021020-20021021), [PRO 24:3](bibleref:20024003); [JER 10:21](bibleref:24010021)

### It gives security
**[PRO 4:6](bibleref:20004006)** *See also* [PRO 1:33](bibleref:20001033), [PRO 2:6-2:11](bibleref:20002006-20002011), [PRO 14:3](bibleref:20014003), [PRO 28:26](bibleref:20028026); [ECC 8:5](bibleref:21008005)
