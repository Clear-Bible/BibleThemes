# 8281: insight

The spiritual quality that enables a person to appreciate God's mind and will in matters of behaviour, truth and providence, especially where right perception is not obvious. Insight is akin to wisdom and understanding. It is given by God and is to be sought by believers.



## Insight is given by God
**2TI 2:7** *See also* PRO 2:6

### Insight through angelic instruction
 JOB 33:23; ; DAN 9:20-9:22

### Insight through Jesus Christ's teaching
**LUK 24:45-24:47** *See also* LUK 24:27; MAT 16:12

### Insight through the Holy Spirit
**1CO 2:9-2:13** *See also* ISA 64:4; EPH 3:4-3:5

### Human insight cannot comprehend spiritual truth
**1CO 2:14** *See also* JOB 9:11; 1CO 1:20-1:21

## Insight into spiritual truth


### Insight into the truth about Jesus Christ
**COL 2:2-2:3** *See also* JHN 16:12-16:15

### Insight into the believer's hope and power
 EPH 1:18-1:19

### Insight into the make-up of the church
 ACT 10:34-10:35; ; EPH 3:2-3:6

### Insight into the number of the beast
 REV 13:18

## Insight into the providence of God


### Joseph's insight
**GEN 50:20** *See also* GEN 45:4-45:8

### David's insight
 2SA 5:12 parallel 1CH 14:2

### Job's insight
**JOB 42:1-42:6** *Job does not learn why he has suffered, but reaffirms his trust in God's superior wisdom and power.* *See also* JOB 15:9 *Human insight into Job's suffering did not bring enlightenment*; JOB 26:3, JOB 34:35

## Insight into correct behaviour
**PHP 1:9-1:11** *See also* PRO 28:7; COL 1:9-1:10; PSA 19:12 *discerning one's own faults*; PSA 119:34 *understanding God's law*; PRO 5:1-5:4 *avoiding evil*

## Insight into the truth about people
 2KI 4:8-4:9 *The Shunammite woman recognises Elisha as a man of God*; ; NEH 6:1-6:2 *Nehemiah discerns the motives of his enemies*; ; MAT 9:3-9:4 parallels MRK 2:6-2:8, LUK 5:21-5:22 *Jesus Christ is aware of the evil thoughts of the Pharisees and the teachers of the law*; ; JHN 4:19 *The woman of Samaria understands some of the truth about Jesus Christ.*

## Insight is to be sought
**PRO 2:3-2:5** *See also* PRO 3:21, PRO 18:15

### Asking God for insight
**JAS 1:5** *See also* 1KI 3:9-3:12 parallel 2CH 1:10-1:12

### Insight through meditation on God's word
**PSA 119:98-119:100** *See also* PSA 119:104; DAN 9:2; MAT 22:29 parallel MRK 12:24

## Examples of people of insight
 1KI 4:29-4:34 *Solomon*; ; 1CH 27:32 *Jonathan, David's uncle*; ; DAN 5:11 *Daniel*

## Examples of lack of insight
 MAT 16:9-16:11 parallel MRK 8:17-8:21 *Jesus Christ's disciples*; ; MAT 16:22 parallel MRK 8:32 *Simon Peter*; ; LUK 24:25 *Cleopas and his companion*; ; JHN 3:10 *Nicodemus*; ; JHN 20:2 *Mary Magdalene*
