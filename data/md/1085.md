# 1085: God, love of

The deepest possible expression of God's character. Though God loves all people, he is especially committed to sacrificial, loyal relationships with his people.



## God's nature is love
**[1JN 4:8](bibleref:62004008)** *See also* [1JN 4:16](bibleref:62004016)

### His love is perfectly expressed within the Trinity
**[MRK 1:10-1:11](bibleref:41001010-41001011)** parallels MAT 3:16-3:17, LUK 3:21-3:22 *See also* [JHN 5:20](bibleref:43005020), [JHN 10:17](bibleref:43010017), [JHN 14:23](bibleref:43014023); [EPH 1:6](bibleref:49001006); [COL 1:13](bibleref:51001013)
