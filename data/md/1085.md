# 1085: God, love of

The deepest possible expression of God's character. Though God loves all people, he is especially committed to sacrificial, loyal relationships with his people.



## God's nature is love
**1JN 4:8** *See also* 1JN 4:16

### His love is perfectly expressed within the Trinity
**MRK 1:10-1:11** parallels MAT 3:16-3:17, LUK 3:21-3:22 *See also* JHN 5:20, JHN 10:17, JHN 14:23; EPH 1:6; COL 1:13

## Characteristics of God's love


### It is eternal
**JER 31:3** *See also* PSA 103:17, PSA 136:1-136:26; ISA 49:15-49:16, ISA 54:8, ISA 54:10

### It is a covenant love
**DEU 7:9** *See also* EXO 20:6 parallel DEU 5:10; DEU 7:12; 1KI 8:23 parallel 2CH 6:14; PSA 106:45; DAN 9:4

### It is lavish
**EXO 34:6-34:7** *See also* NEH 9:17; PSA 103:8; JOL 2:13; JON 4:2; 1JN 3:1

### It is holy and just
**PSA 33:5** *See also* PSA 37:28, PSA 99:4; ISA 61:8

God as a father:
 DEU 1:31; ; HOS 11:1-11:4; ; LUK 15:11-15:32; ; HEB 12:6; ; PRO 3:12; JER 31:32; ; HOS 2:14-2:20; ; REV 21:2

## God's loving actions


### The gift of God's Son is a unique act of love
**1JN 4:9-4:10** *See also* JHN 3:16, JHN 15:13; ROM 5:7-5:8

### God sets his love on the unlovely
**DEU 7:7-7:8** *See also* EZK 16:1-16:14; ROM 5:8; EPH 2:4-2:5

### God always acts in love towards believers
**ROM 8:38-8:39** *See also* 2CO 13:14; 2JN 1:3
