# 1444: revelation, in NT

The NT fulfils and completes the revelation of God which began in the OT. Jesus Christ is the central focus of this self-revelation of God.



## The unity and progress of revelation


### The unity of OT and NT
**MAT 5:17-5:18** *See also* ROM 3:21-3:22; 2TI 3:14-3:15; 2PE 3:15-3:16 *The writings of Paul are presented as having equal status with the OT Scriptures*; REV 22:18-22:19 *These sanctions have the same force as those of Dt 4:2; 12:32.*

### The progress of NT revelation
**HEB 1:1-1:2** *See also* HEB 2:1-2:4, HEB 12:22-12:27

## The NT fulfils and completes God's revelation of himself


### Jesus Christ is the supreme revelation of God
**COL 1:25-1:27** *See also* JHN 1:9-1:18, JHN 14:6; ACT 4:12; GAL 4:4; PHP 2:6-2:8; HEB 2:14

### Jesus Christ is the image of God
**2CO 4:4**; **COL 1:15**

### Jesus Christ has the nature of God
**PHP 2:6**

### Jesus Christ is the exact representation of God
**HEB 1:3**

### Jesus Christ is the incarnate Word of God
**JHN 1:14**

## The role of the Holy Spirit in revelation


### He is the divine agent of revelation
**JHN 16:12-16:15** *See also* JHN 14:16-14:17, JHN 15:26; 1JN 4:6, 1JN 5:6; REV 2:7, REV 2:11, REV 2:17, REV 2:29, REV 3:6, REV 3:13, REV 3:22

### He is the source of revelatory manifestations
**1CO 12:7-12:11** *See also* ACT 2:1-2:12; ROM 12:6; 1CO 12:28-12:30, 1CO 13:8-13:12, 1CO 14:1-14:33; EPH 4:11

## God's purposes in revelation


### To reveal himself in Jesus Christ
**COL 1:15-1:20** *See also* JHN 1:14, JHN 12:44-12:45, JHN 14:9; 2CO 4:4; HEB 1:3

### To reveal his plan through Jesus Christ
**EPH 1:9-1:10** *See also* ROM 16:25-16:27; 1CO 2:7-2:10; EPH 3:3-3:11; COL 1:19-1:20
