# 5257: civil authorities

Divinely appointed servants and agents of God exercising their God-given authority for the good of those they govern and punishing those who do wrong, and to whom all must submit.



## Civil authorities are divinely appointed
**JHN 19:11**; **ROM 13:1** *See also* 1SA 9:17, 1SA 16:1; PSA 78:70-78:71; PRO 8:15-8:16; JER 27:6, JER 43:10; DAN 2:21, DAN 2:37-2:38, DAN 4:17, DAN 4:32, DAN 5:18-5:21, DAN 5:26-5:28

## Civil authorities are God's servants and agents
**ROM 13:4** *See also* ISA 45:1; JER 25:9, JER 27:6, JER 43:10; ROM 13:6

## Civil authority exercised for the good of all and in order to punish wrongdoers
**PRO 21:15**; **1PE 2:13-2:14** *See also* GEN 9:6; EZR 7:26; PSA 72:12-72:14, PSA 78:72; PRO 19:12, PRO 29:4, PRO 29:14; ACT 25:11; ROM 13:3-13:4; 1TI 2:2

## Everyone must submit to civil authorities
**PRO 24:21-24:22**; **TIT 3:1** *See also* ECC 8:2-8:5; MAT 17:24-17:27, MAT 22:15-22:21 parallels MRK 12:13-12:17, LUK 20:20-20:25; ROM 13:1, ROM 13:5-13:7; 1PE 2:13-2:14, 1PE 2:17

## Civil authorities are only to be obeyed in what is lawful according to Scripture
**ACT 5:29** *See also* EXO 1:17; 1KI 21:2-21:3; DAN 1:8, DAN 3:28, DAN 6:7-6:10; MAT 22:21 parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:25; ACT 4:19; HEB 11:23
