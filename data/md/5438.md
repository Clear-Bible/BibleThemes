# 5438: parables

Stories told to convey a general truth or spiritual message, often requiring explanation or interpretation. They were used by the prophets and featured extensively in Jesus Christ's ministry.



## Parables in the OT


### Told by prophets
**HOS 12:10** *See also* 2SA 12:1-12:10; 1KI 20:35-20:42; EZK 17:1-17:18, EZK 20:49, EZK 24:3-24:13

### Told by others
**PSA 78:1-78:2** *See also* JDG 9:7-9:20; 2SA 14:4-14:14; 2KI 14:9-14:10 parallel 2CH 25:18-25:19; PRO 1:5-1:6

## Parables in the ministry of Jesus Christ


### Reasons for Jesus Christ's use of parables
**MAT 13:10-13:15** parallels MRK 4:10-4:12, LUK 8:9-8:10 *See also* ISA 6:9-6:10
