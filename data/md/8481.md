# 8481: self-sacrifice

The willingness to leave one's possessions or to give them to others; to deny oneself to the point of death or to give oneself in the service of God or other people.

## Self-sacrifice as forfeiting one's present comfort for the sake of others
**[RUT 2:11](bibleref:08002011)** *See also* [GEN 44:33-44:34](bibleref:01044033-01044034); [HEB 10:34](bibleref:58010034), [HEB 11:24-11:27](bibleref:58011024-58011027)

## Self-sacrifice as giving one's possessions to others


### Jesus Christ commends the willingness to give away one's possessions
**[MAT 19:21](bibleref:40019021)** parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22 *See also* [LUK 12:33](bibleref:42012033)

### God rewards those who give their possessions away
**[LUK 18:28-18:30](bibleref:42018028-42018030)** parallels MAT 19:27-19:29, MRK 10:28-10:30

### Sacrificing one's possessions as demonstrated in the life of the church
 *See also* [ACT 2:44-2:45](bibleref:44002044-44002045), [ACT 4:32-4:37](bibleref:44004032-44004037); [2CO 8:1-8:5](bibleref:47008001-47008005)

## Self-sacrifice as leaving all and following Jesus Christ


### Such sacrifice is a condition of discipleship
**[LUK 14:33](bibleref:42014033)** *See also* [MAT 10:37](bibleref:40010037) parallel LUK 14:26 *This means loving Jesus Christ over and above one's immediate family.* 

### Those willing to leave everything to follow Jesus Christ
 [LUK 5:27-5:28](bibleref:42005027-42005028); [PHP 3:8](bibleref:50003008)

## Self-sacrifice as the metaphorical laying down of one's life for others


### Followers of Jesus Christ are required to give themselves in the service of others
**[JHN 12:24-12:25](bibleref:43012024-43012025)** *See also* [MAT 10:38](bibleref:40010038) parallel LUK 14:27; [MAT 16:24-16:25](bibleref:40016024-40016025) parallels MRK 8:34-8:35, LUK 9:23-9:24; [LUK 17:33](bibleref:42017033); [1JN 3:16-3:18](bibleref:62003016-62003018)

### Examples of those who demonstrated such self-sacrifice
**[2CO 4:10-4:11](bibleref:47004010-47004011)** *See also* [1CO 9:19-9:23](bibleref:46009019-46009023), [1CO 15:30-15:31](bibleref:46015030-46015031); [2CO 11:23-11:29](bibleref:47011023-47011029), [2CO 12:15](bibleref:47012015)

## Self-sacrifice as the literal laying down of one's life


### Jesus Christ warns his disciples to be prepared to give their lives
**[MAT 10:21-10:22](bibleref:40010021-40010022)** parallels MRK 13:12-13:13, LUK 21:16-21:17

### Sacrificing one's life is of no value without love
 *See also* [1CO 13:3](bibleref:46013003)

### Examples of those willing to give their lives for the sake of God and others
**[ACT 20:23-20:24](bibleref:44020023-44020024)** *See also* [EXO 32:32](bibleref:02032032); [1CH 11:15-11:19](bibleref:13011015-13011019); [EST 4:14-4:16](bibleref:17004014-17004016); [MAT 26:39](bibleref:40026039) parallels MRK 14:35-14:36, LUK 22:42; [PHP 2:17](bibleref:50002017)

### Examples of those who gave their lives in God's service
**[ACT 7:59-7:60](bibleref:44007059-44007060)** *See also* [JDG 11:36-11:38](bibleref:07011036-07011038); [PHP 2:5-2:8](bibleref:50002005-50002008); [HEB 11:36-11:38](bibleref:58011036-58011038); [REV 20:4](bibleref:66020004)
