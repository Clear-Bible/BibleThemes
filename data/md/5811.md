# 5811: compromise

Usually a combination of contrasting values, practices or persons incurring God's disapproval, but it can also be a positive way to achieve a greater good.



## God forbids compromising associations


### Israel was not to associate with paganism
**EXO 20:3** parallel DEU 5:7 *See also* EXO 23:32-23:33, EXO 34:12-34:16; DEU 7:2-7:4; 1KI 18:21

### Christians must not compromise their allegiance
**JAS 4:4** *See also* MAT 6:24 parallel LUK 16:13; ROM 13:14; 2CO 6:14-6:16; GAL 1:6-1:9; JAS 4:8

## Israel's compromises with paganism


### In worship
**1KI 12:28-12:30** *The LORD's action in bringing Israel up out of Egypt is here attributed to the golden calves representing Baal.* *See also* EXO 32:2-32:6; JDG 2:11-2:12, JDG 8:27; 2KI 1:2-1:3, 2KI 16:10-16:16, 2KI 17:32-17:33, 2KI 17:41; 2CH 28:22; REV 2:20-2:23

### In foreign alliances
**ISA 30:1-30:2** *See also* 2CH 19:2, 2CH 20:35-20:37, 2CH 28:16-28:21; ISA 31:1; HOS 5:13, HOS 7:8-7:9, HOS 7:11

### In marriage
**1KI 11:1-11:11** *See also* 1KI 16:31; 2CH 18:1; EZR 9:2; NEH 13:23, NEH 13:25-13:26

## Motives for compromise


### Fear
**GEN 26:7-26:9** *See also* GEN 12:11-12:13, GEN 12:18-12:19, GEN 19:18-19:20, GEN 20:2, GEN 20:11; GAL 2:12-2:13

### Lack of faith
**GEN 16:1-16:2** *See also* GEN 15:2-15:3; 1KI 12:26-12:33

### Desire for material things
**JOS 7:21** *See also* HAG 1:2-1:4; MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:18-4:19, LUK 8:14; ACT 5:1-5:4; 2TI 4:10

## Examples of those who refused to compromise
**EST 3:4** *See also* GEN 39:7-39:12; 1KI 22:13-22:14; DAN 3:18, DAN 6:7-6:10; 2CO 4:2

## Compromise as a mutually acceptable solution


### For the sake of the gospel
**1CO 9:19-9:23** *See also* ACT 15:19-15:20, ACT 15:28-15:29, ACT 21:20-21:26; ROM 14:19-14:22, ROM 15:1-15:2

### As an act of wisdom
 1KI 3:24-3:25, 1KI 12:6-12:7 parallel 2CH 10:6-10:7; ; JER 40:9-40:10

### As a means to peace
 GEN 13:8-13:9, GEN 26:19-26:22, GEN 37:26-37:27; ; PRO 25:8; ; MAT 5:25 parallel LUK 12:58
