# 5843: embarrassment

A state of discomfort and shame, caused by a sense of guilt over something that has been said or done.

## Embarrassment may be a sign of misunderstanding or mistrust
**[JHN 13:6-13:8](bibleref:43013006-43013008)** *See also* [MRK 2:16-2:17](bibleref:41002016-41002017) *Jesus Christ's enemies approached the disciples knowing they would be embarrassed by the company he was keeping* ; [MRK 3:21](bibleref:41003021) *The family of Jesus Christ were embarrassed by what they were hearing about him.*
