# 8736: evil, warnings against

Scripture repeatedly warns against evil. It exposes it and reveals its consequences.



## God opposes evil
**PSA 34:16** *See also* PRO 6:16-6:19; ISA 31:2; MIC 2:1

## Warnings against specific dangers
**LEV 20:23**; **2CO 6:14** *See also* EZK 20:18; LUK 12:1, LUK 12:15; ACT 13:40; 1CO 10:21; 2PE 3:17

## God's law given to counter evil


### By exposing evil
**ROM 7:7** *See also* ROM 3:20, ROM 5:20, ROM 7:13; GAL 3:19

### By leading believers away from evil
**GAL 3:24** *See also* 1TI 1:9-1:11

## The state is ordained to restrain evil
**ROM 13:1-13:4** *See also* DEU 17:7, DEU 17:12-17:13, DEU 21:21; 1TI 2:1-2:2; 1PE 2:13-2:14

## Evil rebounds on evildoers
**PRO 22:8**; **GAL 6:7-6:8** *See also* DEU 19:18-19:19; EST 9:25; PRO 1:29-1:31, PRO 14:22, PRO 17:13; HOS 8:7

## Evil ends in judgment
**ISA 65:12**; **ROM 2:9** *See also* PSA 37:9; PRO 6:12-6:15, PRO 12:7; MAL 4:1; ROM 2:5, ROM 6:23
