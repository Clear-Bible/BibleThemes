# 5663: childbirth

Seen by Scripture as a token of God's favour, and used figuratively to picture a number of central biblical themes.

## Childbirth in the OT


### Childbirth as a blessing
**[GEN 1:28](bibleref:01001028)** *See also* [DEU 7:14](bibleref:05007014); [PSA 127:3-127:5](bibleref:19127003-19127005)

### Childlessness a cause of shame in Israel
**[GEN 30:1](bibleref:01030001)** *See also* [1SA 1:6](bibleref:09001006), [1SA 1:10-1:11](bibleref:09001010-09001011); [LUK 1:24-1:25](bibleref:42001024-42001025)

## The nature of human childbirth


### The pain of childbirth a consequence of the fall
**[GEN 3:16](bibleref:01003016)**

### The need for ceremonial purification after childbirth
 *See also* [LEV 12:1-12:8](bibleref:03012001-03012008) *Under the old covenant, unsanctioned contact with blood was ceremonially defiling* ; [LUK 2:24](bibleref:42002024)

### The role of midwives in childbirth
 [EXO 1:15-1:21](bibleref:02001015-02001021)

### The joy of childbirth
 [GEN 21:6](bibleref:01021006); [JHN 16:21](bibleref:43016021)

## Childbirth and salvation


### The birth of a Saviour
**[GEN 3:15](bibleref:01003015)** *Although childbirth is a focus of judgment, through it the human race continues, and the Messiah comes into the world.*  *See also* [GEN 12:2-12:3](bibleref:01012002-01012003), [GEN 15:3-15:6](bibleref:01015003-01015006); [ISA 7:14](bibleref:23007014), [ISA 9:6-9:7](bibleref:23009006-23009007); [MAT 1:18-1:25](bibleref:40001018-40001025); [LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035), [LUK 2:4-2:7](bibleref:42002004-42002007); [GAL 3:16](bibleref:48003016), [GAL 4:4](bibleref:48004004); [REV 12:5](bibleref:66012005)

### Salvation in childbearing
**[1TI 2:15](bibleref:54002015)** *A Christian mother fulfils her calling in childbearing; some take it to mean she will be kept safe in childbirth. Some, however, take this verse as a reference to Ge 3:15, and the coming of Jesus Christ, as above.*
