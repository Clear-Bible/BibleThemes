# 5663: childbirth

Seen by Scripture as a token of God's favour, and used figuratively to picture a number of central biblical themes.



## Childbirth in the OT


### Childbirth as a blessing
**GEN 1:28** *See also* DEU 7:14; PSA 127:3-127:5

### Childlessness a cause of shame in Israel
**GEN 30:1** *See also* 1SA 1:6, 1SA 1:10-1:11; LUK 1:24-1:25

## The nature of human childbirth


### The pain of childbirth a consequence of the fall
**GEN 3:16**

### The need for ceremonial purification after childbirth
 *See also* LEV 12:1-12:8 *Under the old covenant, unsanctioned contact with blood was ceremonially defiling*; LUK 2:24

### The role of midwives in childbirth
 EXO 1:15-1:21

### The joy of childbirth
 GEN 21:6; ; JHN 16:21

## Childbirth and salvation


### The birth of a Saviour
**GEN 3:15** *Although childbirth is a focus of judgment, through it the human race continues, and the Messiah comes into the world.* *See also* GEN 12:2-12:3, GEN 15:3-15:6; ISA 7:14, ISA 9:6-9:7; MAT 1:18-1:25; LUK 1:35, LUK 2:4-2:7; GAL 3:16, GAL 4:4; REV 12:5

### Salvation in childbearing
**1TI 2:15** *A Christian mother fulfils her calling in childbearing; some take it to mean she will be kept safe in childbirth. Some, however, take this verse as a reference to Ge 3:15, and the coming of Jesus Christ, as above.*

## Childbirth used figuratively


### To describe weakness and vulnerability
 *See also* EZK 16:4-16:6

### To describe weakness under judgment
**2KI 19:3** parallel ISA 37:3 *See also* PSA 48:6; ISA 23:4, ISA 26:17-26:18; JER 4:31; HOS 2:3, HOS 13:13; MIC 4:10

### To describe troubles prior to God's final triumph
**MAT 24:7-24:8** parallel MRK 13:8 *See also* ROM 8:22

### To describe love and compassion
 ISA 49:15

### To describe regeneration
**JHN 3:6-3:7** *See also* JHN 1:12-1:13; JAS 1:18; 1PE 1:23

### To describe pastoral concern
 GAL 4:19

### To describe the disciples' joy
 JHN 16:21-16:22

### To describe triumph and salvation
**ISA 54:1** *See also* GAL 4:27; ISA 26:19, ISA 66:7-66:11

## God's sovereignty in childbirth


### All children are God's gift
 PSA 127:3

### God controls the events of childbirth
 GEN 25:22-25:23; ; PSA 139:13, PSA 139:16

### Childbirth in old age
 GEN 17:16-17:19; ; LUK 1:5-1:13, LUK 1:18-1:20, LUK 1:23-1:25

### Childbirth as an answer to prayer
 GEN 25:21; ; 1SA 1:20; ; LUK 1:13

### Childbirth for the barren woman
**PSA 113:9** *See also* GEN 25:21; JDG 13:2-13:5; 1SA 2:5; ISA 54:1

### Jesus Christ born of a virgin
**ISA 7:14** *See also* MAT 1:22-1:23, MAT 1:18; LUK 1:30-1:31, LUK 1:34-1:37 *“impossible” births demonstrate God's power and grace and show that salvation comes from the Lord.*
