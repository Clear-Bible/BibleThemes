# 8452: neighbours, duty to

Scripture defines the identity of neighbours in various ways, and indicates the nature and extent of believers' obligations to them.



## Neighbours defined


### Those who live nearby
**PRO 3:29** *See also* EXO 3:22, EXO 12:4; DEU 19:14; 2KI 4:3; PRO 27:10; LUK 15:4-15:6

### Those of the same race
**LEV 19:18** *See also* 1SA 15:27-15:28, 1SA 28:17; 1CH 12:38-12:40

### Peoples of surrounding nations
**DEU 1:7** *See also* JOS 9:16; 1SA 7:14; EZR 9:1; NEH 9:30, NEH 10:28, NEH 10:31; PSA 76:11; EZK 16:26

### Those who show mercy to people in need
**LUK 10:29-10:37**

### Fellow Christians
**EPH 4:25** *See also* ROM 15:1-15:2; GAL 5:13-5:16; JAS 4:11-4:12

## Duties to neighbours


### Love
**MAT 22:34-22:39** parallels MRK 12:28-12:34, LUK 10:25-10:28 *See also* LEV 19:18; DEU 6:5; MAT 19:19; ROM 13:8-13:10; GAL 5:14; JAS 2:8-2:9

### Assistance
**EXO 23:4-23:5** *See also* DEU 22:1-22:4; PRO 14:21; MAT 25:34-25:40

### Fair treatment
**EXO 22:14** *See also* EXO 20:17; LEV 6:2-6:5, LEV 19:15; DEU 5:21

### Honest dealings
**LEV 19:13** *See also* ZEC 8:16-8:17; EPH 4:25

### Forgiveness
**EPH 4:32** *See also* MAT 18:21-18:35; LUK 17:3-17:4; ROM 15:1-15:2; COL 3:13

### Reproof
**LEV 19:17** *See also* LUK 17:3; GAL 6:1-6:2

## Prohibition of negative actions towards neighbours


### False witness
**EXO 20:16** parallel DEU 5:20 *See also* LEV 19:16; PRO 24:28

### Oppression
**JER 22:13** *See also* PRO 3:28

### Judgment
**JAS 4:12** *See also* MAT 7:1-7:2; LUK 6:41-6:42; ROM 14:13
