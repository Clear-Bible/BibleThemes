# 5734: relationships

God created people to be in relationship with himself and with one another in friendship, marriage, family, society and the church.



## Human beings are created to be in relationship


### With God
**REV 21:3** *See also* ISA 43:6-43:7; HOS 11:1; 2CO 6:18

### With one another
**GEN 2:18** *See also* PSA 127:4; PRO 17:6, PRO 17:17, PRO 18:24, PRO 27:10; ECC 4:9-4:12

## Relationship with God


### Is entered by faith
**JHN 1:12-1:13** *See also* MAT 12:50 parallels MRK 3:35, LUK 8:21; JHN 17:3; GAL 3:26

### Is expressed through covenant
**EXO 6:7** *This is often known as the covenant formula.* *See also* GEN 17:7; 2SA 7:14-7:15 parallel 1CH 17:13-17:14; LUK 22:20 parallels MAT 26:28, MRK 14:24; 2CO 6:16; LEV 26:12; HEB 10:15-10:18; JER 31:33-31:34

## Relationships between believers


### Believers are to be in relationship with one another in the church
**GAL 3:28** *See also* EPH 2:14-2:16, EPH 2:19

### Believers are to maintain good relationships
**EPH 4:3** *See also* ROM 12:18, ROM 14:19; HEB 12:14

### Believers are to restore broken relationships
**MAT 18:15** *See also* MAT 5:23-5:24; EPH 4:26
