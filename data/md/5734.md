# 5734: relationships

God created people to be in relationship with himself and with one another in friendship, marriage, family, society and the church.

## Human beings are created to be in relationship


### With God
**[REV 21:3](bibleref:66021003)** *See also* [ISA 43:6-43:7](bibleref:23043006-23043007); [HOS 11:1](bibleref:28011001); [2CO 6:18](bibleref:47006018)

### With one another
**[GEN 2:18](bibleref:01002018)** *See also* [PSA 127:4](bibleref:19127004); [PRO 17:6](bibleref:20017006), [PRO 17:17](bibleref:20017017), [PRO 18:24](bibleref:20018024), [PRO 27:10](bibleref:20027010); [ECC 4:9-4:12](bibleref:21004009-21004012)

### Is entered by faith

**[EXO 6:7](bibleref:02006007)** *This is often known as the covenant formula.*  *See also* [GEN 17:7](bibleref:01017007); [2SA 7:14-7:15](bibleref:10007014-10007015) parallel 1CH 17:13-17:14; [LUK 22:20](bibleref:42022020) parallels MAT 26:28, MRK 14:24; [2CO 6:16](bibleref:47006016); [LEV 26:12](bibleref:03026012); [HEB 10:15-10:18](bibleref:58010015-58010018); [JER 31:33-31:34](bibleref:24031033-24031034)
