# 5312: feasting

Taking part in festive meals that were often a feature of Israel's celebrations of religious festivals.



## Feasting on special occasions


### To mark a covenant
**GEN 31:54** *The sacrifice and the meal were important elements in the covenant-making process.* *See also* GEN 26:28-26:31; EXO 24:11; 1CO 11:25-11:26

### Weddings
**JDG 14:10** *See also* GEN 29:22; EST 2:18; MAT 22:2; JHN 2:1-2:10; REV 19:9

### Family celebrations
**GEN 21:8** *See also* LUK 15:23-15:24

### National celebrations
**1KI 1:25** *See also* EST 9:17

## Israel's religious feasts

**EXO 34:22** *Pentecost was also known in the OT as “the Feast of Weeks” or the “Feast of Harvest”.* *See also* EXO 23:16; NUM 28:26

### The Feast of Tabernacles, to celebrate the autumn harvest
**EXO 23:16** *See also* LEV 23:34; DEU 16:13; EZR 3:4; NEH 8:14; ZEC 14:16

**EST 9:17** *See also* EST 9:22, EST 9:26
