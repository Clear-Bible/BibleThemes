# 1615: Scripture, sufficiency of

Scripture is presented as being of itself sufficient for faith and for life.



## God's written revelation is sufficient
**DEU 4:1-4:2** *See also* DEU 12:32; JOS 1:7-1:8; PRO 30:5-30:6; JER 26:2

## The NT records the completion and fulfilment of the OT
**HEB 1:1-1:2** *See also* JHN 12:47-12:50; EPH 2:20

### Warnings against turning from, or adding to, the apostolic gospel, as set forth in Scripture
**REV 22:18-22:19** *See also* GAL 1:6-1:9; COL 1:25-2:8, COL 2:18, COL 2:20-2:23; 2TH 2:1-2:2

## Descriptions of Scripture point to its sufficiency


### Scripture is good
**ROM 7:12** *See also* 1TI 1:8

### Scripture is perfect
**PSA 19:7** *See also* PSA 119:142; JAS 1:25

### Scripture is eternal
**PSA 119:89** *See also* MAT 5:18, MAT 24:35; 1PE 1:24-1:25; ISA 40:6-40:8
