# 8422: equipping, spiritual

Scripture provides examples of individuals who have been equipped to carry out tasks or responsibilities of a spiritual nature.



## Jesus Christ was equipped for his work
**ISA 61:1-61:2** *See also* PSA 72:1; ISA 50:4-50:7; MAT 12:18; ISA 42:1; LUK 4:18-4:19, LUK 19:31; JHN 3:34; ACT 10:38; HEB 10:5

## God's people are equipped for service


### In the OT
**EXO 4:10-4:12** EXO 31:1-31:6, EXO 35:30-36:2; 
LEV 8:7-8:9; ; JDG 3:10; JDG 6:14, JDG 6:34; 
JDG 11:29, JDG 14:19 *Samson*; ; 1SA 10:10; 1KI 3:7-3:9; ; 2CH 2:12; 
2KI 2:9 *Elisha*; ; NEH 13:9; ; ISA 6:6-6:7; ; JER 1:9; ; EZK 1:1, EZK 3:23

### In the NT
**2TI 2:20-2:21** *See also* MAT 4:19; LUK 1:15 *John the Baptist*; LUK 1:41, LUK 1:67 *Zechariah*; LUK 12:33; ACT 4:8 *Peter*; ACT 6:3, ACT 9:17 *Ananias*; ACT 11:24 *Barnabas*; ROM 13:12; EPH 4:12, EPH 6:11-6:18; PHP 4:19; 2TI 3:16-3:17; HEB 13:20-13:21; 1PE 3:15

## Believers are equipped for spiritual tasks


### By the call of Jesus Christ
**MAT 4:19** parallel MRK 1:17 *See also* MRK 10:24-10:31; LUK 9:1-9:2, LUK 10:1-10:3; JHN 1:35-1:42

### By spiritual gifts
**1CO 12:7** *See also* ACT 6:3-6:6; ROM 12:6-12:8; 1CO 12:8-12:11, 1CO 12:27-12:31; EPH 4:7, EPH 4:11-4:12

### By the word of God
**2TI 2:15** *See also* COL 3:16; 2TI 3:16-3:17; 2PE 1:19; 1JN 2:14

### By the empowering of the Holy Spirit
 ACT 2:4, ACT 4:31, ACT 13:9; ; EPH 5:18
