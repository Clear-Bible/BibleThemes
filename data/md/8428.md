# 8428: example

A person who acts as an illustration or model. Scripture provides examples of both good and bad conduct for the guidance of believers.

## Scripture provides examples to inform and warn believers


### Israel is an example to believers
**[1CO 10:6](bibleref:46010006)** *See also* [1CO 10:11](bibleref:46010011)

### Job as an example of perseverance and patience
**[JAS 5:10-5:11](bibleref:59005010-59005011)**

### The example of Jesus Christ is of special importance to Christians
**[JHN 13:15](bibleref:43013015)**; **[1CO 11:1](bibleref:46011001)** *See also* [MAT 11:29](bibleref:40011029), [MAT 16:24](bibleref:40016024); [PHP 2:5-2:11](bibleref:50002005-50002011); [1TI 1:16](bibleref:54001016); [HEB 3:1-3:2](bibleref:58003001-58003002), [HEB 12:2](bibleref:58012002); [1PE 2:21](bibleref:60002021)

### The apostles set an example for believers to follow
**[PHP 3:17](bibleref:50003017)** *See also* [2TH 3:7](bibleref:53003007)

### Christian leaders are to provide examples to their people
**[1PE 5:3](bibleref:60005003)**; **[TIT 2:7](bibleref:56002007)** *See also* [1TI 4:12](bibleref:54004012)

### Examples drawn from everyday life
 [GAL 3:15](bibleref:48003015); [JAS 3:4](bibleref:59003004)

### Faith
 [MAT 15:22](bibleref:40015022) *a Canaanite woman* ; [JHN 4:4-4:29](bibleref:43004004-43004029) *a Samaritan woman* ; [JHN 20:24-20:29](bibleref:43020024-43020029) *doubting Thomas* ; [ROM 4:18-4:21](bibleref:45004018-45004021) *Abraham* ; [HEB 11:4-11:38](bibleref:58011004-58011038) *various OT figures* 

### Forgiving one's enemies and assailants
 [GEN 33:1-33:11](bibleref:01033001-01033011) *Esau* ; [GEN 45:5-45:15](bibleref:01045005-01045015) *Joseph* ; [1SA 24:8-24:22](bibleref:09024008-09024022) *David* ; [LUK 23:34](bibleref:42023034) *Jesus Christ* ; [ACT 7:59](bibleref:44007059) *Stephen* 

### Generous giving
 [RUT 2:15-2:19](bibleref:08002015-08002019) *Boaz* ; [JOB 29:11-29:16](bibleref:18029011-18029016) *Job* ; [MRK 12:41-12:44](bibleref:41012041-41012044) *a widow* ; [ACT 10:2](bibleref:44010002) *Cornelius*
