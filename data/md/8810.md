# 8810: riches, dangers of

Material wealth can be a source of both physical and spiritual danger, against which believers are clearly warned to be on their guard.



## Riches are spiritually dangerous


### Love of riches a root of many evils
**[1TI 6:10](bibleref:54006010)** *See also* [JOS 7:11](bibleref:06007011), [JOS 7:20-7:21](bibleref:06007020-06007021); [1TI 3:3](bibleref:54003003)

### They may lead to divided loyalty
**[MAT 6:24](bibleref:40006024)** parallel LUK 16:13 *See also* [MAT 4:9-4:10](bibleref:40004009-40004010); [JAS 4:4](bibleref:59004004)

### They may hinder people's response to the gospel
 [MAT 13:22](bibleref:40013022) parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14; [MAT 19:21-19:22](bibleref:40019021-40019022) parallels MRK 10:21-10:22, LUK 18:22-18:23

### They may create false security
**[1TI 6:17](bibleref:54006017)** *See also* [JOB 31:24-31:28](bibleref:18031024-18031028); [PSA 52:6-52:7](bibleref:19052006-19052007); [PRO 11:28](bibleref:20011028); [JER 49:4](bibleref:24049004)

### They may make people proud
**[EZK 28:5](bibleref:26028005)** *See also* [PSA 62:10](bibleref:19062010); [PRO 18:23](bibleref:20018023), [PRO 28:11](bibleref:20028011); [HOS 12:8](bibleref:28012008)

### They may cause unbelief and anxiety
**[ECC 5:12](bibleref:21005012)** *See also* [JOB 20:17-20:20](bibleref:18020017-18020020); [PRO 15:6](bibleref:20015006)

### They may lead people to forget God
**[PRO 30:8-30:9](bibleref:20030008-20030009)** *See also* [DEU 8:10-8:14](bibleref:05008010-05008014), [DEU 32:15](bibleref:05032015); [NEH 9:25-9:26](bibleref:16009025-16009026); [HOS 13:6](bibleref:28013006)

## Riches are physically dangerous


### Working for them may cause excessive tiredness
**[PRO 23:4](bibleref:20023004)** *See also* [JHN 6:26-6:27](bibleref:43006026-43006027)

### Enjoying them may lead to self-indulgence
**[JAS 5:5](bibleref:59005005)** *See also* [HAG 1:3-1:5](bibleref:37001003-37001005); [MAT 23:25](bibleref:40023025); [LUK 16:19-16:21](bibleref:42016019-42016021); [1TI 6:8-6:9](bibleref:54006008-54006009)

### Misusing them may foster oppression and injustice
**[LEV 19:13](bibleref:03019013)** *See also* [DEU 24:12-24:15](bibleref:05024012-05024015); [PRO 22:16](bibleref:20022016); [ISA 1:23](bibleref:23001023); [JER 22:13](bibleref:24022013); [MIC 6:12](bibleref:33006012); [MAL 3:5](bibleref:39003005); [JAS 2:6](bibleref:59002006), [JAS 5:4](bibleref:59005004)
