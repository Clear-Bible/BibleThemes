# 8810: riches, dangers of

Material wealth can be a source of both physical and spiritual danger, against which believers are clearly warned to be on their guard.



## Riches are spiritually dangerous


### Love of riches a root of many evils
**1TI 6:10** *See also* JOS 7:11, JOS 7:20-7:21; 1TI 3:3

### They may lead to divided loyalty
**MAT 6:24** parallel LUK 16:13 *See also* MAT 4:9-4:10; JAS 4:4

### They may hinder people's response to the gospel
 MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14; ; MAT 19:21-19:22 parallels MRK 10:21-10:22, LUK 18:22-18:23

### They may create false security
**1TI 6:17** *See also* JOB 31:24-31:28; PSA 52:6-52:7; PRO 11:28; JER 49:4

### They may make people proud
**EZK 28:5** *See also* PSA 62:10; PRO 18:23, PRO 28:11; HOS 12:8

### They may cause unbelief and anxiety
**ECC 5:12** *See also* JOB 20:17-20:20; PRO 15:6

### They may lead people to forget God
**PRO 30:8-30:9** *See also* DEU 8:10-8:14, DEU 32:15; NEH 9:25-9:26; HOS 13:6

## Riches are physically dangerous


### Working for them may cause excessive tiredness
**PRO 23:4** *See also* JHN 6:26-6:27

### Enjoying them may lead to self-indulgence
**JAS 5:5** *See also* HAG 1:3-1:5; MAT 23:25; LUK 16:19-16:21; 1TI 6:8-6:9

### Misusing them may foster oppression and injustice
**LEV 19:13** *See also* DEU 24:12-24:15; PRO 22:16; ISA 1:23; JER 22:13; MIC 6:12; MAL 3:5; JAS 2:6, JAS 5:4
