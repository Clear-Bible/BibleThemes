# 9314: resurrection, of the dead

Scripture speaks of a general resurrection of all people at the end of time, which will be followed by judgment.



## The resurrection of the dead predicted


### In the OT
**[DAN 12:2](bibleref:27012002)**

### By Jesus Christ
**[JHN 5:28-5:29](bibleref:43005028-43005029)**

## The resurrection of the dead will occur at Jesus Christ's return
**[REV 20:12-20:13](bibleref:66020012-66020013)** *See also* [ACT 17:31](bibleref:44017031); [1CO 15:52](bibleref:46015052); [1TH 4:16](bibleref:52004016)

## The resurrection of the dead includes both the righteous and wicked
**[ACT 24:15](bibleref:44024015)** *See also* [MAT 25:31-25:32](bibleref:40025031-40025032)
