# 7471: temples, heathen

Other nations also built temples to their gods, and apostate kings of Judah and Israel were condemned for erecting heathen temples in their own countries.



## Heathen temples
The temple of Dagon:
 JDG 16:28-16:30; ; 1SA 5:2-5:4; 
1SA 31:8-31:10; ; 2KI 5:18 *the temple of Rimmon*; ; NAM 1:14

## Idolatrous temples in Israel and Judah
 1KI 12:26-12:30 *Jeroboam's temples at Bethel and Dan*; 1KI 16:32; ; 2KI 10:21, 2KI 10:23-10:27; 
2CH 23:17 *A temple to Baal in Judah was destroyed in the reign of Joash.*

## The command to destroy pagan places of worship in Israel
**DEU 12:2-12:3**

## Holy things and holy places were used for pagan worship
Ahaz:
 2KI 16:10-16:13; ; 2CH 28:23; 2KI 21:4-21:5; ; 2CH 33:5, 2CH 33:7; 
2CH 24:7 *Athaliah's sons used objects from the temple for the worship of the Baals*; ; EZK 8:12-8:16 *God showed Ezekiel a vision of people worshipping false gods in the temple*; ; 2CH 26:16-26:20 *Uzziah incurred judgment by usurping the priestly task of offering incense in the temple.*
