# 8722: doubt, nature of

Doubt leads to insecurity and lack of trust concerning God's willingness and ability to deliver his people. It also leads to a fear of people and situations.



## Doubting God's truth


### Doubt as a questioning of God's words
**GEN 3:1** *See also* GEN 3:4; ISA 5:19; JER 17:15; 2PE 3:4

### Doubt as lack of faith
**MAT 21:21-21:22** parallel MRK 11:23-11:24 *See also* MAT 17:20; MRK 6:6, MRK 16:14; LUK 17:6

### Doubt as wavering
**JAS 1:6** *See also* 1KI 18:21; ROM 4:20; EPH 4:13-4:14

### Doubt as double-mindedness
**JAS 1:8** *See also* 2KI 17:40-17:41; LUK 16:13; 1CO 10:21; JAS 4:8

## Doubt as insecurity about relationship with God


### Doubting God's compassion
**PSA 77:7-77:9** *See also* PSA 90:13; HEB 12:5-12:6

### Doubting God's concern
**JOB 30:20**; **ISA 49:14** *See also* PSA 13:1-13:2, PSA 22:1-22:2, PSA 35:17; ISA 40:27; JER 8:18-8:22; LAM 3:8; HAB 1:2

### Doubting God's desire to deliver
**JER 45:3** *See also* EXO 5:22-5:23; LAM 2:1-2:9, LAM 3:13-3:20

### Doubting God's ability to deliver
**PSA 78:18-78:22** *See also* EXO 14:10-14:12; PSA 78:41-78:43; MAT 8:26 parallels MRK 4:39-4:40, LUK 8:24-8:25

### Doubting God's justice
**JER 12:1** *See also* JOB 9:23; PSA 73:13-73:16, PSA 82:2; JER 15:16-15:18; HAB 1:13

## Doubt as fear of people and situations
**MAT 14:30-14:31** *See also* GEN 12:12-12:13, GEN 19:30, GEN 26:7; JOS 7:5; 1SA 17:11; ISA 51:12-51:13
