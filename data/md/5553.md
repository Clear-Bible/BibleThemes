# 5553: stairways

Steps which provide access to upper levels, usually of buildings. The term is also used figuratively as a means of ascent from earth to heaven.



## Stairways relating to places of worship


### In Solomon's temple
**1KI 6:8**

### Used by the Levites
**NEH 9:4**

### In Ezekiel's vision of the new temple
**EZK 40:49** *See also* EZK 40:6, EZK 40:22, EZK 40:26, EZK 40:31, EZK 40:34, EZK 40:37, EZK 40:49, EZK 41:7, EZK 43:17

### Steps forbidden for the altar
**EXO 20:26**

## Stairways relating to royalty
**1KI 10:19-10:20** parallel 2CH 9:18-9:19 *See also* 2KI 9:13, 2KI 20:11 parallel ISA 38:8

## Stairways in city walls and fortresses
**NEH 3:15** *See also* NEH 3:19, NEH 12:37; ACT 21:35, ACT 21:40

## Stairways in houses


### For access to upper floors and basements
**ACT 9:39** *See also* 2SA 18:33; 1KI 17:19; ACT 1:13, ACT 9:37, ACT 10:20, ACT 20:8, ACT 20:11

## Figurative use of stairways
**GEN 28:12** *See also* JHN 1:51
