# 8131: guidance, results of

Wise counsel, advice and direction from God or his servants, if carefully followed, will enrich and benefit those to whom it is given.



## Guidance resulting in military victory
**2SA 5:17-5:20** parallel 1CH 14:8-14:11 *See also* JOS 6:1-6:21, JOS 8:1-8:22; JDG 6:33-7:25; 2SA 5:22-5:25 parallel 1CH 14:13-14:16

## Guidance resulting in safety
**MAT 2:1-2:23**

## Guidance resulting in personal benefit
**2SA 2:1-2:4** *See also* GEN 24:10-24:27, GEN 41:1-41:49; 2KI 5:1-5:14

## Guidance resulting in the appointment of leaders
**ACT 13:1-13:3** *See also* 1KI 19:15-19:21; LUK 6:12-6:16; ACT 1:15-1:26

## Guidance inspiring confidence
**ACT 23:11** *See also* GEN 46:1-46:7; ACT 18:9-18:11

## Guidance resulting in spiritual growth
**PRO 9:9** *See also* EXO 16:1-16:30; 1KI 19:3-19:18; ACT 9:1-9:19

## Guidance eliciting praise and worship
**DAN 2:19-2:23** *See also* EXO 14:15-15:21; 2SA 7:1-7:29; PSA 73:23-73:28

## Guidance leading to obedience
**GEN 12:1-12:5** *See also* GEN 6:9-6:22; JDG 6:11-6:27

## Guidance disregarded
**MAT 19:16-19:22** parallels MRK 10:17-10:22, LUK 18:18-18:23 *See also* GEN 3:1-3:6; 1KI 22:1-22:38
