# 5402: market

The market was a central feature of life in biblical times. Some of the biblical terms for redemption are drawn from the market-place.

## Ancient market-places
 [1KI 20:34](bibleref:11020034) *Ahab's treaty with Ben-Hadad suggests that by the 9th century B.C. there was a highly developed system of international trade.* [ISA 23:3](bibleref:23023003); [EZK 27:24](bibleref:26027024)

## Biblical terms for redemption are drawn from market language


### In the gospel call
 [PRO 8:3-8:5](bibleref:20008003-20008005) *The city gates were often places for business deals* ; [ISA 1:18](bibleref:23001018), [ISA 55:1](bibleref:23055001), [ISA 55:2](bibleref:23055002); [MAT 13:45-13:46](bibleref:40013045-40013046)

### God's people have been bought
**[ACT 20:28](bibleref:44020028)** *See also* [EXO 15:16](bibleref:02015016); [PSA 74:2](bibleref:19074002); [1CO 6:20](bibleref:46006020), [1CO 7:23](bibleref:46007023); [2PE 2:1](bibleref:61002001); [REV 5:9](bibleref:66005009), [REV 14:4](bibleref:66014004)

### God's people have been redeemed
**[GAL 4:4-4:5](bibleref:48004004-48004005)** *The Greek word “exagorazo” means “to buy” and was used, for example, of the buying of slaves.*  *See also* [GAL 3:13-3:14](bibleref:48003013-48003014); [REV 14:3](bibleref:66014003) *The Greek “agorazo”, translated here as “redeemed”, may also be translated as “bought”. Elsewhere (e.g., Tit 2:14; 1Pe 1:18), “lutro” meaning “to release” is used, conveying a similar idea.* 

### Redeeming the time
The Greek ``exagorazo'' here translated as ``make the most of'':
