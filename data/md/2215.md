# 2215: Jesus Christ, Son of David

A Messianic title. Jesus Christ comes as a descendant of David to fulfil God's promises in the OT of a future king who would establish the kingdom of God on earth.



## OT promises of a future king from David's line
**[2SA 7:12-7:14](bibleref:10007012-10007014)** parallel 1CH 17:11-17:13 *These promises are known as “the Davidic covenant”* ; **[ISA 16:5](bibleref:23016005)** *See also* [PSA 132:11-132:12](bibleref:19132011-19132012), [PSA 132:17](bibleref:19132017); [ISA 9:7](bibleref:23009007), [ISA 11:1](bibleref:23011001); [JER 23:5](bibleref:24023005), [JER 33:15](bibleref:24033015); [EZK 34:23-34:24](bibleref:26034023-26034024), [EZK 37:24-37:25](bibleref:26037024-26037025); [JHN 7:42](bibleref:43007042)

## NT statements verifying that Jesus Christ is descended from David
**[MAT 1:1](bibleref:40001001)** *See also* [LUK 3:23](bibleref:42003023), [LUK 3:31](bibleref:42003031); [ROM 1:3](bibleref:45001003); [2TI 2:8](bibleref:55002008); [REV 22:16](bibleref:66022016)

## Jesus Christ inherits the throne of David
**[LUK 1:32-1:33](bibleref:42001032-42001033)** *See also* [LUK 1:69](bibleref:42001069); [JHN 1:49](bibleref:43001049), [JHN 18:37](bibleref:43018037); [MAT 27:37](bibleref:40027037) parallels MRK 15:26, LUK 23:38, JHN 19:19

## Jesus Christ is called ``Son of David''


### By those seeking healing
**[MAT 20:30-20:31](bibleref:40020030-40020031)** parallels MRK 10:47-10:48, LUK 18:38-18:39 *See also* [MAT 9:27](bibleref:40009027), [MAT 15:22](bibleref:40015022)

### At his triumphal entry into Jerusalem
**[MAT 21:9](bibleref:40021009)** parallel MRK 11:10 *See also* [MAT 21:15](bibleref:40021015)

## Grounds for identifying Jesus Christ as the Son of David


### His human descent
**[ROM 1:3](bibleref:45001003)** *See also* [MAT 1:1](bibleref:40001001)

### His birth in Bethlehem
 [MIC 5:2](bibleref:33005002); [JHN 7:42](bibleref:43007042)

### His healing ministry
**[MAT 12:22-12:23](bibleref:40012022-40012023)** *One of the signs of the Messianic age was the healing of the sick (see Isa 35:5-6; 42:7).*  *See also* [JHN 7:31](bibleref:43007031)

### His ultimate victory
 [REV 5:5](bibleref:66005005)

## Jesus Christ is more than a merely human king descended from David
**[MRK 12:35-12:37](bibleref:41012035-41012037)** *Jesus Christ claim is that he is not simply a human or political king, he is the Lord, the Son of God.*  *See also* [PSA 110:1](bibleref:19110001); [JHN 18:36](bibleref:43018036)
