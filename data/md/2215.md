# 2215: Jesus Christ, Son of David

A Messianic title. Jesus Christ comes as a descendant of David to fulfil God's promises in the OT of a future king who would establish the kingdom of God on earth.



## OT promises of a future king from David's line
**2SA 7:12-7:14** parallel 1CH 17:11-17:13 *These promises are known as “the Davidic covenant”*; **ISA 16:5** *See also* PSA 132:11-132:12, PSA 132:17; ISA 9:7, ISA 11:1; JER 23:5, JER 33:15; EZK 34:23-34:24, EZK 37:24-37:25; JHN 7:42

## NT statements verifying that Jesus Christ is descended from David
**MAT 1:1** *See also* LUK 3:23, LUK 3:31; ROM 1:3; 2TI 2:8; REV 22:16

## Jesus Christ inherits the throne of David
**LUK 1:32-1:33** *See also* LUK 1:69; JHN 1:49, JHN 18:37; MAT 27:37 parallels MRK 15:26, LUK 23:38, JHN 19:19

## Jesus Christ is called ``Son of David''


### By those seeking healing
**MAT 20:30-20:31** parallels MRK 10:47-10:48, LUK 18:38-18:39 *See also* MAT 9:27, MAT 15:22

### At his triumphal entry into Jerusalem
**MAT 21:9** parallel MRK 11:10 *See also* MAT 21:15

## Grounds for identifying Jesus Christ as the Son of David


### His human descent
**ROM 1:3** *See also* MAT 1:1

### His birth in Bethlehem
 MIC 5:2; ; JHN 7:42

### His healing ministry
**MAT 12:22-12:23** *One of the signs of the Messianic age was the healing of the sick (see Isa 35:5-6; 42:7).* *See also* JHN 7:31

### His ultimate victory
 REV 5:5

## Jesus Christ is more than a merely human king descended from David
**MRK 12:35-12:37** *Jesus Christ claim is that he is not simply a human or political king, he is the Lord, the Son of God.* *See also* PSA 110:1; JHN 18:36
