# 8369: worthiness

Only God truly merits honour and praise but he graciously considers those who humbly serve him with wholehearted devotion to be worthy disciples.

## God the Father is worthy of honour and praise
**[PSA 145:3](bibleref:19145003)** *See also* [2SA 22:4](bibleref:10022004) parallel PSA 18:3; [1CH 16:25](bibleref:13016025) parallel PSA 96:4; [PSA 48:1](bibleref:19048001); [REV 4:11](bibleref:66004011)

## Jesus Christ is worthy of honour and praise
**[REV 5:12](bibleref:66005012)** *See also* [MAT 3:17](bibleref:40003017) parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; [MAT 17:5](bibleref:40017005) parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; [PHP 2:9-2:11](bibleref:50002009-50002011); [HEB 2:9](bibleref:58002009), [HEB 3:3](bibleref:58003003); [2PE 1:17](bibleref:61001017); [REV 5:9](bibleref:66005009)

## People are considered worthy by others
**[LUK 7:4-7:5](bibleref:42007004-42007005)** *Note that the centurion did not consider himself to be worthy (Lk 7:7).*  *See also* [GEN 39:4](bibleref:01039004), [GEN 41:38-41:40](bibleref:01041038-01041040); [1SA 16:6](bibleref:09016006); [1KI 1:42](bibleref:11001042); [DAN 1:18-1:20](bibleref:27001018-27001020), [DAN 2:46-2:48](bibleref:27002046-27002048), [DAN 3:28-3:30](bibleref:27003028-27003030); [ACT 14:11-14:13](bibleref:44014011-44014013)
