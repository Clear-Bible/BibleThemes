# 8369: worthiness

Only God truly merits honour and praise but he graciously considers those who humbly serve him with wholehearted devotion to be worthy disciples.



## God the Father is worthy of honour and praise
**PSA 145:3** *See also* 2SA 22:4 parallel PSA 18:3; 1CH 16:25 parallel PSA 96:4; PSA 48:1; REV 4:11

## Jesus Christ is worthy of honour and praise
**REV 5:12** *See also* MAT 3:17 parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; MAT 17:5 parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; PHP 2:9-2:11; HEB 2:9, HEB 3:3; 2PE 1:17; REV 5:9

## People are considered worthy by others
**LUK 7:4-7:5** *Note that the centurion did not consider himself to be worthy (Lk 7:7).* *See also* GEN 39:4, GEN 41:38-41:40; 1SA 16:6; 1KI 1:42; DAN 1:18-1:20, DAN 2:46-2:48, DAN 3:28-3:30; ACT 14:11-14:13

## God considers no person (other than Jesus Christ) to be worthy by nature
**REV 5:4** *See also* PSA 14:3, PSA 143:2; ECC 7:20; ROM 3:10-3:12, ROM 3:23

## Worthiness, like righteousness, is credited to a person by God
**2TH 1:11** *See also* LUK 20:35; ROM 2:29; 2CO 10:18

## Examples of people who are considered worthy by God


### Those who put God first and deny themselves
**MAT 10:37-10:38** *See also* 1SA 2:30; LUK 9:62; ROM 12:1-12:2

### Those who suffer persecution for the sake of Jesus Christ
**ACT 5:41** *See also* MAT 5:10-5:12; 2TH 1:5; 1PE 2:19-2:20

### Those whose lives show Christlike godliness
**COL 1:10** *See also* 1SA 16:7; EPH 4:1-4:2; 1TH 2:12; 1TI 3:8-3:9, 1TI 3:11; TIT 2:2; REV 3:4

### Those who stand firm for the gospel
 PHP 1:27; ; 1TH 2:4; ; 2TI 2:15

### Those who are faithful in the use of resources which God has given them
**MAT 25:21-25:23** *See also* MAT 24:45-24:47 parallel LUK 12:42-12:44; LUK 19:17; 1TI 5:17

### Those who welcome God's people
**MAT 10:11** *See also* MAT 10:41-10:42; ROM 16:2; 3JN 1:6

## Examples of worthy people
 GEN 6:8-6:9 *Noah*; ; DEU 1:36 *Caleb*; ; DEU 34:10 *Moses*; ; JOB 1:8 *Job*; DAN 9:23, DAN 10:19; 
LUK 1:28 *Mary*; ; JAS 2:23 *Abraham*
