# 8243: ethics, social

Scripture sees ethics as embracing the life of society as a whole, and not simply individual relationships.



## The benefits of social ethics
**PRO 14:34** *See also* 2SA 23:3-23:4; PSA 2:10-2:12; PRO 11:11, PRO 16:12, PRO 25:5, PRO 28:2, PRO 29:4; ISA 54:14

## Believers are to behave ethically in society


### Praying for rulers
**1TI 2:1-2:2** *See also* EZR 6:10; PSA 20:9, PSA 72:1

### Submitting to the authorities
**ROM 13:1**; **TIT 3:1**; **1PE 2:13-2:14** *See also* EXO 22:28; DEU 17:12; 1SA 24:6; 1KI 21:10; PRO 24:21; ECC 8:2; ACT 23:5; ROM 13:5; 1PE 2:17

### Paying taxes
**MAT 22:21** parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:25 *See also* LEV 27:30; DEU 14:28; NEH 10:32; MAT 17:27; ROM 13:6-13:7
