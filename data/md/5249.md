# 5249: census

The counting of individuals, usually for the purpose of war or taxation.

## Motivations for a census


### To count the number of fighting men available
**[NUM 1:1-1:4](bibleref:04001001-04001004), [NUM 26:1-26:4](bibleref:04026001-04026004)** *See also* [NUM 1:5-1:49](bibleref:04001005-04001049), [NUM 26:5-26:65](bibleref:04026005-04026065)

### For ascertaining those qualified to be included in the Jewish community
 [EZR 2:1-2:65](bibleref:15002001-15002065) parallel NEH 7:6-7:67

### For purposes of taxation
**[LUK 2:2](bibleref:42002002)**; **[ACT 5:37](bibleref:44005037)** *See also* [EXO 30:12](bibleref:02030012)

## God's anger at David's unauthorised census
 [2SA 24:1-24:9](bibleref:10024001-10024009) parallel 1CH 21:1-21:6 *David's sin in taking this census was probably that he did so from a motive of self-sufficiency rather than trusting in God.*
