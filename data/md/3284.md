# 3284: Holy Spirit, resisting

The work of the Holy Spirit can be resisted through disobedience and unbelief.



## Scriptural images of resisting the Holy Spirit


### Grieving the Spirit
**[ISA 63:10](bibleref:23063010)**; **[EPH 4:30](bibleref:49004030)**

### Resisting the Spirit
**[ACT 7:51](bibleref:44007051)** *See also* [GEN 6:3](bibleref:01006003); [ACT 6:9-6:10](bibleref:44006009-44006010)

### Blaspheming against the Spirit
**[MRK 3:29](bibleref:41003029)** parallels MAT 12:31, LUK 12:10 *See also* [HEB 10:29](bibleref:58010029)

### Quenching the Spirit
**[1TH 5:19](bibleref:52005019)**

### Lying to the Spirit
**[ACT 5:3](bibleref:44005003)**

### Testing the Spirit
**[ACT 5:9](bibleref:44005009)**

## Ways in which the Holy Spirit is resisted


### Through sin
**[GAL 5:17](bibleref:48005017)** *See also* [EPH 4:30-4:31](bibleref:49004030-49004031)

### Through rebellion
**[PSA 106:33](bibleref:19106033)** *See also* [ISA 63:10](bibleref:23063010)

### Through hardness of heart
**[ZEC 7:12](bibleref:38007012)** *See also* [ACT 7:51](bibleref:44007051)

### Through spiritual blindness
**[1CO 2:14](bibleref:46002014)**
