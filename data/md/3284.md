# 3284: Holy Spirit, resisting

The work of the Holy Spirit can be resisted through disobedience and unbelief.



## Scriptural images of resisting the Holy Spirit


### Grieving the Spirit
**ISA 63:10**; **EPH 4:30**

### Resisting the Spirit
**ACT 7:51** *See also* GEN 6:3; ACT 6:9-6:10

### Blaspheming against the Spirit
**MRK 3:29** parallels MAT 12:31, LUK 12:10 *See also* HEB 10:29

### Quenching the Spirit
**1TH 5:19**

### Lying to the Spirit
**ACT 5:3**

### Testing the Spirit
**ACT 5:9**

## Ways in which the Holy Spirit is resisted


### Through sin
**GAL 5:17** *See also* EPH 4:30-4:31

### Through rebellion
**PSA 106:33** *See also* ISA 63:10

### Through hardness of heart
**ZEC 7:12** *See also* ACT 7:51

### Through spiritual blindness
**1CO 2:14**
