# 2345: Jesus Christ, kingdom of

The present and future realm in which Jesus Christ exercises full authority, and through which he triumphs over all opposition.



## Jesus Christ's kingdom is a heavenly kingdom


### It is not of this world
**[JHN 18:36](bibleref:43018036)** *See also* [REV 5:6](bibleref:66005006), [REV 7:10](bibleref:66007010), [REV 21:1](bibleref:66021001), [REV 21:3](bibleref:66021003)

### The kingdom was given to him by God
**[DAN 7:14](bibleref:27007014)** *See also* [PSA 72:1](bibleref:19072001); [DAN 2:44](bibleref:27002044); [MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018); [JHN 16:15](bibleref:43016015)

### The kingdom is his by right
**[COL 1:15-1:16](bibleref:51001015-51001016)** *See also* [HEB 1:8](bibleref:58001008); [PSA 45:6](bibleref:19045006); [REV 22:13](bibleref:66022013)
