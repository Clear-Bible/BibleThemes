# 2345: Jesus Christ, kingdom of

The present and future realm in which Jesus Christ exercises full authority, and through which he triumphs over all opposition.



## Jesus Christ's kingdom is a heavenly kingdom


### It is not of this world
**JHN 18:36** *See also* REV 5:6, REV 7:10, REV 21:1, REV 21:3

### The kingdom was given to him by God
**DAN 7:14** *See also* PSA 72:1; DAN 2:44; MAT 28:18; JHN 16:15

### The kingdom is his by right
**COL 1:15-1:16** *See also* HEB 1:8; PSA 45:6; REV 22:13

## Jesus Christ inherits the kingdom promised to David
**LUK 1:32** *See also* ROM 15:12; ISA 11:10; REV 2:27; PSA 2:9

## Jesus Christ reveals God's kingdom on earth


### Jesus Christ brought in God's kingdom
**MAT 4:17** *The expressions “kingdom of heaven” and the “kingdom of God” are interchangeable.* *See also* MAT 3:2, MAT 10:7, MAT 12:28 parallel LUK 11:20; MRK 1:15; LUK 17:20-17:21

### Miracles are a sign of the kingdom
**JHN 6:14-6:15** *See also* MAT 8:26-8:27 parallels MRK 4:39-4:41, LUK 8:24-8:25 *authority over the created world*; MAT 11:2-11:5 parallel LUK 7:19-7:22 *authority to heal*LUK 7:11-7:15; REV 1:17-1:18

### Parables are a sign of the kingdom
**MAT 13:1-13:52** *See also* MRK 4:1-4:34; LUK 8:4-8:15, LUK 13:18-13:21

## Jesus Christ exercises kingdom authority today


### Believers enter Jesus Christ's kingdom immediately
**LUK 23:42-23:43** *See also* MAT 5:3 parallel LUK 6:20; MAT 11:11

### Believers are redeemed from the kingdom of darkness
**COL 1:12-1:13** *See also* EPH 5:5

### Believers enjoy the blessings of Jesus Christ's kingdom now
**MAT 16:19** *See also* DAN 7:22, DAN 7:27; LUK 12:32, LUK 22:29-22:30; JAS 2:5; REV 1:5-1:6, REV 1:9, REV 3:21

### Jesus Christ rules over every authority now
**1PE 3:22** *See also* EPH 1:20-1:23; PHP 2:9; COL 2:10; HEB 12:2; REV 12:10, REV 19:16

## Jesus Christ's kingdom will be fully established at his return


### His kingdom will come with power at a specific moment
**MAT 25:31** *See also* ZEC 9:10; MAT 24:30-24:31 parallels MRK 13:26-13:27, LUK 21:27; 2TI 4:1

### His kingdom will replace all earthly authority
**REV 11:15** *See also* 1CO 15:24-15:25, 1CO 15:50-15:52; PHP 3:20-3:21

### All creation will acknowledge Jesus Christ's kingship
**PHP 2:10-2:11** *See also* PSA 2:6-2:8; REV 5:13

### His kingdom lasts for ever
**LUK 1:33** *See also* ISA 9:7
