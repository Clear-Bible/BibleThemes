# 2066: Jesus Christ, power of

The historical and ongoing effective authority of Jesus Christ over natural elements, over human life and ultimately over sin, forces of evil, Satan and death.



## Jesus Christ's power is from God
**ACT 10:38**

### God's creative power is exercised through Jesus Christ
**JHN 1:3** *See also* 1CO 8:6; COL 1:16; HEB 1:2

### God's infinite power is given to Jesus Christ
**MAT 28:18** *See also* MAT 9:6 parallels MRK 2:10, LUK 5:24; MAT 19:26 parallel LUK 1:37; LUK 1:35; JHN 3:34-3:35, JHN 5:19-5:20, JHN 13:3; PHP 2:9

## Jesus Christ's power is revealed on earth


### Jesus Christ's power over nature
**MAT 8:26-8:27** parallels MRK 4:39-4:41, LUK 8:24-8:25 *See also* MAT 14:25 parallels MRK 6:48, JHN 6:19; MAT 21:19 parallel MRK 11:14; MRK 11:21

### Jesus Christ's power over sickness
**MAT 4:23** *See also* MAT 8:2-8:3 parallels MRK 1:40-1:42, LUK 5:12-5:13; MRK 5:27-5:30 parallels MAT 9:21-9:22, LUK 8:44-8:46; LUK 5:17; ACT 10:38

### Jesus Christ's power over human life
**MAT 11:5** parallel LUK 7:22 *See also* MAT 9:18-9:25 parallels MRK 5:22-5:42, LUK 8:41-8:55; JHN 11:43-11:44

### Jesus Christ's power over his own life
**JHN 10:18** *See also* HEB 7:16

### Jesus Christ's power over human behaviour
**MAT 4:20** parallel LUK 5:11 *See also* MAT 9:9 parallels MRK 2:14, LUK 5:27-5:28; MRK 15:5; JHN 11:29, JHN 19:10-19:11

### Jesus Christ's power over forces of evil
**MAT 8:16** parallels MRK 1:32-1:34, LUK 4:40-4:41 *See also* MAT 4:10-4:11 parallels MRK 1:13, LUK 4:12-4:13; MAT 9:32-9:33, MAT 12:22, MAT 17:18 parallels MRK 9:25, LUK 9:42; JHN 14:30

## Jesus Christ has power over sin and death


### Jesus Christ's crucifixion defeats the power of sin
**ROM 6:6** *See also* ISA 53:10-53:12; MAT 9:6; JHN 15:13; 1CO 15:3; TIT 2:14; HEB 9:28; 1PE 2:24; REV 5:9

### Jesus Christ's resurrection defeats the power of death
**ROM 6:9** *See also* PSA 16:10; JHN 2:19, JHN 5:21, JHN 17:2; ACT 2:24, ACT 2:32; ROM 1:4; EPH 1:19-1:20; PHP 3:10

### Jesus Christ's power over sin and death remains effective
**HEB 7:25** *See also* JHN 6:40; ROM 8:2-8:3; 1CO 15:22; HEB 7:26-7:28; 1PE 3:18

## Jesus Christ's power remains available in human experience


### Jesus Christ's forgiving power is effective today
**ACT 13:38-13:39** *See also* JHN 20:23; ACT 5:31; EPH 1:7
