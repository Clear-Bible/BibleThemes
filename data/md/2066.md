# 2066: Jesus Christ, power of

The historical and ongoing effective authority of Jesus Christ over natural elements, over human life and ultimately over sin, forces of evil, Satan and death.



## Jesus Christ's power is from God
**[ACT 10:38](bibleref:44010038)**

### God's creative power is exercised through Jesus Christ
**[JHN 1:3](bibleref:43001003)** *See also* [1CO 8:6](bibleref:46008006); [COL 1:16](bibleref:51001016); [HEB 1:2](bibleref:58001002)

### God's infinite power is given to Jesus Christ
**[MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018)** *See also* [MAT 9:6](bibleref:40009006) parallels MRK 2:10, LUK 5:24; [MAT 19:26](bibleref:40019026) parallel LUK 1:37; [LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035); [JHN 3:34-3:35](bibleref:43003034-43003035), [JHN 5:19-5:20](bibleref:43005019-43005020), [JHN 13:3](bibleref:43013003); [PHP 2:9](bibleref:50002009)
