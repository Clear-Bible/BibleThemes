# 5824: cruelty, examples of

Scripture condemns cruelty, and gives examples of situations in which it was practised.



## Cruelty is an expression of wickedness
**PSA 71:4**; **ROM 1:28-1:31** *See also* PSA 35:16, PSA 86:14; PRO 17:5; ECC 4:1; AMO 5:12

## Forms of cruelty


### The cruel nature of war
**2KI 8:12** *See also* 2KI 15:16; ISA 13:15, ISA 13:18; HOS 10:14, HOS 13:16; NAM 3:10

### The cruel nature of rape
**GEN 34:2** *See also* JDG 19:25; 2SA 13:14; ZEC 14:2

### The cruel nature of words
**JOB 17:1-17:2**; **PSA 42:10**; **MRK 15:31** *See also* 1SA 1:6-1:7; JOB 11:3; PSA 22:7, PSA 35:11, PSA 89:50-89:51, PSA 102:8; JER 20:7; LAM 3:14

## Particular examples of cruelty
 GEN 37:23-37:24 *Joseph's brothers*; ; GEN 49:5-49:7 *Simeon and Levi*; ; EXO 1:22 *Pharaoh*; ; JDG 1:6 *Judah's cruelty to a local king*; ; JDG 4:2-4:3 *Sisera*; ; JDG 10:6-10:8 *the Philistines and Ammonites*; ; 2CH 16:10 *Asa*; ; PRO 5:8-5:9 *the adulteress*; JER 21:7; ; DAN 3:13-3:20; JER 37:15, JER 38:6-38:7; 
EZK 22:29 *Israel*; ; NAM 3:18-3:19 *the king of Assyria*; ; MAT 2:16 *King Herod*; MAT 27:26-27:31 parallels MRK 15:15-15:20, JHN 19:15; LUK 23:33-23:39 parallels MAT 27:35-27:44, MRK 15:24-15:32, JHN 19:18
