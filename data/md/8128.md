# 8128: guidance, receiving God's

God's guidance is promised to those who genuinely desire it and seek it.



## God's readiness to guide his people
**PSA 25:4-25:5**; **JAS 1:5** *See also* EXO 33:12-33:16; JOB 6:24; PSA 5:8, PSA 27:11, PSA 31:3, PSA 61:1-61:2, PSA 86:11, PSA 143:8

## Guidance is given through the truth and counsel of God
**PSA 43:3** *See also* PSA 16:7-16:8, PSA 73:23-73:24, PSA 119:35, PSA 119:105, PSA 119:133; JHN 10:3-10:4, JHN 10:27

## Guidance is given to the humble and penitent
**PRO 3:5-3:6** *See also* 2CH 6:26-6:27; PSA 25:9, PSA 139:23-139:24; ISA 57:15, ISA 57:18-57:19; JER 50:4-50:5; EZK 20:1-20:3, EZK 20:30-20:31

## Guidance is given to those willing to obey God's will
**MAT 26:39** parallels MRK 14:35-14:36, LUK 22:42 *See also* NUM 14:8-14:9; PSA 32:8-32:9; ISA 48:17-48:18; ROM 12:1-12:2

**LUK 12:11-12:12** *See also* PSA 143:10; JHN 16:13; ACT 10:19-10:20, ACT 13:2, ACT 16:6-16:7; GAL 5:18; 1JN 2:26-2:27
