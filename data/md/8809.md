# 8809: riches, nature of

God has created an abundance of good things.  Those who have material wealth should understand its true nature and acknowledge God.



## God and riches


### All riches belong to God the Creator
**PSA 24:1-24:2** *See also* JOB 41:11; PSA 50:12; HAG 2:8

### God gives of his riches
**1SA 2:7** *See also* ECC 5:19; 1CH 29:12; 2CH 1:12

### God gives the ability to produce wealth
**DEU 8:17-8:18** *See also* GEN 26:12-26:13; DEU 26:9-26:10

### Riches may be a sign of God's blessing
**PRO 10:22** *See also* 2CH 25:9; JOB 1:9-1:10; PSA 112:1-112:3, PSA 128:1-128:2; PRO 8:17-8:18

### Riches should be thankfully received
**1TI 4:4-4:5** *See also* MAT 14:19; ROM 1:21; COL 3:17

## The character of earthly riches


### Riches provide great opportunity for doing good
 EXO 35:29; ; 1CH 29:6-29:7; ; LUK 12:33, LUK 16:9, LUK 19:8; ; ACT 4:34-4:35

### Riches do not satisfy
**ECC 5:10** *See also* PRO 13:11, PRO 27:20, PRO 27:24; ECC 4:8, ECC 6:7; LUK 12:18

### Riches are insecure and uncertain
**1TI 6:17** *See also* JOB 1:13-1:19; PRO 23:5, PRO 27:24; JER 48:36; MAT 6:19; 1PE 1:18; REV 18:14-18:20

### Riches are for this life only
**1JN 2:17** *See also* LUK 12:13-12:21; 1TI 6:6-6:8
