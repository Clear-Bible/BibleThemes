# 2505: Jesus Christ, ascension of

The return of Jesus Christ to his Father in order to establish his kingdom, having completed his work on earth.

## Jesus Christ's ascension described
**[ACT 1:9-1:11](bibleref:44001009-44001011)** *As in the OT, the cloud symbolises God's presence.*  *See also* [MRK 16:19](bibleref:41016019); [LUK 24:51](bibleref:42024051); [ACT 1:2](bibleref:44001002); [EPH 4:10](bibleref:49004010); [1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016); [HEB 9:24](bibleref:58009024); [1PE 3:22](bibleref:60003022); [REV 12:5](bibleref:66012005)

## Predictions of Jesus Christ's ascension


### In the OT
**[EPH 4:7-4:8](bibleref:49004007-49004008)** *See also* [PSA 68:18](bibleref:19068018)

### Jesus Christ looks forward to his ascension as his return to his Father
**[JHN 16:28](bibleref:43016028)** *See also* [JHN 6:62](bibleref:43006062), [JHN 7:33](bibleref:43007033), [JHN 14:28](bibleref:43014028), [JHN 16:5](bibleref:43016005), [JHN 16:10](bibleref:43016010), [JHN 17:11](bibleref:43017011), [JHN 17:13](bibleref:43017013), [JHN 20:17](bibleref:43020017)

## Jesus Christ establishes his kingdom by his ascension


### He ascended to send the Holy Spirit
**[JHN 16:7](bibleref:43016007)** *See also* [JHN 7:39](bibleref:43007039), [JHN 14:16-14:19](bibleref:43014016-43014019), [JHN 14:25-14:26](bibleref:43014025-43014026), [JHN 15:26-15:27](bibleref:43015026-43015027); [ACT 2:33](bibleref:44002033)

### He ascended to prepare a place for his people
**[JHN 14:2-14:4](bibleref:43014002-43014004)**

### He ascended to exaltation and glory
 *See also* [LUK 24:26](bibleref:42024026); [ACT 2:33](bibleref:44002033); [EPH 1:20-1:21](bibleref:49001020-49001021); [1PE 3:22](bibleref:60003022)

### He ascended to exercise his high priestly ministry
**[HEB 4:14](bibleref:58004014)** *See also* [HEB 7:25](bibleref:58007025), [HEB 9:24](bibleref:58009024)

### He ascended so that believers may follow him
**[1TH 4:17](bibleref:52004017)**
