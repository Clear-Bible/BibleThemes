# 5522: servants, working conditions of

Those who serve are to be treated fairly and with respect. Jesus Christ himself chose to be a servant and lays responsibility of service on those who follow.



## Servants are to be treated fairly


### They are not to be exploited
**EPH 6:9** *See also* LEV 25:39-25:40, LEV 25:53

### They are to be paid fairly and promptly
**DEU 24:14-24:15** *See also* GEN 29:15; LEV 19:13; NUM 18:29-18:31; 1KI 5:6; JOB 7:1-7:2; JER 22:13; MAT 20:4, MAT 20:8; ROM 4:4; COL 4:1; 2TH 3:10

### Examples of servants treated unfairly
**JAS 5:4** *See also* GEN 31:7, GEN 31:41; EXO 2:23-2:24; JOB 24:10-24:11; MAL 3:5

## Jesus Christ as a servant
**MAT 20:28** *See also* PHP 2:7

## Believers follow Jesus Christ's example as servants


### They are servants of Jesus Christ and his church
**ROM 1:1** *See also* LUK 1:38; 1CO 12:5, 1CO 16:15; EPH 4:12; COL 1:23, COL 3:24

### They work to please God
**EPH 6:5-6:6** *See also* COL 3:22-3:24; TIT 2:9-2:10

## Christian workers should be paid honourably
**1TI 5:17-5:18** *See also* LUK 10:7; 1CO 9:7-9:11; GAL 6:6

## God rewards his servants
**EPH 6:8** *See also* JOB 34:10-34:11; PRO 10:16; MAT 6:3-6:4, MAT 25:23; JHN 4:36; 2TI 4:8

## Service of sin has its reward
**ROM 6:23** *See also* PRO 11:18; HOS 9:1; 2PE 2:15
