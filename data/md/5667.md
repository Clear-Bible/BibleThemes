# 5667: children, responsibilities to God

The responsibilities of children to God as their heavenly Father include honouring and obeying him.



## The responsibilities of children to their heavenly Father


### To honour and obey him in reverent fear
**PSA 34:11** *See also* PRO 2:5, PRO 9:10

### To seek wisdom
**PRO 4:5-4:7** *See also* PRO 1:1-1:4, PRO 8:32-8:33, PRO 10:1

### To listen and learn
**PRO 1:8-1:9** *See also* PRO 4:1-4:4, PRO 23:22

### To praise God
**PSA 8:2** *See also* MAT 21:15-21:16

### To join in worship
**NEH 12:43** *See also* JOS 8:35

## Relationship to Jesus Christ transcends the child-parent relationship
**LUK 18:29-18:30** parallels MAT 19:29, MRK 10:29-10:30
