# 7781: seer


## Seer as an alternative title for a prophet
**[1SA 9:9](bibleref:09009009)** *Literally, a “seer” is “one who sees”, especially one who claims to see into the future or to have insight into the will of God.*  *See also* [2SA 24:11](bibleref:10024011) parallel 1CH 21:9
