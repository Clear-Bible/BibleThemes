# 5103: Moses, significance of

Moses performed a number of major roles in the history of Israel. Many of these roles later found their perfection in Jesus Christ.



## The roles of Moses


### As teacher
 [EXO 18:20](bibleref:02018020); [MAT 23:10](bibleref:40023010); [JHN 1:38](bibleref:43001038)

### As intercessor
 [EXO 17:11](bibleref:02017011), [EXO 32:11](bibleref:02032011); [NUM 12:13](bibleref:04012013), [NUM 21:7](bibleref:04021007); [PSA 106:23](bibleref:19106023); [JHN 17:9](bibleref:43017009); [HEB 7:25](bibleref:58007025)

### As servant of the LORD
 [JOS 1:1-1:2](bibleref:06001001-06001002); [2CH 24:6](bibleref:14024006); [ACT 3:13](bibleref:44003013); [HEB 3:5](bibleref:58003005); [REV 15:3](bibleref:66015003)

### As prophet
 [NUM 12:6-12:8](bibleref:04012006-04012008); [DEU 18:15](bibleref:05018015), [DEU 34:10](bibleref:05034010); [ACT 3:22](bibleref:44003022)

### As deliverer
 [EXO 3:10](bibleref:02003010); [ACT 7:35](bibleref:44007035); [GAL 5:1](bibleref:48005001)

### As judge
 [EXO 18:13](bibleref:02018013)

### As writer
 [EXO 24:4](bibleref:02024004); [DEU 31:9](bibleref:05031009); [LUK 24:27](bibleref:42024027); [JHN 1:45](bibleref:43001045), [JHN 5:46](bibleref:43005046)

### As shepherd
 [EXO 3:1](bibleref:02003001); [PSA 77:20](bibleref:19077020); [JHN 10:11](bibleref:43010011)

### As the friend of God
**[EXO 33:11](bibleref:02033011)** *See also* [EXO 33:17](bibleref:02033017); [MAT 17:5](bibleref:40017005); [JHN 5:20](bibleref:43005020)

### As the mediator of the law and the covenant
 [EXO 19:5-19:6](bibleref:02019005-02019006); [DEU 6:1-6:3](bibleref:05006001-05006003), [DEU 31:9](bibleref:05031009); [JOS 23:6](bibleref:06023006); [2KI 18:12](bibleref:12018012); [GAL 3:19](bibleref:48003019); [HEB 9:15](bibleref:58009015), [HEB 12:24](bibleref:58012024)

## Characteristics of Moses


### He was a man of faith
 [EXO 14:13-14:14](bibleref:02014013-02014014); [HEB 11:24-11:28](bibleref:58011024-58011028)

### He was not eloquent
 [EXO 4:10](bibleref:02004010)

### He understood that the law meant life
**[DEU 32:46-32:47](bibleref:05032046-05032047)** *See also* [MAT 5:17-5:19](bibleref:40005017-40005019), [MAT 19:17](bibleref:40019017); [ACT 7:38](bibleref:44007038)

### He was passionate in his defence of the Lord's holiness
 [EXO 32:19](bibleref:02032019); [NUM 16:30](bibleref:04016030); [LUK 19:45-19:46](bibleref:42019045-42019046)

### He worked miracles that brought glory to God
 [EXO 14:15-14:18](bibleref:02014015-02014018); [JHN 2:11](bibleref:43002011)

**[EXO 12:21](bibleref:02012021)** *See also* [JHN 1:29](bibleref:43001029)

 [NUM 16:46](bibleref:04016046); [ROM 3:25](bibleref:45003025)
