# 5103: Moses, significance of

Moses performed a number of major roles in the history of Israel. Many of these roles later found their perfection in Jesus Christ.



## The roles of Moses


### As teacher
 EXO 18:20; ; MAT 23:10; ; JHN 1:38

### As intercessor
 EXO 17:11, EXO 32:11; ; NUM 12:13, NUM 21:7; ; PSA 106:23; ; JHN 17:9; ; HEB 7:25

### As servant of the LORD
 JOS 1:1-1:2; ; 2CH 24:6; ; ACT 3:13; ; HEB 3:5; ; REV 15:3

### As prophet
 NUM 12:6-12:8; ; DEU 18:15, DEU 34:10; ; ACT 3:22

### As deliverer
 EXO 3:10; ; ACT 7:35; ; GAL 5:1

### As judge
 EXO 18:13

### As writer
 EXO 24:4; ; DEU 31:9; ; LUK 24:27; ; JHN 1:45, JHN 5:46

### As shepherd
 EXO 3:1; ; PSA 77:20; ; JHN 10:11

### As the friend of God
**EXO 33:11** *See also* EXO 33:17; MAT 17:5; JHN 5:20

### As the mediator of the law and the covenant
 EXO 19:5-19:6; ; DEU 6:1-6:3, DEU 31:9; ; JOS 23:6; ; 2KI 18:12; ; GAL 3:19; ; HEB 9:15, HEB 12:24

## Characteristics of Moses


### He was a man of faith
 EXO 14:13-14:14; ; HEB 11:24-11:28

### He was not eloquent
 EXO 4:10

### He understood that the law meant life
**DEU 32:46-32:47** *See also* MAT 5:17-5:19, MAT 19:17; ACT 7:38

### He was passionate in his defence of the Lord's holiness
 EXO 32:19; ; NUM 16:30; ; LUK 19:45-19:46

### He worked miracles that brought glory to God
 EXO 14:15-14:18; ; JHN 2:11

## Institutions established by Moses


### He fathers, and is identified with, the Judaic legal system
 JOS 1:7-1:8; ; 1KI 2:3; ; EZR 3:2; ; NEH 13:1; ; JHN 5:45, JHN 7:23; ; 1CO 9:9

### He inaugurates the ritual slaughter of the Passover lamb
**EXO 12:21** *See also* JHN 1:29

### He sprinkles the people with the blood of the covenant
 EXO 24:8; ; LEV 17:11; ; MAT 26:28; ; HEB 9:13-9:14, HEB 11:28

### He orders the atonement sacrifice
 NUM 16:46; ; ROM 3:25

## Moses enters the glory of the LORD
 EXO 24:17-24:18, EXO 34:29; ; MAT 17:2; ; 2CO 3:7; ; REV 15:3-15:4

## Moses present at the transfiguration
**MRK 9:4** parallels MAT 17:3, LUK 9:30
