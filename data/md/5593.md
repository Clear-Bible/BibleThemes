# 5593: trial

A process of testing or investigation, which may be legal, as in a court case, or spiritual, as in a testing of faith or power. Scripture makes reference to both.



## Legal trials


### The need for legal trials
 DEU 16:18-16:20; ; JHN 7:50-7:51; ; ACT 16:37-16:39

### Trials concerning accidental killings
**JOS 20:9** *See also* NUM 35:9-35:12; JOS 20:1-20:6

### Unjust trials
**MRK 13:9-13:13** parallels MAT 24:9-24:11, LUK 21:12 *See also* 1KI 21:1-21:14; PSA 37:32-37:33; ACT 12:1-12:4, ACT 16:37

### The trial of Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:57-26:68** parallels MRK 14:53-14:65, JHN 18:19-18:24 *See also* MAT 27:11-27:26 parallels MRK 15:2-15:15, LUK 23:2-23:25, JHN 18:28-19:16

### The trial of Paul
**ACT 27:23-27:24** *See also* ACT 22:30-23:11, ACT 24:1-24:23, ACT 25:1-25:12, ACT 25:23-26:32

## Trials of faith


### Through testing or temptation
**JAS 1:2-1:3** *See also* GEN 22:1; LUK 22:28; 2CO 8:1-8:2; 1TH 3:2-3:4; 2TH 1:4; JAS 1:12; 1PE 1:6, 1PE 4:12-4:16; 2PE 2:4-2:9; REV 3:10

### Through burdensome responsibilities
**GAL 4:14**

## Trials of strength or power
**DEU 7:19** *See also* EXO 7:8-7:24; DEU 29:2-29:3; 1KI 18:16-18:40
