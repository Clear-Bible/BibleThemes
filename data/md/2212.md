# 2212: Jesus Christ, head of the church

Jesus Christ rules and governs his people and directs them towards the fulfilment of God's purposes. All power and authority within the church derive from Jesus Christ as the head.



## Jesus Christ rules the universe in the interest of the church
**[EPH 1:22-1:23](bibleref:49001022-49001023)** *See also* [EPH 1:10](bibleref:49001010); [COL 1:18](bibleref:51001018)

## All power and authority within the church derive from Jesus Christ as the head


### Jesus Christ is recognised as head of the church
**[EPH 4:15](bibleref:49004015)** *See also* [EPH 5:23](bibleref:49005023); [COL 2:19](bibleref:51002019)

### Within the church Jesus Christ alone rules with authority
**[MAT 23:8-23:10](bibleref:40023008-40023010)** *See also* [JHN 13:13](bibleref:43013013); [2CO 4:5](bibleref:47004005)

### The church owes obedience to its head
**[JHN 14:15](bibleref:43014015)** *See also* [JHN 14:21](bibleref:43014021), [JHN 14:23](bibleref:43014023); [EPH 5:24](bibleref:49005024); [1JN 3:24](bibleref:62003024)

### All human authority in the church derives from its head
**[EPH 4:11](bibleref:49004011)** *See also* [GAL 1:1](bibleref:48001001)
