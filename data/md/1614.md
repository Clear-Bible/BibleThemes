# 1614: Scripture, understanding

God intends his word to be understood and has provided the directions and means for understanding it.



## Scripture is intended to be clearly understood


### It is accessible to ordinary people
**PSA 119:130** *See also* PSA 19:7

### Some sections are difficult to understand
**2PE 3:15-3:16** *See also* HEB 5:11

## God has prescribed the way to understand Scripture


### Through public reading
**1TI 4:13** *See also* NEH 8:2-8:8, NEH 13:1-13:3

### Through diligent study
**2TI 2:15** *See also* DEU 17:18-17:20; ACT 17:11

### Through thoughtful meditation
**PSA 1:1-1:3** *See also* JOS 1:8; JOB 23:12; PSA 119:15, PSA 119:27, PSA 119:48, PSA 119:97, PSA 119:148

## Assistance in understanding through the Holy Spirit
**1CO 2:9-2:12** *See also* ISA 64:4; LUK 24:45; JHN 14:26, JHN 16:13; 1JN 2:27

## Assistance in understanding through ministers of the word
**NEH 8:2-8:8** *See also* EPH 4:11-4:14; 1TI 4:11-4:16; 2TI 4:1-4:5
