# 7429: Sabbath, in OT

The Sabbath of rest is grounded in God's work of creation. Observance of a Sabbath day is distinctive of the people of God.



## The Sabbath grounded in creation itself
**[GEN 2:3](bibleref:01002003)** *See also* [PSA 118:24](bibleref:19118024)

## The purpose of the Sabbath


### To remember God's work in creation
**[EXO 20:8-20:11](bibleref:02020008-02020011)** *See also* [GEN 2:2](bibleref:01002002); [EXO 35:2](bibleref:02035002)

### To remember the exodus
**[DEU 5:12-5:15](bibleref:05005012-05005015)** *The idea of rest relates the concept of the Sabbath to the ultimate conquest of Canaan under David and to the return from exile.*  *See also* [GEN 8:4](bibleref:01008004); [2SA 7:1](bibleref:10007001), [2SA 7:11](bibleref:10007011); [PSA 95:10-95:11](bibleref:19095010-19095011); [HEB 4:9](bibleref:58004009); [REV 14:13](bibleref:66014013)

### To be a sign of the relationship between Israel and God and to give refreshment
**[EXO 31:17](bibleref:02031017)** *See also* [DEU 5:12-5:14](bibleref:05005012-05005014)

## The Law required the Sabbath to be a holy day free from work
**[LEV 23:3](bibleref:03023003)** *See also* [EXO 34:21](bibleref:02034021), [EXO 35:3](bibleref:02035003); [LEV 23:38](bibleref:03023038); [ISA 56:2](bibleref:23056002), [ISA 58:13](bibleref:23058013)

## The Sabbath was linked with celebration of the New Moon
**[2KI 4:23](bibleref:12004023)** *See also* [ISA 1:13](bibleref:23001013); [EZK 46:3](bibleref:26046003); [HOS 2:11](bibleref:28002011); [AMO 8:5](bibleref:30008005)

## Abuses of the Sabbath
 [EXO 16:27-16:28](bibleref:02016027-02016028) *gathering manna* ; [NUM 15:32](bibleref:04015032) *gathering wood* ; [NEH 13:15-13:18](bibleref:16013015-16013018) *doing ordinary work* [NEH 10:31](bibleref:16010031); [AMO 8:5](bibleref:30008005)
[JER 17:21](bibleref:24017021) *carrying loads*
