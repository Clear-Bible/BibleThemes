# 8031: trust, importance of

Scripture emphasises the importance of putting one's trust in God. This trust, which is nurtured by an understanding of God's revealed truth, finds expression in a life lived according to his purpose. There are examples in Scripture of proper trust being placed in other people.



## Grounds for trust in God


### Trust in God's power and strength
**EXO 14:31**; **2TI 1:12** *See also* 2SA 22:1-22:3 parallel PSA 18:2-18:3; PSA 9:9-9:10, PSA 115:9-115:11, PSA 144:1-144:2

### Trust in God's unfailing love
**PSA 13:5** *See also* EXO 15:13; PSA 17:7, PSA 21:7, PSA 33:18, PSA 52:8, PSA 147:11

### Trust in God's salvation
**ISA 12:2** *See also* 1SA 17:37; PSA 22:4-22:5, PSA 40:2-40:3; ISA 25:9

## Trust nurtured by God's revealed truth
**PRO 22:19-22:21** *See also* PSA 18:30, PSA 119:42; PRO 30:5; JHN 12:36, JHN 14:1-14:3

## Expressions of trust in God


### Praise and worship
 PSA 4:5, PSA 28:7, PSA 64:10; ; DAN 3:28

### Perseverance in faith
 ISA 7:4-7:7; ; JER 51:46-51:47; ; 2TH 1:4; ; HEB 10:35-10:36, HEB 12:2-12:3; ; JAS 1:12, JAS 5:11

### Holding on to God's promise
 1KI 8:56-8:57; ; PSA 130:5; ; ACT 26:7; ; ROM 4:20-4:21; ; COL 1:5

## Jesus Christ's trust in the Father
**LUK 23:46** *See also* PSA 31:5, PSA 22:8; MAT 27:43; HEB 2:13; ISA 8:17

## King Hezekiah's trust in God
**2KI 18:5-18:7** parallel 2CH 31:20-31:21 *See also* 2KI 18:30 parallel ISA 36:15; 2KI 19:14-19:19 parallel ISA 37:14-37:20; 2CH 32:20

## Further examples of trust in God
 RUT 2:12 *Ruth*; ; 1CH 5:20 *the tribes of Reuben, Gad and Manasseh*; ; DAN 3:17 *Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego*; ; DAN 6:23 *Daniel*; ; ZEP 3:12 *the meek and humble*; ; ACT 14:23 *members of the NT churches*

## The results of trusting in God


### Peace
**ISA 26:3-26:4** *See also* ROM 15:13

### Security
**PSA 37:3** *See also* PSA 32:10, PSA 125:1; PRO 29:25; ISA 28:16, ISA 57:13

### Protection from danger
 PSA 25:1-25:3, PSA 31:14-31:15, PSA 32:7, PSA 33:18-33:22, PSA 91:1-91:4

### Freedom from fear
**PSA 27:1** *See also* PSA 23:4, PSA 34:4, PSA 56:3-56:4, PSA 56:10-56:11, PSA 112:7

### Prosperity
 PRO 28:25 *In the OT material prosperity is often seen as a sign of the spiritual wealth that belongs to those who trust God*; ; PRO 16:20; ; JER 17:7-17:8

### Strength
 ISA 30:15, ISA 40:29-40:31

### Physical life
 PSA 25:20; ; JER 39:18, JER 49:11

## Trust in people


### God's servants entrusted with responsibility
**1CO 4:1-4:2** *See also* LUK 16:1-16:2; 1TH 2:4; 2TI 1:14

### Trust at home and work
 PRO 31:10-31:11 *A good wife gives her husband confidence*; ; TIT 2:10 *Employees should be trustworthy.*

### Examples of trusting others
**GEN 39:4** *See also* GEN 41:39-41:40; EXO 19:9 *Moses to be trusted by Israel*; 1CH 9:22 *the gatekeepers appointed by David and Samuel*; NEH 2:6 *Nehemiah is trusted by Artaxerxes to return to Jerusalem*; DAN 5:29-6:2 *Daniel given responsibility*; PHP 2:25 *Paul's confidence in Epaphroditus*; 2TI 2:2 *Timothy to find others to be entrusted to teach the gospel*

### Continued trust in those who fail
**JON 3:1-3:2** *God re-appoints Jonah despite earlier disobedience.* *See also* JHN 21:15 *Jesus Christ reinstates Peter after his denial*; ACT 15:37-15:39 *Barnabas gives John Mark a second chance.*
