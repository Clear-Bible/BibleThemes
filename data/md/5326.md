# 5326: government

God's gracious rule over the nations, particularly Israel. Also, the divinely established ruling of people by God's appointed authorities for the good of society and to prevent anarchy.



## God governs the nations
**PSA 22:28** *See also* 1CH 29:12; 2CH 20:6; PSA 9:7-9:8, PSA 47:2, PSA 47:7-47:8, PSA 66:7, PSA 67:4, PSA 103:19; DAN 4:32, DAN 4:35; 1TI 6:15

## Jesus Christ also governs the nations
**ISA 9:6** *See also* PSA 72:8-72:11, PSA 110:2; ZEC 6:13, ZEC 9:10; REV 2:27; PSA 2:9; REV 12:5, REV 19:15

## The manner of God's government of Israel was unique
**JDG 8:23** *See also* 1SA 8:7, 1SA 12:12

## God governs individuals
**PRO 21:1** *See also* PRO 16:1, PRO 16:9, PRO 19:21, PRO 20:24; JER 10:23; LAM 3:37; DAN 5:23; ACT 17:26; JAS 4:13-4:15

## God has established human agencies for the government of people
**ROM 13:1** *See also* GEN 9:5-9:6; PRO 8:15-8:16; DAN 2:37-2:38, DAN 4:17, DAN 4:32, DAN 5:21; MAT 22:17-22:21; JHN 19:11; 1TI 2:2-2:3; 1PE 2:13

**ROM 13:4** *See also* PSA 72:12-72:14, PSA 82:3-82:4
