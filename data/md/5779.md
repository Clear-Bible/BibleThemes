# 5779: advice

Counsel is essential for gaining wisdom, though human advice is not always reliable. God's advice is necessary and trustworthy for guidance in life.



## Human advice


### A wise person seeks advice from others
**PRO 12:15** *See also* PRO 11:14, PRO 13:10, PRO 15:12, PRO 15:22, PRO 19:20, PRO 24:6, PRO 27:9; ECC 4:13

### Rejecting good advice can lead to disaster
**PRO 1:30-1:31** *See also* 1KI 12:8 parallel 2CH 10:8; 1KI 12:13-12:15 parallel 2CH 10:13-10:15; PRO 1:25-1:26; JER 18:18; ACT 27:11

### Human advice can have unseen dangers
**PRO 12:5** *See also* JOB 21:16; PSA 1:1; ISA 19:11; EZK 11:2

## Examples of good human advice
 EXO 18:17-18:24 *Jethro*; ; 2SA 17:5-17:16 *Hushai*; EST 1:13-1:22, EST 2:1-2:4; 
DAN 4:27 *Daniel*; ; ACT 5:38 *Gamaliel*; ; 2CO 8:10 *Paul*

## Examples of bad human advice
 NUM 31:15-31:16 *Balaam*; 2SA 15:31-15:34, 2SA 16:20-16:23, 2SA 17:1-17:4, 2SA 17:21-17:23; 
1KI 12:28 *Jeroboam*; ; ISA 19:11 *Egyptian advisers*

## Seeking advice from false gods is futile
**LEV 19:31** *See also* DEU 18:14; 2CH 25:15-25:16; ISA 19:3, ISA 41:28-41:29, ISA 47:12-47:13

## God needs no-one to advise him
**ISA 40:13-40:14** *See also* JOB 12:13; PRO 8:14; ISA 28:29

## God gives advice


### To his people
**PSA 32:8** *See also* PSA 16:7, PSA 73:24; REV 3:18

### Through Jesus Christ and his Spirit
**ISA 11:2** *See also* ISA 9:6; JHN 14:26, JHN 15:26, JHN 16:13

### Through his word
**2TI 3:16**

## God frustrates bad advice
**1CO 1:19** *See also* 2SA 17:14; PSA 33:10
