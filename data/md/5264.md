# 5264: compensation





## Compensation under the law of Moses


### For personal injuries
**EXO 21:26-21:27** *See also* EXO 21:32

### For the loss of an animal
**EXO 21:33-21:34** *See also* EXO 21:35-21:36

### For theft
**EXO 22:1, EXO 22:3-22:4** *See also* EXO 22:7-22:9

### For damage to property
 EXO 22:5-22:6

### For breach of trust
 EXO 22:10-22:15; ; LEV 6:1-6:7; ; NUM 5:5-5:7 *Compensation was only paid when someone was at fault.  Similar laws apparently existed in patriarchal times:  see Ge 31:39.*

## Compensation later in the OT


### The law applied by David
 2SA 12:4-12:6

### Sevenfold compensation for theft
 PRO 6:30-6:31 *presumably “sevenfold” here means “manifold”; he will pay in full*

### Adultery beyond compensation
 PRO 6:32-6:35

### Compensation a mark of repentance
 EZK 33:15

## Compensation in the NT


### For losses inflicted
**LUK 19:8** *In his zeal, after being converted, Zacchaeus goes beyond the requirements of the law.* *See also* PHM 1:18-1:19

### For losses sustained for the gospel
**MAT 19:29** parallel LUK 18:29-18:30 *God is far more generous under grace than he required wrongdoers to be under law.*
