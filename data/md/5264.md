# 5264: compensation


## Compensation under the law of Moses


### For personal injuries
**[EXO 21:26-21:27](bibleref:02021026-02021027)** *See also* [EXO 21:32](bibleref:02021032)

### For the loss of an animal
**[EXO 21:33-21:34](bibleref:02021033-02021034)** *See also* [EXO 21:35-21:36](bibleref:02021035-02021036)

### For theft
**[EXO 22:1](bibleref:02022001), [EXO 22:3-22:4](bibleref:02022003-02022004)** *See also* [EXO 22:7-22:9](bibleref:02022007-02022009)

### For damage to property
 [EXO 22:5-22:6](bibleref:02022005-02022006)

### For breach of trust
 [EXO 22:10-22:15](bibleref:02022010-02022015); [LEV 6:1-6:7](bibleref:03006001-03006007); [NUM 5:5-5:7](bibleref:04005005-04005007) *Compensation was only paid when someone was at fault.  Similar laws apparently existed in patriarchal times:  see Ge 31:39.*

## Compensation in the NT


### For losses inflicted
**[LUK 19:8](bibleref:42019008)** *In his zeal, after being converted, Zacchaeus goes beyond the requirements of the law.*  *See also* [PHM 1:18-1:19](bibleref:57001018-57001019)

### For losses sustained for the gospel
**[MAT 19:29](bibleref:40019029)** parallel LUK 18:29-18:30 *God is far more generous under grace than he required wrongdoers to be under law.*
