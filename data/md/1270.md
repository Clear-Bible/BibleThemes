# 1270: right hand of God

A figure of speech that represents God's ultimate power and authority, and where the exalted Jesus Christ now sits.

## The right hand of God as a symbol


### A symbol of power
**[PSA 89:13](bibleref:19089013)**

### A symbol of protection
**[PSA 16:8](bibleref:19016008)** *See also* [PSA 63:8](bibleref:19063008), [PSA 139:10](bibleref:19139010); [ISA 41:13](bibleref:23041013); [LAM 2:3](bibleref:25002003)

### A symbol of God's presence
**[PSA 16:11](bibleref:19016011)** *See also* [PSA 48:10](bibleref:19048010), [PSA 73:23](bibleref:19073023); [ISA 62:8](bibleref:23062008)

### A symbol of God's saving power
**[PSA 60:5](bibleref:19060005)** parallel PSA 108:6 *See also* [PSA 17:7](bibleref:19017007), [PSA 20:6](bibleref:19020006), [PSA 80:15](bibleref:19080015), [PSA 98:1](bibleref:19098001), [PSA 138:7](bibleref:19138007)

### A symbol of victory
 [PSA 18:35](bibleref:19018035), [PSA 44:3](bibleref:19044003), [PSA 78:54](bibleref:19078054); [ISA 41:10](bibleref:23041010)

### A symbol of the defeat of God's enemies
**[EXO 15:6](bibleref:02015006)** *See also* [EXO 15:12](bibleref:02015012); [PSA 21:8](bibleref:19021008), [PSA 74:11](bibleref:19074011)

### A symbol of God's mighty works
**[PSA 45:4](bibleref:19045004)** *See also* [PSA 77:10](bibleref:19077010), [PSA 118:15-118:16](bibleref:19118015-19118016); [ISA 48:13](bibleref:23048013)

### A symbol of judgment
 [LAM 2:4](bibleref:25002004); [HAB 2:16](bibleref:35002016)

## Jesus Christ at God's right hand


### Jesus Christ is exalted to God's right hand
**[HEB 1:13](bibleref:58001013)** *Comparison between Jesus Christ and the angels confirms Christ's absolute supremacy.*  *See also* [PSA 110:1](bibleref:19110001); [MAT 22:44](bibleref:40022044) parallels MRK 12:36, LUK 20:42; [LUK 22:69](bibleref:42022069); [ACT 2:34](bibleref:44002034), [ACT 7:55-7:56](bibleref:44007055-44007056); [COL 3:1](bibleref:51003001); [HEB 1:3](bibleref:58001003), [HEB 12:2](bibleref:58012002)

### Jesus Christ rules every authority at God's right hand
**[1PE 3:22](bibleref:60003022)** *See also* [EPH 1:20-1:21](bibleref:49001020-49001021)

### Jesus Christ continues his work at God's right hand
**[ROM 8:34](bibleref:45008034)** *See also* [ACT 2:33](bibleref:44002033), [ACT 5:31](bibleref:44005031); [HEB 4:14-4:16](bibleref:58004014-58004016), [HEB 8:1](bibleref:58008001)

### Jesus Christ will return at God's right hand
**[MAT 26:64](bibleref:40026064)** parallel MRK 14:62

### Jesus Christ takes a scroll from God's right hand
**[REV 5:1-5:7](bibleref:66005001-66005007)** *The scroll probably refers to God's decrees for the destiny of the world.* 

## The right hand of Jesus Christ


### The place of the righteous on judgment day
**[MAT 25:32-25:34](bibleref:40025032-40025034)**

### A privileged place granted by the Father
**[MAT 20:20-20:23](bibleref:40020020-40020023)** parallel MRK 10:35-10:40

### A place of protection for Christians
**[REV 1:20](bibleref:66001020)** *The angels of the churches are probably the church leaders.*  *See also* [REV 1:16](bibleref:66001016), [REV 2:1](bibleref:66002001)
