# 8835: unbelief, nature and effects of

Unbelief, or the failure to trust God, holds back his blessing and leads to condemnation and spiritual death.



## The nature of unbelief


### Not trusting God
**[PSA 78:22](bibleref:19078022)**

### Rebelling against God
**[HEB 3:12](bibleref:58003012)** *See also* [PSA 78:40](bibleref:19078040); [HEB 3:16](bibleref:58003016)

### Disobeying God
**[HEB 11:31](bibleref:58011031)** *NIV footnote.*  *See also* [EPH 2:2](bibleref:49002002); [HEB 3:18-3:19](bibleref:58003018-58003019), [HEB 4:11](bibleref:58004011)

### Questioning God's truthfulness
**[1JN 5:10](bibleref:62005010)** *See also* [PSA 106:24](bibleref:19106024); [1JN 1:10](bibleref:62001010)

## Unbelief as the result of the devil's work


### Taking away God's word
**[LUK 8:12](bibleref:42008012)** parallels MAT 13:19, MRK 4:15

### Causing spiritual blindness
**[2CO 4:4](bibleref:47004004)** *See also* [ROM 1:21](bibleref:45001021); [2CO 4:3](bibleref:47004003); [EPH 4:18](bibleref:49004018)

## Unbelief as the result of not belonging to God
**[JHN 10:26](bibleref:43010026)** *See also* [JHN 5:38](bibleref:43005038), [JHN 8:44-8:45](bibleref:43008044-43008045), [JHN 12:37-12:40](bibleref:43012037-43012040); [1CO 2:14](bibleref:46002014)

## Unbelief hinders Jesus Christ's miracles
**[MAT 13:58](bibleref:40013058)** parallel MRK 6:5-6:6

## The consequences of persistent unbelief


### Failure to receive God's reward
**[HEB 3:19](bibleref:58003019)** *The “rest” that God's people enjoyed on entering the promised land is here taken to symbolise the final reward prepared by God for his people.*  *See also* [HEB 4:1-4:2](bibleref:58004001-58004002), [HEB 4:6](bibleref:58004006); [JUD 1:5](bibleref:65001005)

### Exclusion from the people of God
**[ROM 11:20](bibleref:45011020)** *See also* [HEB 6:4-6:6](bibleref:58006004-58006006)

### Death in sin
**[JHN 8:24](bibleref:43008024)** *See also* [PSA 106:43](bibleref:19106043); [MRK 4:12](bibleref:41004012); [ACT 10:43](bibleref:44010043)

### God's wrath
**[JHN 3:36](bibleref:43003036)** *See also* [ROM 1:18](bibleref:45001018), [ROM 2:5](bibleref:45002005), [ROM 2:8](bibleref:45002008)
