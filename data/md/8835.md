# 8835: unbelief, nature and effects of

Unbelief, or the failure to trust God, holds back his blessing and leads to condemnation and spiritual death.



## The nature of unbelief


### Not trusting God
**PSA 78:22**

### Rebelling against God
**HEB 3:12** *See also* PSA 78:40; HEB 3:16

### Disobeying God
**HEB 11:31** *NIV footnote.* *See also* EPH 2:2; HEB 3:18-3:19, HEB 4:11

### Questioning God's truthfulness
**1JN 5:10** *See also* PSA 106:24; 1JN 1:10

## Unbelief as the result of the devil's work


### Taking away God's word
**LUK 8:12** parallels MAT 13:19, MRK 4:15

### Causing spiritual blindness
**2CO 4:4** *See also* ROM 1:21; 2CO 4:3; EPH 4:18

## Unbelief as the result of not belonging to God
**JHN 10:26** *See also* JHN 5:38, JHN 8:44-8:45, JHN 12:37-12:40; 1CO 2:14

## Unbelief hinders Jesus Christ's miracles
**MAT 13:58** parallel MRK 6:5-6:6

## The consequences of persistent unbelief


### Failure to receive God's reward
**HEB 3:19** *The “rest” that God's people enjoyed on entering the promised land is here taken to symbolise the final reward prepared by God for his people.* *See also* HEB 4:1-4:2, HEB 4:6; JUD 1:5

### Exclusion from the people of God
**ROM 11:20** *See also* HEB 6:4-6:6

### Death in sin
**JHN 8:24** *See also* PSA 106:43; MRK 4:12; ACT 10:43

### God's wrath
**JHN 3:36** *See also* ROM 1:18, ROM 2:5, ROM 2:8

### Condemnation by God
**JHN 3:18** *See also* MRK 16:16; LUK 12:46; JHN 12:48; 2TH 2:12; REV 21:8
