# 7748: overseers

In the OT a variety of supervisory roles are mentioned. In the NT “overseers” (traditionally translated “bishops”) describes the role of local church leaders.

## Overseers in the OT


### In secular life
 [EXO 5:6](bibleref:02005006); [RUT 2:5-2:6](bibleref:08002005-08002006); [1KI 5:16](bibleref:11005016); [2CH 2:2](bibleref:14002002); [NEH 3:5](bibleref:16003005); [PRO 6:6-6:7](bibleref:20006006-20006007)

### In religious life
 [NUM 8:22](bibleref:04008022); [1CH 25:2](bibleref:13025002); [2CH 23:18](bibleref:14023018); [EZR 3:9](bibleref:15003009); [NEH 11:11](bibleref:16011011); [JER 20:1](bibleref:24020001)

## Overseers in the NT


### In secular life
 [MAT 20:8](bibleref:40020008)

### In the church
**[PHP 1:1](bibleref:50001001)** *A title for local church leaders.* 

### The role of local church ``elders''
**[ACT 20:28](bibleref:44020028)** *Here, as elsewhere in the NT, “elders” and “overseers” are virtually synonymous.*  *See also* [1PE 5:1-5:4](bibleref:60005001-60005004)
