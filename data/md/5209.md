# 5209: armour

Protective covering worn by soldiers to prevent injury in battle. However, those who wore it were not impregnable. Christians are to be equipped with spiritual armour.

## Coat of armour


### The breastplate
**[2CH 26:14](bibleref:14026014)** *See also* [1SA 17:5](bibleref:09017005), [1SA 17:38](bibleref:09017038); [2SA 20:8](bibleref:10020008); [EPH 6:14](bibleref:49006014)

### The belt
**[2SA 20:8](bibleref:10020008)** *The belt was an essential item of armour as it held the protective parts of the tunic in place.*  *See also* [1SA 18:4](bibleref:09018004); [2SA 18:11](bibleref:10018011); [EPH 6:14](bibleref:49006014)
