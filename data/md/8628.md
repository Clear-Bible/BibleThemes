# 8628: worship, hindrances to

True worship goes beyond mere form and can therefore be hindered by a wrong relationship to God or to others.



## Worship that is merely formal is unacceptable
**1SA 15:22** *See also* ISA 1:13; EZK 33:31; HOS 6:6; MAT 6:5; 2TI 3:5

## Worship is hindered by wrong relationships


### To God
**PSA 66:18**; **MAT 15:7-15:9** parallel MRK 7:6-7:7 *See also* ISA 29:13; PSA 32:5-32:6; ISA 59:2, ISA 64:7; JAS 4:3

### To others
**ISA 1:11-1:17**; **MAT 5:23-5:24** *See also* AMO 5:21-5:24
