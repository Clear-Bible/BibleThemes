# 5743: widows

God's tender concern for bereaved wives is declared throughout the whole of Scripture. He defends their rights and expects his people to do the same.



## God is the protector of widows
**PSA 68:5** *See also* DEU 10:18; PSA 146:9; PRO 15:25; JER 49:11

## Jesus Christ's concern for widows
 MRK 12:41-12:44 parallel LUK 21:1-21:4; ; LUK 7:11-7:15, LUK 18:1-18:5

## Widows are to be treated justly
**DEU 27:19** *See also* EXO 22:22-22:24; JOB 24:21-24:22; PSA 94:6; ISA 10:1-10:4; JER 22:3-22:5; EZK 22:25

## Widows are not to be exploited
**EXO 22:22** *See also* ISA 10:1-10:2; EZK 22:7; MAL 3:5; MRK 12:38-12:40 parallel LUK 20:45-20:47

## The law requires God's people to provide for widows
**DEU 24:19** *See also* DEU 14:28-14:29, DEU 16:11, DEU 24:17, DEU 24:20-24:21, DEU 26:12; ISA 1:17 *The prophet encourages God's people to return to the law.*

## The Christian community are to care for widows
**1TI 5:3** *See also* 1TI 5:4-5:16; 1CO 7:8-7:9; JAS 1:27

## Examples of widows and their treatment
 DEU 16:14; ; RUT 1:16-1:17; ; 1KI 17:13-17:14; ; JOB 24:3, JOB 29:13; ; LUK 2:36-2:38 *Anna*; ; LUK 4:25-4:26, LUK 7:11-7:17 *the widow of Nain*; ; ACT 6:1, ACT 9:36-9:41 *Dorcas*

## Women widowed because of God's judgment
**EXO 22:22-22:24** *See also* PSA 109:9; ISA 9:17; JER 15:8; LAM 5:3

## Laws governing the remarriage of widows


### The people generally
**DEU 25:5-25:10** *The OT emphasises continuity of the family line.* *See also* GEN 38:8; RUT 3:12 *Boaz refers to the law which gave the nearest male relative the duty of marrying the widow*; RUT 4:10; MRK 12:19 parallel LUK 20:28

### The priests
**LEV 21:13-21:14** *See also* EZK 44:22

## Widowhood as the personification of a city
**LAM 1:1** *A lament for desolate Jerusalem.* *See also* ISA 54:4-54:5 *a prophecy of the restoration of Jerusalem*; REV 18:7 *the boasts of Babylon*
