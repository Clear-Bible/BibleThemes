# 5805: comfort

The consolation and reassurance of those who are in distress, anxiety or need. Such comfort is an essential aspect of human relationships. Scripture declares that God comforts his people in times of distress.



## The comfort of friends and relations
**JOB 2:11**; **JHN 11:19** *See also* GEN 5:29, GEN 24:67, GEN 37:35; RUT 2:13; 2SA 12:24; 1CH 7:22; JOB 16:5, JOB 29:25, JOB 42:11; JHN 11:31; ACT 20:12

## Parental comfort
**ISA 66:13**; **1TH 2:11-2:12** *See also* ISA 66:11; JER 31:15; MAT 2:18

## God's comfort
**ISA 49:13**; **2CO 1:3-1:4** *See also* PSA 23:4, PSA 71:20-71:21, PSA 119:50, PSA 119:76; ISA 12:1, ISA 40:1, ISA 51:3, ISA 51:12, ISA 52:9, ISA 57:18, ISA 61:2; JER 8:18, JER 31:13; ZEC 1:3, ZEC 1:17; MAT 5:4; LUK 16:25; PHP 2:1

## Comfort as a specific work of the Holy Spirit
**JHN 14:16-14:17** *The title “Counsellor” may be translated “Comforter”.* *See also* ISA 11:2-11:3; JHN 15:26, JHN 16:7

## Comfort within the fellowship of believers
**2CO 1:5-1:7** *See also* 1CO 14:3; 2CO 2:7, 2CO 7:6-7:7; COL 4:11; HEB 12:12
