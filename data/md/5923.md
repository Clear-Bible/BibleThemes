# 5923: public opinion

The concepts and views held by the majority of the population. Scripture urges personal, independent thinking, based on God's word, rather than adherence to popular beliefs.

## God's people should not be concerned about public opinion
**[EXO 23:2](bibleref:02023002)** *See also* [JOB 31:33-31:34](bibleref:18031033-18031034); [ECC 7:21-7:22](bibleref:21007021-21007022)

## Examples of the impact of public opinion
 [1SA 14:45](bibleref:09014045) *Public opinion saved Jonathan's life* ; [1SA 15:24](bibleref:09015024) *Public opinion led to Saul losing his kingdom* ; [MAT 27:15-27:26](bibleref:40027015-40027026) *Public opinion led to Jesus Christ being crucified* ; [ACT 12:3](bibleref:44012003) *Public opinion led to Peter being imprisoned.* 

## Public opinion is often contrary to divine standards
**[HAG 1:2-1:11](bibleref:37001002-37001011)** *See also* [NUM 13:21-14:10](bibleref:04013021-04014010); [DEU 1:19-1:40](bibleref:05001019-05001040); [1SA 8:4-8:9](bibleref:09008004-09008009); [1KI 18:21](bibleref:11018021); [JER 2:31-2:35](bibleref:24002031-24002035), [JER 26:7-26:16](bibleref:24026007-24026016)

## Public opinion is sometimes in accord with divine standards
**[DEU 5:28-5:29](bibleref:05005028-05005029)** *See also* [JOS 24:19-24:24](bibleref:06024019-06024024)
