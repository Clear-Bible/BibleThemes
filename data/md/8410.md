# 8410: decision-making, examples of

Scripture provides examples of good and bad decision-making, as well as offering guidance as to how decisions ought to be made.



## Examples of good decision-making
**JOS 24:15** *See also* GEN 13:8-13:9, GEN 35:2-35:4; EXO 18:24-18:25, EXO 32:26; RUT 1:15-1:16; 1SA 14:6-14:7, 1SA 24:4-24:7; NEH 4:21-4:23; EST 4:15-4:16; PSA 27:4; DAN 1:8, DAN 3:18; MAT 4:19-4:20; MRK 2:14
LUK 10:38-10:42, LUK 19:8; ACT 4:36-4:37, ACT 5:35-5:39; HEB 11:25-11:26

## Examples of bad decision-making
**GEN 3:6** *See also* GEN 12:10-12:13, GEN 13:10-13:11, GEN 16:1-16:3; EXO 32:1-32:4; NUM 13:31, NUM 16:1-16:3; JOS 7:21 *Achan disobeyed the LORD's instructions*; JDG 8:23-8:27, JDG 14:3, JDG 16:1; 1SA 13:8-13:9 *Saul made the offering in Samuel's absence*; 2SA 11:2-11:4; 1KI 12:8-12:15 *Rehoboam followed bad advice*; 2KI 24:1; HOS 2:5; JON 1:1-1:3; MRK 10:22; LUK 15:13; ACT 5:1-5:2, ACT 24:27; MAT 26:14-26:16 parallels MRK 14:10-14:11, LUK 22:4-22:6 *Judas decided to betray Jesus Christ for money*; MRK 15:1 parallel MAT 27:1-27:2 *the decision to put Jesus Christ to death*
