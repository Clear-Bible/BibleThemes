# 6610: adoption, descriptions of

The words used by the NT to describe believers point to their status and privileges as the adopted children of God.



## Believers are no longer slaves but sons
**[GAL 4:7-4:9](bibleref:48004007-48004009)** *See also* [JHN 8:34-8:36](bibleref:43008034-43008036); [ROM 8:15](bibleref:45008015); [PHM 1:16](bibleref:57001016)

## Believers are children of God
**[1JN 3:1-3:2](bibleref:62003001-62003002)** *The title “child of God” is thought by some to be distinct from that of “son of God” (see below). “child” is thought to emphasise God's fatherhood, whilst “son” is thought to emphasise the Christian's privileges. These terms are however, sometimes used interchangeably.*  *See also* [LUK 20:36](bibleref:42020036); [ROM 8:21](bibleref:45008021); [PHP 2:15](bibleref:50002015); [1JN 3:2](bibleref:62003002), [1JN 5:2](bibleref:62005002)

## Believers are sons of God
**[ROM 8:14](bibleref:45008014)** *See also* [ROM 9:26](bibleref:45009026); [GAL 3:26](bibleref:48003026)

## Believers are children of the resurrection
 [LUK 20:36](bibleref:42020036)

## Believers are children of light, sons of light
 [JHN 12:36](bibleref:43012036); [EPH 5:8](bibleref:49005008); [1TH 5:5](bibleref:52005005)

## Believers are heirs of God
**[ROM 8:17](bibleref:45008017)** *See also* [GAL 3:29](bibleref:48003029), [GAL 4:7](bibleref:48004007); [TIT 3:7](bibleref:56003007); [HEB 6:17](bibleref:58006017)

## Believers are brothers of Jesus Christ
**[HEB 2:11-2:12](bibleref:58002011-58002012)** *See also* [MAT 12:48-12:50](bibleref:40012048-40012050); [ROM 8:29](bibleref:45008029); [HEB 2:17](bibleref:58002017)

## Believers are brothers of other believers
**[1JN 3:14](bibleref:62003014)** *See also* [1JN 4:19-4:20](bibleref:62004019-62004020); [REV 12:10](bibleref:66012010), [REV 19:10](bibleref:66019010)

## Believers are members of God's household, his family
**[EPH 2:19](bibleref:49002019)** *See also* [GAL 6:10](bibleref:48006010); [HEB 3:2-3:6](bibleref:58003002-58003006)
