# 5396: lordship, of Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ has authority and dominion over the church and the world on account of his divine status, which was publicly demonstrated through his resurrection from the dead and exaltation to the right hand of God.



## Lord as a title for Jesus Christ
**ROM 10:9** *In view of the OT background to the word “LORD” it is clear that this confession was not just of Jesus Christ's authority but also of his divinity*; **2CO 4:5** *See also* MRK 2:28 parallels MAT 12:8, LUK 6:5; JHN 13:13, JHN 20:28; ACT 9:17; 1CO 8:6, 1CO 12:3; EPH 4:5

## Jesus Christ is the Lord of the church
**COL 1:18** *See also* ROM 14:8; 1CO 5:4-5:5; 2CO 10:8, 2CO 13:10; EPH 1:22; COL 2:6

## Jesus Christ is Lord over all
**MAT 28:18**; **1PE 3:21-3:22** *See also* ACT 10:36; 1CO 15:24-15:25; EPH 1:10; COL 2:10

## The lordship of Jesus Christ is grounded in his resurrection from the dead
**ACT 2:36**; **ROM 14:9** *See also* JHN 13:3; ROM 1:4; EPH 1:20-1:22; PHP 2:6-2:11; HEB 1:3-1:4

### The exaltation of Jesus Christ to God's right hand
**HEB 1:13** *See also* PSA 110:1; MRK 12:36 parallels MAT 22:44, LUK 20:42-20:43; MRK 14:62 parallels MAT 26:64, LUK 22:69; ACT 2:33-2:35; 1CO 15:25; EPH 1:20; COL 3:1; HEB 1:3, HEB 8:1, HEB 10:12-10:13, HEB 12:2

## The coming of the Lord Jesus Christ will reveal his lordship
**PHP 3:20**; **JAS 5:7-5:8** *See also* 1CO 1:7, 1CO 4:5; PHP 4:5; 1TH 3:13, 1TH 4:15-4:17; 2TH 2:1; 1TI 6:14
