# 5178: running

Scripture gives pictures of running in both its literal and metaphorical senses, the image of the Christian life as a race being particularly significant.



## Running away


### Fleeing in battle
**EXO 23:27** *See also* LEV 26:36-26:37; JOS 7:12, JOS 8:3-8:19; 1SA 14:20-14:22

### Escaping from danger
**PRO 18:10** *See also* GEN 19:12-19:22; NEH 6:10-6:11; ISA 10:1-10:3; JER 17:16, JER 48:4-48:6, JER 51:6, JER 51:44-51:45; AMO 2:14-2:16; JHN 10:12-10:13

### Running away from individuals
**1SA 19:11-19:12** *See also* GEN 16:6-16:8, GEN 31:20-31:21, GEN 39:11-39:15; JHN 10:2-10:5

### Running away from God
 JON 1:1-1:10

### Running away from evil
