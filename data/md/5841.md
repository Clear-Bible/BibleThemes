# 5841: ecstasy

An exalted condition of spiritual joy or rapture; usually reflecting the experience of the Holy Spirit.



## Ecstasy in response to prophetic and visionary experiences
**NUM 24:4**; **2CO 12:3-12:4**; **REV 1:10** *See also* NUM 24:16; 1SA 10:10-10:11; ISA 6:1 *Isaiah's vision of the Lord*; EZK 11:24-11:25; DAN 2:19-2:21, DAN 10:7-10:8, DAN 10:16; ACT 9:10 *Ananias' vision*; ACT 10:3 *Cornelius' vision of the angel of God*; ACT 10:10-10:16, ACT 11:5; REV 4:2, REV 17:3, REV 21:10

## Ecstasy in response to the Holy Spirit
**ACT 2:4** *See also* ACT 10:44-10:46, ACT 13:52, ACT 19:6

## Ecstasy in response to Jesus Christ
**LUK 10:21**; **1PE 1:8** *See also* PSA 4:7, PSA 28:7; MAT 28:8

## Ecstasy as a corporate experience
**EZR 3:12** *See also* 1KI 8:11 parallel 2CH 7:2; EZR 6:16

## Transfiguration of appearance through ecstasy
**EXO 34:29** *See also* MAT 17:2; LUK 1:22

## Future ecstasy in response to God's coming kingdom
**ISA 55:12**; **1CO 2:9-2:10** *See also* ISA 35:10, ISA 51:11
