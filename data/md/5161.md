# 5161: kneeling

A bodily posture which expresses an attitude of humility, especially in worship, prayer or submission to the authority of another person.



## Kneeling in worship
**PSA 95:6** *See also* 2CH 7:3, 2CH 29:29

## Kneeling in submission


### To God
**ISA 45:23** *See also* ROM 14:11; PSA 22:29

### To Jesus Christ
**PHP 2:9-2:11** *See also* MAT 27:29 *kneeling in mock submission*; MRK 5:6, MRK 10:17; LUK 5:8

### To other people
**EST 3:2** *See also* 1KI 1:16; 2KI 1:13; MAT 18:26
