# 5259: coat

Various objects were given an outer coating to fit them for their particular purpose.



## An outer coating given to various objects


### An outer coat of pitch made wood and papyrus waterproof
**GEN 6:14** *See also* EXO 2:3

### An outer coat of plaster made stone suitable for writing on
**DEU 27:2-27:4**

### An outer coat of glaze rendered earthenware suitable for food use
**PRO 26:23**

## Coats of armour for protection
**2CH 26:14** *See also* 1SA 17:4-17:7, 1SA 17:38

## Coat used metaphorically
**JOB 41:13-41:17**
