# 6728: regeneration

The radical renewal of a person's inner being by the work of God's Spirit.

## The need for regeneration
**[JHN 3:3](bibleref:43003003)** *See also* [EPH 2:1](bibleref:49002001), [EPH 2:5](bibleref:49002005); [COL 2:13](bibleref:51002013)

## Regeneration is a work of God


### It originates in God the Father
**[JHN 1:12-1:13](bibleref:43001012-43001013)** *Regeneration cannot be controlled by human actions or rituals; it is an act of God's sovereign will.* 

### It is made possible by the resurrection of Jesus Christ
**[1PE 1:3](bibleref:60001003)** *See also* [EPH 2:4-2:5](bibleref:49002004-49002005)

### It occurs through the hearing of the Christian gospel
**[JAS 1:18](bibleref:59001018)** *See also* [1PE 1:23-1:25](bibleref:60001023-60001025) *The “word of God” is the good news about Jesus Christ.* 

### It is effected by God's Spirit
**[JHN 3:5-3:8](bibleref:43003005-43003008)** *See also* [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063); [TIT 3:5](bibleref:56003005)

## Regeneration is given to those who believe in Jesus Christ
**[1JN 5:1](bibleref:62005001)**

## Baptism is the sign of regeneration
**[JHN 3:5](bibleref:43003005)** *Water here is seen by some, though not all, interpreters as a reference to baptism, administered by the church to new converts as a sign of their new birth and their entry into the Christian community.*  *See also* [ACT 2:38-2:39](bibleref:44002038-44002039); [EPH 5:25-5:26](bibleref:49005025-49005026); [TIT 3:5](bibleref:56003005)

## The results of regeneration


### Entry into God's kingdom
**[JHN 3:5](bibleref:43003005)**

### A new holiness of life
**[1JN 3:9](bibleref:62003009)** *See also* [1JN 5:18](bibleref:62005018); [1PE 2:1-2:2](bibleref:60002001-60002002)

### Love for other people
**[1JN 4:7](bibleref:62004007)** *See also* [1JN 5:2](bibleref:62005002)

### Victory over the world's sinful pattern of life
**[1JN 5:4](bibleref:62005004)**
