# 1040: God, fatherhood of

Primarily signifying God's paternal relationship to Jesus Christ, the term also refers to God's fatherly relationship to his creation, especially to believers as the “children of God”.



## Aspects of the fatherhood of God


### He is the Creator and provider
**[1CO 8:6](bibleref:46008006)** *See also* [DEU 32:6](bibleref:05032006), [DEU 32:18](bibleref:05032018); [ISA 64:8](bibleref:23064008); [ACT 17:24-17:28](bibleref:44017024-44017028)

### He shows love and compassion
**[HOS 11:1](bibleref:28011001)** *See also* [PSA 103:13](bibleref:19103013); [JER 3:19](bibleref:24003019); [2CO 1:3](bibleref:47001003)

### He exercises his providence and care
**[PSA 68:5](bibleref:19068005)** *See also* [MAT 6:26](bibleref:40006026), [MAT 6:31-6:33](bibleref:40006031-40006033) parallel LUK 12:29-12:31; [MAT 7:11](bibleref:40007011) parallel LUK 11:13

### He disciplines and corrects
**[DEU 8:5](bibleref:05008005)** *See also* [PRO 3:11-3:12](bibleref:20003011-20003012); [HEB 12:5-12:6](bibleref:58012005-58012006)

## God as the Father of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 3:17](bibleref:40003017)** parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22 *See also* [MAT 11:27](bibleref:40011027); [LUK 2:49](bibleref:42002049); [JHN 5:17-5:18](bibleref:43005017-43005018), [JHN 17:11](bibleref:43017011), [JHN 20:17](bibleref:43020017); [ROM 15:6](bibleref:45015006); [EPH 1:3](bibleref:49001003)**[1PE 1:3](bibleref:60001003)** *See also* [1JN 1:3](bibleref:62001003)

## God is the father of Israel


### He cares for his covenant people
**[JER 31:9](bibleref:24031009)** *See also* [EXO 4:22](bibleref:02004022); [DEU 14:1](bibleref:05014001), [DEU 32:6](bibleref:05032006), [DEU 32:18](bibleref:05032018); [ISA 63:16](bibleref:23063016); [MAL 2:10](bibleref:39002010)

### He is the father of the kings of Israel
**[2SA 7:13-7:14](bibleref:10007013-10007014)** parallel 1CH 17:12-17:13 *This description of God's fatherhood involves the maintenance of a special relationship between God and David's descendants, which ultimately includes Jesus Christ.*  *See also* [PSA 2:7](bibleref:19002007), [PSA 89:26-89:27](bibleref:19089026-19089027)

## God is the father of Christian believers


### He has an intimate relationship with believers
**[MAT 6:9](bibleref:40006009)** parallel LUK 11:2 *See also* [JHN 20:17](bibleref:43020017); [2CO 6:18](bibleref:47006018); [GAL 3:26](bibleref:48003026); [1JN 2:13](bibleref:62002013)

### He adopts believers into his family
**[JHN 1:12-1:13](bibleref:43001012-43001013)** *See also* [ROM 8:14-8:17](bibleref:45008014-45008017); [GAL 4:5-4:7](bibleref:48004005-48004007); [1JN 3:1](bibleref:62003001)

## The fatherhood of God has implications for the church


### Character: children are to reproduce the Father's likeness
**[MAT 5:48](bibleref:40005048)**

### Unity: one Father means one family
**[EPH 4:3-4:6](bibleref:49004003-49004006)** *See also* [MAL 2:10](bibleref:39002010); [MAT 23:9](bibleref:40023009); [EPH 3:14-3:15](bibleref:49003014-49003015)

### Status: believers are related to Jesus Christ
**[MAT 12:50](bibleref:40012050)** parallels MRK 3:35, LUK 8:21 *See also* [ROM 8:29](bibleref:45008029); [HEB 2:11](bibleref:58002011)

### Inheritance: believers are heirs of the Father's kingdom
**[ROM 8:17](bibleref:45008017)** *See also* [LUK 12:32](bibleref:42012032); [GAL 3:29](bibleref:48003029); [COL 1:12](bibleref:51001012); [TIT 3:7](bibleref:56003007)

### Submission: believers are to revere the Father
**[1PE 1:17](bibleref:60001017)** *See also* [MAT 6:9](bibleref:40006009) parallel LUK 11:2; [EPH 3:14](bibleref:49003014)
