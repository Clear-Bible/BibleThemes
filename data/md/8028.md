# 8028: faith, as a body of beliefs

That body of essential truth to which Christians hold. It is revealed by God and is to be passed on by faithful teachers. Believers are urged to remain true to the faith and to contend for it.



## Faith as the truth that Christians believe
**GAL 1:23**; **1TI 2:7** *See also* ACT 6:7; EPH 4:5; 1TI 1:2; TIT 3:15

### Terms equivalent to ``the faith''
The truth:
 JHN 14:6; ; 1TI 3:15; ; 3JN 1:3; 
ACT 2:42 *the apostles' teaching*; GAL 1:6-1:9; ; PHP 1:7; 
2TI 1:13-1:14 *the good deposit*

### Aspects of ``the faith'' summarised
 ACT 2:22-2:24, ACT 2:32-2:33; ; 1CO 15:1-15:4; ; PHP 2:5-2:11; ; COL 1:15-1:20; ; 1TI 1:15, 1TI 3:16; ; 2TI 2:11-2:13; ; HEB 6:1-6:2

## The source and transmission of the faith


### The faith has been given by God
**GAL 1:11-1:12** *See also* JHN 1:17, JHN 8:40; 1CO 11:23; 2TI 3:16; 2PE 1:21

### The faith is passed on by apostles and teachers
**TIT 1:9** *See also* JHN 15:20; ACT 4:20; 2TI 2:2; 2PE 1:16

## Christians must remain true to the faith
**ACT 14:21-14:22**; **1CO 16:13** *See also* HEB 4:14-4:16

### Being strengthened in the faith
**PHP 1:25**; **COL 2:6-2:7** *See also* EPH 4:11-4:13; JUD 1:20

### The danger of turning from the faith
**MAT 24:10**; **HEB 6:6** *See also* ACT 13:8; 2CO 13:5-13:6; 1TI 5:8, 1TI 6:10, 1TI 6:20-6:21; HEB 2:1, HEB 3:12, HEB 10:35, HEB 12:25

## Christians must contend for the faith
**PHP 1:27**; **JUD 1:3**

### Refuting false teachers
 *See also* 1TI 4:1-4:6; 2TI 3:6-3:8; TIT 1:10-1:13; 2JN 1:7

### Witnessing to the truth

 PHP 1:7, PHP 1:15-1:16; ; 2TI 4:7; 
1TI 3:2 *overseers, who must be able to teach*; ; 1TI 3:9 *deacons*; ; 1TI 6:12 *Timothy, responsible for the church at Ephesus*
