# 5974: value

God places inestimable value on the human soul. He paid the redemption price of the blood of his son Jesus Christ to purchase his own people who are precious to him. Everything that achieves or maintains this relationship is to be valued.



## Redemptive values of people, animals and property
 LEV 27:1-27:25 *These are the values for recovering or buying back what has been dedicated to God.  They were designed to teach the Israelites the great cost of redemption.*

## The value of Jesus Christ


### The betrayal price
**MAT 26:14-26:15** parallels MRK 14:10-14:11, LUK 22:3-22:6 *See also* JER 32:6-32:9; ZEC 11:12-11:13; MAT 27:9

### His true value
**PHP 3:8** *See also* MAT 13:44-13:46; 1PE 2:4-2:7

## The value of people


### They are made in the image of God
**GEN 1:26-1:27** *Being made in God's image gives humanity intrinsic worth and dignity.* *See also* PSA 8:3-8:8; COL 3:10; HEB 2:6-2:8

### The inestimable value of a human soul
**PSA 49:7-49:8** *See also* MAT 16:26 parallel MRK 8:37

### The redemptive price of a soul
**1PE 1:18-1:19** *See also* MAT 20:28 parallel MRK 10:45; ACT 20:28; 1CO 6:20, 1CO 7:23; REV 5:9

### The value of God's people to him
**1PE 2:4** *See also* PSA 116:15; ISA 43:3-43:4; MAT 6:26 parallel LUK 12:24; MAT 10:31 parallel LUK 12:7; MAT 12:12
