# 7758: preachers, call of

God declares his word through called and anointed preachers. He overcomes their reluctance, helps their weakness, authorises their message and confirms the truth of what they declare.



## God's call of preachers


### It is not dependent upon background
**[AMO 7:14-7:15](bibleref:30007014-30007015)** *See also* [JER 1:1-1:5](bibleref:24001001-24001005); [MAT 4:18-4:20](bibleref:40004018-40004020) parallels MRK 1:16-1:19, LUK 5:1-5:11; [ACT 9:10-9:16](bibleref:44009010-44009016)

### It is not dependent upon ability
**[EXO 4:10-4:12](bibleref:02004010-02004012)** *See also* [JER 1:6-1:9](bibleref:24001006-24001009); [ACT 4:13](bibleref:44004013)
