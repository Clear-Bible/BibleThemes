# 8276: humility

An attitude of lowliness and obedience, grounded in the recognition of one's status before God as his creatures.



## God commands humility
**MIC 6:8**; **JAS 4:10**; **1PE 3:8** *See also* EXO 10:3; PRO 16:19; ISA 57:15, ISA 58:5; ZEP 2:3; LUK 14:9-14:11; ROM 12:3 *sober assessment of oneself*; 1CO 1:28; EPH 4:2; COL 3:12; TIT 3:2; JAS 3:13; 1PE 5:5

## God exalts the humble
**LUK 1:52** *See also* 2SA 7:8; 1KI 14:7
