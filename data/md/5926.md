# 5926: rebuke

A verbal expression of blame. Scripture gives examples of both divine and human rebuke. Sometimes God issues rebukes directly but more commonly he did so through the OT prophets. In the NT Jesus Christ and the apostles issue rebukes and teach the church how to address those who sin.



## God has an absolute right to rebuke those who depart from his ways


### He sometimes exercises this right directly
**LEV 26:23-26:24** *See also* DEU 28:20; NEH 9:30; JOB 5:17; PSA 39:11, PSA 50:21, PSA 94:12, PSA 105:14, PSA 119:21; JER 2:19, JER 2:30; HEB 8:8

### His rebuke is powerful
 JOB 26:11; ; PSA 80:16, PSA 104:7; ; ISA 50:2

## In the OT much rebuke came indirectly
Balaam's ass rebukes Balaam:
 NUM 22:28; ; 2PE 2:16; 
1SA 2:25 *Eli rebukes his sons*; ; 1SA 15:12-15:26 *Samuel rebukes Saul*; ; 1SA 24:5-24:7 *David rebukes his men*; ; 2SA 12:1-12:12 *Nathan rebukes David*; ; 1KI 18:18 *Elijah rebukes Ahab*; ; EZR 10:9-10:11 *Ezra rebukes the men of Judah and Benjamin*; ; ISA 1:10-1:17 *Isaiah rebukes Judah and Jerusalem*; ; HOS 4:1-4:3 *Hosea rebukes the Israelites.*

## Examples of Jesus Christ issuing rebukes


### To evil spirits
**MAT 17:18** parallels MRK 9:25, LUK 9:42 *See also* LUK 4:41

### To his disciples
 MRK 8:33 parallel MAT 16:23; ; MRK 16:14

## Other examples of rebuke being administered in the NT
Inappropriate rebukes by Jesus Christ's disciples:
 MAT 16:22 parallel MRK 8:32; ; MAT 19:13 parallels MRK 10:13, LUK 18:15; 
LUK 3:19 *John the Baptist rebukes Herod*; ; LUK 23:40-23:41 *The repentant thief on the cross rebukes the other crucified criminal.*
