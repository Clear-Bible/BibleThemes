# 5442: pilgrimage

A journey made to a holy or revered place as an act of devotion or duty. As well as Jerusalem, there were several such holy places in Israel.



## The annual pilgrimages
**EXO 34:23-34:24**; **LUK 2:41-2:42** *See also* EXO 23:14, EXO 23:17; DEU 16:16, DEU 31:11; 2CH 5:2-5:3, 2CH 30:1, 2CH 30:13; LAM 1:4; EZK 46:9; ZEC 14:16-14:19; JHN 5:1, JHN 7:14
