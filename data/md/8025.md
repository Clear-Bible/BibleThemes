# 8025: faith, origins of

Faith is a gift from God himself and is not to be seen as the result of human striving or achievement. Faith is inspired by the word and works of God.



## Faith is a gift from God
**JHN 6:63-6:65**; **EPH 2:8-2:9** *See also* MAT 16:15-16:17; MRK 9:24; LUK 17:5; ACT 3:16, ACT 14:27, ACT 18:27; ROM 12:3; 1CO 4:7, 1CO 12:9; PHP 1:29; JAS 2:5

## Faith comes through God's word


### Faith following a direct word from God
 HEB 11:29-11:30; ; EXO 14:15 *Moses receives God's instructions at the Red Sea*; ; JOS 6:2-6:5 *Joshua receives God's instructions concerning Jericho*; ; ACT 27:23-27:25

### Faith through the Scriptures
 JHN 2:22, JHN 20:30-20:31; ; 2TI 3:15

### Faith comes through hearing God's word preached
**ROM 10:14-10:17** *See also* ISA 52:7, ISA 53:1; JHN 1:7, JHN 4:41-4:42, JHN 17:20; ACT 11:19-11:21; 1CO 2:4-2:5

## Faith comes through a personal encounter with Jesus Christ
 JHN 9:35-9:38, JHN 20:26-20:28

## Faith comes through witnessing miracles
**JHN 14:11** *See also* JHN 2:11 *the miracle at the wedding feast*; JHN 4:53 *Jesus Christ heals an official's son.*JHN 11:45, JHN 12:11
ACT 9:42 *the raising of Tabitha*

## Faith based on knowledge of God


### Knowledge of God's faithfulness leads to faith
 PSA 46:1-46:3; ; LAM 3:19-3:24; ; NAM 1:7; ; ACT 2:25-2:26

### Knowledge of God's achievements leads to faith
 DEU 3:21-3:22; ; 1SA 17:34-17:37; ; JER 14:22
