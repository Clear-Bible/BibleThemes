# 5732: polygamy

The practice of having more than one wife at one time. Although not explicitly forbidden in Scripture as a general practice, it is presented as problematic and monogamy is the clearly preferred option.

## Monogamy is God's intention for marriage
**[GEN 2:24](bibleref:01002024)** *See also* [JER 2:2](bibleref:24002002); [HOS 2:19-2:20](bibleref:28002019-28002020); [EPH 5:31](bibleref:49005031)

## Early examples of polygamy
**[GEN 4:19](bibleref:01004019)** *See also* [GEN 26:34](bibleref:01026034), [GEN 28:9](bibleref:01028009), [GEN 29:28](bibleref:01029028)

## Reasons for the practice of polygamy


### Sealing foreign treaties
**[1KI 3:1](bibleref:11003001)** *See also* [1KI 16:31](bibleref:11016031)

### Producing offspring
**[GEN 16:1-16:3](bibleref:01016001-01016003)**; **[2CH 13:21](bibleref:14013021)** *A large family, and especially numerous progeny, was regarded as a sign of divine blessing.*  *See also* [GEN 30:3-30:5](bibleref:01030003-01030005), [GEN 30:9](bibleref:01030009); [JDG 8:30](bibleref:07008030); [2SA 3:1-3:5](bibleref:10003001-10003005); [1CH 7:4](bibleref:13007004); [2CH 11:23](bibleref:14011023), [2CH 24:2-24:3](bibleref:14024002-14024003)

## Problems associated with polygamy


### Being led away from God
**[DEU 17:17](bibleref:05017017)** *It was common for kings to cement foreign alliances by marriage. Israel's king is warned against the practice* ; **[1KI 11:1-11:8](bibleref:11011001-11011008)** *See also* [NEH 13:25-13:26](bibleref:16013025-16013026); [MAL 2:11](bibleref:39002011)

### Conflicts within families
**[GEN 29:30](bibleref:01029030), [GEN 30:1](bibleref:01030001)**; **[DEU 21:15-21:17](bibleref:05021015-05021017)** *See also* [GEN 16:4-16:6](bibleref:01016004-01016006) *Sarah and Hagar* ; [1SA 1:4-1:7](bibleref:09001004-09001007) *Hannah and Peninnah* ; [2CH 11:21-11:22](bibleref:14011021-14011022); [1KI 1:11-1:13](bibleref:11001011-11001013) *the conflict over the succession to David's throne*

 [GEN 31:50](bibleref:01031050); [EXO 21:10](bibleref:02021010); [LEV 18:18](bibleref:03018018)

## Polygamy forbidden
 [1CO 7:2](bibleref:46007002); [1TI 3:2](bibleref:54003002) *Polygamy is expressly forbidden for church leaders.*
