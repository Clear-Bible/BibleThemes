# 4534: vine

A plant on which grapes grow, cultivated primarily for making wine. Jesus Christ described himself as the true vine, the source of life and growth for all believers.



## The vine as an image of peace and security
**1KI 4:25** *See also* 2KI 18:31 parallel ISA 36:16; MIC 4:4; ZEC 3:10

## Laws relating to the cultivation of vines
**LEV 25:1-25:5** *See also* LEV 25:8-25:12

## Wild vines
**JER 2:21** *See also* 2KI 4:38-4:40

## The destruction of vines as punishment
**JER 8:13** *See also* PSA 78:42-78:47, PSA 105:27-105:33, PSA 80:16; ISA 7:23, ISA 16:8-16:9, ISA 17:10-17:11, ISA 24:5-24:7, ISA 32:9-32:13; JER 5:14-5:17; HOS 2:12; NAM 2:2; HAG 2:19

**ZEC 8:12** *See also* DEU 8:7-8:8; JOL 2:22; MAL 3:11

**GEN 49:10-49:12** *See also* GEN 40:9-40:13

## Vines in parables
**JDG 9:12-9:13** *See also* ISA 5:1-5:2; EZK 17:1-17:10; JON 4:5-4:11
