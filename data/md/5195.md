# 5195: veil

Generally, an item of female dress worn by an unmarried woman in the presence of her future husband and also by a prostitute. Head-coverings were worn by men and women as a sign of mourning. The term is also used metaphorically to indicate a lack of understanding of God's truth. Scripture uses the imagery of “removing a veil” to refer to God's final self-revelation at the end of the age, or the making known of God's will and nature through Jesus Christ or other means.



## The veil as an item of female dress


### Worn by women in general
**SNG 4:1** *The veil covered the lower part of the face, so focusing attention on the eyes and temples.* *See also* GEN 24:65 *Rebekah veils herself in the presence of her future husband, Isaac*; SNG 4:3, SNG 6:7

### Worn by prostitutes
**GEN 38:14-38:15** *See also* SNG 1:7

### Worn by female sorcerers
 EZK 13:18, EZK 13:21

### Worn as a sign of wealth and status
 ISA 3:19, ISA 47:2 *Babylon's veil will be removed as a mark of her humiliation.*

## Veils worn as a sign of grief


### Faces covered in sorrow and dismay
**2SA 15:30** *See also* LEV 13:45 *A veil worn by the ceremonially unclean signifies grief at separation from God*; JER 14:4

### Veils customarily worn by mourners
**2SA 19:4** *See also* EZK 24:17, EZK 24:22

### Faces covered as a sign of disgrace
 EST 6:12, EST 7:8; ; MIC 3:7

## Head-coverings in worship
**1CO 11:4** *A man's uncovered head in worship signifies submission to his spiritual head, i.e., Jesus Christ*; **1CO 11:5-11:6** *A woman's covered head signifies proper submission to her husband.* *See also* 1CO 11:7, 1CO 11:13

## God's sight of humanity is not veiled


### The wicked foolishly boast that God does not see them
**JOB 22:12-22:14** *See also* PSA 10:11, PSA 94:7; ISA 29:15

### God's people complain that God does not see their suffering
**PSA 44:24** *See also* JOB 13:24; PSA 69:17, PSA 88:14

### God sees all humanity
**JOB 28:24** *See also* GEN 16:13; JOB 34:21; PSA 33:13, PSA 139:1-139:3

### People's deeds will be made known
**1CO 4:5** *See also* JOB 28:11; ISA 26:21; MAT 10:26 parallel LUK 12:2; 1CO 3:13

## The truth about God is sometimes veiled


### The veil worn by Moses
**2CO 3:13** *After speaking with God, Moses' face was radiant; the veil, possibly a loose part of his clothing pulled over his face, prevented the Israelites from seeing that the radiance did not last.* *See also* EXO 34:29-34:35

### Lack of understanding of God's truth
**2CO 3:14-3:15** *See also* ISA 44:18; LAM 3:65; 1CO 2:14; 2CO 4:3; EPH 4:18

## The unveiling of God's truth


### God's hidden truth is revealed to believers
**1CO 2:7-2:10**; **COL 1:26** *See also* ISA 64:4; ROM 16:25-16:26; 2CO 3:16-3:18; EPH 1:9, EPH 3:3-3:6; 2TI 1:10; TIT 1:2-1:3

### God's self-revelation at the end of the age
**DAN 12:2-12:4** *See also* ISA 25:7-25:8; ROM 8:19; 1JN 3:2
