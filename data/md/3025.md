# 3025: Holy Spirit, personality of

The Holy Spirit is not an impersonal power but a real person with his own personality.



## The Holy Spirit is involved in personal relationships


### He is in relationship with the Father and the Son
**[MAT 28:19](bibleref:40028019)** *See also* [JHN 16:14-16:15](bibleref:43016014-43016015); [2CO 13:14](bibleref:47013014); [1PE 1:2](bibleref:60001002); [JUD 1:20-1:21](bibleref:65001020-65001021)

### He is in relationship with Christians
**[ACT 15:28](bibleref:44015028)**

### The Spirit can be treated in personal ways
**[ACT 5:3](bibleref:44005003)** *See also* [ACT 5:9](bibleref:44005009); [HEB 10:29](bibleref:58010029)

## Personal characteristics of the Holy Spirit


### The Spirit is wise
**[ISA 11:2](bibleref:23011002)**; **[EPH 1:17](bibleref:49001017)** *See also* [JHN 14:26](bibleref:43014026); [ROM 8:27](bibleref:45008027)

### The Spirit can experience emotion and pain
**[EPH 4:30](bibleref:49004030)** *See also* [ISA 63:10](bibleref:23063010); [HEB 10:29](bibleref:58010029)
