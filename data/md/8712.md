# 8712: denial of Jesus Christ

The making of a conscious, informed decision not to accept Jesus Christ or his claims and teaching.



## Jesus Christ was denied by the Jewish people
**JHN 1:11** *See also* MAT 11:16-11:19 parallel LUK 7:31-7:35; MAT 13:54-13:58 parallel MRK 6:1-6:6; MAT 26:63-26:68 parallels MRK 14:61-14:65, LUK 22:66-22:71; LUK 4:16-4:30; JHN 5:37-5:43; ACT 4:8-4:12; PSA 118:22

## The consequences of denying Jesus Christ


### Denying Jesus Christ results in judgment
**JHN 12:48** *See also* MAT 12:38-12:42 parallel LUK 11:29-11:32

### Denying Jesus Christ means replacement by others
**ACT 13:46** *See also* MAT 21:40-21:41 parallels MRK 12:9, LUK 20:15-20:16; ACT 18:5-18:6

### To deny Christ is to be denied by Christ
**2TI 2:12** *See also* MAT 10:32; MRK 8:38

## Warnings against those who deny Jesus Christ
**2PE 2:1** *See also* 1JN 2:22-2:23; 2JN 1:7; JUD 1:4

## Denial, repentance and restoration: the example of Peter


### Peter's denial predicted by Jesus Christ
**MRK 14:29-14:30** parallels MAT 26:31-26:34, LUK 22:31-22:34

### Peter declares he will never deny Jesus Christ
**MRK 14:31** parallel MAT 26:35

### Peter denied that he knew Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:69-26:75** parallels MRK 14:66-14:72, LUK 22:55-22:62, JHN 18:15-18:18, JHN 18:25-18:27

 *See also* JHN 21:15-21:19; ACT 1:15-1:22, ACT 2:14-2:41
