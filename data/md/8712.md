# 8712: denial of Jesus Christ

The making of a conscious, informed decision not to accept Jesus Christ or his claims and teaching.

## Jesus Christ was denied by the Jewish people
**[JHN 1:11](bibleref:43001011)** *See also* [MAT 11:16-11:19](bibleref:40011016-40011019) parallel LUK 7:31-7:35; [MAT 13:54-13:58](bibleref:40013054-40013058) parallel MRK 6:1-6:6; [MAT 26:63-26:68](bibleref:40026063-40026068) parallels MRK 14:61-14:65, LUK 22:66-22:71; [LUK 4:16-4:30](bibleref:42004016-42004030); [JHN 5:37-5:43](bibleref:43005037-43005043); [ACT 4:8-4:12](bibleref:44004008-44004012); [PSA 118:22](bibleref:19118022)

## The consequences of denying Jesus Christ


### Denying Jesus Christ results in judgment
**[JHN 12:48](bibleref:43012048)** *See also* [MAT 12:38-12:42](bibleref:40012038-40012042) parallel LUK 11:29-11:32

### Denying Jesus Christ means replacement by others
**[ACT 13:46](bibleref:44013046)** *See also* [MAT 21:40-21:41](bibleref:40021040-40021041) parallels MRK 12:9, LUK 20:15-20:16; [ACT 18:5-18:6](bibleref:44018005-44018006)

### To deny Christ is to be denied by Christ
