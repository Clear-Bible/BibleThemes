# 5794: asceticism

Rigorous self-denial of bodily pleasures and needs. This can be motivated either by a praiseworthy desire to dedicate oneself completely to God, or by an erroneous belief that the physical body is evil.

## Types of ascetic practice


### The ascetic practice of fasting
**[EST 4:15-4:16](bibleref:17004015-17004016)** *See also* [JDG 20:26](bibleref:07020026); [2SA 1:11-1:12](bibleref:10001011-10001012); [JER 36:5-36:6](bibleref:24036005-24036006); [LUK 2:37](bibleref:42002037)

### The ascetic practice of wearing sackcloth and ashes
**[DAN 9:3](bibleref:27009003)** *See also* [1KI 21:27](bibleref:11021027); [NEH 9:1](bibleref:16009001); [EST 4:3](bibleref:17004003)
