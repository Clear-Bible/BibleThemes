# 8764: forgetting God

Scripture warns people how easy it is to forget God in the varying circumstances of life and to fail to give him his due place, and points out the consequences of so doing.



## Warnings not to forget God


### Forgetting God's past acts
**DEU 4:9** *See also* DEU 9:7-9:8; ISA 51:12-51:13

### Forgetting God's covenant
**DEU 4:23** *See also* 2KI 17:38

### Forgetting God's ways
**ISA 55:6-55:8** *See also* DEU 18:9-18:13; 1KI 2:1-2:4

## Reasons why people forget God


### Easy circumstances
**DEU 6:10-6:12** *See also* DEU 8:10-8:14

### Idolatry
**JDG 8:33-8:34** *See also* EXO 32:1-32:8; DEU 12:29-12:31; 1KI 11:4-11:6

### Pride and stubbornness
**NEH 9:16** *See also* DEU 9:12-9:13; 2KI 17:14-17:17; JER 7:22-7:26

## Consequences of people forgetting God


### Spiritual adultery
**HOS 1:2** *See also* JER 3:6-3:10; HOS 4:10-4:13, HOS 5:4

### Social decay
**HOS 4:1-4:3**

### The destruction of the nation
**DEU 8:19-8:20** *See also* DEU 6:14-6:15

### The death of the sinner
**PSA 9:17** *See also* JOB 8:11-8:13; PSA 50:22; ISA 65:11-65:12

## Examples of people forgetting God
 JDG 8:33-8:34; ; 2KI 17:7-17:20; ; PSA 78:40-78:43, PSA 106:19-106:22; ; ISA 1:2-1:3; ; JER 3:19-3:21

## Exhortations and resolve not to forget God
 PSA 103:2, PSA 119:61, PSA 119:109

## God's provision to help people not to forget him


### His word
**PSA 78:5-78:7** *See also* DEU 31:24-31:27; JER 20:9

### Covenantal meals and festivals
 DEU 16:1-16:3, DEU 16:9-16:12; ; 1CO 11:23-11:26

### External signs
 GEN 9:12-9:17, GEN 17:9-17:13; ; NUM 15:37-15:39; ; DEU 11:18-11:20

## God's challenge to those who forget him
**HOS 4:1** *See also* ISA 57:11; JER 2:31-2:32; HOS 2:2-2:13

## God's call to those who forget him
**JER 3:19-3:22** *See also* REV 2:4-2:5, REV 3:20

## God's grace even when his people do forget him
**ISA 49:15-49:16** *See also* DEU 4:25-4:31; NEH 9:29-9:31
