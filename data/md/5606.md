# 5606: warfare, nature of

Warfare is characteristic of a fallen world. Scripture describes how some wars are justified and others are not.



## Warfare is characteristic of a fallen world


### It is an aspect of life in a fallen world
 [EXO 17:16](bibleref:02017016); [2SA 3:1](bibleref:10003001); [1KI 14:30](bibleref:11014030)

### It is caused by sin
**[JAS 4:1-4:2](bibleref:59004001-59004002)** *See also* [PSA 140:1-140:2](bibleref:19140001-19140002); [PRO 10:12](bibleref:20010012), [PRO 29:22](bibleref:20029022); [1CO 3:3](bibleref:46003003)

### It is horrific in nature
**[NAM 3:3](bibleref:34003003)** *See also* [2CH 25:12](bibleref:14025012); [ZEC 14:2](bibleref:38014002)

## Warfare as a sign of the end times
**[MAT 24:6-24:7](bibleref:40024006-40024007)** parallels MRK 13:7-13:8, LUK 21:9-21:10

## Sometimes God forbids war
**[DEU 1:41-1:42](bibleref:05001041-05001042)** *See also* [1KI 12:24](bibleref:11012024); [1CH 22:8](bibleref:13022008); [PSA 68:30](bibleref:19068030), [PSA 120:7](bibleref:19120007)

## Sometimes God commands war
**[JOS 8:1](bibleref:06008001)** *See also* [NUM 31:7](bibleref:04031007); [DEU 20:1-20:4](bibleref:05020001-05020004); [JOS 10:40](bibleref:06010040); [JDG 6:16](bibleref:07006016); [PSA 144:1](bibleref:19144001); [ISA 13:3-13:4](bibleref:23013003-23013004)

## Warfare is sometimes a means of judgment
**[1SA 15:2](bibleref:09015002)** *See also* [2KI 21:10-21:15](bibleref:12021010-12021015), [2KI 24:2-24:4](bibleref:12024002-12024004); [ISA 10:12-10:19](bibleref:23010012-23010019); [JER 4:14-4:18](bibleref:24004014-24004018)

## God's will that warfare will finally cease
 [PSA 46:9](bibleref:19046009); [ISA 2:4](bibleref:23002004); [MIC 4:3](bibleref:33004003)
