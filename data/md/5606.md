# 5606: warfare, nature of

Warfare is characteristic of a fallen world. Scripture describes how some wars are justified and others are not.



## Warfare is characteristic of a fallen world


### It is an aspect of life in a fallen world
 EXO 17:16; ; 2SA 3:1; ; 1KI 14:30

### It is caused by sin
**JAS 4:1-4:2** *See also* PSA 140:1-140:2; PRO 10:12, PRO 29:22; 1CO 3:3

### It is horrific in nature
**NAM 3:3** *See also* 2CH 25:12; ZEC 14:2

## Warfare as a sign of the end times
**MAT 24:6-24:7** parallels MRK 13:7-13:8, LUK 21:9-21:10

## Sometimes God forbids war
**DEU 1:41-1:42** *See also* 1KI 12:24; 1CH 22:8; PSA 68:30, PSA 120:7

## Sometimes God commands war
**JOS 8:1** *See also* NUM 31:7; DEU 20:1-20:4; JOS 10:40; JDG 6:16; PSA 144:1; ISA 13:3-13:4

## Warfare is sometimes a means of judgment
**1SA 15:2** *See also* 2KI 21:10-21:15, 2KI 24:2-24:4; ISA 10:12-10:19; JER 4:14-4:18

## God's will that warfare will finally cease
 PSA 46:9; ; ISA 2:4; ; MIC 4:3
