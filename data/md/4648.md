# 4648: goad

A spike used to drive animals on. The term is also figuratively applied to prompting and guiding people into action.

## The goad as a farming implement


### Used with animals
 [1SA 13:21](bibleref:09013021)

### Used as a weapon
 [JDG 3:31](bibleref:07003031)

## Goading as a symbol of discipline


### God's word as a goad
**[ECC 12:11](bibleref:21012011)** *See also* [ACT 2:37](bibleref:44002037)

### Conscience as a goad
**[ACT 26:14](bibleref:44026014)** *See also* [EXO 25:2](bibleref:02025002); [JOB 20:2](bibleref:18020002); [ROM 2:15](bibleref:45002015)

### Encouragement as a goad
**[HEB 10:24](bibleref:58010024)** *See also* [1TH 1:3](bibleref:52001003) *The promptings of love and faith may be seen as symbolic goads.*
