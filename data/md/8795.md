# 8795: persecution, nature of

Persecution by the world and secular powers is a likely consequence of faith. Scripture identifies a number of potential sources of such persistent harassment.



## Persecution is characteristic of this world
**PSA 10:2** *See also* AMO 1:6, AMO 1:9, AMO 2:1; HAB 1:6-1:11; REV 6:8, REV 9:7-9:10

## Satan is the arch-persecutor of the church
**GEN 3:15** *See also* JOB 2:6-2:7; ZEC 3:1; LUK 22:3-22:4; 1PE 5:8; REV 2:10, REV 2:13

## The agencies of persecution


### The world
**JHN 15:18**; **1JN 3:13** *See also* MRK 12:7; JHN 5:18, JHN 15:19, JHN 17:14; ACT 14:22; 1TH 2:14-2:15; 1JN 3:1

### Earthly governments
**MRK 13:9** *See also* 1KI 19:2; NEH 6:1, NEH 6:9; EST 3:6; PSA 119:161; JER 38:6; DAN 3:13-3:17; MAT 10:17; ACT 12:1-12:3

### Religious authorities
**1TH 2:13-2:16** *See also* JER 26:8; AMO 7:12-7:13; MAT 23:34-23:37; ACT 4:27-4:29, ACT 5:17-5:18, ACT 22:4REV 13:7, REV 13:15, REV 17:6

### Family and friends
**MAT 10:36** *See also* MIC 7:6; GEN 4:8; MAT 10:21; MRK 3:21; GAL 4:29

## The experience of persecution
**2CO 12:10** *See also* 1SA 24:14; PSA 59:1-59:4, PSA 64:1-64:4; 2CO 4:8-4:12, 2CO 11:23-11:26; HEB 11:32-11:38

## Believers must expect persecution
**2TI 3:12** *See also* MAT 13:21; MRK 10:29-10:30; 1TH 3:4
