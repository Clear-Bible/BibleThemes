# 8220: courage, in facing enemies

In the OT, courage is shown principally in the leading of God's people against their enemies.



## God commands his people to be courageous
**JOS 1:6-1:9** *The LORD to Joshua as he was about to lead the Israelites into Canaan.* *See also* JOS 1:18, JOS 8:1, JOS 10:8; JDG 7:9-7:11; 2CH 20:15, 2CH 20:17

## Recommended by leaders
**DEU 1:21** *See also* DEU 31:1-31:8; JOS 10:25, JOS 23:6; 2SA 10:12; 2CH 32:7

## Seen in the behaviour of people of God
**1SA 17:32**; **ACT 4:13** *See also* JOS 1:10-1:11, JOS 14:12; 1SA 14:6-14:7, 1SA 17:45-17:47; ACT 13:44-13:47 *Paul and Barnabas*; ACT 19:8-19:10 *Paul at Ephesus*
