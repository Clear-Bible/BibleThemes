# 5782: agony

Intense suffering of body, mind or spirit, brought about by illness, death or the judgment of God.



## Examples of agony


### Agony of body
**PSA 6:2** *See also* PSA 31:10, PSA 42:10; ISA 21:3; JHN 16:21; REV 9:5, REV 16:10

### Agony of heart
**JER 4:19** *See also* DEU 2:25; 1SA 1:16 *Hannah prays for a son*; JOB 6:2, JOB 7:11; PSA 6:3, PSA 25:17, PSA 31:7, PSA 38:8, PSA 55:4; ISA 65:14; MAT 26:38; MRK 14:33; LUK 22:44 *Jesus Christ prays on the Mount of Olives*; ROM 9:2; 2CO 2:4

## Causes of agony


### Illness
**JER 15:18** *See also* JOB 30:16-30:17; PSA 6:2-6:3, PSA 38:7, PSA 69:29

### Death
**ACT 2:24** *See also* JOB 26:5; PSA 18:4-18:6, PSA 42:10, PSA 55:4, PSA 116:3

### The judgment of God
**LUK 16:24-16:25** *See also* JER 4:19; EZK 30:16; MIC 4:10; ZEC 9:5; MAT 22:13, MAT 24:51, MAT 25:30
