## Jesus Christ's resurrection body


### It was different from his pre-crucifixion body
**[JHN 20:26](bibleref:43020026)** *This suggests that the resurrected body of Jesus Christ was not restricted by natural laws: closed doors provided no barrier to Jesus Christ's bodily appearance to his disciples.*  *See also* [MRK 16:12](bibleref:41016012) *The failure to recognise Jesus Christ may be an indication of his changed appearance but also might emphasise the disciples' own sorrow and lack of faith* ; [JHN 20:14](bibleref:43020014), [JHN 20:19](bibleref:43020019) parallel LUK 24:36

### It was a body of flesh and blood
**[LUK 24:39](bibleref:42024039)** *See also* [LUK 24:42-24:43](bibleref:42024042-42024043); [JHN 20:20](bibleref:43020020)
