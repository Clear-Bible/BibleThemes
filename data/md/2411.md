# 2411: cross, predictions of

The death of Jesus Christ fulfils OT predictions and was clearly anticipated in his teaching.



## The cross foreshadowed in the OT
**[PSA 22:1](bibleref:19022001)** *See also* [GEN 3:15](bibleref:01003015); [PSA 22:6-22:7](bibleref:19022006-19022007), [PSA 22:16-22:18](bibleref:19022016-19022018), [PSA 31:5](bibleref:19031005), [PSA 69:21](bibleref:19069021); [ISA 52:12-52:13](bibleref:23052012-23052013); [ZEC 12:10](bibleref:38012010), [ZEC 13:7](bibleref:38013007)

## Jesus Christ predicts his death on the cross


### Jesus Christ's first announcement of his death
**[MAT 16:21](bibleref:40016021)** parallels MRK 8:31, LUK 9:22

### Jesus Christ's second announcement of his death
**[MAT 17:22-17:23](bibleref:40017022-40017023)** *See also* [MRK 9:31](bibleref:41009031)

### Jesus Christ's third announcement of his death
**[MAT 20:18-20:19](bibleref:40020018-40020019)** parallels MRK 10:33-10:34, LUK 18:31-18:33

## Other allusions to the cross


### In the teaching of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 26:31](bibleref:40026031)** *See also* [MAT 20:22](bibleref:40020022) parallel MRK 10:38; [MAT 26:2](bibleref:40026002); [MRK 2:19-2:20](bibleref:41002019-41002020), [MRK 10:45](bibleref:41010045); [LUK 9:44](bibleref:42009044), [LUK 22:42](bibleref:42022042)[JHN 3:14](bibleref:43003014), [JHN 8:28](bibleref:43008028), [JHN 12:32-12:33](bibleref:43012032-43012033)

### In history
**[MAT 17:9-17:13](bibleref:40017009-40017013)** *John the Baptist's fate indicates what is to happen to Jesus Christ.* 

### In the experience of Jesus Christ
**[MAT 26:12](bibleref:40026012)** parallel MRK 14:8 *Jesus Christ's reaction to the woman anointing him seems to suggest that he will die the death of a criminal.* 

### In the Lord's Supper
**[MAT 26:26-26:28](bibleref:40026026-40026028)** parallels MRK 14:22-14:25, LUK 22:19-22:20
