# 1265: hand of God

A figure of speech which points to God's sovereign power in creation and in his actions on his people's behalf, especially in redemption. Also used as a symbol of authority and in taking oaths.

**[ACT 7:50](bibleref:44007050)** *See also* [ISA 66:2](bibleref:23066002); [JOB 10:3](bibleref:18010003), [JOB 10:8](bibleref:18010008); [PSA 8:3](bibleref:19008003), [PSA 19:1](bibleref:19019001), [PSA 95:5](bibleref:19095005), [PSA 138:8](bibleref:19138008); [ISA 48:13](bibleref:23048013), [ISA 64:8](bibleref:23064008)
