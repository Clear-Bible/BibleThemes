# 2081: Jesus Christ, wisdom of

Jesus Christ's deep understanding of God, people and situations derived from his relationship with God his Father and from knowledge of his word. Christ's wisdom is seen in his words, actions and dealings with people.



## The wisdom of Messiah was foretold and recognised in Jesus Christ
**ISA 11:2** *See also* PRO 8:22-8:23; ISA 52:13; JHN 1:1, JHN 4:29; 1CO 1:24, 1CO 1:30; COL 2:2-2:3

## Wisdom was characteristic of Jesus Christ's childhood
**LUK 2:40** *See also* LUK 2:46-2:47, LUK 2:52

## Wisdom was characteristic of Jesus Christ's ministry


### Jesus Christ's wisdom was exceptional
**MAT 13:54** parallel MRK 6:2 *See also* MAT 12:42 parallel LUK 11:31; MRK 11:18; JHN 7:15

### Jesus Christ's wisdom in teaching
**MAT 7:29** *See also* MAT 11:4-11:5 parallel LUK 7:22; MAT 13:34, MAT 16:12, MAT 17:13; MRK 1:22 parallel LUK 4:32; LUK 24:8; JHN 2:17

### Jesus Christ's wisdom in dealing with people
**JHN 4:29** *See also* MAT 18:2-18:3 parallels MRK 9:36-9:37, LUK 9:47-9:48; MAT 20:25-20:26 parallel MRK 10:42-10:43; JHN 8:10-8:11

### Jesus Christ's wisdom in dealing with opposition
**LUK 14:5-14:6** parallel MAT 12:11-12:12 *See also* MAT 22:18-22:22 parallels MRK 12:15-12:17, LUK 20:23-20:26; MAT 27:14 parallel MRK 15:5

## Jesus Christ's wisdom arose from his relationship with God


### It was based on prayer
**MAT 26:36** parallels MRK 14:32, LUK 22:41**LUK 5:16** *See also* MAT 14:23 parallel MRK 6:46; MRK 1:35; LUK 6:12

### It was based on Scripture
**MAT 4:4** parallel LUK 4:4 *See also* DEU 8:3; MAT 5:17, MAT 22:29; LUK 24:27

## Jesus Christ's life embodies the wisdom of God's will and purposes
