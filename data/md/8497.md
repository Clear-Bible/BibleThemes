# 8497: witnessing, approaches to

Scripture provides teaching, supported by examples, concerning when believers should witness, the methods they are to use, and the manner of doing it.



## When and where to witness


### In the home and family
**MRK 5:18-5:19** parallel LUK 8:38-8:39 *See also* 2KI 5:1-5:3; JHN 1:41

### To individuals
**JHN 1:40-1:42** *See also* JHN 4:7-4:26; ACT 8:30-8:35

### To assembled groups
**ACT 17:17** *See also* ACT 5:42, ACT 16:13, ACT 19:8-19:9

## Methods of witnessing


### Sharing personal testimony
**JHN 4:29** *See also* ACT 22:3-22:8 *Paul uses the account of his conversion in witnessing.*

### Explaining the Scriptures
**ACT 8:30-8:35** *See also* ISA 53:7-53:8; PSA 119:172; ACT 17:1-17:3, ACT 17:10-17:12

### Using prophetic gifts
**1CO 14:24-14:25**

### Answering questions
**COL 4:6** *See also* JHN 9:8-9:11, JHN 9:15-9:17, JHN 9:24-9:25; 1PE 3:15

### Through writing
**JHN 20:31** *See also* LUK 1:1-1:4

### Through holy living
**MAT 5:16** *See also* 1PE 3:1-3:2

### Through acts of devotion
**MAT 26:6-26:7** parallel MRK 14:3 *See also* LUK 7:36-7:38; JHN 12:1-12:3

## The manner of witnessing


### With love
**MRK 10:21** *See also* PHP 1:15-1:17

### With urgency
**2CO 5:20** *See also* EZK 33:7-33:9

### Fearlessly
**EPH 6:19-6:20** *See also* ACT 4:29

### Clearly and courteously
**1PE 3:15** *See also* ACT 26:1-26:3; COL 4:4-4:6
