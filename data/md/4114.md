# 4114: angels, and praise of God

Spiritual beings who praise God in a manner that is beyond human capacity.



## Angels worship God in his presence
**[REV 7:11](bibleref:66007011)** *See also* [REV 3:5](bibleref:66003005), [REV 8:2](bibleref:66008002)

## Angels praise God for his works


### Angels praise God's work of creation
**[JOB 38:4-38:7](bibleref:18038004-18038007)** *See also* [PSA 148:1-148:5](bibleref:19148001-19148005); [NEH 9:6](bibleref:16009006)

### Angels praise God's work of redemption
**[LUK 2:13-2:14](bibleref:42002013-42002014)** *The angels are praising God for Jesus Christ's birth.*  *See also* [ISA 44:23](bibleref:23044023), [ISA 49:13](bibleref:23049013); [LUK 15:10](bibleref:42015010); [HEB 1:6](bibleref:58001006); [DEU 32:43](bibleref:05032043)

### Angels praise God's kingdom
**[PSA 103:19-103:21](bibleref:19103019-19103021)** *See also* [PSA 96:10-96:11](bibleref:19096010-19096011) parallel 1CH 16:30-16:31; [PSA 145:10-145:11](bibleref:19145010-19145011); [REV 11:15](bibleref:66011015)

## Angelic beings praise God
**[ISA 6:2-6:4](bibleref:23006002-23006004)** *See also* [REV 4:8](bibleref:66004008), [REV 5:8-5:10](bibleref:66005008-66005010), [REV 7:11-7:12](bibleref:66007011-66007012)

## Thousands of angels worship God
**[HEB 12:22](bibleref:58012022)** *See also* [DEU 33:2](bibleref:05033002); [PSA 68:17](bibleref:19068017); [DAN 7:10](bibleref:27007010); [REV 5:11-5:12](bibleref:66005011-66005012)

## Images of angelic beings decorate Israel's sanctuaries
**[1KI 6:23-6:29](bibleref:11006023-11006029)** parallels 2CH 3:7, 2CH 3:10-3:13 *See also* [EXO 26:1](bibleref:02026001); [EZK 41:18-41:20](bibleref:26041018-26041020), [EZK 41:25](bibleref:26041025) *The images of the cherubim represent the angels who worship God in heaven.* 

## Angels are not to be worshipped
**[REV 22:8-22:9](bibleref:66022008-66022009)** *See also* [MAT 4:9-4:10](bibleref:40004009-40004010) parallel LUK 4:7-4:8; [ROM 1:25](bibleref:45001025); [COL 2:18](bibleref:51002018)
