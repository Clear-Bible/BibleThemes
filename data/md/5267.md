# 5267: control

The exercise of authority and restraint over individuals and events. Scripture stresses that God is in sovereign control over his creation. A limited degree of control over creation is delegated to humanity.

## God controls the whole world
**[GEN 1:1](bibleref:01001001), [GEN 1:3](bibleref:01001003)** *See also* [NEH 9:6](bibleref:16009006); [PSA 102:25](bibleref:19102025); [ACT 17:24](bibleref:44017024); [HEB 11:3](bibleref:58011003)

## God controls the affairs of heaven and earth
 [DEU 4:39](bibleref:05004039); [DAN 4:35](bibleref:27004035)

### He gives wealth
**[DEU 8:18](bibleref:05008018)** *See also* [1CH 29:12](bibleref:13029012)

### He appoints and deposes earthly rulers
 [PSA 75:6-75:7](bibleref:19075006-19075007); [DAN 2:21](bibleref:27002021), [DAN 4:17](bibleref:27004017), [DAN 4:32](bibleref:27004032), [DAN 5:18](bibleref:27005018), [DAN 5:21](bibleref:27005021), [DAN 5:26](bibleref:27005026)

### He controls human hearts
 [PRO 21:1](bibleref:20021001)

### He controls times and seasons
**[GEN 8:22](bibleref:01008022)** *See also* [PSA 31:15](bibleref:19031015); [DAN 2:21](bibleref:27002021); [AMO 4:9](bibleref:30004009); [ACT 1:7](bibleref:44001007)

### He controls the weather
 [AMO 4:7](bibleref:30004007); [MAT 5:45](bibleref:40005045)

 [ROM 9:11-9:16](bibleref:45009011-45009016)

 [MAT 25:34](bibleref:40025034), [MAT 25:41](bibleref:40025041)

## Human control over creation
**[GEN 1:26-1:28](bibleref:01001026-01001028)** *See also* [GEN 2:15](bibleref:01002015), [GEN 2:19-2:20](bibleref:01002019-01002020), [GEN 3:16-3:19](bibleref:01003016-01003019) *After the fall it became difficult for human beings to control creation.*
