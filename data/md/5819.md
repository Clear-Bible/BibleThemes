# 5819: cowardice

An unwillingness to act in a situation of danger or opposition, because of fear. Avoiding the source of one's anxiety or fear, rather than dealing with it directly.



## Cowardice prevents people from acknowledging Jesus Christ publicly
**JHN 12:42-12:43** *See also* JHN 7:13, JHN 9:22-9:23 *parents of the man born blind*; JHN 19:38, JHN 20:19

### Cowardice inhibits bad as well as good behaviour
 MAT 14:5, MAT 21:26, MAT 21:46

### Cowardice leads to avoidance of the difficulty
**MAT 26:56** *See also* 1SA 13:6-13:7 *the Philistine threat*; 1SA 17:24 *Goliath*; 1KI 19:2-19:3; JON 1:10; MAT 26:69-26:74 parallels MRK 14:66-14:71, LUK 22:57-22:60, JHN 18:15-18:18, JHN 18:25-18:27; LUK 8:37 *after the destruction of the pigs*; GAL 2:12 *Peter*

### Cowardice can spread within a group
**DEU 20:8** NUM 13:30-13:33; ; JOS 14:8; 
JDG 7:2-7:3; ; JER 38:4

## God can work through cowardice


### In humbling disobedient people

 JOS 2:8-2:11, JOS 2:24;

**PRO 29:25** *See also* DEU 1:27-1:31; JDG 7:10-7:11; PSA 56:3-56:4; ISA 51:12-51:15; LUK 21:12-21:19ACT 4:5-4:10, ACT 4:13, ACT 4:29-4:31
