# 8257: fruitfulness, natural

In the OT fruitfulness is often used as a way of referring to God's material and physical blessings.



## God's blessing on creation generally
**GEN 1:11-1:12, GEN 1:22** *See also* GEN 8:17

## Fruitfulness in the promised land
**LEV 25:18-25:19** *God's blessing was seen in terms of material prosperity.* *See also* LEV 26:3-26:4; DEU 32:13, DEU 33:13-33:16; EZK 34:27; AMO 9:14; ZEC 8:12

## Fruitfulness in family relationships


### Such fruitfulness promised
**GEN 13:16** *See also* GEN 15:5, GEN 17:6, GEN 17:20, GEN 22:17; LEV 26:9; DEU 7:13, DEU 28:4, DEU 28:11, DEU 30:9; PSA 128:3; JER 23:3; EZK 36:11

### Such fruitfulness commanded
**GEN 1:28** *See also* GEN 9:1, GEN 9:7, GEN 35:11, GEN 48:3-48:4

### Examples of such fruitfulness
**PSA 105:24** *See also* GEN 41:52, GEN 47:27, GEN 49:22; EXO 1:7; PSA 107:37-107:38
