# 5275: cremation





## The burning of a dead body. Cremation was not practised by Israel or the first Christians. In difficult circumstances a corpse might be burnt and the remains buried


## Cremation of corpses
**1SA 31:12-31:13** *This was done to prevent any further abuse of the bodies by the Philistines. David later reburied the remains in a family grave (see 2Sa 21:12-14).*

## Burning a body as a punishment
 *See also* LEV 20:14, LEV 21:9; JOS 7:15, JOS 7:25; 2KI 23:20

These were not for the purpose of cremating the king's corpse, but public demonstrations in his honour:
