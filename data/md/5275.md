# 5275: cremation


## The burning of a dead body. Cremation was not practised by Israel or the first Christians. In difficult circumstances a corpse might be burnt and the remains buried


## Cremation of corpses
**[1SA 31:12-31:13](bibleref:09031012-09031013)** *This was done to prevent any further abuse of the bodies by the Philistines. David later reburied the remains in a family grave (see 2Sa 21:12-14).*

## Funeral fires for the kings of Judah
These were not for the purpose of cremating the king's corpse, but public demonstrations in his honour:
 [2CH 16:14](bibleref:14016014); [JER 34:5](bibleref:24034005)
