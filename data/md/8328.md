# 8328: quietness

A calm, peaceful and restrained attitude to life and way of approaching God frequently commended in Scripture even in adverse circumstances. It is also a condition experienced by God's friends and enemies when confronted by his majesty.



## Quietness of life


### A quiet manner of life commended
**1TI 2:1-2:2** *See also* PSA 35:20, PSA 131:1-131:2; MAT 12:19; ISA 42:2; 1TH 4:11; 2TH 3:12; 1TI 2:11; 1PE 3:1-3:4

### A quiet temperament
**GEN 25:27**

### Quiet activity
**MAT 1:19** *See also* JDG 4:21; RUT 3:7; EZK 24:17; ACT 16:37

### The wisdom of being quiet
**AMO 5:13** *See also* GEN 34:5; JDG 18:19-18:20; EST 7:4; PRO 15:18; ECC 9:17; ACT 19:35-19:36

## Quietness in different circumstances


### In peace and rest
**PSA 23:2** *See also* 1CH 4:40; PRO 17:1; ISA 32:17; MRK 6:31

### After conflict
**1CH 22:9** *See also* JOS 11:23, JOS 14:15; JDG 3:11, JDG 5:31, JDG 8:28; 2CH 14:1, 2CH 20:30; ISA 14:7; JER 30:10 parallel JER 46:27; ZEC 1:11

### When in distress
 2SA 13:20; ; NEH 8:9-8:11; ; ISA 7:4

### Because of a sense of shame
 GEN 3:8; ; NEH 5:8; ; MRK 9:34

### When before a higher authority
 JDG 3:19; ; MRK 1:25 parallel LUK 4:35

### God's quiet voice
 1KI 19:12-19:13

## Absence of quietness


### Because of inner conflict
**JOB 3:26** *See also* RUT 3:18; JOB 6:24; ROM 7:21-7:24

### Because of sin
**ISA 57:20-57:21**
