# 5179: sandal

A form of open shoe worn in warm climates, and the only form of foot-covering to be of major importance in Scripture. Both the wearing and the removal of sandals is often seen as symbolic; to go barefoot was often a sign of shame, mourning or poverty.

## Sandals were normally worn by an official, soldier or messenger
 [1KI 2:5](bibleref:11002005); [EPH 6:15](bibleref:49006015)

## Untying and carrying sandals was the task of a servant
**[MAT 3:11](bibleref:40003011)** parallels MRK 1:7, LUK 3:16, JHN 1:27 *John considers himself unworthy even to be a slave of Jesus Christ.*  *See also* [ACT 13:25](bibleref:44013025)

## The removal of sandals as a gesture


### As a mark of respect in the presence of God
**[EXO 3:5](bibleref:02003005)** *See also* [JOS 5:15](bibleref:06005015); [ACT 7:33](bibleref:44007033)

### To show rejection of any claim to property
 [DEU 25:9-25:10](bibleref:05025009-05025010); [RUT 4:7-4:8](bibleref:08004007-08004008); [PSA 60:8](bibleref:19060008) parallel PSA 108:9

### To predict the defeat and disgrace of Egypt and Assyria
 [ISA 20:2-20:4](bibleref:23020002-23020004)

### As a sign of mourning and repentance
 [2SA 15:30](bibleref:10015030); [EZK 24:17](bibleref:26024017), [EZK 24:23](bibleref:26024023); [MIC 1:8](bibleref:33001008)

## The symbolic use of sandals


### As symbols of readiness for action
**[EXO 12:11](bibleref:02012011)**

### As symbols of oppression and injustice
 *See also* [AMO 2:6](bibleref:30002006), [AMO 8:6](bibleref:30008006)

### Fine sandals as a status symbol
 [EZK 16:10](bibleref:26016010); [LUK 15:22](bibleref:42015022)

## Sandals as an example of the need for minimum possessions in God's service
 [MAT 10:10](bibleref:40010010) parallels MRK 6:9, LUK 10:4; [LUK 22:35](bibleref:42022035)
