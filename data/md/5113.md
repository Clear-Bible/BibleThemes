# 5113: Peter, the disciple

The son of Jonas or John, he was called Simon until Jesus Christ gave him the name Peter meaning “rock”.



## Peter's background and family


### He lived in Capernaum
 [MAT 8:14](bibleref:40008014) parallels MRK 1:29, LUK 4:38; [JHN 1:44](bibleref:43001044) *He originally came from Bethsaida.* 

### He was a fisherman by trade
 [LUK 5:10](bibleref:42005010)

## Peter as Jesus Christ's disciple


### He was called by Jesus Christ
 [MAT 4:18-4:20](bibleref:40004018-40004020) parallel MRK 1:16-1:20; [LUK 5:10](bibleref:42005010); [JHN 1:40-1:42](bibleref:43001040-43001042)

### He witnessed Jesus Christ perform many miracles
 [MAT 8:14-8:15](bibleref:40008014-40008015) parallels MRK 1:30-1:31, LUK 4:38-4:39; [MAT 17:24-17:27](bibleref:40017024-40017027); [LUK 5:4-5:8](bibleref:42005004-42005008); [JHN 21:2-21:6](bibleref:43021002-43021006)

### He was chosen to be with Jesus Christ on certain occasions
 [MAT 17:1-17:2](bibleref:40017001-40017002) parallels MRK 9:2, LUK 9:28-9:29 *at the transfiguration of Jesus Christ* ; [MAT 26:36-26:37](bibleref:40026036-40026037) parallel MRK 14:32-14:33 *in the Garden of Gethsemane* ; [MRK 5:37](bibleref:41005037) parallel LUK 8:51

### He sometimes acted as spokesman for the other disciples
He asked Jesus Christ to teach them:
 [MAT 15:15](bibleref:40015015), [MAT 18:21](bibleref:40018021)[MAT 16:15-16:16](bibleref:40016015-40016016) parallels MRK 8:29, LUK 9:20; [JHN 6:69](bibleref:43006069)
[MAT 17:4](bibleref:40017004) parallels MRK 9:5, LUK 9:33 *He asked Jesus Christ to prolong the transfiguration experience* ; [MAT 19:27](bibleref:40019027) parallels MRK 10:28, LUK 18:28; [MRK 1:36-1:37](bibleref:41001036-41001037); [LUK 5:4-5:5](bibleref:42005004-42005005); [JHN 6:68](bibleref:43006068)

### His impetuous nature interacted with his devotion to Jesus Christ
 [MAT 14:28-14:29](bibleref:40014028-40014029) *He wanted to walk to Jesus Christ on the water* ; [MAT 16:22](bibleref:40016022) parallel MRK 8:32 *He tried to prevent Jesus Christ from going to his death* ; [MAT 26:35](bibleref:40026035) parallels MRK 14:31, LUK 22:33, JHN 13:37 *He vowed that he was willing to die for Jesus Christ* ; [JHN 13:6-13:8](bibleref:43013006-43013008), [JHN 18:10](bibleref:43018010)

### He was the subject of both special commendation and rebuke
 [MAT 16:17-16:19](bibleref:40016017-40016019) *He was commended for his confession of faith in Jesus Christ* ; [MAT 16:23](bibleref:40016023) parallel MRK 8:33 *He was rebuked for his opposition to Jesus Christ's prediction of his suffering* ; [MAT 26:34](bibleref:40026034) parallels MRK 14:30, LUK 22:34, JHN 13:38 *He was warned that he would disown Jesus Christ* ; [MAT 26:40-26:41](bibleref:40026040-40026041) parallel MRK 14:37-14:38 *He was rebuked for falling asleep in the Garden of Gethsemane* ; [JHN 18:10-18:11](bibleref:43018010-43018011), [JHN 21:20-21:22](bibleref:43021020-43021022)

## Peter's denial of Jesus Christ and his reinstatement


### He denied that he knew Jesus Christ
 [MAT 26:69-26:74](bibleref:40026069-40026074) parallels MRK 14:66-14:71, LUK 22:54-22:60, JHN 18:15-18:18, JHN 18:25-18:27

### He repented of his denial
 [MAT 26:75](bibleref:40026075) parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:61-22:62

### The risen Christ appeared to him on his own
 [LUK 24:33-24:34](bibleref:42024033-42024034); [1CO 15:4-15:5](bibleref:46015004-46015005)

### He was reinstated by Jesus Christ in front of other disciples
 [JHN 21:15-21:17](bibleref:43021015-43021017)
