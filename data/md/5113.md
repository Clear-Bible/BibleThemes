# 5113: Peter, the disciple

The son of Jonas or John, he was called Simon until Jesus Christ gave him the name Peter meaning “rock”.



## Peter's background and family


### He lived in Capernaum
 MAT 8:14 parallels MRK 1:29, LUK 4:38; ; JHN 1:44 *He originally came from Bethsaida.*

### He was a fisherman by trade
 LUK 5:10

## Peter as Jesus Christ's disciple


### He was called by Jesus Christ
 MAT 4:18-4:20 parallel MRK 1:16-1:20; ; LUK 5:10; ; JHN 1:40-1:42

### He witnessed Jesus Christ perform many miracles
 MAT 8:14-8:15 parallels MRK 1:30-1:31, LUK 4:38-4:39; ; MAT 17:24-17:27; ; LUK 5:4-5:8; ; JHN 21:2-21:6

### He was chosen to be with Jesus Christ on certain occasions
 MAT 17:1-17:2 parallels MRK 9:2, LUK 9:28-9:29 *at the transfiguration of Jesus Christ*; ; MAT 26:36-26:37 parallel MRK 14:32-14:33 *in the Garden of Gethsemane*; ; MRK 5:37 parallel LUK 8:51

### He sometimes acted as spokesman for the other disciples
He asked Jesus Christ to teach them:
 MAT 15:15, MAT 18:21; MAT 16:15-16:16 parallels MRK 8:29, LUK 9:20; ; JHN 6:69; 
MAT 17:4 parallels MRK 9:5, LUK 9:33 *He asked Jesus Christ to prolong the transfiguration experience*; ; MAT 19:27 parallels MRK 10:28, LUK 18:28; ; MRK 1:36-1:37; ; LUK 5:4-5:5; ; JHN 6:68

### His impetuous nature interacted with his devotion to Jesus Christ
 MAT 14:28-14:29 *He wanted to walk to Jesus Christ on the water*; ; MAT 16:22 parallel MRK 8:32 *He tried to prevent Jesus Christ from going to his death*; ; MAT 26:35 parallels MRK 14:31, LUK 22:33, JHN 13:37 *He vowed that he was willing to die for Jesus Christ*; ; JHN 13:6-13:8, JHN 18:10

### He was the subject of both special commendation and rebuke
 MAT 16:17-16:19 *He was commended for his confession of faith in Jesus Christ*; ; MAT 16:23 parallel MRK 8:33 *He was rebuked for his opposition to Jesus Christ's prediction of his suffering*; ; MAT 26:34 parallels MRK 14:30, LUK 22:34, JHN 13:38 *He was warned that he would disown Jesus Christ*; ; MAT 26:40-26:41 parallel MRK 14:37-14:38 *He was rebuked for falling asleep in the Garden of Gethsemane*; ; JHN 18:10-18:11, JHN 21:20-21:22

## Peter's denial of Jesus Christ and his reinstatement


### He denied that he knew Jesus Christ
 MAT 26:69-26:74 parallels MRK 14:66-14:71, LUK 22:54-22:60, JHN 18:15-18:18, JHN 18:25-18:27

### He repented of his denial
 MAT 26:75 parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:61-22:62

### The risen Christ appeared to him on his own
 LUK 24:33-24:34; ; 1CO 15:4-15:5

### He was reinstated by Jesus Christ in front of other disciples
 JHN 21:15-21:17
