# 5864: futility

All human life and human systems are ultimately useless without God, because death comes to all. God wants to deliver people from their futile ways.



## Human life is futile


### The brevity of life
**PSA 89:47** *See also* JOB 7:6-7:7, JOB 14:1-14:2; PSA 39:4-39:5; ECC 6:12; ISA 40:6-40:7; JAS 4:14

### Death is the destiny of all
 JOB 9:22; ; ECC 3:19-3:20, ECC 9:2

### The dead are forgotten
 PSA 103:15-103:16; ; ECC 1:11, ECC 2:16, ECC 9:5

## Human endeavour is futile
**ECC 1:14** *The writer speaks of human life without reference to God.* *See also* PSA 39:6, PSA 127:2; ECC 2:10-2:23; HAB 2:13; JAS 1:11

## Human thoughts are futile
**PSA 94:11** *See also* 1CO 3:19-3:20; ROM 1:21; EPH 4:17-4:18

## Human attitudes are futile


### Self-confidence is futile
**ISA 16:6** *See also* JER 48:29-48:30; LUK 1:51-1:52

### Relying solely on human help is futile
**JER 17:5** *See also* PSA 60:11 parallel PSA 108:12; PSA 146:3-146:4; ISA 2:22, ISA 30:1-30:5, ISA 31:1-31:3; LAM 4:17

## False religion is futile


### Idolatry is futile
