# 8611: prayer, for others

Believers should pray, not only for their own needs, but for those of others. Scripture provides many examples of intercession, and commends it as pleasing to God.



## Believers must value others
**PHP 2:3-2:4**

## Examples of praying for others


### Moses prays for the Israelites
**DEU 9:18-9:19** *See also* EXO 32:9-32:14, EXO 34:9; NUM 14:11-14:19; DEU 9:25-9:29

### Samuel prays for Israel
**1SA 7:5-7:9** *See also* 1SA 12:19-12:23

### Job prays for his friends
**JOB 42:10**

### Jeremiah prays for Judah
**JER 7:16** *God forbade the prophet Jeremiah to pray for his people because they were unrepentant sinners.* *See also* JER 11:14, JER 14:11

## Jesus Christ intercedes for believers
**ROM 8:34** *See also* ISA 53:12; HEB 7:25; 1JN 2:1

## The Holy Spirit intercedes for believers
**ROM 8:26-8:27**

## Christians are to intercede for others


### Christians are to pray for their enemies
**MAT 5:44** *See also* LUK 6:28, LUK 23:34; ACT 7:60

### Christians are to pray for one another
**EPH 6:18** *See also* 1TH 5:25; PHM 1:22; HEB 13:18-13:19; JAS 5:14-5:16; 1JN 5:16

### Christians are to prayer for rulers
**1TI 2:1-2:2**

## Examples of pleas made to Jesus Christ on behalf of others
 *See also* MAT 8:5-8:13 parallel LUK 7:1-7:10; MAT 15:21-15:28 parallel MRK 7:24-7:30; MAT 17:14-17:20 parallels MRK 9:14-9:29, LUK 9:37-9:42
