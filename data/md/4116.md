# 4116: angels, opposed to God

Spiritual beings who fell with Satan. They are under God's judgment and are subject to the authority of believers.



## Spiritual authorities led by Satan
**[2CO 11:14](bibleref:47011014)** *See also* [JHN 12:31](bibleref:43012031), [JHN 16:11](bibleref:43016011); [EPH 6:12](bibleref:49006012); [REV 9:11](bibleref:66009011)

## Angels opposed to God will be judged
**[JUD 1:6](bibleref:65001006)** *See also* [JOB 4:18](bibleref:18004018), [JOB 15:15](bibleref:18015015); [MAT 25:41](bibleref:40025041); [2PE 2:4](bibleref:61002004); [REV 20:10](bibleref:66020010)

## Jesus Christ has defeated Satan and his angels
**[COL 2:15](bibleref:51002015)** *See also* [GEN 3:15](bibleref:01003015); [EPH 1:19-1:21](bibleref:49001019-49001021)

## Satan's angels are resisted by God's faithful angels
**[REV 12:7-12:9](bibleref:66012007-66012009)** *See also* [DAN 10:13](bibleref:27010013), [DAN 10:20](bibleref:27010020) *The princes of Persia and Greece appear to be spiritual powers who resist God's rule over these regions.*
