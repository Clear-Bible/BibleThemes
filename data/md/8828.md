# 8828: spite

The malicious desire to retaliate in words or action. It may be in response to good or evil. Believers are to rid themselves of any such vindictive spirit.

## Examples of people acting out of spite


### In response to good
 [1KI 19:1-19:2](bibleref:11019001-11019002) *Jezebel* ; [2CH 16:10](bibleref:14016010) *Asa, king of Judah* ; [EST 3:6](bibleref:17003006) *Haman* ; [MAT 14:3-14:12](bibleref:40014003-40014012) parallel MRK 6:17-6:29 *Herodias* ; [JHN 12:10](bibleref:43012010) *the chief priests* [ACT 7:54](bibleref:44007054), [ACT 7:57-7:58](bibleref:44007057-44007058)

### In response to evil
**[PSA 140:3](bibleref:19140003)** *See also* [GEN 27:41](bibleref:01027041) *Esau* [GEN 37:4](bibleref:01037004), [GEN 37:8](bibleref:01037008), [GEN 37:11](bibleref:01037011), [GEN 37:18-37:27](bibleref:01037018-01037027)
[EZK 25:15](bibleref:26025015) *the Philistines* ; [ACT 23:12-23:15](bibleref:44023012-44023015) *some Jews*
