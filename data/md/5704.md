# 5704: inheritance, material

Family property was normally handed down in biblical times through sons, with a special place for the firstborn.  The land of Israel was regarded as the nation's inheritance.



## Inheritance of family property


### It was normally handed down through sons
**GEN 15:3-15:4** *See also* GEN 25:5; JER 49:1; EZK 46:16-46:17; MAT 21:38 parallels MRK 12:7, LUK 20:14; GAL 4:30; GEN 21:10

### The absence of a son as a male heir was seen as a serious disadvantage
 GEN 25:21; ; DEU 25:5-25:6; ; JDG 21:17; ; 1SA 1:10-1:11; ; 2SA 14:4-14:7

### Other relatives could inherit
**NUM 27:8-27:11** *See also* NUM 27:1-27:7, NUM 36:2-36:4; RUT 4:1-4:4; JOB 42:15

### Servants could inherit
 GEN 15:2-15:3

### Inheritance before death
 LUK 15:11-15:12 *The son's asking for his inheritance is perhaps to be seen as an insult.*

### The rights of the firstborn
**DEU 21:15-21:17** *See also* GEN 27:19, GEN 27:32; 2CH 21:3

## Stewardship of one's own inheritance


### It must be preserved
**1KI 21:2-21:3** *See also* PRO 13:22

### It must not be squandered
**HEB 12:16** *See also* GEN 25:29-25:34; LUK 15:13

### It must not be fought over
 LUK 12:13-12:15

## Respect for the inheritance of others
**MIC 2:2-2:3** *See also* EZK 46:18

## The land as Israel's inheritance


### It was promised to Abraham
**GEN 12:6-12:7** *See also* GEN 13:14-13:15, GEN 15:7, GEN 15:18-15:21, GEN 17:8; ACT 7:3-7:5; HEB 11:8

### It was confirmed to Moses and the patriarchs
 EXO 6:2-6:4, EXO 6:8

### Entry was denied because of sin
 NUM 14:22-14:23; ; DEU 4:21

### It was entered by faith
 HEB 11:8-11:9, HEB 11:30

### It was conquered by Joshua
 JOS 1:1-1:4, JOS 11:23

### It was allotted to the tribes
 JOS 14:1-14:5

### It was a land for the godly
 PSA 37:9, PSA 37:11, PSA 37:22, PSA 37:29, PSA 37:34

### It was a good inheritance
**EXO 3:8** *See also* LEV 20:24; NUM 13:21-13:27; DEU 8:7-8:8, DEU 11:10-11:12; PSA 65:9-65:10; JER 2:7

### The LORD's inheritance in the land
 EXO 15:17; ; JOS 22:19; ; ZEC 2:12

### The inheritance was lost through sin
**JER 17:4** *See also* PSA 79:1; LAM 5:2

### God's judgment on the nations
 JER 12:14-12:17

### The land was regained through grace
 EZR 1:1-1:2; ; JER 29:10-29:11

### God renewed his promises
**PSA 69:34-69:36** *The Jews did return to the land; some look, however, for a further fulfilment of these promises in the future; others see them as fulfilled in Jesus Christ.* *See also* ISA 57:13, ISA 58:13-58:14, ISA 61:7, ISA 65:9; ZEC 8:12
