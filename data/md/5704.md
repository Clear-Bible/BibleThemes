# 5704: inheritance, material

Family property was normally handed down in biblical times through sons, with a special place for the firstborn.  The land of Israel was regarded as the nation's inheritance.

### It was normally handed down through sons
**[GEN 15:3-15:4](bibleref:01015003-01015004)** *See also* [GEN 25:5](bibleref:01025005); [JER 49:1](bibleref:24049001); [EZK 46:16-46:17](bibleref:26046016-26046017); [MAT 21:38](bibleref:40021038) parallels MRK 12:7, LUK 20:14; [GAL 4:30](bibleref:48004030); [GEN 21:10](bibleref:01021010)
