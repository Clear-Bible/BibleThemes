# 5959: submission

A humble attitude where obedience is rendered within a relationship; whether it be to God, authorities or other people at work, in the church, in marriage or in the family.

## God requires submission


### From all people
**[PSA 2:9-2:11](bibleref:19002009-19002011)** *See also* [JOB 22:21](bibleref:18022021); [1PE 5:6](bibleref:60005006)

### From Christians especially
**[MAT 6:9-6:10](bibleref:40006009-40006010)** *See also* [HEB 12:9](bibleref:58012009); [JAS 4:7](bibleref:59004007)

## The submission of Jesus Christ to his Father
**[LUK 22:42](bibleref:42022042)** parallels MAT 26:39, MRK 14:36 *See also* [JHN 5:19](bibleref:43005019), [JHN 12:49-12:50](bibleref:43012049-43012050); [1CO 15:27-15:28](bibleref:46015027-46015028); [HEB 5:7-5:8](bibleref:58005007-58005008), [HEB 10:5-10:7](bibleref:58010005-58010007)

## The submissive spirit required in the church


### In believers' relationships with one another
**[EPH 5:21](bibleref:49005021)** *See also* [PHP 2:5-2:7](bibleref:50002005-50002007)

**[EPH 5:24](bibleref:49005024)** *See also* [EPH 1:22-1:23](bibleref:49001022-49001023); [COL 1:18](bibleref:51001018) *As the body is subject to the head, so the church is subject to Christ.*

**[JOS 1:7-1:8](bibleref:06001007-06001008)** *See also* [PSA 119:133](bibleref:19119133); [COL 3:15](bibleref:51003015); [JAS 1:22-1:25](bibleref:59001022-59001025); [REV 22:18-22:19](bibleref:66022018-66022019)

### To church leaders
**[HEB 13:17](bibleref:58013017)** *See also* [ROM 13:1](bibleref:45013001); [1CO 16:15-16:16](bibleref:46016015-46016016); [1TH 5:12-5:13](bibleref:52005012-52005013)

### In prophecy
**[1CO 14:32](bibleref:46014032)** *The implication is that individual prophecies must be submitted to the overall judgment of the church.*
