# 5959: submission

A humble attitude where obedience is rendered within a relationship; whether it be to God, authorities or other people at work, in the church, in marriage or in the family.



## God requires submission


### From all people
**PSA 2:9-2:11** *See also* JOB 22:21; 1PE 5:6

### From Christians especially
**MAT 6:9-6:10** *See also* HEB 12:9; JAS 4:7

## The submission of Jesus Christ to his Father
**LUK 22:42** parallels MAT 26:39, MRK 14:36 *See also* JHN 5:19, JHN 12:49-12:50; 1CO 15:27-15:28; HEB 5:7-5:8, HEB 10:5-10:7

## The submissive spirit required in the church


### In believers' relationships with one another
**EPH 5:21** *See also* PHP 2:5-2:7

### To Jesus Christ
**EPH 5:24** *See also* EPH 1:22-1:23; COL 1:18 *As the body is subject to the head, so the church is subject to Christ.*

### To the Scriptures
**JOS 1:7-1:8** *See also* PSA 119:133; COL 3:15; JAS 1:22-1:25; REV 22:18-22:19

### To church leaders
**HEB 13:17** *See also* ROM 13:1; 1CO 16:15-16:16; 1TH 5:12-5:13

### In prophecy
**1CO 14:32** *The implication is that individual prophecies must be submitted to the overall judgment of the church.*

## The submission of all to civil government
**ROM 13:1** *See also* MAT 22:21 parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:25; ROM 13:2-13:7; TIT 3:1; 1PE 2:13-2:14

## Submission within other relationships


### Of wives to husbands
**EPH 5:22-5:24** *See also* COL 3:18; TIT 2:4-2:5; 1PE 3:1, 1PE 3:5-3:6

### Of children to parents
**EPH 6:1-6:3** *See also* EXO 20:12; GAL 4:2; COL 3:20

### Of slaves to masters
**EPH 6:5-6:8** *See also* COL 3:22-3:24; TIT 2:9-2:10; 1PE 2:18

### Of young to old
**1PE 5:5** *See also* LEV 19:32

### Reciprocal attitudes are commended to safeguard against abuse
 EPH 5:25-5:33, EPH 6:4, EPH 6:9; ; 1TI 2:1-2:2; ; 1PE 5:1-5:4 *Those in authority have a responsibility to set an example.*
