# 7480: wave offering

A gift specially dedicated to God. The term itself may not be intended to convey a particular action.

## The wave offering was part of every fellowship offering
**[LEV 7:34](bibleref:03007034)** *Although given to the priests, the offering belonged to the LORD.*  *See also* [LEV 7:28-7:32](bibleref:03007028-03007032), [LEV 9:21](bibleref:03009021)

## The wave offering was eaten by the priests
 [EXO 29:26-29:28](bibleref:02029026-02029028); [LEV 10:15](bibleref:03010015); [NUM 18:11](bibleref:04018011), [NUM 18:18](bibleref:04018018)

## Occasions for making a wave offering


### As a cereal offering when priests were consecrated
 [EXO 29:23-29:24](bibleref:02029023-02029024); [LEV 8:27-8:29](bibleref:03008027-03008029)

### As a guilt offering sacrificed at a ceremonial cleansing
 [LEV 14:12](bibleref:03014012), [LEV 14:21](bibleref:03014021), [LEV 14:24](bibleref:03014024)

### As part of the ritual when a woman was accused of adultery
 [NUM 5:25](bibleref:04005025)

### When celebrating harvest
 [LEV 23:10-23:11](bibleref:03023010-03023011) *the firstfruits of the harvest* [LEV 23:15-23:17](bibleref:03023015-03023017), [LEV 23:20](bibleref:03023020)

### When presenting a gift to God
Materials given for constructing the tabernacle:
 [EXO 35:22](bibleref:02035022), [EXO 38:24](bibleref:02038024), [EXO 38:29](bibleref:02038029)

### When dedicating the Levites to God
**[NUM 8:11](bibleref:04008011)** *The dedication to God of the Levites symbolised the consecration of the whole nation.*  *See also* [NUM 8:13](bibleref:04008013), [NUM 8:15](bibleref:04008015), [NUM 8:21](bibleref:04008021)
