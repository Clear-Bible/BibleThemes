# 8205: childlikeness

An attitude of simple trust and faith, to be distinguished from childishness. It is commended by Scripture as an appropriate attitude for the believer to take towards God.



## God treats his people like children
**PSA 103:13** *See also* NUM 11:12; ISA 49:15, ISA 66:13; JER 31:20; HOS 11:1-11:4; ROM 8:14-8:17; GAL 4:1-4:7

## God's people should respond to God as children to a parent


### In trust
**PSA 131:2**

### In obedience
**1PE 1:14**

### In humility
**LUK 9:46-9:48** parallels MAT 18:2-18:5, MRK 9:33-9:37 *See also* 1KI 3:7 *Solomon*

### In innocence
**MRK 10:13-10:15** parallels MAT 19:13-19:14, LUK 18:15-18:17 *See also* LUK 10:21

### In imitating a model
**EPH 5:1-5:2** *See also* 1JN 3:10

## Childlikeness is reflected in pastoral care
**1TH 2:7** *See also* 1JN 2:28

## Childlikeness is to be distinguished from immature childishness
**1CO 3:1-3:3** *See also* HEB 5:12-6:1
