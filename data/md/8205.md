# 8205: childlikeness

An attitude of simple trust and faith, to be distinguished from childishness. It is commended by Scripture as an appropriate attitude for the believer to take towards God.

## God treats his people like children
**[PSA 103:13](bibleref:19103013)** *See also* [NUM 11:12](bibleref:04011012); [ISA 49:15](bibleref:23049015), [ISA 66:13](bibleref:23066013); [JER 31:20](bibleref:24031020); [HOS 11:1-11:4](bibleref:28011001-28011004); [ROM 8:14-8:17](bibleref:45008014-45008017); [GAL 4:1-4:7](bibleref:48004001-48004007)
