# 5372: knife


## The knife as a domestic cutting implement


### Used for general purposes
 [PRO 23:2](bibleref:20023002), [PRO 30:14](bibleref:20030014); [JER 36:23](bibleref:24036023) *cutting a scroll* 

## Knives used for shaving
 [LEV 19:27](bibleref:03019027), [LEV 21:5](bibleref:03021005); [JDG 16:17](bibleref:07016017); [ISA 15:2](bibleref:23015002); [EZK 7:18](bibleref:26007018); [MIC 1:16](bibleref:33001016)

## Knives used in religious ceremonies


### In circumcision
Flint rather than metal knives were used because of their sharpness:
 [EXO 4:25](bibleref:02004025); [JOS 5:2-5:3](bibleref:06005002-06005003)

### In sacrifice
 [GEN 15:10](bibleref:01015010), [GEN 22:10](bibleref:01022010); [EXO 29:17](bibleref:02029017); [LEV 8:20](bibleref:03008020)

## Knives used for wounding


### For self-mutilation
This practice was associated with pagan worship, sometimes as an expression of mourning:
 [LEV 19:28](bibleref:03019028), [LEV 21:5](bibleref:03021005); [DEU 14:1-14:2](bibleref:05014001-05014002); [1KI 18:28](bibleref:11018028); [JER 16:6](bibleref:24016006), [JER 41:5](bibleref:24041005), [JER 47:5](bibleref:24047005), [JER 48:37](bibleref:24048037)

### In mutilation of others
As a punishment:
 [EXO 21:23-21:25](bibleref:02021023-02021025); [LEV 24:17-24:20](bibleref:03024017-03024020); [DEU 25:12](bibleref:05025012)[JDG 1:6](bibleref:07001006), [JDG 16:21](bibleref:07016021)[1SA 11:7](bibleref:09011007)

## Knives used in figures of speech


### For self-discipline
 [PRO 23:2](bibleref:20023002); [MAT 5:29-5:30](bibleref:40005029-40005030), [MAT 18:8-18:9](bibleref:40018008-40018009); [MRK 9:43-9:47](bibleref:41009043-41009047)

 [ISA 18:5](bibleref:23018005); [JHN 15:1-15:2](bibleref:43015001-43015002)

### As a symbol of wickedness
 [PRO 30:14](bibleref:20030014)
