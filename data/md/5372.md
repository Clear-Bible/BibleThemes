# 5372: knife





## The knife as a domestic cutting implement


### Used for general purposes
 PRO 23:2, PRO 30:14; ; JER 36:23 *cutting a scroll*

## Knives used for shaving
 LEV 19:27, LEV 21:5; ; JDG 16:17; ; ISA 15:2; ; EZK 7:18; ; MIC 1:16

## Knives used in religious ceremonies


### In circumcision
Flint rather than metal knives were used because of their sharpness:
 EXO 4:25; ; JOS 5:2-5:3

### In sacrifice
 GEN 15:10, GEN 22:10; ; EXO 29:17; ; LEV 8:20

## Knives used for wounding


### For self-mutilation
This practice was associated with pagan worship, sometimes as an expression of mourning:
 LEV 19:28, LEV 21:5; ; DEU 14:1-14:2; ; 1KI 18:28; ; JER 16:6, JER 41:5, JER 47:5, JER 48:37

### In mutilation of others
As a punishment:
 EXO 21:23-21:25; ; LEV 24:17-24:20; ; DEU 25:12; JDG 1:6, JDG 16:21; 1SA 11:7

## Knives used in figures of speech


### For self-discipline
 PRO 23:2; ; MAT 5:29-5:30, MAT 18:8-18:9; ; MRK 9:43-9:47

### As a symbol of pruning
 ISA 18:5; ; JHN 15:1-15:2

### As a symbol of wickedness
 PRO 30:14
