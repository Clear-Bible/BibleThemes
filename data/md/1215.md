# 1215: God, feminine descriptions of

Some expressions attribute feminine qualities to God, especially relating to motherhood.



## Descriptions of God relating to childbirth


### God gives birth to his people
**ISA 44:2** *God giving birth to Israel at the exodus.* *See also* NUM 11:12; DEU 32:18; ISA 44:24

### God gives birth to individuals
**PSA 139:13** *See also* GEN 29:31, GEN 30:22; ECC 11:5; ISA 49:5; JER 1:5

### God as a midwife
**PSA 22:9** *See also* JOB 10:18; PSA 71:6; ISA 66:9

## Descriptions of God relating to motherhood


### God nurses his people
**ISA 49:15**

### God comforts his people
**ISA 66:13** *See also* ISA 40:11

### God as a mother bird protecting her young
**DEU 32:10-32:11**; **MAT 23:37** parallel LUK 13:34 *See also* RUT 2:12; PSA 17:8, PSA 91:4
