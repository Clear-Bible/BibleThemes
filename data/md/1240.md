# 1240: God, the Rock

An OT title for God and a Messianic title signifying that God's people can rely on him for absolute protection and salvation.



## Rock as a title for Israel's God
**[PSA 78:35](bibleref:19078035)** *See also* [GEN 49:24](bibleref:01049024); [DEU 32:15](bibleref:05032015), [DEU 32:18](bibleref:05032018), [DEU 32:30](bibleref:05032030); [2SA 23:3](bibleref:10023003); [PSA 42:9](bibleref:19042009); [ISA 30:29](bibleref:23030029); [HAB 1:12](bibleref:35001012)
