# 7708: apostles, function of

In the NT apostles are servants, first of Jesus Christ and then of the church. Apostolic service embraces a wide range of specific functions.



## The functions of apostles during the earthly life and ministry of Jesus Christ


### Being with Jesus Christ
**[MRK 3:14](bibleref:41003014)** *See also* [MAT 17:1](bibleref:40017001); [MRK 6:30-6:32](bibleref:41006030-41006032) parallel LUK 9:10

### Learning from Jesus Christ
**[MRK 8:31](bibleref:41008031)** *See also* [MAT 18:1-18:4](bibleref:40018001-40018004); [MRK 4:10-4:12](bibleref:41004010-41004012); [ACT 1:2](bibleref:44001002)

### Being sent out on a mission by Jesus Christ
**[LUK 9:1-9:6](bibleref:42009001-42009006)** parallels MAT 10:5-10:10, MRK 6:7-6:13 *As Jesus Christ's representatives their ministry was an extension of his.*  *See also* [MRK 3:14-3:15](bibleref:41003014-41003015)

### Being commissioned to witness to Jesus Christ
**[ACT 1:8](bibleref:44001008), [ACT 10:39](bibleref:44010039)** *See also* [MAT 28:18-28:20](bibleref:40028018-40028020); [MRK 16:15](bibleref:41016015); [LUK 24:46-24:49](bibleref:42024046-42024049); [JHN 17:18](bibleref:43017018), [JHN 20:21](bibleref:43020021)

## Apostles in the early church


### They were witnesses of the resurrection
**[ACT 1:21-1:22](bibleref:44001021-44001022)** *See also* [ACT 2:32](bibleref:44002032), [ACT 3:15](bibleref:44003015), [ACT 4:33](bibleref:44004033), [ACT 13:31](bibleref:44013031); [1PE 5:1](bibleref:60005001)

### They were servants of Jesus Christ
**[2PE 1:1](bibleref:61001001)** *See also* [ROM 1:1](bibleref:45001001); [1CO 4:1](bibleref:46004001); [2CO 11:23](bibleref:47011023); [PHP 1:1](bibleref:50001001); [TIT 1:1](bibleref:56001001)

### They were servants of the church
**[2CO 13:4](bibleref:47013004)** *See also* [ROM 15:25](bibleref:45015025); [2CO 4:5](bibleref:47004005), [2CO 11:8](bibleref:47011008); [1PE 5:1-5:3](bibleref:60005001-60005003)

### They were the foundations of the church
**[EPH 2:20](bibleref:49002020)** *Probably a reference to the apostolic preaching. The absence of the article before “prophets” suggests that “the apostles and prophets” represents one group of people, not two.*  *See also* [1CO 12:28](bibleref:46012028); [GAL 2:9](bibleref:48002009); [JUD 1:17](bibleref:65001017) *the prophetic ministry of the apostles* 

### They preached the gospel
**[ROM 1:1-1:5](bibleref:45001001-45001005)** *See also* [MRK 16:20](bibleref:41016020); [ROM 15:19-15:20](bibleref:45015019-45015020); [1CO 1:17](bibleref:46001017); [GAL 1:15-1:16](bibleref:48001015-48001016)

### They were guardians and teachers of the truth
**[ACT 2:42](bibleref:44002042)** *See also* [JHN 16:13-16:15](bibleref:43016013-43016015); [ACT 5:42](bibleref:44005042), [ACT 6:2](bibleref:44006002); [EPH 3:2-3:6](bibleref:49003002-49003006); [2TH 2:15](bibleref:53002015); [2TI 1:11](bibleref:55001011)

### They founded churches
**[1CO 9:1-9:2](bibleref:46009001-46009002)** *See also* [1CO 3:5-3:6](bibleref:46003005-46003006), [1CO 3:10](bibleref:46003010)

### They were authority figures within the church
 [ACT 6:2-6:6](bibleref:44006002-44006006) *The apostles took the initiative. Whilst they did not themselves appoint the Seven, they confirmed their appointment.* 

### They were called to suffer
**[2TI 1:11-1:12](bibleref:55001011-55001012)** *See also* [ACT 5:18](bibleref:44005018); [1CO 4:9](bibleref:46004009); [2CO 11:23-11:29](bibleref:47011023-47011029)

### They were empowered by the Spirit
**[ACT 4:8](bibleref:44004008)** *See also* [JHN 20:22](bibleref:43020022); [ACT 1:8](bibleref:44001008), [ACT 2:4](bibleref:44002004); [EPH 3:4-3:5](bibleref:49003004-49003005)

### They were able to bestow the Spirit
 [ACT 8:14-8:18](bibleref:44008014-44008018) *Peter and John officially induct Samaritans as members of the body of Christ* ; [ACT 19:6](bibleref:44019006)
