# 5331: hanging

Death by suspension from the neck or impaling on a pole, or the suspension of a body after execution by another means.

## Suicide by hanging
 [2SA 17:23](bibleref:10017023); [MAT 27:5](bibleref:40027005)

## Execution by hanging or impaling
 [GEN 40:18-40:22](bibleref:01040018-01040022)[ACT 5:30](bibleref:44005030), [ACT 10:39](bibleref:44010039)[EZR 6:11](bibleref:15006011); [EST 2:23](bibleref:17002023), [EST 7:9-7:10](bibleref:17007009-17007010), [EST 9:12-9:13](bibleref:17009012-17009013)

## Hanging after execution
 [DEU 21:22](bibleref:05021022); [JOS 8:29](bibleref:06008029), [JOS 10:26](bibleref:06010026); [2SA 4:12](bibleref:10004012), [2SA 21:9-21:10](bibleref:10021009-10021010)

## Anyone hung on a tree was under God's curse
 [DEU 21:23](bibleref:05021023); [GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013)
