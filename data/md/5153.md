# 5153: foot-washing

A practice of hospitality used by Jesus Christ to express his servanthood.



## Foot-washing as a sign of courteous hospitality
**GEN 18:4** *See also* GEN 19:2; JDG 19:21; 1SA 25:41; LUK 7:44

## Jesus Christ washes his disciples' feet
**JHN 13:3-13:5** *The washing of feet was a lowly task normally performed by a servant.*

### As an example of servanthood for his followers
**JHN 13:14** *See also* MAT 20:26-20:27 parallel MRK 10:44-10:45; LUK 22:26-22:27; 1TI 5:10

### As a symbol of spiritual cleansing
**JHN 13:6-13:10**
