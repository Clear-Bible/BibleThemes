# 3275: Holy Spirit, in the church

The church depends upon the activity of the Holy Spirit, without which its effective and faithful service is impossible.



## The Holy Spirit forms the church
**[1CO 12:13](bibleref:46012013)** *See also* [ACT 2:1-2:4](bibleref:44002001-44002004), [ACT 2:16-2:18](bibleref:44002016-44002018); [JOL 2:28-2:29](bibleref:29002028-29002029); [ACT 10:44-10:48](bibleref:44010044-44010048); [EPH 2:21-2:22](bibleref:49002021-49002022)
