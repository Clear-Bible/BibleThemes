# 8831: syncretism

The incorporation into religious faith and practice of elements from other religions, resulting in a loss of integrity and assimilation to the surrounding culture.



## Syncretism in Israel


### God's claim to exclusive worship, the basis of the rejection of syncretism
**EXO 20:3** parallel DEU 5:7 *See also* DEU 6:13-6:15; JOS 24:14-24:15; 1KI 18:21

### Syncretism forbidden to Israel
**EXO 34:15-34:16** *See also* DEU 7:1-7:6; JOS 23:16; 1SA 7:3

### Israelite practice of syncretism
**JER 19:4-19:5** *See also* 2KI 17:34-17:41, 2KI 21:1-21:7 parallel 2CH 33:1-33:7; EZK 8:9-8:16

### The consequence of syncretism
**JER 2:5** *See also* DEU 18:9-18:13; 2KI 17:15-17:17; ISA 2:6

### Punishment for syncretism
**ZEP 1:4-1:5** *See also* DEU 17:2-17:7; JER 19:10-19:13; HOS 2:2-2:13

## Syncretism in NT times


### Mediterranean society was syncretistic
 ACT 14:11-14:13, ACT 17:16-17:23, ACT 19:13-19:16

### NT opposition to syncretism
**HEB 13:9** *See also* 1CO 10:21; GAL 4:9-4:10; COL 2:18

## Monotheism is incompatible with syncretism
**ISA 44:6** *See also* DEU 6:4-6:5; ISA 43:10-43:12; 1CO 8:4-8:6
