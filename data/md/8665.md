# 8665: praise, reasons for

Scripture treats praise as the natural response of believers to God's person and actions.



## Praise is commanded of God's people
**PSA 68:32**; **1PE 2:9**; **REV 19:5** *See also* PSA 30:4, PSA 150:6; ISA 42:10; PHP 2:9-2:11

## Praise is due to God alone
**DEU 10:21**; **1CH 16:25**; **ISA 42:8** *See also* PSA 66:4, PSA 118:15-118:21, PSA 148:13; REV 4:11

## Praise is pleasing to God
**PSA 69:30-69:31** *See also* PSA 135:3, PSA 147:1; ISA 43:20-43:21, ISA 61:10-61:11; JER 13:11

## Praise as an act of witness
**PSA 9:11** *See also* 2SA 22:50; PSA 34:1-34:3; ISA 42:12; 2CO 9:13

## Praise in response to God's nature


### For his greatness
**DEU 32:3**; **PSA 150:2** *See also* 1CH 16:25; NEH 8:6; PSA 104:1; MAT 9:8

### For his righteousness
**PSA 98:8-98:9** *See also* DAN 4:37

### For his faithfulness
**PSA 57:9-57:10, PSA 138:2** *See also* 1KI 8:15-8:20, 1KI 8:56; PSA 89:1

### For his strength
**PSA 59:16** *See also* PSA 28:7, PSA 81:1

## Praise in response to God's deeds


### For deliverance from enemies
**EXO 18:10**; **PSA 18:46-18:48** *See also* GEN 14:20; JDG 16:24; PSA 43:1-43:4, PSA 124:1-124:7

### For answered prayer
**PSA 28:6** *See also* PSA 66:19-66:20

### For sending his Son, Jesus Christ
**LUK 1:68-1:69**; **EPH 1:3** *See also* LUK 2:10-2:14, LUK 2:25-2:28, LUK 24:53; 1PE 1:3-1:6
