# 7933: Lord's Supper

The commemoration and remembrance of Jesus Christ's last supper, and all the benefits that result to believers. Other terms have been used subsequently by Christians, including “Communion” and “Eucharist”.

## Terms for the Lord's Supper in the NT
 [ACT 2:42](bibleref:44002042) *“breaking of bread” could mean both eating together and also celebrating the Lord's Supper* ; [1CO 10:16](bibleref:46010016) *The Greek word “koinonia”, here translated “participation”, is traditionally translated “communion”: hence the term “Holy Communion”* ; [1CO 11:20](bibleref:46011020) *the Lord's Supper* ; [1CO 11:24](bibleref:46011024) *Underlying “had given thanks” is the Greek “eucharisteo”, from which comes the term “Eucharist”.* 

## Jesus Christ's institution of the Lord's Supper
**[1CO 11:23-11:25](bibleref:46011023-46011025)** parallels MAT 26:26-26:28, MRK 14:22-14:24, LUK 22:17-22:20

## Celebrating the Lord's Supper in the NT


### As part of an ordinary meal
**[1CO 11:21](bibleref:46011021)** *The church was not necessarily wrong to include the sacrament in an ordinary meal; this should have been conducted in a charitable way.* 

### On the Lord's day
**[ACT 20:7](bibleref:44020007)** *See also* [JHN 20:26](bibleref:43020026)

### The fourfold formula for breaking bread: taking, giving thanks, breaking, giving
**[MAT 26:26](bibleref:40026026)** parallels MRK 14:22, LUK 22:19 *See also* [LUK 24:30](bibleref:42024030); [JHN 6:11](bibleref:43006011); [1CO 11:24](bibleref:46011024)

### The sharing of the cup
**[1CO 11:25](bibleref:46011025)** parallels MAT 26:27-26:28, MRK 14:23-14:24, LUK 22:20

## Themes connected with the Lord's Supper


### The Passover
**[1CO 5:7-5:8](bibleref:46005007-46005008)** *See also* [JHN 11:50](bibleref:43011050) *Caiaphas' words may have an unintended Passover significance* ; [JHN 13:1](bibleref:43013001), [JHN 19:14](bibleref:43019014), [JHN 19:33](bibleref:43019033), [JHN 19:36](bibleref:43019036); [EXO 12:46](bibleref:02012046); [NUM 9:12](bibleref:04009012)

### The new covenant
**[1CO 11:25](bibleref:46011025)** parallels MAT 26:27-26:28, MRK 14:23-14:24, LUK 22:20 *The reference to the (new) covenant implies a personal relationship with God and sins forgiven.* 

### Remembrance
**[1CO 11:24](bibleref:46011024)** parallel LUK 22:19

### Thanksgiving, fellowship and unity
**[1CO 10:16](bibleref:46010016)** *See also* [MAT 26:26-26:27](bibleref:40026026-40026027) parallels MRK 14:22-14:23, LUK 22:19; [1CO 11:20-11:21](bibleref:46011020-46011021)

### The Lord's return
**[1CO 11:26](bibleref:46011026)** *See also* [MAT 26:29](bibleref:40026029) parallels MRK 14:25, LUK 22:16; [1CO 16:22](bibleref:46016022); [REV 22:20](bibleref:66022020)

### Separation from sin
**[1CO 10:21](bibleref:46010021)** *Paul is referring to feasts in heathen temples.*  *See also* [1CO 11:27-11:32](bibleref:46011027-46011032)

### A foretaste of heaven
**[MAT 26:29](bibleref:40026029)** parallel MRK 14:25
