# 5320: funeral


## Funerals were times for expressing grief
 [GEN 50:10](bibleref:01050010); [ACT 9:36-9:39](bibleref:44009036-44009039)

## Hired musicians sometimes played a funeral dirge
 [MAT 9:23](bibleref:40009023)

## The funeral bier
 [2SA 3:31-3:32](bibleref:10003031-10003032); [LUK 7:14](bibleref:42007014)

## Funeral fires lit in honour of worthy kings of Judah
 [2CH 16:14](bibleref:14016014), [2CH 21:19](bibleref:14021019); [JER 34:5](bibleref:24034005)

## Funerals often concluded with burial in a family tomb
 [GEN 49:29-49:31](bibleref:01049029-01049031)

## Jesus Christ had no funeral ceremony
 [LUK 23:53](bibleref:42023053) parallels MAT 27:59, MRK 15:46, JHN 19:40-19:42
