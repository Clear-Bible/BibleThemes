# 5813: conceit

A state of pride arising out of an overestimation of one's own ability, possessions or importance.



## The definition of conceit
**GAL 6:3**

## Examples of conceit


### In military strength
**2KI 18:33-18:35** *See also* EXO 15:9; 1SA 17:42-17:44

### In the power of idols
**DAN 3:15** *See also* ACT 8:9-8:10

### In personal abilities
**MAT 26:33** parallel MRK 14:29 *See also* GEN 16:4; 1KI 20:11; EST 5:10-5:12

### In false teaching
**1TI 6:3-6:4**

### In possessions
**HOS 12:8**

### In intellectual ability
**PSA 73:7-73:9**

### In national status
**ISA 47:6-47:8** *See also* ISA 16:6; JER 48:29

## God's response to conceit
**2SA 22:28**; **1CO 1:27-1:29** *See also* ISA 2:12, ISA 23:9, ISA 47:8-47:11; OBA 1:3-1:4

## Warnings against conceit
**PHP 2:3** *See also* PRO 3:7; ISA 5:21; JER 9:23-9:24; ROM 12:3, ROM 12:16; 1CO 3:18-3:21, 1CO 4:7, 1CO 8:2; GAL 5:26; 2TI 3:4

## The folly of conceit
**PRO 26:12** *See also* PRO 28:26; ISA 16:6; HOS 10:13-10:14; JAS 3:5, JAS 4:16

## The danger of conceit for Christians
**1CO 10:12** *See also* ROM 11:25; 2CO 12:7; 1TI 3:6; 1JN 2:16

## Remedies for conceit


### Being aware of one's sin
**LUK 18:9-18:14**

### Being prepared to mix with the lowly
**ROM 12:16**

### Considering others as being superior
**PHP 2:3**
