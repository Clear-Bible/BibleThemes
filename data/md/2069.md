# 2069: Jesus Christ, pre-eminence of

Jesus Christ excels over all of creation and reigns supreme over everthing in the created order.



## The scope of Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### Over all people
**[JHN 17:2](bibleref:43017002)** *See also* [MAT 25:31-25:32](bibleref:40025031-40025032); [ROM 10:12](bibleref:45010012)

### Over his enemies
**[1CO 15:25-15:26](bibleref:46015025-46015026)** *See also* [ACT 2:32-2:35](bibleref:44002032-44002035); [PSA 110:1](bibleref:19110001); [EPH 4:8](bibleref:49004008) *Paul probably interpreted “captives” to refer to the spiritual enemies of Christ defeated at the cross.* 

### Over every power and authority
**[COL 2:15](bibleref:51002015)** *See also* [EPH 1:21](bibleref:49001021)

### Over all traditions and institutions
 [MAT 12:8](bibleref:40012008) parallels MRK 2:28, LUK 6:5

### Over all things
**[1CO 15:28](bibleref:46015028)** *See also* [MAT 11:27](bibleref:40011027) parallel LUK 10:22; [MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018); [JHN 3:35](bibleref:43003035), [JHN 13:3](bibleref:43013003), [JHN 16:33](bibleref:43016033); [ACT 10:36](bibleref:44010036); [PHP 3:21](bibleref:50003021); [HEB 2:8](bibleref:58002008)

**[HEB 8:1-8:6](bibleref:58008001-58008006)** *See also* [HEB 4:14](bibleref:58004014), [HEB 6:20](bibleref:58006020), [HEB 7:23](bibleref:58007023), [HEB 7:26-7:28](bibleref:58007026-58007028), [HEB 9:11-9:14](bibleref:58009011-58009014), [HEB 9:24-9:26](bibleref:58009024-58009026)

## Descriptions of Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### He is the head
**[COL 2:10](bibleref:51002010)** *See also* [EPH 1:9-1:10](bibleref:49001009-49001010), [EPH 1:22](bibleref:49001022), [EPH 4:15](bibleref:49004015), [EPH 5:23-5:24](bibleref:49005023-49005024); [COL 1:18](bibleref:51001018)

### He is the capstone
**[MAT 21:42](bibleref:40021042)** parallels MRK 12:10-12:11, LUK 20:17 *By so saying Jesus Christ applied this verse to himself. The capstone/cornerstone was the most significant stone of a building's structure.*  *See also* [PSA 118:22](bibleref:19118022); [ACT 4:11](bibleref:44004011); [EPH 2:20](bibleref:49002020); [1PE 2:7](bibleref:60002007)

### He is the Chief Shepherd
 [JHN 10:11](bibleref:43010011); [HEB 13:20](bibleref:58013020); [1PE 5:4](bibleref:60005004)

## The reasons for Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### His divine origin
**[JHN 3:31](bibleref:43003031)** *See also* [JHN 1:1-1:2](bibleref:43001001-43001002), [JHN 17:24](bibleref:43017024)

### His equality with the Father
**[JHN 10:30](bibleref:43010030)** *See also* [COL 1:19](bibleref:51001019), [COL 2:9](bibleref:51002009)

### His authority derives from the Father
 [JHN 5:22-5:27](bibleref:43005022-43005027)

### He possesses the rights of the firstborn
**[COL 1:15-1:17](bibleref:51001015-51001017)** *As the firstborn son has the rights and privileges of an heir, so Jesus Christ is heir of all things by virtue of his pre-existence and sonship.*  *See also* [ROM 8:29](bibleref:45008029); [HEB 1:6](bibleref:58001006)

### He has a superior name
**[PHP 2:9](bibleref:50002009)** *See also* [ACT 4:12](bibleref:44004012); [HEB 1:4](bibleref:58001004)

### He is the First and the Last
**[REV 1:17](bibleref:66001017)** *This title implies that all things are summed up in Christ and find their purpose in him.*  *See also* [REV 2:8](bibleref:66002008), [REV 22:13](bibleref:66022013)

 [ROM 1:4](bibleref:45001004); [1CO 15:21-15:23](bibleref:46015021-46015023); [REV 1:5](bibleref:66001005)
