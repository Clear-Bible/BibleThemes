# 2069: Jesus Christ, pre-eminence of

Jesus Christ excels over all of creation and reigns supreme over everthing in the created order.



## The scope of Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### Over all people
**JHN 17:2** *See also* MAT 25:31-25:32; ROM 10:12

### Over his enemies
**1CO 15:25-15:26** *See also* ACT 2:32-2:35; PSA 110:1; EPH 4:8 *Paul probably interpreted “captives” to refer to the spiritual enemies of Christ defeated at the cross.*

### Over every power and authority
**COL 2:15** *See also* EPH 1:21

### Over all traditions and institutions
 MAT 12:8 parallels MRK 2:28, LUK 6:5

### Over all things
**1CO 15:28** *See also* MAT 11:27 parallel LUK 10:22; MAT 28:18; JHN 3:35, JHN 13:3, JHN 16:33; ACT 10:36; PHP 3:21; HEB 2:8

## The pre-eminence of Jesus Christ in relation to others


### To other prophets
 MAT 3:11, MAT 12:41 parallel LUK 11:32; ; JHN 1:26-1:27, JHN 1:30, JHN 3:30; ; HEB 1:1-1:2

### To the patriarchs
 JHN 1:17, JHN 8:52-8:58; ; HEB 3:3-3:6

### To Aaron and other high priests
**HEB 8:1-8:6** *See also* HEB 4:14, HEB 6:20, HEB 7:23, HEB 7:26-7:28, HEB 9:11-9:14, HEB 9:24-9:26

### To angels
**1PE 3:22** *See also* HEB 1:4-1:14

### To other lords and kings
**REV 17:14** *See also* 1TI 6:15; REV 19:16

## Descriptions of Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### He is the head
**COL 2:10** *See also* EPH 1:9-1:10, EPH 1:22, EPH 4:15, EPH 5:23-5:24; COL 1:18

### He is the capstone
**MAT 21:42** parallels MRK 12:10-12:11, LUK 20:17 *By so saying Jesus Christ applied this verse to himself. The capstone/cornerstone was the most significant stone of a building's structure.* *See also* PSA 118:22; ACT 4:11; EPH 2:20; 1PE 2:7

### He is the Chief Shepherd
 JHN 10:11; ; HEB 13:20; ; 1PE 5:4

## The reasons for Jesus Christ's pre-eminence


### His divine origin
**JHN 3:31** *See also* JHN 1:1-1:2, JHN 17:24

### His equality with the Father
**JHN 10:30** *See also* COL 1:19, COL 2:9

### His authority derives from the Father
 JHN 5:22-5:27

### He possesses the rights of the firstborn
**COL 1:15-1:17** *As the firstborn son has the rights and privileges of an heir, so Jesus Christ is heir of all things by virtue of his pre-existence and sonship.* *See also* ROM 8:29; HEB 1:6

### He has a superior name
**PHP 2:9** *See also* ACT 4:12; HEB 1:4

### He is the First and the Last
**REV 1:17** *This title implies that all things are summed up in Christ and find their purpose in him.* *See also* REV 2:8, REV 22:13

## Examples of events whereby Jesus Christ's pre-eminence is recognised


### His entry into Jerusalem
 MAT 21:4-21:9 parallels MRK 11:7-11:10, LUK 19:35-19:38, JHN 12:12-12:15; ; ZEC 9:9

### His resurrection
 ROM 1:4; ; 1CO 15:21-15:23; ; REV 1:5

### His exaltation to the Father's right hand
**ACT 5:31** *See also* ACT 2:33, ACT 7:55; EPH 1:20-1:21, EPH 4:8-4:10; PHP 2:9-2:11; COL 3:1; HEB 8:1, HEB 10:12; 1PE 3:22; REV 3:21
