# 5775: abuse

The deliberate misuse of gifts or privileges, and the insulting of individuals. Scripture stresses that neither God's gifts nor his people are to be abused.



## The abuse of God and his people


### Of God himself
**2KI 19:16**

### Of God's people
**NEH 4:1-4:4**; **1PE 4:4** *See also* MAT 5:11; ACT 2:13, ACT 17:32, ACT 18:6; REV 2:9

### Of God's servants
**ACT 2:13** *See also* 2CH 36:16; PSA 22:6-22:8, PSA 69:7-69:12, PSA 123:3; JER 20:7

### Of Jesus Christ
**LUK 16:14** *See also* LUK 23:11

## The abuse of Christian freedom is condemned


### Believers are required to resist sin
**ROM 6:12** *See also* ROM 6:14; HEB 12:1-12:2; 1JN 5:16-5:18

### Grace does not give believers the freedom to sin
**ROM 6:1-6:2** *See also* ROM 6:15, ROM 3:5-3:8; GAL 2:17-2:21

## The dangers of abusing Christian freedom
 1CO 8:9-8:12 *becoming a stumbling-block to others*; ; GAL 5:13 *indulging oneself*; ; 1PE 2:16 *as means of covering up evil*

## Examples of the abuse of Christian freedom


### Sexual excesses
**1CO 5:1-5:2** *See also* 1CO 6:12-6:20; REV 2:20-2:22

### Abuse of giving
 ACT 5:1-5:2

### Abuse of the Lord's Supper
**1CO 11:20-11:22**

### Falling back into sin
 *See also* ROM 6:1-6:2; HEB 12:1; 1JN 1:8-1:10

### Disobedience towards God
 EPH 5:6; ; 1PE 2:8; ; 1JN 2:3-2:4, 1JN 3:4

### Abuse of spiritual gifts
 1CO 14:1-14:20

### Eating food sacrificed to idols
 1CO 8:1-8:13; ; REV 2:14, REV 2:20
