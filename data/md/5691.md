# 5691: friends, good

Those whose commitment from the heart is shown in practical, often sacrificial, care, love and service. They are a source of encouragement, sympathy, comfort and support in time of need. Such relationships are to be cultivated, especially among believers.



## Examples of good friends


### David and Jonathan
**1SA 18:1-18:3** *See also* 1SA 20:17, 1SA 20:42; 2SA 1:26

### Job's friends
**JOB 2:11**

### Ruth and Naomi
**RUT 1:16-1:17**

### Paul's friends in ministry
**ROM 16:3-16:5** *See also* 2CO 2:12-2:13 *Titus*; PHP 2:25 *Epaphroditus*; COL 4:7 *Tychicus*; COL 4:14 *Luke*; 2TI 1:2-1:4 *Timothy*; PHM 1:1 *Philemon*

### Other examples
 1SA 22:23 *David and Abiathar*; ; 2SA 10:2 *David and Nahash*; ; 2SA 15:19-15:21 *David and Ittai*; ; 2SA 15:32-15:37 *David and Hushai*; ; 1KI 5:1 *David and Hiram*; ; 2KI 2:2 *Elijah and Elisha*; ; 2KI 10:15 *Jehonadab and Jehu*; ; MRK 2:3-2:4 *the friends who brought the paralysed man to Jesus Christ*
