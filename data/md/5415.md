# 5415: money, uses of

Monetary transactions have taken place from the earliest biblical times. Originally precious metals were weighed out; coins were introduced later.



## The first mention of money
**GEN 17:12**

## The use of money


### For the purchase and sale of land
**GEN 23:14-23:15** *See also* JER 32:9-32:10; MAT 27:7; ACT 4:34, ACT 5:1-5:2, ACT 7:16

### For the purchase and sale of food
 GEN 41:57, GEN 42:5, GEN 42:35; ; 2KI 7:16; ; JHN 6:5, JHN 6:7; ; REV 6:6

### For other merchandise
**REV 18:11-18:13** *See also* GEN 37:25; JAS 4:13

### For religious offerings
**EXO 30:11-30:16** *See also* NUM 3:44-3:48; DEU 12:4-12:6; 2KI 22:4-22:6 parallel 2CH 34:9-34:11; EZR 7:15-7:17

### For gifts to the poor
**MAT 26:9** parallels MRK 14:5, JHN 12:5 *See also* ACT 3:3

### For wages
**MAT 20:2** *See also* JAS 5:4

### For investment and moneylending
**MAT 25:27** parallel LUK 19:23 *See also* EXO 22:25; NEH 5:10-5:11; LUK 6:34-6:35

### For provision for the future
 ECC 7:12

### For the settlement of disputes
 EXO 21:8-21:11, EXO 21:35, EXO 22:15

### For taxes
**MAT 17:24** *See also* 1KI 10:15; NEH 5:4; MAT 22:17-22:19 parallels MRK 12:15, LUK 20:22-20:24

### For tribute money
 2KI 15:19-15:20

### For the purchase of slaves
 LEV 22:11

### For bribery
 EST 3:9; ; MAT 28:12-28:13; ; ACT 24:26

### Fortune-telling for money
 MIC 3:11; ; ACT 16:16

### Blood money
 MAT 27:6

## Money changers
**MAT 21:12** parallels MRK 11:15, JHN 2:14
