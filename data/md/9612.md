# 9612: hope, in God

A total grounding of one's confidence and expectation in God's goodness and providential care even in the face of trouble.



## Hope in God is commanded
**PSA 131:3**; **1TI 6:17** *See also* PSA 31:24, PSA 130:7; ROM 12:12; HEB 10:23

## Hope can be placed in Scripture as the word of God
**PSA 119:74** *See also* PSA 119:43, PSA 119:49, PSA 119:81, PSA 119:114, PSA 119:147, PSA 130:5; ISA 42:4; ACT 26:6; ROM 15:4; COL 1:5

## Hope can be placed in God in the face of difficulty or trial
**PSA 42:5**; **2CO 1:10** *See also* EZR 10:2; JOB 5:16, JOB 13:15; PSA 9:18, PSA 25:19-25:21, PSA 119:116; JER 14:19; 1TI 5:5

## The outcome of hoping in God


### It brings security and confidence
**PSA 146:5** *See also* JOB 11:18; PSA 25:3 *There is no shame for those who hope in the LORD*; PSA 33:17-33:18, PSA 33:20-33:22 *The LORD helps and protects those who hope in him*; PSA 39:7, PSA 52:9, PSA 71:5, PSA 147:11; JER 14:22; LAM 3:21-3:22; ACT 24:15; ROM 15:12; 1TI 4:10

### It leads to specific results
**PSA 37:9**; **ISA 40:31**; **ROM 15:13** *See also* PSA 33:22, PSA 62:5 *rest*; PSA 71:14; PRO 24:14-24:16 *wisdom*; ISA 51:5; JER 29:11; LAM 3:25; MIC 7:7; ZEC 9:12 *restoration*; ROM 4:18 *the example of Abraham's hope*; ROM 5:2, ROM 5:5; 2CO 1:7, 2CO 10:15; 1TH 1:3; 2TI 2:25; TIT 1:2 *eternal life*; 1PE 3:5; 1JN 3:3
