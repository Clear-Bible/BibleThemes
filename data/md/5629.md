# 5629: work, as ordained by God

God ordained work as the normal routine of living. Every legitimate human task, therefore, is of intrinsic worth, however menial it may seem, and is potentially a means of glorifying God.



## Work is ordained by God
**[GEN 1:27-1:28](bibleref:01001027-01001028)** *See also* [EXO 20:9](bibleref:02020009) parallel DEU 5:13; [PSA 104:23](bibleref:19104023) *Work is part of the rhythm of life.* 

## God's purposes in ordaining work


### That people should be self-supporting
**[GEN 3:19](bibleref:01003019)** *See also* [PSA 128:2](bibleref:19128002); [1TH 4:12](bibleref:52004012)

### That people should find self-fulfilment
**[ECC 2:24](bibleref:21002024)** *See also* [PRO 14:23](bibleref:20014023); [ECC 3:22](bibleref:21003022), [ECC 5:19](bibleref:21005019)

### That people should serve others
**[EPH 4:28](bibleref:49004028)** *See also* [PRO 31:15](bibleref:20031015); [1TH 2:9](bibleref:52002009); [1TI 5:8](bibleref:54005008)

### That people should glorify God
**[COL 3:17](bibleref:51003017)** *See also* [1CO 10:31](bibleref:46010031); [EPH 6:5-6:8](bibleref:49006005-49006008) parallel COL 3:22-3:24
