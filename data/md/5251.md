# 5251: chains

Used to hold people in captivity, both physically and spiritually, but also an emblem of honour and public office.



## Chains as a means of physical restraint
**MRK 5:4** parallel LUK 8:29**ACT 12:6-12:7** *See also* 2KI 23:33; JER 40:1-40:4; ACT 16:26, ACT 21:33, ACT 22:29, ACT 26:29, ACT 28:20; EPH 6:20; PHP 1:7, PHP 1:13-1:17 *Paul in chains for Christ*; COL 4:3, COL 4:18; 2TI 1:16, 2TI 2:9; PHM 1:10, PHM 1:13; HEB 11:36

## Chains as a metaphor of captivity


### Political bondage
**ISA 45:14** *See also* JOB 36:8-36:9; PSA 2:3, PSA 107:10-107:14; ISA 28:22, ISA 52:2; EZK 7:23; NAM 3:10

### Spiritual bondage to sin
**PSA 116:16** *See also* ISA 58:6-58:10; LAM 3:7

### God's binding of sinful angels
**JUD 1:6** *See also* REV 20:1-20:2

### The domination of others
**ECC 7:26**

## Chains as a form of ornament


### As emblems of honour or rank
**DAN 5:29** *See also* GEN 41:42; DAN 5:7, DAN 5:16

### As a form of jewellery
**PRO 1:9** *See also* EXO 28:22-28:25 *the high priest's breastplate*; EXO 39:15-39:18; JDG 8:26; ISA 3:20

### As adornment of idols
**ISA 40:19**

### As adornment of places of worship
**2CH 3:16** *See also* EXO 28:13-28:14; 1KI 6:21, 1KI 7:17; 2CH 3:5
