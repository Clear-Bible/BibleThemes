# 4460: grass

Green vegetation, which grows rapidly after rainfall, and serves as good food for many animals. The withering of grass in hot weather is often used in Scripture as a symbol of the frailty and brevity of human life.



## Grass is food for animals
**1KI 18:5** *See also* NUM 22:4; DEU 11:15; JOB 6:5, JOB 40:15; PSA 104:14, PSA 106:20; JER 14:5; DAN 4:25, DAN 4:32-4:33, DAN 5:21

## Rain is essential for the growth of grass
**JOB 38:25-38:27** *See also* DEU 32:2; 2SA 23:4; PSA 147:8; PRO 19:12; MIC 5:7

## Grass grows quickly and abundantly
**ISA 44:3-44:4** *See also* JOB 5:25; PSA 72:16, PSA 92:7; ISA 35:7, ISA 66:14

## Grass withers, dies and burns easily
**ISA 15:6** *See also* PSA 102:4, PSA 102:11; ISA 5:24; JER 12:4; REV 8:7

## The life cycle of grass is short
**PSA 129:6** *See also* ISA 37:27; MAT 6:28-6:34 parallel LUK 12:27-12:31

## The brevity of human life is compared to that of grass
**ISA 40:6-40:8** *See also* 2KI 19:25-19:26; JOB 8:11-8:13; PSA 37:1-37:2, PSA 90:5, PSA 103:13-103:19; ISA 51:12; 1PE 1:24-1:25
