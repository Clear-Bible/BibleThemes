# 8825: self-righteousness, and the gospel

Self-righteousness is an obstacle to the gospel, which insists that true righteousness can only be found in Jesus Christ.



## The real issue of righteousness
**JOB 9:2** *See also* 1SA 6:20; JOB 4:17, JOB 15:14, JOB 32:2 *Although Elihu had misjudged Job, his main point, that only God was righteous, was correct*; JOB 40:8; PSA 130:3

## True righteousness is not the result of human effort
**ROM 3:20** *Even though God's law tells people what God requires, human beings cannot keep it because of the weakness of their sinful human natures.* *See also* ROM 9:31; GAL 2:16, GAL 3:11; EPH 2:8-2:9; TIT 3:5; 1JN 2:16

## Self-righteousness is a contradiction in terms
**2CH 6:36**; **ISA 64:6** *See also* EZR 9:15; PSA 14:3; ECC 7:20; DAN 9:7; ROM 3:10, ROM 3:23

## God calls people from their pretended self-righteousness
**ISA 2:22** *See also* PSA 75:2-75:4; JER 17:5; MAT 5:20

## True righteouness


### It comes from God
**ROM 10:3** *See also* PSA 98:2; ISA 46:13, ISA 51:5; JER 9:23-9:24; ROM 1:17

### It is grounded in Jesus Christ
**ACT 13:39** *See also* 1CO 1:30; 2CO 5:21

### It comes about by faith
**ROM 5:1** *See also* GAL 5:5; EPH 2:8

### It is received in humility
**LUK 18:13-18:14** *See also* ROM 3:27, ROM 4:2; 1CO 1:31, 1CO 4:7; 2CO 11:30, 2CO 12:9; GAL 6:13-6:14; EPH 2:9; PHP 3:7-3:9
