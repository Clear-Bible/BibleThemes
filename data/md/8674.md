# 8674: study

The application of the mind with a view to acquiring knowledge, particularly of the Scriptures. God promises his help to those who give themselves to this work.

## The study of the works of the LORD
**[PSA 111:2](bibleref:19111002)** *The works mentioned are his acts of providence and grace.* 

## God's people are to study in order to gain wisdom
**[PRO 2:1-2:5](bibleref:20002001-20002005), [PRO 4:5](bibleref:20004005)** *See also* [JOB 28:12](bibleref:18028012), [JOB 28:20](bibleref:18028020); [PSA 90:12](bibleref:19090012); [PRO 3:13](bibleref:20003013), [PRO 15:14](bibleref:20015014), [PRO 23:23](bibleref:20023023)

## The study of human activity, achievement and wisdom
**[ECC 1:13-1:14](bibleref:21001013-21001014)** *See also* [ECC 1:17](bibleref:21001017), [ECC 2:12](bibleref:21002012), [ECC 7:25](bibleref:21007025), [ECC 8:9](bibleref:21008009), [ECC 8:16](bibleref:21008016), [ECC 9:1](bibleref:21009001), [ECC 12:9-12:11](bibleref:21012009-21012011) *The Teacher turned from human wisdom to searching out and setting forth proverbs* ; [ECC 12:12](bibleref:21012012) *He concluded that all human study was wearisome.*
