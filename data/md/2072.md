# 2072: Jesus Christ, righteousness of

Jesus Christ pleased his Father perfectly in his life on earth and in his death. He now gives believers a new status before God and a new power for living.



## The promised Messiah will be righteous
**ZEC 9:9** *See also* PSA 45:7; ISA 53:11; JER 23:5-23:6, JER 33:15

### The promised Messiah will establish righteousness
 PSA 72:1-72:4; ; ISA 9:7, ISA 11:3-11:5

## Jesus Christ's own righteousness


### Righteousness is characteristic of Jesus Christ
**ACT 22:14** *See also* ACT 3:14, ACT 7:52; 2TI 4:8; HEB 1:8-1:9; 1JN 2:1, 1JN 2:29, 1JN 3:7

### Jesus Christ's obedience shows his righteousness
**JHN 8:28-8:29** *See also* JHN 4:34, JHN 6:38, JHN 12:49-12:50, JHN 14:31; PHP 2:8; HEB 5:8-5:9, HEB 10:9

### Jesus Christ judges righteously
**JHN 5:30** *See also* REV 19:11

### Jesus Christ is declared not guilty
**LUK 23:14-23:15** *See also* LUK 23:4; JHN 19:6; ACT 3:13

### Jesus Christ's righteousness recognised by others
**LUK 23:47** *See also* MRK 10:17-10:18 parallel LUK 18:18-18:19; LUK 23:41; ACT 10:38

## Consequences of Jesus Christ's righteousness


### God justifies believers on account of the righteousness of Jesus Christ
**ROM 5:18-5:19** *See also* ROM 3:23-3:26; 1PE 3:18; 2PE 1:1

### All who believe share in the righteousness of Jesus Christ
**1CO 1:30** *See also* ROM 3:21-3:22, ROM 4:6, ROM 4:24, ROM 9:30, ROM 10:3-10:4; 2CO 5:21

### Believers live by and for Jesus Christ's righteousness
**PHP 1:11** *One of Paul's prayers for Christians.* *See also* ROM 6:18, ROM 8:10; 1PE 2:24
