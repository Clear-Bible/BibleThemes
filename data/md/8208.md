# 8208: commitment, to God

Commitment to God arises from faith in his promises, is expressed in worship and adoration and leads to obedience to his commands.



## Commitment to God commanded
**DEU 27:10**; **JOS 24:14**; **ROM 12:1-12:2** *See also* DEU 6:13, DEU 10:12-10:13, DEU 13:4; JOS 22:5 *Joshua reiterates Moses' commands*; JDG 6:10; 1SA 7:3, 1SA 12:24; 1CH 28:9 *David's instructions to Solomon*; 2CH 19:9; JER 38:20; 1TH 4:1; 2TI 2:22

## Obedience to God's commands as a sign of commitment
**DEU 7:9**; **JOS 24:24**; **ROM 6:17** *See also* EXO 19:8; DEU 11:22; 1KI 8:61; NEH 10:29; PSA 40:6-40:8 *The reference may be to the ear-piercing that was a sign of slaves' voluntary commitment to their masters*; JER 7:23, JER 11:3, JER 38:20, JER 42:6; HAG 1:12; ZEC 6:15JHN 14:15, JHN 15:10
