# 8237: doctrine, false

Teaching that distorts or contradicts the revealed truth of God, causing division and distress in the church by leading people away from the truth.



## False doctrine deviates from the truth


### It contradicts the gospel of Jesus Christ
**[JUD 1:3-1:4](bibleref:65001003-65001004)** *See also* [1CO 3:10-3:13](bibleref:46003010-46003013); [2CO 11:3-11:4](bibleref:47011003-47011004); [GAL 1:6-1:7](bibleref:48001006-48001007); [1TI 6:3-6:4](bibleref:54006003-54006004); [2JN 1:7](bibleref:63001007)

### It may be of human origin
**[COL 2:8](bibleref:51002008)** *See also* [COL 2:20-2:23](bibleref:51002020-51002023); [MAT 15:8-15:9](bibleref:40015008-40015009) parallel MRK 7:6-7:7; [ISA 29:13](bibleref:23029013)
