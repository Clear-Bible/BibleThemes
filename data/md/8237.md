# 8237: doctrine, false

Teaching that distorts or contradicts the revealed truth of God, causing division and distress in the church by leading people away from the truth.



## False doctrine deviates from the truth


### It contradicts the gospel of Jesus Christ
**JUD 1:3-1:4** *See also* 1CO 3:10-3:13; 2CO 11:3-11:4; GAL 1:6-1:7; 1TI 6:3-6:4; 2JN 1:7

### It may be of human origin
**COL 2:8** *See also* COL 2:20-2:23; MAT 15:8-15:9 parallel MRK 7:6-7:7; ISA 29:13

## It may be of demonic origin
**1TI 4:1-4:5** *See also* 1JN 4:2-4:3

## The dangers of false doctrine


### It leads to instability and confusion
**EPH 4:14** *See also* ACT 15:24; GAL 1:7; 1TI 1:18-1:20; 2TI 2:16-2:18; TIT 1:10-1:11; HEB 13:9; 2PE 3:17

### It causes division
**ROM 16:17-16:18** *See also* 1TI 1:3-1:4

### It leads to severe punishment
**GAL 1:8-1:9** *See also* 2CO 11:13-11:15; 2PE 2:1, 2PE 2:17; REV 2:14-2:16

## False doctrine is to be dealt with by the church
**TIT 1:10-1:11** *See also* 1TI 1:3; TIT 1:13-1:14

## False doctrine will be common in later times
**1TI 4:1** *See also* MRK 13:5-13:6 parallel LUK 21:8; 2TH 2:3; 2TI 4:3-4:4
