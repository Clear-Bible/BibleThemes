# 5700: headship

The quality of being in a position of leadership or guidance. Headship has to do with God's relation to this created world, and to his ordering of relationships within it. Headship among human beings does not necessarily signify superior status, but rather a role of leadership and care. Scripture sees it as involving servanthood, and views Jesus Christ as the supreme model of this.



## God as head over all things
**1CH 29:11** *See also* ISA 45:23; DAN 4:34-4:35; ROM 9:14-9:21; 1CO 15:24-15:28

## Headship within the Godhead


### The Father's eternal headship
**1CO 11:3** *See also* 1CO 15:24-15:28; PHP 2:6

### The Father's headship in the Son's earthly life and ministry
**JHN 6:38** *See also* MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; PHP 2:6-2:8; HEB 5:7-5:8

## Jesus Christ as head of all creation


### Head of the created order
**COL 1:15-1:17** *The description of Jesus Christ as the firstborn does not imply he is part of creation, but rather stresses his rights of inheritance over it.* *See also* HEB 1:2-1:4

### Head of all earthly and spiritual powers
**EPH 1:20-1:22** *See also* COL 1:15-1:18, COL 2:10; HEB 2:8

### Head of the church
**EPH 4:15-4:16** *See also* EPH 5:23; COL 1:18, COL 2:19

### Head over all things at the end
**EPH 1:9-1:10** *See also* 1CO 15:25-15:27; REV 19:11-19:16

## Delegated headship in God's world


### Headship of humanity over creation
**GEN 1:28** *See also* PSA 8:1-8:9; HEB 2:5-2:8

### Headship of rulers over their nations
**PSA 18:43** *See also* 1SA 15:17; ISA 7:8-7:9; DAN 2:31-2:38; ROM 13:1-13:6; 1PE 2:13-2:14

### Headship of chiefs over their tribes and families
**JOS 22:13-22:14** *See also* NUM 1:1-1:16; JDG 10:17-11:11; 1CH 5:15

### Headship of the husband over his wife
**EPH 5:22-5:24** *See also* 1CO 11:3-11:16 *Women, including wives, were told to cover their heads when praying or prophesying in public as an acknowledgment, apparently, that headship attaches to males*; COL 3:18; 1PE 3:1-3:6

### Headship of leaders over the people of God
**1TH 5:12** *See also* 1SA 24:1-24:7; ISA 3:1-3:7 *The removal of headship and leadership is seen as a mark of God's judgment*; HEB 13:17

### Humility, love and respect are to be the framework for delegated headship
**1PE 5:1-5:3** *See also* EPH 5:21, EPH 5:25-5:30
