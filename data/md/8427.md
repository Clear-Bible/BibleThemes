# 8427: evangelism, kinds of

Scripture recognises that evangelism takes place in a variety of contexts, and offers models for evangelism in today's church.



## Public evangelism


### Preaching in synagogues
**ACT 14:1** *Paul begins his evangelism in Gentile cities by building links within the Jewish community.* ACT 13:14-13:16, ACT 13:42-13:44; 
ACT 17:2 *in Thessalonica*; ; ACT 17:10 *in Berea*; ; ACT 17:17 *in Athens*; ; ACT 18:4 *in Corinth*; ; ACT 19:8 *in Ephesus*

### Preaching in recognised meeting-places
**ACT 17:19-17:23** *See also* ACT 5:25, ACT 10:27-10:28, ACT 16:13, ACT 19:9

### Making a public defence to accusers
**ACT 21:37-22:1** *See also* ACT 4:7-4:12, ACT 7:1-7:2, ACT 24:10, ACT 26:1

## Personal evangelism


### Giving personal testimony
**1JN 1:1-1:3** *See also* MRK 5:19; JHN 4:39; ACT 26:9-26:18; 1CO 7:16; 1PE 3:1-3:2

### Evangelism in homes
**ACT 10:24-10:25** *Peter visits Cornelius' home to speak to those assembled.* LUK 5:29; ; ACT 18:26, ACT 28:30-28:31; ACT 9:17 parallel ACT 22:12; ; ACT 16:32-16:34

## Evangelism in strategic areas


### Going to major centres
 ACT 16:12 *Philippi*; ; ACT 17:1 *Thessalonica*; ; ACT 17:15 *Athens*; ; ACT 18:1 *Corinth*; ; ACT 18:19 *Ephesus*; ACT 23:11, ACT 28:14

### Going into new territories
 ROM 15:20, ROM 15:23-15:24; ; 2CO 10:15-10:16

## Evangelism among strategic people


### Speaking to prominent people
**ACT 18:8** *Though the gospel is for all people without distinction, evangelism among certain people and groups enabled the message to spead further.* *See also* ACT 13:7 *Paul before Sergius Paulus, proconsul of Cyprus*; ACT 24:24 *Paul before Felix and his wife*; ACT 25:8-25:9 *Paul before Festus*; ACT 25:11-25:12 *Paul appeals to Caesar*; ACT 26:2-26:3 *Paul before King Agrippa*; ACT 28:7-28:8 *Paul in the home of the chief official on Malta*

### Evangelising households
**ACT 16:34** *The conversion of the head of the household was often accompanied by the conversion of the whole family group.* *See also* JHN 4:53; ACT 11:14, ACT 18:8

## Using literature in evangelism
**JHN 20:30-20:31** *See also* LUK 1:1-1:4; ACT 1:1-1:2

## Appealing to Scripture in evangelism
**ACT 26:22-26:23** *See also* ACT 8:35, ACT 10:43, ACT 13:22-13:23; ROM 1:2-1:3, ROM 16:25-16:26; 1CO 15:3-15:4

## Signs and wonders in evangelism


### Miracles confirm the message
**ACT 8:6** *See also* MRK 16:17-16:20; ACT 4:14, ACT 19:10-19:12; 1CO 2:4-2:5; HEB 2:3-2:4

### Miracles give opportunity for preaching
**ACT 3:9-3:16** *See also* ACT 2:5-2:14, ACT 14:8-14:11, ACT 16:26-16:31

### Miracles result in people believing
**ACT 9:40-9:42** *See also* JHN 7:31, JHN 11:45; ACT 9:33-9:35, ACT 13:10-13:12

## The response to evangelism


### Acceptance of the message
**ACT 2:37-2:41** *See also* ACT 4:4, ACT 17:4, ACT 17:34, ACT 19:17-19:20; 1TH 2:13

### Rejection of the message
**HEB 4:2** *See also* ROM 10:16; ACT 13:50-13:51, ACT 17:32, ACT 18:6
