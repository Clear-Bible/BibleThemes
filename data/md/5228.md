# 5228: battering-rams


## Battering-rams used to make breaches in the walls of besieged cities


### Historic references to the breaking down of walls
 [2SA 20:15](bibleref:10020015) *The wall of Abel Beth Maacah. References to breached or broken walls are evidence that the use of battering-rams was common* ; [2KI 14:13](bibleref:12014013) parallel 2CH 25:23; [JER 39:2](bibleref:24039002) parallels 2CH 36:19, JER 52:7; [NEH 1:3](bibleref:16001003)

### Figurative references to the breaking down of walls
 [PSA 89:40](bibleref:19089040) *referring to God's displeasure with David's dynasty* 

### Prophetic references to the breaking down of walls as a picture of the LORD's power
 [ISA 22:5](bibleref:23022005), [ISA 24:12](bibleref:23024012); [EZK 21:22](bibleref:26021022), [EZK 26:9](bibleref:26026009); [MIC 2:13](bibleref:33002013)

### Peace is symbolised by no breaching of walls
 [PSA 144:14](bibleref:19144014)

## Model battering-rams were part of the symbolic representation of the siege of Jerusalem
 [EZK 4:2](bibleref:26004002)
