# 7316: blood, and OT sacrifices

The pouring out of animals' blood in sacrifice was God's provision, under the old covenant, for the atonement of sin.



## God's provision of blood sacrifice to establish the old covenant
**[EXO 24:4-24:8](bibleref:02024004-02024008)** *See also* [HEB 9:18-9:20](bibleref:58009018-58009020)

## God's provision of blood sacrifices within the old covenant


### Blood sacrifices and the priesthood
 [EXO 29:10-29:21](bibleref:02029010-02029021) parallel LEV 8:14-8:24; [LEV 9:8-9:14](bibleref:03009008-03009014), [LEV 16:1-16:14](bibleref:03016001-03016014)

### Blood sacrifices and various offerings
 [LEV 1:1-1:17](bibleref:03001001-03001017) *the burnt offering, to gain favour or express devotion* ; [LEV 3:1-3:17](bibleref:03003001-03003017) *the fellowship, or peace, offering, to express gratitude* ; [LEV 4:1-4:35](bibleref:03004001-03004035) *the sin offering, to gain forgiveness* ; [LEV 5:14-5:19](bibleref:03005014-03005019) *the guilt offering, where restitution was required* ; [LEV 16:15-16:22](bibleref:03016015-03016022) *the Day of Atonement, for the cleansing of the nation from unintentional sin* 

### Blood sacrifices and the Passover
**[EXO 12:1-12:14](bibleref:02012001-02012014)** *See also* [2CH 30:15-30:20](bibleref:14030015-14030020), [2CH 35:1-35:19](bibleref:14035001-14035019); [EZR 6:19-6:21](bibleref:15006019-15006021); [MRK 14:12](bibleref:41014012) parallel LUK 22:7-22:8

## Blood sacrifices must come from animals without defect
**[LEV 22:17-22:25](bibleref:03022017-03022025)** *See also* [EXO 12:5](bibleref:02012005); [LEV 1:3](bibleref:03001003), [LEV 3:1](bibleref:03003001), [LEV 4:3](bibleref:03004003), [LEV 5:15](bibleref:03005015); [NUM 28:9](bibleref:04028009), [NUM 28:11](bibleref:04028011); [DEU 15:21](bibleref:05015021); [EZK 43:22-43:27](bibleref:26043022-26043027)

## Blood sacrifices must not come from human beings
**[2KI 23:10](bibleref:12023010)** *See also* [2KI 17:17](bibleref:12017017), [2KI 21:1-21:6](bibleref:12021001-12021006); [EZK 20:25-20:26](bibleref:26020025-26020026); [MIC 6:7](bibleref:33006007)

## The limited effect of blood sacrifices


### They can become mere external ritual
**[ISA 1:11-1:13](bibleref:23001011-23001013)** *See also* [ISA 66:2-66:4](bibleref:23066002-23066004)

### They are worthless without obedience to God
**[HOS 6:6](bibleref:28006006)** *See also* [1SA 15:22-15:23](bibleref:09015022-09015023); [PSA 40:6-40:8](bibleref:19040006-19040008); [AMO 4:4-4:5](bibleref:30004004-30004005), [AMO 5:21-5:27](bibleref:30005021-30005027)

### They are unable to cleanse the conscience
**[HEB 9:9-9:10](bibleref:58009009-58009010)** *See also* [HEB 10:1-10:4](bibleref:58010001-58010004)
