# 7967: spiritual gifts, and responsibility

A spiritual gift may be intended to equip its recipient for a specific function or appointment. Those who are equipped in this way need the enabling power of God's spirit to carry out their appointed tasks.



## OT examples of God's gifts or appointments


### God appoints prophets
**[JER 1:5](bibleref:24001005)**

### God appoints Israel's kings
**[1SA 15:11](bibleref:09015011)** *See also* [2SA 7:8](bibleref:10007008), [2SA 12:7](bibleref:10012007)

### God appoints the Persian king Cyrus
**[ISA 45:1](bibleref:23045001)** *See also* [ISA 41:2](bibleref:23041002)

### God empowers leaders and kings with his Spirit
**[NUM 27:18](bibleref:04027018)** *See also* [JDG 3:10](bibleref:07003010), [JDG 6:34](bibleref:07006034), [JDG 11:29](bibleref:07011029), [JDG 13:25](bibleref:07013025), [JDG 14:6](bibleref:07014006), [JDG 14:19](bibleref:07014019), [JDG 15:14](bibleref:07015014); [1SA 10:6](bibleref:09010006), [1SA 10:10](bibleref:09010010), [1SA 11:6](bibleref:09011006), [1SA 16:13-16:14](bibleref:09016013-09016014)

**[ISA 42:1](bibleref:23042001)** *See also* [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001)
