# 8125: guidance, God's promises of

Scripture bears witness to God's promises of guidance for his people.



## All God's intentions will be realised


### God has purposes
**ISA 14:27** *See also* JOB 36:5; ISA 55:10-55:11; JER 23:20; ROM 8:28; EPH 3:10-3:11; 2TI 1:9; HEB 6:17

### God has plans
**LAM 2:17** *See also* PSA 33:11, PSA 40:5; PRO 16:9; ISA 14:24, ISA 25:1, ISA 37:26

### God desires his purposes and plans to be fulfilled
**LUK 22:42** parallels MAT 26:39, MRK 14:36**ACT 21:14** *See also* 1CH 13:2; PSA 57:2, PSA 138:8; ACT 18:21; ROM 1:10, ROM 15:32; JAS 4:13-4:15

## Examples of God working out his purposes
**ACT 2:23** ISA 46:10-46:11, ISA 48:14; 
JER 29:10-29:11 *restoration of Israel after the exile*; ; ACT 13:36 *David*; ; ROM 9:11-9:12 *Esau and Jacob*; ; ROM 9:17 *Pharaoh*

## God promises to guide Israel and Judah
**EXO 15:13** *See also* EXO 32:34; PSA 23:2-23:3, PSA 48:14; ISA 30:21, ISA 49:10, ISA 58:11; JER 31:8-31:9; EZK 34:11-34:16; LUK 1:76-1:79
