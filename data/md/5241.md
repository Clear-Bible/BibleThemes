# 5241: burial

The burying of a dead body, usually in a family tomb. The lack of a proper burial was regarded as a great misfortune and disgrace. Burial was usually accompanied by mourning.



## Burial in a family tomb
**GEN 49:29-49:31** *See also* GEN 23:19-23:20, GEN 25:9-25:10, GEN 50:13; JDG 8:32, JDG 16:31; 1SA 25:1 *“at his home” probably refers to the family tomb, but could mean more literally under the floor of the house or yard*; 2SA 2:32, 2SA 17:23

## Other places of burial
 GEN 35:8, GEN 35:19-35:20

## Burial of criminals hung on a tree
**DEU 21:22-21:23** *See also* JHN 19:31 *The request of the Jews was probably not only to avoid desecrating a special Sabbath, but also to identify Jesus Christ as one cursed by God.*

## Burial of an executed criminal or enemy under a heap of stones
 JOS 7:26, JOS 8:29, JOS 10:27; ; 2SA 18:17

## Lack of a proper burial
**JER 22:18-22:19** *See also* DEU 28:26; 2SA 21:12-21:14; 1KI 13:22; ECC 6:3; JER 7:33, JER 16:4, JER 25:33

## Common burial-grounds
 2KI 23:6; ; JER 26:23; ; MAT 27:7

## Burial accompanied by mourning
**ACT 8:2** *See also* DEU 34:5-34:8

## Preparation of Jesus Christ's body for burial
**JHN 19:39-19:40** *See also* MAT 26:12 parallel MRK 14:8; MRK 16:1 parallels LUK 24:1, JHN 12:7
