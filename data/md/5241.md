# 5241: burial

The burying of a dead body, usually in a family tomb. The lack of a proper burial was regarded as a great misfortune and disgrace. Burial was usually accompanied by mourning.

## Burial in a family tomb
**[GEN 49:29-49:31](bibleref:01049029-01049031)** *See also* [GEN 23:19-23:20](bibleref:01023019-01023020), [GEN 25:9-25:10](bibleref:01025009-01025010), [GEN 50:13](bibleref:01050013); [JDG 8:32](bibleref:07008032), [JDG 16:31](bibleref:07016031); [1SA 25:1](bibleref:09025001) *“at his home” probably refers to the family tomb, but could mean more literally under the floor of the house or yard* ; [2SA 2:32](bibleref:10002032), [2SA 17:23](bibleref:10017023)

## Other places of burial

## Burial of criminals hung on a tree
