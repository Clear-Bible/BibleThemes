# 1461: truth, nature of

God is the essence of truth and this quality should be reflected in the character of his children. Falsehood is condemned in Scripture, particularly in legal contexts, but truth is praised and advocated. God takes pleasure in truth and honesty, which are marks of an upright person.



## Truth originates with God
**PSA 43:3** *See also* PSA 25:5, PSA 26:3, PSA 86:11; ISA 65:16; DAN 9:13

## Truth is an aspect of God's character


### It is demonstrated in his faithfulness and reliability
**NUM 23:19**; **PSA 33:4** *See also* 1SA 15:29; PSA 31:5, PSA 40:10-40:11, PSA 119:160; ISA 45:19; JER 10:10, JER 42:5; DAN 10:21; MIC 7:20

### It is demonstrated in his justice and mercy
**PSA 96:13** *The Hebraic concept of truth embraces the ideas of faithfulness, reliability and justice.* *See also* PSA 119:30, PSA 119:43-119:44, PSA 119:142, PSA 119:151

## Truth should be reflected in the character of God's people
**ZEC 8:16** *See also* 1KI 17:22-17:24; PSA 15:1-15:5, PSA 51:6, PSA 145:18; PRO 23:23; JER 4:2, JER 5:1; ZEC 7:9, ZEC 8:3, ZEC 8:19; MAL 2:6

## Absence of truth in the human character displeases God
**ISA 59:14-59:15** *See also* ISA 48:1; JER 5:3, JER 7:28, JER 9:3

## Truth as an abstract quality
**PSA 45:4** *See also* PRO 23:23; DAN 8:12

## Truth is the opposite of falsehood
**PRO 16:13** *See also* 1KI 17:24, 1KI 22:16 parallel 2CH 18:15; NEH 6:6; PRO 8:7, PRO 12:17; ISA 45:19; ZEC 8:16

## Truth is subject to proof
**GEN 42:14-42:16** *See also* DEU 13:12-13:15, DEU 17:2-17:5, DEU 19:16-19:19, DEU 22:20-22:21; 1KI 10:6-10:7 parallel 2CH 9:5-9:6; JOB 5:27

## Truth is commended in Scripture
**PRO 12:22** *See also* EXO 20:16 parallel DEU 5:20; EXO 23:1, EXO 23:7; PRO 12:19, PRO 13:5, PRO 14:25, PRO 19:5, PRO 19:9; EPH 4:25; REV 22:14-22:15

## Lack of truth is a characteristic of apostasy
**JER 7:28** *See also* PSA 52:1-52:4; ISA 59:12-59:15; JER 9:5-9:6; AMO 5:10

## Truth as an expression of affirmation
**DAN 11:2** *See also* JOS 7:20; RUT 3:12; 2KI 19:17; JER 26:15; MAT 5:18, MAT 8:10, MAT 21:21; MRK 8:12, MRK 14:30; LUK 4:24, LUK 21:32; JHN 3:3, JHN 8:51, JHN 21:18

## Amen as a liturgical affirmation of truth


### Of oaths and curses
**NUM 5:22** *The word “amen” is closely related to the word for “truth” in Hebrew. It constitutes an affirmation of the truth of what has been said and an acceptance of its consequences.* *See also* DEU 27:15-27:26; NEH 5:13; JER 11:5

### Of benedictions and doxologies
**1CH 16:36** *See also* NEH 8:6; PSA 41:13, PSA 72:19, PSA 89:52, PSA 106:48

### In general use
**JER 28:6** *See also* 1KI 1:32-1:37

### In the NT
**ROM 1:25** *By NT times, the word “Amen” had become a common liturgical phrase of affirmation, used after prayers, benedictions and doxologies.* *See also* MAT 6:13; ROM 9:5, ROM 11:36; 1CO 14:16; 2CO 1:20; GAL 6:18; PHP 4:20; 1TI 1:17; HEB 13:20-13:21; 2PE 3:18; JUD 1:25; REV 1:6-1:7, REV 7:12, REV 22:20
