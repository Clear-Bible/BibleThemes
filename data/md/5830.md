# 5830: delight

A sense of joy and pleasure, experienced especially through achievements or relationships.



## God as the source of delight


### Delight in the person of God
**ISA 61:10** *See also* NEH 1:11; JOB 22:26; PSA 22:8, PSA 37:4, PSA 43:3-43:4; ISA 58:13-58:14

### Delight in the word of God
**PSA 119:16**; **ROM 7:22** *See also* PSA 1:2, PSA 19:7-19:14, PSA 119:24, PSA 119:47; JER 15:16

### Delight in the works of God
 1SA 2:1; ; PSA 35:4-35:10; ; ACT 11:23

### Over his world
**PSA 104:31** *See also* GEN 1:31; PRO 8:30-8:31

### Over his people

**PRO 11:1** *See also* PRO 11:20, PRO 12:22; JER 9:24
