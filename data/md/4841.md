# 4841: narrowness

A state in which something is confined and restricted, lacking in breadth. It usually has pejorative implications, but Jesus Christ described the way of salvation in terms of narrowness.



## Narrowness equated with no escape
**NUM 22:24-22:26** *See also* PRO 23:26-23:28

## Narrowness associated with discomfort
**ISA 28:20**

## The narrow gate
**MAT 7:13-7:14** *See also* LUK 13:22-13:30

## Narrowness in the architecture of the temple
**1KI 6:4** *See also* EZK 40:16, EZK 40:25, EZK 41:16, EZK 41:26, EZK 42:5
