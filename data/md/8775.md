# 8775: libertinism

A form of abuse of Christian freedom which refuses to acknowledge any obligation for Christians to obey the law or to restrain sin.

## Grace must not lead to libertinism
**[ROM 6:1-6:2](bibleref:45006001-45006002), [ROM 6:15](bibleref:45006015)** *See also* [ROM 3:5-3:8](bibleref:45003005-45003008); [GAL 2:17-2:21](bibleref:48002017-48002021)
