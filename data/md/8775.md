# 8775: libertinism

A form of abuse of Christian freedom which refuses to acknowledge any obligation for Christians to obey the law or to restrain sin.



## Grace must not lead to libertinism
**ROM 6:1-6:2, ROM 6:15** *See also* ROM 3:5-3:8; GAL 2:17-2:21

## Believers must resist sin
**ROM 6:12, ROM 6:14** *See also* HEB 12:1-12:2; 1JN 5:16-5:18

## The law remains valid for believers
**MAT 5:17-5:18** *See also* JOS 23:6; 1CH 22:12; PRO 28:7; ROM 7:12; 1TI 1:8

## The dangers of libertinism


### Falling back into sin
 ROM 6:1-6:2; ; HEB 12:1; ; 1JN 1:8-1:10

### Disobedience towards God
**1JN 2:3-2:4** *See also* 1JN 3:4; 2CO 10:6; EPH 5:6; 1PE 2:8

### Selfishness
**1JN 3:17** *See also* LUK 6:32-6:34; 2TI 2:1-2:4; 1JN 2:9-2:11
