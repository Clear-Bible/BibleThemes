# 5004: human race, and sin

As a result of the fall, human beings have become separated from God through sin, and are divided amongst themselves. All are in a condition of spiritual blindness, slavery and death, and are incapable of saving themselves from this situation.



## All the human race is sinful
**[ROM 3:9-3:12](bibleref:45003009-45003012)** *See also* [PSA 14:1-14:3](bibleref:19014001-19014003), [PSA 53:1-53:3](bibleref:19053001-19053003); [GEN 6:5-6:13](bibleref:01006005-01006013); [JOB 15:14-15:16](bibleref:18015014-18015016); [ECC 7:20](bibleref:21007020); [ROM 1:18-1:32](bibleref:45001018-45001032); [REV 18:1-18:5](bibleref:66018001-66018005) *Babylon symbolises human society which in its opposition to God has become thoroughly corrupt.*
