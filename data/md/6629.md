# 6629: conversion, examples of

Scripture gives examples of individuals and peoples who have turned to God in conversion.



## Examples of conversion in the OT


### Peoples who turned to God
 [ISA 19:22](bibleref:23019022) *Egypt* ; [JON 3:5-3:10](bibleref:32003005-32003010) *Nineveh* 

## Individuals who turned to God
 [2KI 5:15](bibleref:12005015) *Naaman* ; [2CH 33:12-33:13](bibleref:14033012-14033013) *Manasseh* ; [DAN 4:34-4:37](bibleref:27004034-27004037) *Nebuchadnezzar* ; [JON 1:16](bibleref:32001016) *sailors on board ship with Jonah* 

## Examples of conversion in the NT


### Examples of people in general
 [JHN 4:42](bibleref:43004042) *people of Sychar* ; [ACT 2:41](bibleref:44002041) *3,000 people on the day of Pentecost* ; [ACT 8:12](bibleref:44008012) *Samaritans* ; [ACT 9:35](bibleref:44009035) *people of Lydda and Sharon* ; [ACT 15:3](bibleref:44015003) *Gentile converts* 

### Examples of individual converts
 [LUK 15:18](bibleref:42015018) *the prodigal son* ; [LUK 19:8-19:9](bibleref:42019008-42019009) *Zacchaeus* ; [ACT 8:35-8:38](bibleref:44008035-44008038) *the Ethiopian eunuch* [ACT 9:3-9:18](bibleref:44009003-44009018) parallels ACT 22:6-22:11, ACT 26:12-26:18
[ACT 10:44-10:48](bibleref:44010044-44010048) *Cornelius* ; [ACT 13:12](bibleref:44013012) *the proconsul, Sergius Paulus* ; [ACT 16:14-16:15](bibleref:44016014-44016015) *Lydia* ; [ACT 16:29-16:34](bibleref:44016029-44016034) *the Philippian jailer*
