# 5619: weights and measures, distance and area

Diverse expressions were used for these measurements, their being determined in the most practical way possible, reckoned by known averages.

### Some distance
**[EXO 33:7](bibleref:02033007)** *A variety of distance often lies behind this expression, as can be seen in the examples that follow.*  *See also* [GEN 35:16](bibleref:01035016), [GEN 36:6](bibleref:01036006); [NUM 2:2](bibleref:04002002); [1SA 26:13](bibleref:09026013); [2SA 15:17](bibleref:10015017); [MAT 8:30](bibleref:40008030)

### A Sabbath day's journey
**[ACT 1:12](bibleref:44001012)** *While this is the only mention of this phrase in Scripture, it was in fact a popular expression, indicating the distance a devout Jew could walk on the Sabbath. It was based on the rabbinical interpretation of Ex 16:29 in the light of Nu 35:5, and was limited to 2,000 cubits.*

## Expressions of area


### Area expressed by the yoke
**[1SA 14:14](bibleref:09014014)** *The area translated “about half an acre” in the Hebrew means “half a yoke”. The yoke was the area of land ploughed by a yoke of oxen in one day, approximately one acre.*  *See also* [ISA 5:10](bibleref:23005010) *The Hebrew describes the area as “ten-yoke”, that is, the land ploughed by ten yoke of oxen in one day.*
