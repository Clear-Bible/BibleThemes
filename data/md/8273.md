# 8273: holiness, ethical aspects of

Behaviour that reflects the holy character of God himself is to be expressed in both social and personal dimensions of life.



## Holiness in practice is a reflection of God's own character
**1PE 1:15-1:16** *See also* LEV 11:44-11:45, LEV 19:2, LEV 20:7; EPH 4:24; 1JN 3:3

## Holiness demands a different way of life


### Shunning practices that defile
**LEV 18:1-18:3**; **EPH 5:11-5:12**; **1TI 5:22** *See also* LEV 18:21-18:24, LEV 18:29-18:30, LEV 20:1-20:3, LEV 20:6-20:7, LEV 20:23-20:26, LEV 21:7; 2CO 6:17-7:1; GAL 5:19-5:21, GAL 5:24; EPH 5:3-5:7; COL 3:5-3:10

### Obedience to God's law
**LEV 20:7-20:8** *See also* LEV 18:4-18:5, LEV 19:37; DEU 6:25, DEU 28:9; PSA 119:9; ROM 7:12; 1PE 1:22

## Holiness is expressed in social behaviour


### Care for the disadvantaged
**LEV 19:9-19:10** *See also* LEV 19:14, LEV 19:33-19:34; 1TI 5:3-5:4, 1TI 5:8; JAS 1:27

### A concern for truth and justice
**LEV 19:15-19:16** *See also* LEV 19:11-19:13, LEV 19:35-19:37

### Loving one's neighbour
**LEV 19:18** *See also* LEV 19:16-19:17 *Most of the OT law can be seen as an expression of holiness.*

## Holiness is expressed in family and sexual relations
**1TH 4:3-4:7** *See also* LEV 18:5-18:20, LEV 18:22-18:23, LEV 19:3, LEV 20:9; EPH 5:3; 1CO 6:13-6:15, 1CO 6:18-6:19

## Holiness is seen in personal character
**COL 3:12** *See also* EPH 4:23-4:24, EPH 4:32-5:2; 2TI 2:22
