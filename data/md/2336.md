# 2336: Jesus Christ, exaltation of

Having completed his work on earth, Jesus Christ is raised to God's right hand where he receives honour, power and glory.



## Jesus Christ is exalted to God's right hand
**[1PE 3:21-3:22](bibleref:60003021-60003022)** *God's right hand is the position of authority second only to the Father.*  *See also* [MAT 26:64](bibleref:40026064) parallels MRK 14:62, LUK 22:69; [ACT 2:33](bibleref:44002033), [ACT 3:20-3:21](bibleref:44003020-44003021), [ACT 7:55-7:56](bibleref:44007055-44007056); [HEB 1:3](bibleref:58001003), [HEB 12:2](bibleref:58012002)

## Jesus Christ is exalted because he first humbled himself
**[LUK 24:26](bibleref:42024026)** *See also* [PHP 2:6-2:11](bibleref:50002006-50002011); [1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016); [REV 5:6-5:14](bibleref:66005006-66005014), [REV 7:17](bibleref:66007017), [REV 22:3](bibleref:66022003)

## Jesus Christ's exaltation follows his resurrection and ascension
**[1PE 1:21](bibleref:60001021)** *See also* [MRK 16:19](bibleref:41016019); [ACT 13:37](bibleref:44013037); [EPH 1:20](bibleref:49001020)

## Jesus Christ's exaltation is predicted in the OT


### As God's servant
**[ISA 52:13](bibleref:23052013)** *This “servant” is a specific prediction of Jesus Christ.*
