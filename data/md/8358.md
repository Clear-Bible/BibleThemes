# 8358: weakness, physical

Lack of human strength may have a number of causes. Believers should be caring and compassionate towards the weak.



## Causes of human physical weakness


### Sin
**ROM 8:3** *See also* PSA 90:10; ROM 5:6; HEB 5:2

### Divine action
**GEN 32:25** *Through wrestling with God, Jacob carried a physical reminder for the rest of his life.* *See also* GEN 32:32; HEB 11:21

### Disobedience
**JDG 16:20** *See also* JDG 16:7, JDG 16:11, JDG 16:13, JDG 16:17; JOB 18:7; EZK 7:17, EZK 21:7; 1CO 11:30

### Satanic action
**2CO 12:7** *See also* ISA 14:12; MRK 9:17-9:18; LUK 13:11

### Disease or disability
 PSA 6:2, PSA 31:9-31:10; ; JHN 5:7; ; GAL 4:13-4:14

### Weakness from birth
**ACT 3:2**

### Weakness in old age
**GEN 27:1** *See also* 2SA 19:34-19:37; 1KI 1:1
