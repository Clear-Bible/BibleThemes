# 5531: skill

A trade or craft acquired by training or practice, or a special ability or expertise that may be an endowment of the Holy Spirit. Scripture lists many skills and abilities, particularly used in the building of the tabernacle and temple.



## Skill offered in God's service
**EXO 35:10**; **1CH 22:15-22:16** *See also* 1CH 28:21

## Skill in particular crafts


### In working metal
**2CH 2:7** JDG 17:4; ; ACT 19:24; 
1KI 7:14-7:15, 1KI 7:41-7:45 parallel 2CH 4:12-4:16; 2CH 3:5-3:7; ; ISA 46:6; 
2TI 4:14

### In working stone
**1CH 22:2** *See also* 1KI 5:15-5:18 parallel 2CH 2:2; 1KI 6:36, 1KI 7:9-7:12

### With timber
**1KI 5:6** parallel 2CH 2:8-2:9 *See also* 1KI 6:14-6:36

### With fabrics
**EXO 38:23** EXO 26:1, EXO 26:36, EXO 28:39; EXO 29:5, EXO 39:1-39:5; 
EXO 35:25 *skill in spinning*

### With music
 1CH 15:22; ; 2CH 34:12-34:13; ; PSA 33:3

### Other skills
 GEN 25:27 *hunting*; 1KI 9:27; ; EZK 27:8; 
JOB 32:22 *flattery*; ; PSA 45:1 *writing*; PSA 58:5; ; ISA 3:3;

### In constructing idols
**ISA 44:13** *See also* ISA 40:19-40:20, ISA 41:7; JER 10:9; HOS 13:2; ACT 17:29

### Doing evil
**JER 4:22** *See also* ISA 25:11; EZK 21:31; MIC 7:3

## Acquiring skill


### Through training
**1CH 5:18**; **2CH 2:13-2:14** GEN 14:14; ; 2CH 26:11; 
1CH 25:7 *musical skill*; ; LUK 6:40 *teaching abilities*; ; 1CO 9:25 *Athletic prowess requires strict training*; ; HEB 5:14 *distinguishing good from evil through proper training*

### Skills given by God
**EXO 31:1-31:6** parallel EXO 35:30-35:35 *These skills may well have been developed through training, but they are recognised as gifts from God.* *See also* EXO 36:1-36:2; DEU 33:11 *Moses prays for Levi's skills to be blessed by God*; ISA 50:4 *Skill in the right use of words comes from God*; ROM 12:6-12:8 *Special abilities are given by God to believers.*
