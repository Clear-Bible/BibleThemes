# 5833: diligence

Conscientious hard work and perseverance, particularly with regard to a relationship with God. In Scripture diligence is highly commended. It may, however, be misguided.



## Diligence and its results are commended
**PRO 21:5** *See also* 1KI 11:28; 2CH 24:13; EZR 5:8, EZR 6:12, EZR 7:23; PRO 12:14, PRO 14:23, PRO 31:17 *characteristic of the wife of noble character*; ECC 3:13; JER 31:16; 2CO 8:11 *regarding the collection for the poor Christians in Jerusalem*; 1TI 5:17; 1PE 5:2

## Diligence contrasted with laziness
**PRO 10:4** *See also* PRO 12:24, PRO 12:27, PRO 13:4; 2TH 3:10

## Diligence is important in the outworking of one's relationship with God
**1CO 15:58**; **1TI 4:15-4:16**

### Examples of diligence
 *See also* 2CH 15:15, 2CH 29:34 *the Levites*; ISA 62:6-62:7; ZEC 6:15; LUK 8:15; ACT 17:11 *the Bereans*ROM 1:15; 1CO 4:12, 1CO 9:24-9:26; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 11:27; 1TH 2:9; 2TH 3:8; 2TI 4:7

**LUK 9:51** *See also* HEB 12:1-12:2
