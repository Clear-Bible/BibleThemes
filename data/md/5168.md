# 5168: muteness

The inability to speak or to make sounds comprehensible as speech.



## Physical muteness


### A disability, sometimes associated with demon-possession, healed by Jesus Christ
**MAT 9:32-9:33** *See also* MAT 12:22 parallel LUK 11:14; MAT 15:30-15:31; MRK 7:31-7:37, MRK 9:14-9:29

**MAT 12:22-12:23** *See also* ISA 32:4, ISA 35:6; MAT 4:23, MAT 9:33
