# 5378: law, OT

OT laws and legal traditions govern every aspect of the life of the covenant people of God.



## Kinds of OT law


### Criminal law
**[EXO 21:12-21:14](bibleref:02021012-02021014)** *Criminal law deals with crimes against persons punishable by a court.* 

### Civil law
**[DEU 16:18-16:20](bibleref:05016018-05016020)** *Civil law deals with the upholding in court of legally established rights against possible abuse.*  *See also* [DEU 15:12-15:18](bibleref:05015012-05015018)

### Social law
**[EXO 22:21-22:22](bibleref:02022021-02022022)** *Social law deals with behaviour or standards of behaviour which are not strictly enforceable in the courts.*  *See also* [DEU 24:19-24:22](bibleref:05024019-05024022)

### Cultic law
Cultic law deals explicitly with the ritual or religious life of the people of God. Leviticus chapters 1-7 are totally devoted to this kind of law:
 [LEV 1:10-1:13](bibleref:03001010-03001013), [LEV 4:13-4:21](bibleref:03004013-03004021), [LEV 7:11-7:18](bibleref:03007011-03007018)
