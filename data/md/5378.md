# 5378: law, OT

OT laws and legal traditions govern every aspect of the life of the covenant people of God.



## Kinds of OT law


### Criminal law
**EXO 21:12-21:14** *Criminal law deals with crimes against persons punishable by a court.*

### Civil law
**DEU 16:18-16:20** *Civil law deals with the upholding in court of legally established rights against possible abuse.* *See also* DEU 15:12-15:18

### Social law
**EXO 22:21-22:22** *Social law deals with behaviour or standards of behaviour which are not strictly enforceable in the courts.* *See also* DEU 24:19-24:22

### Cultic law
Cultic law deals explicitly with the ritual or religious life of the people of God. Leviticus chapters 1-7 are totally devoted to this kind of law:
 LEV 1:10-1:13, LEV 4:13-4:21, LEV 7:11-7:18
