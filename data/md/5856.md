# 5856: extravagance

Exaggerated, excessive or immoderate behaviour often linked with wastefulness which may result in personal harm. God's people are called to be wise stewards of what has been entrusted to them. However, Scripture also points to a right kind of open-handedness and generosity of spirit which reflects love and worship towards God and concern for others.



## Examples of sinful extravagance


### Wastefulness
**LUK 15:13** *See also* PRO 21:20, PRO 29:3

### Drunkenness and gluttony
**PRO 23:20** *See also* DEU 21:20; EPH 5:18; PHP 3:19

## The results of sinful extravagance


### Wastefulness leads to poverty
**LUK 15:14-15:17** *See also* PRO 13:18, PRO 22:16, PRO 23:21

### Extravagant living leads to spiritual complacency
**LUK 12:19-12:20** *See also* ISA 5:11-5:12, ISA 22:12-22:13, ISA 56:11-56:12; AMO 6:1-6:6; ZEP 1:12-1:13

## The alternative to sinful extravagance


### Thankful enjoyment of God's gifts
**1TI 4:3-4:4** *See also* ACT 10:13-10:15; 1CO 10:30-10:31

### Stewardship of God's blessings
**EZK 34:18-34:19** *See also* GEN 2:15; EXO 23:10-23:12; DEU 15:14-15:15; PRO 22:9

### Discipline and self-control
**1CO 9:25-9:27** *See also* GEN 2:16-2:17; MAT 6:16-6:18 *fasting*; ROM 14:6 *abstinence from certain foods*; 1CO 7:5 *temporary abstinence from sexual relationships*

## Examples of commendable extravagance in worship
**MRK 14:3-14:5** parallels MAT 26:6-26:9, JHN 12:3-12:5 *See also* EXO 36:3-36:7 *gifts for the tabernacle*; 2SA 6:14-6:15 *David dancing before the ark*1CH 29:3-29:4; 2CH 24:10PSA 119:171; 2CO 4:15; COL 2:7MRK 12:41-12:44 parallel LUK 21:1-21:4

## Examples of extravagance not commended in worship
**MIC 6:7-6:8** *Extravagant offerings are no substitute for doing what God requires.* *See also* 1SA 15:20-15:22

## Other examples of commendable extravagance


### Extravagance in caring for others
**2CO 8:2-8:4** *See also* MAT 5:39-5:42 parallel LUK 6:29-6:30; 2CO 9:12-9:13; 1TH 3:12

### Extravagance in forgiveness
**MAT 18:21-18:22** *See also* LUK 17:3-17:4; MAT 18:26 *the king's generosity in forgiving his servant's debt*; LUK 15:22-15:23 *celebrations at the return of the prodigal son*
