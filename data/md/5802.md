# 5802: care

A practical outworking of loving vigilance, efforts and tenderness. Care is perfectly shown by God. People should imitate God's care, but human care is often limited, faulty or misplaced in self-centred ways.



## Caring as part of God's character


### God's care for his children
**PSA 121:3** *See also* PSA 8:4, PSA 23:1, PSA 65:9, PSA 115:12; MAT 6:32 parallel LUK 12:30; MAT 10:30-10:31 parallel LUK 12:7; LUK 15:20 *God is the father in this parable.*

### Human care should reflect God's nature
**JHN 15:12** *See also* GEN 37:14; 1SA 10:2; 2SA 18:29; EST 2:11; LUK 10:34-10:35; ACT 20:31; PHP 4:10, PHP 4:14; 1TH 3:10

### God's care contrasted with human care
**ISA 49:15** *See also* PSA 118:9; EZK 34:2-34:3, EZK 34:11-34:12; ZEC 10:2-10:3; JHN 10:13-10:14; 1TI 3:5

## Care involves responsibility
**GEN 2:15** *See also* GEN 39:4; NUM 1:50; PRO 29:7; MAT 21:33-21:34, MAT 21:41 parallels MRK 12:1-12:2, MRK 12:9, LUK 20:9-20:10, LUK 20:16; 1TI 6:20

## God's care reflected in human relationships
**EPH 5:25** *See also* PSA 103:13-103:14; ECC 11:5; ISA 49:15; EPH 6:1, EPH 6:4; 1TI 5:8

## To be careless or uncaring is wrong
**JER 48:10** *See also* DEU 8:14; 2CH 24:5; ISA 47:8; ZEC 7:9-7:10; MAT 12:36

## Care for worldly things is wrong
**PHP 4:6** *See also* ECC 5:12; MAT 6:31-6:32 parallel LUK 12:29-12:30; MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:18-4:19, LUK 8:14; LUK 10:40-10:42, LUK 21:34; 1CO 7:32-7:34

## God relieves his people of cares
**1PE 5:7** *There is a vital distinction between “care” in the sense of “loving care” which Christians are to practise, and “worldly care” in the sense of “anxiety” which Christians should transfer to God, since they and their needs are his responsibility.* *See also* PSA 37:25, PSA 55:22; PRO 3:5-3:6; ISA 53:4; MAT 11:28-11:29; LUK 12:11-12:12; JHN 14:1
