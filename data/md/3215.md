# 3215: Holy Spirit, and peace

The Holy Spirit brings a sense of well-being, contentment and wholeness to believers whatever their outward circumstances. Peace is therefore an indication of the Holy Spirit's presence.



## Peace is part of the nature of the Holy Spirit


### The Spirit is likened to a dove, the symbol of peace
**[MAT 3:16](bibleref:40003016)** parallels MRK 1:10, LUK 3:22, JHN 1:32

### As part of the Godhead the Spirit is the God of peace
 *See also* [PHP 4:9](bibleref:50004009); [1TH 5:23](bibleref:52005023); [HEB 13:20](bibleref:58013020)

## Peace is a resource that the Holy Spirit gives to believers
**[ROM 14:17](bibleref:45014017)** *See also* [JHN 14:15-14:19](bibleref:43014015-43014019) *The Greek word “parakletos” means “one called alongside”, hence “comforter” or “advocate”* ; [ROM 15:13](bibleref:45015013)

## The fruit of the Spirit includes peace
**[GAL 5:22-5:25](bibleref:48005022-48005025)** *See also* [ROM 8:6](bibleref:45008006)

## The Holy Spirit brings peace to God's people concerning their circumstances


### He changes situations to bring peace
**[ISA 32:14-32:18](bibleref:23032014-23032018)** *See also* [ISA 63:14](bibleref:23063014); [ACT 9:31](bibleref:44009031)

### He brings peace of mind to those facing difficult situations
**[PHP 1:19](bibleref:50001019)** *See also* [HAG 2:5](bibleref:37002005); [JHN 16:5-16:21](bibleref:43016005-43016021), [JHN 20:21-20:22](bibleref:43020021-43020022)

## The Holy Spirit brings peaceful relationships


### Between believers
**[EPH 4:3](bibleref:49004003)**

### Between believers and God the Father
**[ROM 8:1-8:2](bibleref:45008001-45008002)** *See also* [ROM 8:11](bibleref:45008011), [ROM 8:14-8:17](bibleref:45008014-45008017) *The result of the Spirit's work is contrasted with fear, the opposite of peace.*
