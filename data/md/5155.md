# 5155: hair

Various conditions, both physical and spiritual, are indicated in Scripture by human hair. Animal hair is used for a variety of purposes.



## Physical qualities of human hair


### A woman's hair is attractive
**SNG 7:5** *See also* 2KI 9:30; SNG 4:1; 1CO 11:15

### A man's hair shows vigour
**JDG 16:17-16:22** *See also* 2SA 14:26; SNG 5:11

### Grey hair shows the glory of old age
**PRO 16:31** *See also* LEV 19:32 *“aged” may be translated “grey-haired”*; PRO 20:29

### Greying hair as a sign of declining strength
 HOS 7:9

### White hair as a sign of age and wisdom
**REV 1:14** *See also* DAN 7:9

## Hair as indicating physical or spiritual conditions


### Unharmed hair speaks of protection
**MAT 10:30** parallel LUK 12:7 *See also* 1SA 14:45; 2SA 14:11; DAN 3:27; LUK 21:18; ACT 27:34

### Hair standing on end indicates fear
 JOB 4:15

### Baldness may indicate God's judgment
**ISA 3:24** *See also* ISA 3:17

### Hair colour may indicate skin disease
**LEV 13:3** *See also* LEV 13:10-13:11, LEV 13:26, LEV 13:30-13:37, LEV 13:40-13:45

## Rituals and customs involving the hair


### Rules concerning hair for those under a vow
**NUM 6:5** *See also* NUM 6:9, NUM 6:18-6:19; JDG 13:5, JDG 16:17; 1SA 1:11; ACT 18:18, ACT 21:23-21:24

### Rules concerning hair for those being cleansed
 LEV 14:8-14:9

### Mourning customs affecting hair
**MIC 1:16** *See also* LEV 10:6; DEU 21:12-21:13; JOB 1:20; JER 16:6, JER 47:5, JER 48:37; AMO 8:10

### Grief signified by tearing or cutting one's hair
**EZR 9:3** *See also* ISA 22:12; JER 7:29

### Humiliating others by shaving or pulling out the hair
**2SA 10:4** parallel 1CH 19:4 *See also* NEH 13:25; ISA 7:20

### Pagan customs to be avoided
**LEV 19:27** *See also* DEU 14:1-14:2

### Customs concerning hair and public worship
 1CO 11:6, 1CO 11:13-11:15; ; 1TI 2:9; ; 1PE 3:3

## Animal hair


### Animal hair used in tabernacle furnishings
**EXO 26:7** *See also* EXO 25:4, EXO 35:26, EXO 36:14

### A prophet's clothing made from animal hair
**2KI 1:8** *See also* ZEC 13:4; MAT 3:4 parallel MRK 1:6

### Animal hair as a disguise
**GEN 27:16** *See also* 1SA 19:13, 1SA 19:16

## Hair used to wipe Jesus Christ's feet
**JHN 12:3** *See also* LUK 7:38, LUK 7:44; JHN 11:2

## Nazirite vows forbade the cutting of hair


### The reason for taking a Nazirite vow, separation to the Lord
 NUM 6:1-6:2 *While attention is often focused on Nazirite prohibitions, it is important to note that the primary reason was for separating oneself to God. Both men and women could make the vow*; ; NUM 6:8

### The requirements of the Nazirite vow
 NUM 6:2-6:4 *Nazirites must abstain from all produce of the vine*; ; NUM 6:5 *Nazirites must not cut their hair*; ; NUM 6:6-6:7 *Nazirites must not touch a dead body.*

### The rules for re-establishing a broken Nazirite vow
 NUM 6:9-6:12

### The regulations for completing the Nazirite vow
 NUM 6:13-6:17 *The Nazirite vow is completed, first, through prescribed sacrifices*; ; NUM 6:18 *The Nazirite vow is completed, second, through cutting off one's hair and burning it on the altar*; ; NUM 6:19-6:20 *The Nazirite vow is completed, third, through ritual acts performed by the priest.*

### Examples of people dedicated to being lifelong Nazirites
Samson was a lifelong Nazirite:
 JDG 13:2-13:7, JDG 16:1-16:22; 
1SA 1:10-1:11 *Samuel was a lifelong Nazirite*; ; LUK 1:13-1:15 *John the Baptist was possibly a Nazirite.*

### Examples of temporary Nazirite vows
 ACT 18:18, ACT 21:20-21:26

### Nazirites to be seen as gifts from God to his people
 AMO 2:11-2:12
