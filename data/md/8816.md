# 8816: ridicule, nature of

Scripture declares that ridicule of the weak, the poor and the godly is unacceptable. Those who ridicule them invite judgment upon themselves.



## Warnings against ridicule


### Ridicule is foolish
**[PRO 11:12](bibleref:20011012)** *See also* [PRO 1:20-1:22](bibleref:20001020-20001022), [PRO 14:6](bibleref:20014006), [PRO 14:9](bibleref:20014009)

### Ridicule creates trouble
**[PRO 9:12](bibleref:20009012)** *See also* [PRO 19:29](bibleref:20019029), [PRO 22:10](bibleref:20022010), [PRO 29:8](bibleref:20029008), [PRO 30:17](bibleref:20030017); [MAT 5:22](bibleref:40005022) *“Raca” probably means “empty-head”, a term of derision.*
