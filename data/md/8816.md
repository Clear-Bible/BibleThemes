# 8816: ridicule, nature of

Scripture declares that ridicule of the weak, the poor and the godly is unacceptable. Those who ridicule them invite judgment upon themselves.



## Warnings against ridicule


### Ridicule is foolish
**PRO 11:12** *See also* PRO 1:20-1:22, PRO 14:6, PRO 14:9

### Ridicule creates trouble
**PRO 9:12** *See also* PRO 19:29, PRO 22:10, PRO 29:8, PRO 30:17; MAT 5:22 *“Raca” probably means “empty-head”, a term of derision.*

### Ridicule is a sign of the last days
**2PE 3:3-3:4** *See also* JUD 1:18

## Examples of ridicule
 JDG 9:28-9:29, JDG 9:38; ; 1SA 17:41-17:44; ; 1KI 22:24; ; 2KI 2:23-2:24, 2KI 18:23-18:25; ; NEH 2:19, NEH 4:1-4:3

## Ridicule associated with divine punishment
**ISA 14:3-14:4** *See also* ISA 37:22-37:23; JER 49:17, JER 50:13; EZK 36:1-36:7; HAB 2:6; ZEP 2:15**MIC 2:3-2:4** *See also* DEU 28:37; 1KI 9:6-9:9 parallel 2CH 7:19-7:22; JER 19:8, JER 24:9; EZK 5:14-5:15, EZK 23:32, EZK 22:3-22:5; HOS 7:16; MIC 6:16

## Prophets ridicule idolatry
**1KI 18:27** *See also* ISA 41:7, ISA 41:21-41:24, ISA 44:12-44:17, ISA 46:1-46:2; JER 10:3-10:5
