# 6678: justification, and Jesus Christ's work

On account of the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ, the demands of the law of God are met, and believers are granted the status of being righteous in the sight of God.



## Justification is grounded in the death of Jesus Christ


### Jesus Christ's death shields believers from God's wrath
**ROM 5:9** *See also* ROM 3:24, ROM 4:25, ROM 5:18; 1PE 2:24

### Jesus Christ's death fulfils the demands of the law of God
**ROM 8:3-8:4** *See also* ROM 3:25-3:26; GAL 3:13; 1JN 2:2

## Justification is grounded in the resurrection of Jesus Christ
**ROM 4:25, ROM 10:9-10:10** *See also* ACT 2:22-2:39, ACT 4:10-4:12, ACT 17:30-17:31; 1PE 3:18-3:21

## Justification means believers are reckoned as righteous through the death of Jesus Christ
**ROM 5:19**; **1CO 1:30**; **2CO 5:21** *See also* 1CO 6:9-6:11; PHP 3:8-3:9 *The term “imputation” is used to refer to the process by which God treats believers as being righteous in his sight on account of Jesus Christ's death.*

## Justification is received by faith
**ROM 1:17** parallel GAL 3:11 *See also* HAB 2:4; ROM 5:1; EPH 2:8

### The example of Abraham
**GEN 15:6** *See also* ROM 4:1-4:5, ROM 4:9-4:22; GAL 3:6-3:9, GAL 3:16-3:18

### The example of David
 ROM 4:6-4:8; ; PSA 32:1-32:2

### Apostolic teaching on the need of faith for justification
**ACT 13:39** *See also* ROM 3:22, ROM 3:25, ROM 3:27-3:30, ROM 4:5, ROM 5:1, ROM 9:30-9:32, ROM 10:10; 1CO 6:11; GAL 2:16, GAL 3:8, GAL 3:14; EPH 2:8

## Justification is a gift of God's grace
**ROM 3:24** *See also* ROM 5:15-5:17, ROM 8:33; TIT 3:7

### Not by works or the law
**GAL 3:11** *See also* ROM 3:20, ROM 4:5; GAL 2:16, GAL 2:21, GAL 3:2-3:5, GAL 3:24, GAL 5:4-5:6; EPH 2:8-2:9
