# 8322: perfection, human

Human wholeness in the image of God, given at creation and lost in the fall, is to be fully restored in Jesus Christ.



## Humanity was created perfect
**GEN 1:26-1:27** *See also* GEN 5:1; EZK 28:12-28:15 *The king of Tyre is described in terms of the creation and fall of humanity*; JAS 3:9

## Perfection was lost through sin
**ROM 3:23** *See also* GEN 2:17, GEN 3:16-3:19, GEN 3:23; JOB 15:14-15:16; EZK 28:15-28:17; ROM 1:21

## The need for moral perfection
**DEU 18:13** *See also* GEN 17:1; JOB 25:4; PSA 24:3-24:4

## Human inability to keep God's law perfectly
**HEB 7:19** *See also* HEB 7:11, HEB 10:1

## Perfection and redemption


### God's purpose for believers
**COL 1:22** *See also* DAN 11:35, DAN 12:10; EPH 5:27 *God's purpose for the church*; HEB 10:14, HEB 11:40, HEB 12:23; JAS 1:4; JUD 1:24

### The aim of Christian ministry
**COL 1:28** *See also* 2CO 11:2

### The aim of the Christian
**PHP 3:12** *See also* 2CO 13:9-13:11; EPH 4:13; HEB 6:1; JAS 1:4
