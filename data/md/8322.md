# 8322: perfection, human

Human wholeness in the image of God, given at creation and lost in the fall, is to be fully restored in Jesus Christ.



## Humanity was created perfect
**[GEN 1:26-1:27](bibleref:01001026-01001027)** *See also* [GEN 5:1](bibleref:01005001); [EZK 28:12-28:15](bibleref:26028012-26028015) *The king of Tyre is described in terms of the creation and fall of humanity* ; [JAS 3:9](bibleref:59003009)
