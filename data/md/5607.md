# 5607: warfare, examples of

From the time of entering the promised land, Israel was obliged to engage in warfare against other nations. Success is attributed to Israel's trust in the LORD and failure to lack of faith and disobedience.



## Israel had to engage in warfare to enter Canaan


### It was commanded by God
**DEU 7:1-7:2** *See also* EXO 23:27-23:31; DEU 9:1-9:5, DEU 20:16-20:18

### Joshua conquers the land
**JOS 11:23** *See also* JOS 6:2, JOS 8:1-8:2, JOS 10:40-10:42; NEH 9:24; PSA 44:1-44:3

### Israel failed to drive out the remaining Canaanites
**JDG 1:19** *Judah distrusted God's power to destroy their enemy's iron chariots.* *See also* JDG 1:28, JDG 2:1-2:3, JDG 2:14-2:15

## Israel was obliged to engage in warfare to defend the land


### When unfaithful they were defeated
**JDG 3:12-3:14** *See also* LEV 26:14-26:17, LEV 26:33; DEU 28:15, DEU 28:25; JDG 3:7-3:8, JDG 6:1-6:3; 2CH 12:1-12:4, 2CH 24:23-24:24

### When faithful they were victorious
**2CH 20:20** *See also* DEU 28:1, DEU 28:7; 1SA 7:7-7:14, 1SA 11:1-11:11; 2CH 20:15-20:24

## Israel's internal struggles
**2SA 3:1**; **1KI 12:21** *See also* 2SA 2:8-2:17, 2SA 15:14; 1KI 15:6-15:7, 1KI 15:16; 2KI 13:12, 2KI 14:9-14:15

## Israel's constant unfaithfulness led to the exile
**1CH 5:25-5:26** *See also* 2KI 17:5-17:8, 2KI 17:22-17:23

## Judah's constant unfaithfulness led to the Babylonian captivity
**2CH 36:16-36:17** *See also* 2KI 25:1 parallel JER 52:4-52:5; 2KI 25:8-25:11 parallel JER 52:12-52:15; 2KI 25:21 parallel JER 52:27; JER 1:13-1:16
