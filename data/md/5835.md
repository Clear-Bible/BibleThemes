# 5835: disappointment

The sadness experienced when people or circumstances do not fulfil expectations.



## Disappointment caused by people or circumstances
**JOB 6:19-6:21**; **JER 8:15** *See also* PSA 10:1; PRO 10:1, PRO 13:12, PRO 17:21, PRO 29:21; ECC 6:2; ISA 49:4; JER 2:36, JER 14:19; LAM 4:17; EZK 37:11

## Disappointment as a result of not understanding God's purposes
**LUK 24:21** *See also* JOB 30:26; PSA 73:13; JER 15:18

## Disappointment as the result of sin
**PRO 11:7**; **HAG 1:9** *See also* JOB 11:20; PRO 10:28; LAM 3:18; HOS 13:15; JOL 1:10; ZEC 9:5

## Examples of disappointment
 RUT 1:20 *Naomi at her losses*; ; 1SA 1:10 *Hannah with her childlessness*; ; 1SA 15:11 *Samuel with Saul*; ; 1SA 30:3-30:6 *David and his men at Ziklag*; ; 1KI 19:4 *Elijah with his own performance*; ; MAT 19:22 parallels MRK 10:22, LUK 18:23 *the rich young man*; ; 2CO 12:21 *Paul's possible disappointment with the Corinthians*; ; GAL 1:6 *Paul with the Galatians*

## God's disappointment with people
**GEN 6:6**; **1SA 15:35** *See also* PSA 78:40; ISA 63:10; EZK 6:9

## God does not disappoint his faithful people
**JOS 21:45**; **ROM 5:5** *See also* JOS 23:14; 1KI 8:56; PSA 22:5, PSA 25:3, PSA 119:116
