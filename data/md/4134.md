# 4134: demons, exorcism of

Jesus Christ drove out many demons during his earthly ministry and he delegated this authority to his disciples.



## Jesus Christ understood exorcism as part of his ministry
**[LUK 13:32](bibleref:42013032)** *See also* [LUK 4:18](bibleref:42004018)

### He drove out demons from individuals brought to him
**[MAT 8:16](bibleref:40008016)** parallels MRK 1:32-1:34, LUK 4:40-4:41 *See also* [MAT 4:24](bibleref:40004024)

### He drove out demons from individuals whom he encountered
**[MRK 1:23-1:26](bibleref:41001023-41001026)** parallel LUK 4:33-4:35 *See also* [MRK 1:39](bibleref:41001039)

## Jesus Christ had power over demons


### He was able to drive demons out at a distance
 [MAT 15:22-15:28](bibleref:40015022-40015028) parallel MRK 7:25-7:30

### He used words of rebuke to drive out demons
**[MAT 17:18](bibleref:40017018)** parallels MRK 9:25, LUK 9:42 *See also* [MRK 1:25](bibleref:41001025) parallel LUK 4:35; [LUK 4:41](bibleref:42004041)

## Jesus Christ identifies the spiritual resources needed for exorcising demons


### The Spirit of God
 [MAT 12:28](bibleref:40012028) parallel LUK 11:20

### Faith
 [MAT 17:19-17:20](bibleref:40017019-40017020)

### Prayer (and fasting)
 [MRK 9:28-9:29](bibleref:41009028-41009029)

## The ministry of exorcism was an indication of the kingdom's presence
**[MAT 12:28](bibleref:40012028)** parallel LUK 11:20

## The results of being delivered from demon-possession


### Visible transformation and healing
**[MRK 5:15](bibleref:41005015)** parallel LUK 8:35 *See also* [MAT 9:33](bibleref:40009033); [LUK 11:14](bibleref:42011014), [LUK 13:13](bibleref:42013013)

### Devotion to Jesus Christ
**[LUK 8:1-8:2](bibleref:42008001-42008002)** *See also* [MRK 5:18](bibleref:41005018) parallel LUK 8:38

### Loss of supernatural faculties
 [ACT 16:16-16:19](bibleref:44016016-44016019)

### Demons seek to return
 [MAT 12:43-12:45](bibleref:40012043-40012045) parallel LUK 11:24-11:26

## The impact of exorcism on those who observe it


### Amazement
 [MAT 9:33](bibleref:40009033), [MAT 12:23](bibleref:40012023); [MRK 1:27](bibleref:41001027) parallel LUK 4:36; [LUK 11:14](bibleref:42011014)

### Fear
 [MAT 8:34](bibleref:40008034) parallels MRK 5:17, LUK 8:37
