# 8617: prayer, advice for effective

Scripture provides guidance concerning what attitudes and actions are appropriate for effective prayer. It also identifies a number of motives which are likely to lead to prayers being unanswered.



## Hindrances to prayer


### Sin
**ISA 59:2** *See also* PSA 66:18; JER 14:10-14:12; LAM 3:42-3:44; MIC 3:4

### Disobedience
**ZEC 7:13** *See also* DEU 1:43-1:45; PRO 1:28-1:31

### Selfishness
**JAS 4:3**

### Injustice
**ISA 1:15-1:17** *See also* PRO 21:13; ISA 58:1-58:7

### Lack of faith
**JAS 1:6-1:7**

## Qualities that lead to effective prayer


### Humility
**LUK 18:9-18:14** *See also* 2SA 7:18; 2CH 7:14; PSA 51:16-51:17; ISA 57:15; MAT 8:8 parallel LUK 7:6

### Obedience
**1JN 3:21-3:22** *See also* 1SA 15:22; JER 7:22-7:23

### Righteousness
**PRO 15:29** *See also* 1KI 3:11-3:12; PSA 34:15

### Single-mindedness
**JER 29:13** *See also* DEU 4:29; 1CH 28:9

### Faith
