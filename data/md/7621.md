# 7621: disciples, calling of

The term is applied to Christians in general, not to the Twelve only. It emphasises the importance of Jesus Christ's followers learning from Christ as their teacher.



## Disciples learning from their master
**[ISA 8:16](bibleref:23008016)**; **[AMO 7:14](bibleref:30007014)** *See also* [1KI 20:35](bibleref:11020035); [NEH 3:8](bibleref:16003008); [ISA 19:11](bibleref:23019011); [MAT 10:24](bibleref:40010024); [MRK 2:18](bibleref:41002018) parallel LUK 5:33; [LUK 6:40](bibleref:42006040); [JHN 1:35](bibleref:43001035), [JHN 9:28](bibleref:43009028)

## Those referred to as disciples in the NT


### The disciples as the apostles
**[MAT 10:1-10:2](bibleref:40010001-40010002)** *The apostles were uniquely appointed as founding pillars of the church, but in some respects they were also representative disciples.* 

### The disciples as believers in general
**[MAT 28:19](bibleref:40028019)**; **[ACT 19:1-19:2](bibleref:44019001-44019002)** *Christian believers, not fully mature.*  *See also* [LUK 6:13](bibleref:42006013), [LUK 6:17](bibleref:42006017); [ACT 6:2](bibleref:44006002), [ACT 11:26](bibleref:44011026)

### The disciples as an intimate circle of believers
 [MAT 18:1](bibleref:40018001); [MRK 4:10](bibleref:41004010)

## How disciples learn from Jesus Christ the teacher


### By repeating the words of Jesus Christ
**[LUK 11:1-11:4](bibleref:42011001-42011004)** *The rhythmical form of much of Jesus Christ's teaching suggests he intended his words to be remembered verbatim.*  *See also* [MAT 5:3-5:10](bibleref:40005003-40005010)

### By signs
**[JHN 2:11](bibleref:43002011)** *See also* [MRK 5:37](bibleref:41005037), [MRK 5:40](bibleref:41005040) parallel LUK 8:51

### By understanding the OT Scriptures
**[JHN 1:45](bibleref:43001045)** *See also* [LUK 18:31-18:33](bibleref:42018031-42018033), [LUK 24:27](bibleref:42024027), [LUK 24:32](bibleref:42024032), [LUK 24:44-24:45](bibleref:42024044-42024045)
