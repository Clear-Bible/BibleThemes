# 5505: roads

Recognised routes for travellers, or streets in towns and cities. In biblical times travelling by road was recognised as dangerous.



## Kinds of road


### In the open country
**EXO 13:17-13:18**; **ACT 19:1** *See also* GEN 16:7; NUM 20:17; DEU 1:2; JOS 10:10-10:11; JDG 21:19; 1SA 17:52; ISA 15:5

### In towns and cities
**ACT 9:11** *See also* EST 6:9; JER 37:21
