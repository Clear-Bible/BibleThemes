# 5534: sleep, spiritual

Scripture often uses sleep as an image of spiritual laziness or a lack of alertness towards God.



## Sleep as an image of spiritual laziness


### Lack of watchfulness
**ISA 56:10** *See also* MAT 24:43-24:44 parallel LUK 12:39-12:40; MRK 13:35-13:36

### Spiritual dullness
**ACT 28:25-28:27** *See also* ISA 6:9-6:10; PRO 23:34-23:35ROM 11:8; ISA 29:10

## God's call to awake from sleep


### The need to arise
**ROM 13:11-13:12** *See also* ISA 51:17, ISA 52:1, ISA 60:1; EPH 5:14; REV 3:2

### The command to be alert
**REV 16:15** *See also* MAT 26:41; MRK 13:32-13:34; LUK 12:35; EPH 6:18; 1TH 5:6-5:8; 1PE 5:8

### The plea for revival
 PSA 80:18, PSA 85:6

## The image of God awaking from sleep


### From his past judgments
**PSA 78:65**

### Prayers for God to rise up against his enemies
**PSA 7:6** *See also* PSA 35:23, PSA 44:23-44:24, PSA 73:20, PSA 80:1-80:2; ISA 51:9

## The contrast between God who never sleeps and false gods
**PSA 121:3-121:4** *See also* 1KI 18:27; HAB 2:19
