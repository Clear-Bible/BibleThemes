# 1443: revelation, in OT

The OT bears witness to God's revelation in the history of Israel and in the inspired testimony of the prophets and other writers of the period. This knowledge of God prepares the way for the full disclosure of God in Jesus Christ in the NT.



## Revelation in Eden and before the flood
**GEN 1:3, GEN 2:16-2:18** *See also* GEN 1:29-1:30, GEN 3:9-3:19, GEN 4:6-4:7, GEN 4:9-4:15, GEN 6:13-6:22

## The covenant framework of OT revelation after the flood


### God's covenant with Noah
**GEN 9:8-9:11** *This covenant covers all living creatures.* *See also* GEN 6:18, GEN 9:12-9:17

### God's covenant with Abraham
**GEN 17:7** *See also* GEN 15:9-15:21, GEN 17:2, GEN 17:4, GEN 17:10-17:14, GEN 22:16-22:18; EXO 6:2-6:6; 1CH 16:14-16:18; PSA 105:8-105:9

### God's covenant with Moses at Sinai
**EXO 34:27** *See also* EXO 24:3-24:8, EXO 34:10-34:14; DEU 5:2-5:4 *Mount Sinai was also known as Mount Horeb*; JER 11:2-11:5

### God's covenant with David
**PSA 89:3-89:4** *See also* 2SA 7:8-7:16; PSA 132:11-132:12

### The promise of a new covenant
**JER 31:31-31:34** *See also* ISA 42:6; MAT 26:27-26:28 parallels MRK 14:24, LUK 22:20, 1CO 11:25; HEB 8:8-8:12, HEB 9:15, HEB 10:15-10:18

## The recipients of OT revelation


### The people of Israel
**AMO 3:2** *See also* EXO 19:3-19:6; DEU 7:6; ISA 65:1; EZK 20:5

### On occasions God spoke to people outside the covenant community
**DAN 2:27-2:28** *See also* GEN 41:25, GEN 41:28-41:32; DAN 4:1-4:37, DAN 5:17-5:28

## Methods of OT revelation


### Direct communication
**DEU 18:18** *See also* EXO 4:12; NUM 12:8, NUM 23:5; ISA 50:4, ISA 51:16; JER 1:9

### Visions and dreams
**NUM 12:6** *See also* GEN 15:1, GEN 28:12, GEN 37:5-37:7, GEN 37:9, GEN 46:2; NUM 24:4; DEU 13:1; JDG 7:13-7:14; JOB 33:14-33:15; EZK 1:1, EZK 1:26-1:28

### Visible manifestations (theophanies)
**GEN 18:1** *See also* GEN 32:24-32:30; EXO 3:1-3:6, EXO 34:4-34:7; NUM 11:25, NUM 12:5, NUM 14:10-14:12; JOS 5:13-5:15

### Scripture
**DEU 31:9** *See also* EXO 17:14; NUM 33:2; ISA 30:8; JER 36:2, JER 51:60

## The content of OT revelation


### Revelation of God's will and purposes
**GEN 17:1-17:2** *See also* GEN 12:1-12:3, GEN 15:1-15:5; EXO 19:5-19:6, EXO 20:1-20:17 parallel DEU 5:6-5:21; EXO 22:31; LEV 11:44-11:45, LEV 19:1-19:2, LEV 20:7-20:8

### Revelation of God's plan
**GEN 3:15** *See also* ISA 9:6-9:7, ISA 11:1-11:10, ISA 42:1; MIC 5:1-5:5; ZEC 9:9-9:13

### Revelation of God's character and being
**EXO 3:11-3:15** EXO 40:34; ; 1KI 8:11; ; EZK 1:28; NUM 14:17-14:19; ; PSA 143:1; DEU 7:7-7:8; ; LAM 3:22; PSA 72:2, PSA 103:6; ; ISA 59:17; PSA 115:3, PSA 135:5-135:6

## The incompleteness of OT revelation
**HEB 11:39-11:40** *The fulness of God's redemption could only become apparent with the fulness of his revelation in Jesus Christ.* *See also* HEB 1:1-1:2; 1PE 1:10-1:12
