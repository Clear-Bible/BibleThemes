# 5409: metaphor

An image which suggests similarities between two different ideas, without implying that they are identical. Scripture uses metaphors extensively, as a way of illustrating aspects of its teaching.



## Examples of metaphors for God


### Father
 DEU 32:6; ; ISA 64:8; ; MAL 2:10; ; EPH 4:6

### Rock
**DEU 32:4** *See also* 1SA 2:2; 2SA 22:2; PSA 62:2, PSA 78:35

### Shepherd
**PSA 23:1** *See also* PSA 28:9, PSA 80:1; EZK 34:11-34:16

## Examples of metaphors for Jesus Christ


### Bread of life
 JHN 6:35-6:51

### Good shepherd
 JHN 10:11, JHN 10:14

### Light of the world
 JHN 8:12, JHN 9:5

## Examples of metaphors for the Holy Spirit


### Counsellor
 JHN 14:16, JHN 14:26, JHN 15:26, JHN 16:7

### Breath

### Wind
 JHN 3:8
