# 8735: evil, origins of

Although Scripture does not reveal the ultimate origin of evil, it identifies a number of intermediate sources.



## Satan as a source of evil
**GEN 3:1** *Identified later (Rev 12:9) with Satan*; **MAT 4:1** parallels MRK 1:13, LUK 4:2**JHN 8:44**; **1JN 3:8**; **REV 12:9** *See also* 1CH 21:1 *Satan incites David to take a census of Israel*; JOB 1:11, JOB 2:5; MAT 5:37, MAT 6:13, MAT 13:38-13:39 *the parable of the weeds*; LUK 13:16 *Satan is responsible for a woman's crippling disease*; JHN 13:2 *The devil prompts Judas to betray Jesus Christ*; JHN 17:15; ACT 5:3 *Ananias lies to the Holy Spirit at Satan's instigation*; 2CO 4:4, 2CO 12:7 *Paul's thorn in the flesh from Satan*; EPH 2:2, EPH 6:11; 1TH 2:18; 1PE 5:8; 1JN 3:12, 1JN 5:19; REV 2:10, REV 20:2

## Other evil powers
