# 8456: obedience, to human authorities

Scripture teaches that all people, Christians included, should submit not only to God himself but also to divinely instituted secular authorities.



## Obedience is owed to rulers
**[ROM 13:1-13:7](bibleref:45013001-45013007)** *See also* [1TI 2:1-2:3](bibleref:54002001-54002003); [TIT 3:1](bibleref:56003001); [1PE 2:13-2:14](bibleref:60002013-60002014), [1PE 2:17](bibleref:60002017)

### Jesus Christ expounds this principle
**[MRK 12:17](bibleref:41012017)** parallels MAT 22:21, LUK 20:25

## Obedience is owed to church leaders
**[HEB 13:17](bibleref:58013017)** *See also* [1PE 5:5](bibleref:60005005)

## Obedience is owed within the household
**[EPH 6:1-6:3](bibleref:49006001-49006003)** *See also* [PRO 15:5](bibleref:20015005); [COL 3:20](bibleref:51003020)

### Jesus Christ sets the example
**[LUK 2:51](bibleref:42002051)**

## Obedience to secular authority is limited by obedience to God
**[ACT 5:29](bibleref:44005029)** *See also* [ACT 4:19](bibleref:44004019)
