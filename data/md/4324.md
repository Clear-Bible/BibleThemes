# 4324: dross

Impurities removed from molten metal during refining. In a spiritual sense, wicked people whom God removes in judgment, and also impurities in believers which are removed by discipline and trials.

## God removes the wicked like dross
**[PSA 119:119](bibleref:19119119)** *See also* [ISA 30:28](bibleref:23030028); [JER 6:28-6:30](bibleref:24006028-24006030); [EZK 22:19-22:22](bibleref:26022019-26022022)

## Dross as a metaphor for impurity in God's people
**[EZK 22:18](bibleref:26022018)** *See also* [ISA 1:21-1:23](bibleref:23001021-23001023); [EZK 24:11-24:13](bibleref:26024011-26024013), [EZK 36:25](bibleref:26036025); [AMO 9:8-9:10](bibleref:30009008-30009010); [JAS 1:21](bibleref:59001021)
