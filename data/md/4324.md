# 4324: dross

Impurities removed from molten metal during refining. In a spiritual sense, wicked people whom God removes in judgment, and also impurities in believers which are removed by discipline and trials.



## God removes the wicked like dross
**PSA 119:119** *See also* ISA 30:28; JER 6:28-6:30; EZK 22:19-22:22

## Dross as a metaphor for impurity in God's people
**EZK 22:18** *See also* ISA 1:21-1:23; EZK 24:11-24:13, EZK 36:25; AMO 9:8-9:10; JAS 1:21

## God purges the dross from his people
**ISA 1:25-1:26** *See also* PRO 17:3, PRO 25:4; DAN 12:10; MAL 3:2-3:3; 2CO 7:1; 1PE 1:7

### By the blood of Jesus Christ
 ZEC 13:1; ; HEB 9:14; ; 1JN 1:7

### By the work of the Holy Spirit
 EZK 36:25-36:27; ; MAT 3:11-3:12 parallel LUK 3:16-3:17

### By testing and affliction
 PSA 66:10-66:12; ; ISA 48:10; ; JER 9:7; ; ZEC 13:9
