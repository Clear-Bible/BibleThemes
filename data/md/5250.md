# 5250: centurion

A Roman military officer in charge of one hundred soldiers.



## The centurion who asked Jesus Christ to heal his servant
**MAT 8:5-8:9** parallel LUK 7:2-7:8

## The centurion present at the death of Jesus Christ
**MAT 27:54** parallels MRK 15:39, LUK 23:47 *See also* MRK 15:44-15:45

## The centurion Cornelius
 ACT 10:1-10:48, ACT 11:1-11:18

## The centurions involved in guarding Paul
 ACT 22:25-22:26, ACT 23:17-23:18, ACT 23:23, ACT 24:23, ACT 27:1, ACT 27:3, ACT 27:6, ACT 27:11, ACT 27:31, ACT 27:43
