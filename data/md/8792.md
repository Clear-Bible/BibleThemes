# 8792: oppression, God's attitude to

God is concerned for the victims of oppression; he hears, sustains and delivers them. Christians are to exhibit the same concern.



## God forbids the oppression of the poor and vulnerable
**ZEC 7:10** *See also* PRO 22:22-22:23

## God warns the oppressor
**JER 7:5-7:7** *See also* ISA 33:14-33:15

## God condemns the oppressor
**PSA 72:4**; **ISA 10:1-10:3** *See also* PRO 17:5; ISA 30:12-30:13; EZK 22:7, EZK 22:13, EZK 22:31; AMO 2:6, AMO 5:11; MIC 2:1-2:3; NAM 3:1, NAM 3:19; LUK 11:42

## God's concern for the oppressed


### Showing mercy to the oppressed
**PSA 103:6**; **DEU 10:18** *See also* 1SA 2:8; PSA 146:7-146:9

### Defending the oppressed
**PSA 72:4** *See also* DEU 10:18; PSA 9:9, PSA 10:17-10:18, PSA 12:5, PSA 68:5, PSA 146:7-146:9; JER 50:33-50:34

### Delivering the oppressed
**JOB 5:15** *See also* EXO 3:7-3:9; DEU 6:21; PSA 35:10, PSA 107:6 parallels PSA 107:13, PSA 107:19; PRO 20:22; ISA 14:3-14:4, ISA 49:26; ACT 7:34

## The oppressed turn to the LORD

### In trust
