# 4135: demons, Jesus Christ's authority over

Demons are subject to the authority of Jesus Christ during his ministry and subsequently in the ministry of the church.



## All spiritual powers are subject to Jesus Christ who has supreme authority
**[EPH 1:19-1:22](bibleref:49001019-49001022)** *See also* [ROM 8:38](bibleref:45008038); [EPH 3:10](bibleref:49003010); [COL 1:16](bibleref:51001016), [COL 2:10](bibleref:51002010), [COL 2:15](bibleref:51002015)

## Demons are powerless before Jesus Christ


### Demons can only do what he permits
**[MAT 8:31-8:32](bibleref:40008031-40008032)** parallels MRK 5:12-5:13, LUK 8:32-8:33

### Demons obey his command
**[MAT 17:18](bibleref:40017018)** parallels MRK 9:25-9:26, LUK 9:42 *See also* [MAT 9:33-9:34](bibleref:40009033-40009034); [MRK 1:34](bibleref:41001034), [MRK 16:9](bibleref:41016009); [LUK 11:14](bibleref:42011014)

### Demons disperse at his word
**[MRK 9:25](bibleref:41009025)** *See also* [MRK 1:25](bibleref:41001025) parallel LUK 4:35

## The name of Jesus Christ has power against demons


### Jesus Christ's name is used by his disciples
**[MRK 16:17](bibleref:41016017)** *See also* [MRK 9:38-9:39](bibleref:41009038-41009039) parallel LUK 9:49-9:50; [LUK 10:17](bibleref:42010017); [ACT 16:18](bibleref:44016018)

### Jesus Christ anticipates the misuse of his own name
**[MAT 7:22-7:23](bibleref:40007022-40007023)** *See also* [ACT 19:13-19:16](bibleref:44019013-44019016) *an example where Jesus' name is used in cases of demon-possession--but not by Jesus Christ's disciples, and not successfully* 

## Jesus Christ's disciples have delegated power over demons


### In Jesus Christ's own lifetime
**[MAT 10:1](bibleref:40010001)** *See also* [MAT 10:7](bibleref:40010007); [MRK 3:14-3:15](bibleref:41003014-41003015), [MRK 6:7](bibleref:41006007) parallel LUK 9:1; [MRK 6:13](bibleref:41006013); [LUK 10:17](bibleref:42010017), [LUK 10:19](bibleref:42010019)

### In the ongoing ministry of the church
**[MRK 16:17](bibleref:41016017)** *See also* [ACT 5:16](bibleref:44005016), [ACT 8:7](bibleref:44008007), [ACT 16:18](bibleref:44016018), [ACT 19:12](bibleref:44019012)
