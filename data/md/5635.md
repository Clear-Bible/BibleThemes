# 5635: work, and redemption

Although human activity cannot merit eternal redemption, work done in this life may, with God's enabling, substantially reflect the divine ideal, in anticipation of the perfect service of heaven.



## Human work cannot merit redemption
 [EPH 2:8-2:9](bibleref:49002008-49002009); [2TI 1:9](bibleref:55001009); [TIT 3:4-3:5](bibleref:56003004-56003005)

## Jesus Christ's finished work secures redemption
**[JHN 17:4](bibleref:43017004)** *See also* [JHN 4:34](bibleref:43004034), [JHN 19:30](bibleref:43019030); [HEB 9:12](bibleref:58009012)

## Faith leads believers to perform good works
**[JAS 2:14](bibleref:59002014)** *See also* [MAT 7:16-7:20](bibleref:40007016-40007020), [MAT 12:33](bibleref:40012033); [LUK 3:8-3:9](bibleref:42003008-42003009); [ROM 2:13](bibleref:45002013); [HEB 6:10-6:12](bibleref:58006010-58006012); [JAS 2:17-2:26](bibleref:59002017-59002026)

## Redeemed workers fulfil God's purpose in work


### By applying biblical virtues in employment
 [PRO 10:4](bibleref:20010004), [PRO 14:23](bibleref:20014023); [GAL 6:9](bibleref:48006009); [COL 4:1](bibleref:51004001)

### By the pursuit of excellence
 [ECC 9:10](bibleref:21009010); [TIT 2:7-2:8](bibleref:56002007-56002008), [TIT 3:8](bibleref:56003008); [1PE 2:12](bibleref:60002012), [1PE 2:15](bibleref:60002015)

### By doing their work as those responsible to God
 [ROM 12:1](bibleref:45012001); [1CO 7:24](bibleref:46007024), [1CO 10:31](bibleref:46010031); [EPH 6:7](bibleref:49006007); [COL 3:23](bibleref:51003023)

## Work done, as for the Lord, will be rewarded by the Lord
**[COL 3:23-3:24](bibleref:51003023-51003024)** *See also* [2CH 31:21](bibleref:14031021); [MAT 25:21](bibleref:40025021); [1CO 15:58](bibleref:46015058)

## Work will continue in the life to come
**[REV 22:3](bibleref:66022003)** *See also* [MAT 25:21](bibleref:40025021), [MAT 25:23](bibleref:40025023); [LUK 19:17](bibleref:42019017), [LUK 19:19](bibleref:42019019)
