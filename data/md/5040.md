# 5040: murder

The unlawful killing of individual human beings, forbidden by God and carrying the threat of severe punishment.

## Murder forbidden by the law
**[EXO 20:13](bibleref:02020013)** parallel DEU 5:17 *See also* [MAT 5:21](bibleref:40005021), [MAT 19:18](bibleref:40019018) parallels MRK 10:19, LUK 18:20; [ROM 13:9](bibleref:45013009); [1TI 1:9](bibleref:54001009); [JAS 2:11-2:12](bibleref:59002011-59002012)
