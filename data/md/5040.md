# 5040: murder

The unlawful killing of individual human beings, forbidden by God and carrying the threat of severe punishment.



## Murder forbidden by the law
**EXO 20:13** parallel DEU 5:17 *See also* MAT 5:21, MAT 19:18 parallels MRK 10:19, LUK 18:20; ROM 13:9; 1TI 1:9; JAS 2:11-2:12

## Murder is to be punished


### Punishment by death
**EXO 21:12** *See also* GEN 9:6; LEV 24:17, LEV 24:21; NUM 35:16-35:21, NUM 35:31; DEU 19:11-19:13

### Punishment by banishing
**GEN 4:13-4:14** *See also* PRO 28:17

### Punishment by exclusion from the kingdom
 REV 21:8, REV 22:15

## Unintentional murderers are protected from avengers
**NUM 35:11** *See also* NUM 35:22-35:29, NUM 35:32; JOS 21:13, JOS 21:21, JOS 21:27, JOS 21:32, JOS 21:38

## Witnesses are required before murder is proven
**NUM 35:30** *See also* DEU 17:6, DEU 19:15; HEB 10:28

## Unpremeditated murder
**NUM 35:22-35:24** *See also* EXO 21:13; DEU 4:41-4:44, DEU 19:4; JOS 20:1-20:3

## Premeditated motives of murder


### Jealousy
**GEN 4:8** *See also* 1JN 3:12

### Lust
**2SA 12:9-12:10** *Many motives contributed to David's plan to remove Uriah. It began with lust.* *See also* 2SA 11:1-11:17

### Rebellion and power
**2KI 12:20** *See also* JDG 3:21; 2KI 9:14, 2KI 9:24, 2KI 19:36-19:37

### To remove opponents
**1KI 2:25** *See also* JDG 9:1-9:6; 2SA 20:9-20:10; 2CH 21:4

### To take revenge
**2SA 13:28-13:29** *See also* EXO 2:11-2:13; 2SA 3:27, 2SA 4:1-4:8

### Robbery
**PRO 1:11-1:13** *See also* LUK 10:30

### Persecution
**MAT 24:9** parallel LUK 21:12-21:17 *See also* 2CH 21:13; JOB 24:14; PRO 29:10; MAT 14:6-14:11, MAT 23:30-23:37; MRK 3:6; ACT 2:23, ACT 7:54-7:60; 2TI 4:6

## Murder as an attitude as well as an action
**MAT 5:21-5:22** *See also* MAT 15:19; JHN 8:44; 1JN 3:15

## Murderers can be pardoned


### Unjustly
**LUK 23:25** parallels MAT 27:15-27:17, MRK 15:6-15:11, JHN 18:39-18:40 *See also* ACT 3:14

### Justly--through God's grace
**2SA 12:9-12:13**; **ACT 26:10**; **1TI 1:13-1:15** *See also* EXO 2:12-3:12; ACT 7:58, ACT 8:3, ACT 9:1, ACT 9:13, ACT 22:4; 1CO 15:9; EPH 3:8
