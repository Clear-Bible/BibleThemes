# 5163: legs

The legs of an animal were among the choice parts offered in sacrifices. In a human being legs provide strength. Bowing the knee symbolises the submitting of one's human strength to God's control.

## Choice parts of a sacrificial animal
**[EZK 24:4](bibleref:26024004)** *See also* [EXO 12:9](bibleref:02012009)[EXO 29:17-29:18](bibleref:02029017-02029018); [LEV 1:9](bibleref:03001009), [LEV 1:13](bibleref:03001013), [LEV 8:21](bibleref:03008021), [LEV 9:13-9:14](bibleref:03009013-03009014)
