# 8658: Lord's Prayer, the

The name given to the prayer Jesus Christ taught his disciples, probably as a model prayer for regular use.



## Aspects of the Lord's Prayer


### A recognition of God as Father and Lord
**MAT 6:9** parallel LUK 11:2 *See also* MAT 7:21, MAT 16:17; LUK 10:21; EPH 3:14-3:15

### A desire for God's perfect will to be done
**MAT 6:10** parallel LUK 11:2 *See also* MAT 12:50, MAT 26:39, MAT 26:42; ACT 21:14; COL 1:9; HEB 10:9

### Asking for physical provision
**MAT 6:11** parallel LUK 11:3 *See also* MAT 6:31-6:32, MAT 7:9-7:11

### Asking for forgiveness
**MAT 6:12** parallel LUK 11:4 *See also* MAT 6:14-6:15, MAT 18:23-18:35; MRK 11:25; LUK 23:34; COL 3:13; 1JN 1:9

### Asking for spiritual protection
**MAT 6:13** parallel LUK 11:4 *See also* 2CO 1:10-1:11; JUD 1:24; REV 3:10

### The doxology
**MAT 6:13** *NIV footnote.*
