# 8630: worship, results of

Worship not only gives God what is due to him but also results in many benefits for his people.



## True worship brings benefits for God's people


### Blessing
**EXO 23:25-23:26** *See also* DEU 11:13-11:15

### Guidance
**ACT 13:2-13:3** *See also* ISA 58:6-58:11; NUM 7:89

### Deliverance
**ACT 16:25-16:26** *See also* PSA 50:14-50:15

### Joy
**1CH 29:21-29:22** *See also* 2CH 29:30; PSA 43:4; ISA 56:7; LUK 24:52

### A sense of God's presence
**2CH 5:13-5:14** parallel 1KI 8:10-8:11 *See also* EXO 40:35

### A deeper sense of Jesus Christ's lordship
**PHP 2:9-2:11** *See also* REV 1:10-1:18

## Boldness to witness
**ACT 4:31** *See also* PSA 57:9; ACT 18:9-18:10

## True worship convicts sinners
**1CO 14:24-14:25**
