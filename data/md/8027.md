# 8027: faith, testing of

The means through which the genuineness of faith is proved and Christian character developed. God promises to help his people during times of testing.



## God allows faith to be tested


### Testing proves the genuineness of faith
**1PE 1:6-1:7** *See also* MAT 13:20-13:21 parallels MRK 4:16-4:17, LUK 8:13

### Testing develops Christian character
**JAS 1:2-1:4** *See also* ROM 5:3-5:4

### Testing purifies God's people
**ISA 48:10** *See also* PSA 66:10-66:12; JER 9:7; ZEC 13:9; MAL 3:2-3:3; 1PE 4:17

### Examples of God testing faith
Abraham is told to sacrifice Isaac:
 GEN 22:1-22:2; ; HEB 11:17-11:19; 
DEU 8:2-8:5 *God tested Israel in the desert*; ; JDG 7:1-7:8 *God reduces Gideon's army*; ; MAT 15:21-15:28 parallel MRK 7:24-7:30 *Jesus Christ appears to discourage the Canaanite woman*; ; JHN 11:1-11:6 *Jesus Christ delays going to Bethany when Lazarus is ill.*

## Means by which faith is tested


### God allows Satan to test faith
**JOB 2:7** *See also* LUK 22:31; 1TH 3:4-3:5; REV 2:10

### Difficult circumstances test faith
 1KI 17:17-17:18; ; 2KI 4:1, 2KI 4:27-4:28; ; ACT 14:22; ; 2CO 11:25-11:27

### Persecution tests faith
 DAN 6:10-6:12; ; ACT 8:1-8:4; ; HEB 11:35-11:38

### Discouraging people test faith
 2KI 18:19-18:25 parallels 2CH 32:10-32:15, ISA 36:4-36:10; ; MRK 5:35-5:36 parallel LUK 8:49-8:50; ; MRK 5:40 parallels MAT 9:24, LUK 8:53

## God's promise of help during testing
**ISA 43:2**; **LUK 22:32** *See also* PSA 91:14-91:15; 1CO 10:13; 2CO 12:7-12:9; 1PE 5:10; 2PE 2:9

### God's help through the encouragement of others
**1TH 3:2-3:3** *See also* 2CO 7:5-7:7

## The response to trials


### Rejoicing at sharing Jesus Christ's suffering
 1PE 4:12-4:13

### Praying for God's help
 HEB 4:14-4:16; ; 1PE 4:19

### Persevering
**JAS 1:12** *See also* 2TH 1:4; HEB 12:1-12:3; JAS 5:11; REV 2:3, REV 2:19

## Examples of faith victorious through testing
 GEN 22:9-22:12 *Abraham*; ; 2KI 6:15-6:17 *Elisha's servant*; ; JOB 1:22 *Job*; ; DAN 3:16-3:27 *Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego*; ; ACT 5:27-5:29 *the apostles*; ; ACT 7:59-7:60 *Stephen*; ; 2CO 4:7-4:9 *Paul*
