# 4029: world, human beings in

The world is intended to be the dwelling-place of humanity, as the height of God's creation. Human beings are organised within the world into groupings and nations and have a God-given authority to rule.



## The responsibility of human beings to rule


### Over the earth
**[GEN 2:15-2:16](bibleref:01002015-01002016)** *See also* [GEN 1:25-1:26](bibleref:01001025-01001026), [GEN 1:29](bibleref:01001029); [PSA 8:4-8:6](bibleref:19008004-19008006)

### Over the animal kingdom
**[GEN 1:28](bibleref:01001028)** *“subdue” and “rule” do not give liberty to exploit and abuse but responsibility for caring for and bringing the best out of God's world.*  *See also* [GEN 9:1-9:5](bibleref:01009001-01009005); [DEU 22:6-22:7](bibleref:05022006-05022007); [PRO 27:23-27:27](bibleref:20027023-20027027); [MAT 6:25-6:26](bibleref:40006025-40006026); [ACT 10:11-10:13](bibleref:44010011-44010013)
