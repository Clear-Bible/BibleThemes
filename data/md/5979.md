# 5979: waste

The misuse and squandering of resources. God condemns the waste of resources which he provides, especially spiritual provisions such as grace and opportunity. Sometimes extravagance in God's service is mistaken for waste.



## Waste of material resources


### Squandering is folly
**LUK 15:13-15:16** *See also* PRO 21:20, PRO 29:3; LUK 16:1; JAS 5:1-5:6; 1PE 4:3-4:5

### As a consequence of yielding to temptation
**PRO 29:3** *See also* PRO 5:7-5:14

### Jesus Christ avoids waste
**JHN 6:12-6:13**

## Spiritual resources should not be wasted


### God's grace
**JON 2:8** *See also* JHN 12:35-12:36; 2CO 6:1; GAL 4:9-4:11; HEB 10:26-10:29, HEB 10:35; 2PE 2:21

### God-given abilities
 MAT 25:14-25:30; ; LUK 19:12-19:27

### Opportunities for service
**EPH 5:16** *See also* JHN 9:4; GAL 6:10; COL 4:5

### Spiritual teaching
**MAT 7:6** *Spiritual teaching is wasted if inappropriate to the spiritual capacity of the hearers.* *See also* PRO 9:7, PRO 23:9; 1CO 2:14

## Examples of wasted lives


### Esau
Esau lived for the pleasures of the moment and threw away his spiritual inheritance:
 GEN 25:29-25:34; ; HEB 12:16

### Samson
 JDG 16:18-16:21 *Though Samson did retrieve something at the end of his life, his spiritual gift of supernatural strength was largely wasted through his indulgence in sexual immorality.*

### King Saul
 1SA 16:21; ; 2SA 1:17-1:27 *Through natural strength and spiritual gifts Saul had the prospect of establishing a successful royal dynasty. The future was lost through disobedience and the end of his reign was wasted through jealousy.*

### Judas Iscariot
 MAT 27:3-27:5 *Judas had the best of opportunities--three years with Jesus Christ. This was thrown away in a moment of personal ambition.*

## Devotion to God is not waste
**MAT 26:6-26:13** parallel MRK 14:3-14:9 *See also* JHN 12:1-12:8

## Waste as the destruction of life and land


### The frailty of human beings
**JOB 13:28** *See also* JOB 33:21; PSA 32:3; LAM 4:9; 2CO 4:16

### Waste as a result of the judgment of God
**JER 34:22** *See also* LEV 26:31-26:33, LEV 26:39; NUM 5:21; DEU 28:22, DEU 29:23, DEU 32:24; PSA 106:43; ISA 24:1, ISA 24:3; EZK 4:17; HOS 5:9
