# 5972: unkindness

Unsympathetic or cruel behaviour. It is contrary to God's character and is condemned in Scripture.



## Unkindness condemned


### In general terms
**PRO 11:17** *See also* PRO 17:5, PRO 25:20; EPH 4:31-4:32

### Treatment of enemies
**MAT 5:43-5:44** *See also* EXO 23:4-23:5; PRO 25:21; LUK 6:27-6:28, LUK 6:35; ROM 12:14

### Treatment of the poor
**PRO 14:31**; **1JN 3:17** *See also* DEU 15:7, DEU 24:14; PRO 21:13; ISA 3:15, ISA 10:1-10:2; EZK 22:29; ZEC 7:10

### Treatment of animals
**PRO 12:10** *See also* LEV 22:28; DEU 22:6, DEU 25:4; LUK 13:15, LUK 14:5

### Inhospitality condemned
**1PE 4:9** *See also* DEU 23:4; MAT 25:41-25:43; LUK 9:52-9:53; HEB 13:2; 3JN 1:10

## Some examples of unkindness
