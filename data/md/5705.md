# 5705: inheritance, spiritual

Believers are privileged to receive a spiritual inheritance from their heavenly Father as a result of their adoption into the family of God through faith.



## Believers have an inheritance in Christ


### Jesus Christ as God's heir
**[HEB 1:2](bibleref:58001002)** *See also* [MAT 21:37-21:39](bibleref:40021037-40021039) parallels MRK 12:6-12:8, LUK 20:13-20:15; [MAT 28:18](bibleref:40028018); [LUK 19:12](bibleref:42019012); [JHN 3:35](bibleref:43003035); [HEB 1:4-1:6](bibleref:58001004-58001006); [PSA 2:7-2:8](bibleref:19002007-19002008)

### Believers are co-heirs with Christ
**[ROM 8:17](bibleref:45008017)** *See also* [EPH 3:6](bibleref:49003006)

### Believers are heirs under the covenant
 [HEB 6:17](bibleref:58006017), [HEB 9:15](bibleref:58009015)

### Believers are heirs by grace
**[GAL 3:18](bibleref:48003018)** *See also* [ACT 20:32](bibleref:44020032); [COL 1:12](bibleref:51001012)

### Believers are heirs through faith
**[GAL 3:29](bibleref:48003029)** *See also* [ROM 4:13-4:17](bibleref:45004013-45004017); [HEB 11:7-11:10](bibleref:58011007-58011010)

## The nature of the spiritual inheritance of believers


### They inherit salvation
**[HEB 1:14](bibleref:58001014)**

### They inherit eternal life
 *See also* [MAT 19:29](bibleref:40019029); [LUK 10:25](bibleref:42010025); [TIT 3:7](bibleref:56003007); [1PE 3:7](bibleref:60003007)

### They have the full rights of sons
 [GAL 4:4-4:7](bibleref:48004004-48004007)

### They have the rights of the firstborn
**[HEB 12:22-12:23](bibleref:58012022-58012023)** *See also* [EXO 4:22](bibleref:02004022); [2KI 2:9](bibleref:12002009)

### They inherit the earth
**[MAT 5:5](bibleref:40005005)** *See also* [PSA 37:11](bibleref:19037011)

### They inherit the kingdom
**[MAT 25:34](bibleref:40025034)** *See also* [LUK 22:28-22:30](bibleref:42022028-42022030); [REV 3:21](bibleref:66003021)

### They inherit all things
 [LUK 15:31](bibleref:42015031); [1CO 3:21-3:22](bibleref:46003021-46003022); [REV 21:7](bibleref:66021007)

### Their inheritance is the word of God
 [PSA 119:111](bibleref:19119111)

### They inherit the Lord himself
**[PSA 73:26](bibleref:19073026)** *See also* [NUM 18:20](bibleref:04018020); [DEU 10:9](bibleref:05010009); [PSA 142:5](bibleref:19142005); [JER 10:16](bibleref:24010016), [JER 51:19](bibleref:24051019)

### It is a good inheritance
 [PSA 16:6](bibleref:19016006)

## The permanence of believers' spiritual inheritance


### It has been prepared from the creation
 [MAT 25:34](bibleref:40025034)

### It is guaranteed for eternity
**[1PE 1:3-1:5](bibleref:60001003-60001005)** *See also* [PSA 37:18](bibleref:19037018); [DAN 12:13](bibleref:27012013)

### It is sealed with God's promise
**[EPH 1:13-1:14](bibleref:49001013-49001014)** *See also* [REV 7:3](bibleref:66007003), [REV 9:4](bibleref:66009004)

## The conditions of spiritual inheritance


### It is conditional upon continuing obedience and faith
**[1PE 3:9-3:12](bibleref:60003009-60003012)** *See also* [PSA 34:12-34:16](bibleref:19034012-19034016), [PSA 25:12-25:13](bibleref:19025012-19025013), [PSA 37:11](bibleref:19037011), [PSA 37:29](bibleref:19037029), [PSA 37:34](bibleref:19037034), [PSA 61:5](bibleref:19061005); [PRO 28:10](bibleref:20028010); [MAT 5:5](bibleref:40005005); [COL 3:24](bibleref:51003024); [HEB 6:12](bibleref:58006012); [JAS 2:5](bibleref:59002005)

### It is denied to the ungodly
**[EPH 5:5](bibleref:49005005)** *See also* [PSA 37:9](bibleref:19037009), [PSA 37:22](bibleref:19037022); [1CO 6:9-6:10](bibleref:46006009-46006010), [1CO 10:1-10:5](bibleref:46010001-46010005); [GAL 5:19-5:21](bibleref:48005019-48005021); [HEB 3:12-3:19](bibleref:58003012-58003019); [REV 21:27](bibleref:66021027)
