# 8760: fools, characteristics of

Scripture portrays fools as those who have rejected God and his ways and are unable or unwilling to appreciate the wisdom of knowing and obeying him.



## The world regards Christians as fools
**1CO 4:10** *See also* 1CO 1:26-1:31

## The world regards the gospel as foolish
**1CO 1:18** *See also* 1CO 1:20-1:25

## Fools do not believe in God
**PSA 53:1** parallel PSA 14:1 *See also* ROM 1:21

### Fools do not trust God
**HOS 7:11** *See also* 2CH 16:7-16:9; ISA 19:11-19:13, ISA 30:1

### Fools lack knowledge of God
**JER 4:22** *See also* JER 5:4, JER 5:21; PSA 92:6; LUK 24:25; EPH 5:15-5:17

## The behaviour of fools


### Fools worship idols
**JER 10:8** *See also* JER 10:14 parallel JER 51:17; ROM 1:22-1:23

### Fools act in a godless manner
**ISA 32:6** *See also* PSA 94:5-94:8; PRO 10:23; ECC 7:25; JER 4:22

### Fools are dishonest
**JER 17:11**

### Fools give vent to their anger
**PRO 29:11** *See also* ECC 7:9

### Fools are disobedient
**1SA 13:13** *See also* 2SA 24:10 parallel 1CH 21:8; PSA 107:17; PRO 10:8

### Fools despise instruction and discipline
**PRO 1:22** *See also* PRO 1:28-1:33, PRO 5:23, PRO 12:15; ISA 65:2; JER 18:12; EZK 3:7

## The speech of fools


### Fools speak unwisely
**PRO 18:6-18:7** *See also* ECC 10:12; EPH 5:4

### Fools are slanderous
**PRO 10:18** *See also* 2PE 2:15

### Fools are mockers
**PRO 14:9** *See also* PRO 1:22, PRO 14:6, PRO 23:9, PRO 24:9

### Fools love quarrelling and dissension
**PRO 20:3** *See also* PRO 19:1; 1TI 1:4, 1TI 6:4; 2TI 2:23; TIT 3:9

### The words of fools are empty
**PRO 15:2** *See also* PRO 14:7, PRO 26:7; ECC 9:17, ECC 10:14

## Fools rely on themselves
**PRO 12:15** *See also* PRO 14:12, PRO 28:26

## Fools rely on words, not deeds
**JAS 2:20** *See also* ECC 5:4; LUK 11:39-11:40
