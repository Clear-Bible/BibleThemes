# 5719: mothers, responsibilities of

The fundamental importance of motherhood is recognised throughout Scripture.



## Motherhood and childbirth


### God plans happiness for women in motherhood
**PSA 113:9**

### The need for a mother's cleansing after childbirth
 *See also* LEV 12:1-12:8; LUK 2:22-2:24

## A mother's relationship with her children


### A mother loves and cares for her children
**1TH 2:7** *Paul's statement assumes that a mother's normal attitude towards her child is one of love and care.* *See also* LUK 2:48

### A mother's role as teacher
**PRO 1:8** *See also* PRO 6:20; 2TI 1:5

### The responsibility to discipline children
**PRO 29:15**

## The joy or sorrow of parents at their children's conduct
**PRO 10:1** *See also* PRO 23:25

## The negative influence of some mothers upon their children
**2CH 22:3** *See also* GEN 27:5-27:17; 1KI 22:52; MAT 14:6-14:8, MAT 20:20-20:22

## Children's reponsibility to honour their parents
**EXO 20:12** parallel DEU 5:16 *“Honour” implies respect, obedience and care for the parents' needs.* *See also* LEV 19:3; MAT 19:19 parallels MRK 10:19, LUK 18:20; EPH 6:1-6:3

### This honour is due especially to mothers in old age
 PRO 23:22

## Penalties for disobeying parents
 EXO 21:15, EXO 21:17; ; DEU 21:18-21:21; ; PRO 20:20; ; MAT 15:4-15:6 parallel MRK 7:10-7:13

## Sexual relations with one's mother or mother-in-law are forbidden
 LEV 18:7; ; DEU 27:23

## Upon marriage the mother-child relationship becomes secondary
**GEN 2:24** *See also* MAT 19:5 parallel MRK 10:7; EPH 5:31

## Care for a mother-in-law
**RUT 2:11** *See also* MAT 8:14-8:15 parallels MRK 1:29-1:31, LUK 4:38

## The ministry of Jesus Christ may produce conflict within families
**LUK 12:53** parallel MAT 10:35-10:36

## Following Jesus Christ involves a commitment to him even deeper than love of one's parents
**MAT 10:37** *See also* LUK 14:26

## Jesus Christ regards those who do God's will as his own family
**MAT 12:49-12:50** parallels MRK 3:31-3:35, LUK 8:19-8:21 *See also* MAT 19:29 parallels MRK 10:29, LUK 18:29-18:30
