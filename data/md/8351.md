# 8351: teachableness

A quality in those who desire to learn, present in the disciples of Jesus Christ and seen in Christ's relationship to his Father. Unwillingness to receive the truth leads to spiritual darkness.



## The importance of teachableness


### For spiritual growth
**HEB 12:5-12:12** *See also* HEB 5:11-5:14

### For a life of obedience
**PHP 4:9** *See also* JAS 1:25; REV 3:3

### For discerning God's guidance
**PRO 3:5-3:6** *See also* PSA 25:4-25:5; ROM 12:2

## The requirements for teachableness


### Eagerness to learn
**PRO 18:15**; **ISA 50:4-50:5** *See also* 2CH 26:3-26:5; PSA 27:11, PSA 43:3, PSA 86:11, PSA 119:9-119:11, PSA 139:23-139:24, PSA 143:8-143:10

### Willingness to learn
From God:
 EXO 15:26; ; DEU 5:27; EXO 20:18-20:19; ; DEU 18:15, DEU 31:12; ; NEH 8:3, NEH 8:9; PSA 34:11; ; PRO 4:10-4:13, PRO 4:20-4:21, PRO 8:32-8:33, PRO 13:1, PRO 23:22-23:23; PRO 1:5, PRO 19:20, PRO 22:17, PRO 25:12; MAT 12:42 parallel LUK 11:31; ; MAT 17:5 parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; ; JHN 10:27; LUK 12:11-12:12; ; REV 2:7

### Readiness to ask for help
**ACT 8:30-8:31** *See also* MRK 4:10, MRK 9:11, MRK 9:28, MRK 10:10, MRK 13:4; LUK 3:12, LUK 11:1

### Readiness to pray for wisdom
**JAS 1:5** *See also* 2CH 20:12; PSA 143:8-143:10

### Readiness to pray for spiritual insight
**EPH 1:17-1:19** *See also* EPH 3:16-3:19; COL 1:9

### Humility
**1CO 3:18-3:20** *See also* JOB 6:24; PRO 11:2; MAT 11:29, MAT 18:3-18:4; 1CO 8:1-8:2; JAS 1:21

### Living by truth already known
**JHN 8:31-8:32** *See also* 2PE 1:5-1:8

## Jesus Christ was willing to be taught by the Father
**JHN 15:15** *See also* JHN 5:30, JHN 8:26-8:28

## Hindrances to teachableness


### The activity of the devil
**2CO 4:4** *See also* 2TI 2:25-2:26

### Not having the Holy Spirit
**1CO 2:14**

### Unwillingness to be taught
**ISA 48:17-48:18** *See also* PSA 32:9, PSA 81:11-81:12; MAT 13:14-13:15 parallels MRK 4:12, LUK 8:10; ISA 6:9-6:10; LUK 11:52 *The “key to knowledge” is the fulfilment of the OT Scriptures in Jesus Christ*; LUK 16:31, LUK 19:41-19:44; JAS 1:22-1:24
