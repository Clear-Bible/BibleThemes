# 5549: speech, positive aspects of

The way in which believers speak should reflect their calling as Christians. Christian speech should be sincere, godly and honouring to God.



## The desire for godly speech


### In oneself
**PSA 19:14** *See also* PSA 17:3, PSA 39:1, PSA 141:3

### In others
**EPH 4:29-4:31** *See also* PRO 4:24; ECC 5:6; EPH 5:4; COL 3:8-3:10; 1PE 3:9-3:10; PSA 34:12-34:13

## Characteristics of godly speech in relation to God


### It gives glory to God
**HEB 13:15** *See also* PSA 34:1, PSA 35:28, PSA 51:15; 1PE 2:9

### It gives thanks and praise to God
**COL 3:17** *See also* DEU 8:10; 1CH 16:4, 1CH 16:7-16:8, 1CH 16:34-16:36; PSA 100:4, PSA 118:1, PSA 118:19, PSA 118:21, PSA 118:28-118:29; JOL 2:26; MRK 8:6-8:7; COL 2:7; 1TH 5:18; JAS 3:9-3:10

### It confesses Jesus Christ as Lord
**ROM 10:9-10:10** *See also* MAT 10:32-10:33 parallel LUK 12:8-12:9; MAT 16:16; 1CO 12:3

## Characteristics of godly speech in relation to others


### It is gentle, gracious and tactful
**COL 4:6** *See also* JDG 8:1-8:3; PRO 10:32, PRO 15:1, PRO 16:24; ECC 10:12; DAN 2:14-2:16; 1PE 3:15

### It is kind
**PRO 12:25** *See also* GEN 50:19-50:21; JOB 4:3-4:4; 1CO 4:13; 2TI 2:24-2:25

### It is instructive and edifying
**COL 3:16** *See also* PRO 12:18, PRO 15:2, PRO 15:4, PRO 15:7, PRO 20:15, PRO 25:11-25:12, PRO 31:26; MAL 2:7; EPH 4:15, EPH 4:29

### It is prudent and restrained
**PRO 17:27** *See also* PRO 15:28, PRO 18:13; ECC 5:2, ECC 9:17; JAS 1:19

### It is honest and unadorned
**MAT 5:37** *See also* PSA 15:2-15:3, PSA 141:5; PRO 24:26, PRO 27:5-27:6, PRO 27:9, PRO 28:23

### It is truthful and sincere
**ZEC 8:16** *See also* EXO 20:16 parallel DEU 5:20; PRO 12:17, PRO 12:19, PRO 12:22; MAL 2:6; 2CO 2:17, 2CO 4:2, 2CO 6:7; EPH 4:25

### It is wise
**PSA 37:30** *See also* PRO 10:13, PRO 10:31, PRO 13:14; ACT 6:10

### It will speak of God and his works
**DEU 6:6-6:9** *See also* PSA 40:9-40:10, PSA 145:7; MAL 3:16
