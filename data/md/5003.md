# 5003: human race, and God

Though vastly superior to the human race, God nevertheless loves, sustains and governs it, sending his Son as a human being to redeem humanity.



## God is incomparably superior to the human race
**ISA 40:15-40:22** *See also* JOB 25:1-25:6, JOB 38:1-38:7; PSA 8:3-8:4; ISA 31:1-31:3; ACT 17:24-17:25; 1CO 1:20-1:25

## The human race is dependent upon God
**JOB 34:14-34:15** *See also* JOB 12:10; PSA 36:5-36:6; ACT 17:24-17:28

## The human race is valued and loved by God


### The human race is the object of God's care and compassion
**PSA 103:13** *See also* PSA 10:17-10:18; LAM 3:31-3:33; MAT 6:25-6:33 parallel LUK 12:22-12:31

### God wants to bless the human race
 GEN 12:1-12:3; ; ROM 10:12

### God wants to save the human race
**JHN 3:16-3:17** *See also* 1TI 2:1-2:4; 2PE 3:8-3:9

### God highly values the human race and human life
**GEN 9:6** *See also* EXO 20:13; MAT 16:26 parallels MRK 8:36-8:37, LUK 9:25; 1PE 1:18-1:19

## God is sovereign and watches over the human race
**PSA 33:10-33:15** *See also* PSA 9:7-9:8; PRO 5:21; DAN 5:18-5:21

## God will judge and punish the wickedness of the human race
**ISA 2:12-2:17** *See also* MAT 13:40-13:43; ACT 17:29-17:31; ROM 2:5-2:11

## God is to be feared and worshipped by the entire human race
**PSA 33:8**; **ROM 1:20-1:25** *See also* 1CH 16:28-16:31; PSA 96:7-96:10
