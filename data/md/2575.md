# 2575: Jesus Christ, temptation of

Repeated yet unsuccessful efforts made by Satan and by human beings to deflect Jesus Christ from his Father's will.



## The wilderness temptations
**[MRK 1:13](bibleref:41001013)**

### Tempted to turn stones into bread
**[MAT 4:2-4:3](bibleref:40004002-40004003)** parallel LUK 4:2-4:3

### Tempted to abuse miraculous power
**[MAT 4:5-4:6](bibleref:40004005-40004006)** parallel LUK 4:9-4:11 *See also* [MAT 12:38](bibleref:40012038), [MAT 16:1](bibleref:40016001) parallel MRK 8:11; [LUK 11:16](bibleref:42011016); [JHN 2:18](bibleref:43002018)

### Tempted by the offer of an easy route to power
**[MAT 4:8-4:9](bibleref:40004008-40004009)** parallel LUK 4:5-4:7

**[MAT 4:3](bibleref:40004003)** parallel LUK 4:3 *See also* [MAT 4:6](bibleref:40004006) parallel LUK 4:9; [MAT 27:40](bibleref:40027040)
