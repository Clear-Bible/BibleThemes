# 2575: Jesus Christ, temptation of

Repeated yet unsuccessful efforts made by Satan and by human beings to deflect Jesus Christ from his Father's will.



## The wilderness temptations
**MRK 1:13**

### Tempted to turn stones into bread
**MAT 4:2-4:3** parallel LUK 4:2-4:3

### Tempted to abuse miraculous power
**MAT 4:5-4:6** parallel LUK 4:9-4:11 *See also* MAT 12:38, MAT 16:1 parallel MRK 8:11; LUK 11:16; JHN 2:18

### Tempted by the offer of an easy route to power
**MAT 4:8-4:9** parallel LUK 4:5-4:7

### Tempted to doubt he was God's Son
**MAT 4:3** parallel LUK 4:3 *See also* MAT 4:6 parallel LUK 4:9; MAT 27:40

## Temptation continued throughout Jesus Christ's life
**LUK 22:28** *Trials can include temptations.*

### Opponents tempted Jesus Christ with trick questions
 *See also* MAT 22:15 parallels MRK 12:13, LUK 20:20

### Jesus Christ tempted to sidestep the cross
**MAT 16:21-16:23** parallel MRK 8:32-8:33 *See also* MAT 26:39-26:44 parallels MRK 14:35-14:41, LUK 22:41-22:44; MAT 27:40 parallel MRK 15:30

## Jesus Christ steadfastly resisted temptation


### Jesus Christ resisted temptation by trusting God's word
**MAT 4:4** parallel LUK 4:4 *See also* MAT 4:7 parallel LUK 4:12; MAT 4:10 parallel LUK 4:8

### Jesus Christ resisted temptation by obeying his Father
**HEB 5:7-5:8** *See also* MAT 26:39 parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42; MAT 26:42

## Results of Jesus Christ's temptations


### Satan left Jesus Christ alone
**MAT 4:11** parallel LUK 4:13

### Jesus Christ remained sinless
**HEB 4:15** *Refers to Jesus Christ as high priest and as sacrificial lamb.* *See also* JHN 8:46; 1PE 2:22

### Jesus Christ helps those who are being tempted
**HEB 2:18** *See also* HEB 4:14-4:16
