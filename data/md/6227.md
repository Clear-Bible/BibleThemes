# 6227: regret

Deep feelings of sorrow arising from past events now seen as sins, mistakes or misfortunes. It can either lead to true repentance or destructive bitterness.

**1SA 26:21** *See also* GEN 27:33-27:35; NUM 12:10-12:12 *Aaron and Miriam when they had spoken against Moses*; NUM 14:1-14:2; JDG 11:34-11:35 *Jephthah when he returned home from fighting the Ammonites*2SA 24:10 parallel 1CH 21:8; 2SA 24:14 parallel 1CH 21:13
