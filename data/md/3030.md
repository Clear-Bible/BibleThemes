# 3030: Holy Spirit, power of

The Holy Spirit equips and empowers believers so that the reign and reality of God is revealed through them in the world.

**[ISA 11:2-11:3](bibleref:23011002-23011003)** *See also* [ISA 42:1](bibleref:23042001)

**[ISA 32:15](bibleref:23032015)**

**[EZK 36:26-36:27](bibleref:26036026-26036027)** *See also* [JER 31:33](bibleref:24031033)

### The Spirit's power will prompt prophecy and visions
**[JOL 2:28-2:29](bibleref:29002028-29002029)** *See also* [ACT 2:17-2:18](bibleref:44002017-44002018)
