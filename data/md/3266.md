# 3266: Holy Spirit, in creation

The Holy Spirit was active with the Father and the Word in creation. He is the active power of God present within creation.



## The Holy Spirit is involved in creative activity
**[GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002)** *See also* [JOB 26:13](bibleref:18026013); [PSA 33:6](bibleref:19033006) *The word translated “breath” is the same word that is used for “Spirit”.* 

## The Holy Spirit is the breath of life throughout creation
**[GEN 2:7](bibleref:01002007)** *See also* [JOB 12:10](bibleref:18012010), [JOB 32:8](bibleref:18032008), [JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004), [JOB 34:14-34:15](bibleref:18034014-18034015); [PSA 104:30](bibleref:19104030)

## The Holy Spirit is present everywhere in creation
**[PSA 139:7-139:8](bibleref:19139007-19139008)**

## The Holy Spirit controls nature and history
**[ISA 34:16](bibleref:23034016)** *See also* [ISA 40:7](bibleref:23040007)
