# 5185: stomach

As well as the physical stomach, the term is used in association with God's judgment or in a figurative sense.

## Stomach as part of the physical body
**[1CO 6:13](bibleref:46006013)** *See also* [JOB 40:16](bibleref:18040016); [SNG 7:2](bibleref:22007002); [DAN 2:32](bibleref:27002032)

### Jonah in the stomach of a huge fish
**[MAT 12:40](bibleref:40012040)** *See also* [JON 1:17-2:1](bibleref:32001017-32002001)

## Ritual laws concerning the stomach
**[MRK 7:18-7:19](bibleref:41007018-41007019)** parallel MAT 15:17 *See also* [LEV 11:41-11:45](bibleref:03011041-03011045)

### Severe stomach pain
