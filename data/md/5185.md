# 5185: stomach

As well as the physical stomach, the term is used in association with God's judgment or in a figurative sense.



## Stomach as part of the physical body
**1CO 6:13** *See also* JOB 40:16; SNG 7:2; DAN 2:32

### Jonah in the stomach of a huge fish
**MAT 12:40** *See also* JON 1:17-2:1

## Ritual laws concerning the stomach
**MRK 7:18-7:19** parallel MAT 15:17 *See also* LEV 11:41-11:45

## Afflictions of the stomach
 LUK 15:16 *hunger*; ; 1TI 5:23 *illness*

## Fatal wounding in the stomach
 JDG 3:21; ; 2SA 2:23, 2SA 3:27, 2SA 4:6, 2SA 20:10

## The stomach and God's judgment


### Severe stomach pain
**JER 30:6-30:7** *See also* NUM 5:20-5:22, NUM 5:27

### Crawling on one's stomach as a sign of submission
**GEN 3:14** *Part of God's curse upon Satan.* *See also* PSA 72:9; ISA 49:23, ISA 65:25; MIC 7:17

### Hunger as a result of God's judgment
**MIC 6:14** *See also* JOB 20:12-20:15, JOB 20:20-20:23; PRO 13:25; ISA 9:20; EZK 7:19

### An empty stomach as a warning
**AMO 4:6**

## The stomach and God's blessing
In the OT a full stomach was one sign of God's blessing:
 PSA 34:9-34:10, PSA 37:19, PSA 37:25; ; PRO 10:3

## Figurative uses of the stomach


### Stomach as a symbol of inner thoughts
**EZK 3:3** *See also* PRO 18:8, PRO 26:22; REV 10:9-10:10

### Stomach as a symbol of worldliness
**PHP 3:19** *See also* ROM 16:18

### Stomach as something to be filled
**PRO 18:20** *See also* JOB 15:2; JER 51:34
