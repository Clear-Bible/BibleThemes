# 1205: God, titles and names of

These reflect the great and varied aspects of God's being and character. They are particularly used by the psalmists to speak of God as a source of strength, security, blessing and hope.



## Titles which denote God's greatness


### God Almighty
**GEN 17:1** *This was the special name by which God revealed himself to the patriarchs. The Hebrew is “El-Shaddai” possibly meaning “God the mountain”, emphasising God's might.* *See also* GEN 28:3, GEN 48:3; EXO 6:3; RUT 1:20-1:21; JOB 5:17JOB 6:4, JOB 37:23
PSA 91:1; REV 16:14, REV 19:15

### God Most High
**GEN 14:18-14:20** *The Hebrew “El-Elyon” denotes the supremacy and sovereignty of God.* *See also* GEN 14:22; DEU 32:8; 2SA 22:14 parallel PSA 18:13; PSA 9:2, PSA 21:7, PSA 46:4, PSA 47:2, PSA 78:35, PSA 97:9; ISA 14:14; DAN 3:26MRK 5:7 parallel LUK 8:28; LUK 1:32, LUK 1:35
LUK 6:35; ACT 16:17

**GEN 21:33** *The Hebrew “El-Olam” only occurs in this verse.* *See also* DEU 33:27; ROM 16:26

**LUK 1:49** *See also* JOS 22:22; PSA 50:1, PSA 132:2, PSA 132:5; ISA 1:24, ISA 49:26, ISA 60:16; MRK 14:62

### The living God
