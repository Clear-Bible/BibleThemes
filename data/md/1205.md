# 1205: God, titles and names of

These reflect the great and varied aspects of God's being and character. They are particularly used by the psalmists to speak of God as a source of strength, security, blessing and hope.

## Titles which denote God's greatness


### God Almighty
**[GEN 17:1](bibleref:01017001)** *This was the special name by which God revealed himself to the patriarchs. The Hebrew is “El-Shaddai” possibly meaning “God the mountain”, emphasising God's might.*  *See also* [GEN 28:3](bibleref:01028003), [GEN 48:3](bibleref:01048003); [EXO 6:3](bibleref:02006003); [RUT 1:20-1:21](bibleref:08001020-08001021); [JOB 5:17](bibleref:18005017)[JOB 6:4](bibleref:18006004), [JOB 37:23](bibleref:18037023)
[PSA 91:1](bibleref:19091001); [REV 16:14](bibleref:66016014), [REV 19:15](bibleref:66019015)

### God Most High
**[GEN 14:18-14:20](bibleref:01014018-01014020)** *The Hebrew “El-Elyon” denotes the supremacy and sovereignty of God.*  *See also* [GEN 14:22](bibleref:01014022); [DEU 32:8](bibleref:05032008); [2SA 22:14](bibleref:10022014) parallel PSA 18:13; [PSA 9:2](bibleref:19009002), [PSA 21:7](bibleref:19021007), [PSA 46:4](bibleref:19046004), [PSA 47:2](bibleref:19047002), [PSA 78:35](bibleref:19078035), [PSA 97:9](bibleref:19097009); [ISA 14:14](bibleref:23014014); [DAN 3:26](bibleref:27003026)[MRK 5:7](bibleref:41005007) parallel LUK 8:28; [LUK 1:32](bibleref:42001032), [LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035)
[LUK 6:35](bibleref:42006035); [ACT 16:17](bibleref:44016017)
