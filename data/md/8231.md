# 8231: discipline, divine

God disciplines his people through his word, through their experiences and through punishment, so that they may live in ways pleasing to him.



## The nature of God's discipline


### It is a sign of God's love
**HEB 12:6** *See also* PRO 3:12; PSA 119:75; REV 3:19

### It is a sign of belonging to God's family
**HEB 12:6** *See also* PRO 3:12; DEU 8:5; 2SA 7:14; HEB 12:7

### It trains God's people
**HEB 12:11**

## The means of God's discipline


### The use of Scripture
**2TI 3:16** *See also* DEU 4:36, DEU 29:29; ISA 8:11

### The knowledge of God's grace
**TIT 2:11-2:12**

### Instructive experiences
 *See also* DEU 8:1-8:5, DEU 11:2-11:7; PSA 90:12; ISA 48:17

### Painful experiences
**HEB 12:7** *See also* 2CO 12:7-12:10; HEB 12:5, HEB 12:11; 1PE 1:6-1:7

### Punishment
**HEB 12:6** *See also* PRO 3:12; LEV 26:18, LEV 26:21, LEV 26:23-26:24, LEV 26:27-26:28; 2SA 7:14; PSA 73:14; JER 32:19; HOS 5:2, HOS 7:15-7:16; 1CO 11:32

## God disciplines all and only his children
**HEB 12:8** *See also* HEB 12:6

## The goals of God's discipline


### Respectful submission to God
**HEB 12:9**

### The good of God's people
**HEB 12:10** *See also* JOB 5:17-5:18; PSA 94:12, PSA 119:67, PSA 119:71, PSA 119:75

### Spiritual growth
**HEB 12:11** *See also* PRO 1:2-1:3

### Preparation for heaven
**2CO 4:17-4:18** *See also* ROM 8:18; JAS 1:12

## Believers should not despise God's discipline
**JOB 5:17** *See also* HEB 12:5; PRO 3:11

## Divine discipline in the church


### By reproof
 2CO 7:8, 2CO 13:2; ; 2TH 3:15

### For the benefit of sinners
 MAT 18:15; ; 1CO 5:1-5:7; ; 2TH 3:14

### As a warning to others
 1TI 5:20

### To be exercised with kindness
 2CO 2:6; ; GAL 6:1

### The role of witnesses
 MAT 18:16; ; 1TI 5:19
