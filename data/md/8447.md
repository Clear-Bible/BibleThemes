# 8447: hospitality, examples of

Scripture provides many examples of friendship and generosity towards strangers, and encourages believers to be hospitable in turn.



## Examples of hospitality in the OT
**GEN 18:2-18:5** *See also* GEN 19:1-19:3 *Lot entertains visitors from God*; GEN 24:22-24:25 *Abraham's servant is welcomed in Rebekah's home*; EXO 2:20 *Moses is welcomed by a priest of Midian*; JDG 13:15 *Manoah receives the angel of the LORD.*JDG 19:1-19:4, JDG 19:20-19:21
2SA 17:27-17:29 *hospitality shown to David and his people*; 1KI 17:7-17:16 *The widow of Zarephath cares for Elijah despite her poverty*; 2KI 4:8-4:10 *a Shunammite provides for Elisha*; NEH 5:17 *Nehemiah shows hospitality to fellow Jews.*

## Hospitality shown to Jesus Christ
**MAT 9:10** parallels MRK 2:15, LUK 5:29 *Jesus Christ received hospitality from people of all social backgrounds.* *See also* MAT 8:14-8:15 parallels MRK 1:29-1:31, LUK 4:38-4:39 *at Peter's house*MAT 26:6-26:7 parallel MRK 14:3; JHN 12:1-12:3
LUK 7:36 *at the house of Simon the Pharisee*; LUK 10:38 *at the home of Mary and Martha*; LUK 14:1 *at the house of a prominent Pharisee*; LUK 19:5-19:7 *at Zacchaeus' house*; LUK 24:29 *with disciples in Emmaus*; JHN 2:2 *at the wedding at Cana*

## Hospitality assisted the mission of the apostles


### Hospitality shown to the disciples
**MAT 10:11-10:12** parallels MRK 6:10, LUK 9:4 *Jesus Christ sends out the Twelve.* *See also* LUK 10:5-10:7

### Hospitality shown to Peter
 ACT 10:32 *at the home of Simon the tanner*; ; ACT 10:48 *with Cornelius and his family*

### Hospitality shown to Paul and his companions
 ACT 16:15 *at the home of Lydia*; ; ACT 16:34 *at the house of the Philippian jailer*; ; ACT 18:2-18:3 *with Aquila and Priscilla in Corinth*; ; ACT 21:8 *at the house of Philip*; ; ACT 21:16 *at the home of Mnason in Jerusalem*; ; ACT 28:2 *on Malta*; ; ACT 28:7 *at the home of Publius*; ; ROM 16:23 *with Gaius*; ; PHM 1:22 *as a guest of Philemon*

## Christians opened their homes for gatherings of believers
**1CO 16:19** *See also* ACT 2:46; ROM 16:3-16:5; COL 4:15; PHM 1:2

## Examples of inhospitable behaviour
**1SA 25:10-25:11** *See also* NUM 20:18 *Edom*; NUM 21:21-21:23 *Sihon*; DEU 23:3-23:4 *Ammonites and Moabites*; JDG 19:15 *Gibeah*; LUK 9:52-9:53 *a Samaritan village*
