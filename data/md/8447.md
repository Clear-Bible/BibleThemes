# 8447: hospitality, examples of

Scripture provides many examples of friendship and generosity towards strangers, and encourages believers to be hospitable in turn.



## Examples of hospitality in the OT
**[GEN 18:2-18:5](bibleref:01018002-01018005)** *See also* [GEN 19:1-19:3](bibleref:01019001-01019003) *Lot entertains visitors from God* ; [GEN 24:22-24:25](bibleref:01024022-01024025) *Abraham's servant is welcomed in Rebekah's home* ; [EXO 2:20](bibleref:02002020) *Moses is welcomed by a priest of Midian* ; [JDG 13:15](bibleref:07013015) *Manoah receives the angel of the LORD.* [JDG 19:1-19:4](bibleref:07019001-07019004), [JDG 19:20-19:21](bibleref:07019020-07019021)
[2SA 17:27-17:29](bibleref:10017027-10017029) *hospitality shown to David and his people* ; [1KI 17:7-17:16](bibleref:11017007-11017016) *The widow of Zarephath cares for Elijah despite her poverty* ; [2KI 4:8-4:10](bibleref:12004008-12004010) *a Shunammite provides for Elisha* ; [NEH 5:17](bibleref:16005017) *Nehemiah shows hospitality to fellow Jews.* 

## Hospitality shown to Jesus Christ
**[MAT 9:10](bibleref:40009010)** parallels MRK 2:15, LUK 5:29 *Jesus Christ received hospitality from people of all social backgrounds.*  *See also* [MAT 8:14-8:15](bibleref:40008014-40008015) parallels MRK 1:29-1:31, LUK 4:38-4:39 *at Peter's house* [MAT 26:6-26:7](bibleref:40026006-40026007) parallel MRK 14:3; [JHN 12:1-12:3](bibleref:43012001-43012003)
[LUK 7:36](bibleref:42007036) *at the house of Simon the Pharisee* ; [LUK 10:38](bibleref:42010038) *at the home of Mary and Martha* ; [LUK 14:1](bibleref:42014001) *at the house of a prominent Pharisee* ; [LUK 19:5-19:7](bibleref:42019005-42019007) *at Zacchaeus' house* ; [LUK 24:29](bibleref:42024029) *with disciples in Emmaus* ; [JHN 2:2](bibleref:43002002) *at the wedding at Cana* 

## Hospitality assisted the mission of the apostles


### Hospitality shown to the disciples
**[MAT 10:11-10:12](bibleref:40010011-40010012)** parallels MRK 6:10, LUK 9:4 *Jesus Christ sends out the Twelve.*  *See also* [LUK 10:5-10:7](bibleref:42010005-42010007)

### Hospitality shown to Peter
