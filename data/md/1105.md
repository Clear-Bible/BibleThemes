# 1105: God, power of

God is all-powerful (that is omnipotent), and is able to do whatever he wills. His power is limited only by his character.



## God is all-powerful
**[MRK 10:27](bibleref:41010027)** parallel MAT 19:26 *See also* [GEN 18:14](bibleref:01018014); [PSA 93:1](bibleref:19093001); [MRK 14:36](bibleref:41014036); [LUK 1:37](bibleref:42001037); [2CO 4:7](bibleref:47004007)
