# 1105: God, power of

God is all-powerful (that is omnipotent), and is able to do whatever he wills. His power is limited only by his character.



## God is all-powerful
**MRK 10:27** parallel MAT 19:26 *See also* GEN 18:14; PSA 93:1; MRK 14:36; LUK 1:37; 2CO 4:7

## God cannot be thwarted
**JOB 42:2** *See also* JOB 9:12; ISA 14:27

## God's names denote his power


### The uniqueness of God's power
**JER 10:6**

### He is the Almighty
``the Almighty'' is Hebrew ``El Shaddai'':
 GEN 28:3, GEN 35:11, GEN 43:14; ; EXO 6:3; ; JOB 11:7, JOB 31:2

### He is the LORD Almighty and God Almighty
``Almighty'' in these verses is usually Hebrew ``Sabaoth'':
 PSA 24:10, PSA 89:8; ; JER 10:16; ; 2CO 6:18; ; REV 11:17, REV 19:6

### He is the Mighty One
 GEN 49:24; ; JOS 22:22; ; PSA 50:1; ; ISA 1:24, ISA 10:21, ISA 60:16; ; MRK 14:62; ; LUK 1:49

## God's power is seen above all in his acts of redemption


### God is powerful to save
**ZEP 3:17**

### God shows his power in the exodus
**DEU 9:29** *See also* EXO 13:9, EXO 14:31; DEU 7:8, DEU 9:26; NEH 1:10; JER 32:21

### God shows his power through the work of Jesus Christ
**ACT 2:24** *See also* ROM 1:4; EPH 1:19-1:21; COL 2:13-2:15; REV 12:10

### God shows his power through the proclamation of the good news about Jesus Christ
**ROM 1:16** *See also* 1CO 1:18, 1CO 1:24-1:25; 2CO 10:4; COL 2:12

## God's power is shown in Jesus Christ's life


### God's power in Jesus Christ's earthly ministry
**LUK 5:17** *See also* LUK 6:19, LUK 8:46; ACT 10:38; 2PE 1:16

### God's power in Jesus Christ's heavenly ministry
**HEB 7:16** *In contrast to all other priests, Jesus Christ exercises his ministry now in heaven because death could not hold him.* *See also* ACT 4:10; 2CO 13:4

### God's power seen in Jesus Christ's second coming
**MRK 13:26** parallels MAT 24:30, LUK 21:27 *See also* 2TH 1:9-1:10

## The Holy Spirit demonstrates God's power
**MIC 3:8** *See also* ACT 1:8, ACT 10:38; ROM 15:19; 1CO 2:4-2:5; 1TH 1:5

## God's power in the world


### God rules the created order
**ROM 1:20** *See also* PSA 19:1, PSA 65:6, PSA 68:34; ISA 40:26; MAT 8:26 parallel MRK 4:39; MAT 21:19; HEB 1:3

### God's works are powerful
**DEU 3:24** *See also* PSA 71:18, PSA 77:12, PSA 145:4; JER 32:17-32:19; LUK 1:51

### The ark symbolises God's powerful presence
**PSA 132:8** parallel 2CH 6:41 *See also* 1SA 5:1-5:12; 2SA 6:6-6:8, 2SA 6:11-6:12; PSA 78:61

### God keeps his promises
**ROM 4:21** *See also* 2CO 9:8

### God gives life to the dead
**1CO 6:14** *See also* MAT 22:29 parallel MRK 12:24; PHP 3:21

## God's power in the lives of believers


### God gives his own power to his people

**2TH 1:11** *See also* MIC 5:4; ACT 4:28; 1CO 5:4; EPH 1:20-1:21
