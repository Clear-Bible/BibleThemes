# 8440: glorifying God

The giving of glory to God through acts of praise and obedience. Believers are to glorify God in their lives, as Jesus Christ glorified him in his life.



## Believers are to glorify God
**PSA 96:1-96:9** parallel 1CH 16:23-16:29**ISA 42:12** *See also* ISA 43:5-43:7, ISA 66:18-66:19; HAB 2:14

### God is entitled to be glorified
**PSA 96:4-96:9** parallel 1CH 16:25-16:29 *See also* PSA 29:1-29:2, PSA 115:1; ISA 24:14-24:15

### Glorifying God is the believer's natural response to him
**PSA 118:28** *See also* PSA 34:3, PSA 69:30, PSA 86:12

## God is glorified in Jesus Christ
**JHN 13:31** *See also* JHN 14:13, JHN 17:4

## Reasons for glorifying God


### His holiness
**PSA 99:9** *See also* REV 15:4

### His great deeds
**PSA 86:8-86:10** *See also* ISA 25:1; 2CO 4:13-4:15

### His loving mercy
**PSA 63:3** *See also* PSA 115:1; ROM 15:8-15:9

### His righteous judgments
**REV 14:7** *See also* EZK 28:22-28:23; ROM 11:33-11:36; REV 11:13

## Means of glorifying God


### By praising God
**REV 4:11, REV 7:12** *See also* LUK 2:13; ROM 11:33-11:36, ROM 16:27; HEB 13:20-13:21; 1PE 4:11; 2PE 3:18; JUD 1:25

### By living for God
**1CO 10:31** *See also* ROM 4:20; 1CO 6:20; 2TH 1:11-1:12; 1PE 4:10-4:13

### By doing good works and bearing fruit
**JHN 15:8**; **PHP 1:9-1:11** *See also* MAT 5:16; GAL 5:22-5:26; HEB 13:20-13:21; 1PE 2:12
