# 5591: treasure

Highly valued objects or accumulated riches. Treasure has at best only short-term benefit but can bring spiritual dangers. Spiritual treasure is of eternal value and comes from knowing and serving God.

## The limitations of accumulated treasure


### It is easily lost
**[ISA 64:11](bibleref:23064011)** *See also* [2KI 24:13](bibleref:12024013); [JER 15:13](bibleref:24015013), [JER 17:3](bibleref:24017003), [JER 20:5](bibleref:24020005); [LAM 1:10-1:11](bibleref:25001010-25001011); [HOS 13:15](bibleref:28013015); [OBA 1:6](bibleref:31001006); [MAT 6:19](bibleref:40006019); [JAS 5:2-5:3](bibleref:59005002-59005003); [REV 18:14](bibleref:66018014)

### It is no security against God's displeasure
**[PRO 11:4](bibleref:20011004)** *See also* [JOB 20:20](bibleref:18020020); [JER 51:13](bibleref:24051013); [EZK 7:19](bibleref:26007019); [HOS 9:6](bibleref:28009006); [ZEP 1:18](bibleref:36001018); [LUK 12:16-12:21](bibleref:42012016-42012021)

### It does not satisfy
**[ECC 5:10](bibleref:21005010)** *See also* [PSA 39:6](bibleref:19039006); [HAG 1:6](bibleref:37001006)

## The spiritual dangers of accumulated treasure


### It can lead to pride
**[1TI 6:17](bibleref:54006017)** *See also* [DEU 8:13-8:14](bibleref:05008013-05008014); [EZK 7:20](bibleref:26007020), [EZK 28:4-28:5](bibleref:26028004-26028005); [DAN 4:30](bibleref:27004030)

### It can lead a person away from God
**[REV 3:17](bibleref:66003017)** *See also* [JOB 31:24-31:25](bibleref:18031024-18031025), [JOB 31:28](bibleref:18031028); [PRO 11:28](bibleref:20011028)

### It can lead to greed
**[LUK 12:15](bibleref:42012015)** *See also* [1TI 6:9-6:10](bibleref:54006009-54006010)

### Acquiring it can lead to dishonesty
 [JOS 7:21](bibleref:06007021); [PSA 62:10](bibleref:19062010); [PRO 21:6](bibleref:20021006)

### It can lead to idolatry
