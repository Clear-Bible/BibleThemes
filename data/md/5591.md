# 5591: treasure

Highly valued objects or accumulated riches. Treasure has at best only short-term benefit but can bring spiritual dangers. Spiritual treasure is of eternal value and comes from knowing and serving God.



## The limitations of accumulated treasure


### It is easily lost
**ISA 64:11** *See also* 2KI 24:13; JER 15:13, JER 17:3, JER 20:5; LAM 1:10-1:11; HOS 13:15; OBA 1:6; MAT 6:19; JAS 5:2-5:3; REV 18:14

### It is no security against God's displeasure
**PRO 11:4** *See also* JOB 20:20; JER 51:13; EZK 7:19; HOS 9:6; ZEP 1:18; LUK 12:16-12:21

### It does not satisfy
**ECC 5:10** *See also* PSA 39:6; HAG 1:6

## The spiritual dangers of accumulated treasure


### It can lead to pride
**1TI 6:17** *See also* DEU 8:13-8:14; EZK 7:20, EZK 28:4-28:5; DAN 4:30

### It can lead a person away from God
**REV 3:17** *See also* JOB 31:24-31:25, JOB 31:28; PRO 11:28

### It can lead to greed
**LUK 12:15** *See also* 1TI 6:9-6:10

### Acquiring it can lead to dishonesty
 JOS 7:21; ; PSA 62:10; ; PRO 21:6

### It can lead to idolatry
 EXO 32:2-32:4; ; ISA 2:7-2:8; ; EZK 7:20, EZK 16:17

## Spiritual treasure


### Treasure consists in knowing God and his wisdom
**COL 2:2-2:3** *See also* JOB 28:12-28:19; PRO 2:4, PRO 3:13-3:15, PRO 8:10-8:11, PRO 24:3-24:4; ISA 33:6

### God's word is supreme treasure
 JOB 23:12; ; PSA 19:9-19:10, PSA 119:72, PSA 119:127

### The kingdom of God is supreme treasure
**MAT 13:44** *See also* MAT 13:45-13:46, MAT 13:52

### Spiritual treasure is more important than material possessions
**HEB 11:26** *See also* JOB 22:23-22:25; MAT 13:44-13:46

### It is of eternal value
**LUK 12:33** *See also* MAT 6:20; 1PE 1:4

### It is gained through generosity and wisdom
**MAT 19:21** parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22 *See also* PRO 8:21, PRO 15:6; 1TI 6:18-6:19

## Treasure reveals true commitment
**MAT 6:21** parallel LUK 12:34 *See also* MAT 6:24 parallel LUK 16:13

## Believers willingly give God their treasures
**MAT 2:11** *See also* EXO 35:22; JOS 6:24; 1CH 29:3-29:5

## God's people are his treasured possession
**DEU 7:6** *See also* EXO 19:5; DEU 14:2, DEU 26:18; PSA 135:4; MAL 3:17

## The gospel as treasure in clay jars
 2CO 4:7
