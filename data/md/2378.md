# 2378: kingdom of God, characteristics of

Those who have entered the kingdom must live according to its values, anticipating the reign of peace which will come when Jesus Christ returns.



## The kingdom of God does not conform to the standards of this world
**[JHN 18:36](bibleref:43018036)**; **[ROM 14:17](bibleref:45014017)**

## Those who inherit the kingdom of God are to bear its fruit
**[1TH 2:12](bibleref:52002012)** *See also* [MAT 25:34-25:36](bibleref:40025034-40025036); [2PE 1:10-1:11](bibleref:61001010-61001011)

## The kingdom of God is and will be a kingdom of peace


### Peace between people
**[ISA 2:2-2:4](bibleref:23002002-23002004)** parallel MIC 4:1-4:4**[JAS 3:18](bibleref:59003018)** *See also* [ISA 9:5](bibleref:23009005), [ISA 19:24-19:25](bibleref:23019024-23019025); [MIC 5:4-5:5](bibleref:33005004-33005005); [MAT 5:9](bibleref:40005009) *The Sermon on the Mount (Mt 5:1-7:29) is often thought of as a description of life in the kingdom.*
