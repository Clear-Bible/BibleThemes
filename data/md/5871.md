# 5871: greed, believers' response to

Greed should have no place in the lives of God's people.



## The righteous are not to be greedy
**PRO 28:6** *See also* EXO 20:17 parallel DEU 5:21; PRO 11:28, PRO 22:1, PRO 23:4; EZK 18:8, EZK 18:17; ROM 13:9

## The Christian leader is not to be greedy
**1TI 3:2-3:3** *See also* TIT 1:7; 1PE 5:2

### The example of Paul
**ACT 20:33** *See also* 1TH 2:5

## The antidotes to greed


### Greed must be repented of
**LUK 3:14** *John the Baptist counselling soldiers on how they must express their repentance.*

### The need to be on guard against greed
**PSA 62:10**; **LUK 12:15** *See also* LUK 12:16-12:21

### The need to be separate from greed and from the greedy
**1CO 5:11**; **COL 3:5** *See also* JOB 36:18; EPH 5:3

### The need to be content
**HEB 13:5** *See also* PHP 4:11-4:13; 1TI 6:3-6:10

### The need to seek God, not gain
**1TI 6:17-6:18** *See also* 1KI 3:11-3:14; PSA 119:14; JER 9:23-9:24
