# 5539: sluggard

Someone characterised by a settled attitude of slothfulness and idleness. Such people are consistently condemned throughout Scripture.



## Sluggards are characterised by slothfulness and idleness


### They are lazy and refuse to work
**PRO 6:6-6:8** *See also* PRO 12:27, PRO 15:19, PRO 19:24, PRO 20:4, PRO 21:25, PRO 24:30-24:31, PRO 26:15

### They love to stay in bed when they should be up and active
**PRO 6:9** *See also* PRO 26:14

### They make excuses to avoid work
**PRO 22:13** *See also* PRO 26:13

### Their desires are not met
**PRO 13:4** *See also* PRO 20:4, PRO 21:25-21:26

### Their laziness leads to poverty
**PRO 6:10-6:11** *See also* PRO 10:4-10:5, PRO 12:24, PRO 18:9, PRO 19:15, PRO 20:13, PRO 24:33-24:34; ECC 4:5, ECC 10:18

### Their attitude is irritating to others
 PRO 10:26

### They are conceited
 PRO 26:16

## Sluggards condemned throughout Scripture


### In the book of Proverbs
**PRO 10:4** *See also* PRO 6:6-6:11, PRO 12:11, PRO 12:24, PRO 12:27, PRO 13:4, PRO 14:23, PRO 18:9, PRO 28:19

### By Jesus Christ
 MAT 20:3, MAT 20:6, MAT 25:24-25:30

### In the epistles
**1TH 5:14** *See also* 2TH 3:6, 2TH 3:10-3:11; 1TI 5:13; TIT 1:12, TIT 3:14; HEB 6:12
