# 5539: sluggard

Someone characterised by a settled attitude of slothfulness and idleness. Such people are consistently condemned throughout Scripture.

## Sluggards are characterised by slothfulness and idleness


### They are lazy and refuse to work
**[PRO 6:6-6:8](bibleref:20006006-20006008)** *See also* [PRO 12:27](bibleref:20012027), [PRO 15:19](bibleref:20015019), [PRO 19:24](bibleref:20019024), [PRO 20:4](bibleref:20020004), [PRO 21:25](bibleref:20021025), [PRO 24:30-24:31](bibleref:20024030-20024031), [PRO 26:15](bibleref:20026015)

### They love to stay in bed when they should be up and active
**[PRO 6:9](bibleref:20006009)** *See also* [PRO 26:14](bibleref:20026014)

### They make excuses to avoid work
**[PRO 22:13](bibleref:20022013)** *See also* [PRO 26:13](bibleref:20026013)

### Their desires are not met
**[PRO 13:4](bibleref:20013004)** *See also* [PRO 20:4](bibleref:20020004), [PRO 21:25-21:26](bibleref:20021025-20021026)

### Their laziness leads to poverty
**[PRO 6:10-6:11](bibleref:20006010-20006011)** *See also* [PRO 10:4-10:5](bibleref:20010004-20010005), [PRO 12:24](bibleref:20012024), [PRO 18:9](bibleref:20018009), [PRO 19:15](bibleref:20019015), [PRO 20:13](bibleref:20020013), [PRO 24:33-24:34](bibleref:20024033-20024034); [ECC 4:5](bibleref:21004005), [ECC 10:18](bibleref:21010018)

### Their attitude is irritating to others
 [PRO 10:26](bibleref:20010026)

### They are conceited
 [PRO 26:16](bibleref:20026016)
