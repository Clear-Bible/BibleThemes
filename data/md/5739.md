# 5739: twins

Two children born of the same parents at the same time. Figuratively used to describe a matching pair.

## Examples of twins
**[GEN 25:21-25:26](bibleref:01025021-01025026)** *Although Jacob and Esau were twins, their very different characters indicate that God deals with and relates to people individually.*  *See also* [GEN 38:27-38:30](bibleref:01038027-01038030); [JHN 20:24](bibleref:43020024) *Didymus means “twin”. It is not known who his twin was* ; [JHN 21:2](bibleref:43021002); [ACT 28:11](bibleref:44028011) *the mythical twin sons of Zeus regarded as special protectors of sailors*
