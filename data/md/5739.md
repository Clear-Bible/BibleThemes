# 5739: twins

Two children born of the same parents at the same time. Figuratively used to describe a matching pair.



## Examples of twins
**GEN 25:21-25:26** *Although Jacob and Esau were twins, their very different characters indicate that God deals with and relates to people individually.* *See also* GEN 38:27-38:30; JHN 20:24 *Didymus means “twin”. It is not known who his twin was*; JHN 21:2; ACT 28:11 *the mythical twin sons of Zeus regarded as special protectors of sailors*

## Twins used to illustrate God's election
 ROM 9:10-9:16

## Twins used figuratively for matching pairs
 SNG 4:2, SNG 4:5, SNG 6:6, SNG 7:3
