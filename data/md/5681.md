# 5681: family, nature of

A unit ordained by God for the comfort and protection of its members. It is usually a group of relatives, but the people of God are also seen as a family.



## The scope of the family in Scripture


### The family unit
**GEN 43:7** *See also* EXO 1:1, EXO 12:3; JDG 11:2; 1SA 1:21, 1SA 27:3; 2SA 2:3; 1KI 17:15; 1CH 13:14 *The family of Obed-Edom is blessed by the presence of the ark*; PRO 27:27, PRO 31:15 *The wife of noble character provides for her family*; MRK 3:21; LUK 12:52; ACT 16:33-16:34 *The Philippian jailer and his family are baptised*; 1TI 5:8

### The extended family or clan
**GEN 24:37-24:38** *See also* GEN 24:40, GEN 46:27, GEN 50:22; NUM 2:2, NUM 18:1 *the priesthood of Aaron and his family*; DEU 25:5 *Levirate marriage provides for the continuity of the family*; JOS 2:17-2:18, JOS 6:23, JOS 6:25; 1SA 22:22; 1KI 14:14; 2KI 11:1 parallel 2CH 22:10 *Athaliah destroys the royal family of Judah*; EZR 10:16; JER 35:3, JER 38:17; DAN 1:3

### The descendants of any individual
**JHN 7:42** *See also* DEU 25:10; JOS 13:29; 1SA 2:31-2:32, 1SA 2:36, 1SA 3:11-3:13; DAN 11:7

### The people of God
**HEB 2:10-2:15** *See also* PSA 22:22; AMO 3:1; GAL 6:10; 1PE 4:17

## Marriage obligations and restrictions within the family


### Marriage within the wider family circle was preferable in early times
**JDG 14:3** *See also* GEN 24:1-24:9, GEN 24:37, GEN 24:40, GEN 27:46-28:2, GEN 28:6-28:9; EXO 2:1

### Incestuous marriage was forbidden
**LEV 18:6-18:18** *See also* LEV 20:17, LEV 20:19-20:21; DEU 27:20-27:23; MAT 14:3 parallel MRK 6:17; 1CO 5:1-5:5

### The obligation to marry the widow of a dead brother
**DEU 25:5-25:10** *See also* GEN 38:8-38:10; RUT 2:19-2:20, RUT 4:1-4:10; MAT 22:23-22:32 parallels MRK 12:18-12:27, LUK 20:27-20:38

## Laws of inheritance kept property within the family
**NUM 27:1-27:11** *See also* LEV 25:25-25:28; JOB 42:13-42:15

## Examples of family solidarity
 GEN 14:8-14:16 *Abraham and Lot*; ; 2SA 18:31-18:33 *David and Absalom*; ; RUT 1:11-1:18 *Naomi and Ruth*; ; NEH 4:13; ; ACT 23:12-23:24; ; 1TI 5:3-5:4 *Family solidarity is encouraged.*
