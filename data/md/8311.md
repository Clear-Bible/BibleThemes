# 8311: morality, and redemption

The morality which is appropriate for God's own people is expressed in the OT law, and in the person and teaching of Jesus Christ.



## Relationship with God is expressed in moral terms


### In the moral standards God expects of his people
**LEV 11:44-11:45**; **MAT 5:48** *See also* MAT 5:43-5:44 parallel LUK 6:27-6:28; LUK 6:35-6:36; EPH 4:31-5:3; 1PE 1:15-1:16; 3JN 1:11

### In God's moral laws
**ISA 51:4**; **ROM 7:12** *See also* EXO 20:1-20:7; DEU 6:25; PSA 119:137-119:138, PSA 147:19-147:20; PRO 29:18; ISA 51:7

### In God's denunciation of immorality and religious hypocrisy
**AMO 2:6-2:7** *See also* ISA 1:15-1:23; AMO 5:21-5:24; MIC 6:8; EPH 5:5; COL 3:5-3:7

### In the life and teaching of Jesus Christ
**JHN 1:14** *See also* MAT 5:17-5:20; JHN 1:17-1:18, JHN 3:19; HEB 4:15

## The incentive for moral change lies in what God has done in redemption


### In the OT
**DEU 24:17-24:18** *See also* LEV 19:13-19:18, LEV 19:35-19:36; DEU 15:12-15:15, DEU 24:21-24:22

### In the NT
**1CO 6:19-6:20** *See also* ROM 12:1-12:2

## Moral change brought about by God's work of renewal
**EZK 36:26-36:27**; **1CO 6:9-6:11** *See also* ROM 8:3-8:4, ROM 8:11-8:14; GAL 5:19-5:24; EPH 4:22-4:24; PHP 2:12-2:13; COL 3:8-3:12; HEB 8:10; JER 31:33
