# 4512: staff





## The staff was a basic item of personal property


### It was carried at most times
**1SA 14:27** *See also* GEN 47:31; JDG 6:21; HEB 9:4

### It was carried by travellers
**GEN 32:10** *See also* EXO 12:11; MAT 10:5-10:10 parallels MRK 6:7-6:13, LUK 9:1-9:6

### It was carried by shepherds
**MIC 7:14** *See also* PSA 23:4; ZEC 11:7, ZEC 11:10-11:14
