# 4627: creatures

Generally, living beings within God's creation, especially the exotic animals seen in visions, which often have a deep symbolic significance.

## Creatures representing angelic beings who worship God
**[EZK 1:5-1:6](bibleref:26001005-26001006)**; **[REV 4:6-4:8](bibleref:66004006-66004008)** *The strange creatures of Ezekiel and Revelation seem to stand for the fulness and variety of God's creation.*  *See also* [EZK 1:7-1:25](bibleref:26001007-26001025), [EZK 3:12-3:13](bibleref:26003012-26003013), [EZK 10:15-10:22](bibleref:26010015-26010022) *The creatures are here identified as cherubim* ; [REV 4:9-4:11](bibleref:66004009-66004011), [REV 5:6-5:14](bibleref:66005006-66005014), [REV 6:1-6:3](bibleref:66006001-66006003), [REV 6:5-6:6](bibleref:66006005-66006006), [REV 7:11](bibleref:66007011), [REV 14:3](bibleref:66014003), [REV 15:7](bibleref:66015007), [REV 19:4](bibleref:66019004)

## Creatures representing kings or kingdoms
**[DAN 7:17](bibleref:27007017), [DAN 8:20-8:21](bibleref:27008020-27008021)** *The “beasts” here are unnatural, and represent the hostility of human empires to the rule of God. The horns represent individual kings within an imperial dynasty.* 

## Creatures representing Satan
The beast here represents Satan, with the horns representing royal authority at a time of Roman persecution of the church:
