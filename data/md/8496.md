# 8496: witnessing, importance of

Sharing the good news about God and Jesus Christ with others is a work to which some believers have a special calling, but in which all believers are to take part.



## Witnessing is sharing the truth of God
**PSA 145:10-145:12**; **LUK 24:46-24:48** *See also* 1CH 16:7-16:9; PSA 71:15; MAT 27:54 parallels MRK 15:39, LUK 23:47; JHN 1:35-1:36, JHN 4:29

## Believers are commanded to witness
**MAT 28:18-28:19** *See also* ISA 12:4-12:5; MAT 24:14 parallel MRK 13:10; ACT 1:8, ACT 9:15-9:16

## Special ministries of witnessing


### By the prophets
**JHN 1:6-1:8** *See also* JER 1:4-1:9; EZK 33:7-33:9; JON 1:2, JON 1:9; JHN 1:15, JHN 1:29, JHN 1:36; ACT 10:43

### By Jesus Christ
**JHN 18:37** *See also* JHN 3:31-3:34; REV 1:5, REV 3:14

### By the apostles
**ACT 4:33** *See also* ACT 2:32, ACT 10:39-10:42, ACT 13:30-13:31; 1JN 1:1-1:3

### By Christian leaders
**2TI 1:8** *Paul, in prison for his faith, encourages the young leader, Timothy.* *See also* ACT 8:5; EPH 4:11
