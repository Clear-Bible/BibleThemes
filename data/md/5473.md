# 5473: proof, through testing

God seeks proof of genuine love, faith and growth in the lives of his people. To this end, he tests them in various ways. However, when people test him, it is often a symptom of lack of trust.



## Reasons for God's testing of Israel


### To refine them
**ZEC 13:8-13:9** *See also* PSA 66:10-66:12; PRO 17:3; ISA 48:10; JER 6:27-6:30, JER 9:7

### To prove their obedience
**EXO 16:4** *See also* EXO 15:25-15:26; DEU 8:2-8:5, DEU 8:15-8:16; JDG 2:22, JDG 3:1-3:4; 1CH 29:17

### To prove their love for him
**DEU 13:1-13:3**

## God tests individuals
**1PE 1:6-1:7** *See also* GEN 22:1-22:18 *Abraham*; JOB 23:10 *Job*; 2CH 32:31 *Hezekiah*; JHN 6:5-6:6 *Philip*; JAS 1:2-1:3, JAS 1:12

## Those who test God are usually motivated by lack of trust
**PSA 78:18-78:22** *See also* EXO 17:1-17:7; NUM 14:22-14:23; DEU 6:16; PSA 78:40-78:44, PSA 78:56-78:57, PSA 106:13-106:15; 1CO 10:9; HEB 3:7-3:11; PSA 95:7-95:11

## God may invite certain people to test him
**MAL 3:10** *See also* ISA 7:10-7:14

## God's people are to seek proof through testing in certain areas


### Believers are to test themselves
**2CO 13:5** *See also* GAL 6:4

### Believers are to test what they hear
**1TH 5:21** *See also* 1CO 14:29; 1JN 4:1-4:3
