# 8836: unbelief, as response to God

The stubborn and sinful refusal to accept God's word and his messengers. Since the coming of Jesus Christ it is frequently expressed in the rejection of his person and teaching.



## Unbelief expressed towards God
**PSA 78:22**

### Rebellion against God
**2KI 17:14-17:15** *See also* NEH 9:16; PSA 78:17-78:19, PSA 95:8-95:11; ISA 65:2; JER 7:26; ACT 7:51; HEB 3:12-3:19

### Rejection of God's promises
**PSA 106:24** *See also* ROM 4:20, ROM 10:16; HEB 4:2

## Unbelief expressed towards Jesus Christ


### Rejection by those close to him
**JHN 1:11** *See also* MAT 13:54-13:58 parallel MRK 6:1-6:6; LUK 4:14-4:30; JHN 7:5

### Rejection of his miracles
**MAT 12:22-12:24** parallels MRK 3:22, LUK 11:14-11:16

### Rejection of his claims
 *See also* LUK 22:67; JHN 5:37-5:38, JHN 6:36, JHN 6:64, JHN 10:24-10:26; 1PE 2:7

### Rejection of his words
**JHN 3:11-3:12** *See also* JHN 5:46-5:47, JHN 8:45

### Demands for miraculous signs
**JHN 4:48** *See also* MAT 12:38-12:39 parallel LUK 11:29; MAT 16:4 parallel MRK 8:12; LUK 11:16

## Unbelief expressed towards God's messengers
 GEN 19:16 *Lot*; ; EXO 4:1 *Moses*; ; 2KI 7:2 *Elisha*; ; 2CH 32:15 *Sennacherib of Assyria urges the inhabitants of Jerusalem to disbelieve Hezekiah's words*; ; ISA 53:1 *Isaiah*; ; MAT 21:32 *John the Baptist*; ACT 13:50, ACT 14:2; 
ACT 17:13 *Paul and Silas*; ACT 19:9, ACT 28:24

## Unbelief expressed in unrighteousness
**TIT 1:15** *See also* ROM 1:18; 2TH 2:12, 2TH 3:2; 1TI 1:13

## Unbelief despite miraculous signs
**NEH 9:16-9:17** *See also* EXO 4:8-4:9; PSA 78:32; JHN 9:18, JHN 10:31-10:33, JHN 12:37

## Means of overcoming unbelief
 JHN 10:37-10:38 *the evidence of Jesus Christ's miracles*; ; ROM 10:14-10:15 *preaching the gospel*; ; 1CO 14:22-14:24 *prophecy in worship*; ; 1PE 3:1 *the behaviour of a believing spouse*; ; 1TI 1:13 *God's mercy*
