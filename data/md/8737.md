# 8737: evil, believers' responses to

Scripture outlines several ways in which believers should respond to evil.



## Evil is to be avoided
**PSA 1:1**; **PRO 4:14**; **1TH 5:22** *See also* JOB 28:28; PSA 119:115; PRO 4:27, PRO 14:16; ISA 52:11; ACT 2:40 *Peter's appeal to the crowd*; ROM 12:9, ROM 13:141CO 5:6-5:7; GAL 5:9
2CO 6:14-6:18; EPH 4:27, EPH 5:3, EPH 5:6-5:7; 2TH 3:6; 1PE 3:11; PSA 34:14

## Evil is to be hated
**PRO 8:13** *See also* PSA 97:10; AMO 5:15; JHN 3:20; ROM 12:9

## Evil is to be rebuked
**MAT 16:23** parallel MRK 8:33**2TI 4:2** *See also* 1SA 2:29, 1SA 13:13, 1SA 15:22; 2SA 12:9 *Nathan rebukes David*; 1KI 18:18 *Elijah rebukes Ahab*; 2CH 16:9, 2CH 24:20, 2CH 26:18; EZR 10:10; PSA 141:5; DAN 4:27, DAN 5:22; MAT 14:4 parallel MRK 6:18 *John the Baptist rebukes Herod*; LUK 23:40; ACT 5:3-5:4 *Peter rebukes Ananias*; ACT 5:9 *Peter rebukes Sapphira*; 1TI 1:3, 1TI 5:20; TIT 1:13, TIT 2:15

## Evil is to be resisted
**PRO 1:10**; **GAL 2:11**; **EPH 6:11**; **JAS 4:7** *See also* 1CO 5:13; EPH 5:11; HEB 12:1; 1PE 2:1, 1PE 5:9

## Evil is to be repaid with good
**LUK 6:35**; **ROM 12:20-12:21** *See also* PRO 25:21-25:22; EXO 23:5; LEV 19:18; MAT 5:44 parallel LUK 6:27; 1TH 5:15; 1PE 3:9

## Believers should pray in response to evil
**MAT 6:13** parallel LUK 11:4**ACT 4:29**; **HEB 4:16** *See also* EXO 17:4; 1KI 18:36; 2KI 19:19; 2CH 20:12; EZR 8:23; NEH 1:4; MAT 26:41 parallels MRK 14:38, LUK 22:46; ACT 12:5; JAS 5:13

## Believers should trust God in the face of evil
**PSA 23:4**; **MAT 26:39** parallels MRK 14:36, LUK 22:42 *See also* PSA 4:8, PSA 20:7, PSA 27:1; ISA 12:2; DAN 3:17-3:18; HAB 3:17-3:18; 2TI 1:12; HEB 13:6; PSA 118:6-118:7
