# 8345: servanthood, and worship of God

The biblical understanding of servanthood has its foundation in people serving God in worship. The terms for “service” and “worship” are often interchangeable.



## Worship is a central aspect of serving God
**EXO 3:12** *See also* EXO 4:23, EXO 8:1, EXO 8:20, EXO 10:8; JOS 22:27; PSA 100:2, PSA 102:22; LUK 4:8

## Worship entails total obedience, not merely correct religious observances
**DEU 10:12-10:13** *See also* AMO 5:22-5:24; MIC 6:6-6:8

## Worship and service to God must be exclusive
**MAT 4:10** parallel LUK 4:8 *See also* DEU 6:13

## Serving false gods


### Worshipping false gods forbidden
**EXO 20:3-20:5** parallel DEU 5:8-5:9 *See also* EXO 23:24, EXO 34:14; JOS 23:7

### Warnings against serving false gods
**2CH 7:19-7:20** *See also* DEU 7:3-7:4; JOS 23:16, JOS 24:20

### Examples of idolatrous service
**1KI 16:31** *See also* JDG 2:11, JDG 2:13; 2KI 17:33; JER 2:20, JER 11:10; ROM 1:25

### Refusal to serve false gods
**DAN 3:12** *See also* JOS 24:16-24:18; DAN 3:18, DAN 3:28-3:29

### Judgment for serving false gods
**JDG 10:11-10:13** *See also* JDG 10:6-10:10; EZK 20:30-20:38

## The service of the Levites and the priesthood


### Consecration of the priesthood
**EXO 29:1** *See also* EXO 28:1, EXO 28:41, EXO 29:44, EXO 40:12-40:16; NUM 3:9-3:13

### Service in the Tent of Meeting
 NUM 4:3, NUM 4:23, NUM 4:30, NUM 4:35, NUM 4:39, NUM 4:43, NUM 18:7

### Service in the temple
**1CH 23:2-23:5** *See also* 1CH 23:24-23:32, 1CH 24:3, 1CH 24:5, 1CH 24:19, 1CH 25:1, 1CH 25:6-25:8 *musicians and singers*; 1CH 26:12 *gatekeepers*; 1CH 26:20 *treasurers*; 1CH 26:29-26:32; EZR 7:7, EZR 8:20; NEH 7:46, NEH 7:60

### The promise of restored service in the new temple
**EZK 44:15** *See also* EZK 44:10-44:14, EZK 44:24, EZK 45:5

### Examples of priestly service
 LUK 1:5, LUK 1:8-1:10, LUK 1:23

### The true service of Jesus Christ as high priest
**HEB 8:1-8:2** *Hebrews sees the OT priesthood and its services as superseded by Jesus Christ.* *See also* HEB 8:3-8:6

## The Christian's life as an act of worship
**ROM 12:1** *See also* REV 1:6, REV 5:10
