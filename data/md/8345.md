# 8345: servanthood, and worship of God

The biblical understanding of servanthood has its foundation in people serving God in worship. The terms for “service” and “worship” are often interchangeable.



## Worship is a central aspect of serving God
**[EXO 3:12](bibleref:02003012)** *See also* [EXO 4:23](bibleref:02004023), [EXO 8:1](bibleref:02008001), [EXO 8:20](bibleref:02008020), [EXO 10:8](bibleref:02010008); [JOS 22:27](bibleref:06022027); [PSA 100:2](bibleref:19100002), [PSA 102:22](bibleref:19102022); [LUK 4:8](bibleref:42004008)
