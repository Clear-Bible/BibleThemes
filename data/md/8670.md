# 8670: remembering

The process of recalling the past, especially the presence and activity of God in the history of his people. Remembering God's work in the past can lead to praise and rejoicing, and to hope for the future. God himself remembers.



## Divine remembering


### God always remembers his covenant
**1CH 16:15** parallel PSA 105:8 *See also* GEN 9:14-9:16; EXO 2:23-2:25; PSA 111:5; LUK 1:69-1:73

### God always remembers his promises
**PSA 105:42** *See also* DEU 6:20-6:23; JOS 1:3; 2PE 3:9

### God always remembers his people
**ISA 49:15-49:16** *See also* GEN 7:24-8:1, GEN 19:29, GEN 30:22; 1SA 1:19-1:20; LUK 1:54-1:55

### Situations in which God will not remember
**HEB 8:10-8:12** *See also* JER 31:33-31:34; ISA 65:16-65:17; EZK 18:21-18:24

## The Holy Spirit helps believers to remember Jesus Christ and his words
**JHN 14:26** *See also* JHN 2:22, JHN 12:16, JHN 16:4

## Human remembering


### Believers are to remember God's person
**DEU 8:18**; **PSA 119:55** *See also* PSA 42:5-42:6; JER 51:50; JON 2:7

### Believers are to remember God's acts in the past
**ISA 46:9** *See also* DEU 5:15, DEU 7:17-7:18, DEU 8:10-8:18; 1CH 16:12; MAT 16:9-16:10

### Believers are to remember God's wrath
**DEU 9:7-9:8** *See also* 2PE 3:1-3:10

### Believers are to remember God's commands
**NUM 15:38-15:40** *See also* DEU 8:1-8:2; PSA 103:17-103:18

### Believers are to remember their responsibilities towards one another
**ACT 20:35** *See also* GAL 2:10; HEB 13:2-13:3

### Believers are to remember the death of Jesus Christ
**1CO 11:24-11:28**; **LUK 22:17-22:20**

## The importance of remembering


### Remembering should lead to rejoicing
**PSA 13:5-13:6** *See also* DEU 26:4-26:11; PSA 66:5-66:6

### Remembering should lead to repentance
**REV 3:3** *See also* EZK 16:59-16:63; MAT 5:23-5:24, MAT 26:75 parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:61-22:62; REV 2:5

### Remembering should lead to understanding
**LUK 24:6-24:8** *See also* JHN 15:20, JHN 16:1-16:4; ACT 11:15-11:18

### Remembering should produce hope for the future
**LAM 3:21-3:24** *See also* PSA 42:4-42:11, PSA 103:1-103:2; HEB 10:32-10:36
