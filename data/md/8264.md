# 8264: gentleness

An expression of compassion, seen in God's dealings with the frail and weak, and expected of believers in their dealings with others.



## The gentleness of God


### In dealing with the wayward
**ISA 40:1-40:2**; **LUK 1:76-1:79** *See also* ISA 30:18-30:19, ISA 54:8, ISA 63:15 *God's gentleness is not always evident*; HOS 2:13-2:15; ROM 2:4 *God's gentle dealings are not always appreciated.*

### In caring for the weak
**ISA 40:11** *See also* 1KI 19:12

## The gentleness of Jesus Christ
**MAT 11:29** *See also* ZEC 9:9; MAT 21:4-21:5, MAT 12:18-12:21; ISA 42:1-42:3; 2CO 10:1; PHP 2:1; HEB 5:2

## Examples of Jesus Christ's gentleness
 MRK 1:40-1:42, MRK 5:25-5:34, MRK 10:13-10:16; ; JHN 8:3-8:11

## Gentleness as strength esteemed by God
**PRO 15:1**; **JAS 3:17** *See also* PRO 25:15; MAT 5:5; 1PE 3:1-3:4

## Gentleness as a mark of Christian character
**COL 3:12** *See also* GAL 5:22-5:23; EPH 4:1-4:2; 1TI 6:11

## Believers are to reflect God's gentleness in their dealings with people


### In correcting the wayward
**GAL 6:1** *See also* 2TI 2:24-2:25

### In reasoning with unbelievers
**1PE 3:15-3:16**

### In nurturing new believers
 *See also* 1TH 2:7

### In showing consideration to all
**TIT 3:1-3:2** *See also* EPH 4:32; PHP 4:5
