# 8787: opposition, to God's person and work

The forces of evil are antagonistic towards God, his people and his work, and believers must oppose them.



## Targets of opposition from evil forces


### The Lord himself
**PSA 2:1-2:3**; **HEB 12:3** *See also* ISA 63:10; ACT 7:51; REV 17:14

### God's people
**ACT 8:1-8:3** *See also* NUM 16:1-16:3; 2SA 16:5-16:8; EST 3:6; JER 18:18; ACT 26:9-26:11; 2TI 4:14-4:15; REV 13:7

### God's work
**1TH 2:2** *See also* EZR 4:4-4:5; NEH 4:7-4:8; ACT 4:18, ACT 13:8; 1CO 16:9
