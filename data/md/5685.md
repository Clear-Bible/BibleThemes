# 5685: fathers, responsibilities of

Fathers have a responsibility to love, instruct and discipline their children in the ways of the Lord. In return they are to be honoured and obeyed by their children.



## The role of fathers


### To love and care for their children
**PSA 103:13** *See also* DEU 1:31; JOB 1:4-1:5; MAT 7:9-7:11 parallel LUK 11:11-11:13; COL 3:21

### To discipline their children
**PRO 13:24** *See also* DEU 8:5, DEU 21:18-21:21; PRO 3:11-3:12, PRO 15:5, PRO 19:18, PRO 22:15, PRO 23:13, PRO 29:15; 1TI 3:2-3:5, 1TI 3:12; HEB 12:7-12:11

### To instruct their children
**PSA 78:2-78:8** *See also* DEU 4:9, DEU 6:6-6:7, DEU 6:20-6:24, DEU 11:18-11:21, DEU 31:13; PRO 13:1, PRO 22:6; EPH 6:4

### To be obeyed by their children
**COL 3:20** *See also* DEU 21:18-21:21; PRO 6:20, PRO 8:32; LUK 2:51; EPH 6:1

### To be heeded by their children
**PRO 23:22** *See also* PRO 1:8, PRO 4:1, PRO 4:10, PRO 13:1
