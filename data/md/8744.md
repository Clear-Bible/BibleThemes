# 8744: faithlessness, as disobedience

A lack of faith and obedience to God, which is seen especially in the lives of unbelievers. However, Scripture makes clear that believers can occasionally lapse into faithlessness towards God.



## Faithlessness among the ungodly


### In their character and lifestyle
**PSA 101:3-101:5, PSA 119:158** *See also* PSA 10:4, PSA 14:1; JHN 16:8-16:9; 2TH 3:2; TIT 1:15-1:16

### Examples of faithless men
 1SA 25:2-25:3 *Nabal*; ; EST 3:5-3:6 *Haman*; ; MAT 2:16 *Herod the Great*; ; ACT 19:23-19:27 *Demetrius*

### The faithless appear to prosper
**JER 12:1** *See also* JOB 21:7; PSA 73:3-73:5, PSA 73:12; MAL 3:15

### Faithlessness results in judgment
**PRO 14:14**; **JHN 3:36** *See also* PSA 73:17-73:18, PSA 73:27; MAL 4:1; MRK 16:16; JHN 8:24; 2TH 2:12; REV 21:8

## Faithlessness among the Jews


### Their failure to trust God for deliverance
**ISA 31:1** *See also* ISA 22:8-22:11, ISA 30:1-30:2, ISA 30:12-30:13

### Their rejection of the gospel
**JHN 12:37-12:38** *See also* ISA 53:1; JHN 3:11-3:12, JHN 5:37-5:40, JHN 8:45, JHN 10:25-10:26

### Specific examples
 MAT 13:58 parallel MRK 6:5-6:6 *the people of Nazareth*; ; MAT 19:21-19:22 parallels MRK 10:21-10:22, LUK 18:22-18:23 *the rich young ruler*; ; ACT 28:24 *Many Jews reject Paul's ministry in Rome*; ; 1TI 1:13 *Paul's own persecution of the church arose from unbelief in Jesus Christ.*

### Israel's estrangement from God
**MAT 21:43** *See also* PSA 118:22; MAT 21:41 parallel MRK 12:9; ROM 9:32-9:33

### The acceptance of the Gentiles
 ROM 11:17-11:21 *The Jews' rejection of the gospel opens the way for Gentiles to be incorporated into the people of God, represented here as branches grafted onto a cultivated olive tree.*

### Israel's faithlessness is partial and temporary
 ROM 11:5 *Though the people as a whole have rejected Jesus Christ, a faithful remnant remains*; ; ROM 11:11-11:12, ROM 11:23-11:27 *God's purpose for the faithful of Israel is their ultimate salvation.*

## Faithlessness among believers


### Lack of faith is rebuked
**MAT 14:30-14:31** *See also* MAT 6:30-6:32 parallel LUK 12:28-12:30 *worrying about food and clothing*; MAT 8:24-8:26 parallels MRK 4:38-4:40, LUK 8:23-8:25 *Fear during a storm at sea*; MAT 17:17-17:20 parallels MRK 9:17-9:19, LUK 9:40-9:42 *inability to cast out a demon*

### Causes of faithlessness
 MAT 16:8-16:10 *failure to learn from the lessons of the past*; ; LUK 24:25 *failure to accept teaching that has been given*
