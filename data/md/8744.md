# 8744: faithlessness, as disobedience

A lack of faith and obedience to God, which is seen especially in the lives of unbelievers. However, Scripture makes clear that believers can occasionally lapse into faithlessness towards God.



## Faithlessness among the ungodly


### In their character and lifestyle
**[PSA 101:3-101:5](bibleref:19101003-19101005), [PSA 119:158](bibleref:19119158)** *See also* [PSA 10:4](bibleref:19010004), [PSA 14:1](bibleref:19014001); [JHN 16:8-16:9](bibleref:43016008-43016009); [2TH 3:2](bibleref:53003002); [TIT 1:15-1:16](bibleref:56001015-56001016)

### Examples of faithless men
 [1SA 25:2-25:3](bibleref:09025002-09025003) *Nabal* ; [EST 3:5-3:6](bibleref:17003005-17003006) *Haman* ; [MAT 2:16](bibleref:40002016) *Herod the Great* ; [ACT 19:23-19:27](bibleref:44019023-44019027) *Demetrius* 

### The faithless appear to prosper
**[JER 12:1](bibleref:24012001)** *See also* [JOB 21:7](bibleref:18021007); [PSA 73:3-73:5](bibleref:19073003-19073005), [PSA 73:12](bibleref:19073012); [MAL 3:15](bibleref:39003015)

### Faithlessness results in judgment
**[PRO 14:14](bibleref:20014014)**; **[JHN 3:36](bibleref:43003036)** *See also* [PSA 73:17-73:18](bibleref:19073017-19073018), [PSA 73:27](bibleref:19073027); [MAL 4:1](bibleref:39004001); [MRK 16:16](bibleref:41016016); [JHN 8:24](bibleref:43008024); [2TH 2:12](bibleref:53002012); [REV 21:8](bibleref:66021008)

## Faithlessness among the Jews


### Their failure to trust God for deliverance
**[ISA 31:1](bibleref:23031001)** *See also* [ISA 22:8-22:11](bibleref:23022008-23022011), [ISA 30:1-30:2](bibleref:23030001-23030002), [ISA 30:12-30:13](bibleref:23030012-23030013)

### Their rejection of the gospel
**[JHN 12:37-12:38](bibleref:43012037-43012038)** *See also* [ISA 53:1](bibleref:23053001); [JHN 3:11-3:12](bibleref:43003011-43003012), [JHN 5:37-5:40](bibleref:43005037-43005040), [JHN 8:45](bibleref:43008045), [JHN 10:25-10:26](bibleref:43010025-43010026)

### Specific examples
 [MAT 13:58](bibleref:40013058) parallel MRK 6:5-6:6 *the people of Nazareth* ; [MAT 19:21-19:22](bibleref:40019021-40019022) parallels MRK 10:21-10:22, LUK 18:22-18:23 *the rich young ruler* ; [ACT 28:24](bibleref:44028024) *Many Jews reject Paul's ministry in Rome* ; [1TI 1:13](bibleref:54001013) *Paul's own persecution of the church arose from unbelief in Jesus Christ.* 

### Israel's estrangement from God
**[MAT 21:43](bibleref:40021043)** *See also* [PSA 118:22](bibleref:19118022); [MAT 21:41](bibleref:40021041) parallel MRK 12:9; [ROM 9:32-9:33](bibleref:45009032-45009033)

### The acceptance of the Gentiles
 [ROM 11:17-11:21](bibleref:45011017-45011021) *The Jews' rejection of the gospel opens the way for Gentiles to be incorporated into the people of God, represented here as branches grafted onto a cultivated olive tree.* 

### Israel's faithlessness is partial and temporary
 [ROM 11:5](bibleref:45011005) *Though the people as a whole have rejected Jesus Christ, a faithful remnant remains* ; [ROM 11:11-11:12](bibleref:45011011-45011012), [ROM 11:23-11:27](bibleref:45011023-45011027) *God's purpose for the faithful of Israel is their ultimate salvation.* 

## Faithlessness among believers


### Lack of faith is rebuked
**[MAT 14:30-14:31](bibleref:40014030-40014031)** *See also* [MAT 6:30-6:32](bibleref:40006030-40006032) parallel LUK 12:28-12:30 *worrying about food and clothing* ; [MAT 8:24-8:26](bibleref:40008024-40008026) parallels MRK 4:38-4:40, LUK 8:23-8:25 *Fear during a storm at sea* ; [MAT 17:17-17:20](bibleref:40017017-40017020) parallels MRK 9:17-9:19, LUK 9:40-9:42 *inability to cast out a demon* 

### Causes of faithlessness
 [MAT 16:8-16:10](bibleref:40016008-40016010) *failure to learn from the lessons of the past* ; [LUK 24:25](bibleref:42024025) *failure to accept teaching that has been given*
