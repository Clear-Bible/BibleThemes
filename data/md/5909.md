# 5909: motives, importance of

God acts from the highest, purest motives and expects his people to do the same.



## God's supreme motive is the honour of his name
**PSA 106:8** *See also* EXO 9:16; PSA 23:3; ISA 43:7; EZK 36:20-36:23; EPH 1:11-1:14

## God's glory is to be the highest motive of his people
**1CO 10:31** *See also* NUM 25:7-25:13; ISA 26:8; COL 3:17; 1PE 4:11

## Purity of motive is essential for acceptable worship
**MAT 6:1-6:18** *See also* PSA 51:6, PSA 51:10, PSA 86:11; MAT 5:8

## Wrong motives hinder effective prayer
**JAS 4:3** *See also* PSA 66:18; 1JN 3:21-3:22

## Motives for good moral behaviour


### Wanting to please God
**COL 3:22-3:25** *See also* EPH 6:5-6:8

### Compassion for others
**DEU 24:17-24:22** *The experience of slavery and the exodus were to promote compassion for the poor and the alien.* *See also* EXO 22:21-22:27; LEV 25:35-25:42, LEV 25:54-25:55

### Fear of punishment for sin
**HEB 13:4** *See also* COL 3:5-3:6; 1TH 4:3-4:6

## God judges the motives of the heart
**PRO 16:2** *See also* 1CH 28:9; PRO 20:27
