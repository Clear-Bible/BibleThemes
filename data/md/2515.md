# 2515: Jesus Christ, birth of

Jesus Christ was born to a poor unmarried Jewish couple in the village of Bethlehem. As Son of God, he was conceived by the Holy Spirit in the womb of his mother Mary.



## Jesus Christ's birth is prophesied in the OT


### The prophecy of a coming ruler
**[ISA 9:6-9:7](bibleref:23009006-23009007)**

### The prophecy of a virgin's son
**[ISA 7:14](bibleref:23007014)**

### The prophecy concerning his birthplace
**[MIC 5:2](bibleref:33005002)** *“Bethlehem Ephrathah” is the village where David grew up (“the town of David”).* 

## Jesus Christ was conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of a virgin
**[MAT 1:18](bibleref:40001018), [MAT 1:23](bibleref:40001023)** *See also* [MAT 1:20](bibleref:40001020); [LUK 1:34-1:35](bibleref:42001034-42001035)

## Jesus Christ's incarnation
**[JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014)**; **[GAL 4:4](bibleref:48004004)** *See also* [ROM 8:3](bibleref:45008003); [2CO 8:9](bibleref:47008009); [PHP 2:7-2:8](bibleref:50002007-50002008); [1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016); [1JN 4:2](bibleref:62004002)
