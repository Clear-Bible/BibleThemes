# 5465: profit

In the financial sense, gain through trading. The word is also used in Scripture of spiritual gain.

## Legitimate profit


### As the result of hard work
**[PRO 14:23](bibleref:20014023), [PRO 21:5](bibleref:20021005)** *See also* [PRO 31:18](bibleref:20031018)

### As the reward of generosity
 [PRO 11:24](bibleref:20011024); [LUK 6:38](bibleref:42006038)

### As the gift of God
 [DEU 8:18](bibleref:05008018)

### It is withheld by God in judgment
 [DEU 28:15-28:20](bibleref:05028015-05028020); [JER 12:13](bibleref:24012013); [HAG 1:9](bibleref:37001009)

### It is for this life only
 [ECC 1:3-1:4](bibleref:21001003-21001004); [1TI 6:7](bibleref:54006007)
