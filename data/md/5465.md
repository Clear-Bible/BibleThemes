# 5465: profit

In the financial sense, gain through trading. The word is also used in Scripture of spiritual gain.



## Legitimate profit


### As the result of hard work
**PRO 14:23, PRO 21:5** *See also* PRO 31:18

### As the reward of generosity
 PRO 11:24; ; LUK 6:38

### As the gift of God
 DEU 8:18

### It is withheld by God in judgment
 DEU 28:15-28:20; ; JER 12:13; ; HAG 1:9

### It is for this life only
 ECC 1:3-1:4; ; 1TI 6:7

## Profiting by oppression


### A common practice of the wicked
**ECC 5:8-5:9**

### It is often cloaked in hypocrisy
 *See also* EZK 33:31

### It is against God's law
**LEV 25:37**

### It is of little worth
 *See also* PRO 11:16, PRO 16:8

### It is short-lived
 JOB 20:18-20:19; ; ISA 23:17-23:18; ; JER 17:11

### It is judged by God
 PRO 1:18-1:19; ; EZK 22:12-22:14; ; MIC 4:13; ; HAB 2:9

### Its avoidance is rewarded
 PRO 28:16; ; ISA 33:15-33:16

## Profiting by dishonesty


### It is common amongst the wicked
 JER 22:17

### It is forbidden to God's servants
 EXO 18:21; ; 1SA 8:3; ; 1TI 3:8; ; TIT 1:7

## Profiting selfishly


### It is common amongst the wicked
 ISA 56:11

### It is avoided by the godly
**PSA 119:36**

## Using religion for profit


### In the OT
 *See also* NUM 22:16-22:17; 1SA 2:12-2:16; JER 6:13, JER 8:10

### In the NT
**2CO 2:17** *See also* ACT 8:18-8:23; 1TI 6:5; TIT 1:11; JUD 1:11

## Profiting spiritually


### Through the Scriptures
 1TI 4:13-4:15; ; 2TI 3:16-3:17

### Through wisdom
**PRO 3:13-3:14**

### Through doctrine
**TIT 3:8**

### Through godliness and contentment
**1TI 6:6** *See also* 1TI 4:8

### Through chastening
 HEB 12:10

### Through spiritual gifts
 1CO 12:7

### Through service
 MAT 25:14-25:30; ; LUK 19:11-19:27

### Through sacrifice
 MAT 19:29 parallels MRK 10:29-10:30, LUK 18:29-18:30; ; HEB 11:35

### Through death
**PHP 1:21** *See also* PHP 1:23

### Doubts expressed about profiting spiritually
 JOB 9:29-9:31, JOB 21:15, JOB 34:9, JOB 35:1-35:3; ; PSA 73:13; ; ECC 2:15, ECC 6:8; ; MAL 3:14

### It is better than worldly wealth
**MAT 16:26** parallels MRK 8:36, LUK 9:25

## Unprofitable things
 *See also* ECC 6:11 *mere words*; ECC 10:11 *delayed action*; ISA 44:10 *idolatry*; PHP 3:7 *legalistic works*; 1CO 13:1-13:3 *zeal without love*; 1CO 14:6 *speech without understanding*; HEB 4:2 *hearing without faith*; TIT 3:9 *needless controversy*
