# 5799: bitterness

A feeling of anger and resentment, caused particularly by perceived unfairness in suffering or by adverse circumstances.



## The causes of bitterness


### Adverse personal circumstances
**JOB 10:1** *See also* GEN 27:34; RUT 1:20 *“Mara” means “bitter”*; 1SA 30:6; 2SA 13:36; 2KI 4:27; PSA 55:4-55:14; PRO 17:25; JER 6:26

### External circumstances
**EZK 3:14** *Because of the exiles' hardness of heart.* *See also* JOB 21:25, JOB 27:2; LAM 3:1-3:20; EZK 21:6

### Human failure
**MAT 26:75** parallel LUK 22:61-22:62 *See also* EZR 10:1; JER 2:19, JER 4:18; LAM 1:4

**ACT 8:23** *See also* ROM 3:10-3:18
