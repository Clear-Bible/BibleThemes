# 5784: amazement

Astonishment and surprise are frequent responses to the actions and words of God and Jesus Christ.



## Amazement at God's actions


### His marvellous deeds
**PSA 98:1** *See also* JOS 3:5 *The LORD promises to do amazing things when his people cross the Jordan*; JDG 13:19; 1CH 16:24; PSA 71:17, PSA 72:18, PSA 86:10, PSA 96:3, PSA 118:23; ISA 25:1; DAN 3:24 *Nebuchadnezzar is amazed at seeing four men in the furnace*; REV 15:1, REV 15:3

### His creation
 JOB 37:5

### His judgment
 ISA 29:9; ; HAB 1:5; ; JHN 5:28

## Throughout his life Jesus Christ inspired amazement
 MAT 27:14 parallel MRK 15:5; ; LUK 2:33, LUK 2:47, LUK 4:22

## Amazement at Jesus Christ's teaching


### At his inherent authority
**MAT 7:28-7:29** *See also* MAT 13:54 parallel MRK 6:2; MRK 1:22 parallel LUK 4:32; MRK 1:27 parallel LUK 4:36; JHN 7:15

### In debate with the Pharisees
 MAT 22:22 parallels MRK 12:17, LUK 20:26

### In theological debate with the Sadducees
 MAT 22:33

### After talking with the rich young man
 MAT 19:25 parallel MRK 10:26; ; MRK 10:24

### After the cleansing of the temple
**MRK 11:18** parallel LUK 19:47 *Here the crowds' amazement causes the chief priests and teachers of the law to fear Jesus Christ.*

## Amazement at Jesus Christ's revelation of himself
 *See also* LUK 24:41; JHN 5:20; 2TH 1:10

## Amazement at Jesus Christ's miracles


### Miracles of healing
**MAT 15:31** *See also* MAT 9:8 parallels MRK 2:12, LUK 5:26 *at the healing of the paralytic*; MRK 5:42 parallel LUK 8:56 *at the healing of Jairus' daughter*; ACT 3:10-3:11 *at the healing of the crippled beggar*

### The casting out of demons
**MRK 1:27** MAT 9:33; ; LUK 11:14; 
MRK 5:20 *Legion*; ; LUK 9:43 *a boy with an evil spirit*

### Miracles of nature
**MAT 8:27** parallel LUK 8:25 *See also* MAT 21:20 *the withered fig-tree*; MRK 6:51 *Jesus Christ walking on the water*; LUK 5:9 *the miraculous haul of fish*

## Amazement at the coming of the Holy Spirit and subsequent events
**ACT 2:12** *See also* ACT 2:7, ACT 8:13 *Simon's amazement at the miracles performed by Philip*; ACT 9:21 *amazement at the change in Saul*; ACT 10:45 *astonishment at the Holy Spirit being poured out on the Gentiles*; ACT 12:16 *amazement at Peter's release from prison*; ACT 13:12
