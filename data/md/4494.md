# 4494: papyrus

A large plant growing in marshland, sometimes to a height of six metres.

## Papyrus was used for making boats
 [EXO 2:3](bibleref:02002003); [JOB 9:26](bibleref:18009026); [ISA 18:2](bibleref:23018002)

## Papyrus indicates an ample water supply
 [JOB 8:11-8:13](bibleref:18008011-18008013); [ISA 35:7](bibleref:23035007) *Papyrus growing in what was once desert depicts the blessing coming on God's redeemed people.*
