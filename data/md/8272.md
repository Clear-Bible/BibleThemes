# 8272: holiness, believers' growth in

Believers are enabled to grow in holiness on account of the sacrificial death of Jesus Christ, foreshadowed by the OT sacrificial system, and through the sanctifying work of the Holy Spirit.



## Holiness begins with God's initiative


### God chooses who and what is to be holy
**2CH 7:16** parallel 1KI 9:3 *See also* EXO 20:11; NUM 16:7; 2CH 29:11; ZEC 2:12

### God chooses and calls his people to holiness
**DEU 7:6**; **EPH 1:4** *See also* DEU 14:2; ROM 1:7; COL 3:12; 1PE 1:2, 1PE 1:15

## Holiness is conferred by the holy God


### Holiness is conferred by the presence of God
**EXO 29:42-29:43** *See also* EXO 3:4-3:5, EXO 19:23; 2CH 7:1-7:2

### Holiness is conferred through covenant relationship with God
**EXO 19:5-19:6** *See also* DEU 28:9; EZK 37:26-37:28; 1PE 2:9

### Holiness is conferred by the sovereign action of God
**1TH 5:23** *See also* LEV 20:8; ISA 4:3-4:4; EZK 36:25; ZEP 1:7; ACT 15:9; HEB 2:11

## Holiness through the OT rituals


### Cleansing from what is unclean
**NUM 8:6-8:7** *See also* EXO 19:14; NUM 19:9; NEH 12:30

### Purification and atonement through sacrifice
**NUM 8:12-8:14** *The OT sacrificial system and holiness laws foreshadow the perfect sacrifice of Jesus Christ that enables believers to grow in holiness through faith.* *See also* EXO 29:35-29:37; LEV 8:14-8:15, LEV 16:5-16:10, LEV 16:15-16:22, LEV 16:29-16:30

### Consecration by anointing
**LEV 8:10-8:12** *See also* EXO 29:21, EXO 40:9

## Holiness through Jesus Christ


### Through the sacrifice of Jesus Christ
**HEB 10:10** *See also* EPH 5:25-5:27; COL 1:22; HEB 1:3, HEB 9:13-9:14, HEB 9:23-9:28, HEB 10:14, HEB 10:19-10:22, HEB 13:12; 1JN 1:7, 1JN 2:2, 1JN 4:10

### Through relationship with Jesus Christ
**1CO 1:2** *See also* 1CO 1:30

## Holiness through the sanctifying work of the Holy Spirit
**2TH 2:13** *See also* JHN 3:5-3:8; ROM 15:16; 1CO 6:11; 1TH 4:7-4:8; TIT 3:5; 1PE 1:2

## The human response to holiness


### Repentance
**1JN 1:9** *See also* EZR 9:1-9:7, EZR 10:1-10:4; PSA 51:1-51:10; ACT 2:38; ROM 6:11-6:13; JAS 4:8

### Faith
**GAL 5:5** *See also* ROM 1:17-1:18; 2TH 2:13

### Obedience
**1PE 1:22** *See also* PSA 119:9; JHN 17:17; ROM 6:16-6:19
