# 5043: names, significance of

Labels that idenitfy individuals. They may also denote aspects of character or reputation.



## A name may reflect an individual's nature
**[1SA 25:25](bibleref:09025025)** *See also* [GEN 17:5](bibleref:01017005) *“Abraham” means “father of many”* ; [GEN 32:27-32:29](bibleref:01032027-01032029) *“Israel” means “he struggles with God”* ; [EXO 34:14](bibleref:02034014); [MRK 5:9](bibleref:41005009) parallel LUK 8:30

### Additional names or nicknames
**[MRK 3:17](bibleref:41003017)** *In NT times an additional name or surname reflecting someone's character was sometimes appended to the given name.*  *See also* [LUK 6:15](bibleref:42006015); [JHN 1:42](bibleref:43001042) *“Cephas” and “Peter” mean “rock” in Aramaic and Greek respectively* ; [ACT 4:36](bibleref:44004036)

## A name may indicate an individual's reputation and standing in the community


### A good name indicates a good reputation
**[PRO 22:1](bibleref:20022001)** *See also* [PRO 3:4](bibleref:20003004), [PRO 10:7](bibleref:20010007); [ECC 7:1](bibleref:21007001); [SNG 1:3](bibleref:22001003)

### A bad name indicates a bad reputation
**[NEH 6:13](bibleref:16006013)** *See also* [DEU 22:13-22:19](bibleref:05022013-05022019); [PRO 25:9-25:10](bibleref:20025009-20025010)

**[RUT 4:9-4:10](bibleref:08004009-08004010)** *See also* [GEN 48:15-48:16](bibleref:01048015-01048016); [NUM 27:1-27:4](bibleref:04027001-04027004); [DEU 25:7-25:9](bibleref:05025007-05025009); [2SA 18:18](bibleref:10018018); [JOB 18:17-18:19](bibleref:18018017-18018019)

### Preservation of a name indicates divine blessing
**[ISA 56:4-56:5](bibleref:23056004-23056005)** *See also* [GEN 12:1-12:3](bibleref:01012001-01012003); [2SA 7:9](bibleref:10007009) parallel 1CH 17:8; [1KI 1:47](bibleref:11001047); [ISA 66:22](bibleref:23066022)
