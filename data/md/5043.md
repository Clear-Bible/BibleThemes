# 5043: names, significance of

Labels that idenitfy individuals. They may also denote aspects of character or reputation.



## A name may reflect an individual's nature
**1SA 25:25** *See also* GEN 17:5 *“Abraham” means “father of many”*; GEN 32:27-32:29 *“Israel” means “he struggles with God”*; EXO 34:14; MRK 5:9 parallel LUK 8:30

### Additional names or nicknames
**MRK 3:17** *In NT times an additional name or surname reflecting someone's character was sometimes appended to the given name.* *See also* LUK 6:15; JHN 1:42 *“Cephas” and “Peter” mean “rock” in Aramaic and Greek respectively*; ACT 4:36

## A name may indicate an individual's reputation and standing in the community


### A good name indicates a good reputation
**PRO 22:1** *See also* PRO 3:4, PRO 10:7; ECC 7:1; SNG 1:3

### A bad name indicates a bad reputation
**NEH 6:13** *See also* DEU 22:13-22:19; PRO 25:9-25:10

## Survival of one's name is important
**RUT 4:9-4:10** *See also* GEN 48:15-48:16; NUM 27:1-27:4; DEU 25:7-25:9; 2SA 18:18; JOB 18:17-18:19

### Preservation of a name indicates divine blessing
**ISA 56:4-56:5** *See also* GEN 12:1-12:3; 2SA 7:9 parallel 1CH 17:8; 1KI 1:47; ISA 66:22

## Obliteration of a name is associated with death
**JER 11:19** *See also* DEU 7:24, DEU 9:14; 2SA 14:7; 2KI 14:27

## Obliteration of a name is often linked with divine judgment
**PSA 9:5-9:6** *See also* DEU 29:20; PSA 109:13; ISA 14:22-14:23, ISA 48:18-48:19; NAM 1:14

## Bearing someone's name indicates ownership or identification
**REV 22:3-22:4** *See also* EXO 23:21; 2CH 7:14; DEU 28:10; ISA 43:6-43:7; JER 14:9; DAN 9:18-9:19; REV 3:12, REV 14:1, REV 22:4

## God knows his people by name
**ISA 43:1** *See also* EXO 33:12, EXO 33:17; ISA 45:3, ISA 49:1; JHN 10:3
