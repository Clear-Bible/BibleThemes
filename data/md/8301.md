# 8301: love, and enemies

God loves even those who oppose him and believers must follow his example in loving their enemies.



## God's love for sinners
**ISA 53:6**; **JHN 3:16**; **2PE 3:9** *See also* GEN 18:32; LAM 3:33; EZK 18:23; MAT 5:45; ROM 5:8, ROM 8:32; 2CO 5:19; 1JN 4:9-4:10

## The example of Jesus Christ
**MAT 23:37** parallel LUK 13:34**1PE 3:18** *See also* ISA 53:5; MAT 20:28 parallel MRK 10:45; LUK 23:34; ROM 5:6; 2CO 5:14, 2CO 8:9; HEB 13:12; 1PE 2:21, 1PE 2:24; 1JN 2:2

## God's people must love their enemies
**LEV 19:18**; **LUK 6:35-6:36** *See also* EXO 23:4; PRO 24:17, PRO 25:21; MAT 5:44; LUK 6:27; COL 3:13; 1TH 5:15; 2TI 2:25; 1PE 3:9
