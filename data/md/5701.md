# 5701: heir

Property and power in biblical times normally descended from father to son. Jesus Christ is therefore called God's heir, and believers are said to inherit in him.

## The heir in ancient Israel


### The heir of property
**[GEN 15:3-15:4](bibleref:01015003-01015004)** *See also* [PRO 13:22](bibleref:20013022), [PRO 19:14](bibleref:20019014)

### The heir of power
**[1KI 2:12](bibleref:11002012)** parallel 1CH 29:28 *See also* [1KI 11:43](bibleref:11011043), [1KI 14:31](bibleref:11014031), [1KI 15:3](bibleref:11015003)

### Lack of an heir regarded as a very serious matter
 [DEU 25:5-25:6](bibleref:05025005-05025006); [JDG 21:17](bibleref:07021017); [2SA 14:4-14:7](bibleref:10014004-10014007) *The woman of Tekoa was interceding indirectly for Absalom, David's heir, who had been banished.* 

### Daughters and other relatives could be heirs if there were no sons
 [NUM 27:1-27:11](bibleref:04027001-04027011)

### Property could be inherited before death
 [LUK 15:11-15:12](bibleref:42015011-42015012) *The prodigal son's request of inheritance before death could perhaps be regarded as an insult, but graciously the father complied out of love.* 

### An heir must not give up his inheritance
**[HEB 12:16](bibleref:58012016)** *See also* [GEN 25:29-25:34](bibleref:01025029-01025034); [NUM 36:6-36:7](bibleref:04036006-04036007); [1KI 21:3](bibleref:11021003); [JER 49:1](bibleref:24049001)

### The firstborn son was heir to a double share
 [DEU 21:17](bibleref:05021017)
