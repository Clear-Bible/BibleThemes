# 5369: kingship, divine

God is sovereign over all the earth. Jesus Christ is the true King of Israel and David's greater son.



## The kingship of God


### God is sovereign over all the earth
**1CH 29:11-29:12** *See also* EXO 15:18; 1CH 16:31; PSA 9:7-9:8, PSA 45:6, PSA 103:19; ISA 37:16

### God is called king
**PSA 29:10, PSA 47:6-47:7** *See also* 1SA 12:12; PSA 47:2, PSA 95:3; ISA 6:5, ISA 44:6; 1TI 1:17, 1TI 6:15; REV 15:3

### God rules as king
**PSA 96:10** *See also* PSA 47:8, PSA 93:1, PSA 97:1, PSA 99:1

### The kingdom of God
**MRK 1:14-1:15** *See also* PSA 145:11-145:13; MAT 4:17, MAT 4:23, MAT 11:12; LUK 4:43, LUK 11:20, LUK 16:16; 1CO 15:24; REV 11:15

## The kingship of Jesus Christ


### Jesus Christ is the true king of Israel and of the world
**JHN 1:49** *See also* MAT 21:5; ZEC 9:9; MRK 15:2 parallels MAT 27:11, LUK 23:3; JHN 18:36-18:37; ACT 17:7; HEB 1:8; PSA 45:6; REV 17:14, REV 19:16

### Jesus Christ is David's greater son
**LUK 1:32-1:33**; **ACT 13:36-13:37** *Jesus Christ is not only David's son but he is greater than David.* *See also* MAT 1:1; MRK 12:35-12:37 parallels MAT 22:41-22:45, LUK 20:41-20:44; LUK 1:69; ACT 2:29-2:36, ACT 13:22-13:23; ROM 1:3; 2TI 2:8; REV 5:5, REV 22:16

## The kingship of believers
**2TI 2:12** *See also* EPH 2:6; 1PE 2:9; REV 2:26-2:27, REV 3:21, REV 5:10
