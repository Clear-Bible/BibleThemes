# 7515: anti-semitism

A modern term, used to refer to a hatred of Jews or Arabs. Traces of anti-semitism may be discerned in biblical times.

## The development of anti-semitism
**[EXO 1:8-1:14](bibleref:02001008-02001014)** *Strictly speaking, this hostility towards the Israelites in Egypt after the death of Joseph was not anti-semitism as this word is now understood.*  *See also* [EXO 1:15-1:16](bibleref:02001015-02001016), [EXO 1:22](bibleref:02001022), [EXO 3:9](bibleref:02003009), [EXO 5:6-5:9](bibleref:02005006-02005009)
