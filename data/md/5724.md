# 5724: offspring

Literally, physical descendants, and figuratively, the recipients of a spiritual inheritance.



## Physical offspring


### Offspring as physical descendants
**GEN 12:7** *See also* GEN 13:15-13:16; NUM 18:19; RUT 4:12; ISA 61:9

### Offspring as a blessing to parents
**PSA 127:3-127:5** *See also* GEN 21:6-21:7; DEU 7:14; LUK 1:41-1:45

### Offspring as a blessing to others
**GEN 28:14** *See also* GEN 22:17-22:18; 2SA 7:12-7:13 parallel 1CH 17:11-17:12; LUK 1:76-1:79

### Absence of offspring seen as a sadness or curse
**GEN 15:2-15:3** *See also* LEV 20:20; PSA 109:9; JER 22:30

### Steps to be taken to provide offspring under the law
**DEU 25:5-25:6** *See also* RUT 4:10, RUT 4:13; MAT 22:24 parallels MRK 12:19, LUK 20:28

### Offspring as a result of God's special intervention
**HEB 11:11** *See also* GEN 21:1-21:2, GEN 25:21; JDG 13:2-13:3; LUK 1:11-1:13; ROM 4:18-4:21

### God's claim on the firstborn offspring
**EXO 13:1-13:2** *See also* EXO 13:11-13:16, EXO 34:19-34:20; NUM 3:11-3:13, NUM 8:15-8:18; LUK 2:22-2:24

### Offspring can inherit parents' blessing or punishment
**EXO 20:5-20:6** parallel DEU 5:9-5:10 *See also* LEV 26:39; JER 22:28-22:30; HOS 9:15-9:17

### The fate of the offspring of the wicked
**JOB 27:13-27:14** *See also* PSA 37:28; ISA 14:20-14:22
