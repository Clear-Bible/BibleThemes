# 5780: advisers

Those who give advice and counsel. Human advice is desirable, but not always reliable. Only God's counsel is wholly dependable.

## Human advisers


### It is wise to consult advisers
**[PRO 15:22](bibleref:20015022)** *See also* [PRO 12:15](bibleref:20012015), [PRO 13:10](bibleref:20013010), [PRO 15:12](bibleref:20015012), [PRO 19:20](bibleref:20019020), [PRO 20:18](bibleref:20020018), [PRO 24:6](bibleref:20024006); [ECC 4:13](bibleref:21004013)

### It is folly to reject wise advisers
**[PRO 11:14](bibleref:20011014)** *See also* [1KI 12:8](bibleref:11012008) parallel 2CH 10:8; [1KI 12:13-12:15](bibleref:11012013-11012015) parallel 2CH 10:13-10:15; [PRO 1:25-1:26](bibleref:20001025-20001026), [PRO 1:30-1:31](bibleref:20001030-20001031); [JER 18:18](bibleref:24018018); [ACT 27:11](bibleref:44027011), [ACT 27:21](bibleref:44027021)

### Unwise advisers are dangerous
**[PSA 1:1](bibleref:19001001)** *See also* [JOB 21:16](bibleref:18021016), [JOB 42:7](bibleref:18042007); [PRO 12:5](bibleref:20012005); [ISA 19:11](bibleref:23019011); [EZK 11:2-11:4](bibleref:26011002-26011004)

### Advisers who trust in false gods must be rejected
**[ISA 8:19-8:20](bibleref:23008019-23008020)** *See also* [2CH 25:15-25:16](bibleref:14025015-14025016); [ISA 19:3](bibleref:23019003), [ISA 47:13-47:15](bibleref:23047013-23047015)
