# 5780: advisers

Those who give advice and counsel. Human advice is desirable, but not always reliable. Only God's counsel is wholly dependable.



## Human advisers


### It is wise to consult advisers
**PRO 15:22** *See also* PRO 12:15, PRO 13:10, PRO 15:12, PRO 19:20, PRO 20:18, PRO 24:6; ECC 4:13

### It is folly to reject wise advisers
**PRO 11:14** *See also* 1KI 12:8 parallel 2CH 10:8; 1KI 12:13-12:15 parallel 2CH 10:13-10:15; PRO 1:25-1:26, PRO 1:30-1:31; JER 18:18; ACT 27:11, ACT 27:21

### Unwise advisers are dangerous
**PSA 1:1** *See also* JOB 21:16, JOB 42:7; PRO 12:5; ISA 19:11; EZK 11:2-11:4

### Advisers who trust in false gods must be rejected
**ISA 8:19-8:20** *See also* 2CH 25:15-25:16; ISA 19:3, ISA 47:13-47:15

## Examples of wise advisers
 EXO 18:17-18:24 *Jethro*; DAN 2:23, DAN 2:48, DAN 4:18, DAN 4:27, DAN 5:11-5:12; 
ACT 5:38-5:39 *Gamaliel*; ; 2CO 8:10-8:12 *Paul*

## Examples of bad advisers
 NUM 31:16 *Balaam*; 2SA 15:31, 2SA 17:1, 2SA 17:7; 
1KI 12:28-12:30 *Jeroboam*; ; ISA 19:11 *Egyptian advisers*; ; JHN 11:49-11:53 *Caiaphas*

## Examples of official advisers
 GEN 26:26; ; 2SA 15:12, 2SA 16:23; ; 1KI 4:5; ; 1CH 27:32-27:33

## God as adviser


### God is an adviser
**PSA 16:7** *See also* 1KI 22:5 parallel 2CH 18:4; PSA 32:8, PSA 73:24; REV 3:18

### The promised Messiah is an adviser
**ISA 9:6** *See also* ISA 11:2

### The Holy Spirit is an adviser
**JHN 14:26** *See also* JHN 14:16, JHN 15:26, JHN 16:13 *The Holy Spirit's counsel defends believers from every accusation and provides all necessary teaching and guidance.*
