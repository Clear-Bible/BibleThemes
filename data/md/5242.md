# 5242: buying and selling

Buying and selling are an integral part of the everyday life of the world. Scripture makes it clear that God expects moral dealings by his people in this area of life as in every other.



## Some biblical transactions


### The first recorded transaction
**GEN 23:3-23:4** *Many other transactions had obviously taken place before this, from the earliest times.*

### Transactions in land
 *See also* GEN 47:20-47:22; RUT 4:3; PRO 31:16; JER 32:9-32:10; MAT 27:7; LUK 14:18; ACT 4:34

### Buying animals
 LUK 14:19

### Buying food and drink
 GEN 41:57; ; DEU 2:28; ; JHN 6:5, JHN 13:29; ; REV 6:6

### Transactions in other goods
 REV 18:11-18:13

### Buying of slaves
 GEN 17:12; ; EXO 21:2; ; LEV 25:44-25:45; ; HOS 3:1-3:2

### The labour market
 LEV 25:39-25:40; ; MAT 20:1-20:2

## God's standards in buying and selling


### Fairness
 GEN 23:5-23:13; ; 2SA 24:24

### Honesty
**LEV 19:35-19:36** *See also* GEN 23:14-23:16; DEU 25:13-25:16; PRO 11:1, PRO 16:11, PRO 20:10, PRO 20:23

## Sins to avoid in buying and selling


### Dishonesty
 PRO 20:14; ; AMO 8:5; ; MIC 6:10-6:11

### Greed
 PRO 11:26

### Exploitation
**LEV 25:14** *See also* LAM 5:4; AMO 8:6

### Extravagance
 LUK 15:13

### Desecration of the Sabbath
 NEH 10:31, NEH 13:15-13:22; ; AMO 8:5

### Desecration of the temple
**MAT 21:12-21:13** parallels MRK 11:15-11:17, LUK 19:45-19:46 *The merchants were operating within the outer court of the temple, itself consecrated as a place of prayer and worship.* *See also* JHN 2:14-2:16

### Worldliness
 EZK 7:12; ; 1CO 7:29-7:31; ; JAS 4:13

## Oppression through the control of buying and selling
**REV 13:16-13:17**

### Sinners sold to sin
 *See also* 1KI 21:20; ROM 7:14
