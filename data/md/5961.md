# 5961: superiority

A sense of being of greater importance or value in comparison with others, which can lead to arrogance and boasting.



## Superiority is a characteristic of the wicked person


### It is the product of human pride
**PRO 21:24**; **3JN 1:9** *See also* 2CH 26:16; PSA 73:3-73:9; ISA 3:16, ISA 9:9-9:10; ROM 1:30; 2TI 3:4; 2PE 2:10

### It involves looking down on others
**1SA 17:42**; **LUK 18:9-18:14** *See also* GEN 16:4-16:5; 1SA 10:27; 2SA 6:16 parallel 1CH 15:29; PSA 31:18, PSA 101:5, PSA 123:3-123:4; PRO 15:20, PRO 23:22; AMO 5:10

### It gives rise to boasting
**EST 5:11**; **ACT 8:9** *See also* 1KI 20:11; ISA 16:6; EZK 28:2; DAN 7:8; HOS 12:8; MAT 26:33-26:35; JAS 3:5; JUD 1:16

## God hates superior behaviour and will judge it
**ISA 2:11-2:12**; **1PE 5:5** *See also* 1SA 2:3; 2SA 22:28; JOB 20:6-20:7; PRO 3:11, PRO 16:5, PRO 16:18, PRO 21:4; ISA 2:17, ISA 10:12, ISA 13:11, ISA 13:19, ISA 23:9; JER 9:23-9:24; OBA 1:12-1:13; MAT 18:10; ROM 11:17-11:18, ROM 14:3, ROM 14:10; 1CO 1:20-1:25; 1TI 3:6, 1TI 6:17; REV 18:7-18:8

## The attitude of the godly is not superior


### They demonstrate a proper humility
**PSA 131:1**; **PHP 2:3** *See also* ROM 12:16; 1CO 4:6-4:7; PHP 2:4-2:8

### Jesus Christ's advice to those who aspire to greatness
**MAT 20:20-20:28** parallel MRK 10:35-10:45 *See also* JHN 13:12-13:17
