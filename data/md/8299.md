# 8299: love, in relationships

Human love is ennobled by being patterned on God's love for his people. It is also safeguarded by God's commands.



## The love between husband and wife


### Conjugal love is commanded
**COL 3:18-3:19** *See also* GEN 2:24; DEU 24:5; PRO 5:18-5:20; ECC 9:9; EPH 5:22, EPH 5:28, EPH 5:33; 1PE 3:7

### It is patterned on God's love for his people
**ISA 54:5**; **EPH 5:25-5:27** *See also* ISA 62:5; JER 3:14; EZK 16:8; HOS 2:19; 2CO 11:2; REV 19:7

## The power of human love
**SNG 8:6-8:7** *See also* GEN 29:20, GEN 29:30; PRO 6:34-6:35

## Examples of love in courtship and marriage
 GEN 24:67 *Isaac and Rebekah*; ; GEN 29:18 *Jacob and Rachel*; ; JDG 16:4 *Samson and Delilah*; ; 1SA 1:5 *Elkanah and Hannah*; ; EST 2:17 *Xerxes and Esther*; ; SNG 1:2, SNG 4:10; ; HOS 3:1; ; MAT 1:19 *Joseph and Mary*

## Safeguards on human love


### Sexual immorality is condemned
**1CO 7:2** *See also* LEV 18:22 *homosexual acts forbidden*; LEV 19:29 *prostitution forbidden*; DEU 23:17-23:18; MAT 5:32; ACT 15:29; ROM 1:26-1:27; 1CO 5:1, 1CO 6:18, 1CO 10:8; EPH 5:3; COL 3:5; 1TH 4:3

### Adultery is condemned
**EXO 20:14** *See also* LEV 20:10; DEU 5:18; PRO 6:24; 1CO 6:9; HEB 13:4

### Restrictions on divorce
**MAT 19:9** *See also* MAL 2:16; MAT 5:32; MRK 10:11-10:12; LUK 16:18; 1CO 7:10-7:11

### Lust is condemned
**MAT 5:28** *See also* JOB 31:1; PRO 6:25; 1CO 7:9; EPH 4:19; 1TH 4:5

### Polygamy is forbidden
**1TI 3:2** *See also* DEU 17:17; MAL 2:15; 1TI 3:12; TIT 1:6
