# 8769: idolatry, in OT

Scripture provides illustrations of idolatry from various periods in the history of the people of God. Idolatry is seen as a constant temptation for believers, especially in times of national or personal stress.



## Idolatry among the Gentiles
 JDG 11:24, JDG 16:23-16:24; ; 2KI 5:18; ; ISA 36:18-36:20, ISA 37:38, ISA 46:1; ; EZK 8:14; ; ACT 14:11-14:13; ; 1CO 8:5

## Idolatry among God's people


### In patriarchal times
**JOS 24:2** *See also* GEN 31:30, GEN 31:34 *these household gods may have been either masks or statuettes*; GEN 35:2

### In the Mosaic period
**EXO 32:4**

### In the period of the judges
**JDG 17:5** *See also* JDG 10:6

### In the early monarchy
**1KI 11:10** *See also* 1KI 12:28 *The golden calves were perhaps pedestals on which the LORD was thought to sit.*

### In the middle monarchy
**1KI 16:33** *An Asherah pole was probably a crude carved wooden image of a Canaanite fertility goddess.* *See also* 1KI 11:7-11:8, 1KI 16:32

### In the late monarchy
**2KI 21:2-21:6**

### After the fall of Jerusalem
**EZK 8:10** *See also* EZK 8:3, EZK 8:14, EZK 8:16

## Objects of false worship


### The sun, moon and stars
**DEU 4:19** *See also* DEU 17:3; JOB 31:26

### Other objects of worship
**DEU 4:28** *See also* DEU 16:22 *sacred stones*; 1KI 12:31 *high places*; ISA 1:29 *oaks*

## Practices associated with idolatry


### The burning of children
**2KI 23:10**

### The superstitious use of religious symbols
**2KI 18:4** *See also* JDG 8:27 *The ephod was a priestly garment sometimes subject to excessive veneration.*

### Sexual deviance
**DEU 23:17** *See also* 1KI 14:24; HOS 4:14
