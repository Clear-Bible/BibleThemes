# 8769: idolatry, in OT

Scripture provides illustrations of idolatry from various periods in the history of the people of God. Idolatry is seen as a constant temptation for believers, especially in times of national or personal stress.



## Idolatry among the Gentiles
 [JDG 11:24](bibleref:07011024), [JDG 16:23-16:24](bibleref:07016023-07016024); [2KI 5:18](bibleref:12005018); [ISA 36:18-36:20](bibleref:23036018-23036020), [ISA 37:38](bibleref:23037038), [ISA 46:1](bibleref:23046001); [EZK 8:14](bibleref:26008014); [ACT 14:11-14:13](bibleref:44014011-44014013); [1CO 8:5](bibleref:46008005)
