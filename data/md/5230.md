# 5230: beggars

People in great need who appeal for help, both from others and from God.



## Appeals for material help
**2KI 6:26** *During a time of famine*; **LUK 16:3** *See also* 2KI 8:3-8:5; JOB 19:16, JOB 24:4-24:5, JOB 29:11-29:12; PSA 37:25 *never having to beg seen as God's blessing*; PSA 109:10 *begging seen as God's judgment*; LAM 4:4

## Appeals for mercy from others
**EST 8:3** *See also* 1SA 15:25; 2SA 14:4; 2KI 1:13; EST 4:8, EST 7:7; JOB 30:28; MAT 18:26-18:32

## Examples of beggars
 MRK 10:46 parallel LUK 18:35 *blind Bartimaeus*; ; LUK 16:20-16:21 *Lazarus*; ; JHN 9:8 *the man born blind*; ; ACT 3:2-3:3 *the crippled man at the temple gate*

## Care for the destitute commended
**ISA 58:6-58:7**; **MAT 26:11** parallels MRK 14:7, JHN 12:8 *Jesus Christ implies that we should care for them.* *See also* DEU 15:11; PRO 19:17 *kindness to the poor rewarded*; PRO 21:13, PRO 22:9, PRO 28:27, PRO 31:9, PRO 31:20 *the behaviour of the wife of noble character*; ZEC 7:10; MAT 5:42 parallel LUK 6:30; MAT 19:21 parallels MRK 10:21, LUK 18:22 *Jesus Christ and the rich young man*; LUK 12:33, LUK 14:13

## Pleas for God's help


### From the Israelites
**EXO 2:23** *In the OT there are many examples of the Israelites begging God for his help in times of dire need.* *See also* JOS 24:7; JDG 4:3, JDG 6:6; 2CH 14:11; AMO 7:5

### From individuals
**PSA 18:6** *See also* 2SA 24:10 parallel 1CH 21:8; PSA 28:2; JON 2:2; HAB 1:2; LUK 16:27

## People begged Jesus Christ for help
**MAT 14:35-14:36** parallel MRK 6:56 *See also* MAT 8:31 parallels MRK 5:12, LUK 8:32; MAT 15:22 parallel MRK 7:26; MRK 7:32, MRK 10:47; LUK 5:12 parallel MRK 1:40; LUK 8:28, LUK 9:38-9:40; JHN 4:47
