# 5230: beggars

**[2KI 6:26](bibleref:12006026)** *During a time of famine* ; **[LUK 16:3](bibleref:42016003)** *See also* [2KI 8:3-8:5](bibleref:12008003-12008005); [JOB 19:16](bibleref:18019016), [JOB 24:4-24:5](bibleref:18024004-18024005), [JOB 29:11-29:12](bibleref:18029011-18029012); [PSA 37:25](bibleref:19037025) *never having to beg seen as God's blessing* ; [PSA 109:10](bibleref:19109010) *begging seen as God's judgment* ; [LAM 4:4](bibleref:25004004)
