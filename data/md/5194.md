# 5194: touch

God prohibits physical contact in certain circumstances and warns of serious consequences of violating the ritual law in this respect. Great sigificance is attached to the touch of God, Jesus Christ and angels and the disciples' touching of the risen Christ.

## God's prohibits touch in certain circumstances


### At Mount Sinai
**[EXO 19:12-19:13](bibleref:02019012-02019013)** *See also* [HEB 12:18-12:24](bibleref:58012018-58012024)

### With the furnishings and articles of the tabernacle
**[NUM 4:15](bibleref:04004015)** *See also* [2SA 6:6-6:7](bibleref:10006006-10006007)

## The law concerning touching certain articles


### Anyone or anything touching what is ritually unclean also becomes unclean
**[NUM 19:21-19:22](bibleref:04019021-04019022)** *See also* [LEV 5:2-5:3](bibleref:03005002-03005003), [LEV 7:19-7:21](bibleref:03007019-03007021), [LEV 11:8](bibleref:03011008), [LEV 11:24-11:40](bibleref:03011024-03011040), [LEV 12:4](bibleref:03012004), [LEV 15:5-15:12](bibleref:03015005-03015012), [LEV 15:19-15:27](bibleref:03015019-03015027), [LEV 22:4-22:6](bibleref:03022004-03022006); [NUM 19:11-19:16](bibleref:04019011-04019016) *the law concerning dead bodies* ; [DEU 14:8](bibleref:05014008) *Pigs are unclean* ; [ISA 52:11](bibleref:23052011); [HAG 2:10-2:14](bibleref:37002010-37002014)

### Touching what is purified confers consecration
 [EXO 29:37](bibleref:02029037), [EXO 30:29](bibleref:02030029); [LEV 6:18](bibleref:03006018), [LEV 6:27](bibleref:03006027)

## The touch of Jesus Christ


### To heal
**[MAT 8:3](bibleref:40008003)** parallels MRK 1:41-1:42, LUK 5:13 *See also* [MAT 9:29-9:30](bibleref:40009029-40009030), [MAT 20:34](bibleref:40020034); [MRK 7:33](bibleref:41007033), [MRK 8:22-8:25](bibleref:41008022-41008025); [LUK 22:51](bibleref:42022051)

### To raise the dead
**[MAT 9:18-9:25](bibleref:40009018-40009025)** parallels MRK 5:22-5:42, LUK 8:41-8:55 *In touching the dead, Jesus Christ shows his willingness to become unclean so that others can be healed.*  *See also* [LUK 7:14](bibleref:42007014)

### To bring reassurance and show acceptance
 [MAT 17:6-17:7](bibleref:40017006-40017007); [MRK 10:13](bibleref:41010013) parallels MAT 19:13, LUK 18:15
