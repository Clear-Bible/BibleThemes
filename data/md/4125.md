# 4125: Satan, agents of

Satan works in this world through people and through those spiritual beings who acknowledge his authority. His agents oppose God's will and purposes.



## Satan works through people


### Satan's human agents pursue his evil desires
**[JHN 8:44](bibleref:43008044)** *Jesus Christ speaking of the relationship of some unbelieving Jews to Satan.*  *See also* [ACT 13:10](bibleref:44013010); [1JN 3:8-3:10](bibleref:62003008-62003010), [1JN 3:12](bibleref:62003012)

### Satan works through people to oppose God's purposes
 [ACT 5:1-5:9](bibleref:44005001-44005009) *Ananias and Sapphira* ; [MAT 16:23](bibleref:40016023) parallel MRK 8:33 *Simon Peter* [LUK 22:3](bibleref:42022003); [JHN 6:70](bibleref:43006070), [JHN 13:2](bibleref:43013002), [JHN 13:27](bibleref:43013027)
[ACT 13:8-13:10](bibleref:44013008-44013010) *Elymas, the sorcerer*
