# 5821: criticism, amongst believers

Harsh criticism is out of place among believers, who are urged to see each other in the best possible light. Criticism of God arises from a failure to understand his actions and purposes.



## Criticism forbidden among believers
**[JAS 5:9](bibleref:59005009)** *See also* [MAT 7:1-7:5](bibleref:40007001-40007005) parallel LUK 6:37-6:42; [ROM 14:1-14:4](bibleref:45014001-45014004), [ROM 14:10-14:13](bibleref:45014010-45014013); [1CO 10:10](bibleref:46010010); [GAL 5:15](bibleref:48005015); [PHP 2:14-2:16](bibleref:50002014-50002016); [JAS 4:11-4:12](bibleref:59004011-59004012)
