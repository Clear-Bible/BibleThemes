# 6735: repentance, examples of

Scripture provides examples to illustrate the importance of repentance for individuals and communities.



## The repentance of individuals


### The call to personal repentance
**[ACT 2:38](bibleref:44002038)** *See also* [2TI 2:19](bibleref:55002019)

### Examples of individual repentance
 [NUM 22:31-22:35](bibleref:04022031-04022035) *Balaam* ; [2SA 24:10](bibleref:10024010) parallel 1CH 21:8 *David, after taking a census* ; [1KI 21:27-21:29](bibleref:11021027-11021029) *Ahab* ; [2KI 22:19](bibleref:12022019) *Josiah* ; [JOB 42:6](bibleref:18042006) *Job* ; [PSA 51:1-51:17](bibleref:19051001-19051017) *David, following his adultery with Bathsheba* ; [LUK 15:21](bibleref:42015021) *the prodigal son* ; [LUK 18:13](bibleref:42018013) *the tax collector in the temple* ; [MAT 26:75](bibleref:40026075) parallels MRK 14:72, LUK 22:61-22:62 *Peter's sorrow after denying Jesus Christ* ; [JHN 21:15-21:17](bibleref:43021015-43021017) *Peter's confession and reinstatement* ; [ACT 8:22-8:24](bibleref:44008022-44008024) *Simon the sorcerer's repentance may not have been genuine.* 

## Corporate repentance


### Examples of corporate repentance
**[JER 18:7-18:8](bibleref:24018007-24018008)**; **[MAT 3:1-3:6](bibleref:40003001-40003006)** parallels MRK 1:1-1:6, LUK 3:1-3:6 *See also* [NUM 21:7](bibleref:04021007) *Israel in the desert* ; [JDG 10:15-10:16](bibleref:07010015-07010016) *Israel in the time of the judges* ; [1SA 7:3-7:4](bibleref:09007003-09007004) *Israel, after asking for a king* ; [ISA 19:22](bibleref:23019022) *Egypt turns to God in repentance* ; [ACT 9:32-9:35](bibleref:44009032-44009035) *the people of Lydda and Sharon* 

### Leaders encouraged corporate repentance
**[EZR 10:1](bibleref:15010001)** *See also* [2KI 23:1-23:7](bibleref:12023001-12023007) *Josiah leads Judah in repentance* ; [2CH 15:8-15:15](bibleref:14015008-14015015) *Asa leads Judah in repentance* ; [2CH 30:6-30:9](bibleref:14030006-14030009) *Hezekiah leads Israel and Judah in repentance* ; [EZR 10:10-10:12](bibleref:15010010-15010012) *Following the exile, Ezra leads the Israelites in repentance* ; [JON 3:6-3:8](bibleref:32003006-32003008) *The king of Nineveh leads the people in repentance.*
