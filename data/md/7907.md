# 7907: baptism, practice of

Baptism is associated with repenting of sin, believing the gospel message and becoming a member of Christ's body.



## Baptism is ordained by Jesus Christ himself
**[MAT 28:19](bibleref:40028019)** *See also* [ACT 9:17-9:18](bibleref:44009017-44009018), [ACT 16:14-16:15](bibleref:44016014-44016015)

## Baptism is linked with repentance
**[ACT 2:38](bibleref:44002038)** *See also* [HEB 6:1-6:2](bibleref:58006001-58006002) *The “baptisms” referred to here may imply teaching about Jewish rituals or the baptism offered by John the Baptist.* 

## Baptism follows the decision to believe
**[ACT 2:41](bibleref:44002041), [ACT 18:8](bibleref:44018008)** *See also* [ACT 8:12-8:13](bibleref:44008012-44008013), [ACT 16:31-16:33](bibleref:44016031-44016033)

## Baptism in the name of God or Jesus Christ


### Baptism in or into the name of Jesus Christ
**[ACT 19:5](bibleref:44019005)** *See also* [ACT 2:38](bibleref:44002038), [ACT 8:16](bibleref:44008016), [ACT 10:48](bibleref:44010048)

### Baptism in the name of the Trinity
 [MAT 28:18-28:20](bibleref:40028018-40028020)

## Baptism was by immersion
**[ROM 6:4](bibleref:45006004)** *The word “buried” implies immersion.*  *See also* [ACT 8:38](bibleref:44008038)

## Baptism is linked with the gift of the Holy Spirit
**[MRK 1:8](bibleref:41001008)**; **[ACT 1:5](bibleref:44001005)** *See also* [ACT 2:4](bibleref:44002004), [ACT 11:16](bibleref:44011016)**[1CO 12:13](bibleref:46012013)** *All the Christians at Corinth had been spiritually baptised into Christ and thus united with him.* 

## Manifestations of the Holy Spirit may follow or precede water baptism
 *See also* [ACT 8:12-8:17](bibleref:44008012-44008017), [ACT 9:17-9:18](bibleref:44009017-44009018), [ACT 10:44-10:48](bibleref:44010044-44010048)

## The person who baptises is of little importance
**[1CO 1:14](bibleref:46001014)** *What matters is not who baptises but the Saviour in whose name it is done.* 

## Baptism ``for the dead''
**[1CO 15:29](bibleref:46015029)** *It is not certain if there was a ceremony of baptism “for the dead” in the NT church, nor whether, if it existed, Paul approved of it.* 

## Passages which may imply infant baptism
The ``households'' (not merely individuals) may well have included children:
 [ACT 16:15](bibleref:44016015), [ACT 16:33](bibleref:44016033), [ACT 18:8](bibleref:44018008); [1CO 1:16](bibleref:46001016)
[MRK 10:13-10:16](bibleref:41010013-41010016); [1CO 7:14](bibleref:46007014); [COL 2:11-2:12](bibleref:51002011-51002012) *If baptism is here equated with circumcision, this suggests baptism is the sign of the new covenant.* 

## Passages apparently negating infant baptism
 [ACT 2:38-2:41](bibleref:44002038-44002041) *infants cannot repent and believe* ; [GAL 3:7](bibleref:48003007) *Under the new covenant it is faith in Jesus Christ, not an outward sign, that makes a person a child of God.*
