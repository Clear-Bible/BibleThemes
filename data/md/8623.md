# 8623: worship, of God alone

God alone is worthy of worship; the worship of other gods is forbidden. In the NT worship is offered to the Son of God.



## God alone is to be worshipped


### He alone is worthy of worship
**1CH 16:25** *The English word “worship” means literally “worth-ship”.* *See also* PSA 48:1, PSA 96:4-96:5, PSA 145:3; 2SA 22:4

### The worship of God the Father
**JHN 4:23** *See also* PHP 2:11

### The worship of God the Son
**MAT 2:11, MAT 14:33**; **JHN 20:28** *See also* MAT 28:16-28:17; JHN 9:35-9:38; PHP 2:9-2:11; HEB 1:6; REV 5:8-5:14

## Angels worship God
**PSA 103:20, PSA 148:1-148:2** *See also* PSA 29:1-29:2; ISA 6:1-6:4; EZK 10:1-10:18; REV 4:8-4:9

## The worship of other gods forbidden
**EXO 20:3** parallel DEU 5:7**2KI 17:35-17:36** *See also* EXO 34:14; DEU 6:13-6:14; NEH 9:6; PSA 86:9-86:10, PSA 97:7; ACT 10:25-10:26, ACT 14:13-14:18

## The worship of angels forbidden
**COL 2:18** *See also* REV 19:9-19:10, REV 22:8-22:9
