# 5933: restlessness

A lack of peace, experienced physically, spiritually or mentally.



## Causes of restlessness


### Worry
**ECC 5:12**; **JER 49:23** *See also* GEN 41:8; EST 6:1; PRO 15:16; DAN 2:1, DAN 6:18-6:19

### Illness
**JOB 7:4-7:5** *See also* PSA 6:2-6:6, PSA 32:3-32:4, PSA 38:3-38:10; ISA 38:13-38:14

### Lack of purpose
**ECC 2:22-2:23** *See also* PSA 107:4-107:5

### Uncompleted business
 RUT 3:18

### Rebelliousness

### Demon-possession
