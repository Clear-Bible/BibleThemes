# 4050: dust

Human beings were created from the dust by God and will return to it at death. Dust is therefore taken as a symbol of poverty, lowliness and humility. In Scripture dust is shaken off the feet to warn of future judgment, and is also used as a means of expressing grief.

## Human beings were created from the dust by God
**[GEN 2:7](bibleref:01002007)** *See also* [GEN 18:27](bibleref:01018027); [PSA 103:14](bibleref:19103014); [1CO 15:47](bibleref:46015047)

## We will all return to dust when we die
**[GEN 3:19](bibleref:01003019)** *God's curse upon Adam affected all humanity* ; **[PSA 90:3](bibleref:19090003)** *See also* [JOB 10:9](bibleref:18010009), [JOB 34:15](bibleref:18034015); [ECC 12:7](bibleref:21012007)

## Dust as a symbol of poverty, lowliness and humility
**[1SA 2:8](bibleref:09002008)** *See also* [GEN 18:27](bibleref:01018027); [1KI 16:2](bibleref:11016002); [JOB 30:19](bibleref:18030019)

## Dust as a simile for a multitude
**[GEN 13:16](bibleref:01013016)** *See also* [GEN 28:14](bibleref:01028014); [PSA 78:27](bibleref:19078027)

## Dust is shaken off the feet as a warning of judgment
**[MRK 6:11](bibleref:41006011)** parallels MAT 10:14, LUK 9:5 *See also* [LUK 10:10-10:11](bibleref:42010010-42010011); [ACT 13:51](bibleref:44013051)
