# 4017: life, animal and plant

All living creatures and plants were made by God and are sustained by him.

### Animals were created by God
**[GEN 2:19](bibleref:01002019)**; **[PSA 104:24](bibleref:19104024)** *See also* [GEN 1:20-1:25](bibleref:01001020-01001025)
