# 3278: Holy Spirit, indwelling of

The Holy Spirit dwells within Jesus Christ and his disciples. Recognisable results in believers' lives include Christlikeness and the fruit of the Spirit.



## The indwelling of the Holy Spirit in the OT
**[GEN 41:38](bibleref:01041038)**; **[EXO 35:31](bibleref:02035031)** *See also* [NUM 27:18](bibleref:04027018); [1SA 10:6-10:7](bibleref:09010006-09010007); [ISA 59:21](bibleref:23059021); [HAG 2:5](bibleref:37002005)

## Results of the Holy Spirit's indwelling in believers


### The Holy Spirit guarantees life
**[ROM 8:11](bibleref:45008011)** *See also* [EZK 37:14](bibleref:26037014); [JHN 7:38-7:39](bibleref:43007038-43007039)

### The Holy Spirit assures believers that they belong to God
**[ROM 8:14-8:16](bibleref:45008014-45008016)** *See also* [2CO 1:21-1:22](bibleref:47001021-47001022); [GAL 4:6](bibleref:48004006); [EPH 1:13-1:14](bibleref:49001013-49001014); [1JN 4:13](bibleref:62004013)

### Wisdom and insight are received
 [GEN 41:38](bibleref:01041038); [DEU 34:9](bibleref:05034009); [DAN 4:8-4:9](bibleref:27004008-27004009), [DAN 4:18](bibleref:27004018), [DAN 5:11](bibleref:27005011), [DAN 5:14](bibleref:27005014)

### Strength is received
 [EPH 3:16](bibleref:49003016)

### Preaching and public testimony is aided
 [MAT 10:20](bibleref:40010020) parallels MRK 13:11, LUK 12:12; [ACT 4:8-4:12](bibleref:44004008-44004012), [ACT 5:29-5:32](bibleref:44005029-44005032)

### The moral law is obeyed
 [EZK 36:27](bibleref:26036027); [ROM 8:4-8:5](bibleref:45008004-45008005), [ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013); [GAL 5:16](bibleref:48005016)

### The fruit of the Spirit is displayed
**[GAL 5:22-5:23](bibleref:48005022-48005023)** *See also* [ROM 5:5](bibleref:45005005), [ROM 14:17](bibleref:45014017), [ROM 15:13](bibleref:45015013), [ROM 15:30](bibleref:45015030)
