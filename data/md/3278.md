# 3278: Holy Spirit, indwelling of

The Holy Spirit dwells within Jesus Christ and his disciples. Recognisable results in believers' lives include Christlikeness and the fruit of the Spirit.



## The indwelling of the Holy Spirit in the OT
**GEN 41:38**; **EXO 35:31** *See also* NUM 27:18; 1SA 10:6-10:7; ISA 59:21; HAG 2:5

## The Holy Spirit indwells Jesus Christ
 ISA 11:2; ; MAT 12:18; ; ISA 42:1; ; LUK 4:18; ; ISA 61:1; ; ACT 10:38

## The indwelling of the Holy Spirit is promised to believers
**JHN 14:17** *See also* EZK 36:27, EZK 37:14

## The Holy Spirit indwells believers
**2TI 1:14** 1CO 3:16, 1CO 6:19; 
EPH 2:22; ; 1JN 2:27, 1JN 3:24

## Results of the Holy Spirit's indwelling in believers


### The Holy Spirit guarantees life
**ROM 8:11** *See also* EZK 37:14; JHN 7:38-7:39

### The Holy Spirit assures believers that they belong to God
**ROM 8:14-8:16** *See also* 2CO 1:21-1:22; GAL 4:6; EPH 1:13-1:14; 1JN 4:13

### Wisdom and insight are received
 GEN 41:38; ; DEU 34:9; ; DAN 4:8-4:9, DAN 4:18, DAN 5:11, DAN 5:14

### Strength is received
 EPH 3:16

### Preaching and public testimony is aided
 MAT 10:20 parallels MRK 13:11, LUK 12:12; ; ACT 4:8-4:12, ACT 5:29-5:32

### The moral law is obeyed
 EZK 36:27; ; ROM 8:4-8:5, ROM 8:13; ; GAL 5:16

### The fruit of the Spirit is displayed
**GAL 5:22-5:23** *See also* ROM 5:5, ROM 14:17, ROM 15:13, ROM 15:30

## Those without the Holy Spirit's indwelling are not Christlike
 GAL 5:17; ; JUD 1:18-1:19
