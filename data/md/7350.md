# 7350: drink offering

A sacrificial offering of wine poured out at the foot of the altar, to accompany a burnt, fellowship or grain offering.

## The drink offering was an ancient custom
**[GEN 35:14](bibleref:01035014)**

## Drink offerings were proportionate to the size of animal sacrificed
 *See also* [NUM 15:5](bibleref:04015005), [NUM 15:7](bibleref:04015007), [NUM 15:10](bibleref:04015010)

## Drink offerings were required on feasts and sacred days
As part of the daily offerings:
 [EXO 29:41](bibleref:02029041); [NUM 28:7-28:8](bibleref:04028007-04028008)
[LEV 23:13](bibleref:03023013) *Firstfruits* [LEV 23:18](bibleref:03023018); [NUM 28:31](bibleref:04028031)[LEV 23:37](bibleref:03023037); [NUM 29:30-29:31](bibleref:04029030-04029031)
[NUM 28:24](bibleref:04028024) *Passover* ; [NUM 28:9-28:10](bibleref:04028009-04028010) *Sabbath offerings* ; [NUM 28:14](bibleref:04028014) *monthly offerings* ; [NUM 29:6](bibleref:04029006) *Feast of Trumpets* ; [NUM 29:11](bibleref:04029011) *Day of Atonement* 

## Occasions for making a drink offering


### To accompany sacrifices for unintentional sins
 [NUM 15:24](bibleref:04015024)

### To mark special occasions
 [NUM 6:17](bibleref:04006017) *after taking a Nazirite vow* ; [1CH 29:21](bibleref:13029021) *at the accession of a king* ; [2CH 29:35](bibleref:14029035) *during a religious reformation* ; [EZK 45:17](bibleref:26045017) *in Ezekiel's vision of restored worship* 

## God's displeasure when drink offerings were poured out to idols
**[JER 7:18](bibleref:24007018)** *See also* [ISA 57:6](bibleref:23057006); [JER 19:13](bibleref:24019013), [JER 32:29](bibleref:24032029), [JER 44:17-44:19](bibleref:24044017-24044019), [JER 44:25](bibleref:24044025); [EZK 20:28](bibleref:26020028)

 [JOL 2:13-2:14](bibleref:29002013-29002014)
