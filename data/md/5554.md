# 5554: status

A person's standing in society in the sight of others. The status of individuals in the sight of the world often differs radically from their status in the sight of God, but it is the latter that really matters.

## Factors contributing to high social status


### High status associated with a position of secular authority
**[DAN 2:37-2:38](bibleref:27002037-27002038)** *See also* [2SA 15:1-15:6](bibleref:10015001-10015006); [1CH 29:26-29:28](bibleref:13029026-13029028)

### High status based upon a position of domestic authority
**[MAL 1:6](bibleref:39001006)** *See also* [PSA 123:2](bibleref:19123002); [LUK 17:7-17:10](bibleref:42017007-42017010); [1CO 11:3](bibleref:46011003)

### High status owing to recognition of religious authority
**[ACT 5:34](bibleref:44005034)** *See also* [MAT 23:1-23:2](bibleref:40023001-40023002), [MAT 23:8-23:10](bibleref:40023008-40023010); [ACT 23:1-23:5](bibleref:44023001-44023005)

### High status owing to pious conduct
**[MAT 23:5-23:7](bibleref:40023005-40023007)** parallels MRK 12:38-12:39, LUK 20:45-20:46 *See also* [MAT 6:2](bibleref:40006002), [MAT 6:5](bibleref:40006005), [MAT 6:16](bibleref:40006016); [LUK 7:4-7:5](bibleref:42007004-42007005)

### High status based on wealth
**[PRO 19:4](bibleref:20019004)** *See also* [1KI 10:4-10:7](bibleref:11010004-11010007) parallel 2CH 9:3-9:6; [JOB 1:1-1:3](bibleref:18001001-18001003); [JAS 2:1-2:4](bibleref:59002001-59002004)
