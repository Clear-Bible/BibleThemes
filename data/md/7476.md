# 7476: thank-offering

Together with vows and freewill offerings this was one of the three kinds of fellowship offering.

## Presenting a thank-offering


### An animal sacrifice
**[LEV 7:15](bibleref:03007015)** *See also* [LEV 22:29](bibleref:03022029)

### Accompanied by bread, wafers and cakes
 [LEV 7:12-7:13](bibleref:03007012-03007013) *Cakes made with yeast can be offered, since these are not to be burned on the altar.* 

### The priest shares the meal
 [LEV 7:14](bibleref:03007014), [LEV 22:29-22:30](bibleref:03022029-03022030)

## The occasions for presenting thank-offerings


### For deliverance from distress, death or sickness
**[PSA 50:23](bibleref:19050023), [PSA 107:21-107:22](bibleref:19107021-19107022)** *See also* [PSA 56:12-56:13](bibleref:19056012-19056013) *deliverance from death* ; [PSA 116:17](bibleref:19116017) *Deliverance from sickness; the earlier verses show the psalmist has been ill.* 

### Following a vow
**[PSA 7:17](bibleref:19007017)** *A vow to praise God in anticipation of an answer to prayer.*  *See also* [PSA 66:13-66:14](bibleref:19066013-19066014)

### At times of religious renewal
 [2CH 29:31](bibleref:14029031) *Hezekiah's reformation* ; [2CH 33:15-33:16](bibleref:14033015-14033016) *Manasseh's repentance* [JER 17:26](bibleref:24017026), [JER 33:11](bibleref:24033011)
