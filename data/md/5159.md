# 5159: hearing

In Scripture, both physical hearing and spiritual apprehension. God hears the prayers of his people and the cry of the oppressed. People must hear and respond to God's word.



## Divine hearing


### God hears the prayers of his people
**2SA 22:7** *See also* PSA 3:4; ISA 65:24; JHN 11:41-11:42; 1PE 3:12; PSA 34:15

### God hears the grumbling of his people
**NUM 11:1** *See also* EXO 16:7-16:9, EXO 16:11-16:12; NUM 12:1-12:2, NUM 14:27

### God hears the cry of the oppressed
**JAS 5:4** *See also* EXO 3:7; PSA 94:9

### God does not hear the unrighteous
**ISA 59:1-59:2** *See also* PSA 66:18; ISA 1:15; JER 11:11, JER 11:14; EZK 8:18; AMO 5:23; JHN 9:31

### Other gods cannot hear
 1KI 18:26-18:27; ; PSA 115:6, PSA 135:17; ; ISA 46:7

## Hearing the word of God


### The importance of hearing God's word
**DEU 5:1**; **EPH 1:13** *See also* DEU 4:1; JER 2:4, JER 7:2; MIC 1:2; MAT 13:16-13:17 parallel LUK 10:23-10:24; JHN 5:24; ROM 10:17

### Having ears to hear
**MAT 11:15** *See also* MAT 13:9; MRK 4:9, MRK 4:23; LUK 8:8; REV 2:7, REV 2:11, REV 2:17, REV 2:29, REV 3:6, REV 3:13, REV 3:22

### The gospel must not only be heard but accepted and acted on
**HEB 4:2** *See also* MAT 7:24-7:27; MRK 4:14-4:20 parallels MAT 13:19-13:23, LUK 8:11-8:15; ROM 2:13; GAL 3:2, GAL 3:5; 1TH 2:13; JAS 1:22-1:24

### It is God who grants effective hearing
**ACT 16:14** *See also* PRO 20:12; ISA 50:5; 2TH 2:13-2:14

### Some people refuse to hear God's word
**JER 6:10** *See also* ISA 42:18-42:20; EZK 12:2; ACT 7:51; 2TI 4:4

### God denies the effective hearing of his word to those who stubbornly resist him
**MAT 13:10-13:15** parallels MRK 4:10-4:12, LUK 8:9-8:10 *See also* ISA 6:9-6:10; JHN 8:43; ACT 28:26-28:27; ROM 11:8; DEU 29:4; ISA 29:10

## The first Christians witnessed to what they had heard
**ACT 22:14-22:15** *See also* ACT 4:20; HEB 2:3; 1JN 1:1

## Physical hearing was restored by Jesus Christ
**MRK 7:35-7:37** *See also* MAT 11:5 parallel LUK 7:22

## The dead will hear the voice of the Son of God
**JHN 5:25** *See also* JHN 5:28-5:29, JHN 11:43-11:44
