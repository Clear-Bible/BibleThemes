# 9513: hell, as incentive to action

The reality of hell should affect the way people live.



## The importance of making the right choice
**[MAT 7:13-7:14](bibleref:40007013-40007014)** *See also* [DEU 30:19](bibleref:05030019); [JER 21:8](bibleref:24021008); [LUK 13:23-13:25](bibleref:42013023-42013025)

## A personal spiritual life is vital


### It is more important than short-term worldly gain
**[MAT 16:26](bibleref:40016026)** parallels MRK 8:36, LUK 9:25 *See also* [LUK 12:20](bibleref:42012020), [LUK 16:19-16:25](bibleref:42016019-42016025)

### It is not just outward appearance
**[MAT 3:7-3:10](bibleref:40003007-40003010)** parallel LUK 3:7-3:9

### Religious activity alone is not only valueless but perilous
**[MAT 7:21-7:23](bibleref:40007021-40007023)** *See also* [PRO 14:12](bibleref:20014012); [JER 23:11-23:12](bibleref:24023011-24023012); [LUK 13:26-13:28](bibleref:42013026-42013028); [JHN 15:6](bibleref:43015006)

## Believers must maintain their loyalty to God
**[MAT 10:28](bibleref:40010028)** parallel LUK 12:4-12:5 *See also* [DEU 4:23-4:24](bibleref:05004023-05004024); [ISA 8:12-8:13](bibleref:23008012-23008013)

## Temptations to sin must be dealt with drastically
**[MAT 5:29-5:30](bibleref:40005029-40005030)** *See also* [MAT 18:8-18:9](bibleref:40018008-40018009); [MRK 9:43-9:48](bibleref:41009043-41009048)

## Some attitudes and practices that lead to hell


### Deliberately continuing in sin
**[HEB 10:26-10:27](bibleref:58010026-58010027)** *See also* [NUM 15:30](bibleref:04015030); [HEB 6:4-6:6](bibleref:58006004-58006006); [2PE 2:20](bibleref:61002020)

### Lack of spiritual response
 [MAT 22:13](bibleref:40022013), [MAT 24:51](bibleref:40024051), [MAT 25:30](bibleref:40025030)

### Wilfully ignoring divine activity
**[MAT 11:20-11:24](bibleref:40011020-40011024)** parallel LUK 10:13-10:15 *Sodom, destroyed by God, was notoriously sinful.*  *See also* [MAT 23:33](bibleref:40023033), [MAT 25:41-25:46](bibleref:40025041-40025046)

### Contempt for fellow humans
**[MAT 5:22](bibleref:40005022)** *The term “fool” implied the despising of another as worthless and godless.*  *See also* [JAS 3:6](bibleref:59003006)
