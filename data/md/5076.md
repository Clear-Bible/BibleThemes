# 5076: Abraham, life of

God called Abraham to live as a nomad in Canaan and promised the land to his descendants, naming him “father of many”, although he had no heir until his old age.



## Abraham's ancestors, marriage and descendants


### The line of descent from Shem to Abraham
 1CH 1:24-1:27

### Abraham's marriage to Sarah
 GEN 11:29-11:30

### Abraham's descendants
 1CH 1:28-1:34

## Abraham was called to live in Canaan


### His migration from Ur to Canaan
**GEN 11:31-12:5** *See also* JOS 24:2-24:3; ACT 7:2-7:4; HEB 11:8-11:9

### His nomadic way of life in Canaan
**HEB 11:9** *See also* GEN 12:6-12:10, GEN 13:1-13:4

### God gives Abraham his new name
**GEN 17:5** *The giving of a new name signifies a new relationship to God. “Abram” has the sense of “revered father”, whereas “Abraham” means “father of many people”.* *See also* GEN 17:15

### Abraham buys a burial site for Sarah
**GEN 23:3-23:4** *This is his only acquisition of land in Canaan.* *See also* GEN 23:17-23:20, GEN 49:29-49:32

## Abraham's dealings with his nephew, Lot
 GEN 12:4-12:5 *Abraham is accompanied to Canaan by Lot*; ; GEN 13:5-13:12 *Abraham and Lot separate*; ; GEN 14:8-14:16 *Abraham rescues Lot from five local kings.*; GEN 18:20-18:33, GEN 19:29

## Significant encounters in Abraham's life
With Melchizedek:
 GEN 14:18-14:20; ; HEB 7:1-7:10; 
GEN 18:1-18:22 *with three angels*

## God's covenant with Abraham


### God makes a covenant with Abraham
**GEN 15:1-15:21** *See also* ACT 7:5-7:7

### God gives Abram the name Abraham, as a sign of the covenant
 GEN 17:1-17:8

### God gives Abraham the gift of circumcision as a sign of the covenant
**GEN 17:9-17:27** *See also* ACT 7:8; ROM 4:11

## Abraham's family


### Abraham and Sarah were childless
**GEN 11:30** *See also* GEN 15:2-15:3, GEN 17:17, GEN 18:11-18:13

### Abraham's pretence that Sarah is his sister
 GEN 12:10-12:20, GEN 20:1-20:18

### The birth of Ishmael
 GEN 16:1-16:4, GEN 16:15-16:16

### The birth of Isaac
 GEN 21:1-21:7

### Abraham dismisses Hagar and Ishmael
 GEN 21:8-21:14

### God tests Abraham over Isaac
**GEN 22:1-22:19** *See also* HEB 11:17-11:19; JAS 2:21

### Abraham's wife Sarah dies
 GEN 23:1-23:2

### Abraham makes arrangements for a wife for Isaac
 GEN 24:1-24:9

### Abraham marries Keturah and has children by her
 GEN 25:1-25:2 parallel 1CH 1:32

### The death of Abraham
 GEN 25:7-25:11

## The time span of Abraham's life
 GEN 12:4 *Abraham leaves Haran at the age of 75.*; GEN 16:3, GEN 16:16; 
GEN 17:1 *Abraham renamed and circumcised at the age of 99*; ; GEN 21:5 *Isaac born when Abraham is 100*; ; GEN 23:1 *Sarah dies when Abraham is 137. Ge 17:17 indicates that Sarah was ten years younger than Abraham*; ; GEN 25:7 *Abraham dies at the age of 175.*
