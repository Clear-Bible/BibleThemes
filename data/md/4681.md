# 4681: ram

The male sheep, part of an agrarian economy and used in sacrifice. Its skin had value and its horn was used in worship.



## Rams as part of an agrarian economy
**GEN 31:38** *See also* GEN 32:14; DEU 32:14; 2KI 3:4; EZK 27:21, EZK 34:17

## Rams were used for religious purposes


### As sacrificial victims
**GEN 22:13** *See also* NUM 23:2; 1SA 15:22; 1CH 15:26; MIC 6:7

### In the making of a covenant
 GEN 15:9-15:10

### For guilt offerings
**LEV 5:15** *See also* LEV 5:16-5:19, LEV 6:6, LEV 19:21-19:22; NUM 5:8; 2CH 29:21; EZR 10:19

### For fellowship or burnt offerings
**LEV 9:4** *See also* LEV 9:1-9:3, LEV 16:3-16:5; NUM 6:14-6:17, NUM 7:17, NUM 15:6-15:7, NUM 28:11, NUM 29:2; EZK 43:23-43:24, EZK 46:4
