# 8723: doubt, results of

Doubt results in uncertainty and ineffectiveness in the spiritual life of individual believers and of the believing community. God appeals to those who waver to make a clear choice.



## Doubt results in spiritual uncertainty


### Uncertainty about commitment
**LUK 9:57-9:62** parallel MAT 8:19-8:22 *See also* GEN 19:17, GEN 19:26; EXO 16:2-16:3; MAT 6:24 parallel LUK 16:13; HEB 11:15

### Uncertainty about God's power
**NUM 13:31** *See also* DEU 1:29-1:33

### Uncertainty about God's love
**ISA 40:27** *See also* DEU 1:27; JOB 9:16-9:18, JOB 30:21

### Uncertainty about the meaning of life
**JOB 3:16** *See also* 1KI 19:3-19:4; PSA 42:3-42:4; JER 20:14-20:18; JON 4:3

### Uncertainty about teaching
**EPH 4:14** *See also* 1TI 6:20-6:21

### Uncertainty about the will of God
**JDG 6:17** *See also* JDG 6:36-6:40; JHN 20:25; 2CO 13:3

## Doubt may result in a spiritual decline


### Doubt may result in drifting from faith
**HEB 2:1** *See also* 2CH 36:12-36:13; NEH 9:16-9:17; PSA 95:8-95:9; LAM 5:20; HEB 3:12-3:13, HEB 6:4-6:6; 2PE 2:20-2:21

### Doubt may result in faithless action
**ISA 31:1**; **ROM 14:23** *See also* GEN 12:13, GEN 16:2-16:3; EXO 32:1; NUM 20:11-20:12; ACT 7:40; ROM 14:14

### Doubt brings the risk of God's judgment
**NUM 20:12** *See also* NUM 11:1, NUM 14:37; HEB 6:7-6:8

## Doubt leads to ineffectiveness in prayer
**JAS 1:6-1:7** *See also* MAT 17:20, MAT 21:21 parallel MRK 11:23

## Doubt spreads
**DEU 1:28** *See also* NUM 13:32; 2KI 17:14

**1KI 18:21** *See also* DEU 30:15-30:17; JOS 24:15; JHN 20:27; JAS 4:8
