# 1412: foreknowledge

Foreknowledge is an aspect of God's knowledge of everything, and thus nothing can surprise him. Jesus Christ had foreknowledge of his own death, resurrection and coming in glory.



## God's foreknowledge


### Everything is within God's knowledge
**ISA 42:9** *See also* 1KI 22:14; DAN 2:28; MAT 24:36 parallel MRK 13:32

### What God knows he ordains
**ISA 46:10-46:11** *See also* ACT 2:23; ROM 8:28-8:30; 1PE 1:1-1:2

### God's foreknowledge of the Messiah
**ACT 3:18** *See also* ISA 11:1-11:3, ISA 53:7-53:9; MIC 5:2; ACT 2:22-2:23, ACT 4:27-4:28; 1PE 1:20

## God's foreknowledge and his election of his people


### God's sovereign choice
**ROM 8:29** *See also* PRO 16:4; ROM 9:11-9:12, ROM 11:2; EPH 1:4; 1PE 1:1-1:2, 1PE 1:20

### Human responsibility
**HEB 2:3** *See also* JHN 3:18, JHN 3:36; 1CO 15:2; 2CO 6:1-6:2; EPH 5:5-5:6; HEB 3:12, HEB 6:4-6:6; 2PE 2:1; HEB 3:15; PSA 95:7-95:8

## Jesus Christ's foreknowledge


### Of events within his general ministry
**JHN 6:64** *See also* MAT 17:27, MAT 26:31-26:34 parallel MRK 14:27-14:30; LUK 22:34; JHN 13:38

### Of his suffering and departure
**JHN 13:1** *See also* MAT 26:18; MRK 8:31 parallel LUK 9:22; LUK 12:50, LUK 22:37; JHN 3:14, JHN 7:33, JHN 13:11, JHN 13:33, JHN 14:28, JHN 17:5, JHN 18:4, JHN 19:28

### Of his coming in glory and judgment
**MAT 16:27** *See also* MAT 24:30-24:31 parallel MRK 13:26-13:27; MAT 25:31; MRK 8:38 parallel LUK 9:26; LUK 24:26
