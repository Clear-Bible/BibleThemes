# 8203: character

The moral and mental features that define a person, whether good or evil. The term also means moral strength, which Scripture regards as something to be highly valued.



## The character of God


### God is the Father of believers
**EPH 1:2** *See also* GAL 1:1; COL 1:12; 1TH 1:3, 1TH 3:13

### God is good
**PSA 34:8** *See also* EXO 33:19; LAM 3:25; MAT 19:17 parallel MRK 10:18; 2PE 1:3

### God is holy
**1SA 2:2** *See also* LEV 11:44-11:45; ISA 6:3, ISA 43:15; EZK 39:7; 1PE 1:15; REV 4:8

### God is faithful
**PSA 36:5** *See also* DEU 7:9; 1KI 8:56; PSA 89:2, PSA 146:6; LAM 3:23; 1TH 5:23-5:24

### God is just and righteous
**ROM 3:25-3:26** *See also* 2CH 12:6; PSA 33:5; ISA 30:18; ZEP 3:5; ACT 17:31; REV 16:5-16:6

### God is loving
**PSA 145:17** *See also* JHN 3:16; ROM 5:8; EPH 2:4; TIT 3:4-3:5; HEB 12:6; 1JN 4:7-4:10

### God is merciful and compassionate
**PSA 86:15**; **LUK 1:50** *See also* NEH 9:31; ISA 63:7; LAM 3:22; JON 4:2; LUK 18:10-18:14; 2CO 1:3; JAS 5:11
