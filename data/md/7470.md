# 7470: temple, significance of

The temple was both idealised and spiritualised by OT and NT writers.



## The OT concept of an ideal temple in the future age of salvation
**[ISA 2:3](bibleref:23002003)** parallel MIC 4:2 *See also* [ISA 56:4-56:7](bibleref:23056004-23056007), [ISA 66:20](bibleref:23066020); [JER 33:11](bibleref:24033011); [EZK 37:28](bibleref:26037028), [EZK 40:2-40:4](bibleref:26040002-26040004) *Ezekiel received a vision of a rebuilt temple to which the glory of the LORD had returned. Chapters 40-48 describe this temple and its worship in detail* ; [HAG 2:6-2:8](bibleref:37002006-37002008); [ZEC 14:16](bibleref:38014016); [MAL 3:1-3:3](bibleref:39003001-39003003)
