# 8251: faithfulness, to God

The proper response to God by his covenant people; seen in a steadfast commitment which reflects God's own faithfulness to the covenant. Encouraged in all believers, it is especially important in leaders.



## Faithfulness is the proper response to God by his covenant people


### God's covenant with his people requires faithfulness
**1KI 2:3-2:4** *See also* EXO 19:5; DEU 5:32-5:33, DEU 10:12-10:13, DEU 29:9; ISA 1:26

### Faithfulness to God seen as marital fidelity
**HOS 2:20** *See also* ISA 54:5; JER 2:2; EZK 16:8; EPH 1:1, EPH 5:22-5:25

### Faithfulness to God reflects God's faithfulness
**DEU 7:9** *See also* 1SA 2:35; 1CO 10:12-10:13; HEB 4:14-4:16, HEB 10:23; 1PE 4:19

## Faithfulness is seen as steadfast commitment to God


### In unwavering devotion
**MIC 4:5** *See also* JOS 24:14-24:15; LUK 9:62; REV 2:10

### In obedience
 DEU 11:13; ; EZK 18:9

### In service
 1SA 12:24; ; 2CH 19:9, 2CH 34:10-34:12 parallel 2KI 22:4-22:7; ; EZK 44:15, EZK 48:11; ; 1TI 1:12

### In giving
 2CH 31:12 *Faithfulness to God is expressed in giving to him for the benefit of others*; ; 2CO 8:1-8:5

### In prayer
 ROM 12:12; ; 1TH 5:17

### In patient endurance
 2TI 2:11-2:13; ; HEB 10:36; ; REV 13:10, REV 14:12

### In fulfilling vows
 NUM 30:2; ; DEU 23:21-23:23; ; PSA 22:25, PSA 61:8; ; ECC 5:4; ; JON 2:9

## Faithfulness is especially important in leaders
**1CO 4:1-4:2**; **3JN 1:3** *See also* EXO 18:21; 1TI 4:13-4:16, 1TI 6:20; 2TI 1:13-1:14, 2TI 2:2, 2TI 2:15, 2TI 4:1-4:2

## Encouragements to faithfulness


### The call to faithfulness
**1CO 15:58** *See also* PRO 3:5-3:6; ISA 26:4; JHN 14:1; 1CO 16:13; GAL 5:1; EPH 6:13

### God watches over the faithful
**PSA 97:10** *See also* PSA 31:23, PSA 37:28, PSA 86:2; PRO 2:8

### God rewards faithfulness
**1SA 26:23**; **MAT 25:21** parallel LUK 19:17 *See also* PSA 101:6; PRO 28:20; MAT 24:45-24:47 parallel LUK 12:42-12:44; 2TI 4:6-4:8; REV 17:14, REV 20:4

## Lack of faithfulness
