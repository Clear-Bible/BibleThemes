# 5828: danger

A condition of threat, physical or spiritual, in which evil, malice, accident or destruction looms.



## Physical danger


### In nature
**LUK 8:23** parallels MAT 8:24, MRK 4:37 *See also* 1KI 19:11-19:12; PSA 83:14-83:15, PSA 107:23-107:28; ACT 16:26-16:30; 2CO 11:26; REV 11:13

### From human violence
**PSA 140:1** *See also* GEN 27:41-27:43; EXO 2:15; 1SA 19:1-19:2; 2SA 22:3; 1KI 19:1-19:3; NEH 4:7-4:9; PSA 7:9, PSA 64:1, PSA 74:20; ECC 12:5; LUK 4:28-4:29; ACT 21:35

### Through foolishness
**PRO 22:3** *See also* PRO 27:12; ECC 2:13-2:14

## Spiritual danger


### From the devil
**1PE 5:8** *See also* GEN 3:1-3:5; JOB 1:6-1:12, JOB 2:1-2:6; MAT 4:1-4:11 parallel LUK 4:2-4:13; JHN 13:2; HEB 2:14; 1JN 3:8; REV 2:10, REV 20:10

### Danger of hell through disobedience
**MAT 5:22** *See also* LUK 12:4-12:5; 1CO 5:4-5:5; HEB 6:4-6:6; 2PE 2:4-2:10

### Danger of loss through unfaithfulness
**1CO 3:13-3:15** *See also* ROM 6:21, ROM 6:21; 1CO 9:26-9:27; 1TI 6:9-6:10

### Danger does not overcome the faithful
**ROM 8:35** *See also* 2TI 3:10-3:11
