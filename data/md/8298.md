# 8298: love, for one another

Scripture instructs God's people to love one another and illustrates what this means in practice.



## Reasons for loving one another


### God commands it
**GAL 5:14** *See also* LEV 19:18LEV 19:34; DEU 10:19
MAT 22:39 parallel MRK 12:31 *the second greatest commandment*; JHN 15:12 *Jesus Christ commands his disciples to love one another*; ROM 13:10; 1TH 4:9; HEB 13:1; JAS 2:8; 1PE 1:22, 1PE 2:17; 1JN 3:23, 1JN 4:21; 2JN 1:5

### God has taken the initiative in showing love
**1JN 4:11** *See also* MAL 2:10; 1CO 8:11-8:13; 1JN 3:16

### God's people are known by their love
**JHN 13:35** *See also* 1JN 2:10, 1JN 3:14, 1JN 4:7, 1JN 4:16, 1JN 4:20

### Love maintains fellowship
**1PE 4:8** *See also* PRO 10:12, PRO 17:9; EPH 4:2

### Love promotes sacrificial service
**1TH 2:8** *See also* PRO 17:17; 2CO 12:15; GAL 5:13; PHP 2:30, PHP 4:10; 1TH 1:3

## Expressing love for one another


### In caring for the sick
**MAT 25:36** *See also* JOB 2:11; GAL 4:14

### In meeting material needs
**MAT 25:35** *See also* DEU 15:7-15:8; 1JN 3:17

### In affectionate greetings
**2CO 13:12** *See also* GEN 33:4, GEN 45:14-45:15; ACT 20:37; ROM 16:16; 1CO 16:20; 1PE 5:14
