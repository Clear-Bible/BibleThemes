# 4836: light, and people of God

Scripture often uses light as a symbol of the people of God, and especially the manner in which believers are able and required to reflect the glory of God in a dark world of sin.



## The people of God as the light of the world
**MAT 5:14-5:16** *See also* PSA 37:5-37:6; PRO 4:18, PRO 13:9; ISA 58:8-58:10, ISA 60:1-60:5, ISA 62:1; DAN 12:3; LUK 2:32; ACT 13:47

## Examples of believers acting as light to the world
Moses on Mount Sinai:
 EXO 34:29-34:30, EXO 34:35; 
2SA 23:3-23:4 *the righteous ruler, fully realised in Jesus Christ*; ; JOB 29:21-29:25 *Job as a counsellor*; ; JHN 5:35 *John the Baptist*; ; ACT 6:15 *Stephen*; ; PHP 2:15 *the Philippian church*

## God is the source of the light of his people


### God and his law as the source of light
**PSA 27:1, PSA 118:27** *See also* JDG 5:31; JOB 11:13-11:17, JOB 22:27-22:28; PSA 19:8, PSA 34:5, PSA 56:13, PSA 97:11; PRO 6:23; ECC 8:1; ISA 60:19-60:20; JHN 1:5

### The gospel of forgiveness as a source of light
**ACT 26:17-26:18** *See also* JOB 33:28-33:30; PSA 112:4; ISA 9:2, ISA 29:18; MAT 4:16; JHN 3:19-3:21; EPH 1:18, EPH 5:27; HEB 6:4, HEB 10:32

## The people of God are called to live in his light
**EPH 5:8-5:14**; **1JN 1:7** *See also* PSA 36:9, PSA 89:15; ISA 2:5; LUK 16:8; JHN 9:4, JHN 12:35-12:36; ROM 13:12; 1TH 5:5-5:6; 1JN 2:8-2:10
