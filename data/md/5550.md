# 5550: speech, negative aspects of

The sinfulness of fallen human nature can express itself in speech, provoking God's displeasure and causing enmity and division amongst people.



## Evil speech comes from a corrupt heart
**MAT 12:33-12:35** *See also* PRO 2:12-2:15, PRO 24:1-24:2; MAT 15:10-15:11 parallel MRK 7:14-7:15; LUK 6:43-6:45

## Evil speech is a feature of a corrupt society
**MIC 6:12-6:13** *See also* ISA 6:5, ISA 59:1-59:4, ISA 59:12-59:15; JER 9:1-9:9; ROM 1:29-1:32; 2TI 3:1-3:5

## Evil speech provokes God's displeasure
**PRO 12:22** *See also* PRO 6:16-6:19

## Kinds of evil speech


### Profaning God's name
**EXO 20:7** parallel DEU 5:11 *See also* EXO 22:28; LEV 19:12; PSA 139:19-139:20

### Lies and deceit
**PSA 5:9** *See also* PSA 36:3, PSA 52:1-52:4, PSA 62:4; PRO 14:5, PRO 14:25, PRO 15:4, PRO 17:20

### Foolish boasting
**PSA 94:4** *See also* PSA 10:2-10:6, PSA 12:3-12:4; ISA 10:12-10:14; 2TH 2:3-2:4; JAS 4:13-4:16; 2PE 2:17-2:18

### Ridicule and insults
**PRO 11:12** *See also* 1SA 17:41-17:44; 2KI 2:23-2:24; NEH 2:19; PRO 9:7; LAM 2:15-2:16; MAT 5:22, MAT 27:39-27:44 parallels MRK 15:29-15:32, LUK 23:35-23:39; JHN 9:28-9:29

### Criticism and complaint
**NUM 12:1-12:15** *See also* NUM 16:1-16:50; 1SA 17:28; PRO 19:13, PRO 21:9, PRO 21:19, PRO 25:24; ACT 11:2-11:3; PHP 2:14-2:15

### Slander
**LEV 19:16** *See also* 1KI 21:1-21:14; PRO 10:18; JAS 4:11

### Cursing
**EXO 21:17** *See also* 2SA 16:5-16:8; PRO 20:20; JAS 3:9-3:10

### Gossip
**PRO 16:28** *See also* PRO 11:13, PRO 17:9, PRO 20:19

### Obscenity
**EPH 5:4** *See also* COL 3:8

## Evil speech is silenced


### By God's actions
**PSA 107:41-107:42** *See also* PSA 63:11; EZK 16:63

### By believers' blameless lives
