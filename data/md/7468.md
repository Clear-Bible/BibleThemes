# 7468: temple, rebuilding of

Following the exile Solomon's ruined temple was rebuilt and became the focus of the Jewish faith worldwide.



## Preparations for rebuilding the temple
 [EZR 1:1-1:4](bibleref:15001001-15001004) parallel 2CH 36:22-36:23 *Cyrus gave permission for the exiled Jews to return to Jerusalem to rebuild the temple (538 B.C.)* ; [EZR 1:7](bibleref:15001007) *He sent back the temple treasures.*
