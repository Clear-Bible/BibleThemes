# 5965: temperament

A person's disposition, especially as shown in mental or emotional responses. God's people should be of an even temperament.



## God's people should be of even temperament
**PRO 16:32** *See also* TIT 2:1-2:14

## Even temperament is a requirement for church leadership
**1TI 3:2-3:3** *See also* 1TI 3:8-3:9, 1TI 3:11; TIT 1:7-1:9

## Examples of people who had an unpleasant temperament


### Saul
**1SA 16:23** *Violent mood swings and bad temper were signs of mental instability in Saul.* *See also* 1SA 16:14-16:16, 1SA 18:6-18:15, 1SA 20:4-20:7, 1SA 20:24-20:33

### Nabal the Carmelite
**1SA 25:17** *See also* 1SA 25:2-25:11, 1SA 25:36-25:38
