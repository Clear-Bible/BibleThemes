# 5965: temperament

A person's disposition, especially as shown in mental or emotional responses. God's people should be of an even temperament.

## God's people should be of even temperament
**[PRO 16:32](bibleref:20016032)** *See also* [TIT 2:1-2:14](bibleref:56002001-56002014)

## Even temperament is a requirement for church leadership
**[1TI 3:2-3:3](bibleref:54003002-54003003)** *See also* [1TI 3:8-3:9](bibleref:54003008-54003009), [1TI 3:11](bibleref:54003011); [TIT 1:7-1:9](bibleref:56001007-56001009)
