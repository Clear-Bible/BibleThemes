# 8343: servanthood, in society

Servants and slaves were an important feature of everyday life in biblical times. The idea of servanthood is used both literally and metaphorically throughout Scripture.



## Laws of servanthood
**[EXO 21:2-21:6](bibleref:02021002-02021006)** parallel DEU 15:12-15:18 *The words “servant” and “slave” are the same in Hebrew, but according to OT law, an Israelite servant was to be accorded more rights than a foreign slave.*  *See also* [GEN 17:13](bibleref:01017013); [EXO 12:44](bibleref:02012044), [EXO 20:10](bibleref:02020010) parallel DEU 5:14; [LEV 25:39-25:55](bibleref:03025039-03025055); [DEU 21:10-21:14](bibleref:05021010-05021014), [DEU 29:10-29:11](bibleref:05029010-05029011); [1CH 2:34-2:35](bibleref:13002034-13002035)
