# 5052: responsibility, to God

Even though God is sovereign, all people everywhere are accountable to him for everything they do, say or think.



## People are responsible for their own sins


### God is not responsible for human sin
**DEU 24:16**; **EZK 18:4** *See also* JOB 19:4; PSA 51:4-51:5; JER 31:30; EZK 18:20, EZK 33:1-33:20; ROM 6:23

### Death is the result of sin
**ROM 5:12, ROM 6:23** *See also* GEN 3:1-3:7; ISA 43:27; EZK 18:4; 1CO 15:21-15:22

## The law heightens human responsibility for sin


### It brings knowledge of sin
**ROM 5:20** *Because the law brings knowledge of sin, it prevents people from claiming ignorance of the nature and seriousness of that sin.* *See also* ROM 7:7-7:9; GAL 3:19

### It applies not only to actions but also to words and thoughts
**MAT 5:22, MAT 5:28** *See also* JER 17:9-17:10; MAT 15:18-15:19

### It brings judgment
**ECC 12:14**; **MAT 12:36-12:37**; **1CO 4:5** *See also* JOB 15:5-15:6; ROM 2:16, ROM 14:12; 2CO 5:10; 1PE 4:5; JUD 1:15

## Ignorance does not remove responsibility
**LEV 5:17** *See also* LUK 12:48; ACT 3:17-3:19; ROM 1:20

## The responsibility of humanity to repent and believe


### It is commanded by God
**ACT 17:30** *See also* EZK 18:30; MAT 4:17; MRK 1:15, MRK 6:12; LUK 24:46-24:47

### Jesus Christ's life and ministry heightens the responsibility to repent
**JHN 3:18, JHN 15:22** *See also* MAT 11:20-11:24, MAT 12:41-12:42 parallel LUK 11:31-11:32; JHN 12:48
