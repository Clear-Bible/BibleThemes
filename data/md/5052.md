# 5052: responsibility, to God

Even though God is sovereign, all people everywhere are accountable to him for everything they do, say or think.



## People are responsible for their own sins


### God is not responsible for human sin
**[DEU 24:16](bibleref:05024016)**; **[EZK 18:4](bibleref:26018004)** *See also* [JOB 19:4](bibleref:18019004); [PSA 51:4-51:5](bibleref:19051004-19051005); [JER 31:30](bibleref:24031030); [EZK 18:20](bibleref:26018020), [EZK 33:1-33:20](bibleref:26033001-26033020); [ROM 6:23](bibleref:45006023)

### Death is the result of sin
**[ROM 5:12](bibleref:45005012), [ROM 6:23](bibleref:45006023)** *See also* [GEN 3:1-3:7](bibleref:01003001-01003007); [ISA 43:27](bibleref:23043027); [EZK 18:4](bibleref:26018004); [1CO 15:21-15:22](bibleref:46015021-46015022)

## The law heightens human responsibility for sin


### It brings knowledge of sin
**[ROM 5:20](bibleref:45005020)** *Because the law brings knowledge of sin, it prevents people from claiming ignorance of the nature and seriousness of that sin.*  *See also* [ROM 7:7-7:9](bibleref:45007007-45007009); [GAL 3:19](bibleref:48003019)

### It applies not only to actions but also to words and thoughts
**[MAT 5:22](bibleref:40005022), [MAT 5:28](bibleref:40005028)** *See also* [JER 17:9-17:10](bibleref:24017009-24017010); [MAT 15:18-15:19](bibleref:40015018-40015019)

### It brings judgment
**[ECC 12:14](bibleref:21012014)**; **[MAT 12:36-12:37](bibleref:40012036-40012037)**; **[1CO 4:5](bibleref:46004005)** *See also* [JOB 15:5-15:6](bibleref:18015005-18015006); [ROM 2:16](bibleref:45002016), [ROM 14:12](bibleref:45014012); [2CO 5:10](bibleref:47005010); [1PE 4:5](bibleref:60004005); [JUD 1:15](bibleref:65001015)

## Ignorance does not remove responsibility
