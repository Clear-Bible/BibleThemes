# 5636: work, and rest

In creation, God has established a pattern of work and rest that is to be a model for believers.



## Work and rest built into creation
**[EXO 20:11](bibleref:02020011)** *See also* [GEN 2:1-2:3](bibleref:01002001-01002003); [PSA 104:19-104:23](bibleref:19104019-19104023)

## The pattern of work and rest confirmed in the OT


### By Sabbath observance
 [EXO 20:10](bibleref:02020010) parallel DEU 5:14; [LEV 23:3](bibleref:03023003)

### By observing holy festivals
 [EXO 12:16](bibleref:02012016); [LEV 16:29](bibleref:03016029), [LEV 23:6-23:8](bibleref:03023006-03023008) parallel NUM 28:17-28:18; [LEV 23:28-23:31](bibleref:03023028-03023031) parallel NUM 29:7; [LEV 23:35-23:36](bibleref:03023035-03023036), [LEV 23:39](bibleref:03023039) parallel NUM 29:12; [EST 9:17-9:19](bibleref:17009017-17009019)

## Work and rest in the ministry of Jesus Christ
**[MRK 6:30-6:32](bibleref:41006030-41006032)** *See also* [JHN 4:6](bibleref:43004006)

## Wisdom needed to balance work and rest


### To avoid idleness
 [PRO 6:9-6:11](bibleref:20006009-20006011), [PRO 10:4-10:5](bibleref:20010004-20010005), [PRO 14:23](bibleref:20014023), [PRO 20:13](bibleref:20020013), [PRO 24:30-24:34](bibleref:20024030-20024034)
