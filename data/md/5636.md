# 5636: work, and rest

In creation, God has established a pattern of work and rest that is to be a model for believers.



## Work and rest built into creation
**EXO 20:11** *See also* GEN 2:1-2:3; PSA 104:19-104:23

## The pattern of work and rest confirmed in the OT


### By Sabbath observance
 EXO 20:10 parallel DEU 5:14; ; LEV 23:3

### By observing holy festivals
 EXO 12:16; ; LEV 16:29, LEV 23:6-23:8 parallel NUM 28:17-28:18; ; LEV 23:28-23:31 parallel NUM 29:7; ; LEV 23:35-23:36, LEV 23:39 parallel NUM 29:12; ; EST 9:17-9:19

## Work and rest in the ministry of Jesus Christ
**MRK 6:30-6:32** *See also* JHN 4:6

## Wisdom needed to balance work and rest


### To avoid idleness
 PRO 6:9-6:11, PRO 10:4-10:5, PRO 14:23, PRO 20:13, PRO 24:30-24:34

### To avoid overwork
 EXO 18:13-18:24; ; PSA 127:2; ; LUK 10:38-10:42

## Work and rest are both potential means of glorifying God
**1CO 10:31** *See also* ECC 2:24; COL 3:17

## Work and rest will be perfectly fulfilled in heaven
**REV 14:13, REV 22:3**
