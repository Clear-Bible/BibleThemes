# 8849: worry

A sense of uneasiness and anxiety about the future. Scripture indicates that such anxiety is ultimately grounded in a lack of trust in God and his purposes.



## The causes of worry


### Being world-centred
**MAT 6:25** parallel LUK 12:22 *See also* MAT 6:34, MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14; LUK 12:29, LUK 21:34; 1CO 7:33

### Lacking confidence in God
**JHN 14:1** *See also* MAT 6:26-6:32 parallel LUK 12:27-12:30; MAT 8:25 parallels MRK 4:38, LUK 8:24; MAT 14:31, MAT 16:8

### Abandoning God
**DEU 28:64-28:65** *See also* LAM 1:3; EZK 4:16-4:17, EZK 12:19

## Worry leads people away from God
**PSA 37:8** *See also* PRO 12:25; MAT 13:22 parallel LUK 8:14; LUK 10:40-10:42

## Worry is futile
**MAT 6:27** parallel LUK 12:25 *See also* ECC 2:22-2:23

## Remedies for worry


### Being God-centred
**ISA 26:3**; **MAT 6:33** parallel LUK 12:31 *See also* JER 17:7-17:8; ROM 8:6

### Prayer
**PSA 55:22**; **PHP 4:6-4:7** *See also* PSA 94:18-94:19, PSA 139:23; 1PE 5:7
