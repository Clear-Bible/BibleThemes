# 8849: worry

A sense of uneasiness and anxiety about the future. Scripture indicates that such anxiety is ultimately grounded in a lack of trust in God and his purposes.

## The causes of worry


### Being world-centred
**[MAT 6:25](bibleref:40006025)** parallel LUK 12:22 *See also* [MAT 6:34](bibleref:40006034), [MAT 13:22](bibleref:40013022) parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14; [LUK 12:29](bibleref:42012029), [LUK 21:34](bibleref:42021034); [1CO 7:33](bibleref:46007033)
