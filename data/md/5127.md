# 5127: back

As the part of the body used for bearing loads it is used both literally and metaphorically.



## The human back used for carrying


### Jesus Christ carried his cross on his back
**JHN 19:17** *See also* MAT 27:32 parallels MRK 15:21, LUK 23:26 *The shape of the cross necessitated it being carried across the shoulders and back.  It appears that Jesus Christ started to carry his own cross but Simon of Cyrene took over as Christ weakened.*

### Weapons carried on the back
 1SA 17:6

## Animals carry people and goods on their backs
**ISA 30:6** *See also* EXO 15:1, EXO 15:21; 2SA 13:29, 2SA 16:1-16:2, 2SA 18:9, 2SA 19:26; 2KI 18:23 parallel ISA 36:8; ISA 21:7, ISA 30:16; JER 6:23; HAG 2:22; REV 9:17

### Riding on the backs of animals may indicate royalty or privilege
**ZEC 9:9** *See also* 1KI 1:33, 1KI 1:38, 1KI 1:44JDG 5:10, JDG 10:4, JDG 12:14REV 6:1-6:8, REV 19:11-19:21

### Jesus Christ mounted a donkey for his triumphal entry into Jerusalem
**MAT 21:7-21:9** parallels MRK 11:7-11:11, LUK 19:35-19:38 *See also* JHN 12:13-12:15

## Carrying a burden on the back represents bearing suffering and trials
**PSA 66:11** *See also* PSA 38:4; ECC 1:13, ECC 3:10; MAT 11:28-11:30

## Injury to the back
 JDG 3:22; ; 2SA 2:23; ; JOB 20:24-20:25

### Pain in the back causing anguish
 PSA 38:7

## The back endures punishment


### Bending of the back indicates loss of physical strength
**PSA 69:23** *See also* ROM 11:10

### Beating on the back is a punishment
 PRO 10:13, PRO 14:3, PRO 19:29, PRO 26:3

### Beating on the back may be an unjust punishment for God's servants
**ISA 50:6** *See also* ISA 53:4-53:5; MAT 21:35 parallels MRK 12:3, LUK 20:11 *The servants in the parable represent the OT prophets.*

### Jesus Christ endured flogging
**MAT 27:26** parallels MRK 15:15, JHN 19:1 *Jesus Christ becomes the suffering servant of Isaiah's prophecies.*

### The apostles endured flogging
**2CO 11:23-11:25** *See also* ACT 5:40, ACT 16:22-16:23, ACT 16:37, ACT 21:32; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 6:9

### Jesus Christ's disciples should expect similar flogging
**MAT 10:17** *See also* MAT 23:34; MRK 13:9

### Whipping on the back used figuratively in a warning
 JOS 23:12-23:13

### Walking over the back used figuratively to indicate defeat
 ISA 51:23

## Turning one's back means desertion


### God makes the enemies of his people flee
**EXO 23:27** *See also* 2SA 22:41 parallel PSA 18:40; PSA 21:12

### Israel flees from her enemies
**JOS 7:12**

### God's people desert him
**NEH 9:29** *“To turn one's back”, in these verses and the following verses, also implies a lack of respect and reverence for God and his ways.* *See also* 2CH 29:6; ISA 1:4, ISA 59:12-59:13; JER 2:27, JER 32:33-32:34; EZK 8:16

### God's people desert his instruction
 ZEC 7:11; ; 2PE 2:21

## Thrusting behind one's back means discarding
**ISA 38:17** *God forgives sins, putting them out of sight*; **EZK 23:35** *See also* 1KI 14:9; NEH 9:26

## God turns his back in his grace
**EXO 33:22-33:23** *To see God's face would bring death.*

## God turns his back against disobedient people
**JER 18:17**
