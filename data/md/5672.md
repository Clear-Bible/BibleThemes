# 5672: concubines

A woman, often a servant or slave, with whom a man had regular sexual relations, but to whom he was not married. A concubine did not have the rights of a wife and her children were not rightful heirs, though a wife might offer a servant to her husband as a concubine to have children on her behalf.

## Concubines and wives


### Concubines were distinguished from wives
**[1KI 11:3](bibleref:11011003)** *Having concubines alongside wives was accepted in a polygamous society.*  *See also* [JDG 8:30-8:31](bibleref:07008030-07008031); [2SA 5:13](bibleref:10005013), [2SA 19:5](bibleref:10019005); [2CH 11:21](bibleref:14011021); [DAN 5:2-5:3](bibleref:27005002-27005003)

### Concubines were sometimes called wives
**[GEN 16:3](bibleref:01016003)** *Hagar was Abraham's “wife” only in a secondary sense.*  *See also* [GEN 25:1](bibleref:01025001) *Keturah was one of Abraham's concubines* ; [JDG 19:1-19:4](bibleref:07019001-07019004)

### Concubines did not have the rights of a wife
 [GEN 21:8-21:14](bibleref:01021008-01021014) *They were more easily dismissed* ; [EXO 21:7-21:11](bibleref:02021007-02021011) *They did have some legal protection.* 

### Maidservants were given as concubines
**[GEN 30:3-30:6](bibleref:01030003-01030006)** *Bilhah's sons would be regarded by Rachel as her own and would thus share the inheritance with Jacob's other sons.*  *See also* [GEN 30:9](bibleref:01030009), [GEN 16:1-16:2](bibleref:01016001-01016002); [GAL 4:22-4:23](bibleref:48004022-48004023)

### The tension between concubines and wives
**[GEN 16:4-16:6](bibleref:01016004-01016006)** *Though Abraham's concubine, Hagar's status was still that of servant.*  *See also* [GEN 21:9](bibleref:01021009); [PRO 30:23](bibleref:20030023); [GAL 4:29](bibleref:48004029)

### Children born to concubines were not rightful heirs
**[GEN 21:10-21:12](bibleref:01021010-01021012)** *See also* [GEN 25:5-25:6](bibleref:01025005-01025006); [JDG 9:18](bibleref:07009018); [ROM 9:7](bibleref:45009007); [GAL 4:30](bibleref:48004030); [HEB 11:18](bibleref:58011018)

## Concubines were a symbol of possession


### By taking Jacob's concubine, Reuben laid premature claim to his father's estate
**[GEN 35:22](bibleref:01035022)** *See also* [GEN 49:3-49:4](bibleref:01049003-01049004); [1CH 5:1](bibleref:13005001)

### Taking the concubine(s) of a king was tantamount to laying claim to the kingdom
**[2SA 3:7](bibleref:10003007)** *Abner is accused here of trying to usurp Ish-Bosheth's inheritance.*  *See also* [2SA 12:8](bibleref:10012008), [2SA 16:21-16:22](bibleref:10016021-10016022); [1KI 2:21-2:22](bibleref:11002021-11002022)
