# 5672: concubines

A woman, often a servant or slave, with whom a man had regular sexual relations, but to whom he was not married. A concubine did not have the rights of a wife and her children were not rightful heirs, though a wife might offer a servant to her husband as a concubine to have children on her behalf.



## Concubines and wives


### Concubines were distinguished from wives
**1KI 11:3** *Having concubines alongside wives was accepted in a polygamous society.* *See also* JDG 8:30-8:31; 2SA 5:13, 2SA 19:5; 2CH 11:21; DAN 5:2-5:3

### Concubines were sometimes called wives
**GEN 16:3** *Hagar was Abraham's “wife” only in a secondary sense.* *See also* GEN 25:1 *Keturah was one of Abraham's concubines*; JDG 19:1-19:4

### Concubines did not have the rights of a wife
 GEN 21:8-21:14 *They were more easily dismissed*; ; EXO 21:7-21:11 *They did have some legal protection.*

### Maidservants were given as concubines
**GEN 30:3-30:6** *Bilhah's sons would be regarded by Rachel as her own and would thus share the inheritance with Jacob's other sons.* *See also* GEN 30:9, GEN 16:1-16:2; GAL 4:22-4:23

### The tension between concubines and wives
**GEN 16:4-16:6** *Though Abraham's concubine, Hagar's status was still that of servant.* *See also* GEN 21:9; PRO 30:23; GAL 4:29

### Children born to concubines were not rightful heirs
**GEN 21:10-21:12** *See also* GEN 25:5-25:6; JDG 9:18; ROM 9:7; GAL 4:30; HEB 11:18

## Concubines were a symbol of possession


### By taking Jacob's concubine, Reuben laid premature claim to his father's estate
**GEN 35:22** *See also* GEN 49:3-49:4; 1CH 5:1

### Taking the concubine(s) of a king was tantamount to laying claim to the kingdom
**2SA 3:7** *Abner is accused here of trying to usurp Ish-Bosheth's inheritance.* *See also* 2SA 12:8, 2SA 16:21-16:22; 1KI 2:21-2:22

## Further examples of concubines
 GEN 22:23-22:24 *Nahor's concubine, Reumah*; ; GEN 36:12 *Eliphaz' concubine, Timna*; ; JDG 19:1 *a Levite's concubine*; ; 2SA 15:16 *David's concubines*; ; 1CH 1:32 *Abraham's concubine, Keturah*; ; 1CH 2:46-2:48 *Caleb's concubines, Ephah and Maacah*; ; 1CH 7:14 *Manasseh's concubine*; ; EST 2:14 *Xerxes' concubines*; ; DAN 5:23 *Belshazzar's concubines*
