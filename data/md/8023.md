# 8023: faith, necessity of

A fundamental duty for all people and the necessary response to God's self-revelation. The only channel through which God's blessings may be received, and the only means by which life may be made meaningful, in relationship with God.



## The call to faith


### In the OT
**PSA 37:3-37:5** *See also* PRO 3:5-3:6; ISA 26:4, ISA 50:10

### In the NT
**JHN 6:28-6:29** *See also* MRK 1:15; ACT 16:30-16:31, ACT 19:4, ACT 20:21; ROM 1:5; 1JN 3:23

## God's self-revelation leaves no excuse for unbelief
**JHN 14:8-14:11**; **ROM 10:17-10:18** *See also* PSA 19:4; JHN 1:10-1:12; ROM 1:18-1:21, ROM 3:1-3:4; PSA 51:4; ROM 16:25-16:27

## The need for faith in God


### The LORD is the only true God
**HAB 2:18-2:20** *See also* PSA 115:2-115:11

### God alone can be trusted absolutely
**PSA 9:10** *See also* PSA 91:1-91:4; ISA 12:2; NAM 1:7

### Faith in God is the basis for peace
**ISA 26:3** *See also* PSA 42:11; JHN 14:1; ROM 15:13; 2PE 1:1-1:2

### Faith is necessary to receive God's blessing
**HEB 11:6** *See also* PSA 40:4; JER 17:7-17:8; JHN 5:24

### Faith is necessary to avoid God's judgment
**JHN 3:36** *See also* JHN 3:18; 2TH 2:12; 1PE 2:6-2:8; ISA 28:16; PSA 118:22; ISA 8:14

## Actions not springing from faith are sinful
**ROM 14:23** *See also* ROM 14:5-14:8, ROM 14:14
