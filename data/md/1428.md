# 1428: prophecy, inspiration of OT

The prophecies of biblical messengers are acknowledged as being of divine origin, inspired through the activity of the Holy Spirit.



## God speaks through his prophets
**[2PE 1:20-1:21](bibleref:61001020-61001021)** *See also* [NEH 9:30](bibleref:16009030); [ZEC 7:12](bibleref:38007012); [HEB 1:1](bibleref:58001001)

## God is the source of prophecy
**[EZK 24:20-24:21](bibleref:26024020-26024021)** *The phrase “the word of the LORD came to ...” is frequently used of (and by) the prophets to indicate that the message they proclaimed came directly from God.*  *See also* [1SA 15:10](bibleref:09015010); [2SA 7:4](bibleref:10007004); [1KI 13:20](bibleref:11013020), [1KI 16:1](bibleref:11016001), [1KI 17:2](bibleref:11017002); [2KI 20:4](bibleref:12020004); [JER 1:2](bibleref:24001002); [EZK 6:1](bibleref:26006001); [JON 1:1](bibleref:32001001); [HAG 2:10](bibleref:37002010); [ZEC 1:1](bibleref:38001001)
