# 1655: numbers, hundreds and thousands

The words “hundreds” and, especially, “thousands”, often refer to large quantities in general; they are also used simply in a superlative sense.



## Hundreds


### A hundred in Jesus Christ's teaching
**MAT 13:23** parallel MRK 4:20 *See also* MAT 13:8 parallels MRK 4:8, LUK 8:8; MAT 18:12 parallel LUK 15:4; MAT 18:28; MRK 10:30

### Abraham becomes a father at 100
 GEN 17:17, GEN 21:5; ; ROM 4:19

### People who lived to over 100
 GEN 5:3-5:32, GEN 9:29, GEN 11:10-11:32, GEN 25:7, GEN 25:17, GEN 50:22; ; DEU 34:7; ; JDG 2:8; ; 2CH 24:15; ; JOB 42:16; ; ISA 65:20
