# 5148: ear

The organ of hearing; the ear is referred to in various OT rites and is a symbol of attention, alertness or readiness to hear.

## The ear in the OT rites


### Piercing the ear: a symbol of servanthood
**[PSA 40:6](bibleref:19040006)** *See also* [EXO 21:5-21:6](bibleref:02021005-02021006); [DEU 15:16-15:17](bibleref:05015016-05015017)

### Blood applied to the ear: a symbol of cleansing and ordination
**[LEV 8:23](bibleref:03008023)** *See also* [EXO 29:20](bibleref:02029020); [LEV 14:14](bibleref:03014014)

## God's ear as a symbol of his alertness to humanity
**[PSA 116:1-116:2](bibleref:19116001-19116002)** *See also* [2KI 19:16](bibleref:12019016); [2CH 7:15](bibleref:14007015); [NEH 1:6](bibleref:16001006)[JOB 34:28](bibleref:18034028); [JAS 5:4](bibleref:59005004)
[PSA 5:1](bibleref:19005001), [PSA 31:2](bibleref:19031002), [PSA 34:15](bibleref:19034015), [PSA 88:2](bibleref:19088002), [PSA 130:1-130:2](bibleref:19130001-19130002); [DAN 9:18](bibleref:27009018)

## God's ear may be turned away from humanity
**[PSA 28:1](bibleref:19028001)** *The refusal to turn an ear towards someone was a symbol of refusing to listen.*  *See also* [DEU 1:45](bibleref:05001045); [LAM 3:56](bibleref:25003056); [EZK 8:18](bibleref:26008018) *God's attending ear is partly dependent on human attitudes to him.* 

## The human ear used symbolically


### Human ears should be open to God
**[ISA 50:4-50:5](bibleref:23050004-23050005), [ISA 55:3](bibleref:23055003)** *See also* [JOB 42:5](bibleref:18042005); [PSA 81:8](bibleref:19081008); [ISA 30:21](bibleref:23030021), [ISA 32:3](bibleref:23032003); [MAT 13:9](bibleref:40013009) parallels MRK 4:9, LUK 8:8 *“He who has ears” implies a potential willingness to listen* ; [MAT 13:16](bibleref:40013016); [MRK 9:7](bibleref:41009007); [REV 2:7](bibleref:66002007)

### Human ears may be closed against God
**[ZEC 7:11-7:13](bibleref:38007011-38007013)** *See also* [PSA 58:4-58:5](bibleref:19058004-19058005); [ISA 6:10](bibleref:23006010)

### The human ear should also be open to others
 [ISA 48:8](bibleref:23048008); [JER 6:10](bibleref:24006010); [EZK 12:2](bibleref:26012002); [ACT 7:51](bibleref:44007051), [ACT 7:57](bibleref:44007057); [2TI 4:4](bibleref:55004004)**[PRO 21:13](bibleref:20021013)**
