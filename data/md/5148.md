# 5148: ear

The organ of hearing; the ear is referred to in various OT rites and is a symbol of attention, alertness or readiness to hear.



## The ear in the OT rites


### Piercing the ear: a symbol of servanthood
**PSA 40:6** *See also* EXO 21:5-21:6; DEU 15:16-15:17

### Blood applied to the ear: a symbol of cleansing and ordination
**LEV 8:23** *See also* EXO 29:20; LEV 14:14

## God's ear as a symbol of his alertness to humanity
**PSA 116:1-116:2** *See also* 2KI 19:16; 2CH 7:15; NEH 1:6JOB 34:28; JAS 5:4
PSA 5:1, PSA 31:2, PSA 34:15, PSA 88:2, PSA 130:1-130:2; DAN 9:18

## God's ear may be turned away from humanity
**PSA 28:1** *The refusal to turn an ear towards someone was a symbol of refusing to listen.* *See also* DEU 1:45; LAM 3:56; EZK 8:18 *God's attending ear is partly dependent on human attitudes to him.*

## The human ear used symbolically


### Human ears should be open to God
**ISA 50:4-50:5, ISA 55:3** *See also* JOB 42:5; PSA 81:8; ISA 30:21, ISA 32:3; MAT 13:9 parallels MRK 4:9, LUK 8:8 *“He who has ears” implies a potential willingness to listen*; MAT 13:16; MRK 9:7; REV 2:7

### Human ears may be closed against God
**ZEC 7:11-7:13** *See also* PSA 58:4-58:5; ISA 6:10

### The human ear should also be open to others
 ISA 48:8; ; JER 6:10; ; EZK 12:2; ; ACT 7:51, ACT 7:57; ; 2TI 4:4**PRO 21:13**

## A discerning ear brings knowledge and understanding
 *See also* JOB 12:11, JOB 13:1, JOB 34:3; PRO 18:15
