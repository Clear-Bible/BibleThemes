# 2024: Jesus Christ, glory of

Jesus Christ's radiance and splendour reflects the glory of the Father, and is both revealed to and reflected in the lives of believers.



## Jesus Christ's glory existed before the incarnation
**[JHN 17:5](bibleref:43017005)** *See also* [JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014), [JHN 17:24](bibleref:43017024)

## Jesus Christ's glory is God's glory


### God's glory is reflected in Jesus Christ
**[HEB 1:3](bibleref:58001003)** *See also* [JHN 12:41](bibleref:43012041) *John makes no distinction between God's glory revealed in Isa 6:3 and the glory of Jesus Christ* ; [JHN 13:32](bibleref:43013032); [2CO 4:4](bibleref:47004004), [2CO 4:6](bibleref:47004006)

### Jesus Christ brings glory to his Father
**[JHN 14:13](bibleref:43014013)** *See also* [JHN 13:31](bibleref:43013031), [JHN 17:1](bibleref:43017001), [JHN 17:4](bibleref:43017004); [ROM 16:27](bibleref:45016027); [EPH 1:12](bibleref:49001012); [JUD 1:25](bibleref:65001025)

### The Holy Spirit glorifies Jesus Christ
**[JHN 16:14](bibleref:43016014)**

## Jesus Christ's glory is revealed on earth


### Through his miracles
**[JHN 2:11](bibleref:43002011)** *See also* [JHN 11:4](bibleref:43011004), [JHN 11:40](bibleref:43011040)

### To his disciples
**[LUK 9:28-9:32](bibleref:42009028-42009032)** parallels MAT 17:1-17:2, MRK 9:2-9:3 *See also* [JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014); [ACT 9:3](bibleref:44009003), [ACT 22:6](bibleref:44022006), [ACT 26:13](bibleref:44026013); [2PE 1:17](bibleref:61001017)

### In his death and resurrection
**[JHN 12:23](bibleref:43012023)** *One of the major themes of John's Gospel is that Jesus Christ is glorified through his death and resurrection.*  *See also* [JHN 7:39](bibleref:43007039), [JHN 12:16](bibleref:43012016); [ACT 3:13-3:15](bibleref:44003013-44003015); [1PE 1:21](bibleref:60001021); [HEB 2:9](bibleref:58002009)

## The glory of the exalted Christ


### His appearance
**[REV 1:13-1:16](bibleref:66001013-66001016)** *See also* [ACT 7:55-7:56](bibleref:44007055-44007056); [REV 2:18](bibleref:66002018), [REV 19:11-19:16](bibleref:66019011-66019016)
