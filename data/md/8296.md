# 8296: love, nature of

Scripture offers an understanding of the source, character and value of love, based on the nature and actions of God.



## God is the source of love


### His very character is love
**1JN 4:7-4:8** *See also* 1TH 3:12; 2TI 1:7; 1JN 4:16, 1JN 4:19

### The love of God is revealed in the cross of Jesus Christ
**1JN 3:16, 1JN 4:10** *See also* JHN 15:13; EPH 5:2, EPH 5:25

### Love is part of the Holy Spirit's fruit
**GAL 5:22** *See also* ROM 5:5, ROM 15:30; COL 1:8

## The loving-kindness of God referred to in the OT
**JER 31:3** *See also* EXO 15:13, EXO 34:6; 2CH 6:42; PSA 6:4, PSA 32:10, PSA 51:1, PSA 107:43; ISA 54:10, ISA 63:7; LAM 3:22; HOS 2:19

## The love of God in the NT


### To describe God's love for humanity
**JHN 3:16** *See also* JHN 5:42, JHN 15:10; ROM 8:39; 2CO 13:14; EPH 2:4; HEB 12:6; 1JN 3:1

### To describe love for God
 LUK 10:27; ; JHN 14:21, JHN 21:15; ; ROM 8:28; ; 1TH 1:3; ; JAS 1:12; ; 1PE 1:8; ; 1JN 5:3

### To describe brotherly love
 JHN 13:35; ; ROM 13:8; ; 1CO 16:24; ; 2CO 8:8; ; GAL 5:13; ; EPH 4:2; ; PHP 1:9; ; COL 2:2; ; PHM 1:5

### To describe love expressed by eating together
 2PE 2:13; ; JUD 1:12

### To describe love in a negative sense
 LUK 11:43; ; JHN 3:19, JHN 12:43; ; 2TI 4:10; ; 2PE 2:15; ; 1JN 2:15

## Characteristics of love
**1CO 13:4-13:8** *See also* ROM 12:9; 1TI 1:5; 1PE 1:22; 1JN 4:18

## Love is shown by deeds
**1JN 3:17-3:18** *See also* JHN 14:15, JHN 14:23; ROM 5:8, ROM 14:15; GAL 2:20; 1JN 4:9, 1JN 5:2-5:3; 2JN 1:6; 3JN 1:5-1:6

## The pre-eminence of love
**1CO 13:13** *See also* MAT 22:37-22:39 parallel MRK 12:29-12:31; ROM 13:9-13:10; 1CO 12:31-13:3; GAL 5:6; EPH 3:17-3:19; COL 3:14; 2PE 1:7; 1JN 2:10
