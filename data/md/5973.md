# 5973: unreliability

The quality of a person or thing that cannot be trusted or relied upon. Scripture notes the unreliability of human nature, while stressing the total reliability of God.

## Human nature is unreliable
**[PSA 78:57](bibleref:19078057)** *See also* [ISA 49:15](bibleref:23049015); [HOS 6:4](bibleref:28006004); [2CO 1:9](bibleref:47001009)

### Examples of unreliable people
 [GEN 40:23](bibleref:01040023) *the chief cupbearer* ; [1SA 13:13-13:14](bibleref:09013013-09013014) *King Saul* ; [HOS 5:13](bibleref:28005013) *Israel* [MAT 26:40](bibleref:40026040) parallels MRK 14:37, LUK 22:45; [MAT 26:56](bibleref:40026056) parallel MRK 14:50
