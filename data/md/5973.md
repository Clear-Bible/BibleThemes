# 5973: unreliability

The quality of a person or thing that cannot be trusted or relied upon. Scripture notes the unreliability of human nature, while stressing the total reliability of God.



## Human nature is unreliable
**PSA 78:57** *See also* ISA 49:15; HOS 6:4; 2CO 1:9

### Examples of unreliable people
 GEN 40:23 *the chief cupbearer*; ; 1SA 13:13-13:14 *King Saul*; ; HOS 5:13 *Israel*; MAT 26:40 parallels MRK 14:37, LUK 22:45; ; MAT 26:56 parallel MRK 14:50

### Unreliable people are not to be trusted
**PRO 25:19** *See also* PSA 146:3-146:4; ISA 2:22

## False gods are unreliable
**ISA 45:20** *See also* DEU 4:28; PSA 115:4-115:8; ISA 40:20; JER 10:5, JER 10:10, JER 10:14-10:15, JER 51:17-51:18; DAN 5:23; HAB 2:18; MAT 24:5 parallels MRK 13:6, LUK 21:8

## Human wisdom is unreliable
**1CO 3:19-3:20** *See also* JDG 20:36; JOB 5:13; ISA 30:12-30:13, ISA 59:4; EZK 28:6-28:7; ROM 1:22; 1CO 1:19-1:21, 1CO 1:25; COL 2:23

## Military strength is unreliable
**PSA 44:6** *See also* 1SA 17:45; PSA 33:16-33:17; ISA 31:1, ISA 36:4-36:6; EZK 33:26; HOS 1:7; HAG 2:22; MAT 26:52

## Material wealth is unreliable
**PRO 11:28** *See also* PSA 62:10; JER 49:4; HAG 1:9, HAG 2:16-2:17; ZEC 9:3-9:4; MAT 6:19, MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:19, LUK 8:14; 1TI 6:9-6:10

## The consequences of unreliability


### Unreliable actions let God down
**HOS 11:2-11:3** *See also* ISA 5:7; JER 2:21; MAT 26:34 parallels MRK 14:30, LUK 22:34; LUK 16:10-16:12

### Unreliability leads to disappointment
**JAS 1:7-1:8** *See also* MAT 21:30, MAT 21:43; LUK 16:10-16:12; ACT 15:37-15:38

## Trust in unreliable things


### Leads to self-delusion
**ISA 44:20** *See also* ISA 47:10; LUK 12:20; REV 3:17

### Leads to judgment
**JER 17:5** *See also* PSA 49:13-49:14, PSA 52:5-52:7; ISA 20:5; JER 46:25; AMO 6:1, AMO 6:7

## Unreliable people are judged
**EZK 13:9** *See also* ISA 20:6; MAL 2:7-2:9; MAT 23:38, MAT 25:26-25:27 parallel LUK 19:22-19:23

## God is never unreliable
**NUM 23:19** *See also* 1KI 8:56; PSA 71:6; JHN 8:26; 2CO 1:20; TIT 1:2; HEB 6:18; 1JN 4:13-4:16
