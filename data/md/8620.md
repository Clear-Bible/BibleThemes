# 8620: prayer, practicalities of

Scripture commends a life of prayer, characterised by simplicity of expression, sincerity of heart and trust in the promises of God.  It gives guidance on how, when and where to pray.



## Scripture stresses the importance of prayer
**1TH 5:16-5:18** *See also* ACT 6:3-6:4; ROM 12:12

## Judgment comes on those who do not pray
**PSA 79:6** *See also* PSA 53:4; JER 10:21; ZEP 1:4-1:6; JAS 4:2

## Prayers should be expressed simply
**MAT 6:7-6:8** *See also* ECC 5:1-5:3; LUK 18:9-18:14

## Prayer should not be ostentatious
**MAT 6:5-6:6** *See also* MAT 14:23 parallel MRK 6:46; LUK 5:16

## Physical positions for prayer


### Sitting while praying
**2SA 7:18** *See also* JDG 20:26; NEH 1:4

### Kneeling while praying
**LUK 22:41** *See also* 1KI 8:54; 2CH 6:13; EZR 9:5; ACT 9:40, ACT 21:5; EPH 3:14

### Standing while praying
**1KI 8:22** *See also* 1SA 1:26; MRK 11:25

### Lying prostrate while praying
**2CH 20:18** *See also* GEN 24:52; NUM 20:6

### Praying with arms outstretched
**EXO 9:29**; **ISA 1:15** *See also* 1KI 8:54; 2CH 6:13

### Praying with hands raised
**1TI 2:8** *See also* EXO 9:29; 1KI 8:22, 1KI 8:54; PSA 63:4, PSA 77:1-77:2

## Prayer can be offered at any time


### Praying several times a day
**DAN 6:10** *See also* PSA 55:17, PSA 88:1

### Praying early in the morning
**MRK 1:35** *See also* PSA 5:3, PSA 119:147

### Praying all night
**LUK 6:12** *See also* 1SA 15:11; LUK 2:37

## Prayer is not confined to any single place
**JHN 4:21-4:24**

### Praying inside a building
**DAN 6:10**; **MAT 6:6** *See also* 1KI 8:28-8:30

### Praying outside a building
**MRK 1:35**; **LUK 5:16** *See also* ACT 10:9, ACT 21:5

## Prayer may be accompanied by fasting
**ACT 13:2-13:3** *See also* EZR 8:23; NEH 1:4; PSA 35:13; DAN 9:3; LUK 2:37, LUK 5:33; ACT 14:23; MAT 17:21 parallel MRK 9:29
