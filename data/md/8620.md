# 8620: prayer, practicalities of

Scripture commends a life of prayer, characterised by simplicity of expression, sincerity of heart and trust in the promises of God.  It gives guidance on how, when and where to pray.



## Scripture stresses the importance of prayer
**[1TH 5:16-5:18](bibleref:52005016-52005018)** *See also* [ACT 6:3-6:4](bibleref:44006003-44006004); [ROM 12:12](bibleref:45012012)

**[PSA 79:6](bibleref:19079006)** *See also* [PSA 53:4](bibleref:19053004); [JER 10:21](bibleref:24010021); [ZEP 1:4-1:6](bibleref:36001004-36001006); [JAS 4:2](bibleref:59004002)
