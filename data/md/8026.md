# 8026: faith, growth in

Christians are called to have a growing faith, built up by prayer and the encouragement of others, and also through testing.



## Examples of growing faith
**2TH 1:3** *See also* ACT 16:5; 2CO 10:15; REV 2:19

## Weak faith


### Weak faith rebuked
**MAT 6:28-6:30** parallel LUK 12:27-12:28 *See also* MAT 8:26 parallels MRK 4:40, LUK 8:25; MAT 14:31, MAT 16:8, MAT 17:20

### Other examples of weak faith
 JHN 12:42-12:43, JHN 19:38; ; ROM 14:1-14:2; ; 1CO 8:1-8:13

## Strong faith


### Strong faith commended and encouraged
**HEB 10:22** *See also* MAT 8:10 parallel LUK 7:9; MAT 9:20-9:22 parallels MRK 5:25-5:34, LUK 8:43-8:48; MAT 15:28 parallel MRK 7:29

### The faith of OT leaders
 1KI 18:3 *Obadiah*; ; 2KI 18:5 parallel 2CH 31:20 *Hezekiah*

### The faith of Christian leaders
**1TI 4:12**; **HEB 13:7** *See also* ACT 6:5 *Stephen*; ACT 11:24 *Barnabas*; 2TI 3:10 *Paul*1TI 6:11; 2TI 2:22

### Other examples of strong faith
**HAB 3:17-3:18** *See also* JOB 13:15, JOB 19:25-19:27; MAT 14:35-14:36 parallel MRK 6:56; 2CO 8:7; COL 2:5-2:7

## The growth of faith


### Praying for more faith
**LUK 17:5** *See also* MRK 9:24

### The encouragement of others
**2CH 32:7-32:8**; **1TH 3:10** *See also* NUM 14:6-14:9; LUK 22:32; ACT 14:22; ROM 1:11-1:12; 1TH 3:2-3:3; JUD 1:20

**ROM 4:18-4:21** *See also* GEN 15:5; JAS 1:2-1:4; 1PE 1:6-1:7
