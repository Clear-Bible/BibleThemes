# 1454: theophany

A temporary visible manifestation of the presence and glory of God. This may be in natural phenomena such as cloud or fire, in human form or in prophetic visionary experience.



## God is manifested in nature


### God's presence in storms, thunder and lightning
**PSA 18:7-18:15**; **EXO 19:16** *See also* EXO 20:18; JOB 37:5; PSA 29:3-29:9, PSA 77:18, PSA 97:4; ISA 30:27-30:33; AMO 1:2; HAB 3:11; ZEC 9:14; REV 11:19

### God's presence in volcanic phenomena
**EXO 19:18** *See also* ISA 30:33

### God's presence in earthquakes
**ISA 29:5-29:6** *See also* JDG 5:4-5:5; PSA 77:18; HAB 3:6

## Specific phenomena associated with the presence of God


### Fire signifies God's presence
**EXO 3:2** *Fire in particular represents the purity, holiness and unapproachability of God.* *See also* EXO 13:21, EXO 19:18, EXO 24:17; LEV 9:24; NUM 14:14; DEU 4:11-4:12, DEU 5:4, DEU 5:22-5:26; JDG 13:20-13:22; PSA 97:3; JOL 2:30

### Smoke signifies God's presence
**EXO 19:18** *See also* EXO 20:18; PSA 144:5; ISA 6:4, ISA 30:27; JOL 2:30; REV 15:8

### Cloud signifies God's presence
**EXO 16:10** *Cloud and smoke convey the mystery and transcendence of God.* *See also* EXO 13:21EXO 19:9, EXO 24:15-24:16, EXO 33:9, EXO 34:5; DEU 31:15
LEV 16:2; NUM 9:15-9:22, NUM 14:14; 1KI 8:10-8:11; EZK 1:4, EZK 10:3-10:4MAT 17:5 parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:34-9:35
REV 14:14-14:16

## God is manifested in human or angelic form
**GEN 16:7-16:13** *See also* GEN 18:1-18:22, GEN 32:24-32:30; JOS 5:13-5:15

## God appears in prophetic visions


### God appears on a throne
**ISA 6:1** *John interprets this verse to refer to Jesus Christ (Jn 12:41).* *See also* EZK 1:26, EZK 10:1; DAN 7:9; REV 4:2, REV 20:11

### God appears attended by angels and other heavenly beings
 ISA 6:2; ; EZK 1:5-1:18, EZK 10:9-10:13; ; REV 4:6-4:11

### God appears like, or with, precious stones
 EXO 24:10; ; EZK 1:26; ; REV 4:3
