# 8715: dishonesty, and God's nature

Dishonesty is contrary to the will and nature of God, and so should not be found among the people of God.



## Dishonesty is contrary to the will and nature of God
**NUM 23:19** *See also* 1SA 15:29; ROM 3:4; TIT 1:2

### Dishonesty, like sin, is of satanic origin
**JHN 8:44** *See also* GEN 3:1-3:5

## God's hatred of dishonesty
**PRO 12:22** *See also* PSA 5:4-5:6; PRO 6:16-6:17; ISA 59:1-59:4, ISA 59:12-59:15

## Kinds of dishonesty


### In business deals
**HOS 12:7** *See also* DEU 25:13-25:16; PRO 20:14; AMO 8:5; MIC 6:10

### In not paying debts
**PSA 37:21** *See also* JAS 5:4

### In exploiting the poor
**EZK 22:29** *See also* JOB 24:2-24:11; ISA 32:7; JER 22:13

## God's judgment on dishonesty


### God's denunciation of dishonesty in general
**HOS 4:1-4:2** *See also* LEV 19:15; JER 7:8-7:10; 1TH 4:6

### The dishonesty of liars and false witnesses
**PRO 19:9**; **ACT 5:1-5:10** *See also* DEU 19:16-19:19; JOS 7:10-7:12 *regarding Achan's sin*; 2KI 5:19-5:27; PSA 5:6; ISA 29:20-29:21; JER 9:4-9:9; REV 21:8

### The dishonesty of false prophets and teachers
 JER 14:14-14:15; ; 2PE 2:1-2:3; ; ISA 9:14-9:15; ; JER 28:15-28:16; ; EZK 13:1-13:23; ; ZEC 10:2-10:3; ; 1TI 4:1-4:2

## God's demands for making amends for dishonesty
 LEV 6:1-6:7; ; LUK 3:12-3:14 *This demonstrated that their repentance was genuine*; ; LUK 19:8 *the result of encountering Jesus Christ*; ; LUK 16:1-16:12 *Jesus Christ was not commending the steward's dishonesty but his shrewdness.*
