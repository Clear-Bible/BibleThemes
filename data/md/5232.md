# 5232: book

Used most frequently to refer to a particular book of the Bible being written, another book of the Bible or a source no longer available. It is used occasionally of books in general or metaphorically of God's record of events.

## References to books being written
 [JER 25:13](bibleref:24025013), [JER 30:2](bibleref:24030002); [NAM 1:1](bibleref:34001001); [JHN 20:30](bibleref:43020030); [REV 22:7](bibleref:66022007), [REV 22:9-22:10](bibleref:66022009-66022010), [REV 22:18-22:19](bibleref:66022018-66022019)

## References to other books of Scripture
 [2CH 35:12](bibleref:14035012); [EZR 6:18](bibleref:15006018); [NEH 13:1](bibleref:16013001); [MRK 12:26](bibleref:41012026); [LUK 3:4](bibleref:42003004), [LUK 20:42](bibleref:42020042); [ACT 1:1](bibleref:44001001) *Luke's Gospel* ; [ACT 1:20](bibleref:44001020), [ACT 7:42](bibleref:44007042), [ACT 8:28](bibleref:44008028)

## References to books no longer available


### The Book of the Wars of the LORD
 [NUM 21:14](bibleref:04021014) *This was probably an ancient collection of war songs in praise of God.* 

### The Book of Jashar
**[JOS 10:13](bibleref:06010013)** *An early account of Israel's wars, possibly in poetic form, but never a part of canonical Scripture.*  *See also* [2SA 1:18](bibleref:10001018)

### The book of the annals of the kings of Israel and Judah
**[2CH 16:11](bibleref:14016011)** *These sources were probably records of the reigns of the kings of Israel and Judah, compiled by the succession of Israel's prophets spanning the kingdom period (see e.g., 1Ch 29:29; 2Ch 12:15; 20:34; 26:22; 32:32).*  *See also* [1KI 11:41](bibleref:11011041), [1KI 14:19](bibleref:11014019), [1KI 14:29](bibleref:11014029); [2KI 1:18](bibleref:12001018), [2KI 8:23](bibleref:12008023); [1CH 9:1](bibleref:13009001), [1CH 27:24](bibleref:13027024); [2CH 24:27](bibleref:14024027), [2CH 27:7](bibleref:14027007)

### The book of the annals
 [NEH 12:23](bibleref:16012023) *This was probably the official temple chronicle, containing various lists and records (compare with Ne 7:5).* [EST 2:23](bibleref:17002023), [EST 6:1](bibleref:17006001), [EST 10:2](bibleref:17010002)
