# 8310: morality, and creation

Human beings have been created moral creatures in the likeness of God, who is the source and standard of all that is good.



## Morality is grounded in the nature of God himself
**[DEU 32:4](bibleref:05032004)**; **[LUK 18:18-18:19](bibleref:42018018-42018019)** *See also* [EXO 33:19](bibleref:02033019), [EXO 34:5-34:7](bibleref:02034005-02034007); [PSA 25:8-25:9](bibleref:19025008-19025009), [PSA 119:68](bibleref:19119068)

## Human beings are morally aware


### They are created in God's image
 [GEN 1:26-1:27](bibleref:01001026-01001027)

### They have a conscience
**[ROM 2:14-2:15](bibleref:45002014-45002015)** *See also* [2CO 4:2](bibleref:47004002)

### They have been given a moral law
 [GEN 2:16-2:17](bibleref:01002016-01002017); [MRK 10:6-10:9](bibleref:41010006-41010009); [GEN 2:23-2:24](bibleref:01002023-01002024) *Standards for marriage are implicit in the creation.* 

### They are capable of sharing moral ideals
 [JHN 1:9](bibleref:43001009); [ROM 13:1-13:7](bibleref:45013001-45013007); [EPH 3:15](bibleref:49003015) *“whole family” is literally “all fatherhood”, implying that every understanding of fatherhood and family is derived from God* ; [PHP 4:8](bibleref:50004008) *refering to pagan virtues* ; [TIT 3:1](bibleref:56003001); [1PE 2:13-2:14](bibleref:60002013-60002014)

### Immoral acts are unnatural
 [ROM 1:26-1:27](bibleref:45001026-45001027)

### God's judgment implies moral responsibility
 [JER 17:10](bibleref:24017010); [EZK 18:20-18:21](bibleref:26018020-26018021); [AMO 1:6](bibleref:30001006); [ROM 2:5-2:11](bibleref:45002005-45002011)
