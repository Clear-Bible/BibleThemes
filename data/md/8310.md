# 8310: morality, and creation

Human beings have been created moral creatures in the likeness of God, who is the source and standard of all that is good.



## Morality is grounded in the nature of God himself
**DEU 32:4**; **LUK 18:18-18:19** *See also* EXO 33:19, EXO 34:5-34:7; PSA 25:8-25:9, PSA 119:68

## Human beings are morally aware


### They are created in God's image
 GEN 1:26-1:27

### They have a conscience
**ROM 2:14-2:15** *See also* 2CO 4:2

### They have been given a moral law
 GEN 2:16-2:17; ; MRK 10:6-10:9; ; GEN 2:23-2:24 *Standards for marriage are implicit in the creation.*

### They are capable of sharing moral ideals
 JHN 1:9; ; ROM 13:1-13:7; ; EPH 3:15 *“whole family” is literally “all fatherhood”, implying that every understanding of fatherhood and family is derived from God*; ; PHP 4:8 *refering to pagan virtues*; ; TIT 3:1; ; 1PE 2:13-2:14

### Immoral acts are unnatural
 ROM 1:26-1:27

### God's judgment implies moral responsibility
 JER 17:10; ; EZK 18:20-18:21; ; AMO 1:6; ; ROM 2:5-2:11

## Human beings are morally flawed
**ROM 3:23** *See also* PSA 64:6; ISA 32:6; JER 17:9; ROM 1:28-1:32, ROM 8:7; 1TI 4:2
