# 5624: witnesses, to Jesus Christ

The NT identifies a variety of witnesses to Jesus Christ, who confirm his identity and importance. It also stresses the importance of believers bearing witness to Christ in the world.



## The witness of the OT
**ACT 26:22-26:23** *See also* LUK 24:27; JHN 5:39-5:40; HEB 12:1-12:2

## The witness of John the Baptist
**JHN 1:6-1:8** *See also* MAT 3:1-3:3; ISA 40:3; MAT 3:11-3:12 parallels MRK 1:7-1:8, LUK 3:15-3:18; JHN 1:15, JHN 1:19-1:31, JHN 3:26-3:30, JHN 5:33-5:35

## The witness of the Holy Spirit
**JHN 15:26** *See also* ACT 5:32; ROM 8:16; 1JN 5:6-5:9

## The witness of the Father
**JHN 8:18** *See also* MAT 3:17 parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22; MAT 17:5 parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; JHN 5:37-5:38

## The witness of Jesus Christ to himself
**JHN 5:36, JHN 8:14** *See also* JHN 13:13, JHN 14:11

## The witness of Jesus Christ's miracles
**JHN 14:11** *See also* JHN 10:25, JHN 15:24
