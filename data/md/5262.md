# 5262: commendation

The act of speaking favourably of someone. Scripture commends those who are faithful and obedient to God.



## Commendation as speaking favourably of others
**PRO 15:2** *See also* PSA 145:4; ECC 8:15

## Examples of those commended by God
**MAT 26:10**; **LUK 7:9** *See also* MAT 15:28 *a Canaanite woman*; MAT 16:17-16:18 *Simon Peter*; LUK 21:3 *a poor widow*; HEB 11:4 *Abel*; HEB 11:5 *Enoch*

## Commendation for obedience and faith
**PSA 40:4**; **HEB 11:1-11:2** *See also* PSA 119:1; PRO 16:20; ISA 56:2; JER 17:7; MAT 25:34; ROM 14:18; 2CO 8:18; HEB 11:39

## Commendation for perseverance
**ISA 30:18** *See also* JAS 1:12, JAS 5:11; 1PE 2:19-2:20

## Examples of human commendation of others
**2SA 14:25**; **2CO 10:18** *See also* GEN 12:15, GEN 49:8; NEH 11:2; EST 2:17; JOB 29:11; PRO 12:8, PRO 27:2, PRO 27:21, PRO 28:4, PRO 31:28, PRO 31:30-31:31; ECC 8:10; SNG 6:9; DAN 10:11; LUK 16:8; JHN 5:41, JHN 5:44, JHN 12:43; ACT 18:27; ROM 2:28, ROM 13:3, ROM 16:1; 1CO 11:2, 1CO 11:17, 1CO 11:22; 2CO 3:1, 2CO 12:11; 1TH 2:6; 1PE 2:14

## Self-commendation is to be avoided
**2CO 5:12** *See also* 2CO 6:4-6:10
