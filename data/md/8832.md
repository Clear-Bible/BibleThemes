# 8832: testing

God allows his people to pass through certain experiences, so that he may see, and prove to them, that their faith in him is genuine. Believers sometimes doubt God and test him, to see if he is true to his word, but this is forbidden in Scripture.



## God tests his people
**JAS 1:2-1:3**; **1PE 1:7** *See also* JOB 7:17-7:18, JOB 23:10; PSA 11:5; ISA 1:25; ZEC 13:9; MAL 3:3; JAS 1:12

## Examples of God testing his people collectively
**EXO 16:4**; **DEU 8:2-8:3** *See also* EXO 20:20 *at the giving of the law*; DEU 8:16 *over the gathering of manna*; DEU 13:3-13:4 *by their attitude to a false prophet*; JDG 2:20-3:4 *by leaving nations in the promised land*

## Examples of God testing his people individually
**GEN 22:1-22:2** *See also* JDG 7:4-7:7 *Gideon*; 2CH 32:31 *Hezekiah*; 2CO 12:7-12:9 *Paul*

## Jesus Christ tested the faith of people when he was on earth
**JHN 6:5-6:6** *See also* MAT 15:22-15:28 parallel MRK 7:25-7:30 *a Canaanite woman*; MRK 8:17-8:21 *his disciples*

## Believers have a responsibility to test the quality of their lives
**ROM 12:2** *See also* PSA 17:3PSA 26:2, PSA 139:23
LAM 3:40; 2CO 13:5; GAL 6:4; 1TH 5:21

## God will make a final test of believers' lives and service
**1CO 3:12-3:15**

## People may test God as an expression of faith and obedience
**MAL 3:10** *See also* JDG 6:39

## People must not test God as an expression of doubt and disobedience
**EXO 17:2** *See also* EXO 17:7; NUM 14:22-14:23; DEU 6:16, DEU 33:8; PSA 78:18, PSA 78:41, PSA 78:56, PSA 95:8; MAT 4:7 parallel LUK 4:12; DEU 6:16; MAT 19:3 parallel MRK 10:2; ACT 5:9, ACT 15:10; HEB 3:8-3:9

## The command to test the spirits
**1JN 4:1**
