# 5513: sceptre

A staff, generally ornate, held as a symbol of sovereign authority.



## God's own authority is symbolised as a sceptre
**PSA 45:6** *See also* GEN 49:10; NUM 24:17; PSA 60:7 parallel PSA 108:8; ISA 30:31

## The sceptre is a symbol of Jesus Christ's reign
**HEB 1:8** *See also* PSA 45:6; GEN 49:10; NUM 24:17; PSA 110:2; REV 2:27; PSA 2:9; REV 19:15, REV 12:5

## Earthly rulers had sceptres as symbols of authority


### Their description
**EST 4:11**; **EZK 19:11** *See also* EST 5:2, EST 8:4; EZK 19:14

### OT examples
**NUM 21:18** *See also* PSA 125:3; ISA 14:5; JER 48:17; EZK 19:11, EZK 19:14, EZK 21:10, EZK 21:13; AMO 1:5, AMO 1:8; ZEC 10:11

## The extension of a ruler's sceptre to one of his subjects was a sign of favour
**EST 5:2** *See also* EST 4:11, EST 8:4

## Jesus Christ's tormentors gave him a mock sceptre
**MAT 27:29**
