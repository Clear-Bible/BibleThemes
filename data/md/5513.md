# 5513: sceptre

A staff, generally ornate, held as a symbol of sovereign authority.

## God's own authority is symbolised as a sceptre
**[PSA 45:6](bibleref:19045006)** *See also* [GEN 49:10](bibleref:01049010); [NUM 24:17](bibleref:04024017); [PSA 60:7](bibleref:19060007) parallel PSA 108:8; [ISA 30:31](bibleref:23030031)

## The sceptre is a symbol of Jesus Christ's reign
**[HEB 1:8](bibleref:58001008)** *See also* [PSA 45:6](bibleref:19045006); [GEN 49:10](bibleref:01049010); [NUM 24:17](bibleref:04024017); [PSA 110:2](bibleref:19110002); [REV 2:27](bibleref:66002027); [PSA 2:9](bibleref:19002009); [REV 19:15](bibleref:66019015), [REV 12:5](bibleref:66012005)
