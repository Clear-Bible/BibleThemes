# 8801: presumption

A form of self-confidence, which makes overconfident assumptions concerning one's importance and rights. It is criticised as a form of arrogance that is unacceptable among believers, whose lives should be characterised by humility.

## Presumption is unacceptable
**[PSA 5:5](bibleref:19005005)**; **[ISA 13:11](bibleref:23013011)** *See also* [NUM 15:30](bibleref:04015030); [DEU 6:16](bibleref:05006016), [DEU 18:20](bibleref:05018020); [ISA 45:9](bibleref:23045009); [ROM 2:4](bibleref:45002004), [ROM 9:20](bibleref:45009020); [1CO 10:9](bibleref:46010009); [JAS 4:13](bibleref:59004013); [2PE 2:10](bibleref:61002010)

## Examples of presumption
 [GEN 11:1-11:4](bibleref:01011001-01011004) *those preparing to build the tower of Babel in defiance of God* ; [NUM 14:40-14:45](bibleref:04014040-04014045) *the Israelites, ignoring Moses' orders* ; [LUK 12:16-12:21](bibleref:42012016-42012021) *the rich fool* 

## Humility is commended in place of presumption
