# 8801: presumption

A form of self-confidence, which makes overconfident assumptions concerning one's importance and rights. It is criticised as a form of arrogance that is unacceptable among believers, whose lives should be characterised by humility.



## Presumption is unacceptable
**PSA 5:5**; **ISA 13:11** *See also* NUM 15:30; DEU 6:16, DEU 18:20; ISA 45:9; ROM 2:4, ROM 9:20; 1CO 10:9; JAS 4:13; 2PE 2:10

## Examples of presumption
 GEN 11:1-11:4 *those preparing to build the tower of Babel in defiance of God*; ; NUM 14:40-14:45 *the Israelites, ignoring Moses' orders*; ; LUK 12:16-12:21 *the rich fool*

## Humility is commended in place of presumption
**MIC 6:8** *See also* LUK 14:10, LUK 22:26; ROM 12:3; JAS 4:10; 1PE 5:5
