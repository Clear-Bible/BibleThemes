# 1355: providence

The continuing and often unseen activity of God in sustaining his universe, providing for the needs of every creature, and preparing for the completion of his eternal purposes.



## God's general providence


### God sustains the created order
**GEN 8:22** *A promise given by God to all peoples.* *See also* NEH 9:6; ISA 40:26; COL 1:17; HEB 1:3

### All life is dependent on God
**1TI 6:13** *See also* 1SA 1:27; JOB 1:21; PSA 127:3; ECC 3:2, ECC 9:9; EZK 24:16; DAN 5:26; MAT 4:4, MAT 10:29

### God controls the elements
**PSA 147:8** *See also* JOB 37:1-37:13; PSA 29:3-29:9, PSA 135:6-135:7; MAT 5:45; ACT 17:25-17:28

### God provides for the created world
**PSA 145:15-145:16** *See also* JOB 38:39-38:41; PSA 104:27-104:28, PSA 136:25, PSA 147:9; LUK 12:6-12:7; ACT 14:17; 1TI 6:17

## God's providence through miraculous means
**JOB 5:9-5:10** *God's regular activity in providing for human beings is regarded as being miraculous in just the same way as special demonstrations of his power.* *See also* EXO 16:11-16:14; NUM 16:28-16:35; JDG 15:18-15:19; 1KI 17:5-17:6; 2KI 4:42-4:44

## God's providence in human history


### God's control of human intentions
**HAB 1:12** *See also* 1KI 22:19-22:20; 2KI 19:27-19:28 parallel ISA 37:28-37:29; ISA 10:15; HAB 1:6

### God's providential actions on behalf of individuals
**ROM 8:28** *See also* JOB 1:12, JOB 2:6; PSA 107:12-107:14, PSA 107:24-107:29, PSA 107:33-107:38; ISA 38:17

### God's saving purposes fulfilled through providence
**GEN 50:20** *See also* GEN 22:13, GEN 45:5-45:8; 2CH 36:22-36:23 parallel EZR 1:1-1:3; EZR 6:14; ISA 44:28-45:1; ACT 2:23, ACT 4:27-4:28; GAL 4:4-4:5

## God's providence prepares for the completion of his ultimate purpose


### God is forming a people for himself
**REV 21:3** *See also* EXO 6:7; JER 31:33; 2CO 6:16; EPH 2:14-2:16; 1PE 1:3-1:5; REV 7:9

### God will bring all things under Jesus Christ's authority
**EPH 1:9-1:10** *See also* ISA 45:22-45:23, ISA 66:23; 1CO 15:24-15:26; PHP 2:10-2:11; COL 1:20; REV 11:15

### God will complete his purpose for creation
**ROM 8:20-8:21** *See also* ISA 65:17, ISA 66:22; 2PE 3:13; REV 21:1

### God directs all things for his glory
**ROM 11:36** *See also* PSA 46:10; ROM 9:23, ROM 11:36; EPH 1:4-1:6, EPH 1:11-1:12
