# 5825: cruelty, God's attitude to

God condemns cruelty. As part of the work of redemption, God suffers cruelty from the world in order to redeem believers.



## God reproves cruelty
**DEU 23:15-23:16**; **PRO 14:31**; **ISA 10:1-10:2**; **EZK 45:9** *See also* EXO 22:21, EXO 23:9; LEV 25:42-25:43, LEV 25:46, LEV 25:53; PRO 12:10; ISA 13:11; JER 22:3; ZEC 7:10

## God allows cruelty as part of his judgment
**ISA 13:9**; **JER 30:14**; **LAM 2:17** *See also* DEU 28:15-28:42 *The LORD promises cruel judgment on those who disobey his laws*; ISA 19:4; JER 6:22-6:23, JER 13:14, JER 20:16; LAM 2:2, LAM 2:21; HAB 1:5-2:1

## God delivers from cruelty
**EXO 3:7-3:9**; **NEH 9:27**; **PSA 12:5** *See also* JDG 2:18, JDG 6:9; 1SA 10:18; 2SA 7:10; PSA 72:4, PSA 72:14; ISA 9:4, ISA 25:4-25:5, ISA 29:20
