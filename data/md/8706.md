# 8706: apostasy, warnings against personal

It is possible for individuals to fall away from faith or turn against Jesus Christ. Scripture identifies this danger and warns against its negative consequences.

**[HEB 6:4-6:8](bibleref:58006004-58006008)** *See also* [MAT 13:20-13:21](bibleref:40013020-40013021) parallels MRK 4:16-4:17, LUK 8:13; [MAT 24:10-24:12](bibleref:40024010-40024012); [GAL 1:6](bibleref:48001006), [GAL 5:4](bibleref:48005004); [2TH 2:3](bibleref:53002003); [2TI 4:3-4:4](bibleref:55004003-55004004); [1TI 4:1](bibleref:54004001); [1JN 2:19](bibleref:62002019), [1JN 5:16](bibleref:62005016)
