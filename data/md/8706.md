# 8706: apostasy, warnings against personal

It is possible for individuals to fall away from faith or turn against Jesus Christ. Scripture identifies this danger and warns against its negative consequences.



## Personal apostasy described
**HEB 6:4-6:8** *See also* MAT 13:20-13:21 parallels MRK 4:16-4:17, LUK 8:13; MAT 24:10-24:12; GAL 1:6, GAL 5:4; 2TH 2:3; 2TI 4:3-4:4; 1TI 4:1; 1JN 2:19, 1JN 5:16

**HEB 10:26-10:31** *See also* MAT 10:33 parallels MRK 8:38, LUK 12:9; JHN 15:6; 2TI 2:12; HEB 3:12; 2PE 3:17
