# 5015: heart, and Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit comes to dwell within believers, renewing them and directing their hearts towards a greater love for God.



## The renewal of the heart by the Holy Spirit
**PSA 51:10**; **EZK 36:26** *See also* PSA 51:12-51:17; ISA 57:15; JER 24:7; EZK 18:31

## The heart as the residence of the Holy Spirit
**ROM 8:9** *See also* JHN 7:37-7:39; ROM 5:5; 2CO 1:22; GAL 4:6; EPH 3:16-3:17; COL 1:27

## The heart bears the fruit of the Spirit
**GAL 5:22-5:23**

### Love for God
**MRK 12:30** parallels MAT 22:37, LUK 10:27 *See also* EXO 25:2; DEU 6:5, DEU 11:13, DEU 30:6; 1KI 15:14

### Faithfulness to God
**PSA 44:18** *See also* 1KI 2:4; 1CH 29:18; NEH 9:8; ACT 11:23

## Other results of the Holy Spirit's indwelling of the heart


### Heartfelt worship of God
**COL 3:16** *See also* 1SA 2:1; 2CH 11:16, 2CH 29:31; JOB 11:13; PSA 9:1, PSA 33:21, PSA 86:12, PSA 138:1; ROM 15:6; EPH 5:18-5:19; PHP 3:3

### Heartfelt prayer to God
**PSA 119:145** *See also* GEN 24:45; 1SA 1:13; 1CH 22:19; PSA 21:2, PSA 27:8, PSA 63:1, PSA 84:2; ROM 8:26-8:27

### Wholehearted obedience to God
**PSA 119:34** *See also* DEU 26:16, DEU 30:14-30:17, DEU 32:46; 1KI 14:8; 2KI 23:3, 2KI 23:25; NEH 2:12; PSA 40:8, PSA 119:2, PSA 119:69, PSA 119:112; ROM 6:17

### Wholehearted trust in God
**PRO 3:5-3:6** *See also* PSA 62:8; MRK 11:23; LUK 24:25; HEB 10:22

### Wholehearted service for God
**ROM 1:9** *See also* DEU 10:12; JOS 22:5; 1SA 12:20, 1SA 12:24
