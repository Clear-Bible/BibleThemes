# 5310: exploitation

Taking advantage of weak and vulnerable groups within society, especially financially; a practice that is prohibited in Scripture. Rather, such groups of people should be treated with special care and concern.

## Exploitation is forbidden
**[PRO 22:23](bibleref:20022023)** *See also* [JOB 22:5-22:9](bibleref:18022005-18022009); [PRO 23:10](bibleref:20023010); [ISA 5:8](bibleref:23005008), [ISA 59:12-59:15](bibleref:23059012-23059015); [EZK 45:9-45:10](bibleref:26045009-26045010); [MIC 2:1-2:3](bibleref:33002001-33002003); [ZEC 7:10](bibleref:38007010)
