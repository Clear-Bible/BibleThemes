# 5292: defence, divine

God defends his people, especially the most vulnerable. Because of God's defence, although Christians sometimes face trials and persecutions their eternal security is never threatened.



## God defends his people
**ISA 41:10-41:12** *See also* EXO 23:20, EXO 23:27; 1SA 2:9; 2KI 6:16-6:17; PSA 34:7, PSA 144:10; ISA 19:20, ISA 51:16, ISA 51:22, ISA 54:16-54:17; JER 50:34, JER 51:36; ZEC 9:8; MAT 16:18; JHN 10:28-10:29; 2TI 4:16-4:18

## God defends especially the most vulnerable of his people
**PSA 10:17-10:18** *See also* DEU 10:17-10:18; PSA 68:5, PSA 146:9; PRO 23:10-23:11

## God defended the city of Jerusalem
**2KI 19:32-19:34** parallel ISA 37:33-37:35 *See also* 2KI 20:6 parallel ISA 38:6; PSA 48:3; ISA 31:5; ZEC 2:4-2:5, ZEC 12:8

## God's defence of his people is pictured in various ways


### As a hiding-place
**PSA 32:7** *See also* PSA 17:8, PSA 27:5, PSA 31:20

### As a refuge
**PSA 57:1** *See also* DEU 33:27; PSA 46:1; PRO 14:26, PRO 18:10; JOL 3:16; NAM 1:7

### As a shield
**PSA 115:9-115:11** *See also* GEN 15:1; PSA 84:11, PSA 119:114; PRO 30:5

### As a combination of images
**2SA 22:1-22:4** parallel PSA 18:2-18:3 *See also* PSA 71:3, PSA 91:1-91:4, PSA 144:1-144:2; ISA 25:4

## The most reliable defence for God's people is to trust only in him
**ISA 31:1** *See also* 2CH 16:7-16:9; PSA 20:6-20:8, PSA 37:16-37:19, PSA 125:1-125:2; ISA 17:7-17:8; DAN 3:16-3:18; HOS 14:3
