# 8829: superstition

Misplaced credulity concerning the supernatural, which leads to irrational fear, misdirected reverence, false religion and magic, and which brings God's judgment. Scripture warns against and condemns such things.



## Superstition causing irrational fear
**JER 10:2** *See also* JOB 15:20-15:24, JOB 18:11

### Fear of Jesus Christ or his disciples
**MAT 14:26-14:27** parallel MRK 6:49-6:50 *See also* MAT 14:2 parallel MRK 6:14; ACT 5:13

### Fear of other gods
**1KI 20:23** *See also* 1KI 18:28, 1KI 20:28; ACT 17:23

## Misunderstanding God's work
**ACT 8:18-8:19** *See also* LUK 24:36-24:37; ACT 12:15, ACT 19:13 *invoking the name of Jesus Christ for exorcism as though it were a magic formula*; ACT 28:3-28:4

## Superstition leading to misdirected reverence
**ACT 14:11-14:13** *See also* ACT 28:5-28:6; REV 22:8-22:9

## False religion
**JDG 10:6-10:7** *See also* 1SA 7:3-7:4; 1KI 13:33, 1KI 14:15-14:16, 1KI 14:22-14:24; 2CH 28:2-28:4 parallel 2KI 16:3-16:4; JER 9:14, JER 44:17-44:18; HOS 11:2

## Unhelpful speculations
**1TI 1:3-1:4** *See also* ISA 65:2-65:3, ISA 66:17-66:18; EZK 13:2, EZK 13:17; 1TI 6:3-6:4

## The true demonic source of superstition
**1TI 4:1** *See also* REV 9:20-9:21
