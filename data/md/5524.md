# 5524: servants, bad

Bad servants are characterised by disobedience, idleness and dishonesty.



## Characteristics of bad servants


### Idleness
**2TH 3:11** *See also* PRO 18:9; MAT 25:24-25:30 parallel LUK 19:20-19:26

### Dishonesty
**LUK 16:12** *See also* GEN 21:25; LEV 6:2; 2KI 5:20-5:25; EPH 6:6; COL 3:22

### Disloyalty
**LUK 16:13** *See also* 2SA 19:24-19:27; JHN 10:13; ACT 27:30

### Quarrelsomeness
**MAT 24:48-24:49** *See also* GEN 13:7, GEN 26:20; MAT 18:23-18:30

### Discontentment
**LUK 3:14** *See also* MAT 20:9-20:15

### Refusing correction
 PRO 29:19

## Examples of bad servants

 2SA 16:1-16:4, 2SA 19:26-19:29;
