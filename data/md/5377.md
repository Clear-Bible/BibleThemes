# 5377: law, Ten Commandments

The basic laws given to Israel through Moses following the exodus from Egypt (Ex 20:1-17; Dt 5:6-21). The first four commandments safeguard Israel's special relation to God; the remaining six protect individuals within the community and promote their well-being.



## The Ten Commandments are to govern the life of Israel as the people of God


### Israel should obey God alone
**[EXO 20:2-20:3](bibleref:02020002-02020003)** parallel DEU 5:6-5:7 *See also* [DEU 6:13-6:15](bibleref:05006013-05006015)

**[EXO 20:4-20:6](bibleref:02020004-02020006)** parallel DEU 5:8-5:10 *See also* [EXO 32:1-32:8](bibleref:02032001-02032008); [LEV 19:4](bibleref:03019004); [1CO 10:7](bibleref:46010007)

### God's name should not be misused
**[EXO 20:7](bibleref:02020007)** parallel DEU 5:11 *See also* [MAT 7:21](bibleref:40007021)

### A day of rest is commanded
**[EXO 20:8-20:11](bibleref:02020008-02020011)** parallel DEU 5:12-5:15 *See also* [EXO 16:23](bibleref:02016023); [LEV 19:3](bibleref:03019003); [ISA 56:2](bibleref:23056002); [JER 17:21-17:22](bibleref:24017021-24017022)
