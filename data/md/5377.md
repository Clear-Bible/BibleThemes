# 5377: law, Ten Commandments

The basic laws given to Israel through Moses following the exodus from Egypt (Ex 20:1-17; Dt 5:6-21). The first four commandments safeguard Israel's special relation to God; the remaining six protect individuals within the community and promote their well-being.



## The Ten Commandments are to govern the life of Israel as the people of God


### Israel should obey God alone
**EXO 20:2-20:3** parallel DEU 5:6-5:7 *See also* DEU 6:13-6:15

### Idolatry forbidden
**EXO 20:4-20:6** parallel DEU 5:8-5:10 *See also* EXO 32:1-32:8; LEV 19:4; 1CO 10:7

### God's name should not be misused
**EXO 20:7** parallel DEU 5:11 *See also* MAT 7:21

### A day of rest is commanded
**EXO 20:8-20:11** parallel DEU 5:12-5:15 *See also* EXO 16:23; LEV 19:3; ISA 56:2; JER 17:21-17:22

### Parents are to be honoured
**EXO 20:12** parallel DEU 5:16 *See also* MAT 15:4; EPH 6:1-6:3

### Murder is forbidden
**EXO 20:13** parallel DEU 5:17 *See also* GEN 4:8-4:16; MAT 5:21

### Adultery is forbidden
**EXO 20:14** parallel DEU 5:18 *See also* LEV 18:20; 2SA 11:2-11:5; MAT 5:27; HEB 13:4

### Stealing is forbidden
**EXO 20:15** parallel DEU 5:19 *See also* LEV 19:11, LEV 19:13

### False witness is forbidden
**EXO 20:16** parallel DEU 5:20 *See also* LEV 19:11

### Coveting is forbidden
**EXO 20:17** parallel DEU 5:21 *See also* LEV 19:17-19:18; JOB 31:9-31:12; ROM 7:7

## The circumstances surrounding the giving of the Ten Commandments


### The Ten Commandments written on stone tablets
**EXO 24:12** *See also* DEU 4:13, DEU 9:9-9:10

### The stone tablets broken
**EXO 32:19** *Moses broke the stone tablets when he saw that the Israelites had sinned by making the golden calf.* *See also* DEU 9:16-9:17

### A second set of stone tablets made
**EXO 34:1, EXO 34:28** *See also* DEU 10:1-10:2

### The second set of stone tablets put in the ark of the covenant
**EXO 40:20** *See also* DEU 10:1-10:2
