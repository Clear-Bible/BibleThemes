# 8612: prayer, and faith

Effective prayer depends on faith, especially on a willingness to trust in God's faithfulness to his promises to his people.



## Faith is necessary in order to approach God
**HEB 11:6**

## Faith is necessary to receive benefits from God
**MRK 6:5-6:6**; **JAS 5:16-5:18** *See also* EPH 3:12; HEB 10:22

## Faith is necessary for effective prayer
**MAT 21:21-21:22** parallel MRK 11:22-11:24**JAS 1:5-1:8** *See also* JAS 5:14-5:15

## Jesus Christ responded to people's need on the basis of faith
**MAT 9:27-9:30** *See also* MAT 8:5-8:13 parallel LUK 7:1-7:10; MAT 9:20-9:22 parallels MRK 5:25-5:34, LUK 8:43-8:48; MAT 15:21-15:28 parallel MRK 7:24-7:30

## Examples of notable prayers of faith
 1KI 18:36-18:37 *Elijah, in contest with the prophets of Baal*; ; JAS 5:17-5:18 *Elijah, praying about rain*; ; 1KI 17:19-17:22 *Elijah and the widow's son*; ; 2KI 4:32-4:35 *Elisha and the Shunammite's son*
