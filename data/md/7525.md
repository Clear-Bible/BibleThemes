# 7525: exclusiveness

The keeping of a distinctive group identity by drawing clear boundary lines, to separate those within the group from outsiders with whom they are unwilling to associate.

## The practice of exclusiveness by God's people, grounded in God's claim to exclusive worship
**[EXO 20:3](bibleref:02020003)** parallel DEU 5:7 *See also* [EXO 34:14](bibleref:02034014); [DEU 6:14-6:15](bibleref:05006014-05006015); [2KI 17:35-17:39](bibleref:12017035-12017039); [ISA 42:8](bibleref:23042008)

## Effects of Jewish exclusiveness


### Division between Jews and Samaritans
**[JHN 4:9](bibleref:43004009)** *See also* [EZR 4:1-4:3](bibleref:15004001-15004003); [MAT 10:5-10:6](bibleref:40010005-40010006) *Jesus Christ was here dealing with his priority for that time* ; [LUK 9:51-9:53](bibleref:42009051-42009053)

### Division between Jews and Gentiles
**[ACT 10:28](bibleref:44010028)** *See also* [MAT 10:5](bibleref:40010005), [MAT 15:21-15:28](bibleref:40015021-40015028) parallel MRK 7:24-7:30 *Jesus Christ restricted his earthly ministry in general to the people of Israel, but instigated a mission to the Gentiles after his resurrection* ; [JHN 18:28](bibleref:43018028)
