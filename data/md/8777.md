# 8777: lust

An overpowering and compulsive desire or passion, especially of a sexual nature. Scripture condemns lust of all kinds, and urges believers to show self-control.

## Lust has its origins in the heart and mind
**[PRO 6:25-6:29](bibleref:20006025-20006029)**; **[MAT 5:28](bibleref:40005028)** *See also* [GEN 3:6](bibleref:01003006); [JOB 31:1](bibleref:18031001); [JAS 1:13-1:15](bibleref:59001013-59001015); [1JN 2:16](bibleref:62002016)

## Lust is natural to unbelievers
**[ROM 1:21-1:27](bibleref:45001021-45001027)** *See also* [ROM 7:5](bibleref:45007005); [1CO 6:9-6:10](bibleref:46006009-46006010); [1PE 4:3](bibleref:60004003); [2PE 2:14-2:18](bibleref:61002014-61002018)

## Believers can and must fight against lust by showing self-control
**[GAL 5:16-5:21](bibleref:48005016-48005021)**; **[COL 3:5](bibleref:51003005)** *See also* [1CO 9:27](bibleref:46009027); [GAL 5:24](bibleref:48005024); [EPH 4:22](bibleref:49004022); [1TH 4:4-4:5](bibleref:52004004-52004005); [2TI 2:22](bibleref:55002022); [TIT 2:12](bibleref:56002012); [1PE 2:11](bibleref:60002011)

## Examples of lust


### Lust expressed as sexual desire
 [GEN 39:6-39:12](bibleref:01039006-01039012) *Potiphar's wife and Joseph* ; [2SA 11:2-11:5](bibleref:10011002-10011005) *David and Bathsheba* 

### Lust for money
**[1TI 6:9-6:10](bibleref:54006009-54006010)** *See also* [1TI 3:3](bibleref:54003003)

### The lustful desire of Israel and Judah for alliances
 [EZK 23:1-23:21](bibleref:26023001-26023021) *Both kingdoms sought security from such alliances, rather than trusting in God.*
