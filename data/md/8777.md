# 8777: lust

An overpowering and compulsive desire or passion, especially of a sexual nature. Scripture condemns lust of all kinds, and urges believers to show self-control.



## Lust has its origins in the heart and mind
**PRO 6:25-6:29**; **MAT 5:28** *See also* GEN 3:6; JOB 31:1; JAS 1:13-1:15; 1JN 2:16

## Lust is natural to unbelievers
**ROM 1:21-1:27** *See also* ROM 7:5; 1CO 6:9-6:10; 1PE 4:3; 2PE 2:14-2:18

## Believers can and must fight against lust by showing self-control
**GAL 5:16-5:21**; **COL 3:5** *See also* 1CO 9:27; GAL 5:24; EPH 4:22; 1TH 4:4-4:5; 2TI 2:22; TIT 2:12; 1PE 2:11

## Examples of lust


### Lust expressed as sexual desire
 GEN 39:6-39:12 *Potiphar's wife and Joseph*; ; 2SA 11:2-11:5 *David and Bathsheba*

### Lust for money
**1TI 6:9-6:10** *See also* 1TI 3:3

### The lustful desire of Israel and Judah for alliances
 EZK 23:1-23:21 *Both kingdoms sought security from such alliances, rather than trusting in God.*
