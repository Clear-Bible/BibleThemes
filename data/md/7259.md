# 7259: promised land, later history of

Established firmly in the land under David and Solomon, God's people ultimately began to neglect the terms of the covenant and were warned by the prophets of the consequent judgment and exile.

## The desolation of the promised land


### By Antiochus Epiphanes
**[DAN 8:9-8:14](bibleref:27008009-27008014)** *The growing horn is Antiochus Epiphanes who tried to wipe out the Jewish faith in 168 B.C., forbidding Jewish practices and defiling the temple with pagan sacrifices and prostitution.*  *See also* [DAN 11:29-11:35](bibleref:27011029-27011035) *The help that would be received by God's people probably refers to the Maccabean revolt and the cleansing of the temple in 165 B.C., although by NT times the land would be occupied by Rome.* 

### By Rome
**[LUK 21:20-21:24](bibleref:42021020-42021024)** parallels MAT 24:15-24:21, MRK 13:14-13:19 *Jesus Christ prophesies the desolation of the nation by Rome in A.D. 70.*
