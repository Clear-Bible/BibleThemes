# 2227: Immanuel

An OT name given to Jesus Christ, meaning “God with us”.

## Actual uses of the name Immanuel
**[ISA 7:14](bibleref:23007014)** *The name was given originally to a child of Isaiah's day who would signify God's salvation and judgment for Judah* ; **[ISA 8:7-8:10](bibleref:23008007-23008010)** *The original fulfilment of the Immanuel prophecy would bring judgment on Judah* ; **[MAT 1:22-1:23](bibleref:40001022-40001023)** *Isaiah's prophecy is fulfilled through the virginal conception of Jesus Christ.*
