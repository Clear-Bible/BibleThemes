# 6157: fall, of Satan and his angels

Satan and his angels rebelled against God and were removed from their positions of authority. They are now destined for judgment.



## The rebellion of Satan and his angels
**[ISA 14:12-14:15](bibleref:23014012-23014015)** *Written about a king in Isaiah's own time, this passage has been used by both Jews and Christians as a description of the fall of Satan* ; **[JUD 1:6](bibleref:65001006)** *See also* [JOB 4:18](bibleref:18004018); [EZK 28:15](bibleref:26028015); [LUK 10:18](bibleref:42010018); [REV 12:7-12:9](bibleref:66012007-66012009), [REV 12:12](bibleref:66012012)
