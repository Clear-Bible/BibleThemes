# 5188: tearing of clothes

Primarily associated with mourning, such action was an expression of deep sorrow and heartfelt grief. It was also a natural reaction at times of great distress and in cases of sincere repentance.

## The tearing of clothes was a sign of mourning


### It was a demonstration of grief
**[2SA 1:11-1:12](bibleref:10001011-10001012)** *See also* [2SA 13:30-13:31](bibleref:10013030-10013031); [2KI 2:11-2:12](bibleref:12002011-12002012); [JOB 1:20](bibleref:18001020), [JOB 2:12](bibleref:18002012)

### It was often associated with wearing sackcloth
**[GEN 37:34](bibleref:01037034)** *See also* [2SA 3:31-3:32](bibleref:10003031-10003032)

### The high priest was forbidden to tear his clothes in mourning
**[LEV 21:10-21:12](bibleref:03021010-03021012)** *See also* [LEV 10:1-10:7](bibleref:03010001-03010007)

## The tearing of clothes indicated deep distress
**[JDG 11:34-11:35](bibleref:07011034-07011035)** *Jephthah had vowed to sacrifice whatever came out of the door of his house to meet him.*  *See also* [GEN 37:29](bibleref:01037029), [GEN 44:12-44:13](bibleref:01044012-01044013); [NUM 14:1-14:9](bibleref:04014001-04014009); [JOS 7:3-7:6](bibleref:06007003-06007006); [2KI 6:26-6:30](bibleref:12006026-12006030), [2KI 18:28-19:4](bibleref:12018028-12019004) parallel ISA 36:13-37:4; [EST 3:13-4:1](bibleref:17003013-17004001); [ACT 14:8-14:18](bibleref:44014008-44014018)

## Repentance was sometimes accompanied by the tearing of clothes
**[1KI 21:20-21:27](bibleref:11021020-11021027)** *See also* [JER 36:21-36:24](bibleref:24036021-24036024) *an indication of the lack of repentance* ; [JOL 2:12-2:14](bibleref:29002012-29002014) *God requires that the tearing of clothes should express genuine repentance.*
