# 7382: house of God

The place where God was seen to dwell with his people, initially the tabernacle and subsequently the temple at Jerusalem.

## Early references to the house of God
**[GEN 28:16-28:17](bibleref:01028016-01028017)** *“house of God” was first used by Jacob to commemorate the place where he met with God* ; **[GEN 28:19](bibleref:01028019)** *“Bethel” means “house of God”.*  *See also* [GEN 28:22](bibleref:01028022)

## The tabernacle as the house of God


### The tabernacle was referred to as such before its construction
**[EXO 23:19](bibleref:02023019)** *See also* [EXO 34:26](bibleref:02034026)

### In the wilderness God said he would dwell with his people in the tabernacle
**[EXO 25:8-25:9](bibleref:02025008-02025009)**

### God's presence came and dwelt in the tabernacle when it was completed
**[EXO 40:33-40:34](bibleref:02040033-02040034)** *See also* [EXO 40:35-40:38](bibleref:02040035-02040038)

## The temple as the permanent house of God


### David's desire to build the LORD a permanent house
**[2SA 7:1-7:2](bibleref:10007001-10007002)**

### This was not the LORD's will for David
**[2SA 7:5-7:7](bibleref:10007005-10007007), [2SA 7:12-7:13](bibleref:10007012-10007013)** *See also* [ACT 7:45-7:47](bibleref:44007045-44007047)

### Solomon builds the temple in Jersualem
 [1KI 5:4-5:5](bibleref:11005004-11005005), [1KI 6:1-6:2](bibleref:11006001-11006002)

### God's presence dwelt there but was conditional upon the faithfulness of the people
**[1KI 8:10-8:13](bibleref:11008010-11008013)** parallel 2CH 5:11-6:2 *See also* [1KI 9:6-9:9](bibleref:11009006-11009009)
