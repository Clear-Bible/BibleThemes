# 1513: Trinity, mission of the persons

All three persons of the Trinity co-operate in God's work of salvation. For this purpose, the Father sends the Son and the Holy Spirit to redeem his people on earth and to live in and among them.



## The Son and the Holy Spirit do God's work on earth


### In the OT, the LORD alone is Saviour
**ISA 43:11** *See also* PSA 19:14, PSA 78:35; ISA 43:3, ISA 49:26, ISA 60:16; JER 14:8; HOS 13:4

### In the NT, the Son is the Saviour
**MAT 1:21** *See also* LUK 2:11; JHN 4:42; GAL 3:13, GAL 4:5; TIT 2:13, TIT 3:6

### In the OT, the LORD lives among his people
**EXO 29:45** *See also* PSA 135:21; ISA 57:15; EZK 43:7; JOL 3:17; ZEC 2:10-2:11

### In the NT, the Spirit lives in the church
**1CO 3:16** *See also* JHN 14:17; ROM 8:9, ROM 8:11; 2TI 1:14

## Father and Son in the mission of Jesus Christ


### The Father sends the Son
**GAL 4:4** *See also* MAT 10:40; MRK 12:6; JHN 3:16-3:17, JHN 7:28, JHN 8:42; 1JN 4:9

### The Son reveals the Father
**JHN 8:38** *See also* JHN 1:18, JHN 15:15, JHN 17:6, JHN 17:26; COL 1:15; HEB 1:3

### The Son does the Father's work
**JHN 5:19** *See also* JHN 4:34, JHN 5:17, JHN 9:3-9:4, JHN 10:37, JHN 14:10

### The Son speaks the Father's words
**JHN 12:49-12:50** *See also* JHN 3:34, JHN 8:28, JHN 14:24, JHN 17:8

### The Father testifies to the Son
**JHN 5:37** *This refers to God's testimony to his Son in the OT Scriptures.* *See also* JHN 5:32, JHN 8:18; 1JN 5:9

## The sending of the Holy Spirit


### The Father gives the Spirit
**LUK 11:13** *See also* ACT 5:32; 2CO 5:5; GAL 3:5; EPH 1:17; 1JN 4:13

### The Son gives the Spirit
**JHN 16:7** *See also* MAT 3:11 parallels MRK 1:8, LUK 3:16; JHN 1:33, JHN 20:22

### The Father sends the Spirit through Jesus Christ
**JHN 14:16-14:17** *The varied terminology about who sends the Spirit reflects the close co-operation between and the shared activity of the three persons.* *See also* JHN 14:26, JHN 15:26; ACT 2:33
