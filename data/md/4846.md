# 4846: shadow

Primarily a symbol of protection, of the brevity of life and of the experience of death, in contrast to God's character and work.



## Shadow as a general symbol of protection
**ISA 30:2-30:3** *See also* JDG 9:15; JER 48:45; HOS 14:7; JON 4:5-4:6; MRK 4:32

### A symbol of God's protection
**PSA 91:1** *See also* PSA 17:8, PSA 36:7, PSA 57:1, PSA 63:7, PSA 121:5; ISA 4:5-4:6, ISA 25:4-25:5, ISA 49:2, ISA 51:16

### A symbol of the protection given by God's chosen one
 ISA 32:2; ; LAM 4:20; ; EZK 17:22-17:23

## Shadow as a symbol of life


### Of the brevity of life
**PSA 144:4** *See also* 1CH 29:15; JOB 8:9, JOB 14:2; PSA 102:11, PSA 109:23; ECC 6:12, ECC 8:13

### Of life's sadness and despair
**ISA 59:9** *See also* JOB 3:3-3:5, JOB 7:2-7:3, JOB 16:16, JOB 17:7

## Shadow as a symbol of the experience of death
**JOB 38:17**; **PSA 23:4** *See also* JOB 10:21-10:22

## Shadow in contrast to God


### Shadow contrasts with God's unchanging nature
**JAS 1:17**

### God's light pierces all shadow
**JOB 12:22** *See also* JOB 34:22; PSA 139:11-139:12; DAN 2:22

### God's salvation reaches to those whose life is in shadow
**MAT 4:16** *See also* ISA 9:2, ISA 60:1-60:2; LUK 1:79

## OT ceremonies as a shadow of the reality in Jesus Christ
**COL 2:16-2:17** *See also* HEB 8:5, HEB 10:1

## Real shadows
 JDG 9:36; ; NEH 13:19; ; SNG 2:17, SNG 4:6; ; JER 6:4

## Real shadows invovled in miracles
 2KI 20:9-20:11 parallel ISA 38:8; ; ACT 5:15
