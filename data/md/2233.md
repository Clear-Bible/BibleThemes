# 2233: Son of Man

A Hebrew and Aramaic term, often used to mean a human being in general. In the Gospels Jesus Christ uses it to refer to himself in his earthly ministry and his future death, exaltation and coming as judge and Saviour.

## Sons of men as a term for humanity in general


### Human beings are inferior to God
**[PSA 115:16](bibleref:19115016)** *The Hebrew term here translated “man” literally means “sons of men”. Many of the OT references cited in this theme follow this pattern.*  *See also* [PSA 11:4](bibleref:19011004), [PSA 33:13-33:14](bibleref:19033013-19033014), [PSA 45:2](bibleref:19045002)

### Human beings are dependent on God's care
**[PSA 8:4](bibleref:19008004)** *See also* [PSA 36:7](bibleref:19036007), [PSA 80:17](bibleref:19080017), [PSA 107:8](bibleref:19107008), [PSA 107:15](bibleref:19107015), [PSA 107:21](bibleref:19107021), [PSA 107:31](bibleref:19107031)

### Human beings are mortal
**[ECC 9:3](bibleref:21009003)** *See also* [ECC 3:18-3:19](bibleref:21003018-21003019); [EZK 31:14](bibleref:26031014)

### Human beings are sinful and untrustworthy
**[PSA 146:3](bibleref:19146003)** *See also* [PSA 14:2-14:3](bibleref:19014002-19014003) parallel PSA 53:2-53:3; [MIC 5:7](bibleref:33005007)

## Son of Man as a term for individual men


### The sons of Adam
 [GEN 4:1-4:2](bibleref:01004001-01004002) *“a man” is literally “son of Adam” in Hebrew* ; [GEN 5:3-5:4](bibleref:01005003-01005004)

### Ezekiel
**[EZK 2:1](bibleref:26002001)** *Ezekiel is called “son of man” 93 times, though this form of address is unique to him in the OT. It may emphasise his remoteness or his humanity.*  *See also* [EZK 2:3](bibleref:26002003), [EZK 2:6](bibleref:26002006), [EZK 2:8](bibleref:26002008), [EZK 3:1](bibleref:26003001)

### Other people
 [JOB 25:6](bibleref:18025006); [DAN 8:17](bibleref:27008017)

## A son of man as a ruler of God's future kingdom
**[DAN 7:13](bibleref:27007013)** *The Aramaic “one like a son of man” stresses this person's humanity and mysterious identity* ; **[DAN 7:13-7:14](bibleref:27007013-27007014)** *He enters God's presence and is given final authority over God's kingdom* ; **[DAN 7:27](bibleref:27007027)** *He shares the kingdom with God's people.*  *See also* [DAN 7:18](bibleref:27007018) *“saints of the Most High” have sometimes been interpreted as angels* 

## Son of Man as a title for Jesus Christ


### Used by Jesus Christ to emphasise his humanity and authority
**[MRK 2:10-2:11](bibleref:41002010-41002011)** parallels MAT 9:6, LUK 5:24**[MRK 8:31](bibleref:41008031)** parallel LUK 9:22

### The Son of Man receives God's kingdom
