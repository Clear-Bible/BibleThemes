# 5414: money, stewardship of

The righteous handling of money is an important practical test of godliness.



## Money must be obtained honestly


### Not by theft
**[EXO 20:15](bibleref:02020015)** parallel DEU 5:19 *See also* [PRO 10:2](bibleref:20010002), [PRO 19:26](bibleref:20019026); [MRK 10:19](bibleref:41010019)

**[LEV 19:13](bibleref:03019013)** *See also* [PRO 11:1](bibleref:20011001), [PRO 13:11](bibleref:20013011), [PRO 20:10](bibleref:20020010); [EZK 28:18](bibleref:26028018)

### Not by usury
**[EXO 22:25](bibleref:02022025)** *See also* [PSA 15:5](bibleref:19015005); [PRO 28:8](bibleref:20028008)

### Not at the expense of justice
**[EXO 23:8](bibleref:02023008)** *See also* [PRO 15:27](bibleref:20015027), [PRO 16:8](bibleref:20016008); [JER 17:11](bibleref:24017011)

### Not by extortion
