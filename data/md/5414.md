# 5414: money, stewardship of

The righteous handling of money is an important practical test of godliness.



## Money must be obtained honestly


### Not by theft
**EXO 20:15** parallel DEU 5:19 *See also* PRO 10:2, PRO 19:26; MRK 10:19

### Not by fraudulent practices
**LEV 19:13** *See also* PRO 11:1, PRO 13:11, PRO 20:10; EZK 28:18

### Not by usury
**EXO 22:25** *See also* PSA 15:5; PRO 28:8

### Not at the expense of justice
**EXO 23:8** *See also* PRO 15:27, PRO 16:8; JER 17:11

### Not by extortion
**LUK 3:13** *See also* EZK 22:12

### Not by oppression
**JAS 5:4** *See also* PRO 22:16; EZK 18:7-18:8; AMO 5:11; MAL 3:5

### Not at the expense of health
 PRO 23:4

### Not at the expense of witness
 GEN 14:22-14:23; ; 3JN 1:7

### Not at the expense of spiritual well-being
 MAT 16:26 parallels MRK 8:36, LUK 9:25; ; LUK 12:16-12:21; ; 1TI 6:9

### By work, trade, investment or inheritance
**2TH 3:12** *See also* GEN 15:2, GEN 34:21; PRO 13:11, PRO 13:22, PRO 14:23, PRO 19:14; MAT 25:27 parallel LUK 19:23

## Money must be cared for diligently


### Personal money
 PRO 27:23-27:24

### Money held on trust
 2KI 22:4-22:7; ; MAT 25:14-25:27; ; LUK 16:10-16:12; ; 1CO 4:2

## Money must be used in a God-honouring way


### For the support of the family
**1TI 5:8** *See also* 1TI 5:16

### For benefiting the poor, especially God's people
**GAL 6:10** *See also* PRO 19:17, PRO 28:27; MAT 6:3-6:4; LUK 12:33; JHN 13:29; ACT 2:45; ROM 15:26; GAL 2:10; 1TI 6:18

### For the work of God's kingdom
**GAL 6:6** *See also* PRO 3:9-3:10; MAL 3:10; LUK 8:3, LUK 16:9; PHP 4:14-4:19; 1TI 5:17-5:18

## Examples of godly people who have used their money well


### Those who were wealthy but godly
 GEN 13:2, GEN 26:14, GEN 30:43; ; 2SA 19:32; ; 2CH 9:22; ; JOB 1:3; ; MAT 27:57; ; ACT 4:34-4:36

### Those who had little but gave much
 MRK 12:41-12:44 parallel LUK 21:1-21:4; ; 2CO 8:1-8:4
