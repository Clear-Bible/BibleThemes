# 7251: pillar of cloud and fire

A physical manifestation of the presence of God among his people; a special form of theophany particularly associated with Israel being offered guidance on the exodus journey.

## Functions of the pillar of cloud and fire


### To lead Israel
**[EXO 13:21-13:22](bibleref:02013021-02013022)** *See also* [EXO 40:36-40:37](bibleref:02040036-02040037); [NUM 9:17](bibleref:04009017), [NUM 14:14](bibleref:04014014); [NEH 9:12](bibleref:16009012), [NEH 9:19](bibleref:16009019)

### To separate and protect Israel from the armies of Egypt
**[EXO 14:19-14:20](bibleref:02014019-02014020)** *This was just before the crossing of the Red Sea.* 

### To represent God's presence, especially to Moses
**[EXO 33:9](bibleref:02033009)** *See also* [NUM 12:5-12:6](bibleref:04012005-04012006); [DEU 31:15-31:16](bibleref:05031015-05031016); [PSA 99:7](bibleref:19099007)

## Effects of the pillar of cloud and fire


### It evoked worship
 [EXO 33:10](bibleref:02033010)

### It caused the nations to speak of God
 [NUM 14:14](bibleref:04014014)

### Experiencing the cloud did not guarantee acceptance by God
 [1CO 10:1-10:5](bibleref:46010001-46010005)
