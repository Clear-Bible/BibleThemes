# 8289: joy, of the church

The church is to rejoice in the salvation brought about by Jesus Christ's faithful life and death, and by the power of his resurrection. Even in adversity, believers know the joy of Jesus Christ.



## Joy at the coming of Jesus Christ
**LUK 2:8-2:11** *See also* JHN 3:27-3:29, JHN 8:56

## Joy as the hallmark of the kingdom of God
**GAL 5:22** *See also* MAT 13:44; LUK 10:17-10:21, LUK 15:4-15:10 parallel MAT 18:12-18:14; JHN 4:34-4:36, JHN 16:20-16:24, JHN 17:13; ROM 12:12, ROM 14:17-14:18; 1TH 5:16-5:18

### Jesus Christ promises joy to his disciples
**JHN 15:9-15:11** *See also* MAT 5:11-5:12 parallel LUK 6:22-6:23; JHN 16:20-16:24, JHN 17:13

**ACT 2:46-2:47** *See also* ACT 15:1-15:3; ROM 15:31-15:32; 2CO 2:3; PHP 2:17-2:18
