# 8300: love, and the world

Scripture promotes the love of family, home and country and contains many examples of such love.



## Parental love


### Aspects of parental love
**PRO 13:24**; **EPH 6:4** *See also* DEU 6:7; 2CO 12:14; COL 3:21; 1TI 3:4; 2TI 3:15

### Examples of maternal love
 GEN 21:16 *Hagar*; ; EXO 2:3 *Moses' mother*; ; JDG 5:28; ; 1SA 2:19 *Hannah*; ; 2SA 21:10; ; 1KI 3:26, 1KI 17:18; 2KI 4:20, 2KI 4:27; 
MAT 15:22 parallel MRK 7:26; ; LUK 2:48, LUK 7:12-7:13 *the widow of Nain*; ; JHN 19:25

### Examples of paternal love

MRK 5:23 parallel LUK 8:41-8:42 *Jairus*
