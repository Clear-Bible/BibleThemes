# 8493: watchfulness, of believers

Alertness to dangers and blessings in the Christian life, and also to opportunities to do good.



## Watchfulness for dangers
**1PE 5:8** *See also* PRO 4:23

### The danger of false teaching
**MAT 7:15** *See also* PSA 94:8; PRO 8:33-8:34; MAT 24:4-24:5; MRK 8:15; ROM 16:17; 1CO 16:13

### The danger of neglecting the word of God
**DEU 4:9** *See also* JOS 22:5; 1KI 2:3; HEB 2:1, HEB 12:25; 2PE 1:19
