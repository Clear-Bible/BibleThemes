# 4835: light, spiritual

Light is often used in Scripture as a symbol of the presence of God in the world. It is often especially associated with the word of God, the salvation brought about by God, or the person of Jesus Christ.



## Light as a symbol of God


### God's purity
**1JN 1:5** *See also* JOB 25:4-25:6; ISA 5:20; 1JN 1:7

### God's glory
**REV 21:23** *See also* PSA 76:4, PSA 84:11; ISA 60:19-60:20; HAB 3:4; 1TI 6:15-6:16

### God's knowledge
 PSA 90:8, PSA 139:11-139:12; ; DAN 2:22; ; 1CO 4:5

### God's unchangeability
**JAS 1:17**

### God's vengeance
 *See also* PSA 94:1; ISA 10:17

### Satan as an imitator of light
**2CO 11:14**

## Light as a symbol of God's favour towards his people
**EZR 9:8** *See also* NUM 6:25; JOB 3:20, JOB 29:2-29:3; PSA 4:6, PSA 31:16, PSA 44:3, PSA 67:1, PSA 80:3, PSA 80:7; MIC 7:8-7:9; REV 22:5

## Light as a symbol of God's word


### As perceived by believers
**PSA 119:105** *See also* PSA 19:8, PSA 119:130; 2PE 1:19

### As perceived by unbelievers
**JHN 3:20** *See also* JOB 24:13-24:17; JHN 1:5

## Light as a symbol of salvation
**1PE 2:9** *See also* ISA 9:2, ISA 51:4; ACT 26:23; EPH 5:14; COL 1:12; 1JN 2:8

### Foretold in the OT
