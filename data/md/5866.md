# 5866: gluttony

Scripture condemns an excessive greed for food. Its unrestrained self-indulgence often leads to deeper iniquity and represents a rejection of godly moderation.

## Gluttony is a form of idolatry and therefore condemned as offensive to God
**[EPH 5:5](bibleref:49005005)**; **[PHP 3:19](bibleref:50003019)** *See also* [PSA 10:3](bibleref:19010003); [ISA 22:12-22:14](bibleref:23022012-23022014) *Gluttony as escapism, trying to avoid God's requirements* ; [ZEC 7:4-7:6](bibleref:38007004-38007006) *Even their religious feasts were nothing but self-indulgence* ; [MRK 7:21-7:23](bibleref:41007021-41007023); [1CO 6:9-6:10](bibleref:46006009-46006010), [1CO 10:7](bibleref:46010007); [COL 3:5](bibleref:51003005); [JAS 5:5](bibleref:59005005)

## Gluttony is closely linked with drunkenness
 [EXO 32:6](bibleref:02032006); [JDG 9:27](bibleref:07009027); [1SA 25:36](bibleref:09025036)
