# 5866: gluttony

Scripture condemns an excessive greed for food. Its unrestrained self-indulgence often leads to deeper iniquity and represents a rejection of godly moderation.



## Gluttony is a form of idolatry and therefore condemned as offensive to God
**EPH 5:5**; **PHP 3:19** *See also* PSA 10:3; ISA 22:12-22:14 *Gluttony as escapism, trying to avoid God's requirements*; ZEC 7:4-7:6 *Even their religious feasts were nothing but self-indulgence*; MRK 7:21-7:23; 1CO 6:9-6:10, 1CO 10:7; COL 3:5; JAS 5:5

## Gluttony is closely linked with drunkenness
 EXO 32:6; ; JDG 9:27; ; 1SA 25:36

## The destructive consequences of gluttony
**PRO 23:20-23:21, PRO 28:7** *See also* PRO 15:27, PRO 28:25; ECC 6:7

## Warnings against gluttony
**PRO 23:1-23:2**; **LUK 12:15**; **EPH 5:3**; **COL 3:5** *See also* LUK 12:19-12:20, LUK 21:34; 1CO 5:9-5:11; GAL 5:19-5:21; TIT 1:12-1:13; 1PE 4:3-4:4

## Examples of gluttony
 GEN 25:29-25:34 *Esau putting food before his birthright*; ; NUM 11:32 *the children of Israel gorging on quail*; ; 1SA 30:16 *an Amalekite raiding party*; ; 1KI 1:24-1:25 *Adonijah feasting with his supporters*; ; DAN 5:1 *King Belshazzar*; ; MAT 11:19 parallel LUK 7:34 *Jesus Christ falsely accused of gluttony*; ; MAT 23:25 parallel LUK 11:39 *the Pharisees*; ; MAT 24:49 parallel LUK 12:45 *the irresponsible servant*; ; LUK 15:13 *the prodigal son*; ; 2PE 2:13-2:14 *false teachers*
