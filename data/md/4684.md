# 4684: sheep

Sheep were a major feature of ancient Israel's rural economy. Although they were of importance as a source of food and wool, they also symbolise helplessness and the need for guidance.



## The economic importance of sheep


### As a measure of wealth and God's blessing
**1SA 25:2**; **PSA 144:13** *See also* GEN 12:16, GEN 30:43; EXO 9:3, EXO 22:1; DEU 22:1-22:2; JOS 6:21; 2SA 12:2-12:3; JOB 1:3, JOB 42:12

### As a source of food
**1SA 25:18** *See also* LEV 7:23; NUM 18:17-18:18; 1SA 14:32; 2SA 17:29; 1CH 12:40

### As a source of wool
**2KI 3:4** *See also* GEN 31:19, GEN 38:12-38:13; DEU 18:4; 1SA 25:7; 2SA 13:23-13:24; EZK 27:18

### Sheep were used in striking a bargain
**GEN 21:27** *See also* GEN 20:14, GEN 30:25-30:43

### The care of sheep was a recognised occupation
**1SA 16:11** *See also* GEN 29:2-29:3, GEN 37:13; 1SA 17:34-17:35; SNG 1:7; HOS 12:12
