# 6639: election, to salvation

God chooses to bring individuals to salvation through faith in Jesus Christ.



## Election is part of God's eternal decree


### Election is from eternity
**[EPH 1:4](bibleref:49001004)** *See also* [2TH 2:13](bibleref:53002013); [2TI 1:9](bibleref:55001009)

**[NEH 9:7-9:8](bibleref:16009007-16009008)** *See also* [GEN 15:7-15:8](bibleref:01015007-01015008), [GEN 15:18-15:21](bibleref:01015018-01015021), [GEN 18:19](bibleref:01018019); [GAL 3:29](bibleref:48003029)
