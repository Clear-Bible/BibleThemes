# 8743: faithlessness, nature and causes of

The abandoning of a commitment to the Lord, seen in disowning him, wandering from the truth and becoming entangled with the world, and resulting in judgment.



## Warnings against faithlessness
**2TI 2:12-2:13** *See also* MAT 10:33 parallel LUK 12:9; LUK 9:62; HEB 10:35-10:39; HAB 2:3-2:4

## Ways in which faithlessness is seen


### Turning away from the Lord
**HEB 3:12**

### Abandoning the gospel of grace
**GAL 1:6**

### Becoming entangled in the world
**2PE 2:20-2:22** *See also* PRO 26:11; 2TI 4:10

## Causes of faithlessness


### False teaching
**2TI 2:17-2:18** *See also* GAL 1:7, GAL 4:8-4:11; 1TI 4:1-4:3; 2TI 4:3-4:4; 2PE 3:17

### Persecution and trials
**MAT 13:20-13:21** parallels MRK 4:16-4:17, LUK 8:13 *See also* MAT 24:9-24:13

### Concern for money and possessions
**1TI 6:10** *See also* PRO 30:8-30:9; MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:18-4:19, LUK 8:14

### Love growing cold
**MAT 24:12** *See also* REV 2:4

### Sexual desire
 1TI 5:11-5:15

## Faithlessness brings punishment


### Church discipline
 1TI 1:18-1:20 *These men had been excluded from the church, a sanctuary from Satan's power.*

### God's judgment
**MAT 10:33** parallel LUK 12:9 *See also* HEB 6:4-6:6, HEB 10:26-10:27

## The faithlessness of those who fall away


### The absence of true faith
**1JN 2:19**

### True faith is proved by perseverance
 *See also* JAS 1:2-1:3; 1PE 1:5-1:7

## Israel's faithlessness in the OT


### Israel's unfaithfulness led to God's rejection
 PSA 78:56-78:59

### Warnings not to repeat Israel's unfaithfulness
 PSA 95:7-95:11; ; HEB 3:7-3:12, HEB 4:1-4:2
