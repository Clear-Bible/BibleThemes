# 8719: distrust

Failure to trust another, or a tendency to be suspicious of others' abilities or intentions. Though sometimes justifiable towards other human beings, such an attitude is never acceptable towards God.



## Distrust between human beings
**GEN 50:15-50:17** *Joseph's brothers distrusted him because they could not believe he had forgiven them.* *See also* GEN 32:6-32:8 *Jacob distrusted Esau because he thought he was seeking revenge*; JDG 11:19-11:20 *Sihon distrusted Israel because he suspected a trick*; ACT 15:37-15:38 *Paul distrusted John Mark, having been let down by him.*

## Distrust of God


### Israel distrusted God
**ZEP 3:2** *See also* DEU 1:32-1:33, DEU 9:23-9:24 *Because that generation distrusted God, they failed to enter the promised land*; PSA 78:7, PSA 78:22 *Israel distrusted God in spite of the miracles he had done for her.*2KI 17:14-17:15; JER 13:25

### Other examples
Distrust in God involves failure to trust God's promises and placing trust in other things:
 JER 13:25; ; EZK 33:13; ; MAL 3:6-3:10; ; MAT 19:22 parallel MRK 10:22

## Distrust by God
**JOB 4:18** *See also* JOB 15:15; JHN 2:24

## Distrust is sometimes justified
**PSA 118:8** *See also* JOB 39:12; PSA 5:9, PSA 44:6, PSA 146:3; JER 7:4, JER 9:4, JER 12:6; MIC 7:5; HAB 2:18
