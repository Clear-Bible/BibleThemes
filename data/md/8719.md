# 8719: distrust

Failure to trust another, or a tendency to be suspicious of others' abilities or intentions. Though sometimes justifiable towards other human beings, such an attitude is never acceptable towards God.

## Distrust between human beings
**[GEN 50:15-50:17](bibleref:01050015-01050017)** *Joseph's brothers distrusted him because they could not believe he had forgiven them.*  *See also* [GEN 32:6-32:8](bibleref:01032006-01032008) *Jacob distrusted Esau because he thought he was seeking revenge* ; [JDG 11:19-11:20](bibleref:07011019-07011020) *Sihon distrusted Israel because he suspected a trick* ; [ACT 15:37-15:38](bibleref:44015037-44015038) *Paul distrusted John Mark, having been let down by him.* 

## Distrust of God


### Israel distrusted God
**[ZEP 3:2](bibleref:36003002)** *See also* [DEU 1:32-1:33](bibleref:05001032-05001033), [DEU 9:23-9:24](bibleref:05009023-05009024) *Because that generation distrusted God, they failed to enter the promised land* ; [PSA 78:7](bibleref:19078007), [PSA 78:22](bibleref:19078022) *Israel distrusted God in spite of the miracles he had done for her.* [2KI 17:14-17:15](bibleref:12017014-12017015); [JER 13:25](bibleref:24013025)

### Other examples
Distrust in God involves failure to trust God's promises and placing trust in other things:
 [JER 13:25](bibleref:24013025); [EZK 33:13](bibleref:26033013); [MAL 3:6-3:10](bibleref:39003006-39003010); [MAT 19:22](bibleref:40019022) parallel MRK 10:22
