# 8738: evil, victory over

Although evil continues to be present and active in the world, Scripture gives an assurance that God will finally triumph over evil in all its forms.



## Satan's power is already limited by God
**JOB 1:12** *See also* GEN 3:14-3:15; JOB 2:6; ZEC 3:2; REV 12:9

## Evil spirits are subject to God's control
**1SA 16:14** *See also* JDG 9:23; 1SA 18:10, 1SA 19:9; 1KI 22:23; COL 2:13-2:15; JUD 1:6

## Jesus Christ's triumph over evil powers
**1JN 3:8** *See also* MAT 12:25-12:29 parallels MRK 3:23-3:27, LUK 11:17-11:22; MRK 1:27 parallel LUK 4:36; LUK 4:13, LUK 10:18; JHN 12:31, JHN 14:30; HEB 2:14-2:15; 1JN 4:4; REV 3:21

## God's transforming power reverses the effects of evil in human lives
**2CO 3:18**; **1JN 3:2** *See also* JON 3:10; MRK 5:15 parallel LUK 8:35; LUK 19:8; ACT 26:17-26:18; ROM 6:14, ROM 12:2; 1CO 15:10; 2CO 12:9; COL 3:10

## God brings good out of evil
**GEN 50:20**; **ROM 8:28** *See also* GEN 45:8; JOB 23:10; JHN 9:3, JHN 12:24; ACT 2:36, ACT 3:13-3:16, ACT 5:30-5:31; PHP 1:12-1:14, PHP 1:17-1:18; JAS 1:2-1:3; 1PE 1:6-1:7

## The removal of the curse on creation
**ROM 8:20-8:21** *See also* MAT 19:28; ACT 3:21; REV 21:1, REV 21:5, REV 22:3

## Jesus Christ's victory over evil is expressed in his thousand-year reign with believers, during which Satan is bound
**REV 20:4** *See also* REV 20:2 *Interpreters differ over whether the “thousand years” refers to a future period or to Jesus Christ's present rule in the world.*

## The final defeat of all evil powers
**REV 20:4**; **ROM 16:20**; **REV 20:10** *See also* MAL 4:1; MAT 13:41-13:42, MAT 25:41; 1CO 15:25; 2TH 2:8; REV 19:20, REV 21:8

## Evil will be excluded from the new heaven and earth
**2PE 3:13** *See also* ISA 65:17; REV 21:4, REV 21:27, REV 22:15
