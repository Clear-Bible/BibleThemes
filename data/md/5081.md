# 5081: Adam, creation and life of

Adam is portrayed as the height of God's creation, made in the image and likeness of God, and entrusted with the stewardship of creation.



## The creation of Adam


### As a unique event
**GEN 2:7**

### In the image of God
**GEN 1:26-1:27** *See also* GEN 5:1; 1CO 11:7; JAS 3:9

### His commission from God

### His incompleteness without Eve
**GEN 2:18-2:24** *See also* GEN 1:27, GEN 5:1-5:2; MAT 19:4-19:6 parallel MRK 10:6-10:9

### His position and privileges
**GEN 1:28-1:29, GEN 2:19-2:20**

**GEN 2:16-2:17** *See also* GEN 3:1-3:3
