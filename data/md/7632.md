# 7632: Twelve, diverse characters of

The Twelve were men of varied background, gifts and character.



## The varied backgrounds of the Twelve
Peter, Andrew, James and John were fishermen:
 [MAT 4:18](bibleref:40004018), [MAT 4:21](bibleref:40004021)
[MAT 9:9](bibleref:40009009) *Matthew was a tax collector* ; [MAT 10:4](bibleref:40010004) *Simon was a political activist.* 

## The varied roles of the Twelve


### Some took a prominent role in the group
**[GAL 2:9](bibleref:48002009)**

### Some played minor but significant roles
 *See also* [JHN 1:40-1:42](bibleref:43001040-43001042) *Andrew brought Peter to Jesus Christ* ; [JHN 1:45-1:46](bibleref:43001045-43001046) *Philip brought Nathanael* ; [JHN 12:20-12:22](bibleref:43012020-43012022) *Philip and Andrew brought the Greeks to Jesus Christ.* 

## The varied temperaments of the Twelve
 [MAT 14:28](bibleref:40014028) *Peter was impetuous* ; [LUK 9:54](bibleref:42009054) *James and John were fiery* ; [LUK 22:4](bibleref:42022004) *Judas Iscariot was a traitorous loner* ; [JHN 20:25](bibleref:43020025) *Thomas was cautious.* 

## The Twelve had a common call to discipleship and witness
 [ACT 1:21-1:22](bibleref:44001021-44001022)

## The fallibility of the Twelve
 [MRK 8:31-8:33](bibleref:41008031-41008033) *Peter misunderstood* ; [MRK 16:14](bibleref:41016014) *Jesus Christ rebuked the Eleven for not believing reports of his resurrection* ; [LUK 9:12-9:13](bibleref:42009012-42009013) *Jesus Christ tested their reaction to human need* ; [JHN 14:8-14:9](bibleref:43014008-43014009) *Philip asked the way to the Father.* 

## Jesus Christ gave the Twelve special teaching
 [MRK 4:10-4:11](bibleref:41004010-41004011) *about his parables* ; [MRK 9:35](bibleref:41009035) *about power and position* ; [LUK 18:31](bibleref:42018031) *about his destiny* 

## Jesus Christ spent his last days and hours with the Twelve
 [MRK 11:11](bibleref:41011011) *after the triumphal entry into Jerusalem* ; [MRK 14:17](bibleref:41014017) *at the Last Supper* 

## The Twelve failed Jesus Christ


### Jesus Christ's concern that they would leave him
 [JHN 6:67-6:70](bibleref:43006067-43006070)

### Judas betrayed him
 [MAT 26:14-26:16](bibleref:40026014-40026016) parallels MRK 14:10, LUK 22:3-22:4

### They failed to support him in his final agony of prayer
 [MAT 26:40-26:41](bibleref:40026040-40026041) parallels MRK 14:37-14:38, LUK 22:45-22:46

### Peter disowned Jesus Christ
 [MAT 26:74-26:75](bibleref:40026074-40026075) parallels MRK 14:71-14:72, LUK 22:60-22:61

### All abandoned Jesus Christ
 [MAT 26:56](bibleref:40026056) *the exception was “the disciple whom Jesus loved”, present at the crucifixion (Jn 19:25-27)*
