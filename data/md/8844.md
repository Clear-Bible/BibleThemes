# 8844: unforgiveness

God remains unforgiving to those described as unrepentant, who blaspheme against the Holy Spirit and who are themselves unforgiving. Being unforgiving and demonstrating true love are mutually exclusive.



## The unforgiveness of God


### Towards the unrepentant
**DEU 29:18-29:21** *See also* EXO 23:21, EXO 34:7; NUM 14:18; JOS 24:19; 2KI 24:4; ISA 30:12-30:14; JER 5:7; LAM 3:42; HOS 1:6; LUK 18:10-18:14; HEB 10:28-10:31

### Towards those whose hearts are hardened
 JOS 11:20; ; MAT 13:13-13:15 parallels MRK 4:11-4:12, LUK 8:9-8:10; ; ISA 6:9-6:10; ; JHN 9:39-9:41, JHN 20:23

### Towards those who blaspheme against the Holy Spirit
**MAT 12:31-12:32** parallels MRK 3:28-3:29, LUK 12:10 *Blasphemy against the Spirit probably involves attributing the authenticating miracles of Jesus Christ to the work of Satan rather than to the power of the Holy Spirit.* *See also* 1JN 5:16

### Towards those who are not forgiving
**MAT 6:14-6:15**; **JAS 2:12-2:13** *See also* MAT 5:23-5:24, MAT 7:1-7:2 parallel LUK 6:37-6:38; MAT 18:32-18:35, MAT 23:23; MRK 11:25-11:26 *NIV footnote at verse 26*; ROM 14:9-14:13

## God's people call upon him not to forgive the wicked
**JER 18:23** *See also* NEH 4:5; ISA 2:9

## True love is forgiving
**ROM 5:8**; **1CO 13:4-13:5** *See also* PSA 86:5, PSA 130:3-130:4; DAN 9:9; MIC 7:18; ACT 13:38; ROM 15:7; 2CO 2:5-2:11; EPH 4:32; COL 3:13
