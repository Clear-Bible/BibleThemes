# 3224: Holy Spirit, and preaching

True Christian preaching is grounded in the word of God and applied by the Holy Spirit to its audience.



## The Holy Spirit is identified with the proclaimed word of God
**[EPH 6:17](bibleref:49006017)** *See also* [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063)

## The Holy Spirit sends God's messengers
**[ACT 13:4-13:5](bibleref:44013004-44013005)**

## The Holy Spirit equips, inspires and instructs God's messengers


### He inspires the OT prophets
**[MIC 3:8](bibleref:33003008)** *See also* [ISA 61:1-61:2](bibleref:23061001-23061002); [EZK 2:2-2:5](bibleref:26002002-26002005); [2PE 1:21](bibleref:61001021)

### He inspires Jesus Christ's teaching ministry
**[LUK 4:18-4:21](bibleref:42004018-42004021)** *See also* [ISA 61:1-61:2](bibleref:23061001-23061002); [MAT 12:18](bibleref:40012018); [ISA 42:1](bibleref:23042001); [JHN 3:34](bibleref:43003034)

### The apostles preach in the Spirit's power
**[1CO 2:4-2:5](bibleref:46002004-46002005)** *See also* [LUK 24:45-24:49](bibleref:42024045-42024049); [ACT 4:8-4:12](bibleref:44004008-44004012) *Peter* ; [ACT 6:10](bibleref:44006010) *Stephen* ; [ACT 13:4-13:5](bibleref:44013004-44013005) *Paul and Barnabas* ; [1TH 1:5](bibleref:52001005); [1PE 1:12](bibleref:60001012)

## The Holy Spirit teaches and applies the preached word of God to its hearers
**[JHN 14:26](bibleref:43014026)** *See also* [JHN 16:15](bibleref:43016015); [1CO 2:13](bibleref:46002013)
