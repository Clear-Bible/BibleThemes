# 8244: ethics, and grace

Ethical conduct on the part of believers rests upon both the grace of God and human obedience.



## People are unable to fulfil the law of God without divine grace
**MAT 15:19** parallel MRK 7:21-7:22**ROM 7:14-7:15** *See also* GEN 6:5; 1KI 8:46; PSA 51:5, PSA 64:6; ISA 53:6; ROM 1:21, ROM 3:23; GAL 3:22; 1JN 1:8

## Ethical conduct also depends on human obedience
**DEU 26:16**; **PHP 2:12** *See also* DEU 4:40, DEU 6:17; JOS 1:8, JOS 22:5; 1KI 2:3, 1KI 11:38; 2KI 17:13; JER 7:23; MAT 5:48, MAT 7:21, MAT 19:17; ROM 6:19; 2CO 7:1; 1TI 6:11; 2PE 1:5-1:7, 2PE 3:14; 1JN 3:3

## God assists believers' ethical endeavours


### He enlightens their minds
**PSA 119:130** *See also* DEU 4:1; PSA 119:144; PRO 6:23; MIC 6:8; ROM 7:7; 1CO 2:12-2:14

### He gives them his Spirit
**GAL 5:16** *See also* EZK 36:27; JHN 14:15-14:17; ROM 8:3-8:5, ROM 8:9, ROM 8:13; GAL 5:22-5:23; 2TH 2:13; 1PE 1:2

### He gives them spiritual resources
**2PE 1:3** *See also* 1KI 8:58; PSA 19:13; JHN 15:5; 1CO 10:13; EPH 6:11, EPH 6:13; PHP 2:13; 2TI 1:7; HEB 2:18; JAS 4:6

## God will bring believers to ethical perfection


### He will deliver them from the power of sin
**ROM 7:24-7:25** *See also* 1CO 1:8; EPH 4:24; COL 3:10; PHP 1:6; 1TH 3:13; JUD 1:24

### He will make them like Jesus Christ
**1JN 3:2** *See also* ROM 8:29; 2CO 3:18

### He is preparing them for a righteous existence
**2PE 3:13** *See also* MAT 13:41-13:43; REV 21:8, REV 21:27, REV 22:15
