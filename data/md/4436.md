# 4436: drinking, abstention from

Scripture indicates that the abuse of drinking, such as drunkenness, is unacceptable. It also indicates the advantages of abstention from drinking in certain situations.



## For some, at certain times, total abstinence is right
 LEV 10:8-10:9; ; EZK 44:21 *priests*; ; NUM 6:1-6:4, NUM 6:20 *Nazirites*; ; JDG 13:2-13:5, JDG 13:7, JDG 13:13-13:14; ; PRO 31:4-31:5; ; ISA 22:12-22:13; ; JER 35:1-35:14; ; DAN 1:5-1:16; MAT 11:18; ; LUK 1:15; 
MAT 27:34 *Jesus Christ*; ; ROM 14:21 *the Christian who does not want to give offence*

## Warnings against the abuse of alcohol
**ISA 5:11** *See also* PRO 20:1; ISA 5:22; HAB 2:15; LUK 21:34; ROM 13:13; 1CO 6:9-6:10, 1CO 11:20-11:21; GAL 5:19-5:21; EPH 5:18; 1TH 5:7-5:8; TIT 2:3

## Christian leaders are to be sober
**1TI 3:2-3:3** *See also* 1TI 3:8; TIT 1:7

## Examples of drunkenness
 GEN 9:20-9:21, GEN 19:30-19:35; ; 1SA 25:36; ; 2SA 11:12-11:13; ; 1KI 16:9, 1KI 20:16; ; EST 1:10; ; ISA 28:7, ISA 56:10-56:12; ; MAT 24:49 parallel LUK 12:45

## Some accusations of drunkenness were unfounded
 1SA 1:13-1:15; ; MAT 11:19 parallel LUK 7:34; ; ACT 2:13-2:15 *the disciples on the day of Pentecost*
