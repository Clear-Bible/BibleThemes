# 8770: idolatry, in NT

The NT world included many religions which promoted the worship of idols. Idolatry was thus of continuing importance to NT writers, especially Paul.



## Idolatry in the Gentile world
**[ACT 17:16](bibleref:44017016)** *See also* [ACT 14:11-14:13](bibleref:44014011-44014013), [ACT 17:22-17:23](bibleref:44017022-44017023), [ACT 19:24](bibleref:44019024); [1CO 8:5](bibleref:46008005); [GAL 4:8](bibleref:48004008)

## Criticism of idolatry


### Idolatry leads to other sinful behaviour
**[ROM 1:24](bibleref:45001024)** *Verses 22 and 24-25 show the link between idolatry and immoral conduct.* 

### Idolatry is an offence against the doctrine of creation
**[ROM 1:20](bibleref:45001020)** *See also* [ACT 17:24-17:29](bibleref:44017024-44017029)

### Idols are futile and degrading
**[ROM 1:22-1:23](bibleref:45001022-45001023)** *See also* [1CO 8:4](bibleref:46008004), [1CO 10:19](bibleref:46010019), [1CO 12:2](bibleref:46012002)

### Idolatrous worship of human beings
**[ACT 12:22](bibleref:44012022)** *See also* [LUK 20:24-20:25](bibleref:42020024-42020025) *Jesus Christ opposes the hypocrisy which objected to Roman coinage as idolatrous on the grounds that the emperor's head infringed the Ten Commandments* ; [ACT 28:6](bibleref:44028006)

### Demonic powers are involved with idolatry
**[1CO 10:20](bibleref:46010020)** *See also* [REV 9:20](bibleref:66009020), [REV 13:4](bibleref:66013004)

### Food sacrificed to idols
**[ACT 15:20](bibleref:44015020)** *See also* [ROM 14:2-14:3](bibleref:45014002-45014003), [ROM 14:6](bibleref:45014006); [1CO 8:4-8:13](bibleref:46008004-46008013), [1CO 10:14-10:31](bibleref:46010014-46010031)

### Encounters with idolatrous practice
**[ACT 19:28](bibleref:44019028)** *See also* [ACT 14:11-14:18](bibleref:44014011-44014018), [ACT 17:18-17:31](bibleref:44017018-44017031)

## Spiritual idolatry
**[1JN 5:21](bibleref:62005021)** *“idols” here may well refer to a wide range of God-substitutes.*
