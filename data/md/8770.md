# 8770: idolatry, in NT

The NT world included many religions which promoted the worship of idols. Idolatry was thus of continuing importance to NT writers, especially Paul.



## Idolatry in the Gentile world
**ACT 17:16** *See also* ACT 14:11-14:13, ACT 17:22-17:23, ACT 19:24; 1CO 8:5; GAL 4:8

## Criticism of idolatry


### Idolatry leads to other sinful behaviour
**ROM 1:24** *Verses 22 and 24-25 show the link between idolatry and immoral conduct.*

### Idolatry is an offence against the doctrine of creation
**ROM 1:20** *See also* ACT 17:24-17:29

### Idols are futile and degrading
**ROM 1:22-1:23** *See also* 1CO 8:4, 1CO 10:19, 1CO 12:2

### Idolatrous worship of human beings
**ACT 12:22** *See also* LUK 20:24-20:25 *Jesus Christ opposes the hypocrisy which objected to Roman coinage as idolatrous on the grounds that the emperor's head infringed the Ten Commandments*; ACT 28:6

### Demonic powers are involved with idolatry
**1CO 10:20** *See also* REV 9:20, REV 13:4

### Food sacrificed to idols
**ACT 15:20** *See also* ROM 14:2-14:3, ROM 14:6; 1CO 8:4-8:13, 1CO 10:14-10:31

### Encounters with idolatrous practice
**ACT 19:28** *See also* ACT 14:11-14:18, ACT 17:18-17:31

## Spiritual idolatry
**1JN 5:21** *“idols” here may well refer to a wide range of God-substitutes.*

## The temptations of Jesus Christ present three main kinds of spiritual idolatry


### Possessions
**MAT 4:3** parallel LUK 4:3 *See also* MAT 6:24 parallel LUK 16:13; LUK 18:23; PHP 3:19; COL 3:5; 1TI 6:10

### Prestige and self-esteem
**MAT 4:6** parallel LUK 4:9 *See also* LUK 3:8, LUK 10:29, LUK 18:11-18:12, LUK 18:21; ROM 2:19

### Power
**MAT 4:8-4:9** parallel LUK 4:6-4:7 *See also* LUK 9:54-9:55, LUK 23:39; JHN 18:10-18:11, JHN 19:10; PHP 2:6; JAS 4:6, JAS 4:10
