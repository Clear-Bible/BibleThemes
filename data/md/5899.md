# 5899: lament

A song of mourning or sorrow. Laments may be occasioned by bereavement, personal trouble, national disaster or the judgment of God.



## Lamentation occasioned by bereavement
**2SA 3:33-3:34** *See also* GEN 50:10; JER 31:15; MAT 2:16-2:18, MAT 9:23 parallels MRK 5:38, LUK 8:52; LUK 23:27; JHN 11:31-11:33

## Lamentation occasioned by personal trouble
 PSA 56:2, PSA 56:8, PSA 102:1-102:2

## Lamentation occasioned by national disaster
**2CH 35:25** *See also* 2SA 1:17-1:27; EST 4:1, EST 9:31; JER 8:21-9:1

## Lamentation occasioned by the judgment of God on Israel
**AMO 5:1-5:2** *The call to lament is sometimes meant as a warning of impending judgment*; **LAM 1:1** *The book of Lamentations is a series of laments after the fall of Jerusalem.* *See also* ISA 3:24-3:26, ISA 29:1-29:2; JER 4:7-4:8, JER 7:29, JER 9:10, JER 9:17-9:21; LAM 2:5; EZK 2:9-2:10, EZK 19:1-19:14

## Lamentation occasioned by the judgment of God on other nations
**ISA 16:9-16:11** *See also* ISA 15:5, ISA 15:8, ISA 16:7, ISA 19:8; JER 48:36; EZK 26:17-26:18, EZK 27:2-27:36, EZK 28:12-28:19, EZK 30:2-30:4, EZK 32:2-32:16

## Lamentation as a sign of repentance
 LAM 1:5, LAM 1:8, LAM 3:39-3:40; ; EZK 9:3-9:6
