# 5899: lament

A song of mourning or sorrow. Laments may be occasioned by bereavement, personal trouble, national disaster or the judgment of God.

## Lamentation occasioned by bereavement
**[2SA 3:33-3:34](bibleref:10003033-10003034)** *See also* [GEN 50:10](bibleref:01050010); [JER 31:15](bibleref:24031015); [MAT 2:16-2:18](bibleref:40002016-40002018), [MAT 9:23](bibleref:40009023) parallels MRK 5:38, LUK 8:52; [LUK 23:27](bibleref:42023027); [JHN 11:31-11:33](bibleref:43011031-43011033)
