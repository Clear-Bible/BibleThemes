# 2330: Jesus Christ, as shepherd

A title for Jesus Christ indicating believers' dependence on him as he protects, tends and guides those whom he knows intimately.



## Prophecies about the shepherd Messiah
**EZK 34:23** *See also* EZK 37:24; MIC 5:2; MAT 2:6; MIC 5:4; ZEC 13:7; MAT 26:31 parallel MRK 14:27

## Jesus Christ's role as the good shepherd


### He has compassion for the helpless
**MAT 9:36** *See also* MRK 6:34; JHN 11:52

### He seeks lost sheep
**MAT 18:12-18:14** parallel LUK 15:3-15:7 *See also* MAT 10:6, MAT 15:24; LUK 19:10; JHN 10:16

### He knows his own sheep
**JHN 10:14-10:15** *See also* MAT 25:32; JHN 10:27

### His sheep know his voice
**JHN 10:4** *See also* JHN 10:3, JHN 10:5, JHN 10:16, JHN 10:27

### He provides for and protects his sheep
**JHN 10:9** *See also* JHN 10:28

### He lays down his life for his sheep
**JHN 10:11** *See also* MAT 26:31 parallel MRK 14:27; JHN 10:14-10:15, JHN 10:17-10:18

### He judges the sheep
 MAT 25:32-25:46

## Jesus Christ shepherds his flock


### Christians are Jesus Christ's flock
**LUK 12:32** *See also* MAT 10:16; JHN 10:16; ACT 20:29; ROM 8:36

### Under-shepherds are appointed over Jesus Christ's flock
**JHN 21:15-21:17** *See also* ACT 20:28; 1PE 5:2-5:4

## Jesus Christ the risen Lord is Shepherd
**REV 7:17** *See also* HEB 13:20; 1PE 2:25, 1PE 5:4
