# 2330: Jesus Christ, as shepherd

A title for Jesus Christ indicating believers' dependence on him as he protects, tends and guides those whom he knows intimately.



## Prophecies about the shepherd Messiah
**[EZK 34:23](bibleref:26034023)** *See also* [EZK 37:24](bibleref:26037024); [MIC 5:2](bibleref:33005002); [MAT 2:6](bibleref:40002006); [MIC 5:4](bibleref:33005004); [ZEC 13:7](bibleref:38013007); [MAT 26:31](bibleref:40026031) parallel MRK 14:27
