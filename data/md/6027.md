# 6027: sin, God's remedy for

Under the old covenant, sin was forgiven through sacrifice, prefiguring the atoning death of Jesus Christ, which brings forgiveness of sins under the new covenant.



## Atonement for sins in the OT was through the shedding of blood
**[LEV 17:11](bibleref:03017011)**

## Kinds of sin offering in the OT


### The sin offering was for unintentional sins
**[LEV 4:1-5:13](bibleref:03004001-03005013)** *See also* [NUM 15:22-15:31](bibleref:04015022-04015031)

### The guilt offering was for unintentional sins, where restitution was required
**[LEV 5:14-6:7](bibleref:03005014-03006007)** *See also* [NUM 5:5-5:10](bibleref:04005005-04005010)

### The annual Day of Atonement cleansed the nation of sin
**[LEV 16:1-16:34](bibleref:03016001-03016034)** *See also* [EXO 30:11-30:16](bibleref:02030011-02030016); [LEV 23:26-23:32](bibleref:03023026-03023032); [HEB 9:7](bibleref:58009007)

### The priests made atonement on occasions of deliberate national sin
 [NUM 16:46-16:48](bibleref:04016046-04016048) *Aaron stops the plague after Korah's sin* ; [NUM 25:13](bibleref:04025013) *Phinehas stops the plague after Moab caused Israel to sin.* 

## Sacrifices of atonement needed to be accompanied by repentance and a willingness to obey


### Worshippers recognised the need for repentance and obedience
**[PRO 21:3](bibleref:20021003)** *See also* [PSA 40:6-40:8](bibleref:19040006-19040008), [PSA 51:16-51:17](bibleref:19051016-19051017)

### The prophets declared the need for obedience
**[1SA 15:22](bibleref:09015022)** *See also* [ISA 1:11-1:17](bibleref:23001011-23001017); [JER 7:21-7:23](bibleref:24007021-24007023); [HOS 6:6](bibleref:28006006); [MIC 6:6-6:8](bibleref:33006006-33006008)

### The prophets warned of judgment to bring people to repentance
**[2KI 17:13](bibleref:12017013)** *See also* [ISA 31:6-31:7](bibleref:23031006-23031007); [JER 4:1-4:4](bibleref:24004001-24004004), [JER 35:15](bibleref:24035015); [EZK 3:16-3:19](bibleref:26003016-26003019); [JON 3:4-3:10](bibleref:32003004-32003010)

## The death of Jesus Christ brings forgiveness for sin


### Jesus Christ died on behalf of sinful humanity
**[ROM 5:6](bibleref:45005006)** *See also* [MAT 26:26-26:28](bibleref:40026026-40026028); [JHN 10:11](bibleref:43010011), [JHN 15:13](bibleref:43015013); [GAL 2:20](bibleref:48002020)

### The early church proclaimed that Christ died for the sins of others
**[1CO 15:3](bibleref:46015003)** *See also* [ROM 4:25](bibleref:45004025); [GAL 1:4](bibleref:48001004); [1PE 3:18](bibleref:60003018)

### Jesus Christ bore sin on the cross
**[2CO 5:21](bibleref:47005021)** *See also* [ISA 53:10-53:12](bibleref:23053010-23053012); [HEB 9:28](bibleref:58009028); [1PE 2:24](bibleref:60002024)

### Jesus Christ has redeemed people by taking their place
**[MAT 20:28](bibleref:40020028)** parallel MRK 10:45 *See also* [GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013); [1TI 2:6](bibleref:54002006); [TIT 2:14](bibleref:56002014)

### Jesus Christ's death is sacrificial
**[ROM 3:25](bibleref:45003025)** *See also* [ROM 8:3](bibleref:45008003); [1CO 5:7](bibleref:46005007); [EPH 5:2](bibleref:49005002); [HEB 7:27](bibleref:58007027), [HEB 10:5-10:13](bibleref:58010005-58010013); [1JN 2:2](bibleref:62002002), [1JN 4:10](bibleref:62004010)

### The shedding of Jesus Christ's blood brings forgiveness
**[1JN 1:7](bibleref:62001007)** *See also* [JHN 1:29](bibleref:43001029); [EPH 1:7](bibleref:49001007); [HEB 9:12-9:22](bibleref:58009012-58009022), [HEB 13:12](bibleref:58013012); [1PE 1:18-1:19](bibleref:60001018-60001019); [REV 7:14](bibleref:66007014)
