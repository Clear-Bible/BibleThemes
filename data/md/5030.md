# 5030: knowledge, of Jesus Christ

The believer's understanding and experience of Jesus Christ, which arises from a personal relationship established through faith.



## Jesus Christ is the supreme revelation of God
**JHN 14:6-14:10** *See also* MAT 11:27; JHN 1:1-1:2, JHN 8:19, JHN 17:26; COL 1:15-1:20; HEB 1:3

## Knowledge of Jesus Christ acquired through a personal relationship with him
**JHN 4:42** *See also* MAT 9:4-9:8 parallels MRK 2:8-2:12, LUK 5:22-5:26; JHN 10:38, JHN 14:11

## Knowledge of Jesus Christ acquired through the work of the Holy Spirit
**JHN 15:26** *See also* 1CO 12:3; 1JN 5:6-5:9

## Knowledge of Jesus Christ is associated with knowing God
**JHN 17:26** *See also* JHN 8:19, JHN 10:14-10:15, JHN 10:27-10:30, JHN 14:6-14:11, JHN 15:15

## Knowledge of Jesus Christ should increase consistently
**PHP 3:10**; **2PE 3:18** *See also* 2PE 1:5-1:8

## Consequences of knowing Jesus Christ


### Reconciliation with God
 2CO 5:19-5:20; ; EPH 2:16

### Access to God
 ROM 5:1-5:2; ; EPH 2:18

### Becoming like Jesus Christ
**PHP 3:10**

### Peace
 *See also* JHN 14:1, JHN 16:33; ROM 5:1-5:2; EPH 2:14-2:18

### Hope
 EPH 1:17-1:18

### Eternal life
**JHN 17:3** *See also* JHN 10:27-10:28; 1JN 5:13, 1JN 5:20

### Renewal
 EPH 4:19-4:22

### Unity among believers
 JHN 17:21

### Rejection by the world
 JHN 15:18-15:21, JHN 16:2-16:3, JHN 17:14

## Knowledge of Jesus Christ is to manifest itself in obedience
**1JN 2:3-2:6** *See also* MAT 7:21-7:23, MAT 25:31-25:46; JHN 14:15, JHN 14:21, JHN 14:23, JHN 15:4-15:5
