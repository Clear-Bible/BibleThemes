# 8780: materialism, as an aspect of sin

The pursuit of possessions and wealth and a preoccupation with physical things is futile and dissatisfying.



## Materialism as the result of human sin


### At the fall
**GEN 3:6**

### Among pagans
**ROM 1:22-1:23** *See also* PSA 115:2-115:8; ISA 44:9-44:11; DAN 3:1, DAN 3:4-3:7; HAB 2:18; ACT 17:16; 2TI 3:2

### Within Israel itself
**EXO 20:4-20:5** *See also* EXO 32:4; LEV 26:1; DEU 4:15-4:19, DEU 7:25; 1KI 11:1-11:6; PSA 106:19-106:20; ISA 42:8; JER 2:11-2:13; 1CO 10:14

## Materialism and possessions


### Possessions are to be seen as a gift from God
**1CH 29:12** *See also* GEN 13:2, GEN 26:12-26:13; DEU 8:18; 2CH 32:29; PSA 112:1-112:3; PRO 10:22; ECC 5:19; HOS 2:8

### Material goods can turn the heart away from God
**DEU 8:13-8:14**; **1TI 6:9-6:10** *See also* JOB 31:24-31:28; EZK 28:5; MAT 19:21-19:22 parallel LUK 18:22-18:23; MAT 26:14-26:16; 1JN 3:17

### Possessions and riches can make it difficult to enter the kingdom
**MAT 19:23-19:24** parallels MRK 10:24-10:25, LUK 18:24-18:25 *See also* MAT 13:22 parallels MRK 4:18-4:19, LUK 8:14; 1TI 6:17-6:19; JAS 2:2-2:5, JAS 5:1-5:6

## The weaknesses of materialism


### It is transitory
**PRO 27:24** *See also* JOB 22:20; PSA 49:10, PSA 49:17; PRO 22:1-22:2, PRO 23:5; ECC 2:18, ECC 2:21, ECC 2:26; JER 17:11; LUK 12:20-12:21; 1TI 6:7

### It does not satisfy
**ECC 5:10** *See also* ECC 4:8, ECC 5:12; HAG 1:5-1:6; MAT 6:19

### It can encourage greed
**1KI 21:2-21:4** *See also* EXO 20:17 parallel DEU 5:21; JOS 7:1, JOS 7:20-7:24; 1KI 10:23-10:27

### It can lead to worldliness
**MAT 16:26** parallels MRK 8:36-8:37, LUK 9:24-9:25 *See also* JER 17:11; LUK 16:15, LUK 16:25-16:26; JAS 1:9-1:11; REV 3:17
