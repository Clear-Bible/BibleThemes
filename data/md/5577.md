# 5577: taxation

Any form of payment levied on people by a supervising authority.



## Taxation exacted from subject nations


### Tribute to Moab
 JDG 3:15, JDG 3:17-3:18

### Tribute to David
 2SA 8:2 parallel 1CH 18:2; ; 2SA 8:6 parallel 1CH 18:6

### Tribute to Solomon
 1KI 4:21

### Tribute to Assyria
 2KI 17:3; ; HOS 10:6

### Tribute to Egypt
 2KI 23:33 parallel 2CH 36:3

### Tribute to Judah
 2CH 17:11, 2CH 26:8; ; ISA 16:1

### Tribute to Persia
 EST 10:1

### Universal tribute
**PSA 72:10** *Universal tribute means universal reign. This psalm is widely regarded as Messianic.*

### Refusal to pay tribute was a sign of rebellion
 *See also* 2KI 17:4; EZR 4:13

## Taxation for the support of the state


### Land tax
**2KI 23:35** *See also* GEN 41:34; NEH 5:4

## Census tax
 EXO 30:11-30:16; ; LUK 2:1

### Trade tax
**1KI 10:14-10:15** parallel 2CH 9:13-9:14

### Such levies may be unjustly burdensome
**1SA 8:10-8:18** *See also* DAN 11:20

## Covenantal taxation: the tithe


### Tithing prescribed
**LEV 27:30-27:33** *As part of their covenantal obligations the Israelites were commanded to offer a tenth of their income to the LORD.* *See also* GEN 14:20

### The tithe supported the priests and the poor
**NUM 18:21** *See also* DEU 14:28-14:29

### The tithe often seems to have been neglected
**MAL 3:8-3:10** *See also* 2CH 31:4

## Taxation for the support of the temple
**MAT 17:24-17:26** *See also* 2CH 24:4-24:12 parallel 2KI 12:4-12:5

## Jesus Christ and taxation


### Jesus Christ was falsely accused of opposing taxation
 LUK 23:1-23:2

### He himself paid tax
 MAT 17:24-17:27

### He commanded others to pay taxes
 MAT 22:15-22:22 parallels MRK 12:13-12:17, LUK 20:20-20:26

## Believers and taxation


### Paying taxes demonstrates submission to God
 ROM 13:1, ROM 13:5-13:6; ; 1PE 2:13

### Paying taxes should silence unjust criticism
 ROM 13:3; ; 1PE 2:15

### Paying taxes is reimbursement for services rendered
