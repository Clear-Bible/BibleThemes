# 7263: theocracy

The form of government among the early Israelites in which God was their supreme ruler, and his laws their laws.

## The covenant at Sinai established the theocracy
**[EXO 19:5-19:6](bibleref:02019005-02019006)** *See also* [EXO 19:8](bibleref:02019008), [EXO 24:3](bibleref:02024003), [EXO 24:7-24:8](bibleref:02024007-02024008); [DEU 5:27](bibleref:05005027)

## Moses and Joshua renewed the theocratic covenant
 [DEU 26:16-26:19](bibleref:05026016-05026019), [DEU 29:9-29:15](bibleref:05029009-05029015); [JOS 24:24-24:25](bibleref:06024024-06024025)

## Under the theocracy God was the Israelites' supreme ruler
**[DEU 33:5](bibleref:05033005)** *“Jeshurun” means “the upright one”, i.e., Israel.*  *See also* [NUM 23:21](bibleref:04023021); [DEU 33:26](bibleref:05033026); [JDG 8:23](bibleref:07008023); [PSA 145:1](bibleref:19145001)

## In the theocracy Israel was governed by God's laws
