# 3050: Holy Spirit, wisdom of

The quality of being discerning and perceptive which is a characteristic of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit is the source of human wisdom.



## Those who knew and can know the wisdom of the Holy Spirit


### Joshua
**[DEU 34:9](bibleref:05034009)** *Either a wise human spirit or the Spirit of God who gives wisdom (NIV footnote).* 

### The Messiah
**[ISA 11:2](bibleref:23011002)**

### Jesus Christ
**[LUK 2:40](bibleref:42002040)** *See also* [COL 2:9](bibleref:51002009) *“fulness” includes wisdom.*

**[EPH 1:17](bibleref:49001017)**
