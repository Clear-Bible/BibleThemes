# 3245: Holy Spirit, blasphemy against

The denial of the action of the Holy Spirit in the ministry of Jesus Christ, or the attribution of his works to demonic influence.



## Jesus Christ warns against blasphemy
**[MRK 3:29](bibleref:41003029)** parallels MAT 12:31-12:32, LUK 12:10 *See also* [1JN 5:16](bibleref:62005016)

## Blasphemy as erroneously attributing the work of the Holy Spirit to demonic influence


### Jesus Christ is accused of being possessed by demons
**[MRK 3:22](bibleref:41003022)** parallels MAT 12:24, LUK 11:15 *See also* [MAT 10:25](bibleref:40010025); [JHN 7:20](bibleref:43007020), [JHN 8:48](bibleref:43008048), [JHN 8:52](bibleref:43008052), [JHN 10:20](bibleref:43010020)

### Jesus Christ is accused of casting out demons by demonic power
**[MAT 9:34](bibleref:40009034)** *See also* [MAT 12:24](bibleref:40012024) parallels MRK 3:22, LUK 11:15

### Jesus Christ refutes these charges
 [MAT 12:25-12:29](bibleref:40012025-40012029) parallels MRK 3:23-3:27, LUK 11:17-11:22
