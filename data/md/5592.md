# 5592: treaty

A formal agreement between individuals or nations, promising commitment to each other's interests. Israel was forbidden to enter into such agreements with other nations in the promised land.



## Treaties between individuals
**1SA 18:3-18:4** *See also* GEN 21:22-21:32, GEN 26:26-26:31; 1SA 20:8, 1SA 20:16-20:17, 1SA 22:8, 1SA 23:18; 2SA 3:12-3:13

## Treaties between individuals acting on behalf of their nation
 1KI 5:12-5:18, 1KI 15:18-15:19; ; ISA 33:8 *Probably the agreement made when Hezekiah paid large sums to Sennacherib (2Ki 18:14)*; ; EZK 17:12-17:14

## Treaties between cities or nations
 JOS 9:3-9:16, JOS 10:1, JOS 11:18-11:19; ; 1SA 11:1-11:2; ; HOS 12:1

## Treaties entered into should be honoured
**EZK 17:15-17:18** *See also* JER 34:8-34:20; DAN 11:22-11:24 *“he will act deceitfully” (verse 23), is a reference to Antiochus Epiphanes (of the Seleucid dynasty) who reneged on the friendship shown to Egypt*; HOS 10:4; AMO 1:9-1:10; GAL 3:15

## Israel forbidden to enter into treaties with other nations in the promised land
**DEU 7:1-7:2** *See also* EXO 23:31-23:32, EXO 34:11-34:12, EXO 34:15-34:16; DEU 23:6; 1KI 20:34, 1KI 20:42-20:43; EZR 9:12
