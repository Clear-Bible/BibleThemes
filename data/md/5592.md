# 5592: treaty

A formal agreement between individuals or nations, promising commitment to each other's interests. Israel was forbidden to enter into such agreements with other nations in the promised land.

**[1SA 18:3-18:4](bibleref:09018003-09018004)** *See also* [GEN 21:22-21:32](bibleref:01021022-01021032), [GEN 26:26-26:31](bibleref:01026026-01026031); [1SA 20:8](bibleref:09020008), [1SA 20:16-20:17](bibleref:09020016-09020017), [1SA 22:8](bibleref:09022008), [1SA 23:18](bibleref:09023018); [2SA 3:12-3:13](bibleref:10003012-10003013)
