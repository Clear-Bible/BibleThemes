# 2327: Jesus Christ, as servant

Jesus Christ laid aside his majesty in order to serve humanity. His death is the supreme example of his servanthood: the fulfilling of the will of God his Father.



## The prophets speak of the Messiah as a servant
**[MAT 12:17-12:21](bibleref:40012017-40012021)** *See also* [ISA 42:1-42:4](bibleref:23042001-23042004), [ISA 49:1-49:7](bibleref:23049001-23049007), [ISA 50:4-50:9](bibleref:23050004-23050009), [ISA 52:13-53:12](bibleref:23052013-23053012); [EZK 34:23-34:24](bibleref:26034023-26034024), [EZK 37:24-37:25](bibleref:26037024-26037025); [ZEC 3:8](bibleref:38003008)
