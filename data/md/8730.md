# 8730: enemies, of believers

Christians must expect to face many enemies on account of their faith. Jesus Christ made it clear that such opposition was the inevitable result of commitment to him. Nevertheless, Christians are called upon to love their enemies.



## Christians are enemies of the world because they belong to Jesus Christ
**JHN 15:18-15:19** *See also* PSA 86:14; JHN 17:14; 2TI 3:12; 1PE 4:15-4:16; 1JN 3:13

## Opposition to Christians is opposition to Jesus Christ
**MAT 25:34-25:40** *See also* MRK 9:42; LUK 17:1; ACT 9:4-9:5

## Particular enemies of Christians


### All kinds of people
**MRK 13:13** parallel MAT 10:22 *See also* JHN 15:21

### Family members
**MAT 10:34-10:36** parallel LUK 12:51-12:53 *See also* GEN 37:1-37:11; MIC 7:6; MAT 10:21 parallel MRK 13:12

### Religious zealots
 ACT 4:18, ACT 5:28, ACT 8:3, ACT 9:1-9:2; ; 2PE 2:1; ; 3JN 1:9-1:10

## Christians may be killed by their enemies
**JHN 16:2** *See also* ACT 7:56-7:60, ACT 12:1-12:2, ACT 26:9-26:10; REV 6:9

## Christian attitudes towards enemies


### To love, do good, bless and pray for them
**MAT 5:44-5:45** *See also* JOB 31:29-31:30; LUK 6:27-6:29, LUK 6:35, LUK 23:34; ACT 7:59-7:60; ROM 12:14; 1PE 3:9

### To forgive them
 MAT 6:12-6:15; ; LUK 11:4, LUK 23:33-23:34; ; ACT 7:59-7:60

### To help and show kindness to them
**ROM 12:20** *See also* PRO 25:21-25:22; LUK 10:25-10:37

### To show kindness to their beasts of burden
**EXO 23:4-23:5**

### Not to rejoice in their misfortunes
**PRO 24:17-24:18**

### To win them with kindness
**ROM 12:21** *See also* 1SA 26:21

## Christians have a spiritual enemy


### Satan opposes Christians
**1PE 5:8** *See also* JOB 1:7-1:9; LUK 22:31; 2TI 4:17-4:18

### The need to engage in spiritual warfare
**1PE 5:9** *See also* 2CO 10:4; EPH 6:10-6:18; 1TI 5:14-5:15; 2TI 4:7; JAS 4:7

## God supports his people against their enemies
**PSA 23:5**; **JHN 17:15** *See also* DEU 33:27; 1SA 12:11; 2SA 22:1-22:3; PSA 18:2, PSA 43:1, PSA 46:1, PSA 59:1-59:2, PSA 71:1-71:4, PSA 91:1-91:4, PSA 119:134, PSA 140:1-140:2, PSA 143:9, PSA 144:11; MAT 10:28-10:31; LUK 1:69, LUK 1:71, LUK 22:32; 2TI 4:8

## Death as the last enemy to be destroyed by Jesus Christ
**1CO 15:26** *See also* PSA 116:8; 2TI 1:10; REV 20:14, REV 21:4
