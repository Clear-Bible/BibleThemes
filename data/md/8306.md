# 8306: mercifulness

An attitude of compassion and care, grounded in the nature of God himself, made manifest in the life and ministry of Jesus Christ, and expected of believers.



## The mercifulness of God
**DEU 4:31**; **PSA 78:38**; **JER 3:12** *See also* DEU 7:7-7:8; NEH 9:31; ZEC 1:16; LUK 1:50-1:54; JAS 5:11

## The expression of God's mercifulness


### In the forgiveness of sins
**PSA 78:38** *See also* 2SA 24:14; 1KI 8:50; PSA 26:11, PSA 51:1; DAN 9:9

### In response to prayer
**PSA 6:2, PSA 27:7** *See also* 1KI 8:28; PSA 9:13

## The mercifulness of Jesus Christ
**MAT 9:36**; **HEB 2:17** *See also* MAT 8:16; HEB 4:16

## The mercifulness expected of believers
**LUK 6:36** *See also* MIC 6:8; ZEC 7:9; MAT 9:13, MAT 18:21-18:35 *the parable of the unmerciful servant*; MAT 23:23; LUK 10:29-10:37 *the parable of the good Samaritan*; ROM 12:8; EPH 4:32-5:2; JAS 2:12-2:13, JAS 3:17; JUD 1:22-1:23

## The blessing promised to the merciful
**MAT 5:7** *Jesus Christ to his disciples.*
