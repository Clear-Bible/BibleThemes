# 5668: children, responsibilities to parents

Children owe their parents certain important responsibilities, including the duty to honour and obey them.



## The duties of children towards their parents


### They are to honour their parents
**EXO 20:12** parallel DEU 5:16 *See also* PRO 17:6; MRK 7:10; EPH 6:1-6:3

### They are to obey their parents
**COL 3:20-3:21** *See also* EPH 6:1

### They are to behave well
**PRO 20:11**

## Children as a blessing and source of joy to their parents
**PSA 127:4-127:5**; **PRO 23:24** *See also* PSA 128:3; PRO 15:20, PRO 27:11, PRO 29:3; LUK 15:23-15:24

## Children born to barren mothers bring special joy
Sarah:
 GEN 18:11, GEN 21:2; GEN 29:31, GEN 30:22; JDG 13:2, JDG 13:24; 1SA 1:5, 1SA 1:20; LUK 1:7, LUK 1:24, LUK 1:57

## Children as a source of grief and disappointment to their parents
**PRO 17:25** *See also* 1SA 8:3; PRO 17:21, PRO 19:13, PRO 19:26, PRO 28:7, PRO 29:15

## The punishment of children


### The punishment of wicked children
 EXO 21:15; ; LEV 20:9; ; DEU 21:18-21:21; ; PRO 30:17; ; 2TI 3:2

## The punishment of children for the sins of their fathers
 EXO 20:5-20:6; ; NUM 14:18; ; JER 31:29-31:30 *Jeremiah here stresses the importance of individual responsibility in the present crisis without denying the notion of corporate responsibility*; ; JER 32:18
