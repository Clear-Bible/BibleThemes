# 6040: sinners

All who have sinned. The term tends to be used in particular of those regarded as outside God's covenant of grace, whose sins are therefore not forgiven.

## The universality of sinful behaviour


### Only Jesus Christ is sinless
**[1PE 2:22](bibleref:60002022)** *See also* [ISA 53:9](bibleref:23053009); [2CO 5:21](bibleref:47005021); [HEB 4:15](bibleref:58004015), [HEB 7:26](bibleref:58007026); [1JN 3:5](bibleref:62003005)

### Everyone else has sinned
**[GAL 3:22](bibleref:48003022)** *See also* [1KI 8:46](bibleref:11008046); [ROM 3:9-3:19](bibleref:45003009-45003019), [ROM 3:23](bibleref:45003023), [ROM 5:12](bibleref:45005012), [ROM 5:19](bibleref:45005019)
