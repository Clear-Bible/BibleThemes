# 5440: perjury

Swearing falsely, usually in a legal context, either by taking a false oath or by breaking one's oath later. God hates perjury.



## God hates perjury
**ZEC 8:16-8:17** *See also* PSA 24:3-24:4; ISA 48:1; JER 5:2, JER 7:9-7:10; HOS 10:4; ZEC 5:3-5:4; MAL 3:5; 1TI 1:9-1:10

## The Law forbade perjury
 EXO 20:16 parallel DEU 5:20; ; EXO 23:1-23:3; ; LEV 19:12; ; NUM 30:2

## Punishment for perjury
 LEV 6:1-6:7; ; DEU 19:16-19:21

## Examples of perjury


### Against Naboth
 1KI 21:1-21:19

### Against Jesus Christ
 MAT 26:59-26:61 parallel MRK 14:55-14:59

### Against Stephen
 ACT 6:12-6:14
