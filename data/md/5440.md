# 5440: perjury

Swearing falsely, usually in a legal context, either by taking a false oath or by breaking one's oath later. God hates perjury.

## God hates perjury
**[ZEC 8:16-8:17](bibleref:38008016-38008017)** *See also* [PSA 24:3-24:4](bibleref:19024003-19024004); [ISA 48:1](bibleref:23048001); [JER 5:2](bibleref:24005002), [JER 7:9-7:10](bibleref:24007009-24007010); [HOS 10:4](bibleref:28010004); [ZEC 5:3-5:4](bibleref:38005003-38005004); [MAL 3:5](bibleref:39003005); [1TI 1:9-1:10](bibleref:54001009-54001010)
