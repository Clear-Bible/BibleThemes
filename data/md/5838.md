# 5838: disrespect

The attitude of heart that leads people to treat God and others in ways that demonstrate a lack of esteem.

## Causes of disrespect for God and Jesus Christ


### Disrespect caused by a wrong view of God and Jesus Christ
**[ROM 1:21-1:23](bibleref:45001021-45001023)** *See also* [LUK 23:8-23:11](bibleref:42023008-42023011)

### Disrespect caused by disobedience to God
**[NEH 9:26](bibleref:16009026)** *See also* [2CH 24:17-24:20](bibleref:14024017-14024020); [2TI 3:1-3:5](bibleref:55003001-55003005)

### Disrespect caused by Satan
**[ACT 5:3](bibleref:44005003)** *See also* [MAT 8:28-8:29](bibleref:40008028-40008029) parallels MRK 5:1-5:7, LUK 8:26-8:28

## Causes of disrespect for others


### Disrespect for others caused by a wrong view of people
**[MAT 19:13-19:14](bibleref:40019013-40019014)** parallels MRK 10:13-10:14, LUK 18:15-18:16 *The lack of respect for the children arose from the erroneous belief that they were not important enough for Jesus Christ to bother with.*  *See also* [LUK 10:25-10:37](bibleref:42010025-42010037) *Those who passed by failed to heed God's command to love their neighbours as themselves* ; [JAS 2:1-2:4](bibleref:59002001-59002004)

### Disrespect for others caused by seeking power and wealth
**[ECC 4:1](bibleref:21004001)** *See also* [JAS 5:1-5:6](bibleref:59005001-59005006)

### Disrespect for others caused by envy
**[MIC 2:1-2:2](bibleref:33002001-33002002)** *See also* [GEN 37:3-37:4](bibleref:01037003-01037004)

### Disrespect for others caused by pride and contempt
**[PSA 31:18](bibleref:19031018)** *See also* [MAT 27:27-27:31](bibleref:40027027-40027031) parallel MRK 15:16-15:20

## Disrespect shown in speech
**[JAS 3:9](bibleref:59003009)** *See also* [MAT 27:39-27:44](bibleref:40027039-40027044) parallels MRK 15:29-15:32, LUK 23:35-23:39; [1TI 6:3-6:5](bibleref:54006003-54006005)
