# 5838: disrespect

The attitude of heart that leads people to treat God and others in ways that demonstrate a lack of esteem.



## Causes of disrespect for God and Jesus Christ


### Disrespect caused by a wrong view of God and Jesus Christ
**ROM 1:21-1:23** *See also* LUK 23:8-23:11

### Disrespect caused by disobedience to God
**NEH 9:26** *See also* 2CH 24:17-24:20; 2TI 3:1-3:5

### Disrespect caused by Satan
**ACT 5:3** *See also* MAT 8:28-8:29 parallels MRK 5:1-5:7, LUK 8:26-8:28

## Causes of disrespect for others


### Disrespect for others caused by a wrong view of people
**MAT 19:13-19:14** parallels MRK 10:13-10:14, LUK 18:15-18:16 *The lack of respect for the children arose from the erroneous belief that they were not important enough for Jesus Christ to bother with.* *See also* LUK 10:25-10:37 *Those who passed by failed to heed God's command to love their neighbours as themselves*; JAS 2:1-2:4

### Disrespect for others caused by seeking power and wealth
**ECC 4:1** *See also* JAS 5:1-5:6

### Disrespect for others caused by envy
**MIC 2:1-2:2** *See also* GEN 37:3-37:4

### Disrespect for others caused by pride and contempt
**PSA 31:18** *See also* MAT 27:27-27:31 parallel MRK 15:16-15:20

## Disrespect shown in speech
**JAS 3:9** *See also* MAT 27:39-27:44 parallels MRK 15:29-15:32, LUK 23:35-23:39; 1TI 6:3-6:5

## The development of disrespect


### Disrespect can grow insidiously
**1KI 11:4** *See also* JER 2:1-2:11MRK 11:15-11:17 parallels MAT 21:12-21:13, LUK 19:45-19:46, JHN 2:13-2:17

### Disrespect can spread infectiously
**DEU 1:27-1:28** *See also* EST 1:16-1:18; MRK 15:9-15:15 parallels MAT 27:17-27:26, LUK 23:13-23:25; ROM 2:24

### Disrespect can end fatally
**LEV 10:1-10:2** *See also* EXO 32:25-32:28; DEU 21:18-21:21; JER 25:4-25:11
