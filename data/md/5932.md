# 5932: response

People are to respond to God in obedience, worship and repentance and in love for others. Lack of response from others can be a sign of death, disobedience and defiance.



## The response of people to God


### Obedience and love for others
**EXO 24:3**; **1JN 4:11** *See also* EXO 19:8, EXO 24:7; 1CH 29:6-29:9; ROM 15:7; GAL 2:2; PHP 2:1-2:4; 1JN 2:3-2:6, 1JN 3:16-3:17

### Worship
**NEH 8:6**; **ROM 12:1** *See also* NEH 12:24; DAN 4:34-4:37

### Repentance
 2KI 22:18-22:20 parallel 2CH 34:26-34:28; ; ACT 2:37-2:38

### Response to the preaching of the gospel
**ACT 16:14** *See also* ACT 16:31-16:34; 1CO 15:1-15:2; EPH 1:13; COL 1:6; 1TH 2:13

### Inappropriate responses
**2CH 32:24-32:25** *See also* DAN 4:24-4:32; ROM 1:21-1:23

## The response of God to people


### God responds to those who seek him in prayer
**PSA 102:17** *See also* PSA 3:4, PSA 40:1-40:3; ISA 19:22; DAN 10:12; MAT 7:7-7:11 parallel LUK 11:9-11:13; LUK 11:5-11:8; JHN 14:13-14:14, JHN 15:7, JHN 16:24

### Sometimes God appears not to respond to prayer
 JOB 19:7; ; PSA 10:1, PSA 13:1-13:2, PSA 22:1-22:2, PSA 42:9-42:10; ; ISA 1:15, ISA 59:1-59:2

## False gods do not respond to people
**1KI 18:26-18:29** *See also* DEU 4:28; ISA 44:18; JER 10:5

## The response of people to others


### Believers should respond to others in kindness and forgiveness
**EPH 4:32** *See also* GEN 50:15, GEN 50:21; MAT 5:38-5:42 parallel LUK 6:29-6:30

### Harsh or aggressive responses
 2SA 19:43; ; ROM 12:19-12:21

## Lack of response


### As an indication of death
 1SA 4:20; ; 2KI 4:31

### As a sign of disobedience
**JER 7:27-7:29** *See also* PRO 29:19; JER 2:30, JER 17:23, JER 32:33

### As a sign of defiance
 2KI 18:36 parallel ISA 36:21

### From not knowing what to say
 JOB 32:15-32:16, JOB 40:3-40:5; ; MAT 22:46; ; LUK 20:26

## Jesus Christ made no response at his trial
 MAT 27:14 parallel MRK 15:5; ; MRK 14:61; ; 1PE 2:22-2:23; ; ISA 53:9
