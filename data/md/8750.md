# 8750: false teachings

Scripture repeatedly warns against false teachings, which deny or distort some aspect of the gospel. The origin of such teachings is attributed either to human error or to demonic inspiration.

## Examples of false teachings
**[REV 2:14-2:15](bibleref:66002014-66002015)** *See also* [MAT 5:19](bibleref:40005019); [2TH 2:1-2:2](bibleref:53002001-53002002); [1TI 4:1-4:3](bibleref:54004001-54004003); [REV 2:20](bibleref:66002020)

## Qualities of false teachings


### They are valueless
**[MAT 15:9](bibleref:40015009)** parallel MRK 7:7 *See also* [ISA 29:13](bibleref:23029013); [EPH 5:6](bibleref:49005006); [COL 2:20-2:23](bibleref:51002020-51002023); [1TI 1:3-1:7](bibleref:54001003-54001007)

### They are destructive in their nature
**[2PE 2:1](bibleref:61002001)** *See also* [MAT 7:15](bibleref:40007015); [ACT 20:29-20:30](bibleref:44020029-44020030); [TIT 1:10-1:11](bibleref:56001010-56001011)

### They have the capacity to lead people astray
**[ACT 20:30](bibleref:44020030)** *See also* [EPH 4:14](bibleref:49004014); [1TI 6:20-6:21](bibleref:54006020-54006021); [HEB 13:9](bibleref:58013009); [2PE 2:14-2:15](bibleref:61002014-61002015); [1JN 2:26](bibleref:62002026)

### They may be popular
**[2PE 2:2](bibleref:61002002)** *See also* [MAT 24:4-24:5](bibleref:40024004-40024005) parallel MRK 13:5-13:6; [2TI 4:3](bibleref:55004003); [2PE 2:18-2:19](bibleref:61002018-61002019); [1JN 4:1-4:5](bibleref:62004001-62004005)

### They may be accompanied by miraculous signs
**[MAT 24:24](bibleref:40024024)** parallel MRK 13:22 *See also* [2TH 2:9-2:10](bibleref:53002009-53002010); [REV 13:11-13:15](bibleref:66013011-66013015)
