# 5893: insults

Expressions of an individual's hostility or contempt for God and his servants or fellow human beings. Believers are to follow Jesus Christ's example in not returning insults.



## Insults to God


### Insults to God coming from open unbelief
 2KI 19:14-19:16, 2KI 19:20-19:23 parallels 2CH 32:16-32:19, ISA 37:14-37:17, ISA 37:21-37:24; ; HEB 10:29

### Insults to God coming through the suffering of believers
**PSA 69:7-69:9** *See also* JER 20:7-20:8; ROM 15:3; HEB 13:12-13:13

### Insults to God lamented by the psalmist
**PSA 74:18, PSA 74:22-74:23** *See also* PSA 10:3

## Jesus Christ insulted
**LUK 18:31-18:33** parallels MAT 20:17-20:19, MRK 10:32-10:34 *See also* MAT 26:67-26:68 parallels MRK 14:65, LUK 22:63-22:65; MAT 27:39-27:44 parallels MRK 15:29-15:32, LUK 23:35-23:39; JHN 8:48-8:49, JHN 18:22-18:23; 1PE 2:23

## Israel insulted
**JER 51:51** *See also* 1SA 17:26; 2SA 21:20-21:21 parallel 1CH 20:6-20:7; EZK 21:28-21:32; ZEP 2:8-2:10

## Kings, prophets and others insulted
 1SA 10:27, 1SA 25:9-25:11, 1SA 25:14; ; 2SA 10:1-10:5 parallel 1CH 19:1-19:5; ; 2KI 2:23-2:24; ; NEH 4:1-4:5; ; ECC 10:20

## The poor insulted
 1CO 11:20-11:22; ; JAS 2:1-2:7

## Christians insulted
**ACT 18:6**; **1PE 4:14** *See also* ACT 2:13, ACT 17:32, ACT 19:8-19:9; 1PE 4:4

## The pain of insult
**PSA 55:12-55:14** *See also* PSA 22:6-22:8; JOB 19:1-19:3, JOB 20:2-20:3; PSA 44:16, PSA 55:3, PSA 89:50-89:51, PSA 109:21-109:25; LAM 3:55-3:63; 1TH 2:2; REV 2:9

## Dealing with insults


### Avoiding insults
 PRO 9:7, PRO 22:10

### Not repaying insults
**PRO 12:16** *See also* MAT 5:39 parallel LUK 6:27-6:29; 1PE 3:9

### Rejoicing in reproach for Jesus Christ's sake
**MAT 5:11-5:12** parallel LUK 6:22-6:23 *See also* ACT 5:41; 2CO 12:10; HEB 10:32-10:34; 1PE 4:13-4:14

### Insult supposed but not intended
 LUK 11:45; ; ACT 23:1-23:5
