# 8407: confession, of Jesus Christ

A public declaration of faith in Jesus Christ, acknowledging both his divinity and his lordship. Without such, salvation is impossible.

## Confession of faith in Jesus Christ is necessary for salvation
**[ROM 10:9-10:10](bibleref:45010009-45010010)** *See also* [MAT 10:32-10:33](bibleref:40010032-40010033) parallel LUK 12:8-12:9; [MRK 8:38](bibleref:41008038); [2TI 2:12](bibleref:55002012); [1JN 4:15](bibleref:62004015), [1JN 2:23](bibleref:62002023)

## Confessing Jesus Christ as Lord is only possible by the Holy Spirit
**[1CO 12:3](bibleref:46012003)**

## Results of confessing faith in Jesus Christ


### Baptism
**[ACT 2:38-2:41](bibleref:44002038-44002041)** *See also* [ACT 18:8](bibleref:44018008), [ACT 22:16](bibleref:44022016)

**[MAT 7:21-7:23](bibleref:40007021-40007023)**; **[2TI 2:19](bibleref:55002019)** *See also* [LUK 19:8-19:9](bibleref:42019008-42019009); [1TI 6:12](bibleref:54006012); [HEB 13:15](bibleref:58013015); [1JN 1:6](bibleref:62001006), [1JN 2:4](bibleref:62002004)

### Persecution
**[JHN 9:22](bibleref:43009022)** *See also* [JHN 12:42-12:43](bibleref:43012042-43012043); [2TI 3:12](bibleref:55003012)
