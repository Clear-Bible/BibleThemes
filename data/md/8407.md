# 8407: confession, of Jesus Christ

A public declaration of faith in Jesus Christ, acknowledging both his divinity and his lordship. Without such, salvation is impossible.



## Confession of faith in Jesus Christ is necessary for salvation
**ROM 10:9-10:10** *See also* MAT 10:32-10:33 parallel LUK 12:8-12:9; MRK 8:38; 2TI 2:12; 1JN 4:15, 1JN 2:23

## Confessing Jesus Christ as Lord is only possible by the Holy Spirit
**1CO 12:3**

## Results of confessing faith in Jesus Christ


### Baptism
**ACT 2:38-2:41** *See also* ACT 18:8, ACT 22:16

### A change in lifestyle
**MAT 7:21-7:23**; **2TI 2:19** *See also* LUK 19:8-19:9; 1TI 6:12; HEB 13:15; 1JN 1:6, 1JN 2:4

### Persecution
**JHN 9:22** *See also* JHN 12:42-12:43; 2TI 3:12

## Demons confessed Jesus Christ's divinity
**LUK 4:41** *See also* MAT 8:28-8:29 parallels MRK 5:7, LUK 8:28

## Ultimately all will confess Jesus Christ's lordship
**PHP 2:9-2:11**

## Notable confessions of faith in Jesus Christ
**JHN 1:49** *See also* MAT 14:33, MAT 16:16 parallels MRK 8:29, LUK 9:20; JHN 6:69, JHN 11:27, JHN 20:28

## The refusal to confess Jesus Christ
**MAT 10:32-10:33** parallel LUK 12:8-12:9 *See also* 2TI 2:12
