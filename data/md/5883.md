# 5883: impatience

A refusal to wait for people or developments, frequently displaying a lack of faith.

## Examples of impatience
 [GEN 16:1-16:2](bibleref:01016001-01016002) *Abraham and Sarah who did not wait for God to give them the son he had promised* ; [GEN 25:30](bibleref:01025030) *Esau's impatience cost him his birthright* ; [NUM 20:9-20:11](bibleref:04020009-04020011) *Moses* [NUM 21:4](bibleref:04021004); [PSA 106:13](bibleref:19106013); [ISA 59:7](bibleref:23059007)
[1SA 13:6-13:12](bibleref:09013006-09013012) *Saul made the offering without waiting for Samuel* ; [2SA 13:2](bibleref:10013002) *Amnon with his half-sister, Tamar* ; [2SA 14:29](bibleref:10014029) *Absalom with Joab* ; [2SA 18:14](bibleref:10018014) *Joab with Absalom* ; [2KI 5:11-5:12](bibleref:12005011-12005012) *Naaman* ; [2KI 6:33](bibleref:12006033) *King Joram* [JOB 6:11](bibleref:18006011), [JOB 21:4](bibleref:18021004)
[JOB 32:16](bibleref:18032016) *Elihu* ; [MAT 18:29-18:30](bibleref:40018029-40018030) *the unforgiving debtor* ; [LUK 15:2](bibleref:42015002) *The younger son could not wait for his inheritance* ; [JHN 21:3](bibleref:43021003) *Peter* ; [ACT 20:16](bibleref:44020016) *Paul* ; [1CO 11:20-11:21](bibleref:46011020-46011021) *the Corinthian Christians at their communal meal* ; [REV 6:10-6:11](bibleref:66006010-66006011) *the martyrs waiting for the Lord to avenge their deaths*
