# 5883: impatience

A refusal to wait for people or developments, frequently displaying a lack of faith.



## Examples of impatience
 GEN 16:1-16:2 *Abraham and Sarah who did not wait for God to give them the son he had promised*; ; GEN 25:30 *Esau's impatience cost him his birthright*; ; NUM 20:9-20:11 *Moses*; NUM 21:4; ; PSA 106:13; ; ISA 59:7; 
1SA 13:6-13:12 *Saul made the offering without waiting for Samuel*; ; 2SA 13:2 *Amnon with his half-sister, Tamar*; ; 2SA 14:29 *Absalom with Joab*; ; 2SA 18:14 *Joab with Absalom*; ; 2KI 5:11-5:12 *Naaman*; ; 2KI 6:33 *King Joram*; JOB 6:11, JOB 21:4; 
JOB 32:16 *Elihu*; ; MAT 18:29-18:30 *the unforgiving debtor*; ; LUK 15:2 *The younger son could not wait for his inheritance*; ; JHN 21:3 *Peter*; ; ACT 20:16 *Paul*; ; 1CO 11:20-11:21 *the Corinthian Christians at their communal meal*; ; REV 6:10-6:11 *the martyrs waiting for the Lord to avenge their deaths*
