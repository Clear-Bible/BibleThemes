# 5916: pessimism

A negative and hopeless attitude, resulting either from viewing the world as being without God or from loss of faith in God.



## The world may appear to be meaningless
**ECC 1:14** *See also* ECC 1:1-1:11

## Wisdom may be pointless
**ECC 1:17-1:18** *See also* ECC 2:12-2:16

## Pleasures may be empty
**ECC 2:1**

## Work may be in vain
**ECC 2:17** *See also* ECC 2:18-2:23, ECC 4:4

## The world may seem corrupt and oppressive
**ECC 4:1** *See also* ECC 4:2-4:3, ECC 5:8-5:9

## Loneliness can be depressing
**ECC 4:8**

## Wealth is no answer
**ECC 5:10** *See also* ECC 5:11-5:15, ECC 6:1-6:6; LUK 12:18-12:20

## Life is short and often miserable
**ECC 6:12** *In the OT, before the resurrection hope was fully developed, this life seemed to be all that there was.* *See also* 1CH 29:15; JOB 7:6, JOB 14:7-14:12, JOB 14:18-14:22, JOB 17:13-17:16

## Suffering may lead to pessimism
**JOB 6:11** *See also* JOB 30:26; LAM 3:16-3:18; EZK 37:11

## Suffering may cause a sense of God's rejection
**JOB 14:18-14:19** *See also* JOB 19:5-19:13

## The godless have no grounds for optimism
**EPH 2:12** *See also* JOB 8:13-8:15; PRO 10:24, PRO 11:7, PRO 24:19-24:20; ISA 19:3; JER 4:9; MAT 7:26-7:27; 1TH 4:13

 2KI 7:4

 ZEC 9:5

 JHN 20:24-20:25

 ACT 27:20

## The antidote to pessimism is faith in God
**PSA 42:5**; **2CO 4:13-4:16** *See also* ISA 41:10; LAM 3:21-3:24; 2CO 4:7-4:9
