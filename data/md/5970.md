# 5970: unhappiness

A sense of despondency or sadness, often linked with disappointment or loss. Scripture documents human unhappiness in response to a number of situations, while recalling God's unhappiness over the folly and rebelliousness of sinful humanity.



## Unhappiness as the result of adverse circumstances
**PSA 90:10**; **MAT 26:21-26:22** parallel MRK 14:19 *See also* GEN 26:34-26:35, GEN 40:6-40:7, GEN 42:24; 1SA 1:8, 1SA 1:15; NEH 1:4, NEH 2:1-2:3 *Artaxerxes queries Nehemiah's sadness*; JOB 17:7, JOB 30:25; PSA 10:1, PSA 13:2, PSA 31:9; PRO 17:21, PRO 17:25; ISA 19:8-19:10; JER 45:3

## Unhappiness caused by loss
**2SA 19:1**; **JHN 16:5-16:6** *Jesus Christ had just foretold his departure.* *See also* GEN 38:12, GEN 42:38, GEN 48:7; 2SA 1:26, 2SA 14:2; JOL 1:8; MAT 17:23; LUK 22:45, LUK 24:17; JHN 20:11 *Mary weeps at Jesus Christ's tomb*; ACT 20:38; PHP 2:27; 1TH 4:13

## Unhappiness over failure and sin
**EZK 9:4**; **1CO 5:1-5:2** *See also* NUM 14:39; JDG 20:26, JDG 21:6; 1SA 2:33; NEH 8:9-8:11; PSA 88:1-88:9, PSA 107:39; LAM 1:4-1:5; EZK 21:6-21:7; ZEC 12:10; MAT 27:3 *Judas seized with remorse at his betrayal of Jesus Christ*; LUK 22:62; ROM 9:2; 2CO 2:2-2:5, 2CO 7:8-7:11

## Unhappiness caused by a person's own selfishness
**1TI 6:10** *See also* 1KI 21:4; EST 6:12; PRO 23:29-23:30; MAT 19:22 parallels MRK 10:22, LUK 18:23

## Jesus Christ knew unhappiness
**JHN 11:35** *See also* MAT 26:38 parallel MRK 14:34; JHN 11:33, JHN 12:27, JHN 13:21

## God also grieves
**GEN 6:6-6:7** *Prior to the flood*; **EPH 4:30** *See also* GEN 18:20; 1SA 15:11, 1SA 15:35; 2SA 24:16; PSA 78:40; ISA 63:10; JER 42:10

## God's remedy for unhappiness
**LAM 3:32-3:33**; **1PE 1:6** *See also* PSA 6:7-6:8, PSA 10:14, PSA 31:9, PSA 116:1-116:6; ISA 35:10, ISA 51:11, ISA 60:20, ISA 61:1-61:3; JER 8:18, JER 31:12-31:13; JHN 16:20-16:22
