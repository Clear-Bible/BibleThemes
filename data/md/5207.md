# 5207: architecture

The design of buildings, for religious or secular use. Building designs were often regarded in Scripture as embodying spiritual truths, and capable of being divinely inspired.



## Examples of architecture


### The tabernacle
**HEB 8:5** *See also* EXO 25:8-25:9, EXO 26:1-26:37

### The temple
**1CH 28:11-28:12** *See also* 1KI 6:1-6:10, 1KI 6:15-6:38; 1CH 28:19; 2CH 3:1-3:17, 2CH 4:1-4:22

### Altars
**JOS 8:31** *See also* EXO 20:25; 2KI 16:10-16:11 *Adopting the design of a pagan altar indicated both idolatry and submission to Assyria.*

### Ordinary dwellings
 DEU 22:8

### Ezekiel's vision of a restored temple
 EZK 40:1-40:49, EZK 41:1-41:26, EZK 42:1-42:20, EZK 43:10-43:17

### Other structures
 GEN 11:4; ; 1KI 7:1-7:12; ; PRO 24:3-24:4 *implies that a house must be more than bricks and mortar*; ; JER 22:13-22:14

## Divinely dictated design


### The ark
 GEN 6:14-6:16 *the earliest recorded example of naval architecture*

### The prophecy of the rejected stone
 PSA 118:22; ; MAT 21:42 parallels MRK 12:10, LUK 20:17; ; 1PE 2:7; ; MAT 7:24-7:27 parallel LUK 6:48-6:49 *the two foundations*; ; 1CO 3:10-3:13 *the work of Christian ministers*; ; EPH 2:19-2:22 *the church*; ; 1PE 2:4-2:7; ; ISA 54:11-54:12 *the heavenly city*; ; HEB 11:10; ; REV 21:9-21:22
