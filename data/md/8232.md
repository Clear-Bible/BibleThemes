# 8232: discipline, in the family

Parents' loving, corrective training of their children, by verbal instruction and punishments, so that they grow up in the way God wants.



## Parents are to discipline their children
**HEB 12:7** *See also* DEU 8:5; HEB 12:9-12:11

## Parents discipline out of love for their children
**PRO 13:24** *See also* PRO 3:12

## The means of parental discipline


### Training
**PRO 22:6** *See also* GEN 18:19; DEU 6:7; PRO 29:17; EPH 6:4; COL 3:21

### Correction
**PRO 22:15** *See also* PRO 19:18, PRO 23:13-23:14, PRO 29:15

### Verbal instruction
**EPH 6:4** *See also* DEU 4:9, DEU 6:7, DEU 6:20-6:25, DEU 11:19, DEU 31:13; PSA 78:5; PRO 1:8, PRO 6:20; 2TI 3:15

### Punishment
**PRO 23:13-23:14** *See also* PRO 22:15, PRO 29:15

## The purpose of parental discipline is to impart wisdom
**PRO 29:15** *See also* PRO 19:18, PRO 22:15, PRO 29:17; HEB 12:9, HEB 12:11
