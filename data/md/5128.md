# 5128: baldness

Loss of hair on the head as the result of natural causes or deliberate action. Baldness may be used to signify judgment, mourning or shame.

## Instructions regarding baldness within Jewish law


### Associated with idolatrous practice
 *See also* [LEV 21:5](bibleref:03021005); [DEU 14:1](bibleref:05014001); [JER 16:6](bibleref:24016006) *In pagan Canaan mourners shaved their foreheads.* 

### Regarding sores on bald heads
 [LEV 13:40-13:42](bibleref:03013040-03013042)

### Cleansing may involve shaving the head
 [LEV 14:8-14:9](bibleref:03014008-03014009); [NUM 6:9](bibleref:04006009), [NUM 6:18](bibleref:04006018); [DEU 21:12](bibleref:05021012)

### Vows that involve shaving the head
 [JDG 13:5](bibleref:07013005), [JDG 16:17-16:19](bibleref:07016017-07016019); [ACT 18:18](bibleref:44018018) *based on the Nazarite rule recorded in Nu 6:1-21* 

## Baldness may signify God's judgment
 [EZK 5:1](bibleref:26005001) *Ezekiel instructed to shave his head to symbolise this* 

### Judgment on Jerusalem and Judah
 [ISA 3:17](bibleref:23003017), [ISA 3:24](bibleref:23003024), [ISA 7:20](bibleref:23007020)

## Baldness as a symbol of mourning
**[JOB 1:20](bibleref:18001020)** *Job here mourns the loss of his children, household and livestock.*  *See also* [ISA 15:2](bibleref:23015002), [ISA 22:12](bibleref:23022012); [JER 48:37](bibleref:24048037); [EZK 27:31](bibleref:26027031); [AMO 8:10](bibleref:30008010); [MIC 1:16](bibleref:33001016)

### Used figuratively to indicate the mourning of a nation
 [JER 47:5](bibleref:24047005)

## Baldness as a symbol of shame
 [2KI 2:23](bibleref:12002023); [EZK 7:18](bibleref:26007018); [1CO 11:5-11:6](bibleref:46011005-46011006)

## Examples of baldness
 [EZK 29:18](bibleref:26029018) *baldness caused by the rubbing of leather helmets in warfare* ; [JDG 16:19](bibleref:07016019) *Samson* ; [2KI 2:23](bibleref:12002023) *Elisha*
