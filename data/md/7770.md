# 7770: priests, tasks in NT times

The priests mentioned in the NT performed various functions, all of which are fulfilled in Jesus Christ and the church, which is his body.



## Priests offered sacrifices


### Sacrifices made by pagan priests
**[ACT 14:13](bibleref:44014013)**

### Jewish sacrifice
**[HEB 10:11](bibleref:58010011)**

### The sacrifice of Jesus Christ
**[HEB 9:14](bibleref:58009014)** *Jesus Christ is both priest and victim: his own body takes the place occupied by sacrifices in the OT ritual.*  *See also* [HEB 10:10](bibleref:58010010)

### The Christian sacrifice of a surrendered life
**[ROM 12:1](bibleref:45012001)** *See also* [HEB 13:16](bibleref:58013016)

### The Christian sacrifice of praise
**[HEB 13:15](bibleref:58013015)**

## Priests made intercession


### The burning of incense
**[LUK 1:9](bibleref:42001009)** *See also* [EXO 30:7-30:8](bibleref:02030007-02030008)

### Christian intercession
**[REV 5:8](bibleref:66005008)** *See also* [1PE 2:5](bibleref:60002005); [REV 1:6](bibleref:66001006), [REV 5:10](bibleref:66005010), [REV 20:6](bibleref:66020006)

## Priests were guardians of tradition


### They were trained in the teaching and application of OT law
 [MAT 2:3-2:6](bibleref:40002003-40002006); [JHN 1:19](bibleref:43001019)

### The Christian ``tradition''
 [1CO 15:1-15:5](bibleref:46015001-46015005); [2TI 1:13-1:14](bibleref:55001013-55001014), [2TI 2:2](bibleref:55002002); [JUD 1:3](bibleref:65001003)

## Priests declared people clean or unclean


### They supplied ritual certification of cleansing
**[MAT 8:4](bibleref:40008004)** parallels MRK 1:44, LUK 5:14 *See also* [LEV 13:2-13:3](bibleref:03013002-03013003), [LEV 14:2](bibleref:03014002); [LUK 17:14](bibleref:42017014)

### Christians pronounce forgiveness
 [JHN 20:21-20:23](bibleref:43020021-43020023); [JAS 5:16](bibleref:59005016)
