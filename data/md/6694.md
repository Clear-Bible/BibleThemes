# 6694: mystery

Normally, God's plan of salvation which, though once unknown to human beings, is now revealed by God.

## God's character and purpose cannot be discerned by human reason
**[JOB 11:7](bibleref:18011007)** *See also* [JOB 38:1-42:6](bibleref:18038001-18042006)

## God reveals his plans through dreams, interpreted by his servant
**[DAN 2:28](bibleref:27002028)** *Nebuchadnezzar's dreams require the interpretation that God discloses to Daniel before they can be understood as a revelation of divine purposes for Babylon and the surrounding nations.*  *See also* [DAN 2:17-2:49](bibleref:27002017-27002049), [DAN 4:9](bibleref:27004009)

### God's mysteries are revealed to a man inspired by his Spirit
**[DAN 4:18](bibleref:27004018)** *See also* [AMO 3:7](bibleref:30003007)

## The mystery of God's kingdom
**[MAT 13:11](bibleref:40013011)** parallels MRK 4:11, LUK 8:10 *God's plan to establish his kingdom is revealed only to those chosen to receive it. To those “outside” it is hidden in parables.* 

## Mystery as God's plan of salvation


### God's plan, now revealed, has been hidden for long ages
**[EPH 3:9](bibleref:49003009)** *The use of “mystery” in this sense is found particularly in Paul's letters.*  *See also* [ROM 16:25](bibleref:45016025); [1CO 2:7](bibleref:46002007); [COL 1:26](bibleref:51001026)

### God's plan is fulfilled in Jesus Christ
**[EPH 1:9](bibleref:49001009)** *See also* [EPH 3:4](bibleref:49003004); [COL 2:2](bibleref:51002002); [1TI 3:16](bibleref:54003016) *The “mystery of godliness” is the revealed secret that produces godliness in people.* 

### God's plan through Jesus Christ is for the salvation of Gentiles as well as Jews
**[EPH 3:6](bibleref:49003006)** *See also* [ROM 11:25](bibleref:45011025); [COL 1:27](bibleref:51001027)

### Ultimately God's plan is for the transformation of the whole creation
**[EPH 1:9-1:10](bibleref:49001009-49001010)** *See also* [1CO 15:51-15:53](bibleref:46015051-46015053); [REV 10:7](bibleref:66010007), [REV 17:5-17:8](bibleref:66017005-66017008)

### God's plan is revealed by his Spirit so that his servants may publicly proclaim it
**[EPH 3:8-3:9](bibleref:49003008-49003009)** *See also* [EPH 3:4-3:6](bibleref:49003004-49003006), [EPH 6:19](bibleref:49006019); [COL 1:25-1:27](bibleref:51001025-51001027), [COL 4:3](bibleref:51004003); [REV 10:7](bibleref:66010007)

### Mystery as other aspects of God's purposes, spiritually discerned
**[2TH 2:7](bibleref:53002007)** *Paul's reference to “the secret power (literally “mystery”) of lawlessness” suggests that we know some things about evil only as God reveals them.*  *See also* [1CO 13:2](bibleref:46013002), [1CO 14:2](bibleref:46014002)

### Mystery as an allegorical understanding of a biblical text, revealed to Paul by God
**[EPH 5:31-5:32](bibleref:49005031-49005032)** *Paul sees Ge 2:24 as symbolising the unity between Jesus Christ and his church; the unity of husband and wife is a human reflection of that relationship.*
