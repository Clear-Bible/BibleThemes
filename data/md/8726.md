# 8726: doubters

Scripture provides examples of believers who express temporary doubts--in particular about the willingness and power of God to deliver his people, the nature of his purpose, and their own place within the divine plan.

## Those doubting God's power


### Abraham and Isaac doubt God's protection
**[GEN 12:12-12:13](bibleref:01012012-01012013)** *Fearing for his safety, Abraham resorts to deception.*  *See also* [GEN 20:2](bibleref:01020002), [GEN 26:7](bibleref:01026007)

### Moses and Israel in the desert
**[EXO 5:22-5:23](bibleref:02005022-02005023)** *See also* [EXO 14:10-14:11](bibleref:02014010-02014011) *The Israelites doubt God's protection at the Red Sea* ; [NUM 11:21-11:22](bibleref:04011021-04011022) *Moses doubts God's provision of food* ; [NUM 13:31-13:33](bibleref:04013031-04013033) *The ten spies doubt God's power to give victory in Canaan* ; [NUM 20:7-20:12](bibleref:04020007-04020012) *Moses strikes the rock twice.* 

### Others doubting God's deliverance
 [1SA 17:11](bibleref:09017011) *The people of Israel are dismayed by the challenge from Goliath* ; [ISA 7:2](bibleref:23007002) *King Ahaz, afraid of the coalition of Syria and Israel turns to Assyria for help* ; [MAT 14:29-14:31](bibleref:40014029-40014031) *Peter, walking on water, is afraid and begins to sink* ; [ACT 12:14-12:15](bibleref:44012014-44012015) *The early church does not believe that God has released Peter from prison.*
