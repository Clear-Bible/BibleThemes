# 8726: doubters

Scripture provides examples of believers who express temporary doubts--in particular about the willingness and power of God to deliver his people, the nature of his purpose, and their own place within the divine plan.



## Examples of those doubting their effectiveness to serve God


### Moses
**EXO 3:11** *See also* EXO 4:1, EXO 4:10-4:13, EXO 6:12, EXO 6:30; NUM 11:13-11:15

### Gideon
**JDG 6:15** *See also* JDG 6:17, JDG 6:36-6:40

### Elijah
**1KI 19:3-19:4** *See also* 1KI 19:10, 1KI 19:14

### Jeremiah
**JER 1:6** *See also* JER 15:10

## Those temporarily doubting God's promises
**GEN 15:8** *See also* GEN 17:17-17:18, GEN 18:12-18:15; LUK 1:18

## Those doubting God's power


### Abraham and Isaac doubt God's protection
**GEN 12:12-12:13** *Fearing for his safety, Abraham resorts to deception.* *See also* GEN 20:2, GEN 26:7

### Moses and Israel in the desert
**EXO 5:22-5:23** *See also* EXO 14:10-14:11 *The Israelites doubt God's protection at the Red Sea*; NUM 11:21-11:22 *Moses doubts God's provision of food*; NUM 13:31-13:33 *The ten spies doubt God's power to give victory in Canaan*; NUM 20:7-20:12 *Moses strikes the rock twice.*

### Others doubting God's deliverance
 1SA 17:11 *The people of Israel are dismayed by the challenge from Goliath*; ; ISA 7:2 *King Ahaz, afraid of the coalition of Syria and Israel turns to Assyria for help*; ; MAT 14:29-14:31 *Peter, walking on water, is afraid and begins to sink*; ; ACT 12:14-12:15 *The early church does not believe that God has released Peter from prison.*

## Believers questioning God


### Questioning God's actions
**JDG 6:13** *See also* NUM 11:10-11:12, NUM 14:3; JOS 7:7-7:8

### Questioning God's commands
 1SA 16:2 *Samuel*; ; 2KI 4:42-4:43 *Elisha's servant*; ; ISA 7:12 *Ahaz*; MRK 6:37 parallels MAT 14:16-14:17, LUK 9:13; 
JHN 11:39 *Martha*; ; ACT 9:13-9:14 *Ananias*
