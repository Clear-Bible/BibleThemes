# 5548: speech, divine

Scripture assumes and affirms that God has spoken to many different people throughout the ages in a variety of ways.



## Speech as a synonym for revelation
**HEB 1:1-1:2** *See also* DEU 8:3; 1SA 3:1, 1SA 3:7; PSA 147:19; JHN 17:6-17:8, JHN 17:14; 1CO 2:6-2:10

## Different modes of divine speech


### The voice of nature
**ISA 30:30-30:31** *See also* PSA 29:3-29:9; JOB 37:1-37:5 *God's voice is depicted as thunder, the most awesome sound in nature*; ISA 33:3

### A literal, audible voice
**MAT 17:5** parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:34 *See also* 1KI 19:9-19:13; DAN 4:31; JHN 12:28-12:29; ACT 9:3-9:7

### Visions
**GEN 46:2** *See also* ISA 6:8-6:13; JER 1:11-1:14; EZK 1:28-2:1; ACT 18:9-18:10, ACT 22:17-22:18

### Dreams
**GEN 31:24** *See also* GEN 20:3-20:7; 1KI 3:5-3:14; JOB 33:15-33:18; MAT 1:20-1:21, MAT 2:13, MAT 1:19-1:20

## Divine speech through human messengers


### Jesus Christ
**JHN 14:10** *See also* JHN 7:16, JHN 8:28, JHN 12:49-12:50, JHN 14:24

### Prophets
**2PE 1:21** *See also* 1SA 8:7-8:9; 1KI 17:24; 2CH 36:15; ISA 38:4-38:8; EZK 3:10-3:11; ZEC 7:4-7:12

### Apostles
**1TH 2:13** *See also* GAL 1:11-1:12; 2PE 3:2; 1JN 1:1-1:3

## Divine speech through angels
**ACT 8:26** *See also* DAN 9:20-9:23; LUK 1:11-1:20, LUK 1:26-1:38

## Examples of divine communication


### Dialogues between individuals and God
**GEN 4:9-4:15** *See also* GEN 18:16-18:33; EXO 3:3-4:17; ACT 9:10-9:16

### Messages received by people
**GEN 35:1** *See also* GEN 12:1-12:3; JOS 1:1-1:9; 2SA 7:4-7:16; 1KI 6:11-6:13

## Descriptions of divine speech


### The sound of God's voice
**EZK 43:1-43:2** *See also* 1KI 19:12; REV 1:15

### The effect of God's word
**ISA 55:10-55:11** *See also* PSA 119:105; JER 23:29; ZEC 1:13; HEB 4:12; REV 19:15

### The character of divine speech
**PSA 12:6** *See also* PSA 18:30, PSA 19:7-19:11, PSA 119:89, PSA 119:160; 1PE 1:24-1:25; ISA 40:6-40:8

## God's word is recognised and loved by his people
**PSA 119:103** *See also* EZK 3:1-3:3; JHN 10:3-10:5, JHN 10:27; ACT 13:48

## God's word is supremely revealed in Jesus Christ
**JHN 1:14** *See also* HEB 1:2
