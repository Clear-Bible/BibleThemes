# 3218: Holy Spirit, and praise

The Holy Spirit inspires believers to extol, worship and thank God. His aim is to glorify the Father and the Son.



## The promise that the Holy Spirit will bring praise
**[ISA 61:1-61:3](bibleref:23061001-23061003)**

## The Holy Spirit inspires praise


### True praise must be inspired by the Spirit
**[JHN 4:23-4:24](bibleref:43004023-43004024)** *See also* [PHP 3:3](bibleref:50003003)

### The Spirit prompts praise
**[2SA 23:1-23:2](bibleref:10023001-10023002)** [LUK 1:67-1:68](bibleref:42001067-42001068), [LUK 2:27-2:28](bibleref:42002027-42002028)
[LUK 10:21](bibleref:42010021) *The Spirit prompts Jesus Christ to praise the Father.* 

## Evidence of the Holy Spirit's work inspires praise


### The Spirit's convicting work inspires praise
 [1CO 14:24-14:25](bibleref:46014024-46014025)

### The Spirit's converting work inspires praise
**[ACT 11:15-11:18](bibleref:44011015-44011018)** *See also* [2TH 2:13](bibleref:53002013)
