# 6646: eternal life, gift of

Eternal life cannot be earned or merited but must be received as a gift from God.



## Fruitless attempts to earn eternal life
**[JHN 5:39-5:40](bibleref:43005039-43005040)** *It is possible to study the mere letter of Scripture while ignoring coming to its focus, Jesus Christ* ; **[GAL 3:11-3:12](bibleref:48003011-48003012)** *See also* [ROM 3:20](bibleref:45003020), [ROM 3:28](bibleref:45003028)
