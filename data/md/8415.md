# 8415: encouragement, examples of

Believers can encourage one another to have greater confidence in God and so to be bolder in living out their faith.



## In the OT
**2CH 32:6-32:7** *King Hezekiah encourages his people.* *See also* JDG 20:21-20:22; 1SA 23:16; 1CH 11:10; 2CH 30:22, 2CH 35:2 *King Josiah*; NEH 9:26; DAN 2:18; ZEC 8:20-8:22; MAL 3:16

## In the NT
**ACT 11:22-11:23**; **1TH 2:11-2:12** *See also* ACT 4:36, ACT 13:43, ACT 14:21-14:22 *Paul and Barnabas*; ACT 15:32, ACT 16:40, ACT 18:27-18:28, ACT 20:1-20:2 *after Paul's ministry in Ephesus*; ROM 1:11-1:12 *Paul to the church at Rome*; 2CO 7:4, 2CO 7:13; EPH 6:21-6:22 *Paul's fellow-worker, Tychicus*; PHP 1:14 *Paul in prison*; COL 4:8; 1TH 3:2 *Timothy*; 1TH 3:7; PHM 1:7; HEB 13:22; 1PE 5:12

## Encouragement as exhortation in the life of faith
**COL 1:28** *Paul describes his ministry.* *See also* LUK 3:18 *John the Baptist*; ROM 12:1, ROM 15:30; 1CO 4:16, 1CO 16:15-16:16; 2CO 2:7-2:8 *dealing with a repentant member*; 2CO 6:1; EPH 4:1; 1TH 4:10; HEB 13:19; 1PE 2:11; JUD 1:3

## Commands to encourage others
**ISA 35:3-35:4** DEU 1:38, DEU 3:28; 
2SA 19:7 *to David*; ; ISA 1:17; ; ROM 12:6-12:8, ROM 15:2; ; 1CO 14:12; ; COL 3:16; ; 1TH 4:18, 1TH 5:11, 1TH 5:14; ; 1TI 5:1; ; 2TI 4:2; ; TIT 1:9 *the church overseer*; ; TIT 2:15; ; HEB 3:13, HEB 10:25

## Failure to encourage
**EZK 34:4** *Condemnation of Israel's leaders.* *See also* JOB 16:2; PSA 69:20; ECC 4:1; EZK 16:49; ZEC 10:2

## Refusing to be comforted
**GEN 37:34-37:35** *See also* PSA 77:2; JER 31:15; MAT 2:18

## Scripture censures those who encourage others to do evil
**2CH 22:2-22:3** *See also* 2SA 11:25 *after David and Joab had arranged Uriah's death*; PSA 64:5 *evildoers*; ISA 41:7 *makers of idols*; JER 23:14, JER 29:8; EZK 13:22-13:23; 1CO 8:9-8:10
