# 8729: enemies, of Jesus Christ

Throughout his life Jesus Christ was opposed by both spiritual and human enemies. He overcame every enemy so that his people might be set free by his victory.



## The enemies of the infant Jesus
**MAT 2:13-2:20** *See also* MAT 2:14-2:20; REV 12:1-12:5

## Satan as the enemy of Jesus Christ


### In the temptation in the wilderness
**MRK 1:12-1:13** parallels MAT 4:1-4:11, LUK 4:1-4:13 *See also* GEN 3:15

### Operating through Jesus Christ's friends
**MAT 16:22-16:23** *See also* LUK 22:3; JHN 13:21-13:27

## The Jewish leaders as enemies of Jesus Christ


### They plotted to kill Jesus Christ
**MAT 12:14** parallels MRK 3:6, LUK 6:11 *See also* PSA 2:2; ISA 53:3; MAT 27:1; JHN 5:18, JHN 7:1, JHN 7:19, JHN 11:53; 1PE 2:21-2:24

### They had no real reason for hating him
**JHN 15:25** *See also* PSA 35:19, PSA 69:4

## Judas as the enemy of Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:14-26:16** *See also* MAT 10:4, MAT 10:36; MIC 7:6; MAT 26:21-26:25, MAT 26:47-26:50; JHN 13:25-13:27

## The enemies of Jesus Christ succeeded in killing him in accordance with God's will


### Jesus Christ was crucified
**ACT 2:23** *See also* MAT 27:22; MRK 15:1; JHN 18:28-18:32; ACT 3:13

### Jesus Christ did not retaliate but prayed for his enemies
 LUK 23:34

## Jesus Christ can change an enemy into a friend
**GAL 1:23** *See also* ACT 9:1-9:6, ACT 22:4-22:5, ACT 26:9-26:11; ROM 5:10; PHP 3:18; COL 1:24

## Jesus Christ will ultimately conquer all his enemies
**1CO 15:25-15:26** *See also* PSA 2:9; MAL 3:2; MAT 22:44; PSA 110:1; ACT 2:34; PHP 2:9-2:11; 2TH 1:6-1:10, 2TH 2:3-2:8; HEB 1:13, HEB 10:13
