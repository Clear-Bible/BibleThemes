# 7551: Pharisees, beliefs of

The Pharisees placed considerable emphasis upon the rigorous upholding of the law, particularly the traditional forms in which the law had been established by their predecessors.



## Pharisaic interpretation of the law


### Pharisees were the recognised custodians of the law
**[MAT 23:1-23:3](bibleref:40023001-40023003)**

### Pharisees emphasised the traditions of the elders
**[MRK 7:3-7:4](bibleref:41007003-41007004)** *For the Pharisees the oral law, the traditions of their predecessors, had an authority equal to that of the written law.*  *See also* [MAT 15:1-15:2](bibleref:40015001-40015002) parallel MRK 7:5; [GAL 1:14](bibleref:48001014)
