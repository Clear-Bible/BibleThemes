# 8488: tithing

The practice of offering to God a tenth of the harvest of the land and of livestock, as holy to the LORD. The idea is also used in a more general sense, meaning offering one-tenth of one's income to the Lord.

## Tithing was practised before the giving of the OT law
**[GEN 14:18-14:20](bibleref:01014018-01014020)** *Abraham offered a tenth of the goods taken in battle, apparently in thankfulness to God for victory.*  *See also* [GEN 28:22](bibleref:01028022); [HEB 7:1-7:3](bibleref:58007001-58007003)

## Tithing under the law


### Tithing extends to all kinds of produce and livestock
**[LEV 27:30-27:32](bibleref:03027030-03027032)** *Cereal and fruit offerings could be commuted to money.*  *See also* [2CH 31:5-31:6](bibleref:14031005-14031006); [NEH 10:35-10:38](bibleref:16010035-16010038), [NEH 13:12](bibleref:16013012)

### All tithes were paid to the Levites
 [NUM 18:21-18:24](bibleref:04018021-04018024); [NEH 10:37-10:38](bibleref:16010037-16010038); [HEB 7:5](bibleref:58007005)

### Tithes were to be paid in a designated place
 [DEU 12:5-12:6](bibleref:05012005-05012006), [DEU 14:22-14:29](bibleref:05014022-05014029), [DEU 26:2](bibleref:05026002) *Tithes were paid in Jerusalem, or every three years in towns where the tithe feast was eaten.* 

### Levites offered a tenth to the LORD
 [NUM 18:25-18:29](bibleref:04018025-04018029); [NEH 10:39](bibleref:16010039)
