# 8488: tithing

The practice of offering to God a tenth of the harvest of the land and of livestock, as holy to the LORD. The idea is also used in a more general sense, meaning offering one-tenth of one's income to the Lord.



## Tithing was practised before the giving of the OT law
**GEN 14:18-14:20** *Abraham offered a tenth of the goods taken in battle, apparently in thankfulness to God for victory.* *See also* GEN 28:22; HEB 7:1-7:3

## Tithing under the law


### Tithing extends to all kinds of produce and livestock
**LEV 27:30-27:32** *Cereal and fruit offerings could be commuted to money.* *See also* 2CH 31:5-31:6; NEH 10:35-10:38, NEH 13:12

### All tithes were paid to the Levites
 NUM 18:21-18:24; ; NEH 10:37-10:38; ; HEB 7:5

### Tithes were to be paid in a designated place
 DEU 12:5-12:6, DEU 14:22-14:29, DEU 26:2 *Tithes were paid in Jerusalem, or every three years in towns where the tithe feast was eaten.*

### Levites offered a tenth to the LORD
 NUM 18:25-18:29; ; NEH 10:39

## Uses of the tithe


### Support of the Levites
**NUM 18:21** *See also* 2CH 31:4

### The tithe meal
**DEU 14:23**

### A three-yearly gift to the poor
**DEU 14:28-14:29**

## Tithing of minor items must not lead to neglect of major matters
**MAT 23:23** *See also* LUK 11:42, LUK 18:9-18:14

## Freewill offerings were made in addition to the tithe


### They were given freely
**EXO 36:3** *See also* LEV 7:12-7:18; NUM 15:3; EZR 1:4; PSA 54:6; EZK 46:12

### They were given according to ability
**DEU 16:10** *See also* DEU 16:17; ACT 11:29; 1CO 16:2; 2CO 8:12 *There is no specific command in the NT for the church to tithe, but many commands to give generously and to support the Lord's work.*

## Blessing promised to those who tithe
**MAL 3:10** *See also* PRO 3:9-3:10

## Failure to tithe and abuses of the tithe
**MAL 3:8-3:10** *See also* LEV 27:33; 1SA 8:15, 1SA 8:17; NEH 13:10
