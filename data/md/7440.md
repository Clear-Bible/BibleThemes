# 7440: scapegoat

A goat which was sent into the wilderness by the high priest, as a symbol of the removal of the sins of the people.

## The scapegoat as part of an OT ritual
**[LEV 16:7-16:10](bibleref:03016007-03016010), [LEV 16:20-16:22](bibleref:03016020-03016022)** *See also* [LEV 16:26](bibleref:03016026); [ISA 53:4](bibleref:23053004)

## The scapegoat anticipates Jesus Christ's atonement
 [JHN 1:29](bibleref:43001029); [2CO 5:21](bibleref:47005021); [HEB 9:11-9:14](bibleref:58009011-58009014) *Jesus Christ's death is here contrasted with the OT sacrificial system.*
