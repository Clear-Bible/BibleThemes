# 8784: nominal religion

An outward show of piety which may appear to be very devout.  However, the heart is far from God and this is confirmed by the person's attitudes and actions.

## The nature of nominal religion


### It has an appearance of devotion which masks an evil heart
**[JER 12:2](bibleref:24012002)** *See also* [PRO 26:23](bibleref:20026023); [ISA 58:2](bibleref:23058002); [EZK 33:31-33:32](bibleref:26033031-26033032); [MAT 23:27-23:28](bibleref:40023027-40023028)

### It is based on what human beings may think is right
**[MAT 15:7-15:9](bibleref:40015007-40015009)** parallel MRK 7:6-7:7 *See also* [ISA 29:13](bibleref:23029013)

### It becomes apparent by the actions of its adherents
**[TIT 1:16](bibleref:56001016)** *See also* [MAT 7:15-7:23](bibleref:40007015-40007023); [1TI 1:3-1:7](bibleref:54001003-54001007); [1JN 2:4](bibleref:62002004)

## The insufficiency of nominal religion
**[1SA 15:22](bibleref:09015022)** *See also* [ISA 1:11-1:15](bibleref:23001011-23001015); [AMO 5:21-5:24](bibleref:30005021-30005024); [MAT 9:13](bibleref:40009013), [MAT 12:7](bibleref:40012007); [HOS 6:6](bibleref:28006006)
