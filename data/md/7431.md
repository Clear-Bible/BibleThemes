# 7431: Sabbath, sabbatical year

Just as the seventh day was observed as a day of rest, so the OT regarded every seventh year as a sabbath year, to be set aside for rest.



## The sabbath year


### As a time for leaving fields fallow
**[EXO 23:10-23:11](bibleref:02023010-02023011)** *See also* [LEV 25:4](bibleref:03025004), [LEV 26:34](bibleref:03026034); [NEH 10:31](bibleref:16010031) *An important feature was the opportunity for the poor to benefit from unharvested fields.* 

### As a time for cancelling debts
**[DEU 15:1](bibleref:05015001)** *See also* [DEU 15:9](bibleref:05015009), [DEU 31:10](bibleref:05031010); [NEH 10:31](bibleref:16010031)

### As a time for freeing slaves
**[DEU 15:12](bibleref:05015012)** *See also* [JER 34:14](bibleref:24034014)
