# 8353: tolerance

The virtue of being prepared to accept people and their viewpoints. While commended within the church, Scripture insists that believers must have a vital concern for maintaining and defending the truth.

## Toleration should be exercised for the benefit of those whose faith is weak
**[ROM 14:1](bibleref:45014001)** *See also* [ROM 14:2-14:6](bibleref:45014002-45014006), [ROM 14:13-14:22](bibleref:45014013-45014022); [1CO 8:8-8:13](bibleref:46008008-46008013)

## Examples of tolerance
By Jesus Christ:
 [MAT 19:13-19:14](bibleref:40019013-40019014) parallels MRK 10:13-10:14, LUK 18:15-18:16 *in contrast to the disciples' intolerance* ; [MRK 9:33-9:36](bibleref:41009033-41009036) parallel LUK 9:46-9:47; [MRK 9:38-9:40](bibleref:41009038-41009040) parallel LUK 9:49-9:50
[PHP 1:17-1:18](bibleref:50001017-50001018) *by Paul*
