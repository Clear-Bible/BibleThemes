# 2369: Jesus Christ, responses to

The NT records a number of responses to Jesus Christ on the part of those who encountered him, both positive and negative.

### Rejection

### Lack of faith
**[MRK 6:4-6:6](bibleref:41006004-41006006)** parallel MAT 13:57-13:58

### Questioning his authority
 *See also* [MRK 11:27-11:33](bibleref:41011027-41011033) parallels MAT 21:23-21:27, LUK 20:1-20:8

### Hardening of hearts

### Attributing his power to satanic forces
**[MAT 9:33-9:34](bibleref:40009033-40009034)**

### Demanding his death
 *See also* [LUK 23:20-23:24](bibleref:42023020-42023024); [JHN 10:22-10:40](bibleref:43010022-43010040)
