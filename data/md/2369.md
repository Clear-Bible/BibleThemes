# 2369: Jesus Christ, responses to

The NT records a number of responses to Jesus Christ on the part of those who encountered him, both positive and negative.



## Negative responses to Jesus Christ


### Rejection
 MAT 21:42; ; MRK 7:5-7:8; ; LUK 4:28-4:30, LUK 10:16

### Lack of faith
**MRK 6:4-6:6** parallel MAT 13:57-13:58

### Questioning his authority
 *See also* MRK 11:27-11:33 parallels MAT 21:23-21:27, LUK 20:1-20:8

### Hardening of hearts
 MAT 13:10-13:17; ; JHN 12:37-12:41

### Attributing his power to satanic forces
**MAT 9:33-9:34**

### Demanding his death
 *See also* LUK 23:20-23:24; JHN 10:22-10:40

## Positive recognition of Jesus Christ's identity


### As Messiah
**JHN 1:41** *See also* LUK 2:11, LUK 9:20; ACT 5:42

### As King
**JHN 1:49** *See also* MAT 2:1-2:2, MAT 27:37

### As Lord
**JHN 6:68** *See also* JHN 13:13

### As Son of David
**MAT 15:22**

### As Son of God
**ACT 9:20** *See also* MAT 8:29; JHN 1:34, JHN 1:49

### As Teacher
**MAT 19:16** parallels MRK 10:17, LUK 18:18 *See also* MAT 23:10; JHN 11:28

## Responses to Jesus Christ by those whom he healed


### Faith
**MRK 10:52** parallel LUK 18:42 *See also* MAT 8:13, MAT 9:27-9:31; JHN 4:43-4:54

### Thankfulness
 LUK 17:11-17:19

### Witnessing to others
 LUK 8:38-8:39; ; JHN 1:40-1:42, JHN 1:45-1:46, JHN 5:10-5:15, JHN 9:13-9:34

## Responses to Jesus Christ by those whom he met and taught


### Amazement
**MAT 7:26-7:29** *See also* MRK 12:17

### Delight
 MRK 12:37

### Faith
 MAT 8:10, MAT 9:2, MAT 9:22, MAT 15:28; ; JHN 12:11, JHN 12:41-12:43

### Repentance
**LUK 19:8** *See also* MAT 11:20; MRK 6:12; LUK 13:2-13:5

### Obedience
 MAT 28:20; ; JHN 15:20, JHN 17:6

### Worship
 MAT 2:2, MAT 14:33; MAT 28:9; ; LUK 24:52

## Responses to Jesus Christ by those whom he redeemed


### Faith
**ACT 16:31** *See also* ROM 10:9-10:10; 1JN 3:23, 1JN 5:5

### Obedience
 ROM 16:26; ; PHP 2:12; ; HEB 5:8-5:9; ; 1PE 4:17

### Calling on his name
 ACT 9:14, ACT 9:21; ; ROM 10:13; ; 1CO 1:2

### Giving thanks in his name
 EPH 5:20

### Glorifying his name
 2TH 1:12

### Proclaiming him to the world
**ACT 5:42** *See also* ACT 8:5, ACT 16:10; ROM 15:19; COL 1:28; 2TI 1:8
