# 1449: signs, purposes of

Signs are given to confirm God's word. They may warn the rebellious or encourage the faithful. People may seek signs as a result of a genuine desire to serve God.



## Signs are given to demonstrate God's power
**DEU 34:10-34:12** *See also* DEU 4:34, DEU 6:21-6:22, DEU 7:19, DEU 26:8; 1CH 16:11-16:12 parallel PSA 105:4-105:5; JER 32:21

## Signs demonstrate the authority of God's servants


### Moses and Aaron
**EXO 4:1-4:9** *See also* EXO 4:30-4:31, EXO 7:8-7:12; NUM 17:1-17:9; ACT 7:36

### Elijah
 1KI 18:36-18:38

### Elisha
 2KI 2:13-2:14

### Jesus Christ
**ACT 2:22** *See also* JHN 2:23, JHN 3:2, JHN 7:21, JHN 20:30

### The apostles
**2CO 12:12** *See also* ACT 2:43, ACT 5:12, ACT 15:12

### Signs may accompany the preaching of the gospel
**HEB 2:3-2:4** *See also* MRK 16:20; ACT 8:6, ACT 14:3

## Signs may warn the ungodly of impending judgment

## Signs may warn God's people against rebellion
**NUM 17:10** *See also* NUM 16:38, NUM 26:10; DEU 28:45-28:46; 1SA 2:34, 1SA 12:16-12:18

## Signs may encourage faith


### By giving guidance
**1SA 14:8-14:10** *See also* GEN 24:10-24:14; EXO 13:21-13:22; 2SA 5:23-5:24 parallel 1CH 14:14-14:15; MAT 2:9-2:10

### By giving assurance of God's presence
**JDG 6:17-6:22** *See also* GEN 28:10-28:17; EXO 3:1-3:5

### By encouraging the fearful and doubting
**ISA 38:7-38:8** parallels 2KI 20:8-20:11, 2CH 32:24 *See also* DEU 7:17-7:19; JDG 6:36-6:40; 2KI 6:15-6:17

**JER 10:2** *See also* MAT 24:6 parallels MRK 13:7, LUK 21:9
