# 5598: victory, over spiritual forces

In spiritual warfare victory is assured since Jesus Christ has already won it on the cross and in the resurrection. Christians will eventually share in Christ's complete victory and through him overcome their spiritual enemies.



## The victories won by Jesus Christ


### Over sin and temptation
**HEB 4:15** *See also* MAT 4:1-4:11; HEB 2:18; 1PE 2:22

### Over the world
**JHN 16:33** *See also* REV 3:21, REV 17:14

### Over the devil
**1JN 3:8** *See also* MAT 12:29 parallels MRK 3:27, LUK 11:22; LUK 10:18; JHN 12:31; COL 2:15; HEB 2:14

### Over death
**ACT 2:24** *See also* ROM 5:17, ROM 6:9-6:10; 1CO 15:54-15:57; 2TI 1:10

### Over every enemy
**1CO 15:24-15:25** *See also* ISA 42:13; PHP 2:9-2:11; HEB 10:13

## Believers have victory through Christ


### Over the power of sin
**ROM 7:24-7:25** *See also* ROM 6:11-6:14; COL 3:9-3:10

### Over temptation
**1CO 10:13** *See also* MAT 6:13 parallel LUK 11:4; HEB 2:18, HEB 4:15-4:16

### Over the world
**1JN 5:4-5:5** *See also* JHN 15:19; TIT 2:11-2:13; 2PE 1:3-1:4

### Over the devil
**REV 12:10-12:11** *See also* ROM 16:20; EPH 6:12-6:13; 1JN 2:13-2:14, 1JN 4:4

### Over death
**1CO 15:22-15:23** *See also* JHN 11:25-11:26; 1CO 15:54-15:57; 1TH 4:13-4:17

## Believers share in Christ's victory
**ROM 8:37**; **1CO 15:57**; **1JN 5:4-5:5** *See also* JHN 11:25; ROM 6:5; 1CO 15:20; 2CO 2:14; PHP 4:13; HEB 11:33-11:34 *Victory is received by faith.*
