# 5790: anger, divine

The anger of God is directed against all human sin, disobedience, rebellion and wickedness, which threaten to thwart his purposes for his creation and people.



## Causes of divine anger


### Disobedience
**ISA 5:24-5:25** *See also* NUM 22:22; JOS 7:1; 2KI 22:13; PSA 78:21-78:22; MIC 5:15; ZEC 7:12; MRK 3:5; ROM 2:5; EPH 5:5-5:6; HEB 3:7-3:12

### Sinfulness
**ISA 57:17** *See also* 1KI 8:46, 1KI 11:9; 2CH 19:2, 2CH 32:25; EPH 2:1-2:3; COL 3:5-3:6

### Apostasy and idolatry
**NUM 25:3** *See also* DEU 4:25, DEU 9:8; JOS 22:18, JOS 23:16; JDG 2:19-2:20, JDG 10:6-10:7; 1KI 14:15; 2KI 22:17; EZR 9:14; JER 7:17-7:18

## The characteristics of God that provoke his anger


### He is a holy God
**PSA 7:11** *See also* JOS 24:19-24:20; JER 10:10

### He is a jealous God
**DEU 6:15** *See also* 1KI 14:22; PSA 79:5; EZK 16:38; NAM 1:2

### He is the universal judge
**PSA 79:6** *See also* EZR 8:22; ISA 34:2; ZEP 3:8

### He is also compassionate and slow to anger
**PSA 103:8** *See also* EXO 34:6; NEH 9:17; PSA 78:38, PSA 86:15; JON 4:2; NAM 1:3

## Consequences of divine anger


### Death and destruction
**DEU 9:8** *See also* EXO 15:7, EXO 32:10-32:11; NUM 11:1-11:2; JOB 4:9; ISA 13:5; JER 32:29

### Present judgment
**LAM 2:2** *See also* NUM 32:13; 2KI 13:3; ISA 51:20; EZK 7:8; HAB 3:12; MAT 21:12-21:13

### Future judgment
**ISA 13:9** *See also* DAN 8:19; MAT 3:7; ROM 2:5

### God's rejection of his people
**JER 7:29** *See also* DEU 31:17; 2KI 17:18, 2KI 23:25-23:27; PSA 78:59, PSA 89:38; LAM 2:6-2:7

## Being saved from the anger of God


### God's anger may be temporary
**PSA 30:5** *See also* PSA 103:9; ISA 54:8, ISA 57:16, ISA 60:10

### God's anger cannot be averted by human means
**ZEP 1:18** *See also* PRO 11:4

### Humility and repentance are required
**ZEP 2:3** *See also* GEN 18:30-18:32; DEU 9:18-9:19; 2CH 12:7, 2CH 12:12, 2CH 30:8; ISA 64:9; JOL 2:13

### God's anger is averted on account of his love for believers
**LAM 3:22** *See also* HOS 11:9

### God's anger is averted on account of the work of Jesus Christ
**ROM 5:9** *See also* JHN 3:36; 1TH 1:10, 1TH 5:9
