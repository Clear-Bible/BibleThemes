# 8632: adoration

An attitude of worship characterised by love and reverence towards God. It can also describe the love between man and woman.



## Adoration of God by his people
**DEU 10:12**

### Adoration and love for God
**PSA 31:23, PSA 116:1** *See also* DEU 6:5, DEU 11:1; JOS 22:5; PSA 18:1, PSA 26:8

### Adoration and reverence for God
**PSA 5:7** *See also* EXO 3:5; DEU 13:4; JOS 5:15; 1CH 16:29; PSA 29:2, PSA 33:8, PSA 95:6, PSA 99:5; DAN 6:26; JON 1:16; HAB 2:20

## Examples of adoration in worship
Moses and the Israelites:
 EXO 15:1-15:2, EXO 15:6-15:7, EXO 15:11-15:12; 
1SA 2:1-2:2 *Hannah*; ; 2SA 22:47-22:51 *David*; ; 1KI 8:23-8:24 *Solomon*; PSA 27:4, PSA 63:2-63:5, PSA 73:25-73:26, PSA 84:1-84:2, PSA 103:1-103:5, PSA 103:20-103:22; 
LUK 1:46-1:49 *Mary*; REV 4:8-4:11, REV 5:8-5:14, REV 7:9-7:12, REV 15:3-15:4, REV 19:6-19:8

## Adoration also describes the love between man and woman
