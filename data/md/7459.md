# 7459: tabernacle, in OT

A prefabricated portable structure that was used for worship from the wilderness period until Solomon built the Jerusalem temple.



## The tabernacle was the place of God's presence
**[EXO 33:9-33:10](bibleref:02033009-02033010)** *See also* [EXO 25:8](bibleref:02025008), [EXO 40:34-40:35](bibleref:02040034-02040035)

### It was built at God's command
**[EXO 25:9](bibleref:02025009)** *See also* [EXO 25:40](bibleref:02025040), [EXO 39:32](bibleref:02039032), [EXO 39:43](bibleref:02039043)

### The materials used in its construction
 [EXO 25:3-25:7](bibleref:02025003-02025007) *Many of these materials were available in the desert. Others, such as gold, would have been obtained from the Egyptians (Ex 12:35-36)* ; [EXO 35:21-35:24](bibleref:02035021-02035024)
