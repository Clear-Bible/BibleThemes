# 8471: respect, for human beings

Each individual, created in the image of God, should be honoured and esteemed, and this should be worked out in every area of life.



## Human beings are to be respected


### They are made in the image of God
**GEN 9:5-9:6** *See also* PRO 14:31, PRO 17:5; COL 3:9-3:11; JAS 3:9-3:10

### The bounds of life belong to God
**EXO 20:13** parallel DEU 5:17 *See also* EXO 21:22-21:25 *NIV footnote at verse 22*; 2SA 1:14-1:15, 2SA 4:9-4:12; 1KI 2:31-2:33; PSA 139:13-139:16

### The equality and dignity of human beings is commanded
**DEU 1:16-1:17** *See also* EXO 23:2-23:3; LEV 19:15; DEU 16:18-16:19; PRO 24:23-24:25, PRO 28:21; ACT 10:24-10:28, ACT 10:34-10:35; JAS 2:1-2:9; 1PE 2:17

## Respect in the family

### Disrespect for parents is condemned
**DEU 27:16** *See also* EXO 21:17; LEV 20:9; PRO 30:17; ISA 45:10; 2TI 3:1-3:2
