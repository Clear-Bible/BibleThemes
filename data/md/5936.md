# 5936: riots

The hostile reactions of crowds who take the law into their own hands, frequently in opposition to a messenger of God.



## Riotous responses to unpopular actions or teaching
 GEN 19:4-19:11 *the hostility of the men of Sodom against Lot*; ; JDG 6:30-6:31 *The men of Ophrah call for the death of Gideon*; ; JER 26:8-26:11 *The people react to Jeremiah's message*; ; LUK 4:28-4:30 *The people of Nazareth expel Jesus Christ*; ; ACT 19:23-19:34 *the anger of the Ephesians over Paul*; ; 2CO 6:5

## Riots stirred up intentionally
 MAT 27:20-27:24 parallel MRK 15:6-15:15; ; ACT 6:8-6:14, ACT 17:5-17:9, ACT 21:27-21:35

## Riotous behaviour avoided or quelled
 MAT 26:3-26:5 parallel MRK 14:1-14:2; ; ACT 19:35-19:41, ACT 24:5-24:12
