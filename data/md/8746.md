# 8746: false Christs

Mistaken and deceptive people claiming to be Jesus Christ. Jesus Christ warns his followers to be on their guard against the appearance of such people.

## Jesus Christ warns that false Christs will appear
**[MAT 24:5](bibleref:40024005)** parallels MRK 13:6, LUK 21:8 *See also* [MAT 24:23](bibleref:40024023) parallel MRK 13:21

## False Christs will perform signs and miracles in order to deceive
**[MAT 24:24](bibleref:40024024)** parallel MRK 13:22

## False Christs can be recognised because the return of Jesus Christ will be obvious to everyone
**[MAT 24:27-24:31](bibleref:40024027-40024031)** parallels MRK 13:24-13:27, LUK 21:25-21:28 *The personal return of the Lord Jesus Christ is not described in terms of an appearance to a select group, but to the whole world. The attempt of anyone to convince others that he is Christ therefore is a sure sign of deception.*
