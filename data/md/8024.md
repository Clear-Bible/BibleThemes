# 8024: faith, and blessings of God

Confidence in the ability and willingness of God to act in supernatural power to advance his kingdom, and a commitment, expressed in prayer and action, to being the means by which he does so.



## God's power is released through faith
**MAT 17:20** *See also* MRK 9:23; LUK 17:6

### Praying in faith
**MAT 21:21-21:22** parallel MRK 11:22-11:24 *See also* JAS 1:5-1:7, JAS 5:14-5:15

### Praying in Jesus Christ's name
**JHN 14:12-14:14**

## In the OT, faith in God's power
**HEB 11:32-11:34** *See also* HEB 11:11-11:12 *Abraham's faith when promised an heir in his old age*; JOS 14:6-14:14 *Caleb receives Hebron*; 1SA 14:6 *Jonathan's confidence in God's power to defeat the Philistines*; 1SA 17:32-17:47 *David's confidence in God's power to overcome Goliath*; 2CH 20:20 *Jehoshaphat's faith in God's power to give success against Moab and Ammon*; 2CH 32:7-32:8 *Hezekiah's faith in God when threatened by the Assyrians under Sennacherib*; DAN 6:23 *Daniel's faith when in the lions' den*

## In the NT, healing in response to faith
**MAT 9:22** parallels MRK 5:34, LUK 8:48 *The woman had exercised faith by touching the edge of Jesus Christ's cloak.* *See also* MAT 9:29-9:30 *Two blind men are healed*; MRK 10:52 parallel LUK 18:42 *Bartimaeus receives his sight*; LUK 17:19 *One of the ten men with leprosy who thanked Jesus Christ*; ACT 3:16 *the crippled man at the temple gate*; ACT 14:8-14:10 *the crippled man in Lystra*

## Powerful ministries marked by faith
**ACT 11:24** *See also* ACT 6:5-6:10 *Stephen*; 1CO 12:9 *Exceptional faith to meet specific needs is a special spiritual gift.*

## Faith and spiritual warfare
**EPH 6:16** *See also* 1TH 5:8; 1JN 5:4-5:5

## The importance of love accompanying faith
**1CO 13:2**
