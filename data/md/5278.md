# 5278: cripples

People whose walk, physical or spiritual, is hindered by deformity, permanent or temporary.

## Physically crippled from birth
**[ACT 3:2](bibleref:44003002)** *See also* [ACT 14:8](bibleref:44014008)

## Physically crippled in life


### By accident
**[2SA 4:4](bibleref:10004004)** *See also* [2SA 9:3](bibleref:10009003), [2SA 9:13](bibleref:10009013)

### By God
**[GEN 32:24-32:25](bibleref:01032024-01032025)** *God had a purpose in the crippling of Jacob: it was a mark of his meeting with God.*  *See also* [GEN 32:31-32:32](bibleref:01032031-01032032); [HEB 11:21](bibleref:58011021)

### By Satan
**[LUK 13:11](bibleref:42013011)** *See also* [LUK 13:16](bibleref:42013016)

## Cripples healed


### By Jesus Christ
**[LUK 7:22](bibleref:42007022)** parallel MAT 11:4-11:5 *See also* [MAT 15:30-15:31](bibleref:40015030-40015031); [JHN 5:1-5:9](bibleref:43005001-43005009)

### By others
**[ACT 3:7](bibleref:44003007)** *See also* [ACT 4:9-4:10](bibleref:44004009-44004010), [ACT 8:7](bibleref:44008007), [ACT 14:9-14:10](bibleref:44014009-44014010)

## Cripples and OT sacrifice
**[LEV 21:18-21:19](bibleref:03021018-03021019)** *A man must be without defect.  Physical disability is a defilement to holiness.*  *See also* [LEV 21:6](bibleref:03021006)

### Crippled animals
**[MAL 1:8](bibleref:39001008)** *Animals, like people, must be holy, without defect, when brought to the LORD.*  *See also* [EXO 12:5](bibleref:02012005); [LEV 3:1](bibleref:03003001), [LEV 4:3](bibleref:03004003), [LEV 22:18-22:25](bibleref:03022018-03022025); [NUM 6:14](bibleref:04006014), [NUM 28:3](bibleref:04028003); [DEU 15:21](bibleref:05015021), [DEU 17:1](bibleref:05017001); [MAL 1:13-1:14](bibleref:39001013-39001014)
