# 5413: money, attitudes to

Money can be a blessing or a snare according to one's attitude.



## Money as a gift from God
**DEU 8:18** *See also* 1SA 2:7; 1KI 3:13; PRO 8:18-8:21; 1TI 6:17

## The dangers of money


### Being ruled by money
**MAT 6:24** parallel LUK 16:13 *See also* EPH 5:5

### Loving money
**1TI 6:10** *See also* ECC 5:10; LUK 8:14, LUK 16:14; 1TI 3:3, 1TI 3:8; 2TI 3:2; TIT 1:7; HEB 13:5; 1PE 5:2

### Trusting in money
**1TI 6:17** *See also* DEU 8:12-8:14; JOB 31:24; PSA 52:7, PSA 62:10; PRO 11:4, PRO 11:28, PRO 27:24; ISA 10:3; JER 48:7; LUK 12:16-12:21 *the parable of the rich fool*; JAS 1:11, JAS 5:1-5:3; REV 18:14

### Boasting in money
**PSA 49:5-49:6** *See also* JER 9:23-9:24; HOS 12:8; JAS 4:13-4:16
