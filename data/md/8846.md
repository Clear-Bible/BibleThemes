# 8846: ungodliness

The state of believing in God, while adopting a lifestyle which seems to deny this. Believers are warned to guard against ungodliness.

## Expressions of ungodliness


### A defiant spirit towards God
**[MAT 7:6](bibleref:40007006)** *Jesus Christ is teaching his disciples not to share the riches of spiritual truth with persons who are persistently unappreciative, vicious and defiant.*  *See also* [JOB 36:8-36:13](bibleref:18036008-18036013); [PSA 73:8-73:11](bibleref:19073008-19073011)

### Mocking God and/or his people
**[2PE 3:3-3:4](bibleref:61003003-61003004)** *See also* [PSA 35:15-35:16](bibleref:19035015-19035016); [ACT 13:40-13:41](bibleref:44013040-44013041); [HAB 1:5](bibleref:35001005)

### Evil behaviour
**[ISA 32:6-32:7](bibleref:23032006-23032007)** *See also* [PRO 11:9](bibleref:20011009); [JON 1:1-1:2](bibleref:32001001-32001002); [1TI 1:9-1:11](bibleref:54001009-54001011)

## Examples of ungodliness
 [2CH 36:15-36:16](bibleref:14036015-14036016) *the southern kingdom of Judah* ; [PSA 52:1-52:3](bibleref:19052001-19052003) *Doeg the Edomite* ; [ISA 9:13-9:17](bibleref:23009013-23009017) *the northern kingdom of Israel* [JER 23:11-23:12](bibleref:24023011-24023012), [JER 23:15-23:17](bibleref:24023015-24023017)[1CO 6:9-6:11](bibleref:46006009-46006011); [EPH 2:1-2:3](bibleref:49002001-49002003)
[2TI 3:1-3:5](bibleref:55003001-55003005) *people in general during the last days (the time between the first and second comings of Jesus Christ)* ; [HEB 12:16](bibleref:58012016) *Esau*

**[2PE 3:7](bibleref:61003007)** *See also* [JOB 27:8](bibleref:18027008); [PRO 3:34](bibleref:20003034); [1PE 4:18](bibleref:60004018); [2PE 2:4-2:10](bibleref:61002004-61002010); [JUD 1:14-1:15](bibleref:65001014-65001015)
