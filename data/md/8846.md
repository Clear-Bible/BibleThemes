# 8846: ungodliness

The state of believing in God, while adopting a lifestyle which seems to deny this. Believers are warned to guard against ungodliness.



## Expressions of ungodliness


### A defiant spirit towards God
**MAT 7:6** *Jesus Christ is teaching his disciples not to share the riches of spiritual truth with persons who are persistently unappreciative, vicious and defiant.* *See also* JOB 36:8-36:13; PSA 73:8-73:11

### Mocking God and/or his people
**2PE 3:3-3:4** *See also* PSA 35:15-35:16; ACT 13:40-13:41; HAB 1:5

### Evil behaviour
**ISA 32:6-32:7** *See also* PRO 11:9; JON 1:1-1:2; 1TI 1:9-1:11

## Examples of ungodliness
 2CH 36:15-36:16 *the southern kingdom of Judah*; ; PSA 52:1-52:3 *Doeg the Edomite*; ; ISA 9:13-9:17 *the northern kingdom of Israel*; JER 23:11-23:12, JER 23:15-23:17; 1CO 6:9-6:11; ; EPH 2:1-2:3; 
2TI 3:1-3:5 *people in general during the last days (the time between the first and second comings of Jesus Christ)*; ; HEB 12:16 *Esau*

## God will judge the ungodly


### In this life
**PRO 11:31** *See also* PRO 1:28-1:33, PRO 3:33-3:34; ISA 10:5-10:7, ISA 33:14; ROM 1:18

### At the final judgment
**2PE 3:7** *See also* JOB 27:8; PRO 3:34; 1PE 4:18; 2PE 2:4-2:10; JUD 1:14-1:15

## Jesus Christ died for the ungodly
 ROM 5:6-5:8, ROM 11:26; ; ISA 59:20

## God's people must guard against ungodliness


### By relying upon God's grace
**TIT 2:11-2:12**

### By putting no confidence in ungodly people
**1CO 6:1** *See also* PSA 1:1; JUD 1:3-1:4, JUD 1:17-1:19

### By avoiding ungodly teaching and behaviour
**2TI 2:16** *See also* 1TI 4:7, 1TI 6:20-6:21
