# 5436: pain

A sense of physical suffering, anguish or distress, which has a number of causes. Scripture indicates the sources of pain and its potential spiritual implications and results.



## Pain is universal
**ROM 8:22** *See also* GEN 3:16-3:17; JOB 5:7

## The origins of pain


### Satanic activity
**GEN 3:15** *See also* JOB 1:12, JOB 2:6-2:7; LUK 13:16; 2CO 12:7

### Human activity
**PSA 37:14** *See also* GEN 4:8; EXO 1:10-1:11; NAM 3:1-3:4; HAB 2:6, HAB 2:8, HAB 2:10, HAB 2:12

### God's judgment and glory
**JHN 9:2-9:3** *See also* EXO 9:11; NUM 12:10-12:11; 2CH 26:19-26:20; PSA 38:3-38:5; PRO 5:11; ACT 12:23, ACT 13:11; 2CO 12:9

## Kinds of pain


### Physical pain
**PSA 38:7** *See also* JOB 30:17; MAT 10:28; LUK 23:33; 2CO 4:16-4:18, 2CO 11:23-11:27

### Mental pain
**JER 15:18** *See also* PSA 42:5-42:6, PSA 42:11; JER 12:6; MAT 26:38; 2CO 2:13; 2PE 2:8

### Bereavement
**JHN 11:33-11:35** *See also* GEN 35:18, GEN 50:1; 2SA 12:22-12:23; PHP 2:27; 1TH 4:13

## Spiritual pain


### Conviction of sin
**JHN 16:8** *See also* PSA 32:3-32:5, PSA 51:1-51:2; LUK 7:38; ACT 26:14

### The anguish of hell
**MRK 9:47-9:48** *See also* MAT 5:22, MAT 8:12; LUK 13:28; REV 6:16

### Perplexity
**ISA 50:10** *See also* JOB 23:8-23:9; PSA 22:1, PSA 88:1-88:3; JER 20:7

### Concern for others
**GAL 4:19** *See also* ACT 20:19; 2CO 2:1-2:5, 2CO 11:28; PHP 3:18; COL 1:28-2:1; HEB 5:7

### The failure of others
**MAT 26:56** *See also* MAT 26:40; JHN 13:21; GAL 4:16; 2TI 4:10, 2TI 4:16; 3JN 1:10

## Fruitful results of pain


### It draws believers to God
**1PE 4:19** *See also* JHN 14:1, JHN 14:18; ROM 8:26; 2CO 7:5-7:6; HEB 4:15-4:16, HEB 5:8

### It equips believers to help others
**2CO 1:4** *See also* ROM 5:3-5:5, ROM 12:15; HEB 2:18, HEB 12:11

### It helps believers to anticipate the resurrection
**1CO 15:54-15:57** *See also* ROM 8:19; 2CO 4:17; 1TH 4:14

### It points believers to heaven
**REV 21:4** *See also* ROM 8:18; REV 7:15-7:17
