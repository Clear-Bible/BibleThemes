# 8811: riches, believers' attitudes towards

God entrusts some of his people with material wealth.  If this is to be a blessing, it must be used in God-honouring ways.



## Believers may be blessed with riches


### Riches can be a blessing from God
**1CH 29:16** *See also* 1CH 29:14-29:15; JOB 1:9-1:102KI 4:1; MAT 8:20; LUK 6:20, LUK 16:20-16:22; ROM 15:26

## The attitude of believers towards riches


### They acknowledge that their riches come from God
**1CH 29:12** *See also* ECC 5:19-5:20

### They devote their riches to God's service
**PRO 3:9-3:10** *See also* 1CH 29:1-29:9; ACT 4:32-4:37

### They support gospel work and workers
 LUK 8:1-8:3; ; ACT 16:15

### They give to the poor
**1JN 3:17** *See also* LUK 19:8; ACT 9:36-9:39; GAL 2:10

### They are generous
**1TI 6:18** *See also* DEU 15:10-15:11; 2CO 9:10-9:11

### They remain humble
 JAS 1:10-1:11

### They do not trust in riches
**1TI 6:17** *See also* JOB 31:24-31:28; PRO 11:28; JER 49:4

### They do not set their heart on riches
**PSA 62:10** *See also* LUK 12:29-12:30

## Believers value heavenly riches most
**MAT 6:20-6:21** *See also* LUK 12:33-12:34; 1TI 6:18-6:19
