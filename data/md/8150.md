# 8150: revival, personal

The bringing back of individuals to life or vigour both at the point of personal regeneration through the work of the Holy Spirit and at other times in believers' lives.



## Aspects of individual revival


### Physical revival
**ISA 38:16** *See also* JDG 15:18-15:19; 1SA 14:27, 1SA 30:11-30:12; 1KI 19:7-19:8; JOB 33:25; PSA 41:3, PSA 116:8-116:9

### Revival of hope
**GEN 45:27** *See also* RUT 4:14-4:15

### Revival of sanity
 DAN 4:26, DAN 4:34, DAN 4:36

### Spiritual revival
**PSA 23:3** *See also* 1KI 19:9-19:15; JHN 21:15-21:19

## The Holy Spirit brings regeneration by giving spiritual life to those dead in sin
 EPH 2:1-2:5; ; TIT 3:5-3:6

## Preparation for personal spiritual revival


### The Holy Spirit convicts
 JHN 16:8-16:11; ; ACT 2:37; ; 1TH 1:5

### Individuals appeal to God
**JER 17:14** *See also* PSA 51:7-51:12, PSA 119:34-119:37

### Individuals long for God
**PSA 42:1-42:2** *See also* PSA 63:1

### Personal repentance is required
**PSA 51:1-51:4** *See also* 2CH 32:26, 2CH 33:11-33:13; JOB 22:23; JER 15:19

## The believer must take responsibility for personal revival
**2TI 1:6**

## God is active in bringing about personal revival
**ISA 57:15** *See also* PSA 43:3-43:4; ISA 40:31; HAG 1:14

### God's law may initiate personal revival
**PSA 19:7** *See also* PSA 119:130, PSA 119:162

## The fruit of personal revival


### God receives praise
**PSA 40:3** *See also* 2SA 22:50 parallel PSA 18:49; PSA 51:15, PSA 59:16, PSA 61:8

### God is obeyed
**2CH 33:15-33:16** *See also* ACT 2:14 *Peter had denied Jesus Christ seven weeks before.*
