# 5615: weights and measures, weights

Ancient weights were usually made of stone or metal, often inscribed with their weight and standard, the shekel being the basic weight of all Semitic nations. Weights of gold and silver served as currency, since coinage was not invented until the seventh century B.C. Weights are given in ascending order, with their approximate imperial and metric equivalents.

### The royal shekel
**[2SA 14:26](bibleref:10014026)** *The royal shekel, also known in Babylonia, was slightly heavier (at about 13 grams) than the standard shekel and reflects David's attempts to bring some standardisation to this weight.* 

### The sanctuary shekel
**[NUM 18:15-18:16](bibleref:04018015-04018016)** *The sanctuary shekel was more precisely regulated than the common shekel, weighing exactly 20 gerahs. The common shekel may have weighed slightly less as a result of greater handling and wider circulation.*  *See also* [EXO 30:13-30:15](bibleref:02030013-02030015), [EXO 30:22-30:25](bibleref:02030022-02030025), [EXO 38:24-38:26](bibleref:02038024-02038026); [LEV 5:15](bibleref:03005015), [LEV 27:1-27:7](bibleref:03027001-03027007); [NUM 3:46-3:50](bibleref:04003046-04003050)

## Mina (50 shekels): about 1 1/4 pounds (about 0.6 kilogram)
**[EZK 45:12](bibleref:26045012)** *Although the mina is valued here at 60 shekels, there is some evidence that in the pre-exilic period it was valued at 50 shekels.*  *See also* [1KI 10:17](bibleref:11010017); [EZR 2:69](bibleref:15002069); [NEH 7:71-7:72](bibleref:16007071-16007072); [DAN 5:25-5:26](bibleref:27005025-27005026) *“Mene” can mean both “numbered” and “mina”* ; [LUK 19:11-19:27](bibleref:42019011-42019027) *By NT times the mina had become coinage rather than simply a weight of silver, and was worth about three months' wages.* 

## Talent (3,000 shekels, 60 minas): about 75 pounds (about 34 kilograms)

## Other minor weights


### Kesitah

### Litra
The word translated ``pint'' in Jn 12:3 and ``pound'' in 19:39 is ``litra'', a loanword from the Latin ``libra'', meaning ``pound'' and weighing 12 ounces (327 grams):
 [JHN 12:3](bibleref:43012003), [JHN 19:39](bibleref:43019039)

### Peres
 [DAN 5:28](bibleref:27005028) *“Peres” means “division” and denotes a half-shekel.*
