# 5490: refuge

A safe retreat; a place of healing and renewal; also a stronghold from which to launch a counter-attack.



## False refuges


### Flight
**GEN 3:8-3:9**; **PSA 139:7-139:12** *See also* ISA 28:15, ISA 28:17

### Idolatry
**ISA 44:9** *See also* DEU 32:31, DEU 32:37-32:38

### Human support
**JER 17:5** *See also* PSA 20:7-20:8, PSA 142:4; ISA 30:1-30:2; ZEC 4:6; PHP 3:3

### False confidence
**JER 6:14** *See also* EZK 13:10-13:12; MAT 7:26-7:27; REV 6:15-6:17
