# 5863: flattery

Insincere and undeserved praise or words of encouragement, used with the intention of gaining favour with others.



## Flattery is deceitful
**PSA 12:2-12:3** *See also* PSA 5:9, PSA 55:20-55:21, PSA 62:4; PRO 27:6; JER 9:8

## The results of flattery are ruinous
**PRO 26:28** *See also* PRO 29:5

## Flattery is rejected by godly people
**JOB 32:21-32:22** *See also* GAL 1:10; 1TH 2:5-2:6

## Honesty is preferable to flattery
**PRO 28:23** *See also* PRO 16:13, PRO 27:9

## False teachers use flattery
**ROM 16:17-16:18** *See also* 2TI 4:3-4:4; JUD 1:16-1:19

## Examples of flattery
 2SA 14:17-14:20 *The woman from Tekoa flatters David*; ; 2SA 15:2-15:6 *Absalom to Israelites*; ; DAN 6:21 *Daniel flatters the king*; ; MAT 22:16 parallels MRK 12:14, LUK 20:21 *the Pharisees and Herodians to Jesus Christ*; ; LUK 20:21 *The spies from the teachers of the law flatter Jesus Christ*; ; ACT 12:22 *The crowds flatter Herod.*
