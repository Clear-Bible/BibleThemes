# 5863: flattery

Insincere and undeserved praise or words of encouragement, used with the intention of gaining favour with others.

## Flattery is deceitful
**[PSA 12:2-12:3](bibleref:19012002-19012003)** *See also* [PSA 5:9](bibleref:19005009), [PSA 55:20-55:21](bibleref:19055020-19055021), [PSA 62:4](bibleref:19062004); [PRO 27:6](bibleref:20027006); [JER 9:8](bibleref:24009008)

## The results of flattery are ruinous
**[PRO 26:28](bibleref:20026028)** *See also* [PRO 29:5](bibleref:20029005)

## Flattery is rejected by godly people
**[JOB 32:21-32:22](bibleref:18032021-18032022)** *See also* [GAL 1:10](bibleref:48001010); [1TH 2:5-2:6](bibleref:52002005-52002006)

## Honesty is preferable to flattery
**[PRO 28:23](bibleref:20028023)** *See also* [PRO 16:13](bibleref:20016013), [PRO 27:9](bibleref:20027009)

## False teachers use flattery
**[ROM 16:17-16:18](bibleref:45016017-45016018)** *See also* [2TI 4:3-4:4](bibleref:55004003-55004004); [JUD 1:16-1:19](bibleref:65001016-65001019)

## Examples of flattery
 [2SA 14:17-14:20](bibleref:10014017-10014020) *The woman from Tekoa flatters David* ; [2SA 15:2-15:6](bibleref:10015002-10015006) *Absalom to Israelites* ; [DAN 6:21](bibleref:27006021) *Daniel flatters the king* ; [MAT 22:16](bibleref:40022016) parallels MRK 12:14, LUK 20:21 *the Pharisees and Herodians to Jesus Christ* ; [LUK 20:21](bibleref:42020021) *The spies from the teachers of the law flatter Jesus Christ* ; [ACT 12:22](bibleref:44012022) *The crowds flatter Herod.*
