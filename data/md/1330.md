# 1330: God, the provider

God supplies the needs of all creation, but gives special care to his own people.



## God is called ``The LORD who provides''
**[GEN 22:13-22:14](bibleref:01022013-01022014)** *The name “Yahweh Yireh” (or “Jehovah Jireh”) is from the verb “to see”, and means “The LORD foresees” or “The LORD will see to it”.*  *See also* [GEN 22:8](bibleref:01022008)
