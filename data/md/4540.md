# 4540: weeds

Uncultivated plants or shrubs, growing wild, often among cultivated crops or plants. Jesus Christ used the image of weeds in some of his parables to illustrate sin and worldliness.



## The presence of weeds indicates desolation and neglect
**ZEP 2:9** *See also* PRO 24:30-24:34; ISA 5:3-5:6, ISA 7:18-7:25, ISA 32:11-32:15, ISA 34:8-34:14, ISA 55:13; HOS 9:5-9:6

## Weeds are worthless
**ISA 7:23** *See also* MIC 7:4; LUK 6:44

## Weeds were a serious problem for the farmer
**HOS 10:4** *See also* JOB 31:38-31:40; MAT 13:24-13:30

## Some varieties of weed were painful to the touch
**NUM 33:55** *See also* JOS 23:9-23:13; JDG 2:1-2:3; 2SA 23:6-23:7; EZK 2:3-2:6, EZK 28:20-28:24

## Weeds were destroyed by burning
**ISA 27:4** *See also* EXO 22:6; ISA 9:18-9:19, ISA 10:16-10:17

## Weeds in Jesus Christ's parables
The parable of the sower and its explanation:
 MAT 13:3-13:8 parallels MRK 4:3-4:8, LUK 8:5-8:8; ; MAT 13:18-13:23 parallels MRK 4:13-4:20, LUK 8:11-8:15; MAT 13:24-13:30, MAT 13:36-13:43
