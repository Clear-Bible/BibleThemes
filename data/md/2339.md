# 2339: Jesus Christ, example of

Jesus Christ sets his disciples an example, especially in servanthood, self-denial and endurance of suffering, so that they may become like him.



## Jesus Christ calls believers to follow his example
**JHN 13:14-13:16** *See also* HEB 3:1, HEB 12:2-12:3; 1PE 2:21; 1JN 2:6

## The qualities of Jesus Christ which set an example for believers


### Self-denial
**PHP 2:4-2:5** *See also* MAT 10:38, MAT 16:24 parallels MRK 8:34, LUK 9:23; LUK 14:27; HEB 12:2

### Endurance of suffering
**1PE 2:21-2:23** *See also* MAT 10:24-10:25; JHN 15:20; 1TH 2:14-2:15; HEB 12:3

### Service
 MAT 20:25-20:28 parallel MRK 10:42-10:45; ; JHN 13:13-13:17

### Faithfulness
 HEB 3:1-3:2

### Love
 EPH 5:1-5:2, EPH 5:25; ; 1JN 3:16

### Patience
 1TI 1:16

### Forgiveness of others
 EPH 4:32; ; COL 3:13

### Gentleness and humility
 MAT 11:29

### Purity
 1JN 3:3

### Prayer
 LUK 11:1

## God wants his people to follow Jesus Christ's example and be conformed to his image
 ROM 8:29; ; 1CO 15:49

## Those who followed Jesus Christ's example
 1CO 11:1 *Paul*; ; 1TH 1:6 *the converts in Thessalonica*; ; ACT 7:60 *Stephen is following the example set by Jesus Christ in Lk 23:34.*
