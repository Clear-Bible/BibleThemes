# 2339: Jesus Christ, example of

Jesus Christ sets his disciples an example, especially in servanthood, self-denial and endurance of suffering, so that they may become like him.



## Jesus Christ calls believers to follow his example
**[JHN 13:14-13:16](bibleref:43013014-43013016)** *See also* [HEB 3:1](bibleref:58003001), [HEB 12:2-12:3](bibleref:58012002-58012003); [1PE 2:21](bibleref:60002021); [1JN 2:6](bibleref:62002006)
