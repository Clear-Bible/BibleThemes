# 5938: sadness

Heaviness of heart brought about through disappointment, disillusionment, failure or sin. It can lead to depression of spirit, but this can be relieved.



## Sadness caused by disappointment or disillusionment
**NEH 2:1-2:3** *See also* GEN 40:6-40:7; 1KI 21:4; ECC 6:1-6:2; ISA 19:1-19:10; MAT 19:21-19:22 parallels MRK 10:21-10:22, LUK 18:22-18:23; MAT 26:21-26:22; LUK 24:17

## Sadness leading to depression of spirit
**NUM 11:14-11:15** *See also* JOS 7:7; 1KI 19:3-19:4; PSA 42:3-42:4; JER 15:10

## The relief of sadness
**PSA 42:5-42:6** *See also* PRO 12:25, PRO 15:30, PRO 17:22, PRO 31:6-31:7; PHP 2:19
