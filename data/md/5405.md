# 5405: medicine

The means that God has given for the cure of physical troubles. Medicine often symbolises the application of the gospel of God's grace in Jesus Christ for the healing of moral and spiritual ills.



## Preventative medicine


### A cheerful disposition
**PRO 17:22**

### Disinfection
 *See also* LEV 14:41, LEV 15:5

### Balm
**JER 51:8** *An example of medicine used as a picture of the gospel. The nation had the cure for its ills in the mercy of God if only it would repent and turn to him.* *See also* JER 8:22, JER 46:11

**EZK 47:12** *See also* REV 22:2
