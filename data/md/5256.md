# 5256: city

A large walled centre of population providing security for its inhabitants. The OT often uses the term as a symbol of the security of heaven.



## Types of city


### Ancient cities
 GEN 4:17, GEN 10:10-10:12

### Fortified cities
 NUM 32:34-32:36; ; DEU 9:1; ; JOS 10:20, JOS 14:12

### Royal cities
 JOS 10:2; ; 1SA 27:5; ; 1CH 11:7

### Cities of refuge
 DEU 4:41-4:43, DEU 19:1-19:13

## Cities were frequently large
**DEU 1:28**; **JON 3:2-3:3** *See also* DEU 6:10, DEU 9:1; NEH 7:4; JON 4:11

## Cities were surrounded by walls
**DEU 3:5**; **EZR 4:12**; **2CO 11:32-11:33** *See also* DEU 28:52; JOS 2:15; 1KI 3:1, 1KI 4:13; 2CH 33:14

## Cities were places of security
**NUM 32:17** *See also* JOS 10:20; 2CH 11:5-11:12, 2CH 14:6, 2CH 17:2; JER 4:5

## Cities could fall
**2SA 5:7**; **NEH 1:3**; **JER 51:58** *See also* DEU 20:12, DEU 20:19-20:20; 1KI 16:17-16:18; 2KI 25:1-25:4; ISA 13:19; JER 32:24-32:25; AMO 5:9

## The holy city
**PSA 48:1-48:2** *See also* 1KI 11:36, 1KI 14:21; 1CH 11:7; NEH 11:1; ISA 52:1; JER 25:29; DAN 9:16; MAT 5:35
