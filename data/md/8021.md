# 8021: faith, nature of

Confidence in and commitment to God and Jesus Christ. These attitudes remain sure even though the objects of faith are unseen. True faith is seen in obedient action, love and continuing good works.



## The object of faith


### God as the object of faith
**HEB 11:6** *See also* PSA 25:1-25:2, PSA 26:1; PRO 29:25; 1PE 1:21

### Jesus Christ as the object of faith
**JHN 14:1** *See also* JHN 3:16, JHN 3:18, JHN 3:36, JHN 6:68-6:69

### False objects of faith
Human resources:
 PSA 20:7; ; HOS 10:13; 
PSA 118:9 *other people*; ; PRO 28:26 *self*; ; ISA 42:17 *idols*

## Faith is personal trust in God
**2SA 22:31** *See also* PSA 18:2-18:6, PSA 27:13-27:14; 1PE 2:23

### True faith cannot be second-hand
**2TI 1:5** *See also* JHN 4:42

## Faith and assurance


### Assurance accompanies faith
**HEB 11:1** *See also* ROM 4:19-4:21; 1TI 3:13; HEB 10:22

### Faith may be mixed with doubt
 MAT 14:31 *Simon Peter*; ; MRK 9:24 *the father of the boy with an evil spirit*; ; JHN 20:24-20:28 *Thomas*

## Faith and sight
**2CO 5:7**

### Faith as trust in what is unseen
**JHN 20:29** *See also* 2CO 4:18; HEB 11:1-11:3, HEB 11:7, HEB 11:27

### Faith looks towards an unseen future
**HEB 11:13-11:14** *See also* HEB 11:8-11:10 *Abraham*; HEB 11:20-11:22 *Isaac, Jacob and Joseph*; HEB 11:24-11:26 *Moses*

## Faith and works


### True faith is demonstrated in good deeds
