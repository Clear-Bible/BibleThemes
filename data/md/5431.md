# 5431: oaths, divine

Solemn promises made by God to individuals and their offspring. He swears by himself because there is nothing greater to swear by.



## God swears by himself
**HEB 6:13-6:14** *See also* GEN 22:16; PSA 89:35; ISA 45:23, ISA 62:8; HEB 6:16-6:18

**GEN 22:15-22:18** *See also* GEN 24:7, GEN 26:3; EXO 33:1; DEU 6:18, DEU 7:8; LUK 1:72-1:73
