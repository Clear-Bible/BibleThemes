# 6672: grace, in human relationships

Undeserved favour or kindness, mercy, compassion and generosity are to be found in human relationships and are strongly encouraged in Scripture.



## Examples of grace in human relationships
**RUT 2:10**; **2SA 9:1-9:7**; **ACT 28:2** *See also* GEN 33:8-33:11; JOS 2:12-2:14; 1SA 15:6, 1SA 24:18; 1KI 2:7; PSA 37:25-37:26; ACT 4:8-4:10; 1CO 4:13

## Grace in relationships encouraged
**LUK 6:27-6:28** parallel MAT 5:44 *See also* PRO 14:9; ROM 15:7; PHM 1:14

### Grace in relationships expressed as mercy and compassion
**EPH 4:32** *See also* 2CH 10:7; ZEC 7:9; MAT 9:13; HOS 6:6; MAT 18:32-18:33; LUK 10:36-10:37, LUK 17:3-17:4; ROM 15:1-15:2; 1PE 3:8; JUD 1:22
