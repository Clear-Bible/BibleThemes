# 9240: last judgment

God's judgment of all people at the end of the age. This has been entrusted to Jesus Christ who, at his return, will reward the righteous and punish the unrighteous. Judgment will be on the basis of deeds, the response to God's revelation and faith in Jesus Christ. Believers need not fear the last judgment, but should live godly lives in anticipation of it.



## The certainty of last judgment
**HEB 9:27** *See also* ECC 3:17; ROM 14:10-14:12; 2CO 5:10; 1PE 4:5

## The time of last judgment


### It was anticipated in the cross
**JHN 12:31** *Jesus Christ is speaking about his approaching death.*

### It will take place at the end of the age
**MAT 13:49** *See also* MAT 13:39-13:43

### It will take place at Jesus Christ's return
**2TH 1:6-1:7** *See also* MAT 25:31-25:33; 1CO 4:5; 2PE 3:10; REV 19:11

## Last judgment is the prerogative of God


### God as judge
**DAN 7:9-7:10** *See also* HEB 12:23

### God entrusts judgment to Jesus Christ
**ACT 17:31** *See also* JHN 5:22; ACT 10:42; ROM 2:16

## The result of last judgment


### Two eternal destinies
**DAN 12:2** *See also* MAT 25:46

### The ungodly will be punished
**2PE 3:7** *See also* MAT 25:41; ROM 2:5; HEB 10:26-10:28; 2PE 2:4, 2PE 2:9; JUD 1:6-1:7, JUD 1:15

### The righteous will be rewarded
**2TI 4:8** *See also* MAT 25:34; 1CO 9:25; REV 11:18

## The basis of God's last judgment


### It is according to works
**REV 20:11-20:15** *See also* MAT 12:36-12:37, MAT 16:27; ROM 2:6-2:10; REV 22:12

### It is according to revelation
**ROM 2:12** *See also* MAT 11:20-11:24 parallel LUK 10:13-10:15 *Those towns and cities which have rejected the revelation that Christ brings will face final judgment and condemnation*; MAT 12:41-12:42 parallel LUK 11:31-11:32ROM 1:18-1:21, ROM 2:14-2:15, ROM 3:23; GAL 3:1

### It is according to attitude to Jesus Christ
**JHN 3:18** *The only basis for salvation is faith in Jesus Christ.* *See also* MAT 10:14-10:15 parallel LUK 10:11-10:12 *Those towns which reject Jesus Christ's messengers will face judgment*; MAT 10:32-10:33 parallel LUK 12:8-12:9 *Those who disown Jesus Christ will be disowned by him*; MAT 25:35-25:40; MRK 8:38; LUK 10:16; ACT 4:12, ACT 16:30-16:31

## The fairness of God's last judgment
**ROM 3:4-3:6** *See also* PSA 51:4, PSA 98:9; ISA 16:5; ROM 3:19

## Believers and last judgment


### Believers need not fear judgment
**JHN 5:24** *See also* ROM 5:9; 1CO 11:32; 1JN 4:17

### Believers should live in the light of final judgment
 1CO 3:12-3:15 *building with materials that will last*; ; 2TI 4:1 *holding to sound teaching*; ; HEB 10:25 *encouraging one another*; JAS 5:7-5:9; ; REV 6:10-6:11; 
2PE 3:11-3:13 *living holy and godly lives*
