# 5915: ornaments

The use of jewellery and other adornments is not forbidden in Scripture, but excessive use can indicate worldliness. Inner spiritual adornment is of greater value.



## Possession and use of various ornaments show wealth


### Ornaments used to beautify
**SNG 1:10-1:11** *See also* GEN 37:3, GEN 37:23, GEN 37:32; 2SA 1:24; JER 2:32

### Rings, ear-rings and nose rings
 GEN 35:4; ; EXO 32:2-32:3; ; JDG 8:24; ; EST 8:8; ; PRO 25:12; ; ISA 3:19, ISA 3:21; ; EZK 16:12; ; LUK 15:22; ; JAS 2:2

### Bracelets and gold chains
 GEN 24:22, GEN 41:42; ; PRO 1:9; ; EZK 16:11; ; DAN 5:29

### Bells
 EXO 28:33-28:35

## Ornaments plundered from Egypt
**EXO 3:22**

## The misuse of ornaments for pride and seduction
**ISA 3:16** *See also* EZK 23:40, EZK 23:42; HOS 2:13

## Ornaments as gifts
**GEN 24:53** *See also* GEN 24:22; JOB 42:11

## Adornment as a picture of God's love
**EZK 16:11-16:13** *See also* ISA 61:10; LUK 15:22

## Ornaments as symbols of authority
**GEN 41:42** *See also* 2SA 1:10; EST 3:10; DAN 5:29

## Ornaments discarded


### In mourning
**EXO 33:4-33:6** *See also* EZK 26:16

### To make idols
**EXO 32:2-32:4** *See also* JDG 8:24-8:27

### As offerings to God
**EXO 35:22** *See also* NUM 31:50

## Inner, not outer, adornment encouraged
**1PE 3:3-3:4**
