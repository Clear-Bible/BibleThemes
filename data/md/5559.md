# 5559: stress

A state of personal anxiety, strain or tension resulting from the pressures of human life. Scripture indicates a number of causes and means of dealing with stress.



## The experience of stress
**PSA 38:8**; **MRK 14:33-14:39** parallels MAT 26:37-26:44, LUK 22:39-22:46**2CO 1:8** *See also* JOB 6:2; PSA 77:2; JER 4:19; JHN 12:27, JHN 13:21; HEB 5:7

## Possible causes of stress


### Disobedience to God's will
**DEU 28:65** *See also* 2SA 24:14 parallel 1CH 21:13; PSA 38:4; MAT 14:9 parallel MRK 6:26

### Family ties
**1CO 7:33** *See also* GEN 21:11, GEN 43:6; 1SA 1:6-1:16

### The burdens of leadership
**2CO 11:28** *See also* NUM 11:14; 2CO 2:4; HEB 13:17

### Threats of injury or violence
**LAM 1:20** *See also* GEN 32:7; 1SA 28:5; 1KI 19:1-19:2; EST 7:4; PSA 56:1-56:2; JER 19:9

### Unjust leaders
 EXO 5:19; ; NEH 9:37

### The wickedness of others
**1KI 19:14** *See also* ROM 9:2-9:3; 2PE 2:7-2:8

### Academic study
 ECC 1:13
