# 8339: self-control

Physical and emotional self-mastery, particularly in situations of intense provocation or temptation.



## Self-control is the mark of a wise person
**PRO 29:11** *See also* PRO 1:1-1:5

## Self-control is an aspect of Christian character
**GAL 5:22-5:23**; **TIT 2:11-2:12** *See also* 1TI 3:2; TIT 1:8, TIT 2:2, TIT 2:5-2:6; 2PE 1:5-1:9

## Self-control affects the whole person


### Physical self-control
**1CO 9:26-9:27** *See also* 1CO 7:36-7:38; 1TH 4:3-4:7

### A mental discipline
**1PE 1:13** *See also* 1TH 5:6-5:8; 1PE 4:7, 1PE 5:8

### Controlled speech
**PSA 141:3**; **JAS 1:19** *See also* PSA 17:3; PRO 16:23, PRO 21:23; ECC 5:2; JAS 3:1-3:12

## Self-control in response to persecution
**MAT 5:39-5:40** parallel LUK 6:27-6:29 *See also* 1PE 2:18-2:23

## Examples of self-control
 GEN 39:7-39:12 *Joseph*; 1SA 24:1-24:7, 1SA 26:7-26:12; ; 2SA 16:9-16:10; JOB 31:1, JOB 31:30; ISA 53:7 *the servant, understood in the NT as a prophecy concerning Jesus Christ*; ; MRK 14:61; ; MAT 27:27-27:30; 1CO 4:12-4:13, 1CO 9:24-9:27

## The dangers of a loss of self-control
**PRO 18:7**; **JER 14:10** *See also* 1SA 18:10-18:11 *Saul*; 2SA 13:7-13:14 *Amnon*; PSA 106:32-106:33 *Moses*; PRO 6:1-6:3, PRO 29:18; ACT 24:25; ROM 1:24-1:31; 1CO 7:5 *aimed at those who thought that abstaining from sexual relations in marriage was spiritual*; 1CO 7:9; COL 2:232PE 2:12-2:14, 2PE 2:18-2:19; JUD 1:4
