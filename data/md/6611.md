# 6611: adoption, privileges and duties of

As adopted members of the family of God, believers receive both the privileges and responsibilities of being children of God.



## The privileges received by believers through adoption


### Believers are given the Spirit of adoption
**[GAL 4:6](bibleref:48004006)** *See also* [ROM 8:15](bibleref:45008015)

### Believers have access to their heavenly Father
**[EPH 2:18](bibleref:49002018)** *See also* [EPH 3:12](bibleref:49003012); [HEB 4:16](bibleref:58004016)

### Believers become heirs with Christ of heaven
**[ROM 8:17](bibleref:45008017)** *See also* [GAL 3:29](bibleref:48003029), [GAL 4:7](bibleref:48004007); [COL 1:12](bibleref:51001012); [1PE 1:4](bibleref:60001004)

## The benefits God gives to those he adopts


### Believers are pitied by him
**[PSA 103:13](bibleref:19103013)**

### Believers are protected
**[PRO 14:26](bibleref:20014026)**

### Believers are provided for
**[MAT 6:31-6:33](bibleref:40006031-40006033)**

### Believers receive loving discipline
**[HEB 12:6](bibleref:58012006)**

### Believers are never forsaken
**[PSA 94:14](bibleref:19094014)**

### Believers are assured by the Spirit
**[ROM 8:16](bibleref:45008016)**

## The responsibilities of God's adopted children


### Believers are to walk in the light
 *See also* [JHN 12:35-12:36](bibleref:43012035-43012036); [EPH 5:8](bibleref:49005008); [1TH 5:4-5:5](bibleref:52005004-52005005)

### Believers are to shun evil
 [2CO 6:17-6:18](bibleref:47006017-47006018); [PHP 2:15](bibleref:50002015)

### Believers are to purify themselves
 [2CO 7:1](bibleref:47007001); [1JN 3:2-3:3](bibleref:62003002-62003003)

### Believers are to live obediently
 [MAT 12:50](bibleref:40012050); [1PE 1:14](bibleref:60001014); [1JN 5:2-5:3](bibleref:62005002-62005003)

### Believers are to live in peace
 [MAT 5:9](bibleref:40005009); [ROM 14:19](bibleref:45014019)

### Believers are to live in love
 [GAL 5:13](bibleref:48005013); [1PE 4:8](bibleref:60004008); [1JN 3:18](bibleref:62003018)

### Believers are to be watchful
 [1TH 5:5-5:6](bibleref:52005005-52005006)
