# 8432: fasting, practice of

Although fasting is a negative practice, it is not an end in itself but is to be undertaken for a positive purpose.



## Fasting is not an end in itself


### Fasting as empty ritual is condemned
**JER 14:11-14:12** *See also* ISA 58:1-58:7; ZEC 7:4-7:7

### Fasting as mere show is condemned
**MAT 6:16-6:18** *See also* MAT 9:14-9:15 parallels MRK 2:18-2:20, LUK 5:33-5:35 *Jesus Christ repudiated fasting for its own sake whilst granting that there is a time and a place for it.*

### Fasting imposed for false motives
 1SA 14:24-14:30

## Attitudes appropriate to fasting


### Humility
**PSA 35:13** *See also* 1KI 21:27-21:29; EZR 8:21; PSA 69:10

### Repentance
**1SA 7:6** *See also* NEH 9:1-9:3; JOL 1:13-1:14, JOL 2:12-2:15

## Fasting and prayer
**NEH 1:4** *See also* EZR 8:21-8:23; PSA 35:13; DAN 9:3; MAT 17:20 parallel MRK 9:29; LUK 2:37, LUK 5:33; ACT 13:3, ACT 14:23; 1CO 7:3-7:5 *The motivation here is similar to that in abstaining from food.*

## Fasting and worship
**ACT 13:2** *See also* LUK 2:37
