# 8217: conformity

Correspondence in character or behaviour; believers are not to conform to the ways of this world but are to be conformed to the image of Christ.



## Believers are not to conform to the ways of this world
**ROM 12:2**; **1PE 1:14** *See also* EXO 23:2; LEV 20:23; DEU 18:9 *Israel was not to imitate the practices of surrounding nations*; 2KI 17:15; EZK 11:12; DAN 1:8 *Daniel refused to conform to the ways of Babylon*; DAN 3:12; EPH 4:17; COL 3:7-3:8; 1TH 5:6; 1TI 1:9-1:11; 1PE 4:3; 3JN 1:11

## Christians will be conformed to the image of Christ
**ROM 8:29** *See also* JHN 13:15, JHN 13:34; ROM 13:14; 1CO 11:1; EPH 5:1-5:2; PHP 2:5

## The means of being conformed to Jesus Christ


### In being completely united with him
**ROM 6:3-6:10**; **2CO 4:10** *See also* ROM 8:10-8:11, ROM 8:17-8:18; PHP 3:10-3:11; 1PE 4:13

### In his death
**PHP 3:10** *See also* GAL 6:14; COL 1:24, COL 3:3; 1PE 2:21, 1PE 4:1

### In his resurrection
**EPH 2:4-2:6** *See also* 1CO 15:20-15:22; COL 3:1
