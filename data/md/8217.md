# 8217: conformity

Correspondence in character or behaviour; believers are not to conform to the ways of this world but are to be conformed to the image of Christ.

## Believers are not to conform to the ways of this world
**[ROM 12:2](bibleref:45012002)**; **[1PE 1:14](bibleref:60001014)** *See also* [EXO 23:2](bibleref:02023002); [LEV 20:23](bibleref:03020023); [DEU 18:9](bibleref:05018009) *Israel was not to imitate the practices of surrounding nations* ; [2KI 17:15](bibleref:12017015); [EZK 11:12](bibleref:26011012); [DAN 1:8](bibleref:27001008) *Daniel refused to conform to the ways of Babylon* ; [DAN 3:12](bibleref:27003012); [EPH 4:17](bibleref:49004017); [COL 3:7-3:8](bibleref:51003007-51003008); [1TH 5:6](bibleref:52005006); [1TI 1:9-1:11](bibleref:54001009-54001011); [1PE 4:3](bibleref:60004003); [3JN 1:11](bibleref:64001011)
