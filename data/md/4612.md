# 4612: birds

Part of the created world, used for food. In OT times they were also used in sacrifices. In Scripture, birds are used in many metaphors.

## Part of creation
**[GEN 1:21](bibleref:01001021)** *See also* [GEN 1:20-1:30](bibleref:01001020-01001030), [GEN 2:20](bibleref:01002020); [1CO 15:39](bibleref:46015039); [JAS 3:7](bibleref:59003007)
