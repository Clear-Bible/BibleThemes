# 5947: shame

An uncomfortable feeling of guilt and humiliation, usually arising from sin or failure.



## Shame is to be avoided
**PSA 25:2** *See also* PSA 25:20, PSA 31:1, PSA 31:17, PSA 44:15, PSA 119:31, PSA 119:80

## Causes of shame


### Military defeat
**2KI 19:26**

### Natural afflictions
**LUK 1:25** *See also* GEN 30:23; ISA 4:1, ISA 54:4 *not only childlessness but widowhood*

### Humiliation by an enemy
**PSA 69:19** *See also* PSA 69:6-69:7; 1SA 11:2; 2SA 10:4-10:5; ISA 3:17; MRK 12:4

### Nakedness
**REV 3:18** *See also* GEN 2:25, GEN 3:10, GEN 9:22-9:23; ISA 47:3; EZK 16:37; REV 16:15

### The sin of relations or associates
**PRO 14:34**

## Heathen gods as a source of shame
**2SA 2:10** *“Bosheth” means “shame”. According to 1Ch 8:33 he was originally Ish-Baal, but the name “Baal”, subsequently associated with a heathen god, was changed by later writers to “Bosheth”.* *See also* JER 3:24, JER 11:13; HOS 9:10

## Shame as a divine judgment
**JER 23:40** *See also* PSA 44:9; JER 46:24

### Shame brought upon God's enemies
 PSA 6:10, PSA 31:17, PSA 35:26, PSA 70:2, PSA 71:13, PSA 109:29, PSA 129:5

## Shame as guilt for sin
**EZR 9:6** *See also* PRO 6:32; DAN 9:5-9:7; ROM 6:20-6:21

### Shame may lead to repentance
 1CO 6:5; ; 2CO 7:9-7:11; ; 2TH 3:14

### Some people are without shame
 JER 3:3, JER 6:15; ; ZEP 3:5

## Jesus Christ bore shame
**HEB 12:2** *See also* DEU 21:23; MAT 27:39-27:44; HEB 6:6

### The temptation of being ashamed of Jesus Christ and the cross
**MRK 8:38** parallel LUK 9:26 *See also* ROM 1:16; GAL 6:14

## Those who trust in God will not be put to shame
**PSA 25:3** *See also* PSA 119:5-119:6; ISA 28:16; ROM 9:33, ROM 10:11; 1PE 2:6, 1PE 4:16
