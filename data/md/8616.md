# 8616: prayerlessness

Without prayer any relationship with God is impoverished because vital communication is lost.



## Reasons for prayerlessness


### Unbelief
**[PSA 14:1-14:4](bibleref:19014001-19014004)** parallel PSA 53:1-53:4 *See also* [JOB 21:15](bibleref:18021015); [ROM 3:11](bibleref:45003011)

### Self-reliance
**[JER 17:5](bibleref:24017005)** *See also* [ZEP 3:2](bibleref:36003002)

### Worship of other gods
**[DEU 32:17-32:18](bibleref:05032017-05032018)** *See also* [JER 2:5-2:6](bibleref:24002005-24002006), [JER 2:8](bibleref:24002008)

### Guilt as a result of disobedience
 [GEN 3:9-3:12](bibleref:01003009-01003012) *Adam and Eve did not respond to their sin by praying. On the contrary they sought to hide from God* ; [JON 1:1-1:6](bibleref:32001001-32001006) *Jonah tried to run away from God.* 

### Tiredness
**[LUK 22:45](bibleref:42022045)** *See also* [MAT 26:40](bibleref:40026040) parallel MRK 14:37; [MAT 26:43-26:45](bibleref:40026043-40026045) parallel MRK 14:40-14:41

### Discord in relationship
 [1PE 3:7](bibleref:60003007)

## Examples of prayerlessness


### Joshua, after a great victory
 [JOS 7:2-7:5](bibleref:06007002-06007005) *Joshua devised his own plan in attacking Ai and did not seek the LORD until after he suffered a disastrous defeat.* 

### King Ahaziah, turning to idols
**[2KI 1:16](bibleref:12001016)** *See also* [2KI 1:1-1:6](bibleref:12001001-12001006)

### The nominal religion of Israel
**[ISA 43:22](bibleref:23043022)** *This verse implies that prayer involves effort--the people only paid lip service to God.*  *See also* [ISA 1:14](bibleref:23001014), [ISA 64:7](bibleref:23064007)

### Israel trusts in other people rather than God
 [ISA 30:1-30:2](bibleref:23030001-23030002), [ISA 31:1](bibleref:23031001)

### Israel's failure to repent of their sins
**[DAN 9:13](bibleref:27009013)** *See also* [ISA 9:13](bibleref:23009013); [JER 5:3](bibleref:24005003)

### The lack of an intercessor among God's people
 [EZK 22:30](bibleref:26022030)

## Consequences of prayerlessness


### Alienation from God
**[JHN 15:6](bibleref:43015006)** *See also* [PSA 73:27](bibleref:19073027)

### God's judgment
**[ZEP 1:4-1:6](bibleref:36001004-36001006)** *See also* [JER 10:21](bibleref:24010021)

### Disloyalty to Jesus Christ
**[MAT 26:74](bibleref:40026074)** parallels MRK 14:71, LUK 22:60, JHN 18:27 *Peter had previously failed to pray in Gethsemane.* 

### Spiritual poverty
**[JAS 4:2](bibleref:59004002)** *See also* [2KI 17:15](bibleref:12017015); [JER 2:5](bibleref:24002005)

### Ineffective ministry
 [1SA 2:12](bibleref:09002012) *This literally means they had no fellowship with the LORD* ; [1SA 12:23](bibleref:09012023); [JHN 15:4-15:5](bibleref:43015004-43015005)

### Conflicts with others because of unfulfilled desires
 [JAS 4:1-4:4](bibleref:59004001-59004004)
