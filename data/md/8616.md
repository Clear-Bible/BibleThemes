# 8616: prayerlessness

Without prayer any relationship with God is impoverished because vital communication is lost.



## Reasons for prayerlessness


### Unbelief
**PSA 14:1-14:4** parallel PSA 53:1-53:4 *See also* JOB 21:15; ROM 3:11

### Self-reliance
**JER 17:5** *See also* ZEP 3:2

### Worship of other gods
**DEU 32:17-32:18** *See also* JER 2:5-2:6, JER 2:8

### Guilt as a result of disobedience
 GEN 3:9-3:12 *Adam and Eve did not respond to their sin by praying. On the contrary they sought to hide from God*; ; JON 1:1-1:6 *Jonah tried to run away from God.*

### Tiredness
**LUK 22:45** *See also* MAT 26:40 parallel MRK 14:37; MAT 26:43-26:45 parallel MRK 14:40-14:41

### Discord in relationship
 1PE 3:7

## Examples of prayerlessness


### Joshua, after a great victory
 JOS 7:2-7:5 *Joshua devised his own plan in attacking Ai and did not seek the LORD until after he suffered a disastrous defeat.*

### King Ahaziah, turning to idols
**2KI 1:16** *See also* 2KI 1:1-1:6

### The nominal religion of Israel
**ISA 43:22** *This verse implies that prayer involves effort--the people only paid lip service to God.* *See also* ISA 1:14, ISA 64:7

### Israel trusts in other people rather than God
 ISA 30:1-30:2, ISA 31:1

### Israel's failure to repent of their sins
**DAN 9:13** *See also* ISA 9:13; JER 5:3

### The lack of an intercessor among God's people
 EZK 22:30

## Consequences of prayerlessness


### Alienation from God
**JHN 15:6** *See also* PSA 73:27

### God's judgment
**ZEP 1:4-1:6** *See also* JER 10:21

### Disloyalty to Jesus Christ
**MAT 26:74** parallels MRK 14:71, LUK 22:60, JHN 18:27 *Peter had previously failed to pray in Gethsemane.*

### Spiritual poverty
**JAS 4:2** *See also* 2KI 17:15; JER 2:5

### Ineffective ministry
 1SA 2:12 *This literally means they had no fellowship with the LORD*; ; 1SA 12:23; ; JHN 15:4-15:5

### Conflicts with others because of unfulfilled desires
 JAS 4:1-4:4
