# 8334: reverence, and God's nature

Reverence for God results from considering his nature and all that he has done.



## Believers should revere God and meditate upon his deeds
**1SA 12:24** *See also* JOS 24:14; JOB 25:2, JOB 37:23-37:24; JER 32:40-32:41; MAL 2:5

## Reverence is to be reserved exclusively for God
**DEU 6:13-6:14**

## Reverence for God's creative activity
**DEU 10:20-10:21** *See also* 1SA 12:18; 1CH 16:25; PSA 33:8-33:9; ECC 3:14; JER 5:22-5:24; HAB 3:2

## Reverence for God's eternal existence
**1TI 1:17** *See also* 1TI 6:16

## Reverence for God's name
**MAL 4:2** *See also* ISA 59:19; MAL 1:14; REV 11:18

## Reverence for God's holiness
**ISA 8:13** *See also* PSA 96:4; ISA 29:23; JER 10:7; REV 15:4

## Reverence for God's word
**ISA 66:2** *See also* DEU 4:10-4:11; EZR 9:4, EZR 10:3

## Reverence for God's works
**ECC 3:14** *See also* EXO 14:31; PSA 64:9

## Reverence for God's mercy and compassion
 PSA 5:7, PSA 22:23, PSA 130:4

## Reverence for God's intervention in healing
 MAT 9:8 parallels MRK 2:12, LUK 5:26

## Reverence for God's judgment
 EXO 14:31; ; AMO 3:8; ; JON 1:16; ; REV 14:7

## Reverence for God's anger
 DEU 6:14-6:15; ; PSA 2:11-2:12, PSA 76:7

## Gentile nations will also revere and fear God
**PSA 67:7** *See also* JOS 4:24; 1KI 8:43 parallel 2CH 6:33; 2CH 20:29; PSA 76:11-76:12, PSA 102:15; ISA 59:19; JER 33:9

## Reverent fear of God is not mere terror but includes trust
**EXO 20:20** *See also* GEN 28:16-28:17 *Jacob feels reverent awe in the presence of the God who has just promised to bless him*; PSA 2:11; ACT 9:31

## Reverent fear of God is a remedy against other fears
 PSA 56:4; ; LUK 12:4-12:5; ; 1PE 3:14-3:15
