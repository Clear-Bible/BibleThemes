# 8824: self-righteousness, nature of

Self-righteousness fosters the sinful human illusions of sinlessness, sufficiency and freedom.



## The claim to self-righteousness
**ROM 10:3** *See also* LUK 10:29, LUK 18:9; PHP 3:4-3:6

## The illusion of self-righteousness


### That people are sinless
**1JN 1:8** *See also* PRO 20:9; HOS 12:8

### That people are guiltless
**PRO 16:2** *See also* JER 2:34-2:35

### That people are deserving
**DEU 9:4** *See also* EZK 28:2

## The results of self-righteousness


### Self-deception
**1JN 1:5-1:6** *See also* PRO 30:12; JER 17:9; MAT 23:23-23:24, MAT 23:27-23:28; LUK 16:15; HEB 3:13

### A sense of superiority
**LUK 18:9-18:11** *See also* PHP 2:3; COL 2:16-2:18

### Separation from God
**1JN 1:10** *See also* ZEP 3:11; 2CO 10:18; GAL 5:4

## Examples of self-righteousness
 NUM 16:3 *some Israelites opposed to Moses*; ; 1SA 15:13-15:21 *Saul*; ; PSA 10:5 *the wicked*; LUK 16:14, LUK 18:9-18:14; ; JHN 9:39-9:41; 
REV 3:17-3:18 *the church at Laodicea*
