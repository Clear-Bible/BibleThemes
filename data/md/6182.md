# 6182: ignorance, and human situation

Ignorance is part of the human condition: as finite creatures, human knowledge is incomplete, but human beings can trust God, whose knowledge is complete, to guide their steps.



## Ignorance of God


### Finite human beings cannot know God completely
**[ECC 11:5](bibleref:21011005)** *See also* [JOB 38:1-38:7](bibleref:18038001-18038007); [ECC 3:11](bibleref:21003011)

### This ignorance will be dispelled at Jesus Christ's return
**[1CO 13:9-13:12](bibleref:46013009-46013012)**

## Ignorance of the future


### Human beings are ignorant of future events
**[ECC 8:7-8:8](bibleref:21008007-21008008)** *See also* [ECC 3:22](bibleref:21003022), [ECC 9:12](bibleref:21009012), [ECC 10:14](bibleref:21010014), [ECC 11:2](bibleref:21011002), [ECC 11:6](bibleref:21011006); [ACT 20:22](bibleref:44020022)

### Human beings are ignorant of the time of Jesus Christ's return
**[MRK 13:32-13:36](bibleref:41013032-41013036)** parallel MAT 24:36-24:44

### Presumptious plans should therefore be avoided
**[JAS 4:13-4:16](bibleref:59004013-59004016)** *See also* [PSA 39:6](bibleref:19039006); [PRO 27:1](bibleref:20027001); [LUK 12:16-12:21](bibleref:42012016-42012021)

### God's guidance is essential
**[PRO 20:24](bibleref:20020024)** *See also* [PRO 19:21](bibleref:20019021); [JER 10:23](bibleref:24010023)
