# 4669: locust

The most destructive of all biblical insects, also used figuratively as a devouring pest.



## There was no defence against locusts
**JOL 2:4-2:5** *See also* PRO 30:27

## Locusts were frequently a sign of God's judgment
**DEU 28:38**; **AMO 4:9** EXO 10:4, EXO 10:12-10:14, EXO 10:19; 
DEU 28:42 *curses for disobedience*; ; 1KI 8:37 parallel 2CH 6:28; ; 2CH 7:13-7:14; ; PSA 78:46, PSA 105:34; ; JOL 1:4, JOL 2:25; ; AMO 7:1

## Locusts were a source of food
**LEV 11:22** *See also* MAT 3:4 parallel MRK 1:6

## Locusts used figuratively


### Of numerous and destructive invaders
**JDG 7:12**; **ISA 33:4** *See also* JDG 6:5; JER 46:23, JER 51:14, JER 51:27; NAM 3:15-3:17

### Of terrifying end-time activity
**REV 9:3** *See also* REV 9:7
