# 5218: authority, in the home

The exercise of God-given authority within the husband-wife and parent-child relationships.



## A husband is given headship over his wife
**[EPH 5:23](bibleref:49005023)**

## The basis for this headship


### God's headship over Jesus Christ
**[1CO 11:3](bibleref:46011003)**

### Jesus Christ's headship over the church
**[EPH 5:23](bibleref:49005023)**

### God's order of creation
**[1CO 11:8-11:9](bibleref:46011008-46011009)** *See also* [GEN 2:20-2:22](bibleref:01002020-01002022)

## This headship has been affected by the fall
**[GEN 3:16](bibleref:01003016)**

## How this headship is to be exercised


### Lovingly and sacrificially
**[EPH 5:25](bibleref:49005025)** *See also* [EPH 5:28](bibleref:49005028), [EPH 5:33](bibleref:49005033); [COL 3:19](bibleref:51003019); [1PE 3:7](bibleref:60003007)

### As Jesus Christ rules as head of the church
**[EPH 5:25-5:29](bibleref:49005025-49005029)**

### Regarding his wife as part of himself
**[EPH 5:28-5:29](bibleref:49005028-49005029)** *See also* [1PE 3:7](bibleref:60003007)

## Christian wives are to submit to their husbands
**[EPH 5:22](bibleref:49005022)** parallel COL 3:18

### They are to follow the example of the submission of the church to Jesus Christ
**[EPH 5:24](bibleref:49005024)**

## Fathers are heads of their families
**[GEN 18:19](bibleref:01018019)** *See also* [GEN 35:2](bibleref:01035002); [JOS 24:15](bibleref:06024015); [EPH 6:4](bibleref:49006004); [1TI 3:4](bibleref:54003004), [1TI 3:12](bibleref:54003012); [TIT 1:6](bibleref:56001006)

## Fathers' authority is to be exercised reasonably
**[EPH 6:4](bibleref:49006004)** *See also* [COL 3:21](bibleref:51003021)

## The response of children to parental authority


### Obedience
**[EPH 6:1](bibleref:49006001)** parallel COL 3:20 *See also* [PRO 6:20](bibleref:20006020), [PRO 23:22](bibleref:20023022)

### Respect
**[EPH 6:2-6:3](bibleref:49006002-49006003)** *See also* [DEU 5:16](bibleref:05005016); [EXO 20:12](bibleref:02020012), [EXO 21:15](bibleref:02021015), [EXO 21:17](bibleref:02021017); [DEU 27:16](bibleref:05027016); [PRO 19:26](bibleref:20019026), [PRO 20:20](bibleref:20020020); [MAT 19:19](bibleref:40019019) parallels MRK 10:19, LUK 18:20

## Kinds of authority within the family


### Authority to discipline
**[PRO 19:18](bibleref:20019018)** *See also* [PRO 23:13](bibleref:20023013), [PRO 29:17](bibleref:20029017)

### Authority to teach
**[PRO 1:8](bibleref:20001008)** *See also* [PRO 3:1](bibleref:20003001), [PRO 13:1](bibleref:20013001)

### Authority to command
**[COL 3:20](bibleref:51003020)** parallel EPH 6:1 *See also* [PRO 6:20-6:23](bibleref:20006020-20006023)
