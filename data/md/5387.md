# 5387: leisure, and pastimes

Scripture gives examples of many different kinds of leisure activity.



## Necessary leisure activities


## Sleeping
**PSA 127:2**; **ECC 5:12** *See also* PSA 4:8; MRK 4:38; ACT 12:6

### Eating and drinking
 GEN 24:54; ; ECC 3:13

## Pleasurable pastimes


### Hospitality
 GEN 18:3-18:5; ; 1PE 4:9

### Feasting
 1KI 3:15; ; NEH 8:10; ; EST 8:17; ; JOB 1:4

### Music
**1SA 16:23** *See also* 1SA 16:16-16:18; 2KI 3:14-3:15

### Dancing
**ECC 3:4** EXO 15:20; ; JDG 11:34; ; 1SA 18:6; ; JOB 21:11; ; JER 31:4; JDG 21:19-21:21; 
MRK 6:22 *dancing in revelry*; 2SA 6:14-6:16, 2SA 6:21; ; PSA 149:3, PSA 150:4

### Physical recreation
**1TI 4:8** *See also* 1CO 9:24-9:27 *Although Paul never mentions Christians taking part in the games (which were often associated with pagan festivals), he does show an appreciation of the discipline necessary*; 2TI 2:5

### Gardening
**ECC 2:5-2:6** *See also* GEN 2:15

### Enjoyment of nature
 PSA 8:3, PSA 104:10-104:14

### Sightseeing
 MRK 13:1; ; ACT 17:16

### Education and literature
**ECC 1:13** *See also* 1KI 4:32-4:34; EST 6:1; PRO 1:5; ECC 12:12; ACT 17:21; 2TI 4:13

### Children's games
**ZEC 8:5** *See also* MAT 11:16-11:17 parallel LUK 7:32

## Harmful pastimes


### Licentious amusements
**1PE 4:3** *See also* GEN 9:20-9:21; EXO 32:19; PRO 23:29-23:30; ROM 13:13; EPH 5:4, EPH 5:12

### Cruel amusements
 JDG 16:25; ; 2SA 2:14
