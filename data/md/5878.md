# 5878: honour

Great respect; the creation is called upon to respect and revere God as its Creator. Since human beings are made in the image of God, they should respect one another.



## The creation honours its Creator


### All creatures honour God
**ISA 43:20** *See also* REV 5:13

### The people of God honour God
**EXO 12:40-12:42**; **PSA 22:23** *See also* PSA 50:14-50:15; ISA 25:1-25:3; JER 3:17, JER 33:7-33:9; 2TH 3:1

## How God wishes to be honoured


### Honouring God by worshipping him
**REV 5:11-5:12** *See also* PSA 50:23; REV 4:9-4:11

### Honouring God by obeying him
**EZK 33:31** *These people honoured God with their words only, but this was not true honour for they failed to obey his commands.* *See also* NUM 25:10-25:13; ISA 29:13

### Honouring God with one's body
**1CO 6:19-6:20** *See also* ROM 12:1; 2CO 4:10-4:11

### Honouring God with one's possessions
**PRO 3:9** *See also* ISA 60:9; 2CO 8:19

### Honouring God by caring for others
**PRO 14:31** *See also* MAT 25:31-25:40; JAS 1:27

## The consequences of not honouring God
**MAL 2:2** *See also* NUM 20:12; DAN 5:22-5:31

## Jesus Christ and honour


### Honour given to Jesus Christ
**HEB 2:9** *See also* ACT 19:17; 2PE 1:17; MAT 17:5; MRK 9:7; LUK 9:35

### The relationship of honour between the Father and the Son
**JHN 5:23** *See also* JHN 8:49-8:50

## God brings honour to people
**1SA 2:30** *See also* PSA 62:7; ZEP 3:19-3:20; JHN 12:26; ROM 2:9-2:11

## Honouring others as a duty to God


### Honouring one's parents
**EXO 20:12**; **EPH 6:2-6:3** *See also* MAL 1:6; MAT 15:1-15:6; LUK 18:20

### Honouring fellow believers
**ROM 12:10** *See also* PHP 2:29; 1TI 5:17
