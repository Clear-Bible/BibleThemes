# 5654: betrothal

The period of engagement preceding marriage; betrothal was a binding contract established between two families and sealed by the exchange of gifts. During this period the couple did not live together; sexual relations with each other at this stage was regarded as equivalent to adultery. Betrothal describes the relationship between God and his people and between Jesus Christ and the church.

## Betrothal and the choice of a spouse


### Wives were often chosen by parents for their sons
**[GEN 21:21](bibleref:01021021)** *It was usual practice for the groom's parents to choose his wife and arrange the wedding.*  *See also* [GEN 24:4](bibleref:01024004), [GEN 38:6](bibleref:01038006)

### Suitable husbands were sought by parents for their daughters
**[RUT 3:1-3:4](bibleref:08003001-08003004)** *Naomi, in the role of parent, assumes responsibility to find a husband for Ruth.*  *See also* [JDG 1:12-1:13](bibleref:07001012-07001013); [1SA 18:17](bibleref:09018017), [1SA 18:21](bibleref:09018021)

### Betrothal following the couple's wishes
**[JDG 14:1-14:4](bibleref:07014001-07014004)** *Sometimes the man chose a prospective bride and his parents negotiated the marriage.*  *See also* [GEN 34:1-34:4](bibleref:01034001-01034004), [GEN 24:57-24:58](bibleref:01024057-01024058), [GEN 26:34-26:35](bibleref:01026034-01026035) *Esau chose a bride against his parents' wishes.*

## An exchange of gifts accompanyed betrothal


### The bride-price
**[GEN 34:11-34:12](bibleref:01034011-01034012)** *This gift, which was given by the groom to the bride's family as compensation, sealed the marriage contract.*  *See also* [GEN 24:53](bibleref:01024053), [GEN 29:18](bibleref:01029018), [GEN 29:27](bibleref:01029027); [EXO 22:16-22:17](bibleref:02022016-02022017); [DEU 22:28-22:29](bibleref:05022028-05022029); [2SA 3:14](bibleref:10003014)

### The dowry
**[1KI 9:16](bibleref:11009016)** *The dowry was a gift from the bride's father to the bride and/or groom.*  [GEN 24:59](bibleref:01024059), [GEN 29:24](bibleref:01029024), [GEN 29:29](bibleref:01029029)
[JDG 1:14-1:15](bibleref:07001014-07001015)

## Betrothal was treated as marriage
**[DEU 22:23-22:24](bibleref:05022023-05022024)** *Though there were no sexual relations within the betrothal period, the commitment was regarded almost as seriously as marriage and infidelity of a betrothed partner was treated as adultery.*  *See also* [GEN 19:14](bibleref:01019014) *The men betrothed to Lot's daughters are described as his “sons-in-law”* ; [MAT 1:18-1:20](bibleref:40001018-40001020) *Mary's pregnancy during betrothal left her open to the charge and consequences of unfaithfulness.*
