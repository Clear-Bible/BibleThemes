# 5654: betrothal

The period of engagement preceding marriage; betrothal was a binding contract established between two families and sealed by the exchange of gifts. During this period the couple did not live together; sexual relations with each other at this stage was regarded as equivalent to adultery. Betrothal describes the relationship between God and his people and between Jesus Christ and the church.



## Betrothal and the choice of a spouse


### Wives were often chosen by parents for their sons
**GEN 21:21** *It was usual practice for the groom's parents to choose his wife and arrange the wedding.* *See also* GEN 24:4, GEN 38:6

### Suitable husbands were sought by parents for their daughters
**RUT 3:1-3:4** *Naomi, in the role of parent, assumes responsibility to find a husband for Ruth.* *See also* JDG 1:12-1:13; 1SA 18:17, 1SA 18:21

### Betrothal following the couple's wishes
**JDG 14:1-14:4** *Sometimes the man chose a prospective bride and his parents negotiated the marriage.* *See also* GEN 34:1-34:4, GEN 24:57-24:58, GEN 26:34-26:35 *Esau chose a bride against his parents' wishes.*

## Betrothal preceded marriage
 GEN 29:21; ; DEU 20:7 *A betrothed soldier was exempted from military service until after his marriage*; ; MAT 1:24; ; 1CO 7:36-7:38

## An exchange of gifts accompanyed betrothal


### The bride-price
**GEN 34:11-34:12** *This gift, which was given by the groom to the bride's family as compensation, sealed the marriage contract.* *See also* GEN 24:53, GEN 29:18, GEN 29:27; EXO 22:16-22:17; DEU 22:28-22:29; 2SA 3:14

### The dowry
**1KI 9:16** *The dowry was a gift from the bride's father to the bride and/or groom.* GEN 24:59, GEN 29:24, GEN 29:29; 
JDG 1:14-1:15

## Betrothal was treated as marriage
**DEU 22:23-22:24** *Though there were no sexual relations within the betrothal period, the commitment was regarded almost as seriously as marriage and infidelity of a betrothed partner was treated as adultery.* *See also* GEN 19:14 *The men betrothed to Lot's daughters are described as his “sons-in-law”*; MAT 1:18-1:20 *Mary's pregnancy during betrothal left her open to the charge and consequences of unfaithfulness.*

## Betrothal portrays the relationship between God and his people
**HOS 2:19-2:20**; **2CO 11:2** *See also* JHN 3:29; EPH 5:25-5:27; REV 19:7-19:9, REV 21:2
