# 5344: imprisonment

Being confined in prison is used both literally and figuratively in Scripture. Release from prison is used as a picture of freedom from spiritual bondage.

## The anticipation or threat of imprisonment
**[MAT 5:25-5:26](bibleref:40005025-40005026)** parallel LUK 12:58 *See also* [ACT 20:23](bibleref:44020023); [REV 2:10](bibleref:66002010)

## Imprisonment for law-breaking
**[EZR 7:26](bibleref:15007026)** *See also* [GEN 40:3](bibleref:01040003); [2KI 17:4](bibleref:12017004); [JER 52:11](bibleref:24052011); [MAT 18:30](bibleref:40018030)

## Imprisonment while on remand
**[NUM 15:33-15:34](bibleref:04015033-04015034)** *See also* [LEV 24:12](bibleref:03024012); [MAT 27:16](bibleref:40027016) parallels MRK 15:7, LUK 23:19
