# 5344: imprisonment

Being confined in prison is used both literally and figuratively in Scripture. Release from prison is used as a picture of freedom from spiritual bondage.



## The anticipation or threat of imprisonment
**MAT 5:25-5:26** parallel LUK 12:58 *See also* ACT 20:23; REV 2:10

## Imprisonment for law-breaking
**EZR 7:26** *See also* GEN 40:3; 2KI 17:4; JER 52:11; MAT 18:30

## Imprisonment while on remand
**NUM 15:33-15:34** *See also* LEV 24:12; MAT 27:16 parallels MRK 15:7, LUK 23:19

## Imprisonment of the innocent, often with malicious intent
**JER 38:28**; **LUK 21:12** *See also* GEN 39:20-39:22, GEN 40:14-40:15, GEN 42:16-42:17 *Joseph planning the welfare of his brothers*; 1KI 22:27 *Ahab orders Micaiah's imprisonment*; 2CH 16:10; JER 32:2-32:3, JER 37:15; MAT 14:3 *John the Baptist imprisoned by Herod*; ACT 8:3, ACT 16:23-16:24 *Paul and Silas in prison*; ACT 22:4, ACT 22:19, ACT 23:29, ACT 24:27, ACT 26:10, ACT 26:31; 2CO 6:5, 2CO 11:23; HEB 11:36

## Release from imprisonment


### By earthly rulers
 GEN 41:14; ; 2KI 25:27 parallel JER 52:31; ; JER 39:13-39:14

### By divine intervention
**PSA 146:7** *See also* PSA 68:6, PSA 102:20, PSA 107:10-107:14, PSA 142:7; ISA 42:7, ISA 49:9, ISA 51:14, ISA 61:1; ACT 12:1-12:11

## Imprisonment used in a figurative or poetic sense
**LUK 4:18** *See also* ISA 61:1; JOB 11:10, JOB 12:14; PSA 66:11; ECC 4:14; ROM 7:23; GAL 3:22-3:23; 1PE 3:19; REV 20:7
