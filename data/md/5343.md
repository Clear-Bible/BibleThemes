# 5343: idleness

Laziness or unproductiveness. As work is hallowed by God's working, idleness is regarded as a sin against oneself and society.



## Idleness reproached


### In terms of daily labour
**1TH 5:14** *Paul to the Christians at Thessalonica.* *See also* PRO 19:24, PRO 10:4, PRO 22:13, PRO 26:13-26:16; 2TH 3:6-3:15; 1TI 5:13; TIT 1:12

### In matters of faith
**HEB 6:11-6:12** *To Christians who were tempted to give up.*

## Idleness discouraged by examples of industriousness
**PRO 6:6-6:8** *See also* PRO 31:27 *the good wife*; ECC 11:6; 2TH 3:7-3:9 *Paul referring to his practice of not depending on others for his living*

## The results of idleness
**PRO 6:9-6:11** *See also* PRO 10:26, PRO 12:24, PRO 13:4, PRO 15:19, PRO 19:15, PRO 20:4; ECC 10:18
