# 5459: prince

The son of a king, or a very important official, leader or ruler. The title is given to Jesus Christ and also to angelic spirits (in particular Michael and Satan).



## A prince as the son of a king
**2CH 11:22** *See also* 2SA 13:32; 2KI 10:6-10:8, 2KI 11:2; PSA 45:16

## A prince as someone occupying a very important position
**GEN 49:26** parallel DEU 33:16 *See also* GEN 23:6; 1SA 2:8; 2SA 3:38; PSA 113:8

## A prince as a very important official, leader or ruler
**EST 1:3** *See also* NUM 21:18, NUM 22:8, NUM 22:13-22:15; JOS 13:21; JDG 5:15; 2CH 21:4, 2CH 22:8; EZR 1:8; EST 6:9; PSA 68:27; PRO 8:16; EZK 21:12EZK 22:6, EZK 45:8-45:9
EZK 38:2-38:3; DAN 9:6; HOS 3:4

## The Messiah is described as a prince
**ISA 9:6** *See also* EZK 34:24, EZK 37:25, EZK 44:3; DAN 8:11, DAN 8:25

## Angelic spirits (whether good or evil) are described as princes
**DAN 10:13** *The prince of the Persian kingdom was possibly a demon, exercising influence in the interests of Satan.* *See also* DAN 10:20-10:21, DAN 12:1

## Satan is described as a prince
**MAT 9:34** *See also* MAT 12:24 parallels MRK 3:22, LUK 11:15; JHN 12:31, JHN 14:30, JHN 16:11; EPH 2:2

## The splendour of the princess
**PSA 45:13** *See also* PSA 45:9
