# 5459: prince

The son of a king, or a very important official, leader or ruler. The title is given to Jesus Christ and also to angelic spirits (in particular Michael and Satan).

## A prince as the son of a king
**[2CH 11:22](bibleref:14011022)** *See also* [2SA 13:32](bibleref:10013032); [2KI 10:6-10:8](bibleref:12010006-12010008), [2KI 11:2](bibleref:12011002); [PSA 45:16](bibleref:19045016)

## A prince as someone occupying a very important position
**[GEN 49:26](bibleref:01049026)** parallel DEU 33:16 *See also* [GEN 23:6](bibleref:01023006); [1SA 2:8](bibleref:09002008); [2SA 3:38](bibleref:10003038); [PSA 113:8](bibleref:19113008)
