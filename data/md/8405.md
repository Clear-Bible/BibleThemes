# 8405: commands, in NT

The NT sets out the commands of God and Jesus Christ for the church.



## Commands made by God
**MAT 22:36-22:40** parallel MRK 12:28-12:31 *See also* MAT 15:3 parallel MRK 7:9; MRK 7:8; ACT 13:46-13:47, ACT 17:30; ROM 1:32; 1TI 1:1; TIT 1:3; 1JN 3:23, 1JN 4:21; 2JN 1:4

### In the Gospels
**JHN 13:34** *See also* MAT 8:26-8:27 parallels MRK 4:39-4:41, LUK 8:24-8:25; MAT 28:19-28:20; MRK 1:25-1:27 parallel LUK 4:35-4:36; MRK 5:43 parallel LUK 8:56; MRK 9:25; JHN 15:12, JHN 15:17
