# 5491: refugees

Fugitives driven from home because of personal, political or religious persecution, or because of physical danger.

## Examples of refugees
 [GEN 16:6-16:8](bibleref:01016006-01016008) *Hagar* ; [GEN 46:1](bibleref:01046001); [EXO 12:37-12:39](bibleref:02012037-02012039); [RUT 1:1](bibleref:08001001); [MAT 2:13-2:15](bibleref:40002013-40002015) *Mary, Joseph and Jesus Christ* ; [ACT 18:2](bibleref:44018002)

## Refugees are to be treated kindly
 [DEU 23:15-23:16](bibleref:05023015-05023016); [MAT 25:35](bibleref:40025035); [PHM 1:10-1:16](bibleref:57001010-57001016)

## Refugees from legal accusation are to be provided for
 [NUM 35:6](bibleref:04035006); [JOS 20:1-20:6](bibleref:06020001-06020006)

## The LORD is a place of spiritual refuge
 [DEU 33:27](bibleref:05033027); [2SA 22:3](bibleref:10022003); [PSA 9:9](bibleref:19009009), [PSA 59:16](bibleref:19059016); [ISA 25:4](bibleref:23025004); [JER 16:19](bibleref:24016019); [NAM 1:7](bibleref:34001007)
