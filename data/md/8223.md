# 8223: dedication

The single-minded devoting of a person, object or action to the honour of God.



## Personal dedication to God must be wholehearted
**JOS 22:5** *See also* DEU 10:20; JOS 24:14-24:15; 1CH 28:9; NEH 10:28-10:29; PSA 119:106; MAT 22:37 parallels MRK 12:30, LUK 10:27; DEU 6:5
