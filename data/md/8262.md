# 8262: generosity, human

Believers should be generous in their dealings with others, following the example of God himself.



## Motivations for generosity


### God's example
**2CO 8:7-8:9** *See also* 1JN 3:16-3:18

### God's dealings with believers
**EPH 4:32-5:2** *See also* MAT 10:7-10:8, MAT 18:21-18:35

## Demonstrations of generosity


### In material giving
**ACT 4:32-4:37** *See also* LEV 25:35; DEU 15:7-15:8; MAT 5:42, MAT 19:21; 2CO 8:3, 2CO 9:5

### In support of God's work
**PHP 4:16** *See also* EXO 36:5; NUM 7:13-7:17; 1CH 28:14-28:18, 1CH 29:9; 2CH 31:9-31:10; MRK 12:41-12:44 parallel LUK 21:1-21:4; GAL 6:6; 1TI 5:17-5:18

### In worship
**MAT 26:6-26:7** *See also* EXO 35:22; 1KI 3:4, 1KI 8:63; 2CH 1:6, 2CH 5:6; EZR 6:9; ROM 12:1-12:2; PHP 2:17

### In acts of mercy
**LUK 10:33-10:35** *See also* 2KI 6:22; 2CH 28:15

### In giving presents
**1KI 10:13** *See also* GEN 24:53, GEN 32:17-32:21, GEN 45:21-45:23; 2KI 5:5, 2KI 8:9

## Meanness and covetousness the opposites of generosity
**ECC 5:13** *See also* 2SA 12:2-12:4; ISA 43:23-43:24; MIC 2:2; MAL 3:8-3:9; MAT 25:45; LUK 12:15
