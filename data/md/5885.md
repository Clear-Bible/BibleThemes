# 5885: indifference

A neutral attitude to God that is as dangerous as hostility. It is condemned as a rejection of God's love and of the needs of others.



## Indifference towards God


### No desire to seek God
**ROM 3:11** *See also* ISA 42:20; JER 5:21; ZEP 1:6; MAT 13:15, MAT 24:12; ACT 28:27; REV 3:15-3:22

### Refusal to heed God's word
**JER 37:2** *See also* PSA 81:11; JER 6:10, JER 7:13, JER 25:3-25:7, JER 35:15; EZK 3:7; ZEC 1:4, ZEC 7:11-7:13

### Refusal to respond to God's discipline
**JER 5:3** *See also* JER 2:30, JER 7:27-7:28, JER 17:23, JER 32:33

### Ignoring God is rebellion against him
**EZK 12:2** *See also* JER 2:19, JER 7:26, JER 36:24; EZK 20:8

### People ignore God and his warnings at their peril
**JER 12:11** *See also* EXO 9:21, EXO 9:25; 2CH 33:10-33:11; NEH 9:30; PRO 1:24-1:27, PRO 13:18; JER 11:8, JER 36:31, JER 44:5-44:6; MAT 22:5-22:7

### For those who ignore God's salvation, there is no hope
**HEB 2:3** *See also* JER 7:24; MAT 12:30 parallel LUK 11:23

## Indifference to God's declared intentions
**EZK 22:30** *See also* NUM 32:6-32:7; JDG 5:15-5:17, JDG 5:23; 2CH 24:5; PSA 22:11; ISA 64:7; JER 48:10

## Indifference of religious leaders condemned
 EZK 34:2-34:4; ; ZEC 11:16-11:17; ; JHN 10:12-10:13

## Indifference towards the needs of others


### Indifference arises from sin
**PRO 29:7** *See also* JOB 21:21; ISA 32:6; JAS 2:15-2:16, JAS 5:1-5:6

### Examples of indifference towards others
 GEN 40:23 *towards Joseph*; ; 1SA 25:10-25:11 *towards David*; MAT 26:40 parallels MRK 14:37, LUK 22:45; 2TI 4:10, 2TI 4:16

## Jesus Christ condemns indifference
**MAT 25:41-25:45** *See also* LUK 10:30-10:32, LUK 16:19-16:21

## Jesus Christ's apparent indifference
**MRK 4:38** parallels MAT 8:24-8:25, LUK 8:23-8:24 *Jesus Christ sometimes delayed responding to cries for help in order to draw out faith.* *See also* MAT 15:26 parallel MRK 7:27; LUK 10:40; JHN 11:6
