# 6166: flesh, sinful nature

The physical aspect of human beings, which distinguishes them from God and is therefore frequently used in the NT as a symbol of human sinful nature in contrast with God's perfection. (The Greek word for “flesh” is sometimes translated by other words and phrases in the passages cited in this theme.).

## Flesh as the bodily substance of human beings


### As individuals or in relation to others
**[PSA 84:2](bibleref:19084002)** *See also* [GEN 2:23-2:24](bibleref:01002023-01002024), [GEN 29:14](bibleref:01029014); [1CO 15:39](bibleref:46015039)[GAL 2:20](bibleref:48002020); [PHP 1:22-1:24](bibleref:50001022-50001024)

### As the means by which Jesus Christ identified with the human race to bring salvation
**[JHN 1:14](bibleref:43001014)** *See also* [EPH 2:15](bibleref:49002015); [HEB 10:20](bibleref:58010020); [1JN 4:2](bibleref:62004002)

### As subject to mortality
**[ISA 40:6-40:7](bibleref:23040006-23040007)** *See also* [PSA 78:39](bibleref:19078039); [ACT 2:31](bibleref:44002031); [1CO 15:50](bibleref:46015050)

### As subject to weakness
**[2CH 32:8](bibleref:14032008)** *See also* [PSA 73:26](bibleref:19073026); [MAT 26:41](bibleref:40026041) parallel MRK 14:38

## Flesh as contrasting human nature with God's perfection


### The powerlessness of human beings contrasted with God's eternal power
**[ISA 31:3](bibleref:23031003)** *See also* [JHN 3:6](bibleref:43003006), [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063)

### Human or worldly standards contrasted with God's standards
**[JHN 8:15](bibleref:43008015)** *See also* [1CO 1:26](bibleref:46001026); [2CO 5:16](bibleref:47005016), [2CO 10:3-10:4](bibleref:47010003-47010004)

## Flesh as denoting the sinful nature of human beings


### The tendency to sin
**[ROM 7:18](bibleref:45007018)** *Paul does not mean that no goodness at all exists in people; nor that the physical aspect of human beings is inherently evil. He means that humans are invariably infected by evil and subject to its power.*  *See also* [JER 17:5](bibleref:24017005)

### The conflict in human experience between the sinful nature and the Spirit of God
**[GAL 5:17](bibleref:48005017)** *See also* [ROM 8:4-8:9](bibleref:45008004-45008009); [GAL 5:19-5:25](bibleref:48005019-48005025)

## The sinful nature is opposed to God and his will


### This opposition finds expression in a range of acts and attitudes
**[GAL 5:19-5:21](bibleref:48005019-48005021)** *See also* [ROM 7:14-7:25](bibleref:45007014-45007025), [ROM 8:7](bibleref:45008007), [ROM 13:13-13:14](bibleref:45013013-45013014); [1CO 6:9-6:11](bibleref:46006009-46006011); [EPH 5:5](bibleref:49005005); [JAS 1:14-1:15](bibleref:59001014-59001015); [1PE 2:11](bibleref:60002011); [2PE 2:10](bibleref:61002010), [2PE 2:18](bibleref:61002018); [1JN 2:16](bibleref:62002016)

### Confidence in the law is futile
**[ROM 8:3](bibleref:45008003)** *Because of the sinfulness of human nature, God's law is powerless to bring people into relationship with God* ; **[GAL 3:3](bibleref:48003003)** *Even the attempt to find acceptance with God through keeping his law is an act of the sinful nature because it involves rejecting his offer of salvation through his grace.*  *See also* [ROM 7:25](bibleref:45007025); [PHP 3:3-3:9](bibleref:50003003-50003009)

### The sinful nature controls human behaviour in ways which run counter to God's purpose
**[ROM 8:8](bibleref:45008008)** *See also* [ROM 7:5](bibleref:45007005)

## The sinful nature therefore makes people subject to God's judgment and to death
**[ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013)**; **[EPH 2:3](bibleref:49002003)** *See also* [GAL 6:8](bibleref:48006008)

## Believers are not controlled by the sinful nature


### Through Jesus Christ's entering into human flesh, God delivers from the power and consequences of human sinfulness
**[ROM 8:3](bibleref:45008003)** *See also* [EPH 2:15](bibleref:49002015)

### Believers have crucified the sinful nature
**[ROM 7:5-7:6](bibleref:45007005-45007006)** *See also* [ROM 8:8-8:9](bibleref:45008008-45008009); [GAL 5:24](bibleref:48005024); [COL 2:11](bibleref:51002011)

### The power of God's Spirit enables believers to continue to resist the sinful nature
**[ROM 8:13](bibleref:45008013)** *See also* [ROM 13:14](bibleref:45013014); [GAL 5:13](bibleref:48005013); [COL 3:5-3:6](bibleref:51003005-51003006); [1PE 2:11](bibleref:60002011)

### God's provision of church discipline in eliminating the sinful nature
**[1CO 5:5](bibleref:46005005)** *The discipline of excluding a sinner from the church community is intended to bring him to repentance and so abandon his sinful course of action. Repentance provoked by physical suffering is possibly also in mind.*
