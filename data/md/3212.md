# 3212: Holy Spirit, and mission

The Holy Spirit directs and empowers believers in their missionary tasks, bearing witness to Jesus Christ and preparing the hearts of men and women to respond to him in faith.



## The Holy Spirit equips God's people


### He inspired the prophets
 [NEH 9:30](bibleref:16009030); [JOB 32:8](bibleref:18032008); [ISA 48:16](bibleref:23048016); [MIC 3:8](bibleref:33003008); [HEB 1:1](bibleref:58001001)

### He empowered Jesus Christ as Messiah
 [ISA 11:2](bibleref:23011002), [ISA 42:1](bibleref:23042001); [MAT 3:16](bibleref:40003016) parallels MRK 1:10, LUK 3:21-3:22, JHN 1:32-1:34; [ACT 10:38](bibleref:44010038)

### He empowered the first Christians
 [ACT 1:8](bibleref:44001008), [ACT 2:16-2:17](bibleref:44002016-44002017), [ACT 8:17](bibleref:44008017), [ACT 10:44](bibleref:44010044), [ACT 11:15](bibleref:44011015), [ACT 19:6](bibleref:44019006) *The outpouring of God's Spirit begins to extend beyond the Jews.* 

### He empowers God's people today
 [JHN 14:16](bibleref:43014016); [ROM 8:26-8:27](bibleref:45008026-45008027); [1CO 12:3-12:7](bibleref:46012003-46012007)

## The Holy Spirit accomplishes the mission of God


### He convicts of sin
**[JHN 16:8](bibleref:43016008)** *See also* [ACT 2:37](bibleref:44002037); [1CO 14:24-14:25](bibleref:46014024-46014025)

### He directs mission
**[ACT 13:2](bibleref:44013002)** *See also* [ACT 8:29](bibleref:44008029), [ACT 10:19-10:20](bibleref:44010019-44010020), [ACT 13:4](bibleref:44013004), [ACT 16:6-16:10](bibleref:44016006-44016010)

### He inspires witnesses and speakers
**[ACT 6:9-6:10](bibleref:44006009-44006010)** *See also* [MAT 10:19-10:20](bibleref:40010019-40010020) parallels MRK 13:11, LUK 21:14-21:15; [ACT 2:14](bibleref:44002014), [ACT 4:31](bibleref:44004031), [ACT 11:15](bibleref:44011015), [ACT 13:9-13:10](bibleref:44013009-44013010); [1TH 1:5](bibleref:52001005); [1PE 1:12](bibleref:60001012)

### He gives signs and wonders
**[ROM 15:18-15:19](bibleref:45015018-45015019)** *See also* [JHN 1:50](bibleref:43001050); [ACT 5:13](bibleref:44005013); [2CO 12:12](bibleref:47012012); [GAL 3:5](bibleref:48003005); [HEB 2:4](bibleref:58002004)
