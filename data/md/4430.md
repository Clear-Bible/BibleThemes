# 4430: crops

Crops represent God's provision and are regulated by the Mosaic law. Their failure is associated with disobedience.



## Crops related to the law of Moses
**DEU 7:12-7:13** *See also* EXO 23:10-23:11, EXO 23:16; LEV 23:39, LEV 25:3, LEV 25:15-25:16, LEV 25:20-25:22, LEV 26:3-26:4; DEU 14:28-14:29, DEU 16:13 *the Feast of Tabernacles*; DEU 22:9, DEU 26:12, DEU 28:11, DEU 30:8-30:9; NEH 10:35-10:37; PRO 3:9-3:10; MAL 3:10-3:11

## Crops representing God's goodness
**PSA 65:9-65:10**; **ACT 14:17** *See also* GEN 26:12; JER 2:7; EZK 34:27-34:29, EZK 36:30; ZEC 8:12; JAS 5:17-5:18

## The failure of crops as a result of disobedience
