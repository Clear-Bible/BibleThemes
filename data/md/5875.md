# 5875: hatred

Hatred for others or God is associated with human sin. Scripture also emphasises God's hatred of sin and evil. Believers are commanded to love those who hate them.



## Hatred as evidence of corrupt human nature
**2SA 13:15**; **TIT 3:3** *See also* GEN 37:4; DEU 7:15, DEU 19:11-19:12; JDG 11:7; 2SA 13:22; PSA 25:19, PSA 38:19; PRO 10:12; MAT 24:10; GAL 5:19-5:20; 1JN 2:9-2:11, 1JN 3:15, 1JN 4:20

## Hatred of God himself
**PSA 81:15**; **ROM 1:29-1:31** *See also* EXO 20:5; DEU 7:10, DEU 32:41; 2CH 19:2; PSA 50:17; PRO 1:29; JHN 3:20, JHN 15:23-15:25; JAS 4:4

## Hatred of Jesus Christ
**JHN 7:7** *See also* ISA 53:3; MAT 12:14, MAT 21:46, MAT 26:3-26:4, MAT 27:22; LUK 19:14; JHN 17:14

## Hatred of the godly and upright
**1KI 22:8**; **MAT 10:22** *See also* EST 9:1; PSA 35:19, PSA 69:4, PSA 109:3-109:5; PRO 29:10; ISA 66:5; AMO 5:10; MAT 24:9-24:10 parallels MRK 13:13, LUK 21:16-21:19; LUK 1:69-1:71, LUK 6:22; JHN 15:18; ACT 9:29, ACT 23:12-23:15; 1JN 3:13

## Believers are to love those who hate them
**LUK 6:27-6:28** *See also* MAT 5:43-5:44

## God's hatred of evil
**PSA 5:4-5:5**; **PRO 6:16-6:19**; **ISA 61:8** *See also* DEU 12:31, DEU 16:21-16:22; PSA 11:5; ISA 1:14; JER 12:8; HOS 9:15; AMO 5:21; ZEC 8:16-8:17; MAL 2:16

## The godly should also hate evil
