# 5386: leisure, nature and purpose of

Scripture affirms that periods of rest and leisure are part of God's creation order. These periods of rest are to be used wisely.



## The need for leisure


### The Sabbath rest
**GEN 2:2**; **EXO 20:8-20:10** parallel DEU 5:12-5:15 *See also* EXO 23:11 *During the sabbath year, the labourers presumably also rested*; LEV 23:3

### Rest commanded by Jesus Christ
**MRK 6:31-6:32**

## Leisure a gift of God
**PSA 23:2** *See also* MAT 11:28; MRK 2:27; REV 14:13

### Loss of leisure a judgment
**ISA 48:22** *See also* ECC 2:23, ECC 5:12; ISA 57:20-57:21; REV 14:11

## Responsible use of leisure


### Work must come first
 PRO 10:5; ; LUK 17:7-17:8

### Remember God's presence
**1CO 10:31** *See also* ECC 9:7-9:10

### Use time wisely
**EPH 5:15-5:16**

## The dangers of leisure


### Idleness
**1TI 5:13** *See also* PRO 6:9-6:11, PRO 24:30-24:31, PRO 26:14; ECC 10:18; 2TH 3:11; HEB 6:12

### Preoccupation with pleasure
**LUK 8:14** *See also* PRO 21:17; ISA 5:11; LUK 15:13; 1TI 5:6; 2TI 3:4; JAS 5:5

### Susceptibility to sin
**1TI 5:13** *See also* 2SA 11:1-11:5; EST 1:10-1:11; AMO 4:1

### Spiritual complacency
**AMO 6:1, AMO 6:4-6:7** *See also* ISA 5:11-5:13; LUK 12:16-12:21, LUK 21:34
