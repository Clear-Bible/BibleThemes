# 5346: injury

Physical or mental hurt, usually resulting from the deliberate actions or words of others.



## Physical injury
**JDG 1:6** *See also* GEN 32:25; LUK 10:30; 2CO 11:23-11:25

### Reparation must be made for physical injury
 EXO 21:19, EXO 21:22; ; LEV 24:17-24:22

### Jesus Christ suffered physical injury
 MAT 16:21; ; JHN 19:1

### God's people may suffer physical injury for his sake
 JER 38:6; ; GAL 6:17; ; COL 1:24; ; 1PE 2:19

## Mental injury


### Injury by slander
 LEV 19:16; ; PRO 30:10; ; MAT 5:11; ; TIT 3:2; ; JAS 4:11; ; 1PE 2:1

### Injury by injustice
 PSA 58:1-58:2; ; ECC 3:16; ; ISA 59:4-59:6; ; EZK 34:4

### Jesus Christ suffered mental injury
 MAT 26:59-26:60; ; 1PE 2:20-2:23

### God's people may suffer mental injury for his sake
**MAT 5:11** *See also* PSA 38:20; JER 15:15; 1TH 2:2; 1PE 2:19

## All deliberate injury ruled out by love
**ROM 13:10**

## Spiritual injury suffered by God's people
 *See also* ISA 1:5-1:6; JER 10:19, JER 30:12; HOS 6:1 *Sometimes this is God's judgment.*
