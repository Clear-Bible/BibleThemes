# 5329: guarantee

A pledge given that a promise will be fulfilled; a deposit or down payment making fulfilment obligatory. All God's promises are guaranteed in and through Jesus Christ.

## Laws relating to guarantees


### Promises must be kept
**[NUM 30:2](bibleref:04030002)** *See also* [PSA 15:4-15:5](bibleref:19015004-19015005)

### The poor must not be oppressed
**[EXO 22:26-22:27](bibleref:02022026-02022027)** *See also* [DEU 24:6](bibleref:05024006), [DEU 24:10-24:12](bibleref:05024010-05024012), [DEU 24:17](bibleref:05024017)

### Special laws relating to women
 [NUM 30:3-30:16](bibleref:04030003-04030016)

### Guarantees could be written or spoken
 [NUM 30:2](bibleref:04030002); [EZR 10:19](bibleref:15010019)
