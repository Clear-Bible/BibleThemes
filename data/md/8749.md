# 8749: false teachers

Those who teach error and in so doing lead others astray. They are to be distinguished from false prophets who are equally condemned in Scripture.

## The OT prophets warned God's people against being led astray by false teachers
**[ISA 9:16](bibleref:23009016)**; **[EZK 34:2](bibleref:26034002)** *See also* [ISA 3:12](bibleref:23003012), [ISA 8:20](bibleref:23008020); [JER 2:8](bibleref:24002008), [JER 10:21](bibleref:24010021), [JER 23:1](bibleref:24023001); [EZK 22:26](bibleref:26022026); [MAL 2:7-2:8](bibleref:39002007-39002008)

## Jesus Christ condemned false teachers


### He opposed legalism and hypocrisy
**[MAT 5:19](bibleref:40005019)**

### He identified the teachers of the law and Pharisees as false teachers
**[MAT 23:1-23:33](bibleref:40023001-40023033)** parallel LUK 11:42-11:52 *See also* [MAT 15:14](bibleref:40015014)

### He warned his followers against them
**[MAT 16:6](bibleref:40016006), [MAT 16:12](bibleref:40016012)** parallels MRK 8:15, LUK 12:1
