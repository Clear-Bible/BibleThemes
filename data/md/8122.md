# 8122: friendship, with God

A relationship of love and faithfulness into which God calls all people through faith.



## Examples of friendship with God


### Abraham
**JAS 2:23** *See also* 2CH 20:7; ISA 41:8

### Moses
**EXO 33:11** *See also* NUM 12:8; DEU 34:10

### Job
**JOB 29:4-29:5** *See also* JOB 16:20-16:21

## Examples of friendship with Jesus Christ


### Mary, Martha and Lazarus
**JHN 11:5** *See also* JHN 11:3, JHN 11:11, JHN 11:35-11:36, JHN 12:1-12:2

### The beloved disciple
**JHN 13:23** *“the disciple whom Jesus loved” is generally thought to be John.* *See also* JHN 19:26, JHN 20:2, JHN 21:7, JHN 21:20

### The disciples
**JHN 15:14-15:15**

## The ministry of Jesus Christ is an expression of friendship to sinners


### In his life on earth
**MAT 11:19** parallel LUK 7:34 *See also* LUK 7:39, LUK 19:7

### In his death on the cross
**JHN 15:13**; **ROM 5:7-5:8** *See also* LUK 12:4; JHN 3:16; 1PE 3:18

## God's delight in friendship with those who he loves
**ISA 5:7**; **HOS 11:1-11:4** *See also* 1JN 1:3

## The reliability of God as a friend
**ISA 54:10** *See also* DEU 7:9; JOS 1:5; PRO 18:24; JHN 13:1; HEB 13:5

## God's invitation for people to know his friendship
**PSA 145:18**; **JAS 4:8**; **REV 3:20** *Sharing a meal is a strong expression of friendship in Scripture.* *See also* ZEC 1:3; MAL 3:7; ACT 17:27; JAS 4:4
