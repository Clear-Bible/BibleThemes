# 5176: ring

An ornament or a status symbol, sometimes used in sacred furnishings or as a charm.

## Rings worn as jewellery


### Gold nose rings, worn especially by women
These were often given by a suitor as a symbol of betrothal:
 [GEN 24:22](bibleref:01024022), [GEN 24:30](bibleref:01024030), [GEN 24:47](bibleref:01024047); [PRO 11:22](bibleref:20011022); [ISA 3:21](bibleref:23003021); [EZK 16:12](bibleref:26016012)

### The signet ring as a sign of authority
 [GEN 41:42](bibleref:01041042); [NUM 31:50](bibleref:04031050); [EST 3:10](bibleref:17003010), [EST 3:12](bibleref:17003012), [EST 8:8](bibleref:17008008); [DAN 6:17](bibleref:27006017)[JER 22:24](bibleref:24022024); [HAG 2:23](bibleref:37002023)

### Gold ear-rings, worn by men and women
Ear-rings were sometimes used as charms or amulets:
 [GEN 35:4](bibleref:01035004); [EXO 32:2-32:3](bibleref:02032002-02032003), [EXO 35:22](bibleref:02035022); [JDG 8:24-8:25](bibleref:07008024-07008025)

## Rings as indications of wealth or status
 [JOB 42:11](bibleref:18042011); [HOS 2:13](bibleref:28002013); [LUK 15:22](bibleref:42015022); [JAS 2:2](bibleref:59002002)
