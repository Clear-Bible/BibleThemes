# 8151: revival, corporate

The experience of God's people both in the OT and NT when prayer is answered and their growth and effectiveness are renewed.



## The recurring pattern of apostasy and revival in the OT
 JDG 2:10-2:19

## God is petitioned to revive his people
**PSA 80:14-80:15** *See also* PSA 85:4-85:7; JER 31:18; LAM 5:21; HAB 3:2

## Repentance is necessary for revival


### God's people are urged to repent
**2CH 30:6** *Hezekiah's appeal to all Israel.* *See also* ISA 55:1-55:3; JER 3:22; LAM 3:40; HOS 12:6, HOS 14:1-14:2; ZEC 1:3; MAL 3:7; REV 2:5, REV 2:16, REV 3:2-3:3

### Wholehearted repentance is required
**JOL 2:12-2:13** *See also* DEU 4:29, DEU 30:2-30:3; JER 24:7, JER 29:13-29:14; ACT 19:18-19:19 *Repentance should be evidenced by deeds.*

### Repentance prepares for revival
**ISA 59:20** *See also* JDG 10:9-10:16; EZR 10:1, EZR 10:10-10:12; NEH 1:9; EZK 18:30-18:32

## Revival is God's work
**HOS 6:2** *See also* PSA 80:3, PSA 80:7, PSA 80:17-80:19; ISA 32:14-32:17

## God promises revival


### God promises to revive his people
**EZK 11:19** *See also* JER 31:33-31:34; EZK 36:26; ZEC 10:6

### The nations are included in the promise of revival
**ISA 2:3** parallel MIC 4:2 *See also* ISA 19:22-19:25, ISA 45:22, ISA 56:6-56:8; ZEC 8:20-8:22

## The revival of Israel


### Under Hezekiah

### Under Josiah
 2KI 23:1-23:4 parallel 2CH 34:29-34:33; ; 2CH 35:1-35:3

### The Holy Spirit is active in the revival of believers
