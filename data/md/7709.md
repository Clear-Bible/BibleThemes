# 7709: apostles, authority of

Apostolic authority derives directly from Jesus Christ, who commissions apostles. It is rigorously upheld within the church.



## Apostolic authority is based on divine commission
**[ROM 1:5](bibleref:45001005)** *See also* [ROM 1:1](bibleref:45001001); [2CO 1:1](bibleref:47001001); [GAL 1:1](bibleref:48001001); [EPH 1:1](bibleref:49001001); [COL 1:1](bibleref:51001001); [1TI 1:1](bibleref:54001001); [2TI 1:1](bibleref:55001001)

## Apostolic authority derives from Jesus Christ
**[MAT 10:40](bibleref:40010040)** *See also* [MAT 28:18-28:20](bibleref:40028018-40028020); [MRK 6:7](bibleref:41006007); [2PE 3:2](bibleref:61003002)
