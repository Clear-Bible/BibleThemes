# 8654: importunity, towards people

Boldness, urgency and persistence in appealing to others; seen in making requests of all kinds, presenting the gospel and giving Christian instruction; seen too in God's patient and persistent pleading with people.

 [GEN 19:3](bibleref:01019003) *Lot prevails on his angelic visitors to stay with him* ; [GEN 39:10](bibleref:01039010) *Potiphar's wife propositions Joseph* ; [NUM 22:37](bibleref:04022037) *Balak summons Balaam* ; [JDG 14:16-14:17](bibleref:07014016-07014017) *Samson's wife presses for the answer to his riddle* ; [JDG 16:6-16:16](bibleref:07016006-07016016) *Delilah nags Samson to know the secret of his strength* ; [2KI 2:16-2:17](bibleref:12002016-12002017) *Elisha is requested to authorise a search for Elijah* ; [EST 3:3-3:4](bibleref:17003003-17003004) *Royal officials plead with Mordecai* ; [EST 8:3](bibleref:17008003) *Esther pleads with the king* ; [PRO 19:7](bibleref:20019007) *A poor man pleads in vain with fair-weather friends* ; [JER 38:26](bibleref:24038026) *Jeremiah pleads with King Zedekiah* ; [LUK 23:23](bibleref:42023023) *The crowds call for Jesus Christ to be crucified* ; [ACT 12:16](bibleref:44012016) *Peter knocks on the door after his release from prison* ; [ACT 25:3](bibleref:44025003) *The chief priests urge that Paul be taken to Jerusalem* ; [2CO 8:4](bibleref:47008004) *The Macedonian churches ask to share in the giving to God's people* ; [PHP 4:2](bibleref:50004002) *Paul pleads with Euodia and Syntyche to agree.*
