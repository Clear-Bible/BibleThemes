# 8654: importunity, towards people

Boldness, urgency and persistence in appealing to others; seen in making requests of all kinds, presenting the gospel and giving Christian instruction; seen too in God's patient and persistent pleading with people.



## Making requests of others
 GEN 19:3 *Lot prevails on his angelic visitors to stay with him*; ; GEN 39:10 *Potiphar's wife propositions Joseph*; ; NUM 22:37 *Balak summons Balaam*; ; JDG 14:16-14:17 *Samson's wife presses for the answer to his riddle*; ; JDG 16:6-16:16 *Delilah nags Samson to know the secret of his strength*; ; 2KI 2:16-2:17 *Elisha is requested to authorise a search for Elijah*; ; EST 3:3-3:4 *Royal officials plead with Mordecai*; ; EST 8:3 *Esther pleads with the king*; ; PRO 19:7 *A poor man pleads in vain with fair-weather friends*; ; JER 38:26 *Jeremiah pleads with King Zedekiah*; ; LUK 23:23 *The crowds call for Jesus Christ to be crucified*; ; ACT 12:16 *Peter knocks on the door after his release from prison*; ; ACT 25:3 *The chief priests urge that Paul be taken to Jerusalem*; ; 2CO 8:4 *The Macedonian churches ask to share in the giving to God's people*; ; PHP 4:2 *Paul pleads with Euodia and Syntyche to agree.*
