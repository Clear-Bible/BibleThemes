# 2377: kingdom of God, entry into

Entering or inheriting the kingdom of God is the privilege of those who acknowledge and live by the rule of God and have become part of the new order of salvation and righteousness in Christ.



## Entry into the kingdom of God is of vital importance


### It is costly
**[MAT 13:44](bibleref:40013044)**; **[ACT 14:22](bibleref:44014022)** *See also* [MAT 8:19-8:20](bibleref:40008019-40008020) parallel LUK 9:57-9:58; [MAT 13:45-13:46](bibleref:40013045-40013046); [LUK 18:29-18:30](bibleref:42018029-42018030); [2TH 1:5](bibleref:53001005); [REV 1:9](bibleref:66001009)

### It is a matter of urgency
**[LUK 9:59-9:62](bibleref:42009059-42009062)** parallel MAT 8:21-8:22

### Childlike trust

## The kingdom of God is a kingdom of grace


### It belongs to those qualified by God
**[COL 1:12-1:13](bibleref:51001012-51001013)** *See also* [LUK 12:32](bibleref:42012032), [LUK 22:29-22:30](bibleref:42022029-42022030)

### It belongs to the poor and the poor in spirit
**[LUK 6:20](bibleref:42006020)** parallel MAT 5:3 *See also* [MAT 11:5](bibleref:40011005) parallel LUK 7:22; [MAT 19:23-19:24](bibleref:40019023-40019024) parallels MRK 10:23-10:25, LUK 18:24-18:25 *It is hard for the rich to enter the kingdom of God* ; [JAS 2:5](bibleref:59002005), [JAS 5:1](bibleref:59005001)

**[MRK 2:17](bibleref:41002017)** parallels MAT 9:12-9:13, LUK 5:31-5:32 *See also* [MAT 21:31](bibleref:40021031)
