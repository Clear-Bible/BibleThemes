# 1220: God, as shepherd

The image of God as a shepherd points to his continual direction, guidance and care for his people.



## Shepherd as a title for God
**PSA 80:1** *See also* GEN 49:24; ECC 12:11

## God's people are his flock


### Israel is God's flock
**PSA 95:7** *See also* PSA 79:13, PSA 100:3; JER 50:7; EZK 34:31

### The church is God's flock
**1PE 5:2** *See also* LUK 12:32; ACT 20:28-20:29

## The tasks undertaken by God the shepherd


### The shepherd leads and guides
**PSA 23:2-23:3** *See also* ISA 40:11

### The shepherd provides
**PSA 23:1** *See also* GEN 48:15; PSA 23:5-23:6; HOS 4:16; MIC 7:14

### The shepherd protects
**PSA 28:9** *See also* GEN 49:23-49:24

### The shepherd saves those who are lost or scattered
**JER 31:10** *See also* PSA 119:176; ISA 53:6; EZK 34:11-34:16; MAT 18:12-18:14 parallel LUK 15:3-15:7

### The shepherd judges
**EZK 34:17-34:22** *See also* JER 23:1; ZEC 10:2-10:3, ZEC 11:16; MAT 25:32-25:46

## God gives shepherds to be leaders over his people


### He gives David's line
**EZK 34:23** *See also* 2SA 5:2 parallel 1CH 11:2; PSA 78:70-78:72; EZK 34:23-34:24, EZK 37:24; MIC 5:4; MAT 2:6

### He gives individual leaders
 ISA 44:28, ISA 63:11

### He gives faithful leaders
**JER 3:15** *See also* JER 23:4; 1PE 5:2-5:4
