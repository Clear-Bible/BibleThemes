# 5569: suffering, hardship

Scripture provides examples of types of hardship which people may expect to encounter.



## Different kinds of hardship


### Poverty
**PRO 10:15** *See also* DEU 15:11; JOB 24:5-24:11; MAT 26:11 parallel MRK 14:7; JHN 12:8

### Hunger and thirst
**PSA 107:4-107:5** *See also* GEN 41:53-41:57; EXO 15:22-15:24, EXO 16:2-16:3, EXO 17:1-17:3; NUM 11:4-11:6; 1KI 17:7-17:12; NEH 5:1-5:5; MAT 4:2 parallel LUK 4:2

### Hard labour
**GEN 3:17-3:19** *See also* GEN 31:38-31:42; 2CH 10:3-10:4; ECC 2:21-2:22; 1TH 2:9

### Oppression
**JOB 35:9** *See also* EXO 1:11, EXO 2:23, EXO 5:8-5:9; 2KI 4:1; PSA 12:5, PSA 31:9-31:13; ECC 4:1; JER 6:6; AMO 3:9; JAS 5:4

### Danger
**NEH 4:21-4:23** *See also* EXO 18:8; 1KI 2:26; 1CH 11:15-11:19; ACT 27:27-27:44

### Israel
**DEU 28:47-28:48** *God's people suffered under his chastisement.* *See also* DEU 8:15 *their suffering in the wilderness*; DEU 26:6-26:7 *their suffering in Egypt*

### David was oppressed by his enemies
**PSA 132:1**

### Paul suffered in serving the Lord
**2CO 6:4-6:5**
