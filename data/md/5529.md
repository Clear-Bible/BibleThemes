# 5529: sieges

Sustained battle against fortified cities, preventing renewal of supplies; a common tactic in OT times. Samaria and Jerusalem both fell to sieges.



## Sieges used by and against Israel
 DEU 20:12, DEU 20:19-20:20; ; JDG 9:50; ; 1SA 11:1; ; 2SA 11:1, 2SA 20:15

## Ben-Hadad II twice besieged Samaria, unsuccessfully
**1KI 20:1** *See also* 1KI 20:15-20:20; 2KI 6:24, 2KI 7:3-7:7

## Shalmaneser V besieged Samaria
**2KI 17:5-17:6** *See also* 2KI 18:9-18:12

## Sennacherib besieged Jerusalem, unsuccessfully
 2KI 18:17, 2KI 18:35-18:36; ; 2CH 32:1-32:5; ; ISA 29:5-29:8, ISA 37:36-37:37

## Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem
**2KI 25:1** *See also* 2KI 25:2-25:11 parallel JER 39:1-39:10; 2CH 36:17-36:20 parallel JER 52:4-52:15

## Jesus Christ warned that Jerusalem would be besieged and destroyed
**LUK 19:43** *The Roman army destroyed Jerusalem in A.D. 70 using an embankment to besiege the city.* *See also* LUK 21:20-21:24

## Sieges caused great suffering and people adopted desperate measures for survival
 DEU 28:51-28:57; ; 2KI 6:24-6:29, 2KI 18:27
