# 5529: sieges

Sustained battle against fortified cities, preventing renewal of supplies; a common tactic in OT times. Samaria and Jerusalem both fell to sieges.

## Sieges used by and against Israel
 [DEU 20:12](bibleref:05020012), [DEU 20:19-20:20](bibleref:05020019-05020020); [JDG 9:50](bibleref:07009050); [1SA 11:1](bibleref:09011001); [2SA 11:1](bibleref:10011001), [2SA 20:15](bibleref:10020015)

## Ben-Hadad II twice besieged Samaria, unsuccessfully
**[1KI 20:1](bibleref:11020001)** *See also* [1KI 20:15-20:20](bibleref:11020015-11020020); [2KI 6:24](bibleref:12006024), [2KI 7:3-7:7](bibleref:12007003-12007007)

## Shalmaneser V besieged Samaria
**[2KI 17:5-17:6](bibleref:12017005-12017006)** *See also* [2KI 18:9-18:12](bibleref:12018009-12018012)

## Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem
