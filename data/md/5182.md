# 5182: skin

The outer covering of the body. Diseased skin is often subject to pain and regarded as unclean.

## Skin as the outer covering of the body
**[JOB 10:10-10:11](bibleref:18010010-18010011)** *See also* [GEN 3:21](bibleref:01003021), [GEN 27:11](bibleref:01027011); [EZK 37:6](bibleref:26037006), [EZK 37:8](bibleref:26037008)

## Violence against the skin
**[MIC 3:2-3:3](bibleref:33003002-33003003)** *See also* [JOB 2:4-2:5](bibleref:18002004-18002005)

## Skin and bones
