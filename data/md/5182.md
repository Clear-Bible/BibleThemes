# 5182: skin

The outer covering of the body. Diseased skin is often subject to pain and regarded as unclean.



## Skin as the outer covering of the body
**JOB 10:10-10:11** *See also* GEN 3:21, GEN 27:11; EZK 37:6, EZK 37:8

## Violence against the skin
**MIC 3:2-3:3** *See also* JOB 2:4-2:5

## Skin and bones
**PSA 102:5** *To be reduced to skin and bones is a mark of severe suffering.* *See also* NUM 4:8-4:14 *animal skins used for covering the ark of the Testimony*; JOB 19:20; LAM 3:4, LAM 4:8

## Skin diseases


### Skin diseases are painful
**JOB 30:30** *See also* JOB 7:5, JOB 18:13; LAM 5:10

### They can be defiling
**NUM 5:2-5:3** *See also* LEV 13:1-13:11, LEV 13:43-13:46, LEV 22:4

### As God's punishment for sin
 NUM 12:10-12:11 *Miriam*; ; 2KI 5:26-5:27 *Naaman*; ; 2CH 26:19-26:20 *Uzziah*

### Healed by Jesus Christ
 MAT 8:2-8:3 parallels MRK 1:40-1:42, LUK 5:12-5:13; ; LUK 17:12-17:14

## Skin cannot be changed
**JER 13:23**

## A person can know God even when the skin has been destroyed
**JOB 19:26-19:27**
