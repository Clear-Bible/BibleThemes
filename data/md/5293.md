# 5293: defence, human

God's people are to defend the needy and those in their care. They are also to defend the truth entrusted to them.



## Defence of the needy


### God commands his people to defend the needy
**PRO 31:8-31:9** *See also* ISA 1:17, ISA 10:1-10:2, ISA 58:6-58:7, ISA 58:9-58:10

### Examples of those who defended the needy
**PSA 72:4** EXO 2:17; ; ACT 7:24; 
RUT 2:15-2:16 *Boaz*

### Neglect of the cause of the needy is condemned
**PSA 82:2-82:4** *See also* JOB 5:4; ISA 1:23-1:24, ISA 29:20-29:21; JER 5:26-5:29, JER 22:15-22:17

## Church leaders are to defend the flock of God
**ACT 20:28-20:31** *See also* JHN 21:15-21:17; 1TI 4:6; TIT 1:10-1:11; HEB 13:17; 1PE 5:2

## Christians are urged to defend the faith


### It is the responsibility of all believers
**JUD 1:3** *See also* PHP 1:27-1:28; 2TH 2:15

### It is the responsibility of Christian leaders in particular
**2TI 1:13-1:14** *See also* 1TI 1:3-1:10, 1TI 6:20-6:21; TIT 1:12-1:14

### Examples of leaders who defended the faith
**PHP 1:16** *See also* ACT 6:8-6:10 *Stephen*; ACT 18:27-18:28 *Apollos*GAL 1:6-1:9, GAL 2:11-2:14; PHP 1:7
