# 5467: promises, divine

The promises of God reveal his particular and eternal purposes to which he is unchangeably committed and upon which believers can totally depend. These promises are, however, conditional upon obedience on the part of believers.



## God's promises are irrevocable


### He is absolutely trustworthy
**NUM 23:19** *See also* TIT 1:2; HEB 6:13-6:18

### He is unchanging
 PSA 110:4; ; MAL 3:6-3:7; ; JAS 1:17-1:18

### He has the power and will to fulfil his promises
**ISA 55:11** *See also* ROM 4:21

### He is faithful in keeping all his promises
 JOS 21:45, JOS 23:14-23:15; ; 1KI 8:56; ; PSA 145:13; ; HEB 10:23

### His promises stem from his goodness and glory
 2PE 1:3-1:4

### God may confirm his promises with an oath
**GEN 22:15-22:18** *See also* GEN 26:3; ISA 45:23; AMO 6:8, AMO 8:7

## Examples of God's promises through covenant relationship
 GEN 9:8-9:17 *with Noah*; GEN 15:9-15:21, GEN 17:1-17:22; ; HEB 11:8-11:9, HEB 11:17-11:19; 
GEN 26:3-26:4 *with Isaac*; GEN 28:13-28:15, GEN 46:2-46:4; EXO 19:1-19:6, EXO 24:1-24:8; 
NUM 25:10-25:12 *with Phinehas*; 2SA 7:5-7:16 parallel 1CH 17:4-17:14; ; 1KI 8:15, 1KI 8:24; 
JER 31:31-31:34 *with Jeremiah*

## The grounding of God's promises in Christ


### God's promises are fulfilled in Jesus Christ
**2CO 1:18-1:20** *See also* MAT 5:17; LUK 4:16-4:21; ACT 2:29-2:31, ACT 3:21-3:26, ACT 7:37; DEU 18:15-18:18; ACT 13:23, ACT 13:32-13:34, ACT 26:6-26:7; ROM 1:2-1:3, ROM 15:8; HEB 9:15

### Jesus Christ brings superior promises through the new covenant
**HEB 8:6-8:8** *Jesus Christ mediates a covenant of inner transformation, willing obedience, intimate relationship with God and forgiveness of sins forever.* *See also* EPH 1:13-1:14; HEB 7:22, HEB 11:13, HEB 11:39-11:40; 2PE 1:1-1:4

### Jesus Christ has the right to make promises on God's behalf
**JHN 3:34-3:35** *See also* JHN 1:1-1:2, JHN 1:14, JHN 8:25-8:29; HEB 1:1-1:3

## God's promises must be received by believers


### They are received by faith
**GAL 3:22** *See also* JHN 1:12; ROM 4:13-4:16

### They are received by perseverance and obedience
**HEB 10:36** *See also* ROM 4:19-4:24; 2CO 7:1; HEB 6:12

### God's promises unite believing Jews and Gentiles
**EPH 3:6** *See also* ACT 2:38-2:39; ROM 9:8; GAL 3:29, GAL 4:28; EPH 2:11-2:18; HEB 11:39-11:40

### Disaster awaits those who reject God's promises
 JOS 23:12-23:16; ; JHN 3:18-3:20, JHN 3:36; ; 2TI 2:11-2:13; ; HEB 6:4-6:12; ; 2PE 3:3-3:10

### The forgiveness of sins

### The peace of God

### Joy in God
 PSA 16:11, PSA 132:16; ; JHN 16:20-16:24

### The knowledge of God
