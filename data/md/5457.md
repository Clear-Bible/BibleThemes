# 5457: power, human

All human power and authority comes from God. God also gives special power to his people.



## All human power comes from God
**DEU 8:17-8:18**; **ROM 13:1** *See also* JDG 3:12; 2KI 13:3, 2KI 13:5; 2CH 25:8; DAN 2:37, DAN 4:29-4:32; JHN 19:10-19:11; ACT 4:28; COL 1:16; 1PE 2:13-2:14
