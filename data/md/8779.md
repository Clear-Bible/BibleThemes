# 8779: materialism, nature of

The attitude to life that places particular emphasis on immediate, physical values rather than on future, spiritual ones or that regards the material world as the only reality.



## The basic features of materialism


### An attitude of godlessness
**PSA 14:1-14:2** parallel PSA 53:1-53:2 *See also* JOB 35:9-35:10; PSA 10:4, PSA 10:11; ECC 1:1-1:11; LUK 12:13-12:19; ROM 1:20-1:22

### A denial of the resurrection
**MAT 22:23** *See also* ECC 4:1-4:3, ECC 9:3-9:6; ACT 4:1-4:2, ACT 23:6-23:8; 1CO 15:12-15:17; 2TI 2:17-2:19

### Desire for wealth and physical gratification
**1CO 15:32** *See also* ECC 2:4-2:9, ECC 9:7-9:10; MAT 6:19-6:24

## Materialism condemned
**PSA 52:7** *See also* JOB 22:4-22:11, JOB 31:25-31:28; PSA 49:10; PRO 18:11, PRO 23:5; ECC 5:10; MAT 16:26; LUK 12:23-12:25, LUK 21:34; JAS 5:1-5:3
