# 8636: asking

The act of making requests of another, as an expression of dependence. Scripture encourages believers to ask things of God in prayer, and gives guidance concerning the motivation in doing so.



## Asking as an aspect of human relationships


### Examples of everyday requests
**JOS 4:20-4:22**; **ECC 7:10**; **1PE 3:15** *See also* EXO 3:22; DEU 32:7; 1SA 12:19 *The people asked for a king*; 2KI 2:3; NEH 2:1-2:2; DAN 2:10-2:11; MAT 2:1-2:2 *the Magi seek the king of the Jews*; LUK 2:46 *Jesus Christ asks questions in the temple*; JHN 4:9; ROM 16:1-16:2; GAL 2:9-2:10; PHM 1:21

### Examples of stronger appeals
**PHP 4:2-4:3** *See also* GEN 50:17; EXO 5:15; LUK 23:20; ACT 25:3; 1TH 4:1
