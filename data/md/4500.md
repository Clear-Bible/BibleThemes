# 4500: poison

A substance obtained from plants or animals that can cause illness, pain, disability or death. It is often used symbolically to refer to distress or bitterness.

## Kinds of poison


### Poison from plants, water or food
 [EXO 15:23](bibleref:02015023); [DEU 32:32](bibleref:05032032); [2KI 2:19-2:22](bibleref:12002019-12002022), [2KI 4:39-4:40](bibleref:12004039-12004040); [HOS 10:4](bibleref:28010004)

### Poison from animals or reptiles
 [NUM 21:6](bibleref:04021006); [DEU 32:24](bibleref:05032024), [DEU 32:33](bibleref:05032033)

## Poison used symbolically


### To refer to distress
In these incidences God himself is portrayed as the one who brings such bitter experiences:
 [JOB 6:4](bibleref:18006004); [JER 8:14](bibleref:24008014), [JER 9:15](bibleref:24009015), [JER 23:15](bibleref:24023015)
