# 5840: eagerness

A willingness, zeal or longing to accomplish a task or fulfil an ambition.



## Eagerness to perform a task
**1CH 12:8**; **NEH 3:20** *See also* EZR 5:8; PRO 31:13; ACT 20:16; 1TH 2:17

## Eagerness to fulfil personal ambitions
**ZEP 3:7**; **1TI 6:10** *See also* PSA 56:6; PRO 1:11-1:12, PRO 28:20; MRK 6:25; GAL 4:17-4:18; PHP 3:6

## Eagerness for personal godliness
**2CH 15:15**; **2PE 1:10** *See also* 1CH 28:9; PSA 27:4, PSA 42:1-42:2, PSA 63:1; PRO 23:17; ACT 26:7; 1CO 14:1; 2CO 7:11; HEB 4:11, HEB 6:11; 1PE 2:2; 2PE 1:5

## Spiritual eagerness may be superficial and unreal
**ISA 58:2** *See also* PSA 78:34-78:37; ISA 48:1

## Believers should be eager to follow and serve their Lord


### Eager to do good works
**TIT 2:14** *See also* 2CO 8:7, 2CO 8:22; TIT 3:1, TIT 3:8, TIT 3:14; 1PE 3:13

### Eager to serve
**ROM 12:11**; **1PE 5:2** *See also* PSA 110:3; ROM 12:8; 1CO 14:12; EPH 4:3; TIT 3:8

### Eager to share the truth
**ROM 1:15**; **2PE 1:15** *See also* ACT 18:25, ACT 21:13; ROM 15:20; PHP 1:20; JUD 1:3

### Eager to receive or understand the truth
**MRK 12:37**; **ACT 10:33** *See also* MRK 10:17 parallels MAT 19:16, LUK 18:18; LUK 2:15, LUK 21:38; JHN 4:40; ACT 13:42, ACT 17:11; 1TI 4:15

### Eager to give
**2CO 8:7** *See also* EXO 35:21-35:22; 1CH 29:9; 2CO 8:11, 2CO 8:19; GAL 2:10; 1TI 6:18

### Eager to pray
**COL 4:2** *See also* ROM 15:30; PHP 1:3-1:4; COL 4:12

### Eager for God's honour
**NUM 25:11** *See also* 1KI 19:14; 2KI 10:16; JHN 2:17

### Eager to see Jesus Christ's return and the end of the world
**1CO 1:7** *See also* MAT 24:44 parallel LUK 12:40; ROM 8:19 *Creation shares the same eager expectation*; ROM 8:23; GAL 5:5; PHP 3:20
