# 5288: dead, the

Human beings who have died. Scripture provides illustrations of the manner in which the dead are to be buried, as well as explaining the doctrines of resurrection and judgment.



## Preparation of the dead for burial


### Washing
**ACT 9:37** *A custom common to both Jews and Greeks.*

### Embalming
**MAT 26:12** *See also* GEN 50:2, GEN 50:26; 2CH 16:14

### Grave clothes
**JHN 11:44** *See also* MRK 15:46 parallels MAT 27:59, JHN 19:40; JHN 20:7; ACT 5:6

## Provision for the dead


### Burial
**GEN 49:29-49:30** *It was the normal practice for the people of God to be buried.* *See also* JOS 24:30; RUT 1:17; 1KI 11:15; ACT 8:2

### Cremation
**JOS 7:25** *Cremation was not a Hebrew practice, except in cases of difficulty.* *See also* 1SA 31:12; AMO 6:10

## The plight of the unburied dead
**PSA 79:2** *To be unburied was a disgrace, a mark of judgment.* *See also* ECC 6:3; ISA 14:19; JER 7:33, JER 16:6

## Personal contact with a dead body conferred uncleanness
**NUM 19:11** *See also* LEV 21:11; NUM 6:6

## The place of the dead


### Sheol and Hades
**EZK 31:16** *Sheol is the OT term for the place of the dead; a grave, an underworld, a pit. Hades is the NT equivalent.* *See also* 2KI 21:26; PSA 88:3-88:4, PSA 89:48, PSA 116:3; ECC 9:10; ISA 14:9-14:10; ACT 2:31

### A place of judgment
**PSA 49:13-49:14** *See also* PSA 31:17, PSA 88:3-88:5; NAM 1:14; MAT 5:22, MAT 11:23 parallel LUK 10:15; MAT 16:18; REV 1:18, REV 20:13-20:14

### A place of deliverance
**PSA 139:8** *See also* PSA 16:10, PSA 30:3, PSA 86:13, PSA 107:20; HOS 13:14; JON 2:2; ACT 2:27

## The plight of the dead outside of Christ


### Physical death is the result of sin
**ROM 6:23** *See also* GEN 2:17; EZK 18:20; ROM 1:32, ROM 5:12, ROM 8:6; JAS 1:15

### Final judgment will follow resurrection
**REV 21:8** *The “second death” is the throwing of the lost into the lake of fire.* *See also* MAT 10:28, MAT 25:41; 1JN 5:16-5:17; JUD 1:12

## Promises to the dead in Christ


### Death is but a sleep
**1TH 4:13-4:14** *See also* JHN 11:11-11:14; 1CO 15:17-15:22, 1CO 15:51-15:53

### Death has no hold on believers
**JHN 8:51** *See also* JHN 11:25-11:26; ROM 8:38-8:39

### Believers will be delivered from the second death
**REV 2:11** *See also* REV 20:6
