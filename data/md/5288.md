# 5288: dead, the

Human beings who have died. Scripture provides illustrations of the manner in which the dead are to be buried, as well as explaining the doctrines of resurrection and judgment.

## Preparation of the dead for burial


### Washing
**[ACT 9:37](bibleref:44009037)** *A custom common to both Jews and Greeks.* 

### Embalming
**[MAT 26:12](bibleref:40026012)** *See also* [GEN 50:2](bibleref:01050002), [GEN 50:26](bibleref:01050026); [2CH 16:14](bibleref:14016014)

### Grave clothes
**[JHN 11:44](bibleref:43011044)** *See also* [MRK 15:46](bibleref:41015046) parallels MAT 27:59, JHN 19:40; [JHN 20:7](bibleref:43020007); [ACT 5:6](bibleref:44005006)

## Provision for the dead


### Burial
**[GEN 49:29-49:30](bibleref:01049029-01049030)** *It was the normal practice for the people of God to be buried.*  *See also* [JOS 24:30](bibleref:06024030); [RUT 1:17](bibleref:08001017); [1KI 11:15](bibleref:11011015); [ACT 8:2](bibleref:44008002)

### Cremation
**[JOS 7:25](bibleref:06007025)** *Cremation was not a Hebrew practice, except in cases of difficulty.*  *See also* [1SA 31:12](bibleref:09031012); [AMO 6:10](bibleref:30006010)

## The plight of the unburied dead
**[PSA 79:2](bibleref:19079002)** *To be unburied was a disgrace, a mark of judgment.*  *See also* [ECC 6:3](bibleref:21006003); [ISA 14:19](bibleref:23014019); [JER 7:33](bibleref:24007033), [JER 16:6](bibleref:24016006)

## Personal contact with a dead body conferred uncleanness
**[NUM 19:11](bibleref:04019011)** *See also* [LEV 21:11](bibleref:03021011); [NUM 6:6](bibleref:04006006)

## The place of the dead


### Sheol and Hades
**[EZK 31:16](bibleref:26031016)** *Sheol is the OT term for the place of the dead; a grave, an underworld, a pit. Hades is the NT equivalent.*  *See also* [2KI 21:26](bibleref:12021026); [PSA 88:3-88:4](bibleref:19088003-19088004), [PSA 89:48](bibleref:19089048), [PSA 116:3](bibleref:19116003); [ECC 9:10](bibleref:21009010); [ISA 14:9-14:10](bibleref:23014009-23014010); [ACT 2:31](bibleref:44002031)

### A place of judgment
**[PSA 49:13-49:14](bibleref:19049013-19049014)** *See also* [PSA 31:17](bibleref:19031017), [PSA 88:3-88:5](bibleref:19088003-19088005); [NAM 1:14](bibleref:34001014); [MAT 5:22](bibleref:40005022), [MAT 11:23](bibleref:40011023) parallel LUK 10:15; [MAT 16:18](bibleref:40016018); [REV 1:18](bibleref:66001018), [REV 20:13-20:14](bibleref:66020013-66020014)

### A place of deliverance
**[PSA 139:8](bibleref:19139008)** *See also* [PSA 16:10](bibleref:19016010), [PSA 30:3](bibleref:19030003), [PSA 86:13](bibleref:19086013), [PSA 107:20](bibleref:19107020); [HOS 13:14](bibleref:28013014); [JON 2:2](bibleref:32002002); [ACT 2:27](bibleref:44002027)

## The plight of the dead outside of Christ


### Physical death is the result of sin
**[ROM 6:23](bibleref:45006023)** *See also* [GEN 2:17](bibleref:01002017); [EZK 18:20](bibleref:26018020); [ROM 1:32](bibleref:45001032), [ROM 5:12](bibleref:45005012), [ROM 8:6](bibleref:45008006); [JAS 1:15](bibleref:59001015)

### Final judgment will follow resurrection
**[REV 21:8](bibleref:66021008)** *The “second death” is the throwing of the lost into the lake of fire.*  *See also* [MAT 10:28](bibleref:40010028), [MAT 25:41](bibleref:40025041); [1JN 5:16-5:17](bibleref:62005016-62005017); [JUD 1:12](bibleref:65001012)
