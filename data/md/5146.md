# 5146: covering

That which literally or metaphorically hides from the human or divine eye what should not be seen.

## Covering for the human body
**[GEN 3:7](bibleref:01003007)**; **[EZK 16:8](bibleref:26016008)** *See also* [GEN 9:23](bibleref:01009023); [EXO 22:26-22:27](bibleref:02022026-02022027), [EXO 28:42](bibleref:02028042); [ISA 47:3](bibleref:23047003), [ISA 57:8](bibleref:23057008); [EZK 16:10](bibleref:26016010); [HOS 2:9](bibleref:28002009); [JON 3:6](bibleref:32003006); [REV 3:18](bibleref:66003018)
