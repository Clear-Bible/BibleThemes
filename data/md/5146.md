# 5146: covering

That which literally or metaphorically hides from the human or divine eye what should not be seen.



## Covering for the human body
**GEN 3:7**; **EZK 16:8** *See also* GEN 9:23; EXO 22:26-22:27, EXO 28:42; ISA 47:3, ISA 57:8; EZK 16:10; HOS 2:9; JON 3:6; REV 3:18

## Covering as a sign of submission or humility
**ISA 6:2** *See also* GEN 24:65; 1CO 11:5-11:15 *Some suggest that this passage refers either to a woman's modesty or to her dignity considering her new Christian status of equality with man.*

## The covering of human sin through atonement
**PSA 32:1-32:2** *See also* GEN 20:16; LEV 4:20 *The word translated “atonement” literally means “covering”*; NEH 4:5; JOB 14:17; PSA 32:5, PSA 85:2; PRO 17:9; ROM 4:7-4:8; JAS 5:20; 1PE 4:8

## Natural phenomena as God's covering
**PSA 18:11** *See also* EXO 19:18; NUM 9:15-9:16; PSA 105:39
