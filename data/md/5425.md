# 5425: net

Instruments of meshed string, used for fishing or hunting. The term can also be used in a symbolic manner, to refer to the way in which people are ensnared by sin or by their enemies.



## Nets used in fishing and hunting
**ISA 51:20**; **EZK 47:10**; **MAT 4:18** *See also* PRO 1:17; ISA 19:8; EZK 26:5; MAT 4:21 parallel MRK 1:19; MAT 13:47; MRK 1:18; LUK 5:2-5:4; JHN 21:6

## Nets used metaphorically


### To describe people being caught by others
**PRO 29:5**; **HAB 1:15-1:17** *See also* JOB 18:8; PSA 9:15, PSA 10:9, PSA 25:15, PSA 35:7, PSA 57:6, PSA 140:5, PSA 141:10; ECC 9:12; EZK 19:8; HOS 5:1; MIC 7:2

### To describe being caught by the Lord
**JOB 19:6**; **HOS 7:12**; **LUK 5:10** *See also* LAM 1:13; EZK 12:13, EZK 17:20, EZK 32:3

## Networks used in design
 EXO 27:4-27:5; ; 1KI 7:17; ; JER 52:22
