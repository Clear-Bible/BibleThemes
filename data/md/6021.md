# 6021: sin, nature of

Scripture portrays sin in terms of wrongness before God in a variety of different ways, such as uncleanness, guilt or rebellion.



## The basic nature of sin


### All sin is directed against God
**[PSA 51:4](bibleref:19051004)** *See also* [GEN 13:13](bibleref:01013013); [EXO 10:16](bibleref:02010016); [JDG 10:10](bibleref:07010010); [PSA 41:4](bibleref:19041004); [LUK 15:18](bibleref:42015018)

### Sin is essentially a lack of faith in God
**[ROM 14:23](bibleref:45014023)** *See also* [HEB 11:6](bibleref:58011006)

## Descriptions of sin


### Corruption of God's good purposes
**[DEU 32:5](bibleref:05032005)** *See also* [GEN 6:11-6:12](bibleref:01006011-01006012); [DEU 9:12](bibleref:05009012), [DEU 31:29](bibleref:05031029); [JDG 2:19](bibleref:07002019)

### Doing evil in God's sight
**[JHN 3:19-3:20](bibleref:43003019-43003020)** *See also* [JDG 2:11](bibleref:07002011); [PSA 34:12-34:16](bibleref:19034012-19034016); [PRO 8:13](bibleref:20008013); [ISA 59:6-59:7](bibleref:23059006-23059007); [MAT 12:35](bibleref:40012035) parallel LUK 6:45

### Ungodliness
**[JUD 1:14-1:15](bibleref:65001014-65001015)** *See also* [ISA 9:17](bibleref:23009017), [ISA 32:6](bibleref:23032006); [1TI 1:9](bibleref:54001009)

### Rebellion against God's authority
**[ISA 30:9](bibleref:23030009)** *See also* [DEU 9:7](bibleref:05009007); [1SA 15:23](bibleref:09015023); [PSA 78:40](bibleref:19078040), [PSA 78:56](bibleref:19078056); [JER 3:13](bibleref:24003013); [HOS 7:13](bibleref:28007013)

### The breaking or transgression of God's laws
**[1JN 3:4](bibleref:62003004)** *See also* [1SA 13:13-13:14](bibleref:09013013-09013014); [1CH 10:13](bibleref:13010013); [NEH 9:29](bibleref:16009029); [MIC 1:5](bibleref:33001005), [MIC 7:18](bibleref:33007018); [ROM 2:23](bibleref:45002023), [ROM 4:15](bibleref:45004015), [ROM 5:14-5:17](bibleref:45005014-45005017); [JAS 2:10-2:11](bibleref:59002010-59002011)

### Straying from the right path
**[ISA 53:6](bibleref:23053006)** *See also* [PSA 58:3](bibleref:19058003), [PSA 95:10](bibleref:19095010), [PSA 119:10](bibleref:19119010), [PSA 119:21](bibleref:19119021), [PSA 119:118](bibleref:19119118)

### Incurring a debt
**[MAT 6:12](bibleref:40006012)** *See also* [MAT 18:21-18:35](bibleref:40018021-40018035)

### Falling short of a standard
**[ROM 3:23](bibleref:45003023)** *The words most commonly used for “sin” in the OT and NT originally signified “missing the mark”.*
