# 8115: discipleship, nature of

The state of following Jesus Christ, and serving and obeying him. The NT stresses the privileges, joys and cost of this calling.

### Learning from God
**JHN 6:45** *See also* ISA 54:13; LEV 11:44-11:45, LEV 19:2, LEV 20:7; EPH 5:1-5:2; 1PE 1:15-1:16

### Learning from Jesus Christ
**MAT 11:29** *See also* JHN 13:15; EPH 4:20-4:21; PHP 2:5; 1PE 2:21; 1JN 2:6

### Learning from the Holy Spirit
**JHN 14:26** *See also* LUK 12:12; JHN 16:13; 1CO 2:13; EPH 1:17, EPH 3:16-3:19; 1PE 1:12

### Learning from other people
**PHP 4:9** *See also* DEU 4:10, DEU 5:1, DEU 31:12; 1CO 4:6, 1CO 4:16, 1CO 11:1; PHP 3:17; 2TH 3:7, 2TH 3:9; 1TI 2:11, 1TI 5:4; 2TI 3:14
