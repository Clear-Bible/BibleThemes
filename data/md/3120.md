# 3120: Holy Spirit, descriptions of

Though himself invisible, the Holy Spirit may appear in symbolic form and his activities may be described metaphorically.



## The dove
**[MAT 3:16](bibleref:40003016)** parallels MRK 1:10, LUK 3:22 *See also* [JHN 1:32](bibleref:43001032)

## The wind or breath
**[ACT 2:2](bibleref:44002002)** *This phenomenon accompanied the filling with the Spirit.*  *See also* [EZK 37:9-37:14](bibleref:26037009-26037014) *The same Hebrew word is here translated “breath”, “wind” and “spirit”* ; [JHN 3:8](bibleref:43003008), [JHN 20:22](bibleref:43020022)

## The fire
**[ACT 2:3-2:4](bibleref:44002003-44002004)** *See also* [ISA 4:4](bibleref:23004004); [MAT 3:11-3:12](bibleref:40003011-40003012) parallel LUK 3:16-3:17; [1TH 5:19](bibleref:52005019)

## The oil of anointing
 [ISA 61:1](bibleref:23061001); [ACT 10:38](bibleref:44010038)

## Water
**[JHN 7:37-7:39](bibleref:43007037-43007039)** *See also* [ISA 44:3](bibleref:23044003); [JHN 4:14](bibleref:43004014); [1CO 12:13](bibleref:46012013)

## A seal
**[EPH 1:13](bibleref:49001013)** *See also* [EPH 4:30](bibleref:49004030)

## A deposit
 [2CO 1:21-1:22](bibleref:47001021-47001022); [EPH 1:14](bibleref:49001014)

## A guide or shepherd
**[ISA 63:14](bibleref:23063014)** *See also* [ISA 34:16](bibleref:23034016)

## A voice
**[HEB 3:7-3:11](bibleref:58003007-58003011)** *See also* [EZK 2:2](bibleref:26002002); [MAT 10:19-10:20](bibleref:40010019-40010020) parallel MRK 13:11; [JHN 16:13](bibleref:43016013)

## God's gift
**[ACT 2:38](bibleref:44002038)** *See also* [ACT 1:4](bibleref:44001004), [ACT 8:20](bibleref:44008020), [ACT 10:45](bibleref:44010045), [ACT 11:17](bibleref:44011017)

## The power of God
**[LUK 1:35](bibleref:42001035)** *See also* [LUK 5:17](bibleref:42005017), [LUK 6:19](bibleref:42006019), [LUK 24:49](bibleref:42024049)
