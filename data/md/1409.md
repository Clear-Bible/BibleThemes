# 1409: dream

A series of images or thoughts experienced in one's mind while asleep. A means by which a divine message may be conveyed while the recipient sleeps. God may communicate directly in the dream, or interpretation may be necessary.



## Direct communication through dreams
**GEN 31:24** *See also* GEN 20:3, GEN 20:6; 1SA 28:6, 1SA 28:15; 1KI 3:5, 1KI 3:15; JOB 33:14-33:15; MAT 1:20, MAT 2:12-2:13, MAT 2:19-2:20, MAT 2:22

## Dreams involving unusual images
 GEN 28:12-28:15, GEN 31:10-31:13, GEN 37:5-37:7, GEN 37:9, GEN 40:9-40:11, GEN 40:16-40:17, GEN 41:1-41:7; ; JDG 7:13; ; DAN 2:31-2:35, DAN 4:10-4:17

## God is the source of interpretation of such dreams
**DAN 2:26-2:28** *See also* GEN 40:8, GEN 41:15-41:16, GEN 41:25, GEN 41:28; DAN 4:24

## Interpretation of unusual images
 GEN 40:12-40:13, GEN 40:18-40:19, GEN 41:26-41:31; ; JDG 7:14-7:15; ; JER 23:28, JER 31:23-31:26; ; DAN 2:36-2:45, DAN 4:19-4:26

## Revelation to prophets through dreams
**NUM 12:6**; **JOL 2:28** *See also* DAN 1:17, DAN 2:19-2:23; ACT 2:17

## Dreams and false prophets
**DEU 13:1-13:3** *See also* DEU 13:5; JER 23:25-23:27, JER 23:32, JER 27:9, JER 29:8-29:9; ZEC 10:2

## Ordinary dreams
**ISA 29:7-29:8** *Not every dream is a vehicle for divine communication.  Most are part of the natural process of sleep.* *See also* JOB 7:13-7:15; PSA 73:20; ECC 5:3, ECC 5:7; ISA 56:10
