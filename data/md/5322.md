# 5322: gallows

A wooden structure, sometimes a tree, used to impale or display a body after death, or for execution by hanging. Those hanged on gallows were deemed to be under God's curse.

## A gallows used for impalement and display of bodies
**[EZR 6:11](bibleref:15006011)** *See also* [JOS 8:29](bibleref:06008029), [JOS 10:26](bibleref:06010026); [2SA 4:12](bibleref:10004012)

## A gallows used for execution
**[EST 7:9-7:10](bibleref:17007009-17007010)** *See also* [EST 2:23](bibleref:17002023), [EST 9:13](bibleref:17009013)

## Those hanged on gallows are under God's curse
**[GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013)** *See also* [DEU 21:23](bibleref:05021023)
