# 5327: governors

Those who ruled with an authority delegated to them by a higher ruler to whom they were responsible.



## Joseph as governor of Egypt
**GEN 42:6** *See also* GEN 41:41, GEN 41:43, GEN 41:55, GEN 45:8, GEN 45:26

**EZR 5:14** *See also* EZR 1:8

## Persian governors


### Tattenai
**EZR 5:3** *“Trans-Euphrates” (literally “beyond the river”, i.e., the Euphrates River) to the Persians included the areas of Aram, Phoenicia and Palestine.* *See also* EZR 5:6, EZR 6:6, EZR 6:13

### Other governors of Trans-Euphrates
**EZR 8:36** *See also* NEH 2:7, NEH 2:9, NEH 3:7

### Other Persian governors
**EST 3:12** *See also* EST 8:9, EST 9:3; DAN 3:2-3:3, DAN 3:27, DAN 6:7
