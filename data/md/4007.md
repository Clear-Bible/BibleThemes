# 4007: creation, and God

The natural world is sustained by God and speaks of God.



## God sustains the creation


### He upholds the natural order
**[HEB 1:3](bibleref:58001003)** *See also* [JOB 38:33-38:37](bibleref:18038033-18038037); [PSA 104:1-104:35](bibleref:19104001-19104035), [PSA 135:6-135:7](bibleref:19135006-19135007), [PSA 145:16-145:17](bibleref:19145016-19145017); [MAT 10:29-10:30](bibleref:40010029-40010030); [COL 1:17](bibleref:51001017)

### He sustains humanity
**[ACT 17:28](bibleref:44017028)** *See also* [JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004); [PSA 36:6](bibleref:19036006); [DAN 4:34-4:35](bibleref:27004034-27004035); [1CO 8:6](bibleref:46008006)

### Creation is upheld for the good of humanity
**[GEN 8:22](bibleref:01008022)** *See also* [GEN 9:12-9:16](bibleref:01009012-01009016); [MAT 6:11](bibleref:40006011)

### God's sustaining power reserves the world for judgment
**[2PE 3:7](bibleref:61003007)** *See also* [2PE 3:9-3:12](bibleref:61003009-61003012) *Time is being given for sinners to repent.* 

## God has given humanity responsibility to preserve creation
