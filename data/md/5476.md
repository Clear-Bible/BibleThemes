# 5476: property, nature of

Possessions are a gift from God to be used for his glory, for human pleasure and in benefiting others.



## Possessions are a cause for thanksgiving
**ECC 5:19** *See also* GEN 39:2-39:4; DEU 8:18; 1CH 29:12; 2CH 31:21; HOS 2:8

## Owning possessions is open to abuse


### They can be coveted
**EXO 20:17** *See also* 1KI 21:1-21:4; JER 6:13; MIC 2:2; LUK 12:15; 1CO 7:29-7:31

### They can cause deceit
 ACT 5:1-5:9

### They can cause pride and forgetfulness
**PRO 30:9** *See also* 2KI 20:13-20:17; DAN 4:30, DAN 5:20; HOS 13:6; MAT 19:24; LUK 12:13-12:21

### Abuse of ownership leads to judgment
 ISA 3:16-3:24, ISA 5:8; ; HAB 2:6-2:9

## Property is for benefiting others


### It is to be used to help others
 ACT 2:44-2:45, ACT 4:32-4:35; ; GAL 6:6, GAL 6:10; ; EPH 4:28; ; 1TI 6:17-6:18; ; JAS 2:15-2:16; ; 1JN 3:17-3:18

### It is to be used to assist strangers
 LEV 25:35; ; NUM 35:15; ; DEU 10:19; ; MAT 5:42

## Property provides no lasting satisfaction
**ECC 2:26** *See also* JOB 27:16-27:17; PSA 39:6; ECC 2:21; EZK 28:4-28:7; HAG 1:6; MAT 6:19-6:21; LUK 12:20-12:21; JAS 5:3

## Laws governing the inheritance of property
 NUM 27:3-27:11; ; DEU 21:15-21:17; ; EZK 46:16-46:18

## Godly men of property
 GEN 13:2 *Abraham*; ; GEN 26:14 *Isaac*; GEN 30:43, GEN 32:5, GEN 36:7; 
2SA 19:32 *Barzillai*; ; 1CH 29:28 *David*; 2CH 1:15, 2CH 9:22;
