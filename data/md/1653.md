# 1653: numbers, 6-10

The number seven is especially associated with completeness and perfection.  The number ten is occasionally used as a symbol of completeness.  Other numbers have a variety of associations.



## Six


### The world was created in six days
**EXO 20:11** *See also* GEN 1:1-1:31; EXO 31:17

### Human work is to be limited to six days a week
 EXO 16:26, EXO 20:9 parallel DEU 5:13; ; EXO 23:12, EXO 31:15, EXO 34:21, EXO 35:2; ; LEV 23:3; ; LUK 13:14

### Other important six-day periods
 DEU 16:8; ; JOS 6:3-6:14; ; MAT 17:1 parallel MRK 9:2; ; JHN 12:1

### Human work restricted to six-year periods
 EXO 21:2, EXO 23:10; ; LEV 25:3; ; DEU 15:12, DEU 15:18; ; JER 34:14

## Seven


### The seventh day is a day of rest
**GEN 2:2-2:3** *See also* EXO 20:10 parallel DEU 5:14; EXO 31:15-31:17, EXO 35:2-35:3; HEB 4:4

### The seventh year symbolises rest and freedom
**LEV 25:4** *See also* EXO 23:10-23:11; LEV 25:2-25:6; DEU 15:1, DEU 15:12; NEH 10:31

### Major festivals lasted seven days
Unleavened Bread:

### The Feast of Weeks, or Pentecost, comes seven weeks after the firstfruits of the harvest

### Festivals in the seventh month

### Important rituals associated with sevens
 LEV 4:6-4:17, LEV 14:7, LEV 14:51, LEV 16:14; ; NUM 28:11; ; 1CH 15:26; ; JOB 42:8

### Ritual uncleanness for seven days
 LEV 12:2, LEV 13:4, LEV 13:21, LEV 13:26, LEV 15:24

### Seven times indicates a completed action
 LEV 26:18; ; JOS 6:4; ; 1KI 18:43; ; 2KI 5:10; ; PSA 119:164

### Seven times indicates total intensification

### The seven spirits of God
**REV 5:6** *See also* ZEC 4:10; REV 1:4, REV 3:1, REV 4:5

**REV 21:9** *See also* REV 8:2, REV 8:6, REV 10:3-10:4, REV 12:3, REV 15:1, REV 15:7, REV 17:3
