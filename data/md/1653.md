# 1653: numbers, 6-10

The number seven is especially associated with completeness and perfection.  The number ten is occasionally used as a symbol of completeness.  Other numbers have a variety of associations.



## Six


### The world was created in six days
**[EXO 20:11](bibleref:02020011)** *See also* [GEN 1:1-1:31](bibleref:01001001-01001031); [EXO 31:17](bibleref:02031017)

### Human work is to be limited to six days a week
 [EXO 16:26](bibleref:02016026), [EXO 20:9](bibleref:02020009) parallel DEU 5:13; [EXO 23:12](bibleref:02023012), [EXO 31:15](bibleref:02031015), [EXO 34:21](bibleref:02034021), [EXO 35:2](bibleref:02035002); [LEV 23:3](bibleref:03023003); [LUK 13:14](bibleref:42013014)

### Other important six-day periods
 [DEU 16:8](bibleref:05016008); [JOS 6:3-6:14](bibleref:06006003-06006014); [MAT 17:1](bibleref:40017001) parallel MRK 9:2; [JHN 12:1](bibleref:43012001)

### Human work restricted to six-year periods
 [EXO 21:2](bibleref:02021002), [EXO 23:10](bibleref:02023010); [LEV 25:3](bibleref:03025003); [DEU 15:12](bibleref:05015012), [DEU 15:18](bibleref:05015018); [JER 34:14](bibleref:24034014)
