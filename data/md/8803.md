# 8803: pride, evil of

Pride is viewed as a great evil because it involves pretending to a greatness and glory that belong rightly to God alone.



## The sinfulness of pride


### It is condemned as evil
**1SA 15:23**; **PRO 21:4**; **JAS 4:16** *See also* MRK 7:22-7:23; ROM 1:29-1:30; 2CO 12:20; 2TI 3:1-3:2; 1JN 2:16

### It is a characteristic of Satan
**EZK 28:2**; **1TI 3:6** *See also* 2TH 2:4 *the antichrist*

## Warnings against pride


### In the book of Proverbs
**PRO 16:5, PRO 16:18** *See also* PRO 3:7, PRO 3:34, PRO 6:16-6:17, PRO 11:2, PRO 25:6-25:7, PRO 25:27, PRO 26:12, PRO 27:1, PRO 29:23

### Elsewhere in Scripture
**PSA 119:21** *See also* LEV 26:191SA 2:3; PSA 12:2-12:3; JER 9:23-9:24
PSA 5:5, PSA 40:4, PSA 138:6; ISA 5:21; JER 13:15-13:17; MAT 23:12 parallel LUK 14:11 *Jesus Christ's teaching on pride*; ROM 12:16; 1CO 10:121PE 5:5; JAS 4:6; PRO 3:34

## The gospel excludes pride
**ROM 3:27** *See also* LUK 18:9-18:14; ROM 4:2-4:3, ROM 11:17-11:20; 1CO 1:26-1:31; EPH 2:8-2:9

## Godliness involves rejecting pride
**PRO 8:13** *See also* PSA 101:5, PSA 131:1; ROM 12:3; 1CO 13:4; GAL 6:14
