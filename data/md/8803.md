# 8803: pride, evil of

Pride is viewed as a great evil because it involves pretending to a greatness and glory that belong rightly to God alone.



## The sinfulness of pride


### It is condemned as evil
**[1SA 15:23](bibleref:09015023)**; **[PRO 21:4](bibleref:20021004)**; **[JAS 4:16](bibleref:59004016)** *See also* [MRK 7:22-7:23](bibleref:41007022-41007023); [ROM 1:29-1:30](bibleref:45001029-45001030); [2CO 12:20](bibleref:47012020); [2TI 3:1-3:2](bibleref:55003001-55003002); [1JN 2:16](bibleref:62002016)
