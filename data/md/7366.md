# 7366: freewill offering

An offering that in some ways resembled the thank-offering or peace offering. It was completely voluntary, prompted by an occasion of celebration or spiritual significance. The animal offered was eaten at a festive banquet.

## Instructions for presenting a freewill offering


### The animal to be offered
 [LEV 22:17-22:22](bibleref:03022017-03022022), [LEV 22:23](bibleref:03022023) *Some slightly deformed animals were perhaps acceptable because the freewill offering was voluntary.* 

### Eating the offering
 [LEV 7:16](bibleref:03007016)

## Freewill offerings were associated with joy and celebration
**[PSA 54:6-54:7](bibleref:19054006-19054007)** *See also* [DEU 16:9-16:12](bibleref:05016009-05016012) *during the Feast of Weeks* 

## Occasions for making freewill offerings


### In maintaining public worship and at times of religious renewal
**[EXO 35:20-35:29](bibleref:02035020-02035029)** *Building the temple.*  *See also* [2CH 31:14](bibleref:14031014) *Hezekiah's reformation* [EZR 1:4-1:6](bibleref:15001004-15001006), [EZR 2:68](bibleref:15002068), [EZR 7:16](bibleref:15007016), [EZR 8:28](bibleref:15008028); [NEH 7:70-7:72](bibleref:16007070-16007072)
[EZK 46:11-46:12](bibleref:26046011-26046012) *Ezekiel's visionary ideal temple* 

### As an additonal offering at certain festivals
 [NUM 29:39](bibleref:04029039)[DEU 12:5-12:6](bibleref:05012005-05012006), [DEU 12:17-12:18](bibleref:05012017-05012018)

## Prophetic criticism of bragging over freewill offerings
**[AMO 4:5](bibleref:30004005)** *A sarcastic attack on the offering of freewill gifts as a way to gain prestige.*
