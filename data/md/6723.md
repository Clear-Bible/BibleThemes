# 6723: redemption, in NT

The culmination of the OT work of redemption is seen in the cross of Jesus Christ, by which believers are liberated from bondage to sin.



## Redemption is achieved through Jesus Christ
**[EPH 1:7](bibleref:49001007)**

## Jesus Christ's teaching on redemption
**[MRK 10:45](bibleref:41010045)** parallel MAT 20:28 *See also* [MAT 26:26-26:28](bibleref:40026026-40026028) parallels MRK 14:22-14:25, LUK 22:20-22:22

## Apostolic teaching on redemption


### Jesus Christ is the redemption of believers
**[1CO 1:30](bibleref:46001030)** *See also* [1TI 2:6](bibleref:54002006); [TIT 2:14](bibleref:56002014)

### Redemption comes through the shedding of the blood of Jesus Christ
**[GAL 3:13](bibleref:48003013)** *See also* [ACT 20:28](bibleref:44020028); [ROM 3:25](bibleref:45003025); [1CO 6:20](bibleref:46006020), [1CO 7:23](bibleref:46007023), [1CO 11:23-11:25](bibleref:46011023-46011025); [1PE 1:18-1:19](bibleref:60001018-60001019); [HEB 9:12](bibleref:58009012); [REV 1:5-1:6](bibleref:66001005-66001006), [REV 5:9](bibleref:66005009)
