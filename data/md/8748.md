# 8748: false religion

The worship of anything that is not God, including any form of religion that fails to do justice to the glory and majesty of God. Scripture criticises both the practices and the beliefs of false religions.



## The nature of false religion


### Man-made images are worshipped
**ACT 17:29** *See also* DEU 4:28; PSA 115:4-115:7, PSA 135:15-135:17; ISA 44:10-44:20; JER 10:3-10:5, JER 16:20; DAN 3:4-3:7; ACT 19:26

### Created things are worshipped
**ROM 1:25** *See also* 2KI 21:3 parallel 2CH 33:5; JER 8:2, JER 19:13; EZK 8:16

### Secular authorities are worshipped
**REV 13:4** *The dragon in Revelation may be understood as the devil and the beast as the antichrist: the forces at work behind the human authorities that defy the rule of God.* *See also* ACT 12:21-12:23; REV 13:8, REV 13:12

### Demons are worshipped
**DEU 32:17** *See also* LEV 17:7; PSA 106:37-106:38; 1CO 10:20; REV 9:20

## False religion is based on man-made traditions, myths and philosophies
 COL 2:8, COL 2:20-2:23; ; 1TI 1:4, 1TI 6:20

## False religious practices


### Immorality
**1KI 14:23-14:24** *See also* EZK 16:16-16:17; HOS 4:13-4:14

### Human sacrifice
**2KI 17:17** *See also* LEV 18:21; PSA 106:37-106:38

## False religion ensnares its adherents


### It offers no help in time of need
**1SA 12:21** *See also* JDG 10:14; 1KI 18:29; 2KI 19:12 parallel ISA 37:12; ISA 16:12, ISA 44:17-44:20, ISA 45:20, ISA 46:7, ISA 57:13; JER 11:12

### It prevents people from finding the truth
**JON 2:8** *See also* MAT 23:13; LUK 11:52; 2CO 4:4

### It devalues, degrades and defiles
**2KI 17:15** *See also* PSA 106:39, PSA 115:8, PSA 135:18; ISA 44:9; JER 2:5; EZK 20:18

### It leads to immorality
**NUM 25:1-25:2** *See also* NUM 25:3, NUM 25:6-25:8; ROM 1:23-1:31; REV 9:20-9:21

### It enslaves
**GAL 4:8** *See also* DEU 12:30; GAL 4:3, GAL 4:9-4:10; COL 2:8

## False religion receives the full force of God's anger
**JER 16:18** *See also* 1KI 13:1-13:3, 1KI 13:33-13:34; 2KI 17:18-17:23; JER 22:8-22:9; MIC 5:13-5:15; NAM 1:14; ZEP 1:4-1:5
