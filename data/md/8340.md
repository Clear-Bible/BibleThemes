# 8340: self-respect

A sense of personal worth and dignity. Scripture points to the need for believers to ground their self-respect in God himself, rather than trusting in the opinion and valuation of other individuals or society as a whole.



## Self-respect is often assumed
**MAT 22:39** parallel MRK 12:31 *See also* MAT 7:12, MAT 19:19; ROM 12:3; EPH 5:29

## Examples of those who had self-respect
**JOB 12:3** *See also* JOB 10:16; PRO 31:25; PHP 3:4

## The basis of self-respect


### A relationship with God
**PSA 139:13-139:14** *See also* PSA 30:6; 1CO 3:16-3:17, 1CO 6:12-6:20; 2CO 6:16; EPH 2:22; 1TI 4:4

### Equality in God's community
**JAS 1:9-1:10** *See also* 1CO 12:22-12:25

### A clear conscience and faithfulness to God
**2CO 1:12** *See also* JHN 7:18; GAL 6:4; HEB 13:18

## God's strength is a source of self-respect
**DAN 10:19** *See also* EXO 15:2; 2SA 22:33; PSA 71:5, PSA 89:17, PSA 118:14; 2TI 4:17

## Self-respect results from disciplined living
**JOB 4:6** *See also* PRO 20:3; 1TH 4:4; TIT 2:2

## Self-respect can be lost through humiliation
**JER 31:19** *See also* 2SA 10:1-10:5 parallel 1CH 19:1-19:5; 2SA 13:1-13:22; DAN 4:33

## Self-respect can rest on wrong foundations
**GAL 6:3** *See also* DEU 8:17-8:18; JOB 20:6-20:7; PHP 3:4-3:7

## Ways in which self-respect can be undermined


### By others
 JOB 30:1-30:15

### By the effects of sin and the work of Satan
 MRK 5:2-5:5 parallel LUK 8:27-8:29; ; MRK 5:25-5:28 parallels MAT 9:20-9:22, LUK 8:43-8:48; ; LUK 15:13-15:16, LUK 17:12-17:13

### By unwise spiritual zeal
 GAL 6:1; ; 1TI 5:1-5:2; ; JUD 1:22
