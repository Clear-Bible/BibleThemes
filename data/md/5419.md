# 5419: mourning

The expression of grief at a time of bereavement or repentance, often accompanied by weeping, tearing of clothes and wearing sackcloth.



## Regulations for the mourning of priests after bereavement
 LEV 21:1-21:4, LEV 21:10-21:11

## Mourning of God's people after bereavement


### Israel for Aaron
**NUM 20:29**

### Israel for Moses
**DEU 34:8**

### David for Saul and Jonathan
**2SA 1:11-1:12** *See also* 2SA 1:17-1:27

### David for Absalom
**2SA 18:33**

### Job for his children
**JOB 1:20-1:21**

### Other examples
 *See also* GEN 37:34-37:35, GEN 50:11; 2SA 13:31, 2SA 14:2; 2CH 35:23-35:25; MAT 2:18; JHN 11:31, JHN 11:33; ACT 8:2
