# 3045: Holy Spirit, sovereignty of

The Holy Spirit has supreme power and acts in accordance with divine purposes, exercising authority and control in the world and over God's people.



## The Holy Spirit is Lord
**[2CO 3:17-3:18](bibleref:47003017-47003018)** *See also* [JDG 3:10](bibleref:07003010)

## The Holy Spirit is sovereign in bringing spiritual life
**[JHN 3:1-3:8](bibleref:43003001-43003008)** *See also* [EZK 37:14](bibleref:26037014); [JHN 6:63](bibleref:43006063); [ROM 8:2](bibleref:45008002); [TIT 3:5](bibleref:56003005); [1PE 1:2](bibleref:60001002)

## The Holy Spirit's will is sovereign


### He determines the distribution of spiritual gifts
**[1CO 12:11](bibleref:46012011)** *See also* [1SA 10:6](bibleref:09010006), [1SA 10:10](bibleref:09010010), [1SA 19:20](bibleref:09019020), [1SA 19:23](bibleref:09019023); [HEB 2:4](bibleref:58002004)

### He controls the appointment of leaders
**[ACT 13:2](bibleref:44013002)** *See also* [ACT 20:28](bibleref:44020028)

## The Holy Spirit demonstrates his sovereignty over creation


### He participates in the work of creation
**[JOB 33:4](bibleref:18033004)** *See also* [GEN 1:2](bibleref:01001002); [PSA 104:30](bibleref:19104030)

### He performs miraculous deeds
**[ACT 8:39](bibleref:44008039)** *See also* [1KI 18:12](bibleref:11018012); [2KI 2:16](bibleref:12002016); [MAT 12:28-12:38](bibleref:40012028-40012038) parallels MRK 3:22-3:30, LUK 11:14-11:23

## The Holy Spirit exercises his rule in the lives of individuals


### Jesus Christ is led by the Spirit
**[MAT 4:1](bibleref:40004001)** parallels MRK 1:12, LUK 4:1

### God's people are led by the Spirit
**[GAL 5:18](bibleref:48005018)** *See also* [1CH 28:12](bibleref:13028012); [PSA 143:10](bibleref:19143010); [MAT 10:20](bibleref:40010020); [LUK 2:27](bibleref:42002027); [JHN 16:13](bibleref:43016013); [ACT 8:29](bibleref:44008029), [ACT 20:22](bibleref:44020022)

### God's people are restrained by the Spirit
**[ACT 16:6-16:7](bibleref:44016006-44016007)**

### God's people are given victory by the Spirit
**[JDG 3:10](bibleref:07003010)** *See also* [JDG 14:6](bibleref:07014006), [JDG 14:19](bibleref:07014019), [JDG 15:14](bibleref:07015014)

## The Holy Spirit's sovereignty is confirmed by the greatness of his attributes
