# 7775: prophets, lives of

God called individuals from differing backgrounds to speak as prophets on his behalf, sometimes in difficult circumstances.



## The backgrounds of the prophets
 [EXO 2:1-2:10](bibleref:02002001-02002010) *Moses was brought up in the Egyptian royal court* ; [1SA 1:21-1:28](bibleref:09001021-09001028) *Samuel was a temple servant at Shiloh* ; [1KI 19:19-19:21](bibleref:11019019-11019021) *Elisha was a farmer* ; [EZK 1:3](bibleref:26001003) *Ezekiel was a priest living in exile in Babylonia.* [AMO 1:1](bibleref:30001001), [AMO 7:14](bibleref:30007014)
