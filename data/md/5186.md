# 5186: stumbling

Faltering or falling in the course of running or walking. The term is also used metaphorically to refer to failure, hesitation or difficulty.



## Physical stumbling
 LEV 19:14; LEV 26:37; ; ISA 21:4; 
ISA 28:7 *through drunkenness*; NAM 2:5, NAM 3:3

## God protects his people from stumbling
**PSA 56:13** *See also* 1SA 2:4; JOB 4:4; PSA 37:24, PSA 116:8

## The enemies of God's people will stumble
**JER 20:11** *See also* PSA 9:3, PSA 27:2

## God's people will stumble if they sin
 ISA 8:14; ; JER 13:16

## Avoiding stumbling by keeping God's law
**PSA 119:165** *See also* PRO 3:23, PRO 3:26, PRO 4:11-4:12; ISA 26:7-26:8, ISA 40:30-40:31
