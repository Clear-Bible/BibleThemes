# 4357: salt

A basic commodity, used for seasoning and preserving food in biblical times.



## The importance of salt in the sacrificial system
**LEV 2:13** *See also* EXO 30:34-30:35 *The addition of salt to incense was probably to make it burn more easily and quickly*; EZR 6:6-6:10, EZR 7:21-7:23; EZK 43:22-43:24

## The covenant of salt
**NUM 18:17-18:19** *See also* LEV 2:13; 2CH 13:4-13:5

## Believers as the salt of the earth
**MAT 5:13** *This teaching reflects the importance of salt as a preservative and seasoning.* *See also* MRK 9:50; LUK 14:34-14:35

## Salt as a symbol of desolation
**ZEP 2:9** *See also* DEU 29:23-29:25; JOB 39:5-39:6; PSA 107:33-107:34; JER 17:5-17:8

## Salt sown on arable land to render it worthless
**JER 48:8-48:9** *See also* JDG 9:42-9:45

## Lot's wife turned into a pillar of salt
**GEN 19:23-19:26**

## The Dead Sea an important source of salt
**EZK 47:11**

## Salt water
**JAS 3:9-3:12** *See also* EZK 47:7-47:9

**JOB 30:4**
