# 2218: Jesus Christ, Son of God

A title emphasising Jesus Christ's deity, his office as Messiah and his pre-eminence as the object of the church's faith and worship and as the content of its gospel.



## ``Son of God'' in the OT


### Israel as God's son
**[EXO 4:22-4:23](bibleref:02004022-02004023)** *See also* [JER 31:9](bibleref:24031009), [JER 31:20](bibleref:24031020); [HOS 11:1](bibleref:28011001)

### The future Messiah/king as God's Son
**[PSA 2:7](bibleref:19002007)** *See also* [PSA 89:27](bibleref:19089027); [ACT 13:33](bibleref:44013033); [HEB 1:5](bibleref:58001005), [HEB 5:5](bibleref:58005005) *Jesus Christ perfectly fulfils all the OT promises concerning God's son, whether referring originally to Israel or to the Messiah.*

**[MAT 3:17](bibleref:40003017)** parallels MRK 1:11, LUK 3:22 *See also* [MAT 17:5](bibleref:40017005) parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35; [2PE 1:17](bibleref:61001017)

**[MAT 14:33](bibleref:40014033)** *See also* [MRK 15:39](bibleref:41015039) parallel MAT 27:54; [JHN 1:34](bibleref:43001034); [ROM 1:4](bibleref:45001004); [HEB 4:14](bibleref:58004014); [2JN 1:3](bibleref:63001003)
