# 8733: envy

A desire for another's gifts, possessions, position or achievements, closely associated with jealousy.



## Causes of envy


### Envy is the result of human sin
**GAL 5:19-5:21** *See also* PRO 23:17; MAT 15:19-15:20 parallel MRK 7:21-7:23; ROM 1:29; 1CO 3:3; 1TI 6:4; TIT 3:3; 1PE 2:1-2:2

### Envy arises from the good fortune of others
 GEN 26:12-26:14; ; 1SA 18:6-18:8; ; PSA 73:3; ; MAT 20:12-20:15

### Love does not envy others
**1CO 13:4**

### Envy arises from the spiritual abilities of others
**PSA 106:16-106:18**; **ACT 13:44-13:45** *See also* NUM 11:28-11:30, NUM 12:2, NUM 16:1-16:3; MAT 27:18 parallel MRK 15:10; ACT 17:5; PHP 1:15

## Examples of envy


### Cain envies Abel
**GEN 4:3-4:5**

### Esau envies Jacob
**GEN 27:41**

### Rachel envies Leah
**GEN 30:1**

### The brothers envy Joseph
**GEN 37:4**

## Results of envy
**JOB 5:2** *See also* PRO 14:30; ECC 4:4; JAS 3:14-3:16

### In some cases, envy can be used by God
**ROM 10:19, ROM 11:11, ROM 11:13-11:14** *Paul hopes that the Gentiles' response to the gospel might make Israel want its blessings as well.* *See also* DEU 32:21

## Envy forbidden
**PSA 37:1** *See also* PRO 3:31, PRO 24:1; GAL 5:26
