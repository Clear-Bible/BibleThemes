# 1090: God, majesty of

The greatness and splendour of God, revealed in his creation and mighty works of deliverance. On account of his majesty, God is worthy of praise and adoration from all people.



## God is majestic
**[HEB 8:1](bibleref:58008001)** *See also* [1SA 15:29](bibleref:09015029); [PSA 24:10](bibleref:19024010) *The word translated “glory” also means “majesty”* ; [HEB 1:3-1:4](bibleref:58001003-58001004)
