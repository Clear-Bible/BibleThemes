# 8444: honouring God

Giving the reverence and respect to God which is his due. All God's people have a duty to honour him in every aspect of their lives, while unbelievers and even animals can also honour him.



## God's people must honour him
**PSA 22:23** *See also* MAL 1:6, MAL 2:1-2:2; JHN 5:22-5:23

## Ways of honouring God


### By worshipping him
**DAN 4:34** *See also* ISA 43:22-43:24; 1TI 1:17, 1TI 6:15-6:16; REV 4:8-4:11, REV 5:6-5:14, REV 7:9-7:12

### By holy living
**1CO 6:18-6:20** *See also* ROM 12:1-12:2; 2CO 8:23; EPH 5:1-5:3; 1TH 4:7-4:8; 1PE 1:15-1:16

### By keeping the Sabbath and religious feasts
**LEV 19:30** *See also* EXO 20:8-20:11 parallel DEU 5:12-5:15; EXO 12:42, EXO 31:12-31:17; LEV 26:2; ISA 58:13-58:14

### By showing kindness to others
**PRO 14:31** *See also* DEU 26:12-26:15; MAT 10:40-10:42

### By honouring his name and his word
**ACT 19:13-19:17** *See also* JDG 13:17; ISA 26:13; MAL 3:16; ACT 13:48; 2TH 3:1

### By acknowledging him as God
**DAN 3:28-3:29** *See also* 1SA 6:4-6:5; ISA 43:20; REV 14:7

### By giving tithes and offerings to him
**PRO 3:9-3:10** *See also* DEU 26:1-26:11; MAL 3:6-3:12; 2CO 8:19-8:21

### By proclaiming his deeds
**ISA 63:7** *See also* 1CH 16:23-16:26; PSA 145:3-145:7; ISA 12:4-12:6; DAN 4:1-4:3

### By obeying him
**NUM 27:12-27:14** *See also* NUM 20:7-20:12

## God honours those who honour him
**1SA 2:27-2:30** *See also* LEV 10:1-10:3; 2CH 26:18; JHN 12:26

## God requires sincerity from those who honour him
**MRK 7:5-7:13** parallel MAT 15:1-15:9 *See also* ISA 29:13; ACT 5:1-5:11

## All people will ultimately honour God
**JER 3:17** *See also* ISA 60:9; ROM 14:9-14:12; ISA 45:23-45:24; PHP 2:9-2:11

## Jesus Christ is to be honoured as God
**MAT 17:5** parallels MRK 9:7, LUK 9:35**JHN 5:22-5:23** *See also* REV 5:12-5:13
